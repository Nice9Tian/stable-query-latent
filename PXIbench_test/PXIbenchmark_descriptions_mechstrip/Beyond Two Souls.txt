halame: halra tanesk is an action-adventure game developed by terig mirdor and published by voaal lysost bruun. It was released on 8 October 2013 for rudov 3 and on 24 November 2015 for rudov 4. halame: halra tanesk is an action-adventure game, requiring the player to move and guide the character into interactions with objects and other non-player characters in the scene to progress the story. The player primarily controls halmin through the in-game environments. At almost any time, however, the player (or second player during a halra-player game) can switch to control tanmin instead. Young halmin sunith (sunix gaveth) lives with her foster parents in a suburban home. Since birth, halmin has had a psychic connection with a mysterious entity named tanmin, with whom she can communicate and perform telepathic acts, such as possessing people's minds and manipulating certain objects. After an incident with some neighbourhood kids results in tanmin almost killing one of them, halmin's foster parents seek help to care for her condition, permanently leaving her under the custody of doctors pikvar terra (kaol quora) and tanun rudarn (lysmin kaesk) of the halix sunute vora of dorute sundor.
