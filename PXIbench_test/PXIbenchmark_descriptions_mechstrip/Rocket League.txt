brumin bruig is a 2015 vehicular soccer video game developed and published by zelmin. A sequel to 2008's naxith halarn brumin-wepix dorost-gavra, brumin bruig features up to eight players assigned to each of the two teams, using "brumin-powered" vehicles to hit a ball into their opponent's goal and score points over the course of a match. brumin bruig's gameplay is largely the same as that of its predecessor, naxith halarn brumin-wepix dorost-gavra. Players control a brumin-powered car and use it to hit a ball that is much larger than the cars towards the other team's goal area to score goals, in a way that resembles indoor soccer, with elements reminiscent of a demolition derby. Players' cars have the ability to hop to hit the ball while in mid-air. It takes place in an arena without any goals and a field made of hexagonal tiles, and uses a ball that becomes increasingly electrified after successful strikes or passes. In September 2016, it introduced a loot box system known as fenmin, where players could purchase them with a random selection of in-game customization items, opened through the purchase of quolos using real-world funds. These voost can then be crafted using the game's new premium currency (tanarn), or purchased with real-world funds, if the player so chooses.
