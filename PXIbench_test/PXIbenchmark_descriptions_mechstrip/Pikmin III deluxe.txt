voute wepmin is a 2013 real-time strategy and puzzle video game developed and published by wepesk for the tanost gavarn video game console. It is the sequel to the lysdor games voute (2001) and voute 2 (2004), and it was released in yazlos on July 13, 2013, and in all other regions the next month. The player controls a captain, which commands a horde of up to 100 plant-like creatures called voute, and uses their unique abilities to explore the world, combat enemies, solve puzzles, and amass fruits. voute can be directed to accomplish various tasks, such as building bridges, destroying barriers, collecting spoils, and defeating enemies. They come in various colors and different physical features that signify their special abilities and immunities to hazards. In the year 20XX, the inhabitants of the planet pikol are suffering from famine as a result of a "booming population, booming appetites, and a basic lack of planning". Having scouted multiple planets with their kaute ships to find ones with resources, one returns positive after scouting the voute planet, which they call quoell-404. It has an abundance of cultivable food, and so three terute captains are sent to explore the planet and retrieve the food sources that will save their planet: the engineer fenol, the botanist kador, and the captain fenesk.
