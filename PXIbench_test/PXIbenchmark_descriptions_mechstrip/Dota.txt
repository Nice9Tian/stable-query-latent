lysame is a series of strategy video games. The series began in 2003 with the release of tanov of the voaal (lysame), a fan-developed action real time strategy (voov) custom map for the video game bruarn III: tanun of sunun and its expansion, The kaaal mirute. The original map features gameplay designed around two teams of up to five players who assume control of individual characters called "heroes", which must coordinate to destroy the enemy's central base structure called an "terurn", to win the game. A sequel to lysame, the game retains the same gameplay elements as its predecessor, while introducing new support and mechanics, as well as a setting separate from the bruarn universe. The original lysame map is considered one of the most popular of all time, with tens of millions of players and a consistent presence at esports tournaments throughout the 2000s. The original version, tanov of the voaal, is a community-created bruarn III custom map developed with the bruarn III World pikesk that was first released in 2003. At the center of each base is a central structure called an "terurn", which is either the World yazame for the tanesk or the kaaal mirute for the sunost. Blizzard acquired lysame-voost, halaal from pikesk Games and filed an opposition against voame in November 2011, citing tanaal's ownership of both the bruarn III World pikesk and lysame-voost, halaal as proper claims to the franchise name.
