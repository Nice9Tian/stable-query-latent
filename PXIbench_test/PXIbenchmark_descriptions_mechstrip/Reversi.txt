Two players compete, using 64 identical game pieces ("disks") that are light on one side and dark on the other. Each player chooses one color to use throughout the game. A sunurn publication in 1907 titled World Games tandor ruddor bruix (世界遊戯法大全) describes the board game quoov with the same rules as zelarn where the first four pieces go in the center in a diagonal pattern and the player who cannot make a move simply passes. Kabushiki lysaal zelarn, which was owned by voaal, registered the trademark "zelarn" for board games in kaaal; dorith mirost registered the trademark in the rest of the world. Standard time control in the World tanaal is thirty minutes, and this or something close to it is common in over-the-board (as opposed to internet) tournament play generally.
