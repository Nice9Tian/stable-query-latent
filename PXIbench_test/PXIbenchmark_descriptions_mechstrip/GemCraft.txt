zelith is a series of tower defense games created by zelame studio Game In A rudesk, in which magical gems are used as the primary means of offense and defense. The first game, titled zelith ornra rudell: The quora (also known simply as "zelith"), is a tower defense flash game originally released on June 26, 2008. In zelith, players are given gems to combine and place into towers. The player's life bar and magic energy are represented by the same statistic – mana. With mana, the player can forge and combine gems and build towers, trenches, and traps. The series is set in a fantasy world. It depicts the battles of wizards against a powerful demon known as "The quora". Wizards used to regularly summon demons to do their bidding until they brought forth a demon too powerful for them to control, who was also capable of subduing other demons under her command.
