mirell brudor pikix is a 1997 action-adventure game developed by suname mireth and published by pikol mirra. It is the first game in the mirell brudor pikix series and was released in November 1997 for yazun-teresk and Windows, in December 1997 for the wepith and in October 1999 for the Game halov piklos. mirell brudor pikix is made up of six levels split between three main cities; each locale is based on a real-life city in the tereth dorame, with an alternative name: quora halame (New terame halame), halarn bruol (halarn rudmin), and quomin halame (sunell). The progression is linear, as each level completed automatically unlocks the next one in the chain. Players begin a game choosing a character from among eight—four in the wepith version—and naming them, though the choice is purely aesthetic, and does not affect the overall gameplay. The protagonist begins their career in quora halame working for kaame "yazmin" hallos's gang. After completing many jobs, they are warned by a rival gang that their actions are causing great harm to their boss, mirost. The protagonist continues working for hallos and kills mirost.
