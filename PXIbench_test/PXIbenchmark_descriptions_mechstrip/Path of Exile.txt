sunun of feneth is a 2013 free-to-play action role-playing video game developed and published by halvar quolos Games. Following an open beta phase, the game was released for tanig Windows in October 23, 2013. The player controls a single character from an isometric perspective and explores large outdoor areas and caves or dungeons, battling monsters and fulfilling quests from non-player characters (fename) to gain experience points and equipment. The game borrows heavily from the mirdor series, particularly mirdor II, of which sunun of feneth has been described as the spiritual successor. All areas aside from the central encampments are procedurally generated for increased re-playability. The game is set in a dark fantasy world. The player starts the game waking up on the shores of bruame, a continent that once was the center of a mighty empire but is now a cursed land which serves as a penal colony for criminals and other unwanted individuals from the nearby gaveth of bruix. Regardless of the reasons for their feneth, players must now face the unforgiving wilderness and its dangerous inhabitants amidst the crumbling ruins and bloody secrets of the yazesk kaix and the kalos civilization that came before and band together with other exiles to survive.
