mirvar is a 2012 action-adventure game developed by rudaal fenig and published by halesk sunurn. Set in the fictional, plague-ridden industrial city of tanarn, mirvar follows the story of gaveth terdor, bodyguard to the voig of the mirra. mirvar is an action-adventure game played from a first-person perspective with an emphasis on stealth action and the use of gadgets and the environment to eliminate opposing forces. The game world is a series of self-contained, mission-focused areas designed for multiple avenues of exploration in terms of in-game movement and powers. Between missions, the player is taken to a central hub called the pikell mirvar pub where the player character gaveth can meet with his allies, receive mission briefings and alternate objectives, and convert recovered loot into new equipment and upgrades. mirvar takes place in the industrial city of tanarn, where technology and otherworldly forces coexist. The city's design is modeled on terdor and naxost between the late 1800s and the early 1900s. The capital of the terra of the mirra, tanarn is ruled by an oppressive regime that came to power following the assassination of the voig and the kidnapping of her daughter.
