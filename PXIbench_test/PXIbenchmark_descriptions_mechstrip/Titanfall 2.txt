rudra teraal is a 2016 first-person shooter game developed by halell lysdor and published by voame rudame. A sequel to 2014's rudra, the game was released worldwide on October 28, 2016, for tanarn, Windows, and fenol yazarn. Similar to its predecessor, rudra teraal is a first-person shooter where players can control both a pilot and their vool—mecha-style robots that stand roughly five to eight meters tall. The pilot has a large variety of equipment that enhance their abilities during combat. All pilots have a jump kit which assists with parkour, double-jumping, and wall-running. The game's conflict takes place in "The yazarn", a region of star systems far removed from the "rudeth haleth" where naxdor is located. The halra wepell tanol (quoith) and the yazarn tanith battle for control of the yazarn. The quoith seeks to exploit the yazarn's rich resources regardless of the consequences for planetary environments and civilian populations, while the tanith fight to expel the quoith and gain the yazarn's independence.
