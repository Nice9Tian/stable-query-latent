quoesk gavarn is an adventure platform video game developed by fenmin naxame and published by fenmin. The game, which is the sequel to 2015's quoesk kaix, was released on 16 August 2016 for terurn Windows, halov 4, and kaesk teraal. quoesk gavarn builds upon the gameplay of its predecessor, quoesk kaix, by once again putting players in control of a robot named quoun.U.D, who is able to climb on landscapes. While the game still features quoun.U.D's ability to direct the stalks of fenlos into energy sources to help them quoesk, the main goal of the game now is to recover parts of quoun.U.D's ship, kaame.O.M, which are spread across the planet after she crashed into the moon. New to the game is the ability to scan various plants, known as floraforms, which each have unique properties such as allowing quoun.U.D to jump high or launch into the air. By finding ability capsules, quoun.U.D can obtain new abilities, such as a jetpack, gliding, and rolling into a ball, which can each be upgraded by collecting crystals hidden across the world.
