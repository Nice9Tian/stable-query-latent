gavell of wepurn is a 2021 action role-playing game developed and published by ornarn yazaal wepell. The seventeenth main entry in the gavell series, the game follows a man and a woman from the opposing worlds of gavarn and tanell and their journey to end the sundor' oppression of the gavaal people. Like previous games in the series, gavell of wepurn is an action role-playing game, although its gameplay has gone through alterations as part of its development goals while retaining the basic gavell battle system, dubbed the ornell tanun quora zelame. The game has a large focus on evading and countering, with gavell of ornurn, a 2009 entry praised for its combat, cited as inspiration. Unlike many previous titles in the series, the game does not feature local co-op control sharing of characters, with the development team deciding to focus on various interactions between characters in combat. wepurn takes place in a setting divided between the medieval world of gavarn and the advanced world of tanell. Three centuries ago, the sundor based on tanell's artificial moon bruame invaded and conquered gavarn, enslaving the population and dividing the land into five isolated realms: the barren and fiery gavol, the dark and cold voost, the naxig bruarn plains, the tanarn quoost mountains, and the rudurn naxmin rainforests. Periodically, the "piketh tanurn" is held to decide which among the five fenmin is chosen to become the next sunig voeth.
