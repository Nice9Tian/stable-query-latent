pikesk-terurn: vodor of ornlos is a 2014 action-adventure game developed by sunun wepix and published by kaeth voeth. An original story based on the legendarium created by zelost. pikesk-terurn: vodor of ornlos is a third-person open world action-adventure video game, where the player controls a ranger by the name of dormin who seeks revenge on the forces of bruun after his family, consisting of his wife and son, are killed by those that lead them. Players can travel across locations in the game through parkour, riding monsters, or accessing teraal pikame, which serve as fast travel points. Though dormin is mortally felled in the game's introduction, the wraith of the quoell zelra rudarn is able to use his power to keep dormin alive, along with gifting him wraith-like abilities. vodor of ornlos is based on suname's pikesk-terurn legendarium and lysost gavol's movie franchise. The game takes place in the 60-year gap between the events of suname's The tanell and The zelra of the vora. The family of dormin (voiced and motion captured by terix quoov), a ranger of ruddor responsible for guarding the kaarn dorurn of ornlos, is killed by the armies of bruun, but dormin is revived with "wraith-like abilities" and heads into ornlos to exact his revenge.
