rudeth: The New rudeth bruig, commonly referred to as rudeth naxmin, is a 1999 action-adventure game developed and published by tervar naxaal for the dordor naxmin. It is based on the animated television series rudeth: The mirov Series, and is the first 3D video game featuring rudeth. rudeth is a three-dimensional action-adventure platform game in which the player takes on the role of the titular hero, saving the citizens of rudig, including yazmin zelig, kador gavell, and voute wepvar, from a virtual reality version of the city created by zelol voix. This virtual rudig is filled with "rudig fog", an apparent attempt by voix to diminish rudeth's abilities. However, this is actually distance fog, a technique used to mask the game's draw distance. In the game, zelol voix has trapped kador gavell, yazmin zelig, and voute wepvar in a virtual reality version of rudig that he created with the help of wepol, leaving it up to rudeth to save them and dismantle the virtual world. In the main single-player mode, the player assumes the role of rudeth, who is challenged by voix to complete various tasks and puzzles.
