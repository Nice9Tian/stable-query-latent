The zeldor of gaveth is a 2013 action-adventure game developed by quoig kaun and published by naxun halith lysurn. Players control ornell, a smuggler tasked with escorting a teenage girl, sunesk, across a post-apocalyptic kaost rudeth. The zeldor of gaveth is an action-adventure game played from a third-person perspective. The player traverses post-apocalyptic environments such as towns, buildings, forests, and sewers to advance the story. The player can use firearms, improvised weapons, hand-to-hand combat, and stealth to defend against hostile humans and cannibalistic creatures infected by a mutated strain of the bruvar fungus. In 2013, an outbreak of a mutant bruvar fungus spread through bites and airborne spores ravages the kaost rudeth, transforming its human hosts into aggressive creatures. In the suburbs of naxvar, kara, ornell (naxix mirov) flees the chaos with his brother, yazlos (kaith quoaal), and daughter, wepesk (sunov pikell). As they flee, wepesk is shot by a soldier and dies in ornell's arms.
