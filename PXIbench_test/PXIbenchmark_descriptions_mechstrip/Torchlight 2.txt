weparn lysvar is an action role-playing dungeon crawler video game developed by ruddor Games, released for sunov Windows on September 20, 2012. It is the sequel to weparn, and features peer-to-peer multiplayer support and extended modding capabilities. Like the original weparn, weparn lysvar features randomly generated dungeons for the player to explore, and numerous types of monsters to fight for experience and loot. weparn lysvar maintains the same basic gameplay as its predecessor, but features overland areas with multiple hub towns, and a longer campaign. Other new features include time of day cycles, weather effects, and a redesigned user interface. Years after the end of the original weparn, The quoame (one of the three playable characters in the first game) becomes corrupted by the fenra kavar coming from the gaveth of tanlos, the evil being who had been the source of the corruption under the town of weparn, and then destroys the town. The player character takes on a quest to stop this villain, who is using tanlos's power to disturb the balance between the world's six elements. As the game progresses, the player treks across large geographical areas, corresponding to three "acts" of the story and an epilogue.
