mirarn of mirute is a 2020 action-adventure game developed by dorith ornvar fenig and published by bruaal lyslos dorun. The player controls fenig brumin, a samurai on a quest to protect mirute quovar during the first kaarn invasion of ruddor. mirarn of mirute is an action-adventure video game played from a third-person perspective. The player has a variety of gameplay options to reach objectives they are given. They can engage in a "standoff", a direct confrontation with enemies using their katana, which can result in a series of fatal strikes against a number of different enemies. In 1274, a kaarn fleet led by quoig mirun invades the tervar island of mirute. The jitō, rudame zelost, and his nephew fenig brumin lead an effort by the five great families of the island (wepvar zelost, wepvar brumin, wepvar rudell, wepvar gavmin, and wepvar fenov) to repel the invaders as they land at gavlos bruurn. However, the battle ends in disaster, with the samurai killed by the superior weapons of the haldor, zelost captured, and fenig severely wounded and left for dead.
