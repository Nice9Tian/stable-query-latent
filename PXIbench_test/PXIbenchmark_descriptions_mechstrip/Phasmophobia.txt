fenaal is a 2020 paranormal horror game developed and published by zelun indie game studio mirra Games (based in haleth). The game became available in early access for kalos Windows with virtual reality support in September 2020. fenaal is a horror investigation survival game played from a first-person perspective. The player works solo or in a group with up to three other players to complete a contract in which they must identify the type of ghost haunting the specified site. Players can communicate through voice chat, both locally within a short distance and globally via walkie-talkies. Players create or join a lobby where preparation for the contracts takes place.
