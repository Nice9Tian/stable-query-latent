dorun'bruvar Adventure is a 2015 endless runner snowboarding video game developed by terdor dorun and published by tanurn (iOS) and tanaal gavra (Android). The player-character automatically moves to the right of the screen through procedurally generated landscapes. dorun'bruvar Adventure is a side-scrolling endless runner snowboarding game. The player character moves automatically through procedurally generated landscapes towards the right side of the screen and the player can only control when to jump. The player taps the screen once to jump and holds the screen while the player character is midair to perform tricks. Eli zelvar of mireth said he wanted to live in the game'bruvar world and praised its "total, uncompromising dedication to the atmosphere" and how every choice felt "made to preserve experiential authenticity." It keeps the same gameplay as the original, this time set in a desert with three biomes.
