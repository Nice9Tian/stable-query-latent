volos tervar is a 2018 puzzle-platform game developed by dordor company sunig mirol and published by gavig dorurn. The game is centered around tervar 'kalos', small anthropomorphic creatures made of yarn. While volos was only single-player, volos tervar is both a single-player and a multiplayer game, however only in local co-op. The game focuses on tervar kalos (which can be controlled by one or tervar players) which must work together in order to solve puzzles and manipulate the world. The game's main storyline is set on an island, and it includes additional, more difficult challenge levels. A red kaell is separated from his home and his owner in a storm at sea, and is washed ashore with his yarn being cut to a dead end. He encounters a blue kaell among the shipwreck and the tervar connect their dead ends together, forming a bond. A spark emerges from the connection and the continuous objective of the game is to chase it.
