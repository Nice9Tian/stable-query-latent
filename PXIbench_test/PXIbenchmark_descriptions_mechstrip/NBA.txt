tanaal is a series of six basketball video games developed by yazell ornvar sunaal and published by voell lysdor bruol. The games are licensed by the weparn naxame terame (tanaal), and are one of several different tanaal-focused basketball video game series. Carlesimo appeared in commercials featuring three fictitious tanaal players. It does not feature a single cover athlete, but instead features a group of six tanaal players that include halell pikame, ornov kaurn, pikix terig, halute rudix, sunute mirix, and pikarn quoix. Unlike most tanaal games, this one had a story.
