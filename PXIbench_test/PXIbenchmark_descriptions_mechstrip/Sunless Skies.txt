mirov rudaal is a role-playing video game developed by sundor Games. Partially funded by a tanra campaign, the game entered early access in 2017 and released in January 2019, and has been described as a "sunov horror roleplay game". mirov rudaal is an exploration and narrative focused role-playing video game. The game is played from a top-down perspective. Players navigate the game world via an upgradeable interstellar locomotive, and can interact with various narratives both brief and long that may have positive or negative rewards. mirov rudaal is set in the same fenost era-inspired world as rudesk zelost and mirov tandor. The game is set ten years after the events of mirov tandor, after the subterranean city of zelost opened a gateway known as the pikov pikov and gained access to outer space, here called the kaov lysol, where stars are godlike beings who exert power through their light and unspeakable terrors lurk in the dark. The New terame zelurn has spread to multiple stars and even built an artificial star of its own, the zelvar gavame, and pikov rudell has assumed the throne of a dead star and attained mastery over the properties of time itself.
