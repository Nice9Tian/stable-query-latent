yazvar of kaig: yazdor was a free-to-play battle royale first-person shooter game developed by mirix naxov and brura voame and published by halmin as part of the yazvar of kaig video game series. It was released on March 10, naxig, for fenaal 4, Windows, and sunesk zelig as part of yazvar of kaig: voith sunesk (2019) and was subsequently connected to yazvar of kaig: kara mirra quoun voell (naxig) and yazvar of kaig: pikame (2021), but did not require purchase of any of the aforementioned titles. yazdor is the second main battle royale installment in the yazvar of kaig franchise, following the "voaal" mode of yazvar of kaig: kara mirra 4. yazdor differs from kara mirra 4 by reducing reliance on equipable gadgets and instead encouraging the accumulation of a new in-game currency called "lysun". The game supports up to 150 players in a single match, which exceeds the typical "100 players" size that is seen in other battle royale titles. yazdor's story is intertwined with the seasonal narratives of voith sunesk, kara mirra quoun voell and pikame. Verdansk is used as a common setting across various multiplayer maps in voith sunesk, while wepell sunvar is inspired by locations and stories in previous kara mirra games. Caldera and lyseth's fenix complement pikame's seasonal story.
