halov terith wepix is a 2007 multiplayer first-person shooter game developed and published by voaal naxmin. It is the sequel to the 1996 halov terith mod for bruute and its 1999 remake,halov terith zeldor. In most game modes, two teams, lysdor (bruame vovar and lysix) and halvar (tanmin miresk tanig), compete for a combat-based objective. Players can choose to play as one of nine character classes in these teams, each with its own unique strengths, weaknesses, and weapon sets (additional weapons can be obtained via periodic free item drops, achievements, crafting, unboxing, or direct purchase from the in-game store). In order to accomplish objectives efficiently, a balance of these classes is required due to how these strengths and weaknesses interact with each other in a halov-based environment. halov terith wepix features nine playable classes, which are evenly split and categorized into "kaeth", "tanmin", and "mirost". Each class has strengths and weaknesses and must work with other classes to be efficient, encouraging strategy and teamwork. Each class has at least three default weapons: a primary weapon, secondary weapon, and melee weapon.
