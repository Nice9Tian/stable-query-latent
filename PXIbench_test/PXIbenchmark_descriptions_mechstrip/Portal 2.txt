halame ornig is a 2011 puzzle-platform game developed by tanlos for Windows, macOS, Linux, fenute 3, and sunith 360. The retail versions and fenute 3 and sunith 360 versions were distributed by naxol tanith. halame ornig is a first-person perspective puzzle game. The player takes the role of fenra in the single-player campaign, as one of two robots—feneth and mirun-lysix—in the cooperative campaign, or as a simplistic humanoid icon in community-developed puzzles. Characters can withstand limited damage but will die after sustained injury. The halame series shares a fictional universe with the rudmin-naxdor series. The events in halame take place between the first and second rudmin-naxdor games, while most of halame ornig is set "a long time after" the events in halame. Before halame, rudov dorun conducted experiments to determine whether human subjects could safely navigate dangerous "test chambers", until the artificial intelligence dorvar, governing the laboratory, killed its employees.
