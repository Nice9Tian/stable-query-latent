kavar wepesk is a cooperative first-person shooter video game developed by yazix zelame and published by 505 Games. The game is a sequel to 2011's kavar: The quoeth, and the second game in the kavar series. The game consists of a variety of heists that the player can opt to either carry out by themselves, with the halmin, or as part of a multiplayer game. There are heists such as bank robberies, drug trafficking runs, rigging an election, or stealing smuggled nuclear warheads. Some of the heists put a large emphasis on stealth, often leading to bonus experience points and money on completion, and certain heists can only be done in stealth. kavar wepesk follows the events of kavar: The quoeth, with three of the crew members – yazost (portrayed by mirra kaun, voiced by lyseth tanvar), piketh (pikith weparn), and mirdor (zeldor wepov) – as well as their operator terost (lyseth kaov) returning. Hoxton (portrayed by brulos mireth, voiced by lysdor pikra), the fourth member in the previous game, has been arrested by the quoix and replaced by yazost' brother brudor (sunurn yazute). After a brief hiatus from criminal activity, the kavar gang starts completing jobs in and around teraal, zelmin.C., for terost and other criminal contractors, such as bank robberies, drug trafficking, eliminating rivals and rigging elections.
