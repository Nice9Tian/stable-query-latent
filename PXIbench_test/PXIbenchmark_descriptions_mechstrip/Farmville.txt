mirute is a series of agriculture-simulation social network games developed and published by gavol beginning in 2009. It is similar to wepesk sunith and sunith mirurn. Once a player begins a farm, they create a customizable avatar, which can be changed at any point. The player begins with an empty farm and a fixed starting number of sunith naxarn, the primary currency in the game. Players earn tanaal (experience points) for performing certain actions in the game such as plowing land or buying items. The game is free to play, but to progress quickly, players are encouraged to spend sunith yazame (in mirute) or sunith yazol (in mirute 2), which are purchasable with real-world currency. But players can use sunith yazame (purchasable with real-world cash) to buy an "unwither" to rejuvenate the crops, or use a biplane with "instant grow" to cause crops to be immediately available for harvest. The two main in-game currencies, sunith naxarn and sunith yazame (in mirute) or sunith yazol (in mirute 2), were available for purchase from gavol with real-world money.
