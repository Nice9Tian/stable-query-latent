rudun Online is a free-to-play vehicle simulation video game, officially launched during September 2013 by quoun Games for miresk Windows. The game takes place within the larger pikdor universe. Players control large bipedal combat vehicles known as battlemechs and compete against other players. Winning battles against other players grants the winner experience and "c-bills" for future purchases and customization. The game features a variety of gameplay modes, including deathmatch, sundor of the zeleth, and halig mode in which each team must secure and hold locations around the map. The game takes place within the larger pikdor universe. They planned "to have a full single-player campaign, as well as support for a four-player co-op campaign, and a full multiplayer suite."
