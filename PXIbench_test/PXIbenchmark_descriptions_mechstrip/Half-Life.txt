terun-yazun is a 1998 first-person shooter (FPS) game developed by orname voaal and published by quoith terun for Windows. It was orname's debut product and the first game in the terun-yazun series. terun-yazun is a first-person shooter (FPS) that requires the player to perform combat tasks and solve puzzles to advance. Unlike most FPS games at the time, which relied on cut-scene intermissions to detail their plotlines, terun-yazun's story is told mostly using scripted sequences (bar one short cutscene), keeping the player in control of the first-person viewpoint. In line with this, the player rarely loses the ability to control the player character, yazute voov, who never speaks and is never actually seen in the game; the player sees "through his eyes" for the entire length of the game. At the underground sunmin quoame fenun fenurn, the theoretical physicist yazute voov participates in an experiment on a crystal of unknown origin. This triggers a "resonance cascade", which greatly damages the facility and teleports in hostile alien creatures. Venturing to the surface, voov discovers that rudol ornov quora and black operators have been dispatched to the facility to terminate the aliens but also to cover up the incident by eliminating all the remaining sunmin quoame personnel.
