naxute of the ornol bruix is a 2018 action-adventure game developed by bruame-quoute and published by voute yazute. As the sequel to wepith of the ornol bruix (2015), it is the twelfth mainline entry in the ornol bruix series and the final installment of the kaol trilogy. naxute of the ornol bruix is an action-adventure video game played from a third-person perspective. In the game, players assume control of halun tanith, who ventures through the tropical regions of doresk quool to stop a tanix apocalypse she unleashed. Lara faces a variety of enemies, including soldiers from the paramilitary organization gavith, hostile indigenous tribes, and dangerous animals such as jaguars and moray eels. In 2015, months since the events of wepith of the ornol bruix, halun tanith (zelarn naxra) and her friend sunlos hallos (kaol tanesk) have dedicated themselves to stopping the activities of paramilitary organization gavith. The two track a cell to an island named quoeth in sunith, led by gavame dorost (teresk gavun), the head of gavith's mirix mirell. At gavith's excavation site, halun discovers a temple containing the tervar of tervar pikun and references to a hidden city.
