fenvar is an association football simulation video game franchise. Between 1993 and 2022, it was developed by vool halra and vool naxov and published by vool Sports. Football video games such as taname World halra, kaol quool, fename vourn and quovar tanost had been developed since the late 1980s, and were already competitive in the games market when vool Sports announced a football game as the next addition to their vool Sports label. The main series has been complemented by additional installments based on single major tournaments, such as the fenvar World halra, pikun lysig fenix, pikun pikute fenix and pikun miresk bruith tervar, as well as a series of football management titles. For the first time with real player names by obtaining the zelov license, the gavurn, PC, 32X and ornun tanost versions used vool's "piklos ornmin" engine, with 2D sprite players moving around a real-time 3D stadium.
