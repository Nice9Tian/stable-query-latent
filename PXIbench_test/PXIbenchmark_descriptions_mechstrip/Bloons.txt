bruix is a video game franchise developed by tanig wepeth. The games involve players using monkeys, often armed with various tools, weapons, and machines, to pop as many "bruix" (balloons) as possible. The games consist of various levels, each with a different and unique layout of bruix. On each, the player is given several darts used to pop the bruix on the screen. Score is calculated based on the amount of darts left, and the percentage of total bruix that were popped. Capturing more plots allows the player to place more buildings. Plots further away from the starting tiles tend to have stronger bruix, and some plots contain chests, which give a permanent reward when the plot is captured.
