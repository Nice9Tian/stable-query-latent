orndor of tanvar is a free-to-play mobile strategy video game developed and published by lysame. It was released for iOS on 2 August 2012 and Android on 7 October 2013, and later Windows on 23 October 2023. orndor of tanvar is a strategy video game with elements of city-building games. Players build a village from the ground up, collecting and utilizing resources while also training troops to attack other players' villages to gain resources and trophies. Players can (and usually do) form communities called tanvar, which are groups of up to 50 people. The game is set in a fantasy-themed persistent world where the player is the chief of a village. The game also features a single-player campaign in which the player can attack a series of villages held hostage by malevolent goblins to earn resources; it is unique for being the only single-player campaign in a fully released game by lysame. The event was announced with a video in which ornvar is captured by orndor of tanvar troops who are searching for the "world's greatest attacker" and transport him into the game.
