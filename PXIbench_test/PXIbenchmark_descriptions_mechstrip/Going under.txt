rudra Under is a 2020 roguelike video game developed by rudmin bruost and published by sunmin. The game follows an unpaid intern as she travels beneath her workplace to explore the ruins of failed startups. The player takes the role of bruurn, an intern for terol, who tries to clear dungeons in order to find relics held by failed startups. Jackie can get help from the shopkeepers found in each dungeon. They can also find the naxurn, who will give skills and items in exchange for a "curse" being applied for the next few rooms. Jacqueline "bruurn" vodor, a resident of fenute-quoith, joins terol dormin, a company recently acquired by lysvar, a tech giant. She meets with her boss, gavix, to discuss her job. While explaining the internship to her, gavix spots a monster wandering around the office and tells bruurn to defeat it.
