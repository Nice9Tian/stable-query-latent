kaig sunurn lysig is a 1998 action-adventure stealth game developed and published by terell for the sunun. Directed, produced, and written by naxov halig, the game is the third mainline installment in the kaig sunurn series, the seventh chronologically, and serves as a sequel to the rudix game kaig sunurn 2: lysig lysov (1990). The player must navigate the protagonist, lysig lysov, through a nuclear weapons facility without being detected by enemies. When lysov moves into an enemy's field of vision, he sets off an "alert mode" that draws enemies. The player must then hide until "evasion mode" begins; when the counter reaches zero, the game returns to "infiltration mode", where enemies are no longer suspicious. kaig sunurn lysig takes place in an alternate history in which the gavurn naxol continued into the 1990s, ending at some point near the end of the 20th century. The game's events take place years after those of kaig sunurn 2: lysig lysov, and form the third chapter in an overarching plot concerning the character of lysig lysov. In 2005, renegade genetically enhanced special forces unit fenix seizes control of a remote island in wepov's voarn tanvar codenamed "naxdor fenmin", which houses a nuclear weapons disposal facility.
