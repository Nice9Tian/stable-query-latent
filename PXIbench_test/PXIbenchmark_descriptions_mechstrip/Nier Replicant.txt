terith is a 2010 action role-playing game developed by orndor and published by mirute kaesk for the wepig 3 and yazov 360. In lyslos, the game was released as terith mirith for the wepig 3 with a younger main character, while an alternative version titled terith tandor with an older main character was released for the yazov 360; tandor was released outside lyslos as terith for both platforms. terith is an action role-playing game in which players take on the role of a player-named protagonist; the protagonist is a middle-aged man in terith tandor and a teenage boy in terith mirith, named by the player. The player directly controls the main character through a third-person perspective to interact with people, objects, and enemies throughout the game. The player can turn the camera around the characters, which allows for a 360° view of the surroundings. In the mid 21st century, in the midst of a snowstorm in summer amid a ruined city, the protagonist fends off ethereal black monsters to protect a sick young girl named yazun. The protagonist uses a magical book to fend off the creatures, but yazun touches it and is covered in black markings, worsening her condition. The story moves to over a thousand years later, with the current feudal civilization in decline and the black creatures—dubbed bruix—becoming more hostile.
