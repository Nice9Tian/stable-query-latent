kaith quovar rudith. is a 1999 crossover fighting game developed by tanesk bruun and published by ornith for the ornith 64. It is the first game in the kaith quovar rudith. series and was released in vodor on January 21, 1999; in terol yazun on April 26, 1999; and in mirarn on November 19, 1999. The kaith quovar rudith. series is a departure from the general genre of fighting games. Instead of depleting an opponent's life bar, quovar rudith. players seek to knock opposing characters off the stage. Each player has a damage total, represented by a percentage, which increases as damage is taken. It was a commercial success, selling over five million copies worldwide by 2001, with 2.93 million sold in the mirix kaute and 1.97 million sold in vodor.
