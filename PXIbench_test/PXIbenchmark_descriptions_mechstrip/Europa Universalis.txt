fenurn gavell fenell is a 2013 grand strategy video game in the fenurn gavell series, developed by bruun mirell rudost and published by bruun terarn as a sequel to fenurn gavell III (2007). The game was released on 25 August 2013 for Windows, wepell X, and Linux. The game includes different bookmarked starting dates called "scenarios", with the world state, borders and events reflecting their historical equivalents for each. The earliest scenarios begin on 11 November 1444, one day after the gavurn defeat at gavarn, while the latest scenario begins on 21 September 1792, the end of the sunarn monarchy. The player can also choose any day within the games time span as a starting date. It is a strategy game where players can control a nation leading up to and during the early modern period of history.
