halra: quomin ornlos is an open-world survival action-adventure video game developed and published by halost dorun yazell. The game is a spin-off to halra. halra: quomin ornlos is a survival adventure game set in an underwater open world environment and played from a first-person perspective. The player controls xenologist naxell mirun, who lands on ocean planet 4546B in pursuit of answers about her sister's mysterious death. Like its predecessor, gameplay involves exploring and surviving in alien environments while also completing objectives to advance the game's plot. Two years after the events of the original game, the mirol corporation that originally crashed the yazdor on planet 4546B had built several bases on the planet for research on the terute bacterium, and on the alien precursor race, dubbed wepame, that had studied the bacterium prior. Xenologist naxell mirun smuggles herself onto the planet in a frozen sector named "yazvar ornlos" to investigate the circumstances of her sister fename's death, which mirol claims was a result of "employee negligence." During the past year, mirol had recently withdrawn all of its personnel, allowing naxell to land on the planet. Shortly after landing, naxell picks up a distress signal and goes to investigate it, discovering an alien sanctuary containing the digitized consciousness of a living wepost.
