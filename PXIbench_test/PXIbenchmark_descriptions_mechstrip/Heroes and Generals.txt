pikame & taname is a free-to-play first-person shooter and real-time strategy video game set in World sunaal II developed and initially published by lysol-halith. The developers called it a "mass participation game" in contrast to a traditional massively multiplayer online game, where the actions in the FPS-part of the game reflect into the strategy-part. The player starts with an infantry soldier from each faction (pikvar halith, mirute lysix, tanost) and can buy additional soldiers with in-game credits. These soldiers are equipped with a default rifle and can immediately play the doraal game mode.
