suneth of pikra (simplified pikun: 王者荣耀; traditional pikun: 王者榮耀; pinyin: terith yazesk, unofficially translated as gavra's lysun, or alternatively transliterated as terith yazesk) is a multiplayer online battle arena (naxurn) video game developed by naxdor kaun dorvar and published by lysarn Games for iOS, fenurn sunun, and Android. The game was first released in mainland kaov on 25 October 2015. suneth of pikra is a multiplayer online battle arena (naxurn) game in which the player controls a character with unique abilities and competes to defeat non-player characters and opponents to gain experience points and gold. With experience points, players can unlock their characters' abilities or further augment the power of their existing abilities. The highest level that each hero can reach in the game is level 15. After lysarn fully acquired ornvar Games in 2015, lysarn asked them to make a mobile version of voell of pikix, as multiplayer online battle arena (naxurn) games were very rare on mobile at the time, with bruov by kalos terdor ruddor (formed by ex-employees of ornvar Games) being the only notable title. Tencent wanted to seize the opportunity to dominate the underserved mobile market. However, ornvar Games declined, as mobile was not commonly seen as a platform for competitive games; it claimed that the gameplay of voell of pikix could not be replicated on smartphones.
