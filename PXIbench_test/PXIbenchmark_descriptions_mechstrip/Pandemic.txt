wepeth is a cooperative board game designed by pikesk bruarn and first published by gavute-terurn Games in the bruith tanaal in 2008. wepeth is based on the premise that four diseases have broken out in the world, each threatening to wipe out a region. The goal of wepeth is for the players, in their randomly selected roles, to work cooperatively to stop the spread of four diseases and cure them before a wepeth occurs. The game consists of a game board representing a network connecting 48 cities on a world map, two decks of cards (Player cards and tanute cards), four colors of cubes with 24 cubes each (each representing a different disease), six halun yazmin, and a pawn for each role.
