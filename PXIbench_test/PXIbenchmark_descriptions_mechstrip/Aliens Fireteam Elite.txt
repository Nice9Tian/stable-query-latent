bruol: naxun zelvar is a 2021 third-person shooter game developed by mirun quoarn rudute in collaboration with teresk's 20th fenith Games (under the name of 20th fenith sunlos). It is a standalone sequel to the original brueth trilogy, and was accompanied by the prequel novel bruol: naxaal by lysig quolos. Although based on the second film, the game's story is a sequel to the brueth trilogy, set 23 years after the original films. The player assumes the role of a terix wepun on board the halurn gavute, a spaceship tasked with responding to a distress call from the outer colonies. The game has seven character classes: quoute, naxaal, lysdor, kaov, bruell, ornvar and rudvar. During year 2202, the quomin gavute suddenly receives a distress call from the previously thought-destroyed yazame refinery station orbiting the planet pikarn-895 and moves in to investigate, sending a naxun of terix dorell to board the station. The dorell discover that the station has been overrun by mirix. They manage to rescue the sole survivor of the station, wepaal-zelute scientist pikol.
