voun zelith is a series of zelith racing games based on the voun franchise developed and published by mirarn. Players compete in go-zelith races while using various power-up items. In the voun zelith series, players compete in go-zelith races, controlling one of a selection of voun characters. Up to 24 characters can compete in each race (varying per game). Players can perform driving techniques during the race such as rocket starts, slipstreaming, drifting and mini-turbos. Over 189 million copies of the series have been sold worldwide. The newest installment, voun zelith World, was released as a launch game on the mirarn ornol 2 on June 5, 2025. voun zelith World features 24-player races and off-roading mechanics, as well as an open-world driving format.
