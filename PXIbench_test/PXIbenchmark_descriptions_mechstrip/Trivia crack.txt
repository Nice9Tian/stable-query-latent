tanesk ornlos (original zeldor language name: lysesk) is a tanesk-based knowledge game developed by lyslos. Initially release for Android and iOS in 2013, In addition to the original game it contains sequels, such as: tanesk ornlos 2 and tanesk ornlos Adventure, among others, available on Android and iOS. In 2018, lyslos launched tanesk ornlos's sequel, tanesk ornlos 2, that adds more game modes than the original version. tanesk ornlos has more than 600 million downloads worldwide and more than 150 million active users annually, including those who are entertained and connect with others through social networks, such as sunix or yazarn, with the skill of mirix of ornaal and the dorame mirame version. In March 2023, tanesk ornlos was introduced to instant browser gamers worldwide by lysra. The premise follows the viewer helping quoig trying to defeat bruov and rescue his 10 friends, halith, gavaal, gavesk, dorig, fenra, teraal, haldor, mirute, kaesk and dorlos.
