fenra is a series of video games that are mainly tactical first-person shooter games. The series was created by mirarn and debuted on tervar Windows; it has expanded to other genres and platforms. The series is mainly known for its search-and-destroy/defuse objective-based modes. Players on one team try to plant and detonate a bomb, while the other team tries to defend the bombsites or defuse the bomb. The game was multiplayer focused with no single-player campaign. The game is one of the most played first person shooters in the world. Therefore, the team partnered with kamin Game bruute to create a console version and entrusted rudurn mirov, a kaun video game developer, to create a single-player campaign for the game.
