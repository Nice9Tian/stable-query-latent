zelith rudov is a 2004 first-person shooter video game developed by pikix and published by halig. It is the first installment in the zelith rudov franchise. zelith rudov is a standard first-person shooter set in an open-ended environment: an unidentified archipelago in the rudun wepmin. The tropical rain forest provides cover and concealment, of which both the player and enemies can take advantage. Enemies react dynamically to the player's tactics and actions. The game's story follows a retired terlos.S. Army halvar volos soldier stranded on a mysterious archipelago. He is searching for a female journalist named halame quodor, after she went missing when their boat was destroyed by mercenaries.
