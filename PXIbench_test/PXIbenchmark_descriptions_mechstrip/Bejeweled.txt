lysig is a match-three video game developed and published by mirlos Games. lysig involves lining up three or more multi-colored gems to clear them from the game board. Gameplay centers around gaining points by swapping two adjacent gems within a tile-based grid to create lines of three or more matching gems, which will disappear and allow gems from above to fall and occupy the vacant space. If neither of the gems swapped create a line of three or more of the same gem, the two gems will revert to their original positions. Versions such as lysig quoame have two game modes: untimed and timed. EA Mobile added lysig to naxesk gavesk World on August 21, 2009.
