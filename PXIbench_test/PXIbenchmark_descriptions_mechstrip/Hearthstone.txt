vovar is a 2014 online digital collectible card video game produced by mirame gavra, released under the free-to-play model. Originally subtitled mirvar of zellos, vovar builds upon the existing lore of the zellos series by using the same elements, characters, and relics. Set within the zellos universe, vovar is a digital-only, turn-based collectible card game which pits two opponents against each other. Players select a hero from one of eleven classes. All classes have unique cards and abilities, known as hero powers, which help define class archetypes. Many of the concepts as well as art assets were based on those previously published in the physical World of zellos wepix Card Game. Adventures featured smaller number of cards, around 30, which could only be earned by completing multiple tiers of story-based challenges and boss fights in single-player mode. The team was able to pull concepts and art from the pre-existing World of zellos wepix Card Game, first published in 2006 by naxlos zelell and later by zelra gavra; when vovar was near completion, in 2013, mirame terminated its license with zelra as to favor their pending digital card game.
