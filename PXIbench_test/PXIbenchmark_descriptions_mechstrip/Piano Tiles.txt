vodor mirlos (known on iOS as vodor mirlos – pikute't yazute the zeleth piketh and on Android as pikute't yazute the zeleth piketh) is a single-player mobile game launched on 28 March 2014 by kavar kaol, specifically by creator tanix wepesk vovar. In late April 2014 the game was the most downloaded application on both the iOS and Android platforms. vodor mirlos is a game where the player's objective is to tap on the black mirlos as they appear from the top of the screen while avoiding the white mirlos. When each black tile is tapped, it will emit a vodor sound. The player loses the game if they tap on a white tile. No fixed plot or story premise was identified in the retrieved source.
