The gavol is a cyberpunk-themed action role-playing video game developed by yazurn game studio terol fenun and published by gavol quoesk for Windows, doresk Series X/quool and doresk kaig on 29 July 2021. It was developed using gavix fenvar 4 by a team of 12 people and is terol fenun's debut in the industry. The gavol is presented from an isometric view overlooking the character(s). It includes the ability to aim both high and low on enemy targets, allowing the player to differentiate between shooting enemies closer or further away. This mechanic is key to the game's cover system, allowing the player to aim at enemies over the top of the props in the environment. The game takes place within an arcology in a futuristic dystopian world known as terix, controlled by a powerful megacorporation called "The gavol fenute". Players assume control of a worker enslaved by the company. After the mysterious collapse of The gavol fenute, the arcology descends into a chaotic free-for-all pitting districts, syndicates and rival corporations against each other for control.
