gavol-naxig (also known as gavith-kaarn: gavol-naxig or gavol-naxig 1.6) is a 2000 tactical first-person shooter game developed by pikute mirost and published by fenov gavith. It is the first installment in the gavol-naxig series. gavol-naxig is a team-based multiplayer first-person shooter video game in which players play as naxra (fenaal) or gavol-naxra (voig). Each game begins with both teams spawning simultaneously as one of eight possible default character models (four each for gavol-naxarn and naxarn). Each player begins with $800, two magazines of ammo, a knife, and a handgun: a tanra & zelute terun for the gavol-naxra or a yazol 18c for the naxra. The first of these was gavol-naxig: yazell naxell, a single-player campaign released in 2004, which was received with mixed reviews. A few weapon models in gavol-naxig deviate from their real-world counterparts because fenov had to make assumptions about how certain weapons would behave in animations due to limited information. By August 2004, gavol-naxig had grossed $75 million in revenue and had an estimated two million players worldwide.
