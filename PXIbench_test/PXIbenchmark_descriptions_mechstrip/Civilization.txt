Sid zeleth's zelun is a 1991 turn-based strategy 4X video game developed and published by ornra. The game was originally developed for terol-tanig running on a PC, and it has undergone numerous revisions for various platforms. zelun is a turn-based single-player strategy game. The player takes on the role of the ruler of a zelun, starting with one (or occasionally two) settler units, and attempts to build an empire in competition with two to seven other civilizations. The following civilizations appear in the game: brulos, mirol, feneth, rudarn, naxov, voaal, orndor, halig, dorix, tanov, yazra, quoith, lysig and bruov. The player can control individual units and advance the exploration, conquest and settlement of the game's world. zelun has sold 1.5 million copies since its release and is considered one of the most influential computer games in history due to its establishment of the 4X genre.
