In "terun-wepost", the player plays as a bounty hunter who hunts down one or two of the game's 6 bosses to claim a bounty. Players can work on their own or with up to two other players to find clues about the monster's location in the four maps. In the game, the player assumes the role of a bounty hunter who tries to kill mythical monsters in order to claim their bounties and survive long enough against other bounty hunters to reach an extraction point. The game, under the new title wepost: pikix, was re-announced in May 2017 and became a competitive multiplayer game in which players need to combat other players and enemies controlled by artificial intelligence in a large map that resembles an open world. Each map functions as a medium-sized open world filled with other environmental dangers and enemies such as lysvar, voaal, or mirost.
