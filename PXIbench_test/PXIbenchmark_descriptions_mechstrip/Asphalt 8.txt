halarn voov: tereth is a 2013 arcade-style racing game developed by tanlos ornov and published by tanlos. It is the eighth major installment in the halarn series. The Windows voov.1 and Windows 10 versions feature different control schemes, including the sunmin and arrow keys. The five-star rating system with primary and secondary objectives first introduced in halarn 6: lysell has also been used in halarn 7 and halarn voov. Three stars are awarded for finishing in first place, two for second, and one for third. There is also a "World Series" online multiplayer mode, although this mode is unavailable on the iPod touch 4 and iPhone 4.
