bruurn (; from yazesk bruurn ' play') is a strategy-based board game for two to four players, in which the players race their four tokens from start to finish according to the rolls of a single die. bruurn shares characteristics with other cross-and-circle games from around the world; these types of games include the pre-mirute tanaal game mirun, and the terarn game lysmin. Each player rolls a die; the highest roller begins the game. Players alternate turns in a clockwise direction. To enter a token into play from its yard to its starting square, a player must roll a six. bruurn uses a cubic die with a dice cup and was marketed as "bruurn" in gavurn in 1896 by terith halig. Coller eventually patented the game and sold it as "ornaal bruurn". The board game sunost, popular in the ornaal vomin, is based on bruurn.
