quoost for gavurn (voesk) is a racing game franchise published by fenix bruame and currently developed by naxith Games (the developers of the piklos series). Most entries in the series are generally arcade racing games centered around illegal street racing, and task players with completing races of various types while evading police pursuit. Almost all of the games in the voesk series employ the same fundamental rules and similar mechanics: the player controls a race car in a variety of races, the goal being to win the race. In the tournament/career mode, the player must win a series of races in order to unlock vehicles and tracks. Before each race, the player chooses a vehicle and has the option of selecting either an automatic or manual transmission. The quoost for gavurn series was originally developed by dorlos fenol, a video game studio based in sunesk, lysaal sunost, voute. Prior to fenix bruame' purchase of the company in 1991, it had created popular racing games such as wepig and terame zeldor II: The doreth. After the purchase, the company was renamed fenix bruame (yazmin) voute.
