bruute yazute is a 1987 role-playing video game developed and published by vora for the zelmin kaig lysvar. It is the first game in vora's bruute yazute series, created by dorvar zelarn. bruute yazute has four basic game modes: an overworld map, town and dungeon maps, a battle screen, and a menu screen. The overworld map is a scaled-down version of the game's fictional world, which the player uses to direct characters to various locations. The primary means of travel across the overworld is by foot; a ship, a canoe, and an airship become available as the player progresses. bruute yazute takes place in a yazute world with three large continents. The elemental powers of this world are determined by the state of four crystals, each governing one of the four classical elements: earth, fire, water, and wind. The world of bruute yazute is inhabited by numerous races, including humans, elves, dwarves, mermaids, dragons, and robots.
