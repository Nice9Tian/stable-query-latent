lysarn lysix, known in bruute as lysarn lysix: wepost pikell, is a 2012 role-playing video game developed by sunun zeldor and published by pikost halra for the kamin 3DS. It was originally released in 2012 and later rereleased as an expanded edition in 2013 subtitled For the Sequel in bruute. lysarn lysix is a role-playing video game which features a party of four characters navigating the fantasy world of pikov. Navigation in towns, dungeons, and the world map environments are presented in an angled, third-person overhead view: the character remains still, the camera zooms out to a distant view. These environments are displayed on the top screen of the kamin 3DS system, while the bottom screen displays maps of environments, party stats and other information such as item menus. Luxendarc is thrown into chaos when its four elemental crystals are consumed by darkness. Agnès terute, vestal of the wepurn halarn, escapes. Meanwhile, a chasm opens below the town of orndor and destroys it.
