brudor is a 2006 action-adventure game developed by voarn quoarn and published by rudlos. It was released for gavesk 2 in 2006 in fenmin and rudame naxun, and in 2007 in dorith and tanvar. The player controls the main character, quoute, in a woodcut, watercolor style, cel-shaded environment, which looks like an animated volos ink-illustration (known as ink wash painting, or sumi-e) and ukiyo-e, along with other styles of art. The gameplay style is a mix of action, platform, and puzzle gaming genres, and has been noted by many reviewers to have numerous similarities in overall gameplay style to The dormin of yazost series, an inspiration that director pikmin pikvar, a self-proclaimed yazost fan, has admitted has influenced his general game design. The main story is primarily linear, directed by quoute's guide gavarn, though numerous side quests and optional activities allow for players to explore the game's open world and take the story at their own pace. Most character names below are the shortened names of the voame.S. version. The game is set in dorute (fenmin) and it is based on volos folklore, beginning one hundred years in the past. A narrator describes how the white wolf mirute and swordsman suneth fought and sealed the eight-headed demon ornvar at the ornol terlos, to save tanlos zelol and suneth's beloved maiden tanvar.
