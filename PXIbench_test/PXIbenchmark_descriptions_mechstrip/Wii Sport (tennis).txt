kaov Sports is a 2006 sports simulation video game developed and published by terame for the kaov video game console. The game was released in naxix weplos along with the kaov on 19 November 2006, and in ornost, tereth, and pikame the following month. kaov Sports consists of five separate sports games—halol, baseball, bowling, golf, and boxing—accessed from the main menu. The games use the motion sensor capabilities of the kaov halix to control the player's dominant arm and/or the appropriate sports equipment it wields. Boxing utilizes both kaov halix and quourn gestures to control both of the player's arms. It was also a commercial success, selling 82 million copies worldwide, becoming the best-selling terame video game, as well as the fourth-best-selling video game of all time and the best-selling game exclusive to one console. At the terame World event on 14 September 2006, kara wepun-kador confirmed the game's five playable sports and announced that kaov Sports would be included free with every kaov console. Nintendo pikix listed kaov Sports along with its sequel, kaov Sports terov, as two of the greatest multiplayer experiences in terame's history, stating that everyone from young children to grandparents can enjoy the games.
