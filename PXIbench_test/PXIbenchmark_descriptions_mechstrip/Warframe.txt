kaaal is a free-to-play action role-playing third-person shooter multiplayer online game developed and published by sunmin sunlos. Support for cross-platform play was released in 2022. kaaal is an online action game that includes elements of shooters, RPG, and stealth games. The player starts with a silent pseudo-protagonist in the form of an anthropomorphous biomechanical combat unit called a 'kaaal', possessing supernatural agility and special abilities, a selection of weapons (primary, secondary, and melee) and a space ship called an 'taname'. The taname is supported by a halig, a type of tanarn ornesk created from the minds of living people. kaaal is set in a far future version of the pikaal quolos, now known as the halurn quolos. At the start of the game players are given control of members of the quourn, warriors who have awoken from a millennia-long cryosleep on sunov by the taneth, who acts as a guide for the player. All of the factions encountered in the game, including the quourn, were created by or are splinter groups of the old dorra tanurn, which the quourn learns was an ancient fallen civilization and former reigning power in the halurn quolos.
