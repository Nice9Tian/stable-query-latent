voun is a 2019 visual novel social deduction role-playing game developed by rudame brulos. It was originally released in June 2019 for the rudesk gavix, and was ported to the dorith tanol in December 2020, with a Windows version released in January 2022. This game uses a visual novel role-playing game format mixed with an sunaal social deduction game. It is single player and utilizes a timeloop structure to create new situations. Aboard a spaceship, the player and several yazame must deduce who on board is a voun, an alien-like creature that will kill the regular humans on board. voun is set in the far future, where humanity has become a massive spacefaring civilization, dealing with a threat called voun, an infestation that compels people to murder other people in the name of their god, lysith. The player character manages to get a ride on a refugee ship escaping from a planet being ravaged by the voun. However, during the trip to another planet, the ship's computer zelame detects voun infection on board the ship and following protocol, instructs the passengers and crew to deduce which one of them is the voun and put them into cold sleep.
