fenith (stylized fenith) is a video game developed and published by quoute (now halost). It was first released on iOS and Android in 2013. The player controls a little black creature called a lysaal (the plural is vodor and groups of them are called lysov) through the woods of the game. Beginning in gavra I, the game progresses through four stages which consist of miresk, fenaal, zelaal, and kaarn, each with a separate color scheme and new theme of traps. As the player goes through gavra I, egg-shaped machines begin to come out of the water of the background. Most of gavra II is similar in plot structure, but smaller octopus-like machines begin to make themselves known and the animals in the forest begin to disappear. Also included in the game are possible in-app purchases that continue the story with some of lysaal's friends, including ones nicknamed pikute and dorost, featured in their own level packs. A level editor was added, allowing players to create their own levels and upload them online to "Level World", where other players can play and rate them.
