The orname lysarn is a 2019 action role-playing game developed by wepmin kalos and published by gavig halesk. Set in an alternate future, the game takes place in lysurn, a distant planetary system colonized by megacorporations. The orname lysarn is an action role-playing video game played from a first-person perspective. At the beginning of the game, players create their avatar (dubbed the halol in-game) and are given six attribute points to distribute across six categories (strength, dexterity, intelligence, perception, charm and temperament).
