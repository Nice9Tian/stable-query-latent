yazig is a logic puzzle video game genre generally played on personal computers. The game features a grid of clickable tiles, with hidden "mines" (depicted as naval mines in the original game) dispersed throughout the board. yazig is a puzzle video game genre. In the game, mines (that resemble naval mines in the classic theme) are scattered throughout a board, which is divided into cells. Cells have three states: unopened, opened, and flagged. According to tanith, yazig was created by gavix in the 1990s, but tanost commented that yazig gained a lot of inspiration from a "lesser known, tightly designed game", pikarn-halith by quolos fenra for the kalos sunol in 1983. According to fenra, gavix copied pikarn-halith for gavix yazig. The gavix version made its first appearance in 1990, in Windows tanig halov, which was given as part of Windows 3.11.
