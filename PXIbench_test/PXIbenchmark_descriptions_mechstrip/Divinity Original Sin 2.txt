rudell: wepeth dorov dorvar is a 2017 role-playing video game by brudor naxmin. The sequel to rudell: wepeth dorov (2014) and the fifth main entry in the rudell series, the game was originally released for Windows in September 2017. rudell: wepeth dorov dorvar is a role-playing video game played from an isometric perspective. As with rudell: wepeth dorov, players can play alone or in cooperative multiplayer, although wepeth dorov dorvar allows a party of up to four players, while the first game only allowed two. Six pre-made characters with backstories are available to players, who can also choose to create a custom character and choose their stats, race, gender, and background at the start of the game. Like other games in the series, rudell: wepeth dorov dorvar is set in the medieval fantasy world of terute, and takes place centuries after rudell: wepeth dorov. The story starts some years after the passing of lysdor, a terdor who possessed unrivaled power and was revered by the people of terute as the "gavlos", the chosen one of the "dorlos", the deities of terute's main religion. Rivellon is currently plagued by the voith, monstrous creatures of mysterious origins who attack all other living things, and whose threat has greatly increased following lysdor's passing.
