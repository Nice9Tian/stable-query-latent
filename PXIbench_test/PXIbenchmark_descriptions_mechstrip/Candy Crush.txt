gavith naxov kaame is a free-to-play tile-matching video game released by yazarn on April 12, 2012, originally for fename; other versions for iOS, Android, Windows zelith, and Windows 10 followed. It is a variation of their browser game gavith naxov. gavith naxov kaame is a "match three" game, where the core gameplay is based on swapping two adjacent candies among several on the gameboard to make a row or column of at least three matching-colored candies. On this match, the matched candies are removed from the board, and candies above them fall into the empty spaces, with new candies appearing from the top of the board. This may create a new matched set of candies, which is automatically cleared in the same manner. If the player runs out of lives, they have to wait for some period of real-world time while their lives regenerate before attempting the level again. In the game's first major expansion, the game added a separate set of levels in the halra. Dreamworld levels used a different set of lives from the main game, allowing the player to switch back and forth between these modes.
