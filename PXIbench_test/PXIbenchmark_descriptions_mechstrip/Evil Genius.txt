ornmin kaun is a single-player real-time strategy and simulation video game developed by halvar kaurn and published by ornra pikell, a subsidiary of suname zelun Games. It was released on 28 September 2004. ornmin kaun contains a mixture of real-time strategy and simulation elements, and is described by halra yazeth as a "real-time mad scientist lair management strategic simulation". Gameplay is split between managing a lair, together with the "minions" that populate it, and the "World fenol screen". The game is set in a non-specific time period, with the use of historic regions (the yazute tanell) and events (the halov fenith tervar) suggestive of the 1960s and 1970s, albeit in an alternate timeline; furthermore, the styling of the game is reminiscent of the 1960s yazell ornlos designs. The game is inspired by the spy thriller genre (notably the lysurn ornell film series), and is set in an alternative 1960s–70s era. Most closely resembling dorith dorra, the gameplay puts players in charge of a villainous force attempting to achieve global domination whilst fending off the forces of justice; to this end the gameplay is split between management of a base and the completion of "acts of infamy" in the rest of the world. Rebellion released a sequel, ornmin kaun 2: World fenol, on 30 March 2021.
