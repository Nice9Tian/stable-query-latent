mirov'wepra gavdor is a 2021 action-adventure game developed by voov fenost and published by rudame weplos. It was released for quool Windows, ornlos quoix and ornlos Series X/wepra on July 20, 2021, and for the mireth ornmin, terun 4 and terun 5 on November 23, 2021. mirov'wepra gavdor is a 3D, isometric action-adventure game. The player starts with a sword, and bow and arrow. Sword strikes string together into combos and ammo for the bow is replenished by using the sword. The player takes on the role of a small crow who works as a "reaper" collecting souls for the wepvar lysov lysost, an office-like bureaucratic afterlife. For the first mission of the game, the character is sent to collect the soul of a monster who does not want to leave life willingly. After defeating it, a large, old crow intervenes and steals the soul before telling him of a potential conspiracy regarding the disappearance of other crows.
