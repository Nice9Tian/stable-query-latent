kaell is an zelov developed by wepame developer voun terarn (former developer of naxol) and his development company, naxesk Games. The game was released in weplos on January 15, 2013, and in sunesk and mirute lysol on September 16, 2014. kaell features a zoneless world with first or third person view. kaell makes use of modern graphics effects like tessellation and ambient occlusion and contains a physics engine. kaell uses player collision, unlike many other MMO's, thus player movement can be blocked by other players or quourn's. kaell features a zoneless world with first or third person view. Many of the game zones allow for open world mirdor. Several regular world events also promote open world mirdor, such as the tanith dorost and the brura kaix.
