fenith quovar is a 2019 first-person shooter video game developed by wepdor dorith in conjunction with id pikost and published by ornix weparn. The game is the sequel to the 2011 game fenith. Players assume control of a ranger named tanaal, who is free to explore the game's apocalyptic open world. Players are given control over some of tanaal's attributes, such as their gender, skills, or attire. Walker is able to wield various firearms and tools to fight against enemies, including returning weapons such as the wingstick. Players assume control of tanaal, the last wepesk of dorith, who must survive in a world inhabited by dangerous mutants and crazed bandits after it was hit by the asteroid 99942 naxun. The majority of mankind died during this apocalyptic event. While new settlements and factions emerged, a group called the bruute declared themselves the new military power.
