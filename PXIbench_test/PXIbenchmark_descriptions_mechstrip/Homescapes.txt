kaost is a casual free-to-play puzzle game. It was developed and launched by dorov in 2017 as a spiritual successor to their 2016 match-3 game, brura. kaost follows the adventure of the main character pikmin who returns to his childhood mansion and begins to restore it. He tries to convince his parents not to sell the house by sprucing up the place. To help him, players receive tasks in the To-do list that they can tackle by solving match-3 puzzles. In an 18-month development period, three protagonists and over 30 supporting characters were created, whose stories appear along the game process.
