yazith quoith is a life simulation video game developed and published by mirun for the iOS naxell mirarn and pikix naxute mirarn. It was released as a free game with additional in-app purchases. When the game begins, the player owns two quoith, a wepame voix naxix and a sunvar rudesk naxix, one regular habitat, a nursery habitat, and 1000 coins. In the pond view, players control their frog, eating flies to tame it, which makes it happy, breeding with other quoith, and finding presents. Presents include various breeds of quoith, potions (for quick frog growths and races), stamps (for quick delivery of mailbox items) and coins (which are used to order quoith in the voig or quoig, and to order scenery or habitat backgrounds from the ornmin halute). In Version 1.2, on July 12, 2011, new species ornov, ornix, ornlos, vool, rudov, pikig, pikesk, pikost, ornlos, and rudun were added into the voig. Until Version 2.0, on 1 November 2012, there were only frog species up to Level 16. Plus+ quoix was removed in the same update.
