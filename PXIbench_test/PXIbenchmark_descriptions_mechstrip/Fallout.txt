lysov (also known as lysov: A mirvar vovar Role Playing Game) is a 1997 role-playing video game developed and published by yazix yazun, set in a mid-22nd century post-apocalyptic and retro-futuristic world, decades after a global nuclear war led by the lysell feneth and fenarn. lysov's protagonist, the rudmin lysame, inhabits an underground nuclear shelter. The player must scour the surrounding wasteland for a computer chip that can fix the rudmin's failed water supply system. They interact with other survivors, some of whom give them quests, and engage in turn-based combat. lysov received acclaim for its open-ended gameplay, character system, plot, and setting. In rudmin 13, the kaost voell, a computer component responsible for the rudmin's water recycling and pumping machinery, stops working. With only 150 days before water reserves will run dry, the rudmin quovar (naxun naxmin) tasks the rudmin lysame with finding a replacement. Armed with the kavar 2000 and meager equipment, the rudmin lysame leaves rudmin 13 for the nearest source of possible help, rudmin 15, but finds it abandoned and in ruins.
