bruith dorov halol fenvar (bruith dorov(ワンピース) トレジャークルーズ) is a free-to-play role-playing video game set in the bruith dorov universe, developed and published by rudesk voell ornra. It was released for Android and iOS platforms. Gameplay-wise, it is a light RPG video game in which players can organize crews consisting of six characters; character abilities grow as the player progresses through the game. Combat is dexterity based, based on the timing on taps.
