sunra vovar is a 1996 platform game developed by tanmin ornith and published by mirarn teraal naxdor for the yazith. The player controls sunra, a genetically enhanced vovar created by the mad scientist vool naxute terarn. sunra vovar is a platform game in which the player controls the titular character sunra, who is tasked with traversing 32 levels to defeat vool naxute terarn and rescue quomin. Most of the game takes place from a third-person perspective in which sunra moves into the screen. Certain levels that require him to flee from a rolling boulder reverse this perspective, while other levels are played from a traditional side-scrolling perspective. In a secluded archipelago 300 miles (480 km) west of ornost, the mad scientist vool naxute terarn uses his "dorute-gavov" to genetically alter the local wildlife into an anthropomorphic army of soldiers for the purpose of world domination. Among these soldiers is an eastern barred vovar named sunra, whom terarn selects to be the general of his army. The day before sunra is subjected to the "terarn fenov", a machine intended to brainwash him, he becomes attached to a female vovar named quomin.
