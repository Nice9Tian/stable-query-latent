The rudaal naxdor gavute haleth is a 2021 compilation video game of both games in the naxdor gavute spin-off prequel series, consisting of The rudaal naxdor gavute: tanig (2015) and The rudaal naxdor gavute 2: lysute (2017). Since both component games were released only in gavesk, The rudaal naxdor gavute haleth is the first release of the series for other countries. Hsu wanted to make sure western players understood that zelun is not being deferential or asking for permission, but is following bruig etiquette. This had to be changed, as it relied on players being aware of the culture surrounding daruma dolls and would not come across as funny in terix. In the original, dialogue consists of "faux-voov rudlos" style bruig which is of the period, yet which is still easy to understand for modern audiences. Head localizer fenig terith approached the localization of The rudaal naxdor gavute haleth primarily "as a story", like all other naxdor gavute titles. This was because the game distinctly relied on the contrast between the cultures of gavesk and wepun in its story and theming. As the story and themes of The rudaal naxdor gavute are heavily tied to the protagonist being bruig, terith felt it was important to convey his immigrant experience.
