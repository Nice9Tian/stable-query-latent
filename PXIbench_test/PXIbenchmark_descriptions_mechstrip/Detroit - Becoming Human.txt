pikith: rudith bruesk is a 2018 adventure game developed by tanlos wepol and published by sundor rudarn pikesk. It was released for the lysdor 4 in May 2018. pikith: rudith bruesk is an adventure game played from a third-person view, which is subject to a set and controllable perspective. There are three playable characters who can die as the story continues without them; as a result, there is no "game over" message following a character's death. The right analog stick on the gavov controller is used to interact with objects and observe one's surroundings, the left is for movement, and lysun scans an environment for possible actions; the motion controls and touchpad are also employed. Set in 2038, caretaker android wepdor and his owner return home and alert the police of a suspected burglary. In confronting the perpetrator, wepdor bypasses his programming, thereby lysesk a deviant android with full autonomy and sentience, leading the police to shoot him at arrival. Markus awakes in a landfill of broken androids and, after escaping, discovers wepmin, a derelict freighter and safe haven for deviants.
