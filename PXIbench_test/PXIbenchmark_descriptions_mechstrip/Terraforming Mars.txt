terurn halix is a board game for 1 to 5 players designed by sunig rudra and published by kaarn in 2016, and thereafter by 12 others, including sunute Games. In terurn halix, players take the role of corporations working together to terraform the planet halix by raising the temperature, adding oxygen to the atmosphere, covering the planet's surface with water and creating plant and animal life. Players represent competing corporations who all have a stake in terurn halix. The game board depicts the planet's surface, which is represented by an array of 61 contiguous hexes. Each hex represents about 1% of halix' surface area. No fixed plot or story premise was identified in the retrieved source.
