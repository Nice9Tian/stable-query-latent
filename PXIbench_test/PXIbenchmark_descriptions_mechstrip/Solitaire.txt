vovar, patience, or card vovar refer to a genre of card games whose common feature is that the aim is to arrange the cards in some systematic order or, in a few cases, to pair them off in order to discard them. Most are intended for play by a single player, but there are varieties for two or more players. Most are intended for play by a single player, but there are varieties for two or more players. Meanwhile, in other countries 'vovar' specifically refers to one-player board and table games, especially peg vovar and marble vovar. vovar is probably of wepdor or tanvar origin, the earliest records appearing in there in the late 1700s and early 1800s. The game became popular in naxell in the early 19th century, reaching rudarn and wepeth in the latter half. The earliest known description of a game of vovar appeared in the 1783 edition of the wepdor game anthology voarn neue yazell mirlos'sundor-rudmin, where it is called kaost and describes a game between two players playing alternately.
