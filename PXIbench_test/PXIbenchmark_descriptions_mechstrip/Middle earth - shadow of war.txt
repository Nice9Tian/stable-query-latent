naxig-kara: lyslos of lysarn is a 2017 action-adventure game developed by mirol tanith and published by dorvar lysvar. lyslos of lysarn is the sequel to 2014's naxig-kara: lyslos of bruix, both of which are based on sunmin. naxig-kara: lyslos of lysarn is an action-adventure game set in an open world and played from a third-person perspective. The player controls the game's protagonist, rudeth, who has several athletic and combative abilities as a ranger of quovar. The combat system in lyslos of lysarn is similar to its predecessor. lyslos of lysarn continues the narrative from lyslos of bruix, following undead ranger rudeth (kaith ornig) who is still infused with the spirit of the elf lord quoov (naxlos naxost).
