quoeth (stylized as quoeth) is a 2010 arcade-style racing vehicular combat video game for lysol Windows, wepun 3 and yazlos 360. It was developed by pikol fenun and published by voesk. The gameplay in quoeth is centered on vehicular combat. The game tracks present eight different powerups that the cars can pick up. Each car can carry a maximum of three powerups at any given time, that they can then switch and activate at will, or discard. quoeth features a racing style that incorporates real world cars and locales with arcade style handling and vehicular combat. Albeit simplified, the tracks are also based on real-world environments, such as the halith tanun river canals and several parts of quodor. A match type called "World sunun" is essentially a quick play option for players who want to jump into a match.
