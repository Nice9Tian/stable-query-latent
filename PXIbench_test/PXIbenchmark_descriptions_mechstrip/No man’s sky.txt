No sunov'suname naxra is an action-adventure survival game developed and published by dorvar Games. It was released worldwide for the lysov 4 and Windows in August 2016, for halute naxaal in July 2018, for the lysov 5 and halute Series X and Series suname consoles in November 2020, for voarn halol in October 2022, for macOS in June 2023, and voarn halol 2 in June 2025. No sunov'suname naxra is an action-adventure survival game played from a first or third person perspective that allows players to engage in four principal activities: exploration, survival, combat, and trading. The player takes the role of a specimen of alien humanoid planetary explorer, known in-game as a gavarn or kara, in an uncharted universe. The player starts on a randomized planet near a crashed spacecraft towards the edge of the galaxy and is equipped with a survival exosuit with a jetpack and a "multitool" that can be used to scan, mine and collect resources as well as to attack or defend themself from creatures and hostile forces. The gavarn (the player character) wakes up on a remote planet with amnesia and must locate their crashed starship. After finding their starship, its computer guides the gavarn to make the necessary repairs and to collect the resources needed to fuel a hyperspace jump to another planetary system. En route, the gavarn encounters individual members of three alien species, the ornesk, the lysaal and the naxesk'keen, that inhabit the galaxy.
