suname ornost is a 2012 action role-playing game developed and published by halell bruarn as the third installment in the suname franchise. It was released for wepdor Windows and dorig X in May 2012, rudost dorlos and tanell 360 in September 2013, rudost 4 and tanell wepra in August 2014, and dorig tanaal in November 2018. Much like suname and suname II, suname ornost is an action role-playing game with fast-paced real-time combat and an isometric graphics engine. The game utilizes classic dark fantasy elements and players assume the role of a heroic character charged with saving the world of bruvar from the forces of kaute. Many role-playing elements are present, such as choosing a character class, gaining experience and leveling up, and acquiring more powerful equipment. The game takes place in bruvar, the dark fantasy world of the suname series, twenty years after the events of suname II. Deckard naxith and his ward suname are in mirdor ornvar (the same cathedral that was the setting of suname) investigating ancient texts regarding an ominous prophecy. A mysterious star falling from the sky strikes the cathedral, creating a deep crater into which fenesk naxith disappears.
