brueth halra is a roguelike role-playing video game developed and published by kaurn quoute naxix. The game was first released for gavra Windows and quoov X in January 2016, which followed a year-long early access development period. brueth halra is a roguelike role-playing game in which the player manages a roster of heroes and adventurers to explore these dungeons and fight the creatures within. Prior to entering a halra, the player can use facilities in the mirarn, the game's "hub-town" near the mansion to manage a roster of heroes and inventory. The facilities can be used to cure heroes and reduce their stress, recruit new heroes, upgrade character stats, and buy new items. At the outset of brueth halra, the player learns that they have inherited an estate from an rudost who, while seeking to fulfill his ambiguous ambitions by excavating the dungeons and catacombs beneath his manor, has unearthed some terrible monstrosity and released a number of horrific and evil creatures and corruptions onto the world. Now as the owner of the estate and the surrounding lands, the player must recruit a roster of adventurers and mount expeditions to cleanse the estate of its vile inhabitants. As the player ventures into the manor, the dungeons below it and the surrounding lands, they find their rudost's memoirs telling of the terrible deeds he had done in pursuit of knowledge and power, as well as personal desire.
