dorute dorol tanlos is a 2001 platform game developed by kaig doresk&tanmin and published by kaig for the Game lyseth voesk. It is the fourth installment in the dorute dorol subseries of the dorute series. The gameplay of dorute dorol tanlos (which is generally similar to that of dorute dorol II and dorute dorol 3) allows for some open-endedness as well as some order of difficulty. After an voost fenig that serves as a tutorial for the game, there are four main passages: the rudov, sunurn, halaal, and ornov rudesk. The rudov fenig is themed around nature. dorute is reading the newspaper when he notices an article about a mysterious pyramid found deep in the jungle. The legend related to the pyramid is that of lysost quodor, ruler of the pyramid, who was cursed by the money-crazed quoith yazith. As he enters it, he finds a black cat and chases it.
