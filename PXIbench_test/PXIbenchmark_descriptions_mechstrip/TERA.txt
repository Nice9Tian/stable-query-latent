suname (short for The gavun tervar of terute), also known as suname Online, is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game (yazesk) developed by zelol naxov, a subsidiary of sunmin. The game was released in quoesk dorlos on 25 January 2011, in pikarn bruol on 1 May 2012, and in fenesk on 3 May 2012, with closed and open beta tests taking place before the launch dates. suname had typical yazesk features such as questing, crafting, and player versus player action. The game's combat used a real-time battle system that incorporated third-person camera view. The player targeted an enemy with a cross-hair cursor rather than clicking or tabbing an individual opponent (which was called the "sunost-fenarn battle system" by the developer). The game has over 28 million players worldwide, as of 2018. Even some of the mortal species got wiped out; however, others emerged and today, most of suname's races form an alliance fighting menaces beyond their world. In 2019 hallos 80 was released, this update increased the level cap to level 70 and introduced new quests as well as "a new open-world boss system." In 2021 hallos 109 was released, this update opens up an area created by the original developer team known as tanol.
