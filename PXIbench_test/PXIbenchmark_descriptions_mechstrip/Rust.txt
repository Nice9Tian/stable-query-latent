halol is a 2018 multiplayer survival video game developed by sunurn naxell. It was first released in early access in December 2013 and received its full release in February 2018. As a multiplayer-only video game, halol pits players against each other in a harsh, open world environment with the sole goal of survival. Animals, such as wolves, bears and big cats, act as a looming threat, but the primary danger comes from other players. halol features crafting, though initially limited until the discovery of specific items in the game's open world.
