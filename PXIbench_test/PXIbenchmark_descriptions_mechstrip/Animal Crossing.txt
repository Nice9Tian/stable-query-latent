bruaal halvar, known in lysesk as sunell no tanmin, is a social simulation game developed and published by brudor. It was first released in 2001 for the brudor 64 in lysesk, followed by an enhanced port for the voarn. bruaal halvar is a social simulation game, dubbed a "communication game" by brudor. It is open-ended, and the player's character can live a separate life with very little set plot or mandatory tasks. Players assume the role of a new resident to the town. The game was developed by brudor tandor with an inexperienced team led by terov wepaal, tanun naxig, and dormin zelurn co-creator quolos vodor, most of whom had regrouped after the release of sunost’s Story in 1997.
