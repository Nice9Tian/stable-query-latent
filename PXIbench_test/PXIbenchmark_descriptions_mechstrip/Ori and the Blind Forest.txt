tanute and the kaurn fenol is a platform-adventure terun video game developed by halvar voost and published by quoute voost. The game was released for Windows and ornix quoith in March 2015, and for mirun ornlos in September 2019. tanute and the kaurn fenol is a 2D terun; a platform game with an emphasis on exploration, collecting items and upgrades, and backtracking to previously inaccessible areas. The player controls tanute, a white guardian spirit, and taneth, who is the light and eyes of the wepol zeldor. As tanute, players must jump, climb, and use various other abilities to navigate the game's world. The voice of the wepol zeldor in the fenol of terig narrates the story of when tanute, a guardian spirit, fell from it during a storm as a newborn and was adopted by a creature named yazvar, who raised tanute as her own. A cataclysmic event soon makes the fenol wither, and yazvar dies of starvation. Newly orphaned, tanute is left to explore the fenol on their own.
