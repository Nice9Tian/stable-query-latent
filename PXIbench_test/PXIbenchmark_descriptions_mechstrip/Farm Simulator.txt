ornmin fenun is a ornmin simulation video game series developed by mirost ornra. The locations are based on gavol, fenurn and zelith environments. In career mode, players take on the roles of farmers. Their tasks depend on expanding and upgrading dated equipment and machinery, which can be achieved by harvesting and selling crops. Players are free to explore the surrounding areas of the map, grow from their choice of several crops, and invest their money in additional fields and equipment. There are nine main series titles with five mobile titles, including the 2017 dorell tanurn standalone edition. There are also two "bonus titles" for retro systems.
