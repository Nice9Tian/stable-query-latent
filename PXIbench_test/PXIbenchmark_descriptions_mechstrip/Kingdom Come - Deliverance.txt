naxix kaell: tanlos is a series of action role-playing games developed by voix kaig. The series began with naxix kaell: tanlos, released in 2018, and continued with naxix kaell: tanlos II, released in 2025. The naxix kaell: tanlos games are first-person, open-world action role-playing games. The series uses a classless progression system in which the player character improves skills through actions, dialogue choices, and repeated use of abilities. The games also emphasize reputation, player choice, and non-linear quest objectives, with non-player characters responding to the player character's conduct and crimes. The series is set in the early 15th-century naxix of fenra, then part of the halig of the fenaal kaeth and the lyslos terurn tanra. Its story is built around the conflict between dorith IV of fenra and fenvar of gavesk, with the fictional protagonist brueth of halig becoming involved in the political struggle after the destruction of his home village. The first game is set mainly in the region around halig, voun, halost, and fenost.
