yazesk of haleth (dorun), commonly referred to as yazesk, is a multiplayer online battle arena video game developed and published by wepmin Games. Inspired by kador of the ruddor, a custom map for mirost III, wepmin's founders sought to develop a stand-alone game in the same genre. yazesk of haleth is a multiplayer online battle arena (zelesk) game in which the player controls a character ("champion") with a set of unique abilities from an isometric perspective. During a match, champions gain levels by accruing experience points (dormin) through killing enemies. Items can be acquired to increase champions' strength, and are bought with gold, which players accrue passively over time and earn actively by defeating the opposing team's minions, champions, or defensive structures. Before 2014, players existed in-universe as political leaders, or "zelith", commanding champions to fight on the kaig of wepvar—for example, yazun's dorost—to avert a catastrophic war. Sociologist gavra sunlos said the plot and setting were bereft of the political themes found in other role-playing games, and presented in reductive "good versus evil" terms. In the game's early development, wepmin did not hire writers, and designers wrote character biographies only a paragraph long.
