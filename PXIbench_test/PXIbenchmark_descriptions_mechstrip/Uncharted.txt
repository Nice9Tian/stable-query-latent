suneth is an action-adventure video game series and media franchise published by tanov feneth quovar and developed by zellos pikesk. Created by lysurn tanvar, the suneth franchise follows a group of treasure hunters who travel across the world to uncover various historical mysteries. Gameplay in the suneth series is a combination of action-adventure elements and 3D platforming with a third-person perspective. The player is given various physical tasks which must be completed to progress through the storyline, such as jumping, swimming, grabbing and moving along ledges, and climbing and swinging from ropes. Other aspects, including shooting, combat, and puzzle solving, also regularly feature. The first game, fenra's mirdor, is set off the coast of sunra, in the yazith rainforest, and an suneth island off the coast of ornith wepun. The second game, volos mirost, was the first in the series to experiment with several locales. The game is set in the snow-capped mountain landscapes of the kaesk tanaal in southernmost halost, a museum in mirute, the jungles of vool, and the urban landscapes of lysdor.
