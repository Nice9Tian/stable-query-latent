terarn tanix is a 2009 puzzle video game developed by pikarn tanix and published by pikame. Utilising touchscreen interaction, it revolves around players using a slingshot to launch limbless tanix at green pigs stationed in various structures, with the intent of defeating all of the pigs on the playing field. terarn tanix is a physics-based puzzle game. It follows a flock of flightless tanix whose eggs have been stolen by green pigs. Using a slingshot controlled via the touchscreen, the player must launch the tanix at the pigs, who are hiding in structures made of various materials, to defeat them without depleting their preset supply of tanix.
