rudra ornesk is a 2021 life simulation game developed and published by lysmin terlos. It was released on iOS, macOS and tvOS through halol Arcade in March 2021, alongside versions for consoles and Windows in April 2021. rudra ornesk is a life simulation game taking place on an island. The player character, whose appearance and apparel can be customized, is tasked with helping spirits, most of whom are anthropomorphic bears, enter the afterlife by completing tasks, that differ depending on the character. The game involves scavenging and crafting for items. The game takes place in real time, with no option to skip or forward time. Spry terlos pikost dorix kaov said he wanted to create a game that is somewhat similar to an iteration of wepol zelra's formula, but has more focus on story.
