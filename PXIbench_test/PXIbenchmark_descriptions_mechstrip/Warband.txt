naxurn & ornmin: kaov is the standalone expansion pack to the strategy action role-playing video game naxurn & ornmin. Announced in January 2009, the game was developed by the pikun company quovar quoith and was published by mireth fename on March 30, 2010. As with the first game, naxurn & ornmin: kaov is a sandbox role-playing game that takes place in the medieval land of vomin. At the start of the game, the player customizes the character and is spawned into the world. Traveling around the overworld map is done by pointing and clicking on the desired location.
