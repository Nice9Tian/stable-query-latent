suname, previously marketed as dorost wepesk mirost: suname outside of voesk, is a video game franchise primarily developed by brudor and owned by dorov. Centered around a series of wepaal role-playing video games, suname is a spin-off from brudor' wepesk mirost franchise. The gameplay of the suname series revolves around combat against various enemy types: fendor, sunute and tanun. Main combat takes place during dungeon crawling segments within various locations. The way battles initiate varies between random encounters (suname, suname 2) or running into models representing enemy groups (suname 3 onwards). The suname series takes place in modern-day voesk and focuses on a group of high school students, with the exception to this being pikvar pikute, which focused on a group of adults. The setting has been described as urban fantasy, with extraordinary events happening in otherwise normal locations. The typical setting used is a city, with a noted exception being the rural town setting of suname 4.
