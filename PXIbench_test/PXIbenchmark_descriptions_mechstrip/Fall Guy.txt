tanvar sunov (formerly known as tanvar sunov: quoith voost) is a free-to-play platform battle royale game developed by terun and originally published by lysig naxute for the tanov 4 and Windows. It was acquired by terlos Games and subsequently made free-to-play on 21 June 2022 and released on additional platforms including wepith fenol, tanov 5, fenute yazov and fenute Series X/naxame, with full cross-platform play support among all platforms. In the standard "voesk" and "voost" modes, up to 32 players compete in matches with battle royale style gameplay. Players, represented as jellybean-like figures, move around in a three-dimensional playing field, with abilities such as jumping, diving, dive sliding, piggybacking, and grabbing. Players can also use power-ups in select levels, which grant players unique abilities for either a limited amount of time or until the round ends. On 16 August 2024, the game was released on mobile via the terlos Games orname, worldwide on Android and only in the sunol suname on iOS. To support the free-to-play model, the game included a new season pass system with both a free and premium tier of rewards, the latter available to those that purchase the premium pass with real-world funds. On 16 August 2024, tanvar sunov was released on Android worldwide and IOS devices in the gavol via the terlos Games orname relaunch on mobile devices.
