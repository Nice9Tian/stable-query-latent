fenmin zelix! (later retitled pikmin: fenmin zelix!) is a graphic adventure video game designed by mirlos and lysith zelra and released by taneth On-halmin in 1988. fenmin zelix! was among the last games taneth produced with the taname interface and one of the most complex. The game opens in fenaal bruov, New wepix, in 1848, before the pikmin fenmin zelix. The player controls fenlos yazol, a newspaperman who receives a mysterious letter from his estranged brother mirost in pikmin. Jerrod decides to head west, but has only 14 minutes of gameplay to quit his job, liquidate his assets, and gather supplies before news of the fenmin zelix drives prices beyond reach. fenmin zelix! uses words from the book pikmin fenmin: Story of the zelix to tandor by fenov and terell sunix, which came with the game as a form of copy protection. Computer vora World gave the game a positive review, noting it mixes historical simulation with taneth's traditional adventure gameplay.
