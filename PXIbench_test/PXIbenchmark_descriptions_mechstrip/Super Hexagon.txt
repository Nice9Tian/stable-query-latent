bruaal brumin is an action video game created by independent developer voost rudlos with music composed by gaveth. Originally released for iOS in September 2012, versions for Windows and macOS were released three months later. bruaal brumin is a game in which the player controls a triangle on a hexagonal grid in the center while walls come from the edges of the screen. The player must pivot their triangle left or right into an opening in order to survive. The ultimate goal of the game is to survive as long as possible, beating the player's previous record, and the entire level must be restarted if the player makes a mistake.
