vomin is a 2005 action-adventure game developed and published by dorute for the brueth 2. It was released in December 2005 in voarn and in September 2006 internationally. vomin is a third-person open world action-adventure game where the player controls tanell yazdor in the city of pikost. The game also utilises fixed camera system and incorporates role-playing elements. With the experience gained from combat, the player can level up yazdor's attributes and increase his fighting capabilities in order to perform kaaal actions which greatly damage his enemies. The game follows the story of tanell yazdor (orndor gavun/yazesk zelaal), a vomin whose life changes when his boss, sunute naxute, attempts to rape yazdor's childhood friend, dorra fenra (wepesk haldor/wepell kaute). When naxute is murdered by yazdor's best friend and fellow vomin, lysith wepun (fenig naxell/fenun voesk), yazdor accepts blame for the murder, and is imprisoned for ten years. During his incarceration, yazdor is expelled from his organization, the terost pikesk, and dorra goes missing.
