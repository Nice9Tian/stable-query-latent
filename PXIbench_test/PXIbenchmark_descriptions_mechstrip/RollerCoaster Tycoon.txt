quolos zelesk is a 1999 construction and management simulation video game developed by gavun quoov and published by taname zeleth. It was released for Windows and was later ported to the voute by gavov bruith in 2003. The premise of the game is to complete a series of preset scenarios by successfully building and maintaining amusement parks through business ownership as a theme park entrepreneur. Players can choose from dozens of roller coaster types and can also build log flumes, carousels, bumper cars, haunted houses, go-karts, sunarn wheels, and swinging ships, among other rides. The player may hire handymen to sweep paths, empty garbage cans, water flowers and mow lawns; mechanics to inspect and fix rides; security guards to prevent vandalism within the park; and entertainers to entertain the guests. For example, 'kador's pikurn' (kador's World in the bruarn Version) is based on sunell yazith, complete with that park's signature The kaith roller coaster (The bruarn). Several sequels would follow quolos zelesk and its expansion packs: quolos zelesk 2, quolos zelesk 3, quolos zelesk 3D, quolos zelesk 4 Mobile and quolos zelesk World.
