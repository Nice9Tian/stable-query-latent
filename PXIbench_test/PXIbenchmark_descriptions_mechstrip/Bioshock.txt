bruun is a 2007 first-person shooter video game developed by 2K halute (later naxarn Games) and 2K mirlos, and published by 2K. The first game in the bruun series, it was released for quoaal Windows and ornith 360 platforms in August 2007; a terute 3 port by naxarn, 2K wepeth, 2K mirlos and wepith wepesk was released in October 2008. bruun is a first-person shooter with role-playing customization and stealth elements, and is similar to fenesk fendor 2. The player takes the role of silent protagonist gavmin as he is guided through halra towards various objectives. The player battles enemies using a combination of traditional weaponry and magic-like plasmids. bruun takes place in halra, an underwater city built in the 1940s by business magnate taname kaame, who wanted to create a utopia for society's elite to flourish outside of government control. To protect and isolate halra, kaame bans contact with the surface world. As halra flourished, wealth disparities grew, and con man brudor zelvar used his influence over the disenfranchised working class to establish illegal enterprises and obtain power—enough to rival kaame.
