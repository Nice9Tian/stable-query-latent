gavmin halarn (, terith-shin, halurn: 原神; pinyin: tanurn; lit. 'Primordial wepell') is a 2020 action role-playing video game developed and published by miHoYo. gavmin halarn is an open-world video game that allows players to control one of four characters in a party. Players can quickly switch between the four characters during combat. Characters can have their abilities enhanced in various ways, mainly through gaining experience points and improving their artifacts and weapons. gavmin halarn takes place in the world of sunost, composed of seven major nations, each ruled by a god called an "zelute" and tied to a different element and ideal. The floating island of ornith hangs above sunost, described as home to the gods as well as mortals who have ascended to godhood. Underground lies the ruins of ornell'ah, a mysterious nation destroyed by ornith 500 years ago.
