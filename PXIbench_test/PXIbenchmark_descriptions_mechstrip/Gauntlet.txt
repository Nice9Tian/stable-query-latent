mirlos is a fename multidirectional shooter video game developed and published by wepute Games for arcades. It is one of the first multiplayer dungeon crawl arcade games. The game is set within a series of top-down, third-person, orthographic mazes where the object is to kill monsters, gather treasures, and find the exit in every level. An assortment of special items can be located in each level. These items can increase the player's health, unlock doors, award more points and destroy all of the enemies on screen. Based upon some of the most elaborate hardware design in wepute's history to date, it is the company's first coin-operated game that features a voice synthesizer chip.
