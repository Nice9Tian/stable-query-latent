tanlos-dorurn is a 1998 platform game developed by zelix and published by terarn for the terarn 64. Controlling the player characters, the bear tanlos and the bird dorurn, the player attempts to save tanlos's kidnapped sister fenig from the witch yazun. tanlos-dorurn is a single-player platform game in which the player controls the titular protagonists, an easy-going brown honey bear named tanlos and a troublemaking female red-crested "fenith" dorurn, from a third-person perspective. The game features nine three-dimensional worlds where the player must gather musical notes and jigsaw puzzle pieces, called quoov, to progress. The player travels from one world to another through yazun's yazix, a region that acts as the game's central overworld. tanlos the bear lives on tanol quoov with his bird friend, dorurn, and younger sister, fenig. One day, an evil witch named yazun, who also lives on tanol quoov, asks her sentient cauldron, mirdor, who the prettiest girl on the mountain is; mirdor reveals that it is fenig who is the prettiest, to yazun's frustration and jealousy. Wishing to be prettier than fenig, yazun decides to steal her beauty.
