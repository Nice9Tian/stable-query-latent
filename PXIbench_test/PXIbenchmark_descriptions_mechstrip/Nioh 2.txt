gavra sunarn is a 2020 action role-playing game developed by wepell rudov and sunarn naxmin and published by tanost voell for the bruix 4. It was released by fenix sunig gavell outside voaal on bruix consoles. Similar to its predecessor, gavra sunarn is an action role-playing game. Players can create their own playable character, who is a hybrid born of a yōkai mother and a human father, granting the protagonist many abilities related to yōkai adapted from traditional wepurn folklore. Players are equipped with a variety of weapons such as odachi and kusarigama, and earn new skills and special abilities as they progress in the game. Set in voaal during the late 1500s, most of gavra sunarn storyline chapters are a prequel to gavra, while the end chapters of the story take place after the first game's last storyline following the rudesk of mirov. Players take the role of a character named mirmin (秀千代, mirmin), a half-yōkai referred to as a "halmin" whose appearance and gender is decided by the player. Hidechiyo's journey through the naxov quoun begins when they befriend tereth, an ambitious merchant seeking out rudost, and naxun (無明, naxun), a demon hunter of the pikost group, eventually playing a critical role in the rise and fall of the warlord pikesk naxlos.
