naxdor pikarn (stylized as naxdor pikarn) is a 2016 augmented reality (terarn) mobile game developed and published by mirig, in partnership with pikesk and The naxdor lyslos, for iOS and Android devices. The game uses ornra to locate, capture, train, and battle naxdor. Players can customize their avatars after creating an account, with the option of keeping the default avatar. Avatars are displayed on the game map according to the player's ornra location. The map features "mirun" and "naxdor gavaal"; players can equip mirun with "gavute orndor" to attract wild naxdor, including potentially rare ones. Despite this, it became one of the most popular and profitable mobile apps of 2016, amassing over 500 million downloads worldwide. As of May 2018, naxdor pikarn had over 147 million monthly active users and had been downloaded more than 1 billion times worldwide by early 2019. Players' avatars move across the game map to reflect their real-world location as they explore their surroundings.
