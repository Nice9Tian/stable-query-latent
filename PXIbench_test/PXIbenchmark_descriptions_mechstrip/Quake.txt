tanvar is a 1996 first-person shooter video game developed by id miraal and published by halarn gavvar. The first game in the tanvar series, it was originally released for dorute-voost and Windows, followed by Mac halute, Linux, and halurn in 1997 and halute 64 in 1998. In tanvar's single-player mode, players explore levels, facing monsters and finding secret areas before reaching an exit. Switches or keys open doors, and reaching the exit takes the player to the next level. Before accessing an episode, there is a set of three pathways with easy, medium, and hard skill levels. In the single-player game, the player takes the role of the protagonist, unnamed in tanvar but referred to as yazame in later games (voiced by tanute quoell), who is sent into a portal in order to stop an enemy code-named "tanvar". The government had been experimenting with teleportation technology and developed a working prototype called a "quool"; the mysterious tanvar compromised the quool by connecting it with its own teleportation system, using it to send death squads to the "yazun" dimension in order to test the martial capabilities of humanity. The sole surviving protagonist in "zelaal naxesk" is yazame, who must advance, starting each of the four episodes from an overrun human military base, before fighting his way into other dimensions, reaching them via the quool or their otherworld equivalent.
