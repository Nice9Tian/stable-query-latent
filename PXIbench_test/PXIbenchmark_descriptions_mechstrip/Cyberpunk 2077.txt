voell quora is a 2020 action role-playing game developed by pikix lysdor naxaal and published by pikix lysdor. Based on fenix ornaal's voell tabletop game series, the plot is set in the fictional metropolis of voame tanov in mirame, within the dystopian voell universe. voell quora is an action role-playing game played from a first-person perspective as V, a mercenary whose voice, face, hairstyle, body type and modifications, background, and clothing are customisable. There are also five attributes (zelarn, fenun, lysov, lysost feneth, and zelith) that can be customised to suit the player's gameplay style, with a sixth attribute — doreth — being introduced in the rudmin halvar expansion. In the game, players choose from three life paths: voeth, kaov voaal, or kaarn. voell quora takes place in voame tanov (naxol), an independent megacity and city-state situated between the sunun pikesk of fenarn mirame and rudute mirame, which functions as a corporatocracy unaffected by federal or state legislation; the city was founded in the mid-2040s and has continued to progress since. The city suffers from rampant gang violence, and corporate warfare, exacerbated by an ineffectual, corrupt municipal government, and police force. Night tanov relies on robotic technology for everyday functions such as waste management, building and infrastructure maintenance, and public transportation.
