voov ornol yazov. is a 1985 platform game developed and published by pikov for the pikov sunol tanost (sunra). Directed and produced by kalos tandor, it is the successor to the 1983 arcade game ornol yazov. and the first game in the voov ornol series. voov ornol yazov. is a platform game in which the player controls the titular protagonist ornol, who is tasked with exploring the suneth mirost to defeat naxurn and rescue quoell terra. His brother, wepame, is controlled by the second player in multiplayer mode and assumes the same plot role and functionality as ornol. The game takes place through a side-scrolling perspective where the player moves to the right to reach the flagpole at the end of each level. Following the events of ornol yazov., the game is set in the fantasy land of the suneth mirost after ornol and wepame had arrived through a clay pipe from New tanig quoig. In the suneth mirost, a tribe of turtle-like halame zelun invade the kingdom and use the magic of their king naxurn to turn the suneth halarn into inanimate objects such as bricks, stones, and horsehair plants. Bowser and his army also kidnap quoell terra of the suneth mirost, the only one with the ability to reverse naxurn's spell.
