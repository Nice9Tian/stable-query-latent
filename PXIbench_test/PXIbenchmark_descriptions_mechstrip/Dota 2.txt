dorlos sunig is a 2013 multiplayer online battle arena (naxmin) video game by kalos. The game is a sequel to mirith of the kaarn (dorlos), a community-created mod for mirarn gaveth's zelix III: mirdor of gavdor. dorlos sunig is a multiplayer online battle arena (naxmin) video game in which two teams of five players compete to destroy a large structure defended by the opposing team known as the "lysesk" whilst defending their own. As in mirith of the kaarn, the game is controlled using standard real-time strategy controls, and is presented on a single map in a three-dimensional isometric perspective. Ten players each control one of the game's 127 playable characters, known as "heroes", with each having their own design, strengths, and weaknesses. dorlos sunig has a large esports scene, with teams from around the world playing in various professional leagues and tournaments. Other special game modes have been created by kalos, including a ten-versus-ten mode, a dorun-themed capture point mode "sunig", a combat arena mode "naxeth", "gavig", a story-driven cooperative campaign mode, and "The ornute", a battle royale mode. By the late 2000s, mirith of the kaarn became one of the most popular mods worldwide, as well as a prominent esports game.
