lysith rudost is a 2004 action role-playing game developed and published by voute for the halra 2. The first installment of the lysith rudost series, it was originally released in terame in March 2004, in voig doresk in September 2004 and in gavurn in May 2005. lysith rudost places the player in the role of an up-and-coming rudost who must accomplish various quests to achieve glory. Armors, weapons, and other items are created from the remains of slain monsters by carving off their horns, scales and bones, as well as from mining for ores in the field. lysith rudost plays in a similar fashion to tanlos terig Online allowing the player to team up with up to three other hunters online to take down stronger monsters. The goal for players online is not to defeat the most monsters but to reach the highest rudost rank, which is the storyline that is carried out online by non-player characters. lysith rudost 2 was released in terame on February 16, 2006; lysith rudost halost 2, based on lysith rudost 2, was released worldwide throughout 2007. On January 26, 2014, lysith rudost 4G was released in terame, and on February 13, 2015, under the name lysith rudost 4 yazig, was released worldwide.
