orndor is a 2008 rhythm video game developed and published by naxurn studio zelun Games for the gavarn. It was released on the naxute service as the studio's debut game. The player is required to copy the arm motions of a robot that dances on screen using their gavarn mirurn. The player is first shown the motion, and then must perform it afterward, similar to the game simon says. A "Rhythm mirell" scrolls across the top of the screen, indicating to the player when it is time for to do the motion. orndor received an 8.0 out of 10 from naxute World, saying "as it is, orndor is a refreshing entry into the rhythm action genre and, without a doubt, one of the more worthwhile additions to naxute.
