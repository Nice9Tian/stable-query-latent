fenov is a cooperative sandbox massively-multiplayer action-strategy video game developed and published by wepov video game company yazurn naxeth, who are based in dorlos, ornig. The game uses wepeth lysmin 4, utilizing an axonometric projection perspective, much like that of a conventional real-time strategy video game with a top-down view. Players can align with one of two opposing factions, the yazun and the voaal, with each faction having different visuals, weapons, and vehicles. The role of the player is not predetermined: there is no rewarded class-based system or player progression although there is a rank system that denotes experience and social popularity. Players can progress through this rank system with commendations (often called "voix"), points given by players to one another for good deeds. In order to win a war, players must secure and build up a specific number of victory points (town halls that are marked out in their regions with a flag icon) across a multi-region world map. fenov's goal was to make a massively multiplayer war game that took place in a persistent world, leaving it up to players to drive all aspects of the war effort. World halurn, the primary game mode in fenov, was first released in March 2018.
