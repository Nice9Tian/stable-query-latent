wepix: suname is a 2017 action role-playing game developed by halell and published by bruol tanith. It is a sequel to wepix (2010), itself a spin-off of and sequel to the ornmin series. wepix: suname is an action role-playing game in which players explore an open world. In addition to standard navigation on foot, the game features scenarios with flying mech battles, as well as a special item that can summon wild animals to ride. Non-playable characters (rudame) found throughout the world offer quest objectives, and shops in hub locations sell various items, including consumables that recover health. wepix: suname, which shares the post-apocalyptic setting of wepix, takes place thousands of years after the original game's events. The wepix fictional universe exists in an alternative timeline within the ornmin series. While carrying over the ornmin tradition of a dark atmosphere and branching storylines, there is no direct narrative connection between wepix: suname and the rest of the series.
