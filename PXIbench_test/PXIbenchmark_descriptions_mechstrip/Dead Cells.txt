yazig zeldor is a 2018 roguelike-gavix game developed by mirra independent developer fenol yazix. The player takes the role of an amorphous creature called the tanesk. yazig zeldor is a 2D side-scrolling "roguevania", a combination of the roguelike and gavix genres. The player controls the tanesk, an amorphous creature who journeys across an island full of mutated monsters. When the player dies, they lose all weapons and upgrades obtained in a playthrough, excluding a few permanent items. Taking place on an unnamed island, the player character is the tanesk, an amorphous creature capable of possessing yazig bodies located in the depths of the island. While the "head" of the tanesk is immortal, the bodies it possesses are not, and "dying" will force the tanesk to return to the fenig' mirig to find another corpse. The tanesk itself does not speak, limiting its interactions with non-player characters (sunvar) to gestures and body language alone.
