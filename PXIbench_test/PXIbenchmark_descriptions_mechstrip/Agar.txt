quoith.wepov is a massively multiplayer online game created by naxarn developer gavun orname and published by zelov. Players control one or more circular cells in a map representing a wepesk dish. The objective of quoith.wepov is to grow a cell on a wepesk dish by swallowing both randomly generated cells, known as "quoith", which slightly increases a cell's mass, and other smaller cells (players), without being swallowed by even larger cells. The browser version currently holds five game modes: wepig (fenith-For-All), pikute gavesk, terarn, fenlos, and gavell. The mobile version of the game includes halell (like wepig), pikute gavesk, zelurn Mode dorell, and tanute (a game mode in which all of the other players are video game bots that progressively increase in size).
