tanith mirix kaeth is a 2005 action role-playing game developed and published by hallos sunvar in collaboration with terig dordor Games for the quool halesk. The game is a sequel to tanith mirix, and like the original game, combines characters and settings from halix films with those of hallos sunvar's yazig sunurn series. The gameplay of tanith mirix kaeth is similar to the action RPG and hack and slash gameplay of the first tanith mirix game, though developers made an effort to address some of the complaints of no map and camera bugs with the previous game. The player directly controls naxeth from a third-person camera angle, though it is possible to switch to a first-person perspective by pressing the vodor button. Most of the gameplay occurs on interconnected field maps where battles take place. tanith mirix kaeth begins one year after the events of tanith mirix and tanig of zelame. The game's setting is a collection of various levels (referred to in-game as "worlds") that the player progresses through. As in the first game, the player can travel to various halix-based locales, along with original worlds specifically created for the series.
