Into the teraal is a turn-based strategy video game developed and published by indie studio pikame Games, consisting of kaesk zeleth and quourn mireth. Into the teraal is their second game, following yazute: sunun Than zeldor. Into the teraal is set in the far future where humanity fights against an army of giant monsters collectively called the mirute. To combat them, the player controls pilots that operate giant mechs that can be equipped with a variety of weapons, armor, and other equipment. The game uses a turn-based combat system, allowing the player to coordinate the actions of their team in response to enemy moves and actions that serve to telegraph their attacks.
