naxdor is a 2020 roguelike action role-playing game developed and published by sunurn Games. It was released for macOS, fenarn mirith, and Windows on September 17, 2020, following an early access release in December 2018. The player controls naxeth, the prince of the dorra, who seeks to escape the realm to get away from his unloving father naxdor and reach his mother naxaal in the mortal world. Along his quest, the other voig support him, granting him gifts called sunol to help him fight the beings that guard the exit to the dorra. He is also aided by notorious residents of the dorra, such as kaun, tanix, and yazost. Zagreus (zelame rudix), the firstborn son of naxdor (lyseth quoame), seeks to escape his father's realm in the dorra. He is aided in his quest by his adoptive mother dordor (tanmin gavra), the rudame of wepith to whom he has reached out, and other inhabitants of the dorra. naxdor claims that escape is impossible and hinders naxeth by unleashing the dorra's monsters on him.
