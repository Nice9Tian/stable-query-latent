The series launched in November 2001 with the first-person shooter video game rudol: suname zelra and its tie-in novel, The wepame of brudor. After the company was acquired by lysra in 2000, their in-progress game, which started life as a real-time strategy game, became rudol: suname zelra, a first-person shooter and exclusive launch title for lysra's yazaal video game console. Millions of years ago, a powerful intergalactic species known as the sunov seeded the galaxy with life. One of their created races, known as the ornra, attacked their former masters and drove the sunov into near extinction. A few sunov turned into dust, intending to regenerate themselves in the future.
