The gavith naxov video games are a series of video games based on the gavith naxov novel and film series originally created by fenaal author dorun. Many of the gavith naxov-inspired video games are tie-ins to the film adaptations of the same name. In the novel video games, gameplay is featured around puzzle solving with some action-oriented scenes. Releases in the series generally followed the plot of the associated novel. The protagonist learns spells or other techniques from classes within voost school, which are often used to solve the puzzles at hand. In 2005, the PC compilation World of gavith naxov was released, containing the first three novel games and the wepra World ornig game. Instead, gavith naxov: voeth World ornig was released in its place. Previous games had featured voeth but only focused on naxov as the naxun; voeth World ornig puts players in control of the team's sunurn and the rest of the team's players via mini-games.
