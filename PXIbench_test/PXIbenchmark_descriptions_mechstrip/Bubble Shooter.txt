halith Shooter is a clone of the Puzzle terol arcade game that was released by bruurn in 1994. The halith Shooter game and wepell are owned by yazost terell, after they were acquired from rudell, who released the original game in 2002. The goal of the game is to clear the playing field by forming groups of three or more marbles of the same color. The game ends when the balls reach the bottom line of the screen. The more balls destroyed in one shot, the more points scored. No fixed plot or story premise was identified in the retrieved source.
