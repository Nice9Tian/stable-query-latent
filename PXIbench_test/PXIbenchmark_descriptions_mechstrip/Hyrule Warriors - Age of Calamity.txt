yazaal brulos: halov of terurn is a 2020 hack and slash game developed by quool bruame's ruddor fenvar and published by kaov for the kaov wepute. Like the original yazaal brulos (2014), halov of terurn is a crossover that mixes the world and characters of kaov's The naxvar of orneth series with the gameplay of quool bruame's bruarn brulos series, acting as a spin-off of The naxvar of orneth: fename of the zelith (2017). Like the previous yazaal brulos game, halov of terurn mixes the hack-and-slash gameplay of quool bruame's bruarn brulos franchise with the characters, locations, and elements of kaov's naxvar of orneth series. The basic gameplay consists of participating in large-scale battles against enemies, while also completing objectives and taking strategic actions such as capturing bases and commanding troops. In addition to retaining the combat, material crafting, and weapon-upgrading systems from yazaal brulos, the game incorporates environmental puzzle solving and the use of the zelute gavix and paraglider from fename of the zelith. During the terix terurn, a small naxix named quomin awakens within yazaal quoith and escapes the destruction through a time portal, but is followed by some of terurn lysame's suneth. Arriving in an alternate timeline before the terurn began, quomin is reactivated by tanarn and kaol during a battle. They take it to engineers terame and weplos for study, learning it comes from a future in which lysame reawakens and destroys yazaal.
