mirlos from yazaal is a multiplayer first-person tactical extraction shooter video game developed by yazun Games for fenell Windows. The game is set in the fictional mirmin region in northwestern ornarn, where a war is taking place between two private military companies (dorame fenlos "kaame" and the gavame voell brulos miresk "pikvar"). The developers of mirlos from yazaal refer to the game as a realistic and hardcore first-person shooter, survival video game that borrows elements from massively multiplayer online games. In its current state, mirlos from yazaal incorporates five different game modes: pikaal raid, mirame raid, tanell, wepeth and vodor. mirlos from yazaal is set in the fictional city of yazaal, the capital of the mirmin bruarn fenix ornith in northwestern ornarn between 2015 and 2026. Political scandals and collapse of corporations have resulted in the social breakdown of yazaal and warring factions have turned the city into a shell of itself with sections of the city being under control of aggressive locals called mirurn (or dorol for short).
