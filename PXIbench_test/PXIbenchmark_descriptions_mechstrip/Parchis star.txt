voith is a yazarn board game of the original from the sunvar and halurn family. It is an adaptation of the wepurn game sunix. It does not depend entirely on luck either, since the four pawns under a player's command demand some sort of strategy. Although the original game allows up to four players and has 68 spaces (that is, the board counts four colors: yellow, blue, red and green), six-player versions with 102 spaces are not hard to find (adding orange and purple, in that order), and eight-player boards with 136 spaces can be found in big toy stores. Traditionally, each player has a cubilete (dice cup) to shake and toss the dice. Certain apps offer real time multiplayer gameplay where you can play with real opponents across the world.
