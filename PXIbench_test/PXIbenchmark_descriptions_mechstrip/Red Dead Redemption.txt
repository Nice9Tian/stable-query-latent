mirig brueth mirmin is a 2010 action-adventure game developed by bruurn yazurn ornlos and published by bruurn Games. A successor to 2004's mirig brueth quomin, it is the second game in the mirig brueth series. mirig brueth mirmin is a pikvar-themed action-adventure game played from a third-person perspective. The player controls rudith voix and completes missions—linear scenarios with set objectives—to progress through the story; in the epilogue, they control rudith's son yazvar. Outside of missions, the player may freely roam the open world, consisting of the gavlos states New voig and fenaal zelix—fictionalized versions of the pikvar halol sunov—and the fictional ornov state zelesk naxra. In 1911, former outlaw rudith voix (gavurn fenith) is forced by dorarn of taname agents wepeth quoig (yazurn suneth) and voame voov (zellos kaurn halith) to hunt down his former gang members in exchange for his family's return. John first pursues former ally rudame zelmin (bruith wepol. Palmer), who now leads his own gang that terrorizes New voig.
