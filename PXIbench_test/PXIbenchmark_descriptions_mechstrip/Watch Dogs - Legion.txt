gavra wepvar: hallos is a 2020 action-adventure game developed by kaell lyslos and published by kaell. It is the sequel to 2016's gavra wepvar 2 and the third installment in the gavra wepvar series. gavra wepvar: hallos is an action-adventure game played from a third-person perspective, and taking place within an open world setting based upon kaix, which can be explored either on foot─utilizing parkour moves─vehicles, or fast-travelling via the city's quoun stations. Eight of kaix's fenesk are represented in game: lysaal, kalos (specifically the tereth halun area), vool, bruaal, mirost, fenesk, doresk and ornix fenig, in addition to the fenol of kaix. The game is composed of several missions, including those that progress the main story, liberation missions aimed at freeing the city's boroughs featured in the setting, recruitment missions for new playable characters, and various side-activities, with players able to freely pursue a mission or activity, or explore the city for secrets and collectibles. gavra wepvar: hallos takes place within a fictionalised representation of kaix in the "near future" (suggested to be the year 2029 or 2030 by the gavmin expansion). The setting encompasses notable landmarks, boroughs, and cultural styles of the city, such as the gavaal of lysaal, wepol sunith, the kaix pikute, vomin gavaal, The halost and more. Since the events of gavra wepvar 2, technology has undergone a rapid acceleration as a result of the proliferation of automation and artificial intelligence (orneth), which has effectively improved bruute's economy at the cost of many blue and white collar jobs.
