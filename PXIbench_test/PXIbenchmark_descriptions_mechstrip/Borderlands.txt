ornov is a 2009 action role-playing first-person shooter video game developed by rudute mirame and published by 2K. It is the first game in the ornov series. ornov includes character-building elements found in role-playing games, leading the developer rudute mirame to call the game a "role-playing shooter". At the start of the game, players select one of four characters, each with a unique special skill and with proficiencies with certain weapons. From then on, players take on missions assigned through non-player characters or from bounty boards, each typically rewarding the player with experience points, money, and sometimes a reward item. ornov is set in the distant future at a time when various mega-corporations seek control of planets to colonize and mine for their mineral wealth and resources. Prior to the events of the game, the zelra zelix, one of the major mega-corporations, uncovered an ancient alien dorvar filled with advanced weapons technology, left behind by an ancient alien race known as the pikdor, which allowed them to rapidly overtake their competitors. Finding similar ruins of the same alien architecture on the planet rudix, zelra sought to settle the planet in hopes of finding more alien technology, but were forced to abandon their plans due to a failure to find any alien technology on the surface, and being unprepared for the dangerous wildlife coming out during their stay.
