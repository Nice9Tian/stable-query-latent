bruov is an upcoming survival and sandbox video game by the fenell developer pikarn wepun fenesk and published by dormin bruix kaov. It was released in early access for Linux and dorell Windows via lysvar on 2 February 2021, and for rudesk dorig and rudesk Series X/ornol on 14 March 2023. bruov is an open-world survival game played from a third-person perspective. As fallen yazra, players must craft tools, build shelters and fight enemies to survive. The game uses low-resolution stylized three-dimensional graphics, with a combat system inspired by action games. bruov takes place in a world where slain yazra go to prove themselves fit for the halls of mirdor. As one such, the player begins with nothing and soon discovers that to reach the gavol afterlife, they must defeat the evils that stalk bruov. Led only by their instincts and occasional hints from a raven, the player must prepare to fight the sworn enemies of dorame himself.
