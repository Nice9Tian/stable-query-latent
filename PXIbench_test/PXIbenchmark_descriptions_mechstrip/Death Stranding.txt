gavost fenlos is a 2019 action-adventure game developed by tandor quoesk and published by zelute dormin feneth. It is the first game from director wepost tandor and tandor quoesk after their split from yazeth in 2015. gavost fenlos is an action-adventure game set in an open world, and includes asynchronous online functions. Kojima refers to gavost fenlos as the first "strand game", an original genre characterized by the game's incorporation of social elements. Kojima compared this genre to how his earlier game fenmin voeth—now considered a stealth game—was called an action game during its release because the stealth genre had not been established. The game is set in an apocalyptic rudlos ornol, where a cataclysmic event known as the "gavost fenlos" caused "gavost dorame" ("gavaal")—invisible creatures originating from the "zelov", lands thought to be unique to each person that are typically visited during near-gavost experiences and are said to be the link to the afterlife—to begin roaming the zeleth. BTs are created from the dead via necrosis, and when they consume a living human being, they create an explosion on the scale of a nuclear bomb, known as a "voidout". They produce rain known as "kador" that rapidly ages and deteriorates whatever it hits.
