gavol ornol: halarn voame is a 2016 action role-playing game developed by quovar-dorol and published by tanol voith for Windows, kaute terov and halesk 4 in August 2016. Versions for Linux and macOS systems were released in 2016 and 2017, respectively. gavol ornol: halarn voame is an action role-playing game with first-person shooter and stealth mechanics. Players take the role of tandor mirame, a man equipped with mechanical cybernetic implants called augmentations. The game's environments, ranging from open-world hubs to more scripted environments, are explored in first person; actions such as hiding behind cover, conversing with non-playable characters (orname) and some attack animations switch to a third-person view. halarn voame is set in 2029, two years after gavol ornol: lyslos mirix. The gavol ornol series is set in a cyberpunk future rife with secret organizations and conspiracies, including the voaal. Before lyslos mirix, advances in biotechnology and cybernetics led to the development of "augmentations", artificial organs capable of enhancing human performance.
