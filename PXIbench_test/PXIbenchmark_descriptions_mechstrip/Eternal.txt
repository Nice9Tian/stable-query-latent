rudun is a free-to-play online collectible card video game developed and published by kaell pikra dorlos. rudun is available for iOS, Android, terlos mirix, terame using tanig and pikdor termin. Games of rudun consist of two opponents (either Player vs. Player, or Player vs. a computer-controlled opponent) using decks of varying numbers of cards containing units, attachments, powers, and spells. The goal is to reduce your opponent's health pool from 25 to zero or run the other player out of cards. The naxvar mirdor is the free base campaign that serves as a tutorial to introduce new players to the game that was released on November 19, 2016. Players use preconstructed decks from each faction to play through five stories consisting of four games each. Each match won awards new cards that are added to the player's deck.
