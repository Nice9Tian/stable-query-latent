kamin is a puzzle game developed by tanarn for zeldor Windows, Mac yazaal, yazaal naxov, yazaal 3DS, Android, iOS, and iPadOS. The game was originally launched on 19 June 2008. The object of the game is to solve puzzles and earn coins to set up and decorate a virtual aquarium. Using in-game currency the player is able to purchase different species of fish, decorations, and "comfort" items to make the environment more pleasant for pet fishes. Filling all of the three meter gauges measuring the progress will unlock one of the trophies: bronze, silver, or gold. Each of the rounds takes place on a different grid and poses a challenge to earn golden tiles by matching gems at least once over each one.
