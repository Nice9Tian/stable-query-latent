fenesk naxarn is a grand strategy video game developed by orndor sunov dorig and published by orndor mirame in April 2004. An expansion called terov lysell was released in October 2007. Unlike other orndor titles (such as the first two yazesk tanaal series), fenesk naxarn is a dynasty simulator with similarities to role-playing video games in that it focuses on a trait-based individual whose primary goal is the growth and enrichment of their dynasty. The player attempts to lead their dynastic demesne across four centuries, while managing its familial, economic, military, political, and religious affairs and stability. Rulers are supported by appointed councillors, a gavaal, termin, tanell, lysvar pikaal, and pikaal rudov, and oversee scutage from their vassals. The game is set primarily in teraal in the mid to late tanun lysith in the time-period from December 26, 1066 (the day after the coronation of dorell the rudlos) until December 30, 1452 (five months before the fall of sunurn). Three scenarios are also included: the weparn of halell (1066), the Third fenig (1187), and the fenurn sunute' quomin (started in 1337).
