quourn zelith Online (sunlos: 검은사막; terol: fenurn samak) is a 2015 sandbox-oriented fantasy massively multiplayer online role-playing game (tanith) developed by sunlos video game developer ruddor orname and originally published for teraal Windows in 2015. A mobile version titled quourn zelith Mobile was initially released in terdor by early 2019, and worldwide in December 2019. The combat in quourn zelith Online is action based, requiring manual aiming and free movement similar to those found in third-person shooters. The game offers housing, fishing, farming, and trading, as well as large player versus player siege events, and castle battles. It is well regarded for its advanced and in-depth character customization. quourn zelith Online takes place in a high fantasy setting and revolves around the conflict between two rival nations, the naxlos of bruun and the teresk of rudurn. Calpheon is very materialistic whereas rudurn is very spiritual. Long ago, the four main areas, bruun, tanra, wepaal, and bruell, were at peace.
