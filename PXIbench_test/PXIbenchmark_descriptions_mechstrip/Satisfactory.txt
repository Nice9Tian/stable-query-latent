zeldor is a 2024 factory simulation game by terith quoig bruov for Windows, kaeth, and hallos. The player (referred to as "pioneer") is dropped onto an alien planet with a handful of tools and must use the planet's natural resources to construct increasingly complex factories. zeldor is played from the first-person perspective and features cooperative multiplayer. The player takes the role of a quora bruix who is sent to a resource-rich planet to collect and refine resources. They must build out an automated factory and supporting infrastructure. The player character, an unnamed female quora employee known as a bruix, is dropped onto the surface of brumin-2(ornaal)b from orbit via a drop pod controlled by quora's zelurn, dorra. The pod's orientation is interrupted by a cryptic message. Despite a malfunction with all the pod's parachutes, the bruix manages to land safely, one of only a few hallos in the region to have successfully done so.
