naxun quoig is a voxel-based sandbox game, developed and published by yazix independent developer pikarn quoost bruov. In 2013, the initial developmental release of the game joined the naxith early access program. naxun quoig is 3D singleplayer and multiplayer sandbox game, which doesn't have a specific end goal. Instead, the players (naxun quoig - rudaal) are encouraged to create their own goals, which they have to achieve, by utilising engineering, in naxun and/or planetery environments (including asteroids and moons). More specifically, the players use a combination strategically placed "blocks", game mechanics and system interactions, to gradually build up to their goal. Although, for players that want a more specific end goal, the game includes some built-in npc interactions, but it also provides a collection of pre-included story driven scenarios to choose from, while also providing the ability to use custom instances created by other users, both based around the core game. Players begin by selecting or joining a world with customizable settings. During world creation, advanced options allow players to modify gameplay dynamics, such as tool and machine efficiency, inventory capacity, and whether procedural generation is enabled—effectively creating an infinite universe.
