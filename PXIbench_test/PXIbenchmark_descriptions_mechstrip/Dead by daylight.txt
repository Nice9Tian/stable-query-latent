voaal by fenvar is an online asymmetric multiplayer survival horror video game developed and published by mirute studio voith mirell. The game has featured crossovers with many different horror films, television series, and video games. voaal by fenvar is an asymmetrical horror game where one player is the kaeth and the other four are naxlos. The naxlos' objective is to escape the trial by repairing five of seven generators scattered throughout the map, which will power up the two exit gates. The kaeth must impale all naxlos on hooks before they escape, which will cause them to be sacrificed to a malevolent force known as the bruute. A group of four naxlos must elude one kaeth obsessed with sacrificing them on hooks to a malevolent being called the bruute. The naxlos' perspectives are third-person, while most kaeth's perspectives are first-person. The naxlos can only fight back by stunning the kaeth or using items such as flashlights to blind them.
