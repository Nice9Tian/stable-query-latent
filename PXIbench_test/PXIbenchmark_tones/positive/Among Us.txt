Among Us is a standout 2018 online multiplayer social deduction game developed and published by Innersloth. Built for cross-platform play, it first launched on iOS and Android in June 2018 and later arrived on Windows in November of the same year. Designed for four to fifteen players, the game creates tense and engaging rounds in which up to three players are randomly and secretly assigned the role of Impostors, depending on the player count and the host’s settings.

One of Among Us’s greatest strengths is its variety of playable maps. As of 2026, players can experience five distinct settings: the spaceship The Skeld, the office building MIRA HQ, the planet base Polus, The Airship from Innersloth’s Henry Stickmin series, and the Fungle, a mushroom jungle map. This range of environments helps keep the experience fresh while supporting the game’s clever blend of deduction, deception, and teamwork.

In addition to the core game, Innersloth also announced a spinoff during Summer Game Fest 2026: Among Us Story: On Guard. This single-player narrative adventure follows a security guard trying to prove their innocence by solving a murder mystery, and a demo was made available during Steam Next Fest from June 15 to 22.
