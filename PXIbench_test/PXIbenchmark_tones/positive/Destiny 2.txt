Destiny 2 is a free-to-play online first-person shooter developed by Bungie, set in a rich “mythic science fiction” universe. Like its predecessor, it blends fast-paced shooting with role-playing and MMO-inspired elements, creating a multiplayer shared-world experience that feels both expansive and engaging.

The game offers a strong variety of activities beyond its main story missions. In PvE, players can take on three-player strikes, challenging dungeons, and six-player raids, all of which add depth and replayability to the experience. Its shared-world structure encourages interaction and cooperation, while the multiplayer design supports a dynamic sense of adventure.

Destiny 2 has been praised for its improvements over the original Destiny, especially its initial story, as well as its gameplay, visuals, exploration focus, multiplayer, and public events. These strengths help make it a standout title that combines atmosphere, action, and social play in a highly polished package.
