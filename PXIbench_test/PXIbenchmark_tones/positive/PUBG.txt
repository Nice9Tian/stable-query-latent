PUBG: Battlegrounds, originally released as PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds, is a landmark 2017 battle royale game published by Krafton and developed by PUBG Studios. Inspired by the Japanese film *Battle Royale* and built from earlier mods created by Brendan “PlayerUnknown” Greene, the game grew into a standalone experience under Greene’s creative direction. It stands as one of the most influential entries in the genre, helping define the modern battle royale formula.

At its core, PUBG is a player-versus-player shooter where up to one hundred players compete in a large-scale last-man-standing deathmatch. Players can join the battlefield solo, in duos, or as part of a small team of up to four. The objective is simple but intensely engaging: survive against all others and become the final player or team alive to claim victory.

PUBG’s impact has been widely recognized, earning several Game of the Year nominations and setting seven Guinness World Records. Its commercial success has been equally impressive, with worldwide revenue reaching $13 billion by Q3 2022, including earnings from its highly successful mobile version. This achievement places it among the highest-grossing video games of all time.

Even as the game continued to evolve, PUBG Corporation showed a commitment to improving the experience through the “Fix PUBG” campaign launched in August 2018, acknowledging ongoing bugs and performance issues. With its massive scale, competitive tension, and lasting cultural influence, PUBG: Battlegrounds remains a defining title in the history of multiplayer gaming.
