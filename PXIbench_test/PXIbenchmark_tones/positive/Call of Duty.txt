Call of Duty is a landmark 2003 first-person shooter developed by Infinity Ward and published by Activision, and it proudly launched the long-running Call of Duty franchise. Released for Microsoft Windows on October 29, 2003, the game delivers an immersive World War II combat experience centered on infantry action and authentic period weaponry.

As a first-person shooter, Call of Duty puts players directly into the role of a soldier on the battlefield. Combat is built around a wide range of realistic World War II firearms, reinforcing the game’s historical atmosphere and giving each encounter a strong sense of authenticity. Missions are structured around clear objectives, which are displayed on the heads-up display’s compass. Players must complete every objective in a mission to move forward to the next one, creating a focused and disciplined campaign flow. The game also allows saving and loading at any time, offering a flexible system that differs from the checkpoint-based approach used in later entries in the series.

The American campaign begins in August 1942 with Private Martin, a newly enlisted member of the 506th Parachute Infantry Regiment in Baker Company, as he completes basic training at Camp Toccoa under the supervision of Cpt. In June 1944, Martin later helps establish a landing zone for soldiers taking part in Operation Overlord. After the paratroopers are scattered, Martin is left in a mixed unit made up of members from different companies, led by his CO Foley, and together they seize a nearby town from the Germans.

With its authentic weapons, objective-driven missions, and wartime setting, Call of Duty stands out as a strong and memorable introduction to one of gaming’s most influential franchises.
