Genshin Impact is a 2020 action role-playing game developed and published by miHoYo that stands out for its expansive open-world design and dynamic party-based combat. Set in the richly imagined world of Teyvat, the game invites players to explore a land made up of seven major nations, each governed by an Archon—an all-powerful god associated with a specific element and ideal. Above this world floats Celestia, a mysterious island said to be home to the gods and to mortals who have ascended to godhood, while beneath the surface lie the ruined remains of Khaenri'ah, a lost nation destroyed by Celestia 500 years ago.

One of Genshin Impact’s most engaging features is its four-character party system. Players control one character at a time, but can switch quickly between all four during combat, creating flexible and energetic battles. This design encourages strategic use of each character’s strengths and abilities, making combat feel lively and responsive. Characters can also be strengthened in several ways, especially by gaining experience points and improving their artifacts and weapons, allowing players to steadily develop their team over time.

With its open-world structure, elemental worldbuilding, and fast-paced character swapping, Genshin Impact offers a polished and inviting adventure that blends exploration, progression, and combat into a compelling experience.
