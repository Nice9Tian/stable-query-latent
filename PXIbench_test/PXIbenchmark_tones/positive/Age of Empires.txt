Age of Empires is a landmark 1997 real-time strategy game set in the ancient world, developed by Ensemble Studios and published by Microsoft. As the first entry in the long-running Age of Empires series, it helped define a style of historical strategy gameplay that remains influential. Built on the Genie Engine, a 2D sprite-based game engine, the game presents its world with clarity and charm while focusing on deep strategic decision-making.

At the heart of Age of Empires is the challenge of guiding a civilization from a small group of hunter-gatherers into a powerful Iron Age empire. Progress depends on careful resource management and smart long-term planning. Players must gather resources to fund new units, construct buildings, and unlock more advanced technologies, making every decision important. The game also emphasizes permanence and scarcity: resources must be used wisely because no new resources appear as the game advances. For example, trees that are cut down do not grow back, adding a strong layer of realism and strategic tension.

Age of Empires also offers four single-player campaigns, each built around a series of scenarios with specific objectives. These campaigns are completed in a linear fashion, providing structured historical challenges that reward thoughtful play and steady advancement. Together, these elements create a rich and satisfying strategy experience that showcases the strength of its classic design and the enduring appeal of its historical setting.
