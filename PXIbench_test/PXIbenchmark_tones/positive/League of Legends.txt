*League of Legends* is a highly influential multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) developed and published by Riot Games. Inspired by *Defense of the Ancients*, a custom map for *Warcraft III*, it was created as a standalone game in the same genre and has since become one of the defining titles of competitive online gaming.

In *League of Legends*, players control a champion, each with a distinct set of unique abilities, from an isometric perspective. Matches center on strategic team play, where champions grow stronger by earning experience points through combat and by defeating enemy units. As the game progresses, players can purchase items to increase their champions’ power, using gold that is earned passively over time and actively through defeating enemy minions, champions, and defensive structures.

The game’s early setting framed players in the role of political leaders known as “Summoners,” who commanded champions to battle on the Fields of Justice, such as Summoner’s Rift, in order to prevent a catastrophic war. While its narrative was presented in relatively straightforward “good versus evil” terms, the game’s core appeal lies in its deep competitive mechanics, champion variety, and strategic progression system. In its early development, Riot Games did not employ dedicated writers, and character biographies were initially kept brief, yet the game still established a recognizable universe around its roster of champions.

Overall, *League of Legends* stands out for its accessible premise, rich tactical gameplay, and enduring competitive structure, making it a landmark title in the MOBA genre.
