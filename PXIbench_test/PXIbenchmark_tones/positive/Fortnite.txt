Fortnite is a standout online video game and game platform developed and released by Epic Games, first launched in 2017. It began with Save the World and Battle Royale as early access titles, followed by Creative on December 6, 2018. Over time, Fortnite has grown into a remarkably successful and influential platform, with Battle Royale emerging as a true cultural phenomenon, attracting more than 125 million players in less than a year and generating hundreds of millions of dollars per month.

One of Fortnite’s greatest strengths is the breadth of its available experiences. Save the World, Battle Royale, and Creative each offer distinct ways to play, while newer modes such as Lego Fortnite, Rocket Racing, Fortnite Festival, and Fortnite Ballistic are also available across all platforms. This wide variety helps make Fortnite feel fresh, ambitious, and accessible to different types of players.

The game also stands out for its strong cross-platform support. Most game modes allow players on different supported devices to play together, reinforcing Fortnite’s role as a highly connected and widely accessible multiplayer experience. Save the World is available on macOS, PlayStation 4, Windows, and Xbox One, while Battle Royale and Creative extend further to Android, iOS, and Nintendo Switch as well. Through its range of modes, broad platform availability, and massive popularity, Fortnite has established itself as one of the most successful and enduring titles in modern gaming.
