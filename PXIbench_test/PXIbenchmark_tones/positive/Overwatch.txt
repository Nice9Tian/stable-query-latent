Overwatch is a standout multimedia franchise centered on team-based first-person shooter gameplay, developed by Blizzard Entertainment. Since its debut in 2016, it has built a strong identity around hero selection, class-based teamwork, and a rich transmedia approach to storytelling that extends far beyond the game itself.

At its core, Overwatch is a hero shooter where players choose from a large and diverse roster of characters, with 52 heroes available as of Season 2. These heroes are divided into three clear class types, each serving a distinct role in battle. Tanks are durable frontline protectors with higher health, designed to shield teammates from incoming damage, though their larger size makes them easier targets. Damage heroes serve as the team’s offensive specialists, leading attacks and applying pressure. Support heroes provide healing, buffs for allies, and debuffs to weaken enemies, making them essential to a well-balanced team.

The series began with Overwatch in 2016, a game that notably did not include a traditional story mode. Instead, Blizzard used comics, literary media, animated shorts, and other forms of storytelling to develop its lore and bring its characters to life. This approach gave the franchise a distinctive narrative style that deepened player engagement while keeping the focus on multiplayer action.

Overwatch 2 launched in 2022, and the original game was taken offline when it released, before Blizzard renamed the game back to Overwatch in 2026. Both entries in the series remain centered on coordinated team play and the signature hero-based structure that helped define the franchise.

Beyond the game itself, Overwatch has also been strongly supported by Blizzard’s investment in esports. The company helped establish and promote a competitive scene that included the annual Overwatch World Cup, the Overwatch League, a minor league, and the Overwatch Champions Series, which borrowed elements from traditional American sports leagues. This combination of fast-paced team combat, memorable heroes, expanded storytelling, and competitive support has made Overwatch an influential and highly regarded franchise.
