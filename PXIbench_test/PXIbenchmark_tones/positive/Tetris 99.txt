Tetris 99 is a standout 2019 battle royale puzzle game developed by Arika and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. As an online multiplayer adaptation of the classic Tetris formula, it brings a fresh and exciting competitive twist to one of gaming’s most enduring puzzle experiences.

In the game, players rotate and drop tetrominoes onto a board, working to complete and clear rows by filling them completely with pieces. The core challenge remains true to traditional Tetris: managing the falling shapes efficiently while avoiding overflow at the top of the board. What sets Tetris 99 apart is its intense multiplayer structure, where 99 players compete at the same time with the goal of being the last one remaining. This battle royale format transforms every placement into a strategic decision, adding pressure and excitement to the timeless puzzle gameplay.

Tetris 99 succeeds by combining the familiar elegance of Tetris with a fast-paced competitive format that feels both accessible and thrilling. It delivers a polished, highly engaging puzzle experience built around skill, speed, and survival.
