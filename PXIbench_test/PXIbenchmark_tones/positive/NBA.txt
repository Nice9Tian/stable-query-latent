NBA is a basketball video game series developed by San Diego Studio and published by Sony Computer Entertainment. Licensed by the National Basketball Association, it stands as one of the notable NBA-focused basketball game franchises, bringing the excitement of professional basketball to video game players.

One of the series’ distinctive qualities is its presentation and approach. Rather than featuring a single cover athlete, it showcases a group of six NBA stars: Carmelo Anthony, Carlos Boozer, Kobe Bryant, LeBron James, Paul Pierce, and Dwyane Wade. This roster of prominent players highlights the game’s strong connection to the league and its celebration of basketball excellence.

The series also includes some memorable promotional elements. Carlesimo appeared in commercials alongside three fictitious NBA players, adding a creative and recognizable touch to its marketing.

What further sets this game apart from many other NBA titles is that it includes a story. This narrative element gives the experience an added layer of personality and helps distinguish it within the basketball game genre. Overall, NBA offers a unique and officially licensed basketball experience that combines star power, presentation, and storytelling in an appealing way.
