The Elder Scrolls is a celebrated series of action role-playing video games primarily developed by Bethesda Game Studios and published by Bethesda Softworks. Known for its expansive open-world design and free-form gameplay, the series offers players a rich and flexible adventure experience. It combines action RPG foundations with elements drawn from action and adventure games, creating a style that feels both immersive and dynamic.

Across the series, character progression has evolved in interesting ways. In Arena, players advance by defeating monsters and earning experience points until they reach a set value and level up. In Daggerfall, Morrowind, and Oblivion, the franchise shifted to a skill-based approach to advancement, giving players a more personalized sense of growth through the abilities they use. This progression system is one of the many features that helps define the identity of the series.

Set in a high fantasy world influenced by real-world cultures, The Elder Scrolls presents a setting that is both familiar and imaginative. Its tone is typically serious and its scope is epic, often focusing on grand conflicts against supernatural or evil forces. Magic and sorcery, mythical creatures, politically driven factions, medieval walled cities and strongholds, and stories shaped by prophecies and legends are all central to its world-building. These elements combine to make The Elder Scrolls a standout fantasy series with a deep sense of adventure and atmosphere.
