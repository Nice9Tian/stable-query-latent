Among Us is a 2018 online multiplayer social deduction game developed and published by Innersloth. It supports cross-platform play and was released on iOS and Android in June 2018, followed by a Windows release in November of the same year.

The game is designed for four to fifteen players. In each round, up to three players are randomly and secretly assigned the role of Impostors, depending on the number of players and the host’s settings. Players then take part in a social deduction format centered on identifying who the Impostors are while participating in the round’s gameplay.

As of 2026, Among Us includes five playable maps: The Skeld, a spaceship; MIRA HQ, an office building; Polus, a planet base; The Airship, a setting from Innersloth’s Henry Stickmin series; and the Fungle, a mushroom jungle map.

At Summer Game Fest 2026, Innersloth also announced a spinoff titled Among Us Story: On Guard. This is a single-player narrative adventure in which players follow a security guard trying to prove their innocence by solving a murder mystery. A demo for the spinoff was available during Steam Next Fest from June 15 to 22.
