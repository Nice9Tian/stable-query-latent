Super Mario Bros. is a 1985 platform game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES). Directed and produced by Shigeru Miyamoto, it is the successor to the 1983 arcade game Mario Bros. and the first entry in the Super Mario series.

The game follows Mario, who is sent to explore the Mushroom Kingdom, defeat Bowser, and rescue Princess Toadstool. In multiplayer mode, the second player controls Luigi, who shares the same role and gameplay functions as Mario. The game is presented from a side-scrolling perspective, with players moving to the right in order to reach the flagpole at the end of each level.

The story is set after the events of Mario Bros. in the fantasy world of the Mushroom Kingdom, where Mario and Luigi have arrived through a clay pipe from New York City. In this setting, the turtle-like Koopa Troopas, led by King Bowser, invade the kingdom and use magic to turn the Mushroom People into objects such as bricks, stones, and horsehair plants. Bowser and his army also kidnap Princess Toadstool, the only one able to reverse his spell.
