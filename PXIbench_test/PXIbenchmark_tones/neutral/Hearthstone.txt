Hearthstone is a free-to-play online digital collectible card game released by Blizzard Entertainment in 2014. Originally subtitled *Heroes of Warcraft*, it draws on the lore of the *Warcraft* series, using familiar characters, elements, and relics from that universe.

The game is a digital-only, turn-based card game in which two opponents compete against each other. Players choose a hero from one of eleven classes, and each class has its own unique cards and abilities, known as hero powers. These class-specific features help shape the game’s different archetypes and play styles.

Hearthstone also makes use of concepts and artwork that were previously developed for the physical *World of Warcraft Trading Card Game*. In that earlier game, published first by Upper Deck in 2006 and later by Cryptozoic Entertainment, Blizzard had already established a foundation of themes and assets that would later be adapted into Hearthstone. During Hearthstone’s development, the team drew heavily from this existing material. The game also included Adventure content, which featured around 30 cards and could only be unlocked by completing multiple tiers of story-based challenges and boss fights in single-player mode.
