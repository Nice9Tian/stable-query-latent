Tom Clancy’s Rainbow Six Siege is a 2015 tactical shooter developed by Ubisoft Montreal and published by Ubisoft. It is a first-person shooter that places strong emphasis on environmental destruction and teamwork. Players take control of a range of operators from the Rainbow team, each with different nationalities, weapons, and gadgets.

The game uses an asymmetrical structure, meaning the teams are not always equally balanced in terms of abilities. Rather than featuring a traditional campaign, it includes offline training modes that can be played solo. The developers focused on the core identity of the Rainbow Six franchise and aimed to let players step into the roles of top counter-terrorist operatives from around the world.

Rainbow Six Siege also includes a premium currency called R6 Credits, which can be purchased with real-world money. These credits can be used to unlock operators faster in-game or to obtain cosmetic items such as weapon and operator skins.
