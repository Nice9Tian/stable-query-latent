Grand Theft Auto is a 1997 action-adventure game developed by DMA Design and published by BMG Interactive. It is the first entry in the Grand Theft Auto series and was released in November 1997 for MS-DOS and Windows, in December 1997 for the PlayStation, and in October 1999 for the Game Boy Color.

The game is structured into six levels spread across three main cities, each inspired by a real-world U.S. city under an alternate name: Liberty City, based on New York City; San Andreas, based on San Francisco; and Vice City, based on Miami. Progression is linear, with each completed level automatically unlocking the next one in the sequence.

At the start of a new game, players choose and name a character from a set of eight options, or four in the PlayStation version. This selection is purely cosmetic and does not affect gameplay. The story follows the protagonist’s early career in Liberty City, where they work for Robert “Bubby” Seragliano’s gang. After completing numerous jobs, a rival gang warns the protagonist that their actions are causing serious harm to Seragliano’s boss, Sonetti. The protagonist continues working for Seragliano and eventually kills Sonetti.
