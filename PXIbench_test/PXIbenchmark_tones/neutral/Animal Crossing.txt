Animal Crossing, known in Japan as *Dōbutsu no Mori*, is a social simulation game developed and published by Nintendo. It was first released in 2001 for the Nintendo 64 in Japan, and later received an enhanced port for the GameCube.

Nintendo describes the game as a “communication game.” It is open-ended, with very little set plot or mandatory tasks, allowing the player’s character to live a separate life in the town. Players take on the role of a new resident and can progress at their own pace.

The game was developed by Nintendo EAD with an inexperienced team led by Katsuya Eguchi, Hisashi Nogami, and Super Mario co-creator Takashi Tezuka. Most of the team had regrouped after the release of *Yoshi’s Story* in 1997. The game was also shown at Nintendo Space World 2000, where it included playable versions of *Balloon Fight*, *Ice Climber*, *Clu Clu Land*, and *Donkey Kong*.
