Dota 2 is a 2013 multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed by Valve and a sequel to Defense of the Ancients (DotA), a community-created mod for Blizzard Entertainment’s Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos. The game places two teams of five players against each other, with each side trying to destroy the opposing team’s large structure known as the “Ancient” while defending its own.

The game uses standard real-time strategy controls and is presented on a single map from a three-dimensional isometric perspective. Each match features ten players, with each player controlling one of the game’s 127 playable characters, called heroes. These heroes have distinct designs, strengths, and weaknesses, contributing to the game’s strategic variety.

Dota 2 also has a significant esports presence, with teams from around the world competing in professional leagues and tournaments. In addition to its core mode, Valve has created several special game modes, including a ten-versus-ten mode, a Halloween-themed capture point mode called Colosseum, a combat arena mode called Overthrow, Siltbreaker, a story-driven cooperative campaign mode, and The Underhollow, a battle royale mode. The game follows in the tradition of Defense of the Ancients, which had become one of the world’s most popular mods and a prominent esports title by the late 2000s.
