NBA is a series of six basketball video games developed by San Diego Studio and published by Sony Computer Entertainment. The series is licensed by the National Basketball Association (NBA) and is one of several NBA-focused basketball video game series.

The games include a distinctive presentation approach, featuring a group of six NBA players rather than a single cover athlete. These players are Carmelo Anthony, Carlos Boozer, Kobe Bryant, LeBron James, Paul Pierce, and Dwyane Wade. The series also included commercials featuring three fictitious NBA players, with Carlesimo appearing in those commercials.

Unlike most NBA games, this one included a story element.
