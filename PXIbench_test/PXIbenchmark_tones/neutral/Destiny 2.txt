Destiny 2 is a free-to-play online first-person shooter developed by Bungie. Set in a “mythic science fiction” world, it presents a multiplayer shared-world environment that incorporates role-playing game elements. Like its predecessor, the game combines first-person shooting with RPG and MMO features.

The game includes normal story missions as well as PvE content such as three-player strikes, dungeons, and six-player raids. Its multiplayer structure supports interaction within the shared world, and the original Destiny featured on-the-fly matchmaking, with communication initially limited to players matched by the game until in-game text chat was later added. 

Destiny 2 was praised for improvements in several areas, especially its initial story, gameplay, visuals, exploration focus, multiplayer, and public events.
