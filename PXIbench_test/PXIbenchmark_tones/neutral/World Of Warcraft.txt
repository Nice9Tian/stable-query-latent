World of Warcraft is a 2004 massively multiplayer online role-playing game developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment for Windows and macOS. Set in the Warcraft fantasy universe, the game takes place on the fictional planet Azeroth, roughly four years after the events of Warcraft III: The Frozen Throne.

Players experience the Warcraft world through their characters in a 3D environment. As characters develop, they gain talents and skills, allowing players to further shape and specialize their abilities. Characters can choose two primary professions, which may focus on crafting items such as tailoring, blacksmithing, or jewelcrafting, or on gathering resources such as skinning or mining. In addition, all characters can learn three secondary skills: archaeology, cooking, and fishing.

World of Warcraft shares the same universe and art direction as the Warcraft real-time strategy series. Its setting combines fantasy, steampunk, and science fiction elements, featuring creatures and themes such as gryphons, dragons, elves, steam-powered automata, zombies, werewolves, horror monsters, time travel, spaceships, and alien worlds.
