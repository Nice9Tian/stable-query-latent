FIFA is an association football simulation video game franchise developed by EA Vancouver and EA Romania and published by EA Sports from 1993 to 2022. It entered a competitive football video game market that had already seen titles such as Tehkan World Cup, Sensible Soccer, Kick Off, and Match Day since the late 1980s.

The main FIFA series was expanded with additional releases tied to major football tournaments, including the FIFA World Cup, UEFA Champions League, UEFA Europa League, and UEFA European Football Championship. It also included a series of football management titles.

A notable feature of the series was its use of licensed real player names after obtaining the FIFPro license. On the PlayStation, PC, 32X, and Sega Saturn, the games used EA’s “Virtual Stadium” engine, which presented 2D sprite players moving within a real-time 3D stadium. FIFA 95 added club teams, while FIFA 96 further developed the series. FIFA 97 introduced polygonal player models and an indoor soccer mode. FIFA: Road to World Cup 98 is described as an early pinnacle of the series.
