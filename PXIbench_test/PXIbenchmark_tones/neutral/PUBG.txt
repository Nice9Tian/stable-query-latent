PUBG: Battlegrounds, previously titled PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds, is a 2017 battle royale video game published by Krafton and developed by Krafton's PUBG Studios. Inspired by the Japanese film *Battle Royale* (2000), the game was built from earlier mods created by Brendan “PlayerUnknown” Greene for other games and later expanded into a standalone title under his creative direction.

PUBG is a player-versus-player shooter in which up to one hundred players compete in a battle royale format, a large-scale last-man-standing deathmatch. Players can enter a match solo, as a duo, or in a small team of up to four people. The objective is to survive until only one player or team remains, and that final survivor or surviving group wins the match.

The game received several Game of the Year nominations and set seven Guinness World Records, among other accolades. By Q3 2022, PUBG had generated $13 billion in worldwide revenue, including revenue from its more successful mobile version, making it one of the highest-grossing video games of all time. In August 2018, PUBG Corporation launched the “Fix PUBG” campaign, acknowledging that the game still had lingering bugs and other performance issues at that time.
