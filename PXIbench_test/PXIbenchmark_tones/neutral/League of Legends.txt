League of Legends (LoL), commonly referred to as League, is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) video game developed and published by Riot Games. Inspired by Defense of the Ancients, a custom map for Warcraft III, the game was created by Riot’s founders as a stand-alone title in the same genre.

In League of Legends, players control a character called a “champion” from an isometric perspective. Each champion has a set of unique abilities. During matches, champions gain levels by earning experience points through defeating enemies. Players can also purchase items to strengthen their champions, using gold that is accumulated passively over time and earned more actively by defeating the enemy team’s minions, champions, or defensive structures.

Before 2014, the game’s fictional setting described players as political leaders, or “Summoners,” who commanded champions to fight on the Fields of Justice, such as Summoner’s Rift, in order to prevent a catastrophic war. The game’s early narrative has been described as lacking the political themes seen in some other role-playing games, instead presenting its conflict in simplified “good versus evil” terms. In its early development, Riot did not employ writers, and character biographies were written by designers and kept to only a paragraph in length.
