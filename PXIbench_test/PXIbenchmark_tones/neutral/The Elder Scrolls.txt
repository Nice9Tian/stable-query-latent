The Elder Scrolls is a series of action role-playing video games primarily developed by Bethesda Game Studios and published by Bethesda Softworks. The series is known for its open-world structure and free-form gameplay, allowing players to explore and progress through its worlds with considerable freedom.

Across the series, The Elder Scrolls combines action and role-playing elements with features drawn from action and adventure games. Its approach to character advancement has varied over time. In Arena, players progress by defeating monsters and earning experience points until reaching a preset value, at which point they level up. In Daggerfall, Morrowind, and Oblivion, the series adopted a skill-based system for character development.

The games are set in a high fantasy world influenced by real-world cultures. They generally present a serious tone and an epic scope, often centered on a grand conflict against supernatural or evil forces. Common elements include magic and sorcery, mythical creatures, factions with political ambitions, medieval cities and fortresses, and stories shaped by prophecies and legends.
