Overwatch is a multimedia franchise centered on a series of multiplayer first-person shooter games developed by Blizzard Entertainment. The series is built around team-based hero shooter gameplay, where players choose from a large roster of hero characters divided into three class types: Tanks, Damage, and Support.

Tanks have higher health and are generally intended to protect teammates from damage, though their larger size makes them easier to hit. Damage heroes serve as the team’s offensive leads, while Support heroes heal allies, provide buffs, or apply debuffs to opponents. As of Season 2, the roster included 52 heroes.

The original Overwatch was released in 2016 and did not include a traditional story mode. Instead, Blizzard used transmedia storytelling to develop the game’s lore, publishing comics and other literary works, along with animated media such as short films. Overwatch 2 was released in 2022, at which point the original game was taken offline, although Blizzard renamed it back to Overwatch in 2026.

The franchise also supported a competitive esports scene. Blizzard helped establish and promote events including the annual Overwatch World Cup, the Overwatch League, described as a minor league, and the Overwatch Champions Series, which borrowed elements from traditional American sports leagues.
