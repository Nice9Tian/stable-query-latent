Fortnite is an online video game and game platform developed and released by Epic Games in 2017. It launched with Save the World and Battle Royale as early access titles, followed by Creative on December 6, 2018. In addition to these core modes, Lego Fortnite, Rocket Racing, Fortnite Festival, and Fortnite Ballistic are also available on all platforms.

The game has seen significant success, especially Fortnite Battle Royale, which became a major cultural phenomenon. It attracted more than 125 million players in less than a year and generated hundreds of millions of dollars per month. Save the World and Creative have also been successful for Epic Games.

Fortnite supports cross-platform play across many of its supported devices. Save the World is available on macOS, PlayStation 4, Windows, and Xbox One. Battle Royale and Creative are also available on those platforms, as well as on Android, iOS, and Nintendo Switch.
