Valorant is a 2020 first-person tactical hero shooter developed and published by Riot Games. The game is free-to-play and draws inspiration from the Counter-Strike series, incorporating mechanics such as the buy menu, spray patterns, and weapon inaccuracy while moving.

Set in the near future, Valorant is built around team-based tactical matches. Players take on the role of one of several Agents, characters drawn from countries and cultures around the world. In the main game mode, teams are divided into attackers and defenders, with five players on each side. The game also includes Premier, which was first introduced in alpha testing in Brazil before being rolled out worldwide by 2024.
