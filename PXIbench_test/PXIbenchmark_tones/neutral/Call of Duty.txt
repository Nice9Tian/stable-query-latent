Call of Duty is a 2003 first-person shooter developed by Infinity Ward and published by Activision. It was released on October 29, 2003, for Microsoft Windows, and it is the first entry in the Call of Duty franchise.

In the game, players control an infantry soldier using a range of authentic World War II firearms in combat. Missions are structured around a sequence of objectives, which are displayed on the heads-up display compass. To progress, the player must complete all objectives in a mission before advancing to the next one. Unlike later games in the series, Call of Duty allows saving and loading at any time rather than using a checkpoint system.

The American campaign begins in August 1942, with Private Martin, a newly enlisted member of the 506th Parachute Infantry Regiment in Baker Company, undergoing basic training at Camp Toccoa under the supervision of Cpt. In June 1944, Martin helps establish a landing zone for soldiers taking part in Operation Overlord. After the paratroopers are scattered, Martin becomes part of a mixed unit formed from several companies, led by his CO Foley, and the unit seizes a nearby town from German forces.
