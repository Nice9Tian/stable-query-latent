*Assassin’s Creed* is a 2007 action-adventure game developed by Ubisoft Montreal and published by Ubisoft. It is the first installment in the *Assassin’s Creed* series and is set in an open-world environment played from a third-person perspective.

The player primarily controls Altaïr Ibn-La’Ahad, with the story experienced through the protagonist Desmond Miles. The main objective is to complete a series of assassinations assigned by Al Mualim, the leader of the Assassins, after Altaïr fails the first mission at Solomon’s Temple and must prove himself again.

The narrative begins in 2012, when bartender Desmond Miles is kidnapped by agents of Abstergo Industries and taken to their headquarters in Rome. There, Warren Vidic and his assistant Lucy Stillman force him to use the Animus, a machine that can turn his ancestors’ genetic memories into a simulated reality. Vidic orders Desmond to relive the early life of Altaïr Ibn-La’Ahad, a senior member of the Assassin Brotherhood during the Third Crusade.
