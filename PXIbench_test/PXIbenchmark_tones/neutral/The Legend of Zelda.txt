The Legend of Zelda is a 1986 action-adventure game developed and published by Nintendo for the Family Computer Disk System. It is the first entry in the long-running Legend of Zelda series and combines elements of action, adventure, and role-playing.

The game follows Link from a flip-screen overhead perspective as he explores the overworld, a large outdoor map made up of varied environments. At the beginning of the game, Link is equipped only with a small shield. He can then enter a cave, where an old man gives him a sword and warns him, “It’s dangerous to go alone!”

Within the official Zelda chronology, The Legend of Zelda is set in an era called “The Era of Decline,” which belongs to the “Defeated Hero” timeline. This timeline connects to an alternate reality in which the Hero of Time is defeated by Ganondorf in Ocarina of Time. In this era, Hyrule has shrunk into a small kingdom, and its residents now live in caves, forming the setting for the game.

The story begins with Princess Zelda splitting the Triforce of Wisdom into eight fragments and hiding them in secret underground dungeons in an effort to stop Ganon from obtaining it.
