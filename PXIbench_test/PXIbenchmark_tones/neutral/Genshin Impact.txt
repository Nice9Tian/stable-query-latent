Genshin Impact is a 2020 action role-playing game developed and published by miHoYo. It is an open-world title in which players control a party of four characters and can switch between them quickly during combat. Character progression is based on several enhancement systems, including gaining experience points and upgrading artifacts and weapons.

The game is set in Teyvat, a world made up of seven major nations. Each nation is ruled by an Archon, a god associated with a particular element and ideal. Above Teyvat is the floating island of Celestia, which is described as the home of the gods and of mortals who have ascended to godhood. Beneath the surface are the ruins of Khaenri'ah, a mysterious nation that was destroyed by Celestia 500 years ago.
