Among Us is a 2018 online multiplayer social deduction game developed and published by Innersloth, and its basic premise is simple in a way that can feel almost bare. The game supports cross-platform play and was first released on iOS and Android in June 2018, then later on Windows in November of the same year. It is designed for four to fifteen players, with up to three players secretly assigned the role of Impostors each round depending on player count and the host’s settings.

The game’s structure leans heavily on hidden roles and deduction, but that formula can also make it feel repetitive rather than deep. As of 2026, it offers five playable maps: The Skeld, a spaceship; MIRA HQ, an office building; Polus, a planet base; The Airship, a setting taken from Innersloth’s Henry Stickmin series; and the Fungle, a mushroom jungle map. These locations provide variety on paper, but they still serve the same underlying loop of suspicion, deception, and elimination.

In 2026, Innersloth also announced a spinoff titled Among Us Story: On Guard during Summer Game Fest 2026. Unlike the main game, it is a single-player narrative adventure about a security guard trying to prove innocence by solving a murder mystery. A demo was released during Steam Next Fest from June 15 to 22. Even so, the announcement mainly highlights how much the franchise is moving away from the original game’s limited social-deduction structure.
