NBA is a basketball video game series that consists of six titles developed by San Diego Studio and published by Sony Computer Entertainment. The series is officially licensed by the National Basketball Association, but that legitimacy does little to distinguish it from the crowded field of NBA-focused basketball games.

One of its oddities is the marketing approach: instead of centering on a single cover athlete, the game features a group of six NBA players—Carmelo Anthony, Carlos Boozer, Kobe Bryant, LeBron James, Paul Pierce, and Dwyane Wade. Carlesimo also appeared in commercials alongside three fictitious NBA players, which only adds to the series’ awkward and somewhat gimmicky presentation.

Unlike most NBA games, this one includes a story, but that unusual addition does not necessarily make it more compelling. Rather than relying purely on basketball gameplay, the series tries to stand out through narrative elements and promotional quirks, yet it remains an unremarkable entry in the broader landscape of NBA video games.
