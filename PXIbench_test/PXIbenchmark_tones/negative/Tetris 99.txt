Tetris 99 is a 2019 online multiplayer adaptation of Tetris developed by Arika and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch, but its appeal is mostly limited to the sheer novelty of turning a classic puzzle game into a battle royale. Up to 99 players are thrown into the same match at once, all trying to be the last one standing, which sounds energetic on paper but often just turns the familiar Tetris loop into a crowded and stressful scramble.

The gameplay remains rooted in traditional Tetris: players move and drop tetrominoes onto a board, rotate the pieces, and try to clear rows by filling them completely. Lines disappear when completed, and failure comes when the stack overflows past the top of the board. That core structure is intact, but the battle royale format adds little beyond pressure, making the experience feel more like a competitive survival exercise than a meaningful evolution of the formula.

There is no fixed plot or story premise, which is not surprising given the game’s abstract nature, but it also means Tetris 99 relies entirely on its mode gimmick to hold attention. In the end, it is still just Tetris, only noisier, more ruthless, and less relaxed than the classic version.
