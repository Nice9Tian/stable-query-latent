*Fallout* is a 1997 role-playing game from Interplay Productions that places its player in a bleak mid-22nd century post-apocalyptic world, long after nuclear war has reduced civilization to ruins. The premise is straightforward and grim: the protagonist, known as the Vault Dweller, lives in Vault 13, an underground shelter whose water system is failing because the Water Chip has stopped working. With only 150 days before the vault’s water supply is exhausted, the Vault Overseer sends the player out into the wasteland to find a replacement.

What follows is a typical survival quest wrapped in a desolate retro-futuristic setting. The Vault Dweller begins with little more than the PIPBoy 2000 and basic equipment, then searches the surrounding wastes for help. The supposed first lead, Vault 15, turns out to be abandoned and ruined, immediately undercutting any hope of a simple solution. Along the way, the player talks to other survivors, some of whom offer quests, and fights in turn-based combat.

Even though the game was praised for its open-ended structure, character system, plot, and setting, the actual experience is built on desperation and scavenging. Its world is defined by scarcity, its mission by time pressure, and its gameplay by repeatedly negotiating with strangers or battling through the wreckage of a civilization that is already gone.
