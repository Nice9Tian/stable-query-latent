Lunara is a free-to-play online collectible card game available on iOS, Android, Xbox One, PCs through Steam, and Nintendo Switch. Matches pit two opponents against each other in either player-versus-player or player-versus-computer formats, with each side building a deck from a variable number of cards, including troopers, bindings, energies, and incantations. The aim is to reduce the opposing health pool from 25 to zero or to leave the other player without any cards to draw.

The game includes a free introductory campaign called Aether Veil, which launched on November 19, 2016 and serves as a tutorial for new players. In this mode, players use preconstructed decks from each clan to progress through five stories, with four matches in each story. Winning a match rewards new cards, which are added to the player’s deck.
