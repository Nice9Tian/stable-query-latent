This action-adventure game is presented from a third-person perspective and places a strong emphasis on direct combat and progression through equipment and abilities rather than on traditional role-playing systems. Compared with the studio’s earlier fantasy action series, it offers fewer RPG elements: there is no character creation, no broad stat-leveling system, and no real-time multiplayer. Instead, advancement comes through gear upgrading, a skill tree, a set of special techniques tied to a prosthetic arm, and a limited form of build customization.

The story is set during the late Sengoku-like era, when the warband of the iron-clad lord Kharen seizes a territory said to be blessed with divine favor. Within this land lies a mysterious foreign deity, the Astral Wyrm, whose power is linked to immortality through a sacred bloodline known as the Emberline, as well as through the healing Verdant Springs and their sediment.

At the center of the narrative is a nameless orphan taken in by a wandering shinobi called Rook. Rook renames the child Wren and trains him in the shinobi way. As Wren matures, Rook gives him the responsibility of protecting a young noble named Calen, the last living heir of the Emberline.
