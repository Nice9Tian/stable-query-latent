**Moonveil** is a 2017 survival-horror title developed and published by the same studio. It draws heavily on Thai folklore, giving its atmosphere a distinctly local and supernatural edge. Rather than relying on constant combat, the game emphasizes vulnerability, tension, and careful movement through haunted spaces.

A central part of the experience is stealth. The player must hide from hostile spirits and avoid being discovered. If a ghost or demon finds the player in a hiding place, there is still a chance to escape through a quick-time event; failure usually results in death and a return to the last save point. This creates a steady pressure in which concealment and timing matter as much as observation.

Exploration also plays an important role. Many areas contain no immediate threats, allowing the player to move through the environment and uncover the story at a measured pace. The main gameplay loop focuses on evading supernatural pursuers while following the journey of the protagonist, **Tarin**. The developers later noted that the story was added to make the game more compelling, suggesting that narrative became an important part of the overall design.

Several characters and ideas in **Moonveil** were inspired by real-world references. One example was based on an actual bathroom shrine at a Thai institute, showing how the game blends folklore, local legend, and concrete locations into its horror setting.
