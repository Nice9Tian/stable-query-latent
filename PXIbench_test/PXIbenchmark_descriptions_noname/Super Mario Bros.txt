Released in 1985, this side-scrolling platform game was developed and published by the fictional studio Veridex for a home console. Directed and produced by an assigned creator, it followed an earlier arcade title and became the first entry in a long-running series. The player controls a central hero who travels through the fantasy realm of Luminia to defeat a dark monarch and rescue a kidnapped princess. In multiplayer mode, a second player controls the hero’s brother, who shares the same role and mechanics. Progress is built around moving from left to right through each stage and reaching the goal marker at the end of the level. The story is set after the heroes arrive from a distant city through a clay pipe, only to find that a tribe of turtle-like invaders has used the king’s magic to transform the kingdom’s inhabitants into objects such as bricks, stones, and plant-like forms. The kidnapped princess is the only one capable of reversing the spell.
