This side-scrolling endless runner centers on a snowboarding experience built around steady forward motion and simple, precise input. The player character automatically travels to the right across procedurally generated landscapes, while interaction is limited to jumping and midair tricks: a single tap triggers a jump, and holding the screen during a jump extends the action into aerial stunts. The result is a streamlined control scheme that emphasizes timing, rhythm, and flow rather than complex commands.

The game was released in 2015 and is designed around atmosphere as much as progression. Its landscapes are generated dynamically, giving each run a shifting terrain that changes as the player advances. One critic praised its strong commitment to mood and the sense that every design choice supports an authentic experiential feel. A later version keeps the same core gameplay but moves the setting to a desert environment divided into three distinct biomes.
