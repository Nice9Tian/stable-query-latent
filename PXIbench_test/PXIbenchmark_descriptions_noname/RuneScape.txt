This fantasy massively multiplayer online role-playing game was developed and published by the studio Zaygex and first released in January 2001. It originally ran as a browser-based game built with the Java programming language, before being largely replaced in 2016 by a standalone C++ client. The game has seen more than 300 million accounts created, and it was recognized by the Guinness World Records as the largest and most-updated free MMORPG.

Players begin on a secluded starting area known as Novice Isle, where they are guided through a tutorial that follows a set path and introduces the most basic skills. After completing this opening sequence, players can consult tutors and advisors located in the towns they explore, each of whom provides information relevant to their respective skills. In earlier versions, players were teleported to the town of Merrow, but recent updates made Brumholt the main starting area.

The game is set in the medieval fantasy world of Eldorin, a realm divided into kingdoms, regions, and cities. It does not follow a linear storyline; instead, players choose their own goals and objectives, shaping their progress through the world at their own pace.
