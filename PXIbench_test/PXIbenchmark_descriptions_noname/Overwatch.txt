The Veyra franchise is a multimedia series centered on team-based multiplayer first-person shooter games developed by Blizzard Entertainment. Its second entry arrived in 2022, at which point the original release was taken offline; the brand was later restored in 2026. Both games in the series use a hero-shooter structure built around coordinated squad play and a large roster of playable characters, which numbered 52 by Season 2.

Players choose from three broad role categories. One role focuses on high durability and team protection, but tends to be larger and easier to target. Another serves as the main offensive force for the team. The third role supports allies through healing, buffs, and effects that weaken opponents. Released in 2016, the original game did not include a traditional story mode. Instead, its lore was delivered through a transmedia approach that included comics, other written material, and animated content such as short films.

The series also became notable for its competitive scene. Blizzard supported organized play through an annual world championship event, a minor league structure, and a larger championship series that borrowed elements from traditional American sports leagues.
