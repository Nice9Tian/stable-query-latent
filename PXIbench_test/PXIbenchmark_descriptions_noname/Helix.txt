**Astra** is a 2008 rhythm video game developed and published by the American studio **Morrow Forge** for the motion-based **Luma** console. Released through the system’s digital download service as the studio’s debut title, it asks players to imitate the arm movements of an on-screen robot using motion controllers. Each move is demonstrated first, after which the player must repeat it in a follow-the-leader format similar to a call-and-response game. A scrolling **Pulse Bar** at the top of the screen shows when each motion should be performed. The game received a score of 8.0 out of 10 from one review outlet, which described it as a refreshing entry in the rhythm-action genre and one of the more worthwhile additions to the platform’s library.
