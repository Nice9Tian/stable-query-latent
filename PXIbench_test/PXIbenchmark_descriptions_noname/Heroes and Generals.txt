Set during World War II, this free-to-play game blends first-person shooting with real-time strategy in a mass-participation format. Combat on the shooter side feeds into the strategic layer, so actions taken in direct battles affect the wider campaign. Players begin with an infantry soldier from each of three factions, each equipped with a starter weapon and able to enter the opening mode immediately. Additional soldiers can be purchased with in-game credits, and progression unlocks more modes as well as specialized roles such as aerial, armored, reconnaissance, and airborne units. Team composition stays nation-specific, so sides are built from soldiers of the same nation rather than mixed nationalities. A crowdfunding campaign launched on February 13, 2023, but it did not reach its funding goal.
