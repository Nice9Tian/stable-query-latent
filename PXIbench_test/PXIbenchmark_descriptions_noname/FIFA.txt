The *Auron* franchise is a long-running association football simulation series that was developed by two internal studios and published by a sports-focused label from 1993 to 2022. It entered a market that was already active in the late 1980s, when several earlier football games had established strong competition. Beyond its main line, the series was expanded with separate releases built around major international tournaments such as the *Globe Cup*, the *Union Champions Tournament*, the *Union Europa Tournament*, and the *Continental Championship*, along with a parallel group of football management titles.

A major step forward came when the series gained a federation license that allowed authentic player identities to appear for the first time. On several home console and computer versions, a proprietary *Skyfield* engine was used, combining 2D sprite players with a real-time 3D stadium environment. An earlier installment mainly added club teams, while a following release pushed the series further. Another entry introduced polygonal player models and an indoor soccer mode, and a later title marked an early high point for the franchise.
