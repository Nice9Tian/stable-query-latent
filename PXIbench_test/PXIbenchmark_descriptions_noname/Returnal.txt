This is a 2021 third-person shooter that blends roguelike structure with psychological horror, originally released for a console platform and later ported to Windows in 2023. Set in a futuristic science-fiction world, it places the player in control of a space pilot named Veyra, who wears a specialized suit and uses advanced weapons while stranded on the alien planet of Nareth. The story centers on a forbidden mission to investigate a mysterious signal known as the "Ivory Echo," which feels oddly familiar to her.

After her ship, the Asteris, crashes and suffers severe damage, Veyra is unable to contact her home organization and begins exploring the planet on her own. The game emphasizes repeated cycles of death and resurrection: each time she dies, she returns to continue the descent through hostile and unfamiliar environments. As she moves deeper into the changing world, she battles extraterrestrial entities, uncovers growing visions, and encounters the unsettling sight of her own corpse, reinforcing the sense of isolation and disorientation that defines the experience.
