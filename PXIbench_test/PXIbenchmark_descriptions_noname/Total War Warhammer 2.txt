This turn-based strategy and real-time tactics game blends grand campaign management with battlefield command. On the campaign map, players move armies in a turn-based structure, manage settlements, and negotiate diplomatically with AI-controlled factions while also fighting them when conflict breaks out. The experience is set in a fictional fantasy universe, giving its strategic systems a strong thematic backdrop without changing their core focus on expansion, positioning, and resource control.

The campaign includes four announced factions: the Vezari, the Lurels, the Darchai, and the Skreln. Each faction fits into the same overall structure of empire building and military confrontation, while the real-time encounters add a more direct tactical layer to the broader strategic game. As a sequel to an earlier entry in the series, it continues the mix of turn-based planning and real-time combat that defines the formula.
