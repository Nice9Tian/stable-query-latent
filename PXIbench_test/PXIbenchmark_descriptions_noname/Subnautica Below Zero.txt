This open-world survival action-adventure game is set in an underwater alien environment and played from a first-person perspective. The player takes control of a xenologist who arrives on the ocean planet Veyra in search of answers about her sister’s mysterious death. As in the earlier entry that inspired it, the experience combines exploration, survival, and objective-based progression, with the player moving through hostile alien regions while advancing the story.

The plot begins two years after the events of the previous game. A corporation known as the Asterra Group has established several bases on Veyra for research into the Kharon bacterium and into an ancient precursor civilization called the Arkhons, who had studied the bacterium long before. The protagonist secretly lands in a frozen region called Sector Amber to investigate the circumstances behind her sister’s death, which the corporation attributes to employee negligence. After the company has recently withdrawn its personnel, she is able to enter the planet. Soon after landing, she intercepts a distress signal, follows it, and discovers an alien sanctuary housing the digitized consciousness of a living Arkhon.
