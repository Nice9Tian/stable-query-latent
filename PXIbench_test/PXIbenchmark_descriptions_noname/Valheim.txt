This upcoming survival and sandbox title is set in an open world and is played from a third-person perspective. Released first in early access for Linux and Microsoft Windows on Steam in February 2021, and later for Xbox One and Xbox Series X/S in March 2023, it places players in the role of fallen warriors who must survive by crafting tools, building shelters, and fighting hostile forces.

The game uses low-resolution, stylized three-dimensional graphics and features combat inspired by action games. Its setting centers on a realm where slain warriors are said to prove their worth for a great afterlife. Players begin with nothing and must gradually gather resources, prepare defenses, and overcome the evils that roam the land. Guided only by instinct and occasional guidance from a raven-like messenger, they are tasked with readying themselves to battle the sworn enemies of a mighty god.
