**Spirefall** is a 2019 roguelike deck-building game developed by the American indie studio **Morrow Forge**. It was published by **Morrow Forge** for its initial PC release and by **Lantern Parcel** for home consoles and mobile platforms. The game follows a run-based structure in which the player chooses one of four preset heroes at the start of each playthrough. Each hero begins with a set amount of health and gold, a unique starting talisman that grants a special ability, and an opening deck made mostly of basic attack and defense cards. Each also has hero-specific cards that are available from the start and can be added throughout a run.

Combat uses a collectible card game-style system, with new cards awarded through battles and other sources. Progress therefore depends on building an effective deck over the course of the climb, using deck-building strategy to adapt to changing encounters. A crowdfunding campaign for a related tabletop version launched on November 2, 2022, reached its funding goal in just 6 minutes, raised US$1.4 million on its first day, and finished with nearly US$4 million in total pledges.
