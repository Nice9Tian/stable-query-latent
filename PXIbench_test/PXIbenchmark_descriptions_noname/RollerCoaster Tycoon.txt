This 1999 construction and management simulation game was developed by Chris Sawyer and published by Hasbro Interactive. Released first for Windows and later ported to the Xbox by Frontier Developments in 2003, it places players in the role of a theme park entrepreneur tasked with completing a series of preset scenarios by building and maintaining successful amusement parks.

The gameplay centers on designing parks, selecting from dozens of roller coaster types, and adding a wide range of attractions such as log flumes, carousels, bumper cars, haunted houses, go-karts, Ferris wheels, and swinging ships. Management is a major part of the experience: players can hire staff to sweep paths, empty trash cans, water flowers, and mow lawns; bring in mechanics to inspect and repair rides; assign security guards to reduce vandalism; and use entertainers to keep guests engaged.

The game also includes scenario-specific content, including one case inspired by a real amusement park and featuring its signature coaster under a different fictional name. Its success led to multiple sequels and expansion packs, establishing it as the first entry in a long-running series of park management titles.
