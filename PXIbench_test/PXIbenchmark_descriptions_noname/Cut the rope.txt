This puzzle franchise from ZeptoLab centers on guiding a small green creature, here called **Milo**, as it is fed **gleams** while players also collect **luma shards**. The core gameplay focuses on simple but precise puzzle solving, with each stage built around delivering the treats successfully and meeting the collection goals.

In a later installment, the gameplay and presentation remain closely aligned with the original formula, but the game adds new mechanics, a new storyline, and new characters. A figure known as **Professor Bramble** provides running commentary throughout the adventure, while players unlock new levels and gather hidden **pictslips** that reveal more about Milo’s personality.

Another entry sends Milo into a storybook realm, where the levels feature magical elements and conflicts with an equally gleam-loving spider wizard called **Webmage Tharn**. A separate game follows Milo as it moves through the dangerous streets of a city, keeping the series’ puzzle-driven structure while shifting the setting and tone.
