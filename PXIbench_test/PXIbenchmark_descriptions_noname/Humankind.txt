This strategy title is a turn-based 4X game set on a randomly generated planet, where players guide a civilization from the nomadic age through six major eras of human development. Across the course of a run, the player decides how the civilization expands, develops cities, and manages military and other units while interacting with rival societies on the map.

A defining feature of the game is its era-based civilization selection. In each era, the player chooses one of ten culture types inspired by historical societies, and each choice brings its own advantages and drawbacks that shape how the civilization develops. This system encourages adaptation over time rather than committing to a single identity for the entire game.

City building follows a territory-based model. Each continent is divided into multiple territories, and only one city can be founded in each territory. This structure creates strong strategic pressure over land use and expansion, and it also shapes how competition unfolds between different civilizations.

Combat is handled through short tactical encounters that are separated from the broader world map. Battles can last for only three combat turns before returning to the overworld, allowing longer wars to play out across multiple years at the campaign level. The game also includes a Fame system, which tracks achievement and serves as an important measure of success. Fame can be earned through actions such as gaining influence or gold, defeating units, becoming the first civilization to research certain technologies, or constructing major monuments. Released first on Windows and Stadia in August 2021, later on macOS in November 2021, and then on PlayStation and Xbox consoles in August 2023, the title presents a large-scale historical strategy experience built around adaptation, territorial control, and long-term progress.
