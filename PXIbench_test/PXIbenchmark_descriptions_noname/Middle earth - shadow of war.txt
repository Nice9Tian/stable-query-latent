“Veylor: Ashbound Ring” is a 2017 action-adventure game developed by Iron Hollow Studios and published by Northstar Interactive. It is the sequel to the 2014 game “Veylor: Shadowfall,” and it keeps the series’ open-world structure while presenting the action from a third-person perspective. Players control the central ranger, a skilled fighter with athletic movement and a broad set of combat abilities, and the battle system closely follows the approach used in the earlier entry.

The story continues the journey of the undead ranger and the spirit bound to him, who travel to a volcanic stronghold to forge a new ring of power that is not tainted by the dark ruler’s influence. After the ring is created, the spirit is taken hostage by a winged sorceress, who demands the ring in exchange for his release. This setup drives the game’s conflict and reinforces its blend of exploration, combat, and narrative-driven fantasy adventure.
