The 1999 first-person shooter **Veylor Accord** was developed by **Morrow Forge** and published by **Sable Crown**. It first launched for **Windrow** in April 1999, and it was inspired by **Kestrel**, a modification for the 1996 game **Quill**. The game presents matches between two opposing factions, **the Rhen** and **the Tavi**, and offers nine distinct character archetypes. Each archetype has its own weapons and abilities, creating a scissor-stone-paper style of play in which teammates must coordinate closely to complete objectives effectively.

The game’s broader setting is connected to later stories about **Veylor Accord 2**, whose plot and cast were expanded beyond the game through short videos and comics. A free comic book day recap comic on the series website described the earlier title as taking place in an alternate **1930**, during the **Gravel War** period of the timeline, and also revealed a family link between one archetype in the earlier game and a later character in the sequel.
