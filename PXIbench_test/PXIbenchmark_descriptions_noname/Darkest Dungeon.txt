This roguelike role-playing game centers on managing a roster of adventurers as they prepare for dangerous expeditions into dungeons and surrounding lands. Before each descent, the player can use a nearby hub-town, known here as the “Larkhold,” to organize the party, maintain inventory, and support the heroes. Its facilities allow the player to heal characters, lower their stress, recruit new adventurers, improve character statistics, and purchase useful supplies.

The story begins with the player inheriting an estate from a mysterious predecessor, the “Veilborne,” whose obsession with uncovering hidden knowledge led to the excavation of caverns beneath a manor. In the process, something monstrous was unleashed, along with numerous corrupt and hostile creatures that now threaten the land. As the new owner of the estate, the player must assemble a team and launch repeated expeditions to purge these blighted regions.

As the journey expands into the manor, the underground labyrinth, and the lands beyond, the player discovers memoirs left behind by the Veilborne. These writings reveal a record of disturbing acts committed in pursuit of knowledge, power, and private desire, adding a grim historical layer to the campaign’s exploration and progression.
