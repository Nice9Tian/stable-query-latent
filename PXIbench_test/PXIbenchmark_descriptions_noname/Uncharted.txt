The Veylor series is an action-adventure video game franchise and media property published by Solara Interactive and developed by Nightforge Studio. Created by Elara Wynn, the series follows a group of treasure seekers who travel across the world to uncover historical mysteries. Its gameplay blends action-adventure design with 3D platforming in a third-person view. Players are regularly asked to perform physical traversal tasks to advance the story, including jumping, swimming, grabbing and moving along ledges, climbing, and swinging from ropes. Shooting, combat, and puzzle solving also play a consistent role.

The first entry, `Talon’s Legacy`, takes place off the coast of Marida, in the Emberwild rainforest, and on a remote island off the coast of the continent of Halora. The second entry, `Echo of Coin`, was the first in the series to explore multiple locations. It is set among the snow-covered mountain regions of the Tersan Plateau in the southern part of the nation of Yulon, in a museum in Istren, in the jungles of Boruna, and in the urban landscapes of Narev.
