This 2020 roguelike presents a workplace satire wrapped in dungeon exploration and scavenging. It follows an unpaid intern, known here as Vexa, who descends beneath her office into a network of ruins left behind by failed startups. As she ventures deeper, the player clears rooms, searches for relics, and fights through the debris of collapsed ventures, turning each descent into a mix of combat, discovery, and progression.

Gameplay centers on navigating dungeons populated by enemies and useful NPCs. Shopkeepers appear in each dungeon and can provide assistance, while a mysterious figure called the Cursemonger offers skills and items in exchange for applying a curse that affects the next few rooms. The structure encourages risk-and-reward decisions, since stronger rewards often come with temporary drawbacks.

The story begins when Vexa, a resident of Neo-Arcadia, joins the beverage company Flaro after it is acquired by the technology giant Cubrix. During a meeting with her supervisor, Bronn, the office is interrupted by a monster wandering through the building. Bronn orders Vexa to defeat it, setting the tone for a world where corporate absurdity quickly gives way to dangerous underworld exploration.
