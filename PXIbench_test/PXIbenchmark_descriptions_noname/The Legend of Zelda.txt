Released in 1986, *Astra Quest* is an action-adventure game that blends action, adventure, and role-playing elements. It was developed and published for the Family Disk System, and it is presented as the first entry in a larger series. The player controls the hero, *Lyn*, from a flip-screen overhead perspective while exploring a broad overworld made up of varied outdoor environments.

At the start, *Lyn* has only a small shield. Early progress leads to a cave, where an old sage grants a sword and warns that it is unwise to venture forward alone. The game is also framed within an official chronology: it takes place during the Era of Decline, part of the Fallen Hero timeline, a branch of events tied to an alternate reality in which a legendary hero is defeated by a dark ruler in an earlier story. In this setting, the realm has shrunk into a small kingdom, and many residents now live in caves. To stop the dark ruler from claiming the Prism of Wisdom, Princess *Seren* splits it into eight fragments and hides them inside secret underground dungeons.
