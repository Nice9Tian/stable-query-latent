The series known in Japan as **Nightbrand** and internationally as **Graveshade** is a Japanese horror game and media franchise created by a major game studio. It includes survival horror, third-person shooter, and first-person entries, and players are typically tasked with surviving in environments overrun by zombies and other mutated creatures. Throughout the series, the developers have used a wide variety of control schemes and gameplay systems, with puzzle-solving remaining one of its most prominent features.

One later installment, **Ironveil**, brought major changes to the established formula. It shifted the camera system from fixed perspectives to a tracking camera and moved the gameplay in a more action-oriented direction, while still retaining the franchise’s focus on survival and combat.

Development of the first game began in 1993, when a producer instructed a designer and other co-workers to create a new project using elements from an earlier 1989 game made by the same company. In late 1994, as marketing staff prepared for a U.S. release, they found that using the original title would likely be difficult because the name had already been registered by a DOS game and also belonged to a New York hardcore punk band. A company-wide naming contest was held, and the winning title became the name used for the game in the West.
