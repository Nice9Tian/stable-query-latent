This 2005 action-adventure release, developed and published by a major Japanese studio for a home console platform, is a third-person open-world game set in the city of Norikawa. It also uses a fixed-camera system and includes role-playing elements that let progression shape combat performance over time.

Players control a central protagonist, a hardened member of an organized crime group, as he moves through the city and fights enemies in close-quarters encounters. Experience earned in combat can be used to raise his attributes and strengthen his abilities, unlocking powerful special techniques that deal heavy damage during battles.

The story begins when the protagonist’s life is thrown into turmoil after his boss attempts to assault his childhood friend, Aeri. Soon afterward, the boss is killed by the protagonist’s best friend and fellow gang member, Rensho. The protagonist then takes responsibility for the murder, resulting in a ten-year prison sentence. During his imprisonment, he is removed from his organization, the Black Lotus Clan, and Aeri disappears.
