This 2016 free-to-play 3D science-fantasy action role-playing game follows a squad of up to three "Asterites" in fast-paced real-time battles. Players can switch freely between characters during combat and reposition across the battlefield as encounters unfold. Each Asterite has distinct attack, dodge, swap, and ultimate abilities, along with a defined type that shapes their role in battle.

The story is set on an alternate version of Earth devastated by repeated catastrophes caused by an entity known as the "Nullbrand." This force is portrayed as hostile and seemingly self-directed, with the ability to corrupt humans through mental manipulation and illness, transform them into undead beings, create a wide range of monsters, and grant certain individuals god-like power. These abilities allow it to drive large-scale apocalyptic events and keep the world in a state of ongoing crisis.
