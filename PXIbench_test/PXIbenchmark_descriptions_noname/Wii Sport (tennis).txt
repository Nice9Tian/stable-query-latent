This 2006 motion-controlled sports compilation was developed and published by the company Nivora for the Rii console. Released in North America alongside the console on 19 November 2006, it arrived in Japan, Australia, and Europe the following month. The package centered on five separate events accessible from a main menu: Aster, Bravio, Bolo, Golin, and Boxin. Each event used the console’s motion-sensing features to translate the player’s dominant arm and the equipment it represented into on-screen action.

Most of the events were controlled with a single motion wand, while Boxin required both the motion wand and the secondary grip to manage both of the player’s arms. The design emphasized simple physical gestures rather than complex button inputs, making the experience easy to understand and immediately approachable. At a major event on 14 September 2006, a representative confirmed the five playable events and announced that the title would be included free with every Rii console.

The result was a major commercial success, selling 82 million copies worldwide. That performance made it the best-selling game from its publisher, the fourth-best-selling video game of all time, and the best-selling title tied to a single console. It was also recognized as a broadly appealing multiplayer experience, with praise for being accessible to players of many ages, from young children to grandparents.
