Released in 2021, this life simulation title places the player on a secluded island and builds its experience around quiet, daily progress. The player creates a customizable character and spends time helping wandering spirits move on to the afterlife by completing tasks that vary from one character to another. Many of these spirits are represented as anthropomorphic bears, giving the island a gentle and unusual cast.

Gameplay centers on scavenging and crafting, with resources gathered and combined to support the island’s inhabitants and the player’s ongoing responsibilities. The world runs in real time, and there is no option to skip ahead or fast-forward, so progress unfolds at a natural pace. According to the project’s creator, the design draws inspiration from a familiar life-sim formula while placing greater emphasis on story.
