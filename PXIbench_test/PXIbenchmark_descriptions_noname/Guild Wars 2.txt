This free-to-play, massively multiplayer online role-playing game is set in the high-fantasy world of Lyrentha, where players step into a continent shaped by ancient conflict, shifting power, and the return of world-threatening ancient beings known as the Ember Titans. The main storyline follows the revival of a once-disbanded adventuring band, the Veilbound Circle, whose purpose is to resist these immense, corruption-spreading forces.

Character creation centers on a choice among five playable peoples: the aurians, the krann, the selk, the nyr, and the sylth. Players also select from nine distinct callings grouped into three armor tiers: scholars in light armor, wanderers in medium armor, and sentinels in heavy armor. Several of these callings return from an earlier era of the setting, while the sylth remain unique to this installment.

The game takes place 250 years after a previous age-defining victory over the Great Devourer, long after the continent entered a new period of upheaval. Since then, five dormant Titan entities beneath the land have awakened, causing widespread devastation and twisting the living world around them. Humanity, once the dominant force across Lyrentha, has fallen into decline as natural disasters and war have reshaped the balance of power, while the krann have reclaimed the last remnants of their ancestral territory from human control.
