This 2011 sandbox action-adventure game blends role-playing and platforming elements into a structure centered on exploration, building, crafting, combat, survival, and mining. It gives players no fixed objective; instead, they begin by creating a character, selecting a difficulty level, and entering a two-dimensional procedurally generated world where they gather resources, fight enemies, and craft equipment. The game supports both single-player and multiplayer play.

Over time, it has expanded through updates that added new items, non-player characters, enemies, and world difficulty options, along with various quality-of-life improvements and collaborations with other games. Its lack of a tutorial on PC has been a point of criticism, while the tutorial worlds included in several console and handheld versions were well received.
