Released in 1998, “Vortex Blade” is a fighting game developed and published by the studio “Aster Forge” for the “Lumen Station” console. It is the first entry in the “Vortex Blade” series, and it centers on one-on-one battles where the player chooses from ten fighters available at the start and must win two out of three rounds to advance.

Its combat system uses a six-button layout: four buttons handle attacks, including one punch, one kick, and two weapon strikes, while the remaining two are assigned to special actions. The game also includes instant-kill techniques that can end a match immediately if they connect successfully.

The story background begins in 2010, when humanity discovers an unlimited energy source of immense power, referred to as “ether.” Although this discovery offers a solution to the global energy crisis, warfare continues. After a long period of conflict, a group formed around a figure identified as “Orin R.”
