“Vesper Vale: The Final Ledger” is a 2016 action-adventure game presented from a third-person perspective. Players move through a range of environments through platforming, traveling across towns, buildings, and wilderness areas to progress through the story. Combat blends firearms, melee attacks, and stealth, allowing players to confront hostile opponents in several different ways.

Set years before the events of the first chapter in the series, the story follows two brothers, Taren and Sol, as they pursue the treasure of the legendary Marax. Joined by an associate named Rell, they infiltrate a prison in Panora to reach the former cell of Marax’s first mate. There, Taren uncovers a hollow relic, but when the prison warden who helped them demands a share, Rell kills him on impulse, forcing the group into a frantic escape.
