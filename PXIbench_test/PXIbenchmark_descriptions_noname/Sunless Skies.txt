This role-playing title was developed by the studio Brineworks, partly financed through a crowd-funding campaign, and entered early access in 2017 before its full release in January 2019. It is best described as a Gothic horror role-playing experience that emphasizes exploration and narrative. Played from a top-down perspective, it has the player command an upgradeable interstellar locomotive across a vast setting, where both short encounters and extended storylines can lead to either beneficial or harmful outcomes.

The story takes place in the same Victorian-inspired universe as the earlier works of Eldergate and Nightwake. It is set ten years after the events of Nightwake, after the subterranean city of Marrowgate opened a passage called the Veyr Threshold and reached the High Expanse, a region of outer space where stars are treated as godlike powers whose light carries influence, while unspeakable horrors wait in the darkness. In this era, the New Crown Empire has expanded to multiple stars and built its own artificial star, the Gear-Sun, while Queen Aurelia has taken the throne of a dead star and gained command over the nature of time itself.
