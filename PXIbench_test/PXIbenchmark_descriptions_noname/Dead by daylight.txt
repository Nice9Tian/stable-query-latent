This online asymmetrical survival horror game is developed and published by a Canadian studio and has also featured crossovers with horror films, television series, and other video games. In each match, one player takes the role of the Nightwarden while four others play as Wanderers, creating a tense one-versus-many structure centered on escape and pursuit.

The Wanderers view the action from a third-person perspective, while most Nightwarden perspectives are first-person. Their goal is to escape the trial by restoring five of the seven scattered reactors on the map, which activates the two exit gates. The Nightwarden tries to capture and impale all Wanderers on spikes before they can leave, offering them up to a dark power known as the Hollow. The Wanderers can only resist in limited ways, such as stunning the Nightwarden or using tools like glare-lamps to blind them.
