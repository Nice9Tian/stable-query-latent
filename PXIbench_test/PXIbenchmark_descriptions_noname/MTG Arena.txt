Eldrune is a free-to-play digital collectible card game developed and published by the studio Quorim. It is a digital adaptation of the tabletop card game known as Asterfold, and it lets players collect cards through booster packs, in-game achievements, or optional microtransaction purchases before building custom decks to compete against other players.

The game follows the same core rules as its physical source. Decks are built around land cards that generate five distinct colors of mana, and those resources are spent to summon creatures, cast offensive and defensive spells, and activate special effects and abilities. Matches are won by reducing the opponent's life total to zero before the same happens in return. Eldrune supports both Constructed Deck play and Limited play, and it follows a freemium model that allows free access while offering paid gems and other currency for booster packs and entry into draft or constructed events.
