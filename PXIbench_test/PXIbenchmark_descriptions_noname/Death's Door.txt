Released in 2021, this is a 3D isometric action-adventure title developed by the studio Aster Nerve and published by Ember Crown. It launched on Windows, the Koro Box line, and the X-Series consoles on July 20, 2021, and later arrived on the Switchleaf, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5 on November 23, 2021.

The game follows a small crow-like reaper named Vell, who works for the Ledger Hall, a bureaucratic afterlife office that assigns soul-collecting duties. In the opening mission, Vell is sent to retrieve the soul of a monster that refuses to die willingly. After the creature is defeated, an older crow named Oren intervenes, steals the soul, and warns Vell about a possible conspiracy tied to the disappearance of other crows.

Gameplay centers on melee and ranged combat. The player begins with a blade and a bow, with sword attacks chaining into combos and bow ammunition restored by using the sword.
