This 2020 paranormal horror title is a first-person investigation survival experience developed and published by a British indie studio based in Southampton. It launched in early access for Microsoft Windows in September 2020 and includes virtual reality support.

The game places players in a contract-based structure where they can work alone or in a group of up to four. Each contract requires the team to enter a specified site and determine what kind of haunting presence is active there. Communication is an important part of play: players can speak through voice chat both within a limited local range and over global walkie-talkies. Before heading out, players gather in a lobby where preparation for the contract takes place.
