**Verdant Vale** is a series of agriculture-simulation social network games developed and published by **Lunara** beginning in 2009. It is often compared to two earlier farming titles, **Harvest Haven** and **Field Market**, with which it shares a similar casual, community-based structure.

At the start of a new farm, the player creates a customizable avatar that can be changed at any time. The farm itself begins empty, along with a fixed amount of **Coinleaf**, the game’s primary currency. Players gain experience points by carrying out actions such as tilling land or purchasing items. The game is free to play, but faster progression is encouraged through the use of a premium currency, **Goldsprig**, which can be bought with real-world money. This premium currency can be used for convenience features such as restoring withered crops with an **revitael** item or triggering an **airglide** option that makes crops ready for harvest immediately. Across the series, the two main currencies—Coinleaf and Goldsprig, or in the later installment, **Plutobuck**—were available for purchase with real money from the publisher.
