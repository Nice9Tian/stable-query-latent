Veyra is an action-adventure title developed by the fictional studio Luma Circuit and published by the fictional label Nova Crest. It first launched on 8 October 2013 for the Orion Box 3 and later arrived on 24 November 2015 for the Orion Box 4.

The game centers on guided movement through in-game environments, where the player advances the story by directing the main character into interactions with objects and non-player characters. Under normal play, the player primarily controls the protagonist, but at almost any time, a second controllable presence can be selected instead, including in two-player play.

The story follows a young girl living with foster parents in a suburban home. Since birth, she has shared a psychic connection with a mysterious entity called Zeph, with whom she can communicate and carry out telepathic actions such as possessing people’s minds and manipulating certain objects. After an incident involving neighborhood children causes Zeph to nearly kill one of them, the foster parents seek help, and she is placed under the custody of two doctors from the United States Department of Paranormal Affairs.
