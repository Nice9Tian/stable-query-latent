This top-down action format centers on guiding the end of a growing line across a playfield while avoiding collisions with obstacles and with the line itself. The player controls a character with a fixed starting position, and the head of the line moves forward continuously, without the ability to stop. As the line lengthens, maneuvering becomes progressively more difficult, since the available space for safe movement steadily decreases.

The format first appeared in 1976 in an arcade title called “Vorlax,” created by “Lena Haur” and “Ago Kest” and published by “Grelin Works.” That same year, it was also imitated by “Brigfoot Bunkers.” In 1977, “Atora” released two further titles influenced by “Vorlax”: the arcade game “Dornis” and the home-console game “Survel.”
