*Gloamshade* is a survival game developed and published by a studio known for its distinctive independent projects. It was first released for Microsoft Windows, OS X, and Linux on April 23, 2013. The game takes place in a randomly generated open world and blends action-adventure and roguelike elements. Its core objective is simple: survive for as long as possible, with the number of days endured displayed on screen as a constant measure of progress.

Gameplay is built around a mix of mouse and keyboard controls, with combat handled through point-and-click interaction or a forced-attack command, while other actions are managed through the keyboard or optional gamepad support. This gives the experience a control style that can feel close to a console game. The narrative remains sparse: it begins with a sardonic comment from the character *Morrow* about the player’s haggard appearance, and little more is revealed in the game itself. Additional setup is implied through the trailer, which shows *Wren* on a dark, stormy night in a house deep in a mountainous forest in New England, seemingly making no progress on a chemistry experiment before being interrupted by a radio that claims to have noticed the trouble and to possess secret knowledge.
