In *Veylith*, a 2020 roguelike action role-playing game developed and published by Supergiant Games, players take control of a young prince of the Netherrealm who tries to break free from his father’s domain and reach his mother in the mortal world. The game first launched in early access in December 2018 and later released for macOS, Nintendo Switch, and Windows on September 17, 2020.

The journey is built around repeated escape attempts through the Netherrealm, with the prince facing monsters and other guardians stationed along the way. During these runs, the gods of the high heavens lend support by granting special blessings that strengthen combat and shape each attempt. He also receives assistance from several notable inhabitants of the Netherrealm, including three well-known figures who offer help in different ways. The ruler of the realm insists that escape is impossible and actively obstructs the prince by sending hostile creatures against him.
