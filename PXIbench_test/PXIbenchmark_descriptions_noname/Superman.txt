This 1999 action-adventure title for the N64 is an early 3D outing built around a hero from an animated television adaptation. In the game, the player controls the central hero in a third-person, three-dimensional platforming adventure set in a virtual version of the city of Metron. The story follows the villainous Lord Vex, who has trapped several key civilians — Lyra Vale, Jori Moss, and Professor Halden — inside this simulated city with the assistance of the entity Brannox, leaving the hero to rescue them and bring down the false world.

The single-player campaign presents a series of tasks and puzzles that the hero must complete after being challenged by Lord Vex. The city itself is filled with a mysterious zarnite fog that appears to weaken the hero, though this effect is actually a technical distance-fog solution used to conceal the game’s draw distance. The result is a game that combines action-adventure progression with platforming, puzzle-solving, and mission-based objectives.

According to its early design direction, the project was originally intended to pursue a more ambitious style of play, with a fully three-dimensional open world and real-time strategy elements. That concept aimed to let players behave more authentically like a superhero, even though the final game focused on a more constrained action-adventure structure.
