Released in 2015, this open-world action role-playing game was developed and published by Varda Forge. It serves as the third entry in the broader series and follows a 2011 predecessor. Played from a third-person perspective, it places the player in control of a seasoned monster hunter known as a Veilwalker.

The protagonist can walk, run, roll, dodge, and, for the first time in the series, also jump, climb, and swim. The story unfolds across the Contara, a fictional fantasy world inspired by Slavic folklore. The setting exists alongside parallel dimensions and otherworldly realms, where humans, elves, dwarves, monsters, and various other creatures live together. In the northern territories, non-human groups are often persecuted because of their differences.
