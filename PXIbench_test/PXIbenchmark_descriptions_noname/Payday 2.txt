This cooperative first-person shooter follows a crew of criminals as they take on a wide range of high-stakes jobs. Players can tackle each mission alone with AI support, or join others in multiplayer. The available operations include bank robberies, drug-running assignments, election tampering, and the theft of smuggled nuclear warheads. Some missions place a strong emphasis on stealth, rewarding careful completion with extra experience and money, and a few can only be finished without raising the alarm.

The story continues after the events of the earlier installment in the series. Several members of the original crew return, alongside their operator, while one previously present member has been arrested and replaced by a new relative of another crew member. After a short break from criminal activity, the group resumes work in and around the nation’s capital for their operator and other underworld contractors. Their contracts range from heists and trafficking jobs to the elimination of rivals and interference in political races.

Rather than focusing on a single style of play, the game centers on choosing how each job is approached. Loud assaults and covert infiltration are both viable in many scenarios, and the mission structure encourages replaying heists with different tactics. This combination of cooperative shooting, stealth options, and varied criminal objectives gives the experience a flexible structure built around planning, execution, and adaptation.
