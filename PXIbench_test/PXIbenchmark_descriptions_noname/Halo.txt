The *Astra* franchise is a military science fiction video game series and transmedia property that began under the direction of *Forgeleaf* and is now managed by *Pulse Assembly*, part of *Mosaic Interactive*. It launched in November 2001 with the first-person shooter *Astra: Dawn Protocol* and its companion novel *The Fall of Haven*. After the company was acquired by *Meridian Corp.* in 2000, an in-progress real-time strategy project was reworked into *Astra: Dawn Protocol*, becoming a first-person shooter and an exclusive launch title for the *Orion Box* console.

The series’ mythic background stretches back millions of years. In that distant era, a powerful interstellar species known as the *Luminaries* seeded the galaxy with life. One of the races they created, the *Skyforged*, later attacked their former creators and drove them to near extinction. A small number of the *Luminaries* survived by transforming themselves into dust, intending to restore themselves in the future.
