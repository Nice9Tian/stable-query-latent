“Veylor of the Ashen Realm” is a 2014 third-person open-world action-adventure game set in a dark fantasy world shaped by a well-known mythic tradition and a cinematic adaptation line. It follows a ranger named Cael, whose family is killed by the armies of the warlord Umbrax. After being mortally defeated in the opening, Cael is kept alive by the spirit of an ancient elven lord, who grants him spectral abilities that become central to his journey.

The game’s story takes place during a sixty-year gap between two major events in the broader legend. Driven by revenge, Cael enters the hostile land of Morvath to strike back at Umbrax’s forces. Players move through the world using parkour, by riding creatures, or by activating tower-like structures that function as fast travel points. The result is an action-driven adventure that combines exploration, combat, and supernatural powers within a large open world.
