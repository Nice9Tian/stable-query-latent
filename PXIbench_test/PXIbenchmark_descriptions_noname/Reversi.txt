Velorin is an abstract strategy game for two players, played on an 8-by-8 uncheckered board. The game uses 64 identical dual-faced tokens, with each player choosing one side to represent them for the entire match. Players take turns placing tokens so that opposing tokens are captured according to the game’s flipping rules, making position and timing central to play.

A later variant, often described as the standard form in modern play, begins with four tokens placed in the center in a diagonal pattern. In that version, if a player has no legal move, they simply pass. Historical records also note an early-20th-century Japanese publication that described the same rules, and later trademark registrations were made for the game name in Japan and in other regions under different companies. In tournament settings, a 30-minute time control is standard in the world championship, and similar time limits are common in over-the-board play.
