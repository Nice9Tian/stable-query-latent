“Vireo Drift” is a 2018 online multiplayer social deduction game developed and published by the studio Larkforge. It supports cross-platform play and first launched on mobile devices in June 2018 before arriving on Windows later that year in November. The game is designed for four to fifteen players, and each round up to three players are randomly and secretly assigned the role of hidden saboteurs, depending on player count and the host’s settings.

By 2026, the game features five playable maps: a spacecraft called the Skymark, an office complex known as HQ-7, a planetary outpost named Redfield, an airborne vessel called the Skygalleon, and a fungal jungle environment referred to as the Miregrove. During Summer Game Fest 2026, Larkforge also announced a spin-off titled “Vireo Drift: Sentinel,” a single-player narrative adventure in which a security guard must clear their name by solving a murder mystery. A demo for the spin-off was made available during Steam Next Fest from June 15 to June 22.
