Released in 2020 for Android and iOS, “Velmora” is a puzzle game and the first title from its developer. The player follows a character named “Mira” as she helps her grandmother, “Elda,” at an old “stonehold” that has fallen into disrepair. The story begins when Elda hands Mira a set of “latchkeys,” which turn out to open the gate to the property. From there, the player completes tasks to clean, repair, and renovate the surrounding areas, with the risk that the whole place could be condemned if it is not restored.

As progress continues and new areas are unlocked, the narrative gradually expands and reveals more family secrets tied to Elda and Mira. The game also gained attention for its promotional campaigns, which were described as weird, dramatic, plot-driven, and designed for virality. Those ads attracted notable online discussion, including attention from well-known YouTube creators.
