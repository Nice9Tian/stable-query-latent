Aster Vale is a first-person shooter and the third entry in a post-apocalyptic series inspired by a novel line. Set in the year 2035, it follows Eren, who leaves the tunnels of Varden and travels across the ruined lands of the former eastern federation and the Kazrin region. The story takes place one year after the events of the earlier chapter’s “Reclamation” ending.

The game unfolds over a single in-game year, with environments shifting as the seasons change. Across its journey, the player faces mutated creatures and hostile humans while moving through a world devastated by nuclear war. Much of the planet is no longer habitable because of radiation, forcing survivors into scattered communities and factions, some peaceful and some aggressive, that struggle against one another as well as against the dangerous beings that roam both the surface and the underground.
