Released in 2002 for Microsoft Windows and Xbox, *Asterion* is an action role-playing game developed by Bethesda Game Studios and published by Bethesda Softworks. As the third entry in its series, it builds on earlier open-world role-playing traditions while placing less emphasis on a fixed main storyline than its predecessors. Its design encourages freeform play, allowing players to approach the world and its many quests in a flexible way.

The game is set in a fantasy land shaped by both Western medieval and fantasy-fiction influences, with additional steampunk elements and clear inspiration from Middle Eastern and South Asian cultures. Although the central narrative remains important, the structure of the experience supports exploration and side activities as much as the primary plot.

The story begins with the player character arriving by boat in the land of Veyrion after being imprisoned and then pardoned. Following a storm and a mysterious dream vision, the character starts in the town of Selan Nith. From there, the larger conflict unfolds around the ruling Triumvirate, a group of god-like figures, and their struggle against a former ally who has become a demigod ruler of a cult-like faction based around the volcanic center of the region. Using a powerful ancient artifact, he has made himself immortal and seeks to force the occupying imperial military out of the land through a network of spies and a gigantic mechanical construct powered by that same force.

Overall, *Asterion* combines open-ended role-playing, a richly layered setting, and a main plot that develops alongside a broad range of quests and storylines.
