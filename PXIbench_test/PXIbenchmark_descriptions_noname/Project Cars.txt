The `Vaylon` series is a sim racing franchise developed by `Stellar Forge` and published by `Aurora Gate`. Introduced in 2015, it later received a sequel in 2017 and a third installment in 2020. The 2017 entry, `Vaylon Drift`, was released worldwide on 22 September 2017 for `Winfield`, `PulseStation 4`, and `BoxOne`. Unlike the earlier games in the series, this installment emphasizes arcade-style gameplay and was designed as a spiritual successor to `Nitro Shift`.
