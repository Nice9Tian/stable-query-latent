This free-to-play online first-person shooter, developed by the studio Quor, is set in a mythic science-fiction universe and built around a multiplayer shared-world structure. It blends first-person shooting with role-playing and MMO-inspired systems, creating an experience that combines solo progression with large-scale online interaction. Like its predecessor, it uses on-the-fly matchmaking, which initially limited direct communication to players grouped by the game’s matching system. Early communication outside those matches relied on each platform’s messaging tools, before an in-game text chat was added later.

Alongside standard story missions, the game offers cooperative PvE activities designed for different group sizes, including three-player strikes and dungeons, as well as six-player raids. Its design emphasizes exploration and public events, while also supporting broader multiplayer play. Reception highlighted several improvements, especially the opening story, along with its gameplay, visuals, exploration focus, multiplayer structure, and public event design.
