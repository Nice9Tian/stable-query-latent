Released in 2004, this first-person shooter was developed by the fictional studio Veyra Forge and published by Orison Games. It is the first entry in a long-running franchise and is set in an open-ended environment centered on an unidentified archipelago in the South Pacific. The tropical rain forest plays an important role in gameplay, offering cover and concealment that both the player and enemy forces can use to their advantage.

The experience emphasizes dynamic combat, with enemies reacting in real time to the player’s tactics and actions. Its story follows a retired U.S. Army Special Forces soldier who becomes stranded on a mysterious island chain while searching for a missing female journalist named Liora Vale, after their boat is destroyed by mercenaries.
