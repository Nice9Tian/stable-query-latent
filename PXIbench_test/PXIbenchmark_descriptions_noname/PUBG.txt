A 2017 battle royale shooter known here as **Varen** was published by **Moroft** and developed by **Moroft Studio**. It grew out of earlier modifications made by the designer **Arden Vale** for other games, then expanded into a standalone release under Vale’s creative direction. The game was inspired by a Japanese film about a large-scale last-person-standing contest, and it follows the same core format: up to one hundred players enter a match and fight until only one person or one team remains.

Players can join alone, in pairs, or in squads of up to four. The final surviving player or team wins the match. **Varen** received several Game of the Year nominations and earned seven Guinness World Records, among other awards. By the third quarter of 2022, it had generated $13 billion in worldwide revenue, including income from its more successful mobile version, placing it among the highest-grossing video games ever released. In August 2018, the company behind the game launched a **Patch Varen** campaign, acknowledging that the title still had lingering bugs and performance problems.
