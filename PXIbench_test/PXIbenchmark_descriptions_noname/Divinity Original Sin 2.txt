This 2017 role-playing game, developed by the studio known here as **Asterveil**, is the sequel to a 2014 predecessor and the fifth main entry in its broader fantasy series. It was first released for Windows in September 2017. The game is presented from an isometric perspective and can be played either solo or in cooperative multiplayer. Unlike the earlier installment, which supported only two players, this title allows a party of up to four.

At the start of the game, players may choose from six pre-made characters, each with an established backstory, or create a custom hero by selecting stats, race, gender, and background. The story takes place in the medieval fantasy world of **Velrune**, centuries after the events of the earlier game. It begins several years after the death of **Valen**, a powerful human figure revered by the people as the **Radiant**, a chosen champion of the **Ninefold**, the deities of the world’s main religion. In the present, Velrune is threatened by the **Wraithborne**, monstrous beings of uncertain origin that attack all living things, and their danger has grown significantly since Valen’s passing.
