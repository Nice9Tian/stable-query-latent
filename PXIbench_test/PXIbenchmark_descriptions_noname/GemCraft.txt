The **Veylcraft** series is a set of fantasy tower defense games developed by a Hungarian studio, with magical crystals serving as the main tools for both attack and protection. The first entry in the series, **Veylcraft: Ember Hollow**—originally released on June 26, 2008—introduced the core format that defines the franchise.

In **Veylcraft**, players receive crystals that can be combined and placed into defensive structures. A single resource, called mana, governs both survival and construction: it represents the player’s life and magical power at the same time. Mana is used to forge and merge crystals, as well as to build towers, trenches, and traps. The story takes place in a fantasy realm where wizards battle a powerful demon known as **the Umbra One**. According to the setting, wizards once summoned demons to serve them, but eventually called forth a being too powerful to control, one that could also dominate other demons.
