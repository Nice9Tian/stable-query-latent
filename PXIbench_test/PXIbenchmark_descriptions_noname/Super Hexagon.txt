“Vexa Spiral” is an action video game developed by an independent creator and accompanied by an original soundtrack from a separate composer. It first appeared on iOS in September 2012, followed by releases for Windows and macOS three months later.

The game centers on guiding a small geometric craft around the middle of a hexagonal arena while obstacles advance from the outer edges of the screen. Players must quickly pivot left or right to slip into the next opening and keep going. The main objective is to survive for as long as possible and improve on previous scores, with the run ending immediately and the level restarting after a single mistake. The source material does not identify any fixed story, plot, or narrative premise.
