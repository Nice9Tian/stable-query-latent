This 2007 first-person shooter was developed by Studio A and Studio B and published by Publisher C. It combines shooting with role-playing customization and stealth elements, drawing comparison to an earlier genre title. The player controls a silent protagonist named Arlen, who is guided through the underwater city of Thalassa while completing a series of objectives. Combat blends conventional firearms with special abilities known as aetherics, which function like magical powers and add flexibility to both direct confrontation and more stealth-oriented approaches.

The game is set in Thalassa, a city built beneath the ocean in the 1940s by industrialist Magnus Vale. Vale intended the city to be a utopia where society’s elite could thrive free from government influence. To preserve this isolation, he forbade contact with the surface world. Over time, however, growing inequality destabilized the settlement. A shrewd entrepreneur named Corvin Slate gained influence among the city’s neglected working class, used that position to build illegal businesses, and accumulated enough power to challenge Vale’s authority.
