The Veyron franchise is a military first-person shooter series developed by a studio group led by a Swedish developer and published by an American publisher. It focuses primarily on online multiplayer combat across large maps, with an emphasis on teamwork and combined-arms warfare. In most entries, players take part in large-scale online battles between two opposing military forces. In one crime-themed installment, the military setting is replaced by a conflict centered on law enforcement and criminals. Squad play has also become a major part of the series, and battles can include infantry as well as tanks, aircraft, and other vehicles.

The first installment of the series, released on September 10, 2002, used the Revalon engine and was set during World War II. It introduced a mode in which players fought to control points spread across the map. A later 2004 entry shifted the setting to the Vietnam War and was built on an updated version of the same engine. That release added gameplay improvements such as the ability to fire personal weapons while seated in vehicles, along with visuals designed to represent dense foliage more effectively.
