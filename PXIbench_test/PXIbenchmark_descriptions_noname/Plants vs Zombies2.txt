Virelume: Chrono Siege is a free-to-play tower defense game and a sequel in its series. The player defends the left side of the battlefield from waves of undead advancing from the right. To do this, the player places a variety of botanical units with different functions: some attack enemies directly, while others are designed to slow them down and make it easier to hold the line.

The page also describes a staggered mobile release. It notes an early launch in Australia and New Zealand on the iOS App Store on July 9, followed by a worldwide iOS release on August 15, 2013. For Android, the text lists a Google Play release on October 2, 2013, and also a worldwide Android release on October 23, 2013.
