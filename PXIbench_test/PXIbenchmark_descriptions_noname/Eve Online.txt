Aethergate Online is a space-based, persistent-world massively multiplayer online role-playing game developed and published by Fenris Creations. Set in a single shared universe, it centers on player-driven activity rather than scripted progression, allowing participants to take part in mining, piracy, manufacturing, trading, exploration, and combat against both the environment and other players.

Players begin by selecting an existing avatar or creating a new one, and each account can support up to three avatars. New avatars are created by choosing one of four playable lineages: Arken, Velora, Mintra, or Caldarix. The game is known for its unscripted economic competition, warfare, and political maneuvering, and one large-scale battle involving thousands of players in a single star system lasted 21 hours and was recognized as one of the largest and most expensive battles in gaming history.
