“Luma Strike” is a virtual reality rhythm game developed by Nova Beatworks and originally published by the same studio before it was later acquired by Orbit Studios in 2019. Players use VR controllers to guide a pair of glowing “glowblades,” which are shown in contrasting colors by default for the left and right hands, though those colors can be changed.

In each song, the game sends a continuous stream of approaching “pulse blocks” toward the player, arranged to match the song’s beats and notes within one of 12 positions on a 4-by-3 grid. Some blocks include directional arrows, indicating one of eight possible cuts that must be made through them. The experience takes place across a variety of surreal neon environments, with gameplay centered on slicing musical beats in rhythm using the pair of brightly colored blades.
