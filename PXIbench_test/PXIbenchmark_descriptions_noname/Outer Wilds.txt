This 2019 action-adventure game places the player in a small planetary system trapped in a 22-minute time loop. The player character, an unnamed space explorer referred to here as the Vell, investigates the system in a self-directed way, using each reset to uncover its secrets. Death always returns the Vell to the start, and the cycle also ends automatically when the star goes supernova and destroys the system. Over time, the player pieces together the history of the ancient Naru, an alien civilization whose ruins are scattered across the planets and whose legacy is tied to the mystery of why the star is exploding.

The setting is built around a set of worlds with strongly different identities. One planet resembles a wooded campsite with brown and green tones, while another features blue-green rocky shores. Each world also has its own soundscape, including a recurring song played by a member of the space exploration program on a distinct instrument that can be heard from anywhere in the system through the Soryn device. The system also includes a quantum moon that changes orbit when unobserved, an icy comet, and space stations orbiting the star and one of the larger planets, all left behind by the Naru long before the current events begin.
