This 2010 action-adventure title, developed by Varrow Studio and published by Cinder Crown Games, is the second entry in a Western-themed series and follows the 2004 predecessor that established its setting and tone. Played from a third-person perspective, it places the player in control of a former outlaw named Joren. The main structure centers on linear missions with clear objectives that advance the story, while a later epilogue shifts control to Joren’s son, Lio. Between missions, the player can freely explore a large open world made up of two fictional American states, Ash Ridge and Hollow Creek, along with the fictional Mexican state of San Vezaro.

The story begins in 1911, when Joren is forced by two bureau agents, Edden and Arlen, to track down members of his former gang in exchange for his family’s return. His first target is a former ally, Vess, who now leads his own gang and threatens the people of Ash Ridge. This setup combines open-world exploration with mission-based progression, giving the experience a mix of narrative-driven structure and player freedom across a frontier setting.
