Veldra Hunt is a 2004 action role-playing game developed and published by Brimforge for the Aster-2 console. The first entry in the Veldra Hunt series, it was originally released in one region in March 2004, in another in September 2004, and in a third in May 2005. The game places the player in the role of an aspiring ranger who must complete a variety of quests to earn glory.

Progress centers on crafting gear from the remains of defeated beasts. Armor, weapons, and other supplies are made by harvesting horns, scales, and bones, along with mining ores in the field. The game plays in a style similar to Starline Quest, allowing up to four players to team up online to bring down stronger beasts. In online play, the main objective is not to defeat the greatest number of beasts but to reach the highest ranger rank, following a storyline carried by non-player characters.

A sequel was released in Japan on February 16, 2006, and a portable version based on that sequel was released worldwide throughout 2007. On January 26, 2014, the fourth major entry was released in Japan, and on February 13, 2015, under a different subtitle, it was released worldwide.
