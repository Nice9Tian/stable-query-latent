This 1998 action-adventure stealth game for the PlayStation was developed and published by the studio **Varex**, with design, production, and writing led by **H. Kairo**. It is the third main entry in its series and the seventh storywise, serving as a sequel to an earlier MSX2 installment. Set in an alternate timeline where the Cold War continued into the 1990s and ended near the close of the century, the story takes place years after the previous chapter in a larger narrative centered on the covert operative **Vex**.

The player guides Vex through a nuclear weapons facility while avoiding detection. Stealth is the core of the experience: if the character enters an enemy’s line of sight, the game shifts into an alert state and hostile forces are drawn in. The player must then stay hidden until an evasion state begins, and once the countdown reaches zero, the game returns to infiltration mode, where enemies are no longer suspicious. In 2005, a renegade unit of genetically enhanced special forces seizes control of a remote island in Alaska’s **Frost Arc** region, known by the codename **Iron Hollow**, where a nuclear weapons disposal facility is located.
