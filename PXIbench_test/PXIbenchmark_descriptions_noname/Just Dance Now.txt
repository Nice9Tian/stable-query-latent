Rhythm Drift is a music game developed and published by Studio Luma, released on September 25, 2014, through the Aster Store and PlayMosaic. It is designed for players who may not have access to a console but still want to experience the style of the original entries in the Glimmer series. As in those games, players are asked to follow the choreography of an on-screen dancer in time with a chosen song. The app connects to the Rhythm Drift web service through a television, computer, laptop, tablet, BeamCast, or OrbView device. No fixed plot or story premise is presented.
