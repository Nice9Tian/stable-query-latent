This 2000 social simulation title was developed by the studio Varex and published by Orion Works. It lets players create and control virtual people, here referred to as “Lumes,” and guide their daily lives in a suburban environment. Structured as an agent-based artificial life program, the experience emphasizes simulation and ongoing management rather than a fixed objective. Its artificial intelligence is notably advanced for its time: the Lumes react to external conditions on their own, although player intervention is often needed to keep them on course.

The game is designed for effectively unlimited replay value, since there is no true win state and play can continue indefinitely. When an option is enabled, the Lumes have a degree of free will and can interact with their world autonomously, though most of these actions can be overridden by canceling them in the action queue at the top of the screen. Commercially, the title was highly successful, surpassing 41 million copies sold worldwide across the base game and its expansions by 2004, and an estimated 70 million copies worldwide in total, making it one of the best-selling PC games of all time.
