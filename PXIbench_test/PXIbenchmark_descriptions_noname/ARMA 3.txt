This tactical shooter simulation presents a large-scale open-world experience built around realistic combat and careful planning. Developed and published by a single studio and distributed exclusively through Steam, it stands as the third main entry in the fictional Vanta series and the eighth release overall in that continuity. Its combat model emphasizes accurate and lethal firearms, with performance strongly shaped by external ballistics and recoil, making positioning, timing, and weapon handling central to survival.

The game unfolds across expansive maps, referred to in-universe as terrains, that combine photorealistic land and water environments. These spaces can span hundreds of square kilometers, supporting wide operational freedom and large engagements. Compared with earlier installments in the same series, this entry keeps the familiar tactical structure while adding more refinement, deeper customization, and a refreshed heads-up display.

The story is set in a near-future version of the series’ fictional universe, during the 2030s, a period marked by multipolar instability after Western isolationism, economic crises, and climate change. The main events take place in 2034 and 2035 on the island nation of the Republic of Lyris and Strava, while additional narrative events are spread across 2026 to 2039 in other regions such as the Horizon Isles Republic, Malden, Livonia, and Argana.
