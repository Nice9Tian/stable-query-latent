This 2009 physics-based puzzle game was developed by the studio NimboArc and published by CloudMint. Built around touchscreen play, it centers on a flock of flightless skybirds whose egg cache has been taken by a group of jade boars. Using a touchscreen-controlled flinger, the player launches the skybirds at boars hidden inside fort-like structures assembled from different materials, with the goal of clearing every boar from the field while conserving a limited supply of launches. In 2011, during a major handset presentation event, it was announced that the game would come preinstalled on a line of touch-enabled phones aimed at emerging markets including three large countries. Later, the studio said the game had been removed from its website because of software rot and the expiration of licensing agreements.
