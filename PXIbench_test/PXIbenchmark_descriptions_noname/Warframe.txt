This free-to-play action role-playing third-person shooter blends shooter, RPG, and stealth elements into a multiplayer online experience. It was developed and published by a single studio, and support for cross-platform play was added in 2022. The player begins as a silent pseudo-protagonist inside an anthropomorphous biomechanical combat unit known as a Veyra, which has supernatural agility and access to special abilities. Combat is supported by a set of weapons, divided into primary, secondary, and melee categories, along with a spacefaring vessel called an Orbis.

The Orbis is aided by a Cephalon, an artificial intelligence formed from the minds of living people. The story takes place in a far-future version of the solar system, here called the Nadir System. At the start, the player controls members of a warrior group known as the Sura, who are awakened from a millennia-long cryosleep on Earth by a guiding figure named the Luma. As the narrative develops, the player learns that all of the factions encountered, including the Sura, were created by or split off from an older imperial power called the Auric Empire. This empire is revealed to have been an ancient fallen civilization and the former ruling authority of the Nadir System.
