Released in 2013, this grand strategy game places the player in command of a nation across the early modern era. Developed by the studio “Myradon Forge” and published by “Imperial Crest,” it launched on Windows, OS X, and Linux as a sequel to an earlier entry in the same strategy line. Its scale is centered on long-term statecraft, with players guiding a country through diplomacy, warfare, expansion, and economic planning over a broad historical period.

A major feature is its set of bookmarked starting points, known as scenarios, which present the world with borders, events, and conditions aligned to their historical counterparts. The earliest scenario begins on 11 November 1444, just after the Christian defeat at “Varnor,” while the latest starts on 21 September 1792, marking the end of the “Loravian” monarchy. Players may also choose any day within the game’s time span as a starting date. With more than 500 nations available, the game supports a wide range of strategies, including world conquest through military force, building a colonial superpower, or establishing trade dominance.
