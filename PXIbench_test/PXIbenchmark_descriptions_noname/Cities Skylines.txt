Varel is a 2015 single-player city-building simulation developed by Miro Forge and published by Aster Works. It offers an open-ended approach to urban planning, beginning with a small 2-by-2-kilometre plot of land, a highway interchange, access to water, and an initial budget. From there, the player expands by laying roads, designating residential, industrial, and commercial districts, and providing essential utilities such as power, water, and sewage in order to attract inhabitants and support employment.

As the settlement grows and population thresholds are reached, new systems become available for managing the city more effectively. These include educational, emergency, health, and waste services, along with tax controls, administrative policies, and mass transit options. When enough residents and funds have been accumulated, additional nearby land parcels can be purchased, eventually allowing expansion across eight extra plots within a 10-by-10-kilometre area. The game also includes several pre-made maps and a map editor that lets players create custom terrain, including landscapes based on real-world geographic features.
