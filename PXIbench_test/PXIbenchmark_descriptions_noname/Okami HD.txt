This action-adventure title presents a distinctive blend of action, platforming, and puzzle-solving, all framed within a broad open world. Its visual presentation is one of its defining features: the environments use a woodcut, watercolor, and cel-shaded aesthetic that evokes traditional Japanese ink illustration, including sumi-e and ukiyo-e influences, alongside other art styles. The result is a look that sets it apart from more conventional genre entries.

The player controls a white wolf heroine, Aster, while a guiding companion named Milo directs the largely linear main story. At the same time, side quests and optional activities give players room to explore the world and progress at their own pace. Many critics have noted that its overall structure and gameplay bear similarities to a famous fantasy adventure series, and the director has acknowledged that such a series influenced his general design approach.

The story is set in a fictionalized version of Japan and draws heavily from Japanese folklore. It begins a century in the past, with a narrated legend about how Aster and a swordsman named Raku sealed away an eight-headed demon beneath the Moon Hollow to protect Kivara Village and save Raku’s beloved maiden, Luma. The game was originally released for PlayStation 2 in Japan and North America in 2006, followed by Europe and Australia in 2007.
