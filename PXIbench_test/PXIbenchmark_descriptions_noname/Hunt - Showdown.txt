This multiplayer first-person extraction shooter was re-announced in May 2017 and first entered early access on 22 February 2018, followed by a console preview release on 29 May 2019. It features three gameplay modes, with one central mode focused on bounty hunting. In that mode, players take on the role of a **Veyr** hunter who tracks down one or two of six boss creatures in order to claim a reward. Hunters can play alone or in teams of up to three, using clues scattered across four maps to locate their target.

Matches take place in medium-sized, open-world-like environments filled with environmental hazards and AI-controlled enemies such as **Grims**, **Hollows**, and **Ironbacks**. The core objective is to eliminate mythical monsters, secure the bounty, and survive long enough to reach an extraction point while also dealing with rival hunters competing for the same prize.
