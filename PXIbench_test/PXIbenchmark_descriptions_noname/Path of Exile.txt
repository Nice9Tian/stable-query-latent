Released in 2013, this free-to-play action role-playing game places a single protagonist in an isometric adventure through a dark fantasy world. After an open beta period, it launched on Windows and sends players across broad outdoor regions, caves, and dungeons where they fight monsters, complete quests from non-player characters, and collect experience and equipment. Its structure emphasizes repeated exploration, with every area except the main encampments generated procedurally to support replayability.

The game is widely seen as drawing strong inspiration from the `Dreadfall` series, especially `Dreadfall II`, and is often described as a spiritual successor to that style of play. The story begins on the shores of `Veylan`, a cursed continent that once formed the heart of a powerful empire but later became a penal land for criminals and other exiles sent from the nearby island of `Orinth`. From the start, players must endure harsh wilderness, hostile creatures, and the crumbling ruins and buried secrets of the fallen `Aurelian` Empire and the earlier `Vaelor` civilization, often relying on cooperation with other exiles to survive.
