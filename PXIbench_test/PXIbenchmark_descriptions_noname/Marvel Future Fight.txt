This 2015 action role-playing game, developed and published by a Korean studio, lets players assemble a team of well-known heroes and villains from a fictional universe and send them into fast-paced battles. The gameplay centers on completing missions and quests across different storylines while fighting waves of enemies in real time. Players directly control character movement and trigger powerful attacks through touch controls. In one storyline, the collapse of several dimensions leads three figures to investigate the crisis, only to confront alternate versions of themselves before reporting back to their commander. At the same time, three defenders hold a central tower against an attack led by a mechanical enemy.
