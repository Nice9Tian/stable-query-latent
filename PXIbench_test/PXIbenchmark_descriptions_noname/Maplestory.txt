Aetherleaf is a free-to-play, 2D side-scrolling massively multiplayer online role-playing game developed by a South Korean studio and published by a major game company. Several regional versions are available, each handled by different publishers depending on the country or territory. The game uses a 2D scrolling platform structure, with controls handled through both the keyboard and mouse. Many keyboard functions can be rearranged to fit the player’s preferences, allowing for a flexible control setup.

Its story takes place across several distinct worlds, including the Verdant Realm, the Astral Current, and the Grand Horizon. These worlds were created by beings known as the Overseers, who assigned three guardians to watch over them: the Warden of Time, the Warden of Life, and the Warden of Light. The first major story arc centers on the main antagonist of the Verdant Realm, known as the Umbral Mage.
