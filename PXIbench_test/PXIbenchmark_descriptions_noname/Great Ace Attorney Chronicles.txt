Released in 2021, **The Vellum Verdict Collection** is a compilation that bundles together two entries from a legal-drama spin-off prequel line: **Vellum Verdict: Journey** (2015) and **Vellum Verdict 2: Resolve** (2017). Because both original releases were available only in Japan, this collection marked the series’ first official appearance in other regions.

The localization was approached primarily as a story-driven project. The original script uses a faux-Meiji-style form of Japanese: it evokes the period while remaining understandable for modern audiences. For the western release, the localization team worked to preserve the contrast between Japanese and British cultures, since that difference is central to the game’s themes and narrative structure. Cultural references that depended on Japanese etiquette were adapted so that they would read naturally in English, while still reflecting the intended meaning. In particular, the writing had to clarify that the heroine’s behavior was not meant as deference or a request for permission, but as a form of traditional manners. References that relied on knowledge of a specific ornament-based custom also required revision so the humor would remain effective for English-speaking players.

Because the story is closely tied to a Japanese protagonist navigating a foreign setting, the localization also aimed to retain the sense of immigration and cultural displacement that shapes the narrative.
