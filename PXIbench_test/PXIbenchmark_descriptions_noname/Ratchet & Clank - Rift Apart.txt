The 2021 title is a third-person shooter platform game developed by Insomniac Games and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment for the PlayStation 5. It is the ninth main entry in the series and follows the 2013 sequel in the storyline. Its design keeps the familiar structure of earlier entries, combining strafing, gunplay, bolt collection, automatic weapon and health upgrades, manual weapon enhancement through Raritanium, and gadget-based progression.

The story follows a hero and his robotic companion as they are celebrated as galactic saviors during a parade on the world of Corson V. A repaired Dimensionator, a device that can open rifts to other dimensions, is revealed as a means for the hero to search for the Lombax race and his missing family. The celebration is interrupted when Doctor Nefarious attacks the event and tries to steal the Dimensionator.
