Asterfall is a 2016 4X turn-based strategy title developed by the studio Ember Quill and published by North Harbor. It also received versions for mobile devices and a hybrid console through the publisher Skybridge.

In Asterfall, one or more players compete against computer-controlled rivals to guide a chosen realm from a small tribal settlement into a global power across several eras of development. The experience centers on the familiar 4X framework of exploring, expanding, exploiting, and exterminating, with progress measured through a range of victory conditions. These paths can include military dominance, technological advancement, or cultural influence over human and AI opponents.

Players shape their realm by researching new technologies and civic advancements, forming a government structure, and building an influential culture. They establish cities and strengthen them through mines, farms, and other improvements, while also training military forces for offense and defense. The world is randomly generated, encouraging exploration and contact with other realms and hostile barbarians. Trade and negotiation are also important, allowing players to interact with other leaders while balancing growth, conflict, and long-term strategic planning.
