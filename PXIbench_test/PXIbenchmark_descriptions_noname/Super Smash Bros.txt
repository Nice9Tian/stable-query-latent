“Vanta Clash” is a 1999 crossover fighting game developed by Mira Studio and published by Luma Works for the Zeta 64 console. It launched in Japan on January 21, 1999, followed by North America on April 26, 1999, and Europe on November 19, 1999. As the first entry in its series, it introduced a format that differs from standard fighting games: instead of draining an opponent’s life bar, players try to knock rival fighters off the stage. Each fighter has a damage total shown as a percentage, and that value rises as damage is taken.

The game became a commercial success, selling more than five million copies worldwide by 2001. Reported sales included 2.93 million units in the United States and 1.97 million units in Japan.
