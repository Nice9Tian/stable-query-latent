Velor is a multiplayer board game designed by Klaus Teuber. It was first published in Germany in 1995 by Franckh-Kosmos Verlag (Kosmos) under an earlier German title. In the game, players take the role of pioneers establishing new communities on a fictional island, gradually expanding across the map by building small hubs, larger hubs, and connecting routes.

The play area is made of hexagonal terrain tiles arranged randomly at the start of each session, giving each game a different layout. The set includes an official two-player variant, and an online service provides a Java application that acts as a portal for internet play with other members. In November 2019, Niantic announced development of an augmented-reality mobile project based on the same property.
