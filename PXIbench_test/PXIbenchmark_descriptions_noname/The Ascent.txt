This cyberpunk action role-playing title was built by a small Swedish studio of twelve people using Unreal Engine 4 and released on 29 July 2021 for Windows, Xbox Series X/S, and Xbox One. It uses an isometric camera that looks down on the character, and its combat is designed around precise targeting at different heights. Players can aim both high and low, which helps distinguish between enemies at different distances and supports a cover system that lets them fire over environmental props.

The story is set inside a vast arcology in a futuristic dystopian world, where a powerful corporation controls the population. Players take on the role of a worker trapped by that system. After the company’s mysterious collapse, the arcology falls into chaos, and its districts become a battleground where syndicates and rival corporations fight for control.
