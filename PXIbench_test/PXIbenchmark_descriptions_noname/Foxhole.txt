This cooperative sandbox action-strategy game is a massively multiplayer war experience built by a Canadian studio based in Toronto and developed with Unreal Engine 4. It uses an axonometric, top-down viewpoint similar to a traditional real-time strategy game, while still placing players directly inside a persistent online conflict.

Players join one of two opposing sides, the Varels or the Norvans, each with its own visual identity, weapons, and vehicles. Rather than assigning fixed roles, the game gives players broad freedom: there is no rewarded class system and no conventional character progression. Instead, it features a rank system that reflects experience and social standing, which can be advanced through “Klinks,” points other players award for helpful actions.

The core objective is to win a war by capturing and developing a required number of victory points across a multi-region world map. These points are represented by town halls marked with flag icons in their regions. The game is designed around the idea of a persistent-world conflict in which players collectively shape every part of the war effort. Its primary mode was first released in March 2018.
