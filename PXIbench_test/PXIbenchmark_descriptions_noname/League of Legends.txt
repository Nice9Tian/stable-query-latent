This multiplayer online battle arena was developed and published by a major studio after its founders drew inspiration from a custom map for an earlier strategy game and set out to build a standalone title in the same genre. In play, the player controls a single combatant, here called a “verdan,” from an isometric viewpoint, and each verdan has a distinct set of abilities.

Matches center on progression through combat. Verdans earn experience points by defeating enemies, which allows them to level up during a game. They can also buy equipment to become stronger, using a currency that accumulates passively over time and is also earned by destroying enemy drone units, opposing verdans, or defensive structures. The result is a match structure that combines character growth, tactical positioning, and objective-based advancement.

Before 2014, the fiction framed players as political leaders known as “stellars,” who directed verdans in battles across the “Fields of Accord,” including a prominent arena called the “Shardway.” The stated purpose of these conflicts was to prevent a wider catastrophe. One sociologist argued that the setting lacked the political complexity seen in other role-playing games and instead relied on a simplified good-versus-evil framework. In the game’s early development, the studio did not employ dedicated writers, and character backstories were written as brief paragraphs by the designers themselves.
