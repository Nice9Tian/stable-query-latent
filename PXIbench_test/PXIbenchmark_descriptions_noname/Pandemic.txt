This cooperative board game, first published in the United States in 2008, challenges players to work together from randomly assigned callings to halt four worldwide outbreaks before a collapse spreads too far. The experience is built around a world-map network of 48 connected cities, two card stacks, four colors of cubes with 24 cubes in each color, six Beacon hubs, and one pawn for each calling. One stack contains city-linked cards, special Glyph cards that can be played at almost any time to help the group, and Surge cards; the other tracks how the outbreaks spread.

Beyond the base game, the most prominent offshoot is a Chronicle line that spans three seasons and adds an ongoing storyline along with permanent changes to the play experience.
