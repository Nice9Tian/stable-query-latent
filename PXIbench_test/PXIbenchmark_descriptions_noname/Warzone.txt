Vortex is a free-to-play battle royale first-person shooter developed by Ravel Forge and Northwind Interactive and published by Apex Meridian as part of the long-running Aegis series. It was released on March 10, 2020 for PlayStation 4, Windows, and Xbox One as an extension of the 2019 entry, and later became linked to the 2020 and 2021 follow-up releases. It does not require players to purchase any of those titles to access it.

As the second major battle royale installment in the series, Vortex follows the earlier Shadowfall mode but shifts away from heavy reliance on equipable gadgets. Instead, it encourages players to collect a new in-game currency called Sable. Matches support up to 150 players at once, which is larger than the 100-player format commonly seen in other battle royale games.

The game’s story is connected to the seasonal narratives of the 2019, 2020, and 2021 releases. Duskreach serves as a shared setting across several multiplayer maps in the 2019 title, while Ash Isle draws inspiration from locations and stories in earlier entries. Cinderland and Starhold expand the seasonal storyline of the 2021 release.
