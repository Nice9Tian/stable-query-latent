The **Veyra** series is a collection of adventure games published through a major publisher’s external studio label. Developed by a French studio, the series first appeared with its self-titled opening entry, which was released in five episodes during 2015. Presented from a third-person perspective, the games focus on exploration, object interaction, and dialogue-driven progression.

Gameplay typically involves examining the environment, collecting or using items, and solving puzzles through fetch-style objectives and changes made to the surrounding world. Players move through different locations, search for clues, and speak with non-player characters to advance the story and uncover new tasks. The structure emphasizes narrative progression and environmental problem-solving rather than action-heavy mechanics.

The series emerged after the studio’s 2013 action-adventure project failed to meet both critical and commercial expectations. In response, the developer reorganized and adopted a new approach centered on smaller teams and shorter production cycles. According to the studio’s leadership, the shift was necessary after earlier ambitions for larger projects proved unsustainable.
