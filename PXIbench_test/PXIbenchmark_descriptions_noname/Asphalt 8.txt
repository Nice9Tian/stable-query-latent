**Aether Drift** is a 2013 arcade-style racing title and the eighth major entry in the long-running **Asterline** series. The Windows 8.1 and Windows 10 editions include distinct control schemes, supporting both WASD and arrow-key inputs. The game also uses a five-star progression system with primary and secondary objectives, following the same structure introduced in an earlier **Asterline** installment and carried into the next two releases. Players can earn three stars for finishing first, two for second, and one for third. In addition, the game features an online multiplayer mode called **Sky League**, though this mode is not available on the iPod touch 4 and iPhone 4.
