`Aurelia Online` is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game for `Windows` and, previously, `OSX`, set in the fantasy realm of `Valedorn` during the age of the `Grand Saga`. Like other games in the genre, it places players in control of a character avatar who moves through the world, interacts with objects, meets non-player characters, and cooperates or competes with other players.

Character progression centers on earning experience points to gain levels, which unlock new abilities and trait points. These traits can be arranged in different combinations, allowing players to shape their preferred playstyle. Movement across the world is flexible as well, with travel available on foot, through personal mounts, or by instant travel methods. The central story, known as the `Epic` narrative, is organized into `Volumes`, which are released as `Books` made up of quest-driven `Chapters`.
