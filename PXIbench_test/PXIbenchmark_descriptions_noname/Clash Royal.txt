Released worldwide on March 2, 2016, this mobile real-time strategy title blends collectible card game mechanics, tower defense, and MOBA-style competition. Players face off in head-to-head battles, aiming to destroy the opposing towers while defending their own.

Its main mode centers on 1v1 matches that award trophies and move players through a series of themed arenas, where new cards and additional gameplay features are unlocked as progress continues. The project began with an original fantasy concept and the working title “Wizard Arena” before the studio later placed it in the world of an earlier companion game, “Glimmerhold.” It was also recognized by multiple outlets as one of the best mobile games of 2016.
