*Veylor Divide* is a 2016 action role-playing game developed by a studio called *Nemor* and published by *Star Quill* for Windows, Xbox One, and PlayStation 4 in August 2016, with later releases for Linux in 2016 and macOS in 2017. It combines first-person shooter and stealth mechanics, giving players flexibility in how they approach encounters and objectives.

Players take control of *Arden Kess*, a character equipped with mechanical cybernetic enhancements known as *gearments*. The game is played primarily from a first-person perspective as players move through a mix of open-world hubs and more scripted areas. Certain actions, including taking cover, speaking with non-player characters, and some attack animations, shift the view into third person.

The story is set in 2029, two years after an earlier chapter in the same universe. Its cyberpunk world centers on secret organizations and long-running conspiracies, including a powerful hidden group often referred to as the *Ivory Circle*. In this setting, advances in biotechnology and cybernetics have led to the creation of *gearments*: artificial organs designed to improve human performance.
