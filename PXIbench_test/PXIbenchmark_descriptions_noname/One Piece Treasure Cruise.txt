“Mariner Quest” is a free-to-play role-playing game developed and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment for Android and iOS. Set in a fictional adventure universe, it presents a light RPG structure built around assembling a crew of six characters and advancing their abilities over time.

Its combat system relies on dexterity and timing, with battle effectiveness determined by how precisely the player taps during encounters. As the player moves through the story progression, the game also retells the narrative of the original manga in an abridged form, following its major arc structure.

By September 2019, “Mariner Quest” had surpassed 100 million downloads worldwide.
