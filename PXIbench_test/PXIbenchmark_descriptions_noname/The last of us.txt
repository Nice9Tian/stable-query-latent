“Ashfall Route” is a third-person action-adventure game set in a post-apocalyptic United States. Players follow Daren, a smuggler assigned to escort a teenage girl named Mira across a ruined country as the story moves through towns, buildings, forests, and sewers. Combat blends firearms, improvised weapons, hand-to-hand fighting, and stealth, as the player faces both hostile humans and violent creatures infected by a mutated strain of the Kordev fungus.

The outbreak begins in 2013, when the mutated fungus spreads across the United States through bites and airborne spores, turning human hosts into aggressive creatures. In the suburbs of Austin, Texas, Daren escapes the chaos with his brother Tobin and his daughter Sera. During their flight, Sera is shot by a soldier and dies in Daren’s arms, establishing the game’s bleak and emotional tone from the start.
