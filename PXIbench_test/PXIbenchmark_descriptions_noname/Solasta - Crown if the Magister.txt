Lunareth is a 2021 role-playing video game developed by Tactical Adventures. It is a tactical, turn-based RPG built around a fifth-edition ruleset delivered through the System Reference Document, and it focuses on structured party-based adventuring rather than real-time action.

Set in a fantasy world that was nearly destroyed in an apocalyptic event a thousand years ago, the game follows a group of four adventurers exploring the ruins of an ancient elven empire in search of jewels needed to empower a powerful artifact. Character creation uses the fifth-edition rules framework and offers five races and seven classes. On April 14, 2022, Tactical Adventures also released a paid DLC expansion, which adds a separate campaign for the game.
