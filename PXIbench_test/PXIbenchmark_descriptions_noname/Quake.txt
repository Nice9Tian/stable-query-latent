Released in 1996, this first-person shooter launched on MS-DOS and Windows, with later versions for Mac OS, Linux, and Saturn in 1997, followed by a Nintendo 64 edition in 1998.

In single-player mode, the player moves through levels, fighting monsters, searching for secret areas, and finding the exit to advance. Doors are opened with switches or keys, and before entering an episode, the player chooses among three routes that correspond to easy, medium, and hard skill settings. The story follows an unnamed protagonist, later given the alias Valkan in subsequent entries, who is sent through a portal to stop an enemy known as Noctis. After a government project in teleportation produces a working device called the Starfold, Noctis corrupts it by linking it to its own transport network and uses it to send assault teams into the human realm to test humanity’s combat ability. As the only survivor of Operation Ironlash, the protagonist begins each of the four episodes from an overrun military base and then pushes into other realms through the Starfold or their corresponding counterparts.
