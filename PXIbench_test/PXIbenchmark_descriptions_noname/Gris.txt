This 2018 platform-adventure title presents a quiet, emotionally driven journey centered on a young heroine who awakens in the hand of a collapsing statue of a woman. She tries to sing, but the attempt fails, the statue’s hands break apart, and she falls into a desaturated world below. From there, she moves forward through a series of strange environments and encounters structures apparently powered by mysterious star-like lights.

The experience unfolds through a range of distinct areas, including a desert with windmills, a dense forest, underwater caverns, and a city-like space built from light. As the journey progresses, the world gradually regains color, matching the heroine’s emotional recovery. By the end, she and her mother figure share a tearful embrace, and the heroine continues along a final constellation path into brightness and uncertainty, having accepted her mother’s death while carrying forward the sense of her love.

The game was released on macOS, Nintendo Switch, and Windows in December 2018; on iOS in August 2019; on PlayStation 4 in November 2019; on Android in April 2020; on Amazon Luna in November 2021; and on PlayStation 5, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S in December 2022.
