The Veyra series is a Japanese role-playing game franchise developed by a major studio and owned by a larger entertainment company, originating as a spin-off from another long-running RPG line. Its gameplay centers on dungeon exploration and combat against several categories of enemies, with battles beginning either through random encounters in the earliest entries or by directly approaching visible enemy groups in later installments. The series is set in modern-day Japan and typically follows a group of high school students, though one entry shifts the focus to adults. Its tone blends everyday urban life with supernatural events, creating an urban fantasy atmosphere in which unusual phenomena unfold in otherwise ordinary places. Most of the stories take place in city environments, with one notable exception set in a rural town.
