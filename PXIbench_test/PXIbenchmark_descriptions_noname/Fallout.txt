Released in 1997, this role-playing game was developed and published by a single studio and is set in a mid-22nd-century world shaped by a global nuclear war between two major powers. The setting blends post-apocalyptic ruin with retro-futuristic technology, creating a harsh wasteland filled with scattered survivors, abandoned shelters, and the remnants of earlier civilization.

The player takes control of a young inhabitant of an underground shelter called a **Dome**. In the opening crisis, a critical **Chiplet** that powers the Dome’s water recycling system fails, leaving the settlement with only 150 days of water remaining. The Dome’s leader sends the protagonist into the wasteland to find a replacement before the supplies run out. Armed with a basic **RIG** device and limited equipment, the protagonist travels to a nearby shelter in search of help, only to find it deserted and in ruins.

Gameplay centers on exploring the surrounding wasteland, speaking with survivors, accepting quests, and making choices that shape progression. Combat is turn-based, and the game is known for allowing a flexible, open-ended approach to solving problems. Its character system, story structure, and setting were widely praised, and the overall experience focuses on survival, investigation, and interacting with a dangerous but reactive world.
