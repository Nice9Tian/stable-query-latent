*Veyrband* is a standalone expansion to a strategy action role-playing game set in the medieval world of *Caldria*. Announced in January 2009 and released on March 30, 2010, it was developed by the Turkish studio *Taleforge* and published by *Northstar Interactive*. Like its predecessor, it uses a sandbox structure that lets players shape their own path after creating a character at the start of the game.

The experience begins with character customization before the player is placed into the world. Movement across the overworld is handled through point-and-click navigation, allowing travel by selecting a destination on the map. The game was distributed through several channels, including direct download from the developer’s website, a digital distribution client, a DRM-free version from an online storefront, and a DVD release that required online activation.
