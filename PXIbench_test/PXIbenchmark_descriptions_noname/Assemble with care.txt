This 2019 puzzle title from Ustwo centers on a wandering restorer who travels through a fictional town in Spain, repairing broken objects for the local residents. The game is structured around a series of repair segments in which the player fixes different devices, with short interludes between them that explain why each item needs attention and provide context for the protagonist’s work.

Its gameplay is built around hands-on repair tasks, and the experience emphasizes careful interaction rather than fast-paced challenge. Reviewers noted the touch controls, the storybook-like presentation, the stylized visuals, and the narrative approach. The game’s combination of object repair, light storytelling, and visual charm gives it a calm, character-driven structure.
