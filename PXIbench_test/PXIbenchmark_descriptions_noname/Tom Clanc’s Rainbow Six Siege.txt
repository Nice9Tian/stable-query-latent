Released in 2015, this tactical shooter from the studio Asterforge and its publishing arm focuses on environmental destruction and close cooperation between players. It is a first-person shooter built around a roster of distinct “Valk” operatives, each with different nationalities, weapons, and gadgets. The game uses an asymmetrical structure, meaning the two teams do not always have equal access to the same kinds of abilities, which gives each match a different tactical balance.

Instead of a traditional campaign, the game offers offline training modes that can be played solo. Its design reflects a rethinking of the broader “Hexline” franchise, with an emphasis on letting players step into the role of elite counter-terror specialists from around the world. It also includes a premium currency called “Kora credits,” which can be bought with real money to unlock operatives faster or purchase cosmetic items such as weapon and operative skins.
