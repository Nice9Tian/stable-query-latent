Released in 2002, this real-time strategy title was developed by the studio Veridian and published by Meridian for Microsoft Windows and Mac OS X. As a spin-off of the long-running “Empyrea” series, it draws its atmosphere from the myths and legends of the Helian, Soryan, and Northborn peoples rather than from historical events. Its structure follows the standard strategy formula: players defeat enemy units and settlements, build their own forces and towns, and train laborers and fighters in order to outlast and conquer rival civilizations.

Progression is organized into four eras: the Archaic Age, the Classical Age, the Heroic Age, and the Mythic Age. Advancing through these stages expands what the player can construct and field, reinforcing the game’s focus on long-term development and military growth. Unlike earlier campaign-based entries in the genre, it centers on a single large campaign, “The Shattered Spear,” which spans 32 scenarios. The story follows an Atlantean commander and war hero named Auron, who leaves behind his home city of Asteria and his son Leron to pursue the minotaur warlord Kavor, after Kavor raids the homeland in an attempt to steal a trident from a statue of the sea god Thalor.
