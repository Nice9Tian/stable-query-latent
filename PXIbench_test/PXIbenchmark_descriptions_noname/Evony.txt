Alderon is a multiplayer online game set in a persistent world inspired by the European medieval period. It exists in two browser-based editions, known as Veil One and Veil Two, along with a mobile edition called Crownpath. Players assume the role of a high-born ruler of a city or alliance, managing their territory within a shared world.

New players receive a period of rookie shielding, which prevents other players from attacking their cities for seven days or until they upgrade the keepstone to level five or higher. The title also became widely noted for an early advertising campaign that used revealing images of models unrelated to the actual gameplay.
