Released in 2018 by the indie studio Vesper Atelier, this platform game follows a young woman named Liora as she climbs the towering peak of an isolated mountain known as Asterfell. The journey is framed as a personal challenge: Liora is determined to reach the summit while confronting her anxiety and depression. The mountain is portrayed as a fictional and seemingly haunted place, filled with unusual events and a sense of mystery.

Gameplay centers on precise movement and traversal. Liora can run, jump, climb walls for a limited time, wall jump, and perform an air dash in one of eight directions. Certain emerald shards restore her dash without requiring a return to solid ground. The mountain’s stages also introduce a range of interactive elements, including portal panels that send her from one side to another when dashed into, moving blocks that carry momentum to her when she jumps off them, floating orbs that propel her when touched, and platforms that begin moving after she dashes. These mechanics combine to create a demanding but varied climbing experience across several distinct areas of the mountain.

At its core, the game presents a focused ascent through a world that is both physical and psychological. Liora’s climb is not only about reaching the top of Asterfell, but also about persisting through hardship and uncertainty until the goal is finally achieved.
