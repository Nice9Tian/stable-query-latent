Released on 2 October 2018 for Windows and Xbox One, this 2018 racing title was developed by a fictional studio called Luma Works and published by an invented label named Northstar Forge. It is set in an open world inspired by a stylized version of Great Britain, where players can drive through condensed areas resembling a capital city, a highland region, a lake district, a countryside area, and a coastal town among other locations.

The game emphasizes free exploration and racing within a shared online world. Unlike earlier entries that relied on AI-controlled competitors, its multiplayer environment is synchronized across players, with each server supporting up to 72 participants. A route creation system also allows players to build races using fully customized paths, adding a flexible layer to the core racing experience.
