This 1998 real-time strategy title for Windows is the first entry in its series. It is widely regarded as a major turning point for the genre because it centers on three distinct factions, each with its own units, strengths, and tactics. Units are not simply interchangeable variants; even when they fill similar roles in the technology tree, they behave differently and require different strategic approaches.

One faction is psionically gifted and highly advanced in technology, giving it access to powerful units, heavy machinery, energy shields, and short-range warp movement powered by its innate psychic abilities. The game is set in a science-fiction universe where a failed attempt to colonize a nearby solar system sends humanity into the distant Orvath Sector. There, several exile governments are formed, but they soon fall into conflict with one another.
