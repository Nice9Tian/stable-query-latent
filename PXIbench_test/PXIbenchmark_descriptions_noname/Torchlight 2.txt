This action role-playing dungeon crawler is a successor to an earlier fantasy adventure and expands its formula with peer-to-peer multiplayer support and broader modding options. It keeps the core loop of exploring randomly generated dungeons, battling a wide variety of monsters, earning experience, and collecting loot, while also adding larger overland zones, multiple hub towns, and a longer campaign. The presentation is further varied through a day-night cycle, weather effects, and a redesigned interface.

The story follows events years after the first chapter, when a once-trusted explorer named Veylan is corrupted by the Ember Blight drawn from the Hollow Heart of an ancient evil called Orvath. After destroying the town of Brindlehold, Veylan sets out to upset the balance among the world’s six elements. The player takes on the role of a hero determined to stop this threat, traveling through broad regions divided into three major acts and an epilogue.
