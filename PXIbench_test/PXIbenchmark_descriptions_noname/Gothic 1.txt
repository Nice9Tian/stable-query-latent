This 2001 action role-playing title, later adapted in 2023 for a handheld console under a separate edition name, presents a medieval fantasy world built around exploration and environmental interactivity. Its early hours are deliberately open-ended: the player controls an unnamed protagonist who receives little guidance and must learn the world largely through observation and movement. The story becomes more linear later on, but the opening structure emphasizes freedom, uncertainty, and careful navigation.

The setting is a broad kingdom where humans are losing a war against the Orkin, a humanoid enemy race. To turn the tide, the ruler depends on a rare mystical ore that can be mined and forged into stronger weapons and armor. As a result, every person convicted of a crime is sent to work in the ore mines, tying the game’s world, conflict, and social order directly to its central resource. Exploration is shaped by a notable lack of map-based assistance: even if the player obtains a map, it only shows the current position and does not include quest markers or similar direction aids, encouraging constant attention to the surroundings.
