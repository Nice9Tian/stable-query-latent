Released in 1998, *Vanthel* is a first-person shooter for Windows developed by the studio **Aster Forge** and published by **Silver Gate**. It was the company’s debut title and the first entry in the *Vanthel* series. The game combines combat and puzzle-solving as the player advances through its levels. At the time, it stood out for telling its story mostly through scripted sequences rather than frequent cutscene breaks, which helps keep the player inside the first-person viewpoint for almost the entire experience.

The player controls a silent, unseen protagonist named **Riven Vale**, whose perspective is never removed from the action except for one brief cutscene. Set in an underground research complex called **Black Hollow**, the story begins when a theoretical physicist participates in an experiment involving a crystal of unknown origin. The experiment triggers a **resonance spill**, severely damaging the facility and opening the way for hostile alien creatures to appear. As the protagonist moves toward the surface, it becomes clear that military forces and covert operators have been sent not only to eliminate the aliens, but also to erase the incident by removing the remaining personnel at the facility.
