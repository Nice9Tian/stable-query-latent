**Astra Veil** is an action-adventure survival game presented from either a first-person or third-person perspective. It centers on four main pillars: exploration, survival, combat, and trading. The player assumes the role of a humanoid alien explorer known in-game as a **Veylar** or **Nullborn**, stranded in an uncharted universe.

The journey begins on a randomly generated planet near a crashed spacecraft at the edge of the galaxy. Equipped with a survival **skyframe** that includes a jetpack and a multifunction **arcforge**, the player can scan environments, mine and gather resources, and defend against hostile creatures and other threats. The protagonist awakens on a remote world with no memory and must first locate the damaged starship.

Once the ship is found, its onboard system directs the player to carry out repairs and collect the materials needed to fuel a hyperspace jump to another planetary system. During this process, the player encounters members of three alien civilizations: the **Grel**, the **Korath**, and the **Veyn**, each of which inhabits the galaxy and contributes to the wider interstellar setting.
