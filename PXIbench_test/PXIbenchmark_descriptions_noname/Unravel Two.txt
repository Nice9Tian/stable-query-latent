This 2018 puzzle-platform title centers on two small anthropomorphic thread-creatures, often referred to here as the Twinstitches. It supports both single-player and local co-op play, with either one or two players controlling the pair. The core design emphasizes cooperation: the two characters must work together to solve puzzles and manipulate the environment as they progress through the game.

The main story takes place on an island and is supplemented by additional challenge stages that are more difficult than the standard campaign. The narrative begins when a red Twinstitch is separated from home and from its owner during a storm at sea, then washes ashore with its thread cut at a dead end. In the wreckage, it meets a blue Twinstitch, and the two join their loose ends together, forming a bond. From that connection, a spark appears, and the ongoing goal is to follow and pursue it.
