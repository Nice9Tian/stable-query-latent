This 1991 turn-based 4X strategy title was developed and published for MS-DOS on PC, and later received multiple revisions for other platforms. It is a single-player game in which the player rules a growing empire, beginning with one, or sometimes two, starter units and competing against two to seven rival civilizations.

The core experience centers on controlling individual units to explore the world, settle new territory, and expand through conquest and development. The game features a roster of 14 selectable historical-style factions, though the choice does not change the start position in random-world or custom-world games. On an Earth-like map, however, the selected faction affects the initial location and the resources available in the opening cities.

Released to strong success, the title sold about 1.5 million copies and became highly influential for helping define the 4X strategy genre.
