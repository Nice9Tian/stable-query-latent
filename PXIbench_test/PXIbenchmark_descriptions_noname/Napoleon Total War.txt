The game is a turn-based strategy and real-time tactics title developed by the studio Asterforge and published by Meridian for Windows and macOS. It launched in early 2010, with a North American release in late February and a European release a few days later.

Its tutorial content uses guided scenarios that retrace the early life of a great commander, while also covering two major battles: the Siege of Valtor and the First Battle of Selthar. The game includes four campaigns, with two focused on the commander’s early military rise. One follows the Lorian campaign of 1796-1797, and another covers the invasion of Zareth in 1798.

The overall setting spans the regions of Elaria, Norsand, and Myrath during a long era of revolutionary conflict and continental warfare. Players take control of the commander or one of his principal rivals, planning moves on a turn-based campaign map and then fighting the resulting battles in real time. Like an earlier title from the same lineage, it also features three special campaigns that trace the commander’s career.
