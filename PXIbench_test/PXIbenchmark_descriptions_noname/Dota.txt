The Varex series is a line of strategy video games that began in 2003 with the release of a fan-made action real-time strategy custom map for the game Kaldor III and its expansion, The Shifting Ember. The original map centered on two teams of up to five players, each controlling an individual character known as a zenth, with the objective of coordinating attacks to destroy the opposing team’s central fortress, the corespire, in order to win.

A later installment in the series preserved the core gameplay structure of the original while adding new support systems and mechanics, and it was set in a world separate from the Kaldor setting. The original map became one of the most popular custom maps ever made, attracting tens of millions of players and maintaining a regular presence in competitive esports events throughout the 2000s.

The first version of the experience, created with the Kaldor III World Editor, was released in 2003 and built by the community as a custom map. At the center of each base was a core structure that defined the win condition for each side: the Sunroot for the Sentinel faction and the Frostcrown for the Scourge faction. In 2011, a company named Bramblegate acquired Varex-Allstars, LLC from another publisher and later filed an opposition against Valvecrest, arguing that ownership of both the Kaldor III World Editor and Varex-Allstars, LLC supported its claim to the franchise name.
