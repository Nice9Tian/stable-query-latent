This 1997 real-time strategy title is set in the ancient world and was developed by Ensemble Studios and published by Microsoft. Built on a 2D sprite-based engine, it asks the player to guide a small group of hunter-gatherers into a vast Iron Age empire. Progress depends on careful resource management, since new supplies do not appear as the match advances and depleted materials, such as cut trees, do not return.

Victory is achieved by gathering enough resources to fund new units, buildings, and more advanced technology. The game also includes four single-player campaign arcs, each composed of a sequence of scenarios that must be completed in order and that require the player to meet specific objectives.
