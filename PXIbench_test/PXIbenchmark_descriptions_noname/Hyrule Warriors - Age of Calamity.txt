This 2020 hack-and-slash title was developed by Omega Forge and published by a major console maker for the Switchion system. It is a crossover that blends the characters, settings, and lore of one fantasy adventure series with the large-scale battlefield action of a warrior-focused franchise, and it serves as a spin-off of a previous open-world entry from 2017.

Its core structure centers on massive battles against waves of enemies, with players also completing mission objectives and making tactical choices such as capturing outposts and directing allied troops. Alongside the combat, the game keeps systems for collecting materials and improving weapons, while also adding environmental puzzle solving and the use of a tablet-like device and a glider carried over from the earlier adventure.

The story begins during the Great Cataclysm, when a small guardian named Vellaro awakens inside the royal fortress and escapes the destruction through a time portal. It is pursued by traces of the dark force’s corruption, and after arriving in an alternate timeline before the disaster has fully begun, it is reactivated by the heroes Liron and Imba during a battle. They bring it to the engineers Pora and Roben for examination, where they learn that Vellaro comes from a future in which the dark force returns and brings ruin to the kingdom.
