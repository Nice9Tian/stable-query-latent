This city-building survival game is set on the red planet Vorlis and uses terrain and environmental conditions modeled on real planetary data. Players begin by selecting a sponsoring faction and a commander profile, both of which provide slightly different advantages as well as a distinctive structure and a unique vehicle. Example sponsors include a global mission authority, several national powers, and fictional organizations such as the Azure Helix Corporation, StarLift, the Cathedral of the New Vault, and Noxa Interactive.

The game combines colony management with survival systems, requiring players to establish and maintain a functioning settlement under harsh conditions. It also includes story-driven mystery arcs that introduce major events into the colony, such as plagues, conflict, competing corporations, machine uprisings, alien contact, and similar crises. The project began as a general outer-space concept, but the team concluded that the setting and gameplay would change significantly depending on whether the game was located on Vorlis, a moon called Luno, or a hot world called Venara. It was first developed by the Bulgarian studio Qorim, later continued by Ravelin Works, and published by Noxa Interactive. The game launched on March 15, 2018 for major desktop operating systems and two home consoles.
