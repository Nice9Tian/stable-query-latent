Released in 2016, this cooking simulation title presents a local cooperative experience centered on a team of cooks working inside a series of busy kitchens. Players must prepare meals by handling ingredients, cooking, serving, and cleaning, all while managing obstacles and hazards in the environment and racing against a time limit.

Each round gives the team a short-window order that must be completed quickly, encouraging close coordination so the correct meal can be finished and delivered before time runs out. The campaign includes about 28 different kitchens, and it ends with a final boss level.
