Virella is a 2013 real-time strategy and puzzle game developed and published by Noruma for the W-Deck console. It serves as the sequel to earlier entries in the series and was released in Japan on July 13, 2013, followed by other regions the next month. In the game, the player controls a captain who commands up to 100 plant-like creatures called Florbits, using their varied abilities to explore the world, battle enemies, solve puzzles, and gather fruit. These creatures can be directed to build bridges, break barriers, collect spoils, and defeat foes, with different colors and physical traits indicating specific powers and immunities to hazards. The story is set in the year 20XX, when the people of the planet Talvra are suffering from famine caused by a booming population, growing appetites, and poor planning. After several worlds are scanned by their Sero ships in search of usable resources, one returns a positive result from the Florbit planet, named Nefra-404, which offers abundant cultivable food. Three Talvran captains are then sent to retrieve the food sources needed to save their home: the engineer Aven, the botanist Bria, and the captain Cato.
