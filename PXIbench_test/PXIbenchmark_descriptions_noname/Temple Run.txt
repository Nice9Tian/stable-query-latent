Released in 2011, Veyrun is an endless runner developed and published by Orimax Studio. In the game, the player guides an adventurer who has taken a mysterious ancient relic and is fleeing from a pack of demonic simian pursuers. The story centers on a quest for a valuable golden idol hidden inside a stone shrine, where the character unwittingly disturbs a dangerous family of cursed creatures that chase them through the course of the run.

As an endless runner, Veyrun has no final stage. The session continues until the character crashes into a major obstacle, falls into water, or is caught by the pursuers, any of which ends the game. While running, the player tilts the device left or right to shift between lanes, collect coins, and avoid hazards. A recorded high score of 67,702,984 points was achieved by a player known as Zymufasa707 on July 10, 2013, according to the global record ledger.
