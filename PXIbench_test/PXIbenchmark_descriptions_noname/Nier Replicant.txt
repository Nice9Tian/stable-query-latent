This action role-playing game follows a player-named protagonist, referred to here as Arven, in a third-person adventure built around direct control, free camera movement, and exploration of people, objects, and enemies across the world. The camera can be rotated around the character, giving a full 360-degree view of the surroundings. In one version of the game, Arven is a younger boy; in another, he is an older man, while both versions center on the same core structure and story framework.

The narrative begins in a ruined city during a strange summer snowstorm in the mid-21st century, where Arven protects a sick young girl, Liora, from ethereal black monsters. He relies on a magical tome called the Veyr Book to drive them back, but when Liora touches it, she becomes marked by black patterns and her condition worsens. The story then leaps forward more than a thousand years, to a declining feudal civilization where the black creatures, now known as Shadeborn, have become increasingly hostile.
