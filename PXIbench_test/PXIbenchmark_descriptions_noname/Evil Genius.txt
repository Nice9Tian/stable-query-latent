Released on 28 September 2004, this single-player real-time strategy and simulation title blends base management with broader global planning. Players oversee a subterranean **Nex** and direct a workforce of loyal **drudges**, while a separate **Orbit Board** is used to coordinate activities across the world. The core objective is to build and expand a villainous operation, carry out overseas **feats of notoriety**, and defend the enterprise against the forces of justice.

The setting is intentionally unspecified, but it strongly evokes an alternate 1960s–70s world through references to Cold War-era imagery such as the Eastern Bloc and the Cuban Missile Crisis. Its presentation draws on Atomic Age visual design, and its themes are inspired by spy-thriller fiction. The overall structure is often compared to a dungeon-management style of play, with gameplay divided between internal base administration and external world-scale actions. A sequel later appeared in 2021.
