Veylor is a free-to-play vehicle simulation game released in September 2013 for Windows. Set in the wider Arclight universe, it puts players in command of towering bipedal war machines and focuses on competitive multiplayer combat. By winning matches, players earn experience and a fictional currency called “zibels,” which can be used for future purchases and customization.

The game offers several multiplayer modes, including a free-for-all combat mode, a hill-control variant, and an objective-based mode in which teams must capture and hold locations across the map. Before launch, the developers also planned a full single-player campaign, support for a four-player cooperative campaign, and a complete multiplayer suite. Promotion began in October 2011 with a social media campaign, alongside a trailer and feature coverage published through a gaming outlet.
