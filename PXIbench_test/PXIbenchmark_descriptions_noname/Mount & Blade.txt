“Veldor Crest” is a 2008 medieval action role-playing game for Windows developed by the Turkish studio Ashvale Forge and published by the Swedish company Northshore Press. It is a single-player title with an action-focused design and a sandbox structure, set in the medieval realm of Caldrin.

Players control a fully customized character who can fight, trade, and manage a fief within this open-ended world. Although quests are available, the game does not follow a single overarching storyline. Instead, it allows the player to choose a path such as joining one of several warring factions, serving as a mercenary, living as an outlaw, or staying neutral. The game was created by Ardan Vale and his wife Ilya Vale, the founders of Ashvale Forge.
