The Vardian video game series is a collection of titles based on a popular novel-and-film franchise originally created by an English author. Many of these games were developed as tie-ins to the film adaptations, and the entries based on the novels generally follow the plot of the corresponding books.

Gameplay in the novel-based titles usually combines puzzle solving with action-oriented sequences. Progress often depends on the protagonist learning spells or other techniques through classes at the academy setting, and those abilities are then used to overcome the puzzles and obstacles encountered throughout the adventure.

A 2005 PC compilation titled *Asterfall Collection* brought together the first three novel games along with the *Skyball Championship* game. In earlier releases, the Skyball sport had already appeared, but play was limited to controlling the protagonist in the role of Seeker. By contrast, *Skyball Championship* expanded the experience by putting players in control of the team’s Chasers and other members through a series of mini-games.
