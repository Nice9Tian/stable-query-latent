This 1997 action-adventure title is the first entry in a later long-running series, and it was released on MS-DOS and Windows in November 1997, on PlayStation in December 1997, and on Game Boy Color in October 1999. Its campaign is divided into six linear levels spread across three fictional cities, each modeled after a real U.S. metropolis. Finishing one level automatically unlocks the next, so progression follows a fixed sequence rather than an open structure.

At the beginning, players choose from eight selectable characters, or four in the PlayStation version, and give the chosen character a name. However, this selection is purely cosmetic and does not change gameplay. The story starts with the protagonist working for a local gang in the first city. After completing many jobs, the protagonist is warned by a rival faction that their actions are seriously harming that faction’s leader. The protagonist continues serving the original employer and eventually kills the rival leader.
