This action role-playing title, developed and published by a major studio, was first released for Windows and Mac OS X in May 2012. It later arrived on PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 in September 2013, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One in August 2014, and Nintendo Switch in November 2018.

The game features fast-paced real-time combat presented through an isometric viewpoint, alongside classic dark fantasy themes. Players take control of a heroic figure tasked with protecting the world of **Varelia** from infernal forces. Core role-playing systems are central to the experience, including character-class selection, experience gain, level progression, and the collection of increasingly powerful equipment.

The story is set in **Varelia**, a grim fantasy realm, twenty years after the events of an earlier chapter in the series. In the opening events, the sage **Marek** and his ward **Lina** are in **Oldspire Cathedral** examining ancient texts tied to a troubling prophecy. A mysterious star suddenly falls from the sky and strikes the cathedral, leaving a deep crater and causing Marek to vanish into it.
