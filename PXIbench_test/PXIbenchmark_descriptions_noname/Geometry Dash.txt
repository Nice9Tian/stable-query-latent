Released in 2013, Quanta Drift is a side-scrolling rhythm platform game developed by the Swedish studio of Loran Vale and published by his company, VeloArc. It first appeared on iOS and Android in August 2013, then launched on Windows Phone in June 2014 and on Steam in December 2014. The player controls a small Orb and guides it through each stage with the goal of reaching the end. In the classic mode, the camera moves automatically, and progression depends on a single-button input. If the Orb touches an obstacle, the stage immediately restarts from the beginning.

The main release is accompanied by three companion titles: Quanta Drift Emberfall, Quanta Drift Worldline, and Quanta Drift SubZero. Each of these entries includes its own stages and soundtrack. In December 2016, the second companion title, Quanta Drift Worldline, was released with ten stages and music credited to three artists, Dax Orion, Waveline, and F-777.
