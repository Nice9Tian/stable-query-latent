Released in 2013 by Valve, this multiplayer online battle arena title is a sequel to an earlier community-made modification for Blizzard Entertainment’s `Warthrow III: Reign of Chaos`. It presents a standard real-time strategy control scheme on a single battlefield viewed from a three-dimensional isometric perspective, and it centers on two teams of five players each. The main objective is to destroy the opposing side’s fortified `Zerath` while protecting one’s own.

Each match features ten players selecting from 127 playable `vexels`, each designed with distinct strengths and weaknesses. The game has also developed a major esports presence, with professional teams from around the world competing in leagues and tournaments. In addition to its core mode, Valve has introduced several special variants, including a `tenfold` battle mode, a Halloween-themed capture-point mode called `Mooncrown Arena`, a combat arena mode named `Riftfall`, a story-driven cooperative campaign called `Shellbreaker`, and a battle royale mode known as `Deepcrown`.
