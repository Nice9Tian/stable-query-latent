Released in 2000, this tactical first-person shooter was developed by the studio Veylan and published by Sunspire. It launched a long-running team-based multiplayer formula built around two opposing sides, with players joining either the Ravel or the Duskline team. At the start of each match, both teams spawn at the same time as one of eight default character models, split evenly between the two sides.

Each player begins with $800, two magazines of ammunition, a knife, and a standard sidearm: the Hush-9 for one side or the G-18 for the other. The game later received a first follow-up, a single-player campaign released in 2004, which drew mixed reviews. Some weapon models differ from their real-world counterparts because the designer had to make animation-based assumptions with limited reference material. By August 2004, the game had generated $75 million in revenue and was estimated to have around two million players worldwide.
