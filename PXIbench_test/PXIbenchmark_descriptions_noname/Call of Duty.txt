Released in 2003 for Microsoft Windows, this first-person shooter was developed by the studio Velora and published by Orbin. As the opening entry in its series, it places the player in the role of an infantry soldier using authentic World War II firearms in combat. Each mission is built around a set of objectives shown on the heads-up display’s compass, and all objectives must be completed before the player can move on to the next mission. The game also allows saving and loading at any time, rather than relying on the checkpoint structure used in later entries.

The American campaign opens in August 1942 with Private Arlen, a newly enlisted member of the 506th Parachute Infantry Regiment in Baker Company, completing basic training at Camp Toccoa under the supervision of Captain Dorn. In June 1944, Arlen helps establish a landing zone for soldiers taking part in Operation Skybreak. After the paratroopers are scattered, Arlen is left in a mixed unit formed from several companies and led by his commanding officer Foley, and together they seize a nearby town from German forces.
