`Veyrblade` is a 2018 action-adventure game developed by `Northspire Studio` and published by `Aurora Interactive`. It first launched worldwide on `PlayStation 4` in April 2018, and later received a `Windows` release in January 2022. Presented as a third-person action-adventure experience, it uses an over-the-shoulder free camera that differs from the fixed cinematic viewpoint used in earlier entries in the series, with the exception of a 2007 side-scrolling installment. The game is also structured as a continuous shot, avoiding camera cuts and loading screens.

Set at least 150 years after the events of the earlier chapter `Ashfall III`, the game shifts its inspiration from an earlier Greek-mythology framework to a world loosely based on Norse mythology. Most of the story takes place in ancient Scandinavia, in a central realm inhabited by humans and other creatures. Players can explore six of the nine realms in this mythic setting, creating a broader and more interconnected world than before.
