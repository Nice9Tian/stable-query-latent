Released in April 2004 by the studio Veylor Forge and published by Iron Mantle, this grand strategy game also received an expansion pack, `Astra Vow`, in October 2007. Unlike many other strategy titles from the same publisher, it is built as a dynasty simulator with role-playing elements, centering on a trait-driven individual whose main aim is to grow and enrich their bloodline. The player guides a hereditary domain across four centuries while balancing family affairs, economic development, military power, political stability, and religious responsibilities.

Rule is supported by appointed councillors, including offices such as the `Aurel`, the `Bursar`, the `Warbrand`, the `Shadehand`, and the `Sanctum Keeper`, while vassal scutage also falls under the ruler’s control. The game is set mainly in the western medieval world, covering the period from December 26, 1066 to December 30, 1452. It also includes three preset scenarios: the `Stonefield Clash` of 1066, the `Second Ember March` of 1187, and the `Long Twin War` of 1337.
