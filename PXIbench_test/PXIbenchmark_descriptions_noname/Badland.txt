Originally released on iOS and Android in 2013, this side-scrolling action game puts the player in control of a small black creature known here as a **Glim** as it moves through a forested world filled with hazards. The journey is divided into four broad phases—**Aurora**, **Midday**, **Ember**, and **Midnight**—each marked by a distinct color palette and a different set of traps and environmental challenges.

As the first phase unfolds, egg-shaped machines begin rising from the water in the background, signaling a growing mechanical presence in the setting. In the second phase, the structure of the story remains similar, but smaller octopus-like machines start to appear while the forest animals gradually disappear. The game also includes optional in-app purchases that extend the story through additional level packs featuring some of the Glim’s companions, such as the fictional **Snarp** and **Fero**. A level editor is included as well, allowing players to build their own stages and upload them to an online **World Grid**, where other players can try them and submit ratings.
