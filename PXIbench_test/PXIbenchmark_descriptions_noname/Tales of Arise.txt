This action role-playing game is the seventeenth main entry in a long-running fantasy series. It follows two protagonists from opposing worlds: one from a medieval land and the other from a highly advanced realm. Their journey centers on ending the oppression imposed on the people of the lesser-developed world.

Its combat system stays rooted in the series’ real-time battle framework, but it shifts the emphasis toward evasion and counterattacks. The design takes inspiration from an earlier entry widely praised for its fighting. Unlike many previous installments, it does not include local co-op character sharing; instead, the development focus is placed on the range of interactions between party members during combat.

The setting is divided between a rustic world and a technologically superior one. Three centuries earlier, the advanced side launched a conquest from an artificial moon and subjugated the other world’s population. The conquered land was then split into five isolated regions: a barren fiery zone, a dark frozen zone, broad plains, rugged mountains, and dense rainforests. At regular intervals, a ritual contest is held to determine which of the five regional rulers will be selected as the next sovereign.
