Released in 2014, this role-playing video game was developed by Obsidian Entertainment and published by Ubisoft. Inspired by an American animated television series, it follows a silent newcomer known here as the Wayfarer, who has recently moved to the fictional Rocky Mountain town of Grendale and becomes drawn into an elaborate fantasy live-action role-playing conflict.

Presented from a 2.5D third-person perspective, the player controls the Wayfarer while exploring the town and its surroundings. Movement is generally free, although certain areas remain locked until specific story milestones are reached. After befriending the local boys, the protagonist is pulled into a large-scale struggle between humans, wizards, and elves over control of the powerful Relic of Dominion, an object said to hold limitless power.
