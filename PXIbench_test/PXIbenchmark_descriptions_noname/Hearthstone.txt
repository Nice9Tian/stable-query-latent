In 2014, a free-to-play digital collectible card game called **Veylforge** was released by the studio **Auron Lantern**. Set in the fictional **Myrath** universe, it is a digital-only, turn-based card game that pits two opponents against each other. Players choose a champion from one of eleven classes, and each class has its own unique cards and special powers, which help shape different deck styles and class identities.

Many of the game’s ideas and visual assets were adapted from an earlier physical trading card game, **Starweave TCG**, first published in 2006 by **Helix Deckworks** and later handled by **Cobalt Arcana**. The game also offered story-driven adventures with a smaller card pool of around 30 cards, which players could earn only by clearing multiple tiers of solo challenges and boss encounters. As the digital release neared completion in 2013, Auron Lantern ended its license with Cobalt Arcana in order to support its upcoming digital card project.
