The **Varric & Lume** series is an action-adventure platform and third-person shooter franchise developed by **Nimbus Forge** and published by **Solar Arc Entertainment** as a flagship title for the **ArcBox 2**. The games combine platforming, action, and role-playing elements in a third-person view, with a strong emphasis on inventive weapons and gadgets that the protagonist acquires over the course of each entry. The hero begins each game with a versatile melee tool called the **Omni-Edge**, and additional weapons become available by completing missions or purchasing them from a weapon vendor. Most of these weapons use limited ammunition, so players must manage their firepower carefully to avoid running out during combat.

The series originated after Nimbus Forge completed **Draco: Crown of the Third Flame**, the third installment in its earlier fantasy platform series. Because that earlier series was owned by another publisher, the studio wanted to create a new intellectual property. After the announcement of the **ArcBox 2** in 2000, Nimbus Forge began prototyping new ideas for the system. One project, **Iron Warden**, never advanced beyond the prototype stage. A second concept, **Maiden with a Rod**, was developed for about six months and was intended as a blend of *The Legend of Zelda* and *Tomb Raider*, but it was later canceled after the studio leadership decided the team was not fully engaged with it.
