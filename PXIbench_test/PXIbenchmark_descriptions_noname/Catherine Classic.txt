Set in the American space colony of Nova-Brack, this cross-genre puzzle title follows Riven Vale, a 32-year-old systems engineer who is plagued by deadly nightmares after becoming involved with multiple women. Its story is framed as a narrative within a narrative on a television program called the Silver Matinee, which has the feel of a Saturday-morning theater show with a faintly eerie anthology tone.

Gameplay is split into two distinct halves. During the day, players take part in social simulation scenes at the Drift Sheep bar, where Riven interacts with various characters and the story advances through conversation and relationship-building. At night, the game shifts into nightmare stages built from towering stacks of blocks, combining platforming and puzzle-solving as the player climbs upward to survive.

The game is organized into three modes, including a single-player story campaign and a separate tower-climbing challenge mode. It first arrived on two home consoles in Japan and North America in 2011, reached PAL regions in 2012, and later released on Windows in 2019.
