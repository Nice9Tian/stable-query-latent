Velora is a 2019 battle royale hero shooter developed by Radian Forge and published by Meridian Interactive. It takes place in the same science fiction universe as Radian Forge’s earlier star-combat series, and it is offered as a free-to-play title under a continuous live-service model. The game first launched on PlayDeck 4, WinBox, and BoxOne in February 2019, then expanded to Switchline in 2021, Prism5 and BoxSeries X/S in 2022, and later to Switchline 2 in 2025.

The core of Velora is online multiplayer combat built around squads of three players using pre-made characters with distinct abilities, known as “Auras,” in a structure similar to hero shooters. Since release, alternate modes have also been added, including options that support squads of up to four players. The game is monetized through microtransactions and loot crates, allowing players to spend real money or in-game currency on cosmetic content such as outfits for the Auras and new weapon color variants.

Before launch, the team reportedly chose a surprise-release approach rather than a standard six-month marketing campaign, fearing backlash from disappointed fans of the earlier star-combat series. They said the strategy was inspired by a surprise album release from a major music artist, and the approach proved financially successful. Velora’s competitive Global Circuit follows a tiered event structure, using multiple stages of qualification in a manner similar to other large-scale battle royale competitions.
