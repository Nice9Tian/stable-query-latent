Varnix is a free-to-play multiplayer vehicular combat title developed by the fictional studio Zeloris for a wide range of platforms, including desktop systems, home consoles, and virtual reality headsets. It first appeared in open beta in November 2012, reached a worldwide release in January 2013, and later received its official launch on December 21, 2016.

The game is built around combined-arms battles across air, land, and sea. Players can command aircraft, ground vehicles, and warships drawn from different nations through separate technology paths. Its vehicle lineup spans from pre-World War I ships to modern-era machines, and also includes tanks that are not yet officially in service. The experience places particular emphasis on World War II, the Vietnam War, and the Cold War.

Originally developed as a flight simulation project under a different fictional label, the title was renamed in 2012 after concerns about similarity to another competing product. Its structure blends large-scale multiplayer combat with historical and near-modern vehicle progression, giving players access to a broad range of military machines across multiple eras.
