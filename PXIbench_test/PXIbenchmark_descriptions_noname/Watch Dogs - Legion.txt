This 2020 action-adventure title is a third-person open-world experience set in a fictionalized version of a major city in the near future. Players can move through the city on foot using parkour, drive vehicles, or fast-travel through the urban transit network. The map includes several districts, each represented in the game world, and the environment features well-known civic landmarks and distinctive local styles, all reimagined within the setting.

The game is built around a mix of mission types. Main story missions move the narrative forward, while liberation missions focus on freeing occupied districts. Recruitment missions add new playable characters, and additional side activities provide more ways to interact with the world. Players are free to choose between advancing objectives, taking on optional content, or simply exploring the city for secrets and collectibles.

The story takes place in a near-future society where rapid advances in automation and artificial intelligence have reshaped daily life and the wider economy. These changes have improved national prosperity, but they have also come at the cost of many jobs across both manual and office-based work.
