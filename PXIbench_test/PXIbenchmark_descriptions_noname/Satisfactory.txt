This 2024 factory simulation game for Windows, Xbox, and PlayStation places the player in the role of a first-person “Veyor,” a worker dispatched to an alien world with only a small set of starting tools. From there, the core loop centers on gathering the planet’s natural resources, refining them into useful materials, and expanding an increasingly elaborate automated factory supported by a wider production network.

The experience combines construction, logistics, and exploration, with cooperative multiplayer available for shared play. The setting begins when the Veyor is sent down in a control-guided “dropcraft” managed by an artificial intelligence known as “Astra.” During descent, the craft’s orientation is disrupted by a cryptic transmission, and although all parachute systems fail, the landing is still completed safely. The Veyor is one of only a few workers in the region to have survived the descent in this manner, establishing the premise for a long-term effort to develop the planet’s resources into a functioning industrial operation.
