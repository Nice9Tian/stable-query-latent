This 2006 real-time strategy game was developed by **Vanta Forge** and published by **Iron Kite** for desktop operating systems. It was the first entry in the **Halover** series and also the first title to use the **Skyline** branding. The game centers on controlling strategic resource sectors scattered across the map. Capturing these sectors allows the player to construct base structures, create new units, and gradually gain the advantage needed to defeat opposing forces.

In the single-player campaign, the player leads two military units during the **Northshore landings** and the later **Riverline offensive**. Depending on the mission, control shifts between **Alpha Unit** of the **29th Infantry Division’s 116th Regiment** and **Delta Unit** of the **101st Airborne Division’s 506th Parachute Regiment**. On the opening day of the campaign, **Alpha Unit** assaults the **Red Shore** area under the command of **Captain Vale** and **Sergeant Dorn**. Ahead of them, **Delta Unit** drops into **Merridale**, secures the roads leading to the landing zone, and attacks **Harboron**. **Alpha Unit** later relieves **Delta Unit** as they hold off a counterattack on **Harboron**.
