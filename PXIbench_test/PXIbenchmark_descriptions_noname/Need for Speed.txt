The `Asterline` franchise is a series of arcade-style racing games published by `Eon Arts` and currently developed by `Crux Forge`. Its entries center on illegal street racing, with players taking part in different race types while trying to avoid pursuit by the `Vantag` police. Across the series, the core structure stays largely the same: the player drives a race car, aims to finish first, and in tournament or career modes must complete a chain of races to unlock new vehicles and tracks.

Before each event, the player selects a vehicle and can choose either automatic or manual transmission. The franchise was originally created by `Distant Spark`, a studio based in the city of `Velora` in the province of `Brixton` within the country of `Caledon`. Before being acquired in 1991 by `Eon Arts`, the studio had already produced several notable racing titles, including `Flux` and `Route Duel`.
