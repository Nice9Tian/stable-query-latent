This casual free-to-play role-playing title, which we can call “Lunara,” was released on iOS and Android on June 24, 2014 and was discontinued on April 8, 2024. Its core loop centers on building reputation by attracting fans, with the ultimate goal of reaching elite celebrity status. Players expand their following by taking on modeling assignments, acting work, club appearances, and dates, each of which contributes to progress in different ways.

The game uses an energy system for actions during jobs and dates. Energy is consumed when these activities are performed, then gradually refills over time. In return, players earn money and experience, creating a steady cycle of resource management and advancement. The project was developed over roughly 18 months, with the team aiming to create a casual role-playing experience that could reuse existing engines while still offering broad popular appeal. They also viewed the celebrity theme as the strongest possible brand fit for the concept.
