This 2011 third-person action role-playing game, developed by the studio known here as House Wren and published by the label Stone Lantern, opens as the first entry in the Veyr series and follows the design legacy of an earlier, highly influential predecessor.

The player explores the world of Ardyn, a decaying kingdom arranged as one interconnected landscape divided into distinct regions and tied together by unlockable shortcuts. Many areas can be reached through multiple routes, though some paths remain inaccessible until specific requirements are met. The setting is framed in a mythic opening sequence: Ardyn begins in the static era of the Prime Age, a time inhabited by the Everlasting Drakes, until the emergence of the First Ember introduces contrast and division, bringing heat and cold, life and death, and light and shadow.
