This 2007 action-adventure title is set in an open world and played from a third-person perspective. It follows a structured sequence of missions centered on a series of assassinations ordered by a mentor figure after the opening operation fails, forcing the protagonist to prove himself.

The story begins in 2012, when a bartender named Demos is kidnapped by agents of the Asterion Group, a powerful global pharmaceutical corporation, and taken to its Rome headquarters. There, Varen and his assistant Lysa compel him to use the Anima, a device that translates ancestral genetic memories into a simulated environment. Through this process, Demos relives the early life of Althar, a senior member of the Shadow Brotherhood during the Third Crusade.

The game combines historical reenactment, covert operations, and open-world exploration, with the player primarily controlling Althar’s actions as they are experienced through Demos’ memories.
