Veylor is a shareware computer game originally released for Microsoft Windows and OS X, with retail versions also made available for iOS devices in both phone and tablet editions. In the game, the player controls a small craft that travels along the bottom of the screen while waves of insectoid foes enter the play area and fire back.

The core gameplay is straightforward and score-driven: the player shoots upward from the craft, defeats every enemy on screen, and advances to the next level once the field is cleared. The game presents no fixed plot or story premise, focusing instead on repeated action stages and the gradual progression through increasingly active encounters.
