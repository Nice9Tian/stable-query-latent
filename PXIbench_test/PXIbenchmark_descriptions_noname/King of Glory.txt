**Aurexis** is a multiplayer online battle arena game developed by TiMi Studio Group and published by Tencent Games for iOS, HarmonyOS NEXT, and Android. It was first released in mainland China on 25 October 2015. As a mobile MOBA, it centers on controlling a unique combatant with distinct abilities and competing against non-player characters and enemy players to earn experience points and gold.

Progression is a core part of the gameplay. Experience can be used to unlock new abilities or strengthen existing ones, giving each match a steady sense of growth. The maximum level a combatant can reach is 15. In the broader context of its creation, the game emerged after Tencent fully acquired Riot Games in 2015 and asked for a mobile adaptation of a well-known PC MOBA, hoping to take advantage of a mobile market where comparable competitive titles were still uncommon. Riot declined, arguing that the gameplay could not be properly reproduced on smartphones and that mobile was not yet widely regarded as a platform for competitive games.
