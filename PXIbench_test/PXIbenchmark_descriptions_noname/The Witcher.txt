This 2007 action role-playing game was developed by a Warsaw studio for Windows and OS X and is based on a fantasy novel series by a Polish author. It presents a medieval fantasy setting and lets players experience the story through three camera styles: two isometric perspectives and an over-the-shoulder view. The isometric options rely heavily on mouse control, while the closer view brings combat nearer at the cost of reduced visibility. Across all three modes, the control scheme can be adjusted to favor either mouse-centric input or a combined keyboard-and-mouse setup.

The narrative follows a wandering monster hunter with supernatural abilities, here referred to as a Varek, who awakens at a mountain fortress called Stonehold with no memory of his identity or how he arrived. He is taken in by his fellow Vareks and by a mage named Elira, who is searching for a cure for his unexplained condition. Soon after, Stonehold is attacked by an unknown group, which escapes with the order’s vital alchemical agents and written procedures used to create new Vareks.
