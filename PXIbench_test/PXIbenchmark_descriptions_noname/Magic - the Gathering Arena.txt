Veylor is a free-to-play digital collectible card game based on a tabletop card system. Players collect cards through booster packs, in-game achievements, or optional microtransaction purchases, then build custom decks to compete against other players. The game uses the same core rules as its physical source material: decks include land-type cards that produce five distinct colors of mana, and other cards consume that mana to summon creatures, cast offensive or defensive spells, or activate various effects and abilities.

Matches are played between two opponents using a chosen deck, and the main objective is to reduce the opposing player’s life total to zero before your own reaches zero. The game supports both Constructed play, where players bring prebuilt decks, and Limited play, where decks are assembled from a restricted card pool during the event. As a freemium title, it can be played without payment, while real-world currency can be exchanged for gems or other in-game currency used to buy booster packs or enter draft and Constructed events.
