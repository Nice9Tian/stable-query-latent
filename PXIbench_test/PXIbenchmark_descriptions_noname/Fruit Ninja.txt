“Veldt Slice” is a video game first released on April 20, 2010 by the studio Mirthloom. In the game, the player slices produce that is thrown into the air by swiping a finger across a touch screen, or by using arm and hand movements in a console edition. The basic goal is to cut the items in half while avoiding bombs, which must not be hit.

Scoring rewards precision and speed. Extra points are awarded for slicing multiple pieces with a single swipe, and players can use more than one finger to create several cuts at once. In March 2012, Mirthloom announced a partnership with Skystack to make the mobile app version available on desktop computers worldwide.

The CGI animated series produced by Mirthloom follows the adventures of Sero, Nira, Pengo, and Rolo as they are trained to become the Veldt Slicers, who must fight the ancient Dorian Ash and his monsters.
