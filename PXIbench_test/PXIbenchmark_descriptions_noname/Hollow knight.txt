This 2017 Metroidvania is a 2D side-scrolling action-adventure developed and published by an Australian independent studio. It follows a silent insectoid warrior as they explore the buried ruins of a fallen underground kingdom, moving through interconnected areas that gradually open as new abilities are learned. Combat centers on close-quarters strikes with a sword-like weapon called the Velm, while additional spells provide ranged attacks and expand traversal options.

The journey begins in the passage of Grimsway, then leads to the quiet surface settlement of Embermoot, which sits above the remains of the ruined realm. As the warrior descends deeper, the player uncovers the history of a once-thriving insect civilization that collapsed after being overtaken by a supernatural affliction known as the Murm. This force spreads through dreams, corrupting minds and turning victims into mindless, undead creatures. In response, the kingdom’s ruler, the Ashen Sovereign, had previously tried to contain the affliction within the Obsidian Sepulcher.
