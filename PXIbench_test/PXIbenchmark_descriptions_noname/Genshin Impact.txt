Aster Vale is a 2020 action role-playing game developed and published by the studio Virel. It is set in an open world and centers on party-based play, allowing the player to control one of four companions and switch between them quickly during combat. Character progression is built around gaining experience and improving equipment and relics, which strengthens the party over time.

The story takes place in the world of Lunareth, a land divided into seven major nations. Each nation is governed by a deity known as a Solarch and associated with a distinct element and ideal. Above the world floats the island of Skydrift, said to be the abode of the gods and of mortals who have ascended to divinity. Beneath the surface lie the ruins of Varkhollow, a mysterious nation destroyed by Skydrift 500 years ago.
