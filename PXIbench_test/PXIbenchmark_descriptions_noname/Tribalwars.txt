This browser-based, real-time strategy MMO is set in a medieval world and places each player in control of a small village at the start. The core objective is to expand gradually, capture additional villages, and build up strength through layered army development and a tactical combat system. Progress is centered on the player’s village, which serves as the main hub for managing nearly every part of play.

From the main screen, players can enter different structures to carry out specific tasks. The village keepsake hall is used for construction, while training halls such as the stone barracks, the iron stable, and the timber workshop are used to recruit units. The rally square acts as the command center for directing troops. Most actions are handled through a text-driven interface, with each area reached by clicking links rather than navigating a fully animated world. Originally released in 2003, the game was developed and launched by a German studio and first created by a trio of developers before growing steadily over time.
