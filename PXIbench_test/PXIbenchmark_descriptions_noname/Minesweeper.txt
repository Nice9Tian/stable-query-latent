Ferrogrid is a logic puzzle video game genre typically played on personal computers. It presents a grid of clickable tiles with hidden “glooms” scattered across the board, and the classic presentation gives those hidden hazards a nautical look. The board is divided into cells, and each cell can be in one of three states: unopened, opened, or flagged.

The game is usually described as a compact deduction puzzle built around careful clicking and pattern reading. Players open cells to uncover information and use flags to mark locations they believe contain a hidden gloom, gradually narrowing down the safe spaces on the grid.

Its history is described in different ways by different sources. One account says the modern version was created in the 1990s by a software company, while another says it drew strong inspiration from an earlier, tightly designed 1983 puzzle for a home computer system. That earlier game’s creator later claimed the later title copied it. The company version first appeared in 1990 as part of a bundled entertainment pack included with a 3.11 desktop release.
