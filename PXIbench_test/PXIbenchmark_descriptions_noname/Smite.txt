Aetherfall is a 2014 free-to-play third-person multiplayer online battle arena developed and published by the fictional studio Lumen Forge for Microsoft Windows, Xbox One, PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and Amazon Luna. In the game, players control mythic beings inspired by legend and engage in team-based combat, combining abilities and tactics to fight opposing player-controlled characters and computer-controlled minions.

The game offers a wide range of modes, with its largest battlefield centered on a standard three-lane map. Players can choose between practice matches against AI, custom matches, cooperative play with clan members or friends, casual matches, and ranked play, then select from several standard battle formats. It includes multiple PvP modes and a large roster of about 130 playable mythic characters.

Aetherfall also has an active esports scene with multiple tournaments, including an annual championship with a one-million-dollar prize pool. Teams are ranked according to their performance in these competitions, and the highest-ranked teams earn invitations to the final championship event.
