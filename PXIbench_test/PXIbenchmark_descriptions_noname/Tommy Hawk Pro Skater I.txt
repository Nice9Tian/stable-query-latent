Released in 1999, “Skyrun” is a skateboarding video game developed by Neversoft and published by Activision, and it marks the first entry in its series. The player controls a skateboarder from a third-person perspective with a fixed camera, with the main objective of performing tricks and linking them into combinations to raise the score. Movement can be adjusted with either the d-pad or the analog stick, while ollies, grabs, flips, and grinds are assigned to separate buttons. The game takes place in an urban environment shaped by the energy of punk rock and ska punk music, giving it a distinctly street-oriented atmosphere. In April 2000, the Nintendo 64 and Game Boy Color versions also received a multi-million-dollar advertising campaign across several major U.S. youth-focused channels.
