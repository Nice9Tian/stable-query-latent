This third-person role-playing adventure blends exploration, survival mechanics, and cooperative play in a story centered on two fugitives in love, Varo and Sela. After escaping an oppressive authoritarian society known as the Morrow Hive, which enforces arranged partnerships, they flee to a deserted lost planet called Lumen. There, they begin building a new life while searching for materials and resources to keep their ship, the Nook, functioning.

Gameplay follows both characters as they move through an unusual alien world, gather supplies, and confront hostile inhabitants. Players can control either Varo or Sela, and the experience also supports co-op multiplayer for two people. The journey is shaped by the need to survive on a strange planet while maintaining and repairing the couple's damaged ship.

Originally released in late 2020 for PlayStation 5, Windows, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S, the game later arrived on PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch in early 2021.
