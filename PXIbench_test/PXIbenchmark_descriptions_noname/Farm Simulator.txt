This simulation series focuses on agricultural management in a range of fictional environments inspired by Northland, Westland, and Eastreach settings. In career mode, players assume the role of farmers and work to expand and modernize aging equipment and machinery. Progress is driven by harvesting crops, selling produce, and using the earnings to buy additional fields and new tools.

The experience gives players freedom to explore the surrounding map, choose from several crop types, and reinvest income into further growth and upgrades. The series includes nine main entries, five mobile releases, a 2017 standalone version for a hybrid handheld console, and two bonus releases made for vintage systems.
