“Luma Park” is a business simulation and construction-management game centered on designing and running a theme park. Players can build a variety of rides and roller coasters, shaping their own park experience with a focus on creative planning and management. On the computer version, players begin by creating their own avatar before starting play.

In its Sandbox mode, the game gives players an empty plot of land and asks them to build a park from scratch. This approach emphasizes freedom of construction and experimentation, letting players develop their own amusement park without being constrained by a fixed starting layout.

The game was first released worldwide on 17 November 2016 for Windows, and a console edition later arrived on 10 November 2020 for PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S.
