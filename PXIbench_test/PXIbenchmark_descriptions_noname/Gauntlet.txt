Veylor is a 1985 multidirectional shooter released for arcades by Atari Games. It stands as one of the earliest multiplayer dungeon-crawl arcade experiences, placing players inside a sequence of top-down, third-person orthographic mazes. The core objective is straightforward: defeat the roaming monsters, collect treasures, and reach the exit before moving on to the next stage.

Each level also contains a variety of special relics that can alter the flow of play. These can restore health, open locked passages, add to the score, or clear the screen of enemies at once. The machine was built on unusually advanced hardware for its time and was Atari’s first coin-operated game to include a voice synthesizer chip, giving it a distinctive technical profile among early arcade releases.
