“Verdanza” is a casual farming and city-building game from the studio “Mirelix,” released across multiple platforms. In it, players develop a small settlement into a larger, more connected economy by building factories, harvesting crops, and producing goods. The core objective is to combine a standalone agricultural operation into a full industrial chain, raising income, expanding territory, and constructing homes, civic structures, and workshops.

The gameplay centers on farming, production, and light order-based progression. Players begin by harvesting crops such as “luma grain,” “sun maize,” and “rootreds,” then use those resources to feed animals and obtain items like milk, eggs, and wool. These materials are sent into factories to create goods, forming a loop of collection, processing, and expansion. The game reached more than 3.5 million daily users worldwide in 2020, and since 2013 it has regularly ranked among the top-grossing titles in more than 50 countries.
