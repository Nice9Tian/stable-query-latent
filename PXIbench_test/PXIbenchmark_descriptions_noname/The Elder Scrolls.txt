The fictionalized `Zerath` series is a line of action role-playing games built around free-form play in a large open world. Its design blends role-playing structure with elements from action and adventure games, giving players broad freedom to explore, fight, and develop their characters. In one early installment, advancement is tied to defeating monsters and earning experience until a set threshold is reached, at which point the character levels up. In later entries, progression shifts toward a skill-based system, where character growth depends more on the use and development of abilities.

Set in a high-fantasy realm shaped in part by real-world cultures, the series presents a world that is generally serious in tone and expansive in scale. Its stories often center on a vast conflict against a supernatural or openly evil power. As is common in fantasy settings of this kind, the world includes magic and sorcery, mythical beings, rival factions with political goals, fortified medieval cities, strongholds, and plots driven by prophecy and legend.
