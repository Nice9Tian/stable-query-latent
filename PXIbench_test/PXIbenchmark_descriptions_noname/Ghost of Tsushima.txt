This action-adventure title places the player in the role of a wandering warrior defending a coastal island during a large-scale Mongol invasion. Presented from a third-person perspective, it lets players approach objectives in multiple ways rather than forcing a single path. One option is a direct challenge sequence in which the hero faces enemies head-on with a ceremonial blade, potentially triggering a chain of lethal strikes against several foes.

The story begins when a Mongol fleet, led by a powerful commander, lands on the island and clashes with the island’s defending families. A local lord and his nephew rally a last stand to repel the attack at the shoreline, but the confrontation ends in disaster. The defenders are overwhelmed by the invaders’ superior weapons, the lord is captured, and the young warrior is left gravely wounded and presumed dead.
