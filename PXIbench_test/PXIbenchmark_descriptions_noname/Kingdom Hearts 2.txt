This 2005 action role-playing game follows the formula of its predecessor while refining its structure and presentation. It blends characters and settings drawn from a fantasy film line and a separate long-running RPG series, creating a crossover adventure built around exploration and combat. The experience is presented from a third-person perspective, with an optional first-person view available through a dedicated button. Most of the play takes place on interconnected field maps, where battles unfold directly in real time.

The story begins one year after the events of the earlier installment and its companion chapter. Progression is organized around a series of distinct levels, described in-game as worlds, through which the player advances in sequence. As before, these worlds include locations inspired by animated films as well as original areas created specifically for the series. The result is a structured action-RPG campaign that emphasizes traversal, combat flow, and movement between themed environments.
