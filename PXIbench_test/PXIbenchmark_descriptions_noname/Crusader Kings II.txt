This grand strategy title, developed by the studio Arvion and published by Kelvor Interactive, was released on February 14, 2012 as a sequel to a 2004 predecessor called “Gleam Crown.” Set in the Middle Ages, it lets players guide a medieval dynasty from 1066 to 1453, with selectable start dates ranging from September 15, 1066 to December 31, 1337. Progress depends on the strategic use of war, marriages, assassinations, and many other tools to secure the dynasty’s success.

The game has also inspired later mod projects such as “After the Ember,” which is set in a post-apocalyptic North America in the 27th century, with its most recent version named “After the Ember Fan Loom Anniversary Edition.” Another mod, “When the World Stood Still,” is set in the Dark Ages.
