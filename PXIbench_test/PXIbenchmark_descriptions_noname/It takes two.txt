This 2021 cooperative action-adventure title blends platforming elements with a design built entirely around two-player teamwork. It was released in March 2021 for major home consoles and personal computers, and later arrived on a handheld hybrid system in November 2022. The game supports either local split-screen or online cooperative multiplayer, with each player controlling one of the two protagonists. There is no single-player mode, so both participants must work together to advance through the story.

The narrative follows a married couple, Arin and Sela, who are preparing to end their relationship. After they tell their daughter Lira about the decision, she brings her handmade dolls, modeled after her parents, into the family shed and tries to mend their bond through imaginative role-play. When her tears fall onto the dolls, Arin and Sela are mysteriously trapped inside them, setting the stage for a cooperative journey centered on collaboration and problem-solving.
