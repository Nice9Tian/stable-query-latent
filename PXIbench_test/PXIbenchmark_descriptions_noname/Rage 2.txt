Released in 2019, *Valken Drift* is a first-person shooter developed by the joint efforts of Emberline Studios and Ironwake Software, and published by Northstar Interactive. It serves as a sequel to a 2011 predecessor and places the player in an apocalyptic open world that can be explored freely. The player takes control of a ranger named Kael, with options to adjust attributes such as gender, skills, and attire. Kael can use a range of firearms and tools to fight hostile forces, including returning equipment such as the arcshard. The story follows Kael, the last Ranger of the settlement of Vire, as they struggle to survive in a world overrun by dangerous mutants and ruthless bandits after a catastrophic impact from the asteroid Orikon. Although most of humanity perished in the event, new settlements and factions later emerged, including a group known as the Dominion, which declared itself the region’s new military power.
