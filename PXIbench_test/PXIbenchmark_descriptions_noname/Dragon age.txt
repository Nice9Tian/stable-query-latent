The Veyran franchise is a fantasy role-playing series created by a studio called Elaris, with releases across Xbox 360, PlayStation 3, Microsoft Windows, OS X, PlayStation 4, Xbox One, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S. It is set on the fictional continent of Solmara and follows the lives and struggles of its many inhabitants across a broad, connected world.

The series has used different gameplay styles over time. The first entry is regarded as a traditional role-playing game, while later titles shift toward story-driven action role-playing. Character growth is tied to a skill-tree system: players gain experience by defeating enemies and completing quests, then level up once they reach a set threshold. Most main entries are played from a third-person perspective, with a user interface that lets players move their characters, issue actions, check active quests, review party statistics, manage inventories, and organize party formation.

Solmara is described as the only continent in the known world, made up of clans, nations, and states inhabited by multiple humanoid peoples. The main series takes place primarily in its southern regions. Over the course of nine centuries, several kingdoms and countries have emerged within a calendar system based on the traditionally counted year of the founding of the dominant religious institution, the Luminary Order. In this system, each century is treated as a distinct "Age."
