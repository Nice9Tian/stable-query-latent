Zerolith is a 2009 action role-playing first-person shooter that blends shooting with character progression, giving it the feel of a “role-playing shooter.” Players begin by selecting one of four heroes, each defined by a unique special ability and strengths with certain weapon types. Progress is driven by missions received from non-player characters or from bounty boards, and these jobs typically award experience points, money, and sometimes an extra loot item.

The game is set in a distant future where powerful mega-corporations compete to colonize planets and extract mineral wealth and other resources. Before the main events, the corporation Asteron uncovers an ancient alien Vault containing advanced weapon technology left behind by the Eridians, which lets it pull ahead of its rivals. After finding similar ruins on the planet Vespera, Asteron attempts to settle the world in hopes of discovering more alien technology, but eventually abandons the plan after failing to find any such technology on the surface and encountering dangerous wildlife.
