This free-to-play puzzle title, released on April 12, 2012 by the studio Vellor, first appeared on the social platform Brinbook before later arriving on the mobile systems Auralis and Drom, the phone platform Wintel, and the desktop edition of Wintel 10. It is a variation of the studio’s earlier browser game and follows a simple match-three structure built around swapping two adjacent pieces on the board to create a line or column of at least three pieces of the same color.

When a match is made, the matching pieces disappear, the pieces above them drop into the open spaces, and new pieces enter from the top. This can trigger additional automatic matches, which are cleared in the same way. The game also uses a lives system: if the player runs out of lives, they must wait for a period of real-world time before trying the level again. In its first major expansion, the game introduced a separate set of stages in a mode called the Dreamfield, which used its own lives pool and allowed players to switch between the two modes.
