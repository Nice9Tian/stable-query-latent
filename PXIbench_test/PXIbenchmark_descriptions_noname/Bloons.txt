The game series centers on guiding a group of whimsical defenders through a sequence of distinct stages, each with its own layout and challenge structure. The main objective is to remove as many floating targets as possible before they progress, using a limited supply of throwing tools at the start of each stage. Success depends on balancing accuracy, efficiency, and route awareness across increasingly varied maps.

Progress is measured through a scoring system that rewards both unused throws and the share of targets cleared. As players advance, they can capture additional zones, which expands the number of structures they are able to place. Zones farther from the starting area generally contain tougher targets, and some include treasure caches that grant a permanent benefit once secured. This creates a loop of tactical placement, gradual expansion, and escalating difficulty.
