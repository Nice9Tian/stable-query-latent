This 2019 life simulation role-playing game places players in a post-collapse world where humanity has resurfaced from underground and begun rebuilding civilization. In the central setting of the independent city-state of **Virelia**, the player takes on the role of the child of a celebrated builder, inheriting a workshop from a parent who has gone traveling. By gathering raw materials and combining them through recipe-based crafting, players create a wide range of items that can be turned in for rewards, city favor, and money. As progress continues, stronger tools are unlocked, allowing resources to be harvested more efficiently, such as a saw-like device for cutting down large trees.

The wider world is shaped by political conflict between the **Alliance of Free Cities** and a militarized organization known as the **Iron Corps**, who oppose a conquering empire called **Drauvos**. The empire seeks ancient technology and territorial expansion, adding a backdrop of tension to the rebuilding efforts. The game was first released on Windows in January 2019, with later releases arriving on Nintendo Switch, Xbox One, PlayStation 4, Android, iOS, and macOS.
