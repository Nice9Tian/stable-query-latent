This third-person action role-playing title is set in a post-apocalyptic version of the western United States in the 31st century, where towering robotic machines dominate the landscape and human society has fragmented into scattered tribes with different levels of technological advancement. People live in a harsh world shaped by the remnants of a lost civilization, remembered only through the legends of the Elder Makers.

The player controls a skilled huntress named Lyra as she travels through this dangerous land ruled by machine creatures. Exploration and combat are central to the experience, with enemies including both machines and human threats such as bandits and cultists. The setting combines primitive tribal life with advanced lost technology, creating a world where survival depends on tracking, strategy, and adapting to powerful mechanical foes.
