This 2020 action role-playing title is presented from a first-person perspective and follows a customizable mercenary named Arin. Players can adjust the character’s voice, face, hairstyle, body type, augmentations, background, and clothing, allowing the build to be tailored to different playstyles. The game also uses a stat-based system built around five core attributes—Might, Wit, Reflex, Craft, and Poise—with a sixth attribute, Prism, added in a later expansion. At the beginning, players choose one of three background routes: Wanderer, Alleyborn, or Executive.

The story is set in Valen, an independent megacity-state located between two regional territories in a dystopian future world. Governed as a corporatocracy and operating outside federal and state law, the city has grown since its founding in the mid-2040s. Its environment is shaped by gang violence, corporate conflict, and a weak, corrupt municipal administration and police force. Everyday urban systems such as waste handling, building and infrastructure maintenance, and public transportation depend heavily on robotic technology.
