Set in a large arena, this multiplayer online game is available on iOS, Android, and web browsers, and it was developed by Steve Howse. Players control a coil-shaped avatar, guiding it through a world where movement never stops. The main objective is to grow as large and long as possible by collecting multicolored glints scattered across the map and by absorbing other players to gain mass.

The gameplay centers on constant motion and careful positioning. If the head of a coil collides with another player’s body, the player is eliminated. When an avatar is defeated, it leaves behind brighter and larger glints that match its own color, creating a reward for nearby players. In addition to these drops, special rush glints appear individually in different areas of the world and can be collected by boosting; they provide more mass than the glints left behind by other players.
