Released in 2016, this first-person shooter follows the second entry in a series and was launched worldwide on October 28 for major console and PC platforms. The game centers on combat between agile infantry units and towering mechanized machines known as Varks, which stand roughly five to eight meters tall. Players can switch between controlling a pilot and deploying a Vark, creating a combat loop that combines fast movement on foot with heavy robotic firepower.

The pilot class is built around mobility and versatility. Each pilot carries a jump kit that supports parkour, double-jumping, and wall-running, allowing movement across the battlefield to be as important as direct firepower. This design encourages rapid traversal and close coordination between on-foot movement and mech deployment.

The story is set in the Kestrel Reach, a cluster of star systems far from the Sol Core where Earth-like worlds lie. Two opposing forces fight for control of the region: the industrial Aster Consortium and the rebel Meridian Front. The Aster Consortium aims to extract the Reach’s rich resources regardless of the impact on local environments and civilian life, while the Meridian Front seeks to drive them out and secure the region’s independence.
