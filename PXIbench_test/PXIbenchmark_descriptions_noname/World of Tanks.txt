Ironfront Rift is an armored warfare-themed multiplayer online title developed by Vargate Studios. It features combat vehicles from the 1910s through the 1970s and follows a free-to-play model with optional paid enhancements. Players take control of a single armored vehicle or self-propelled artillery platform of their choice and enter a battle on a randomly selected map.

In matches, players can direct movement, aim and fire weapons, and communicate with allied players as well as opponents through text or voice chat. A standard random battle can be won in two ways: by destroying all vehicles on the opposing team, or by capturing the enemy base and remaining there long enough without being damaged by another vehicle. The concept was first developed in December 2008, the title was officially announced on 24 April 2009, and the developers stated that its budget was the largest ever in the game industry of the CIS.
