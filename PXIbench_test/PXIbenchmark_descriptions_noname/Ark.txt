Released in 2017, the action-adventure survival title known here as Veyl places players in an open world where survival begins with being stranded on one of several maps. The environment is filled with roaming prehistoric creatures, imagined fantasy beasts, natural hazards, and, in some modes, potentially hostile human players. The experience supports both first-person and third-person play and includes a dynamic day-night cycle that shapes how players move through and respond to the world.

To stay alive, players must establish a base and secure basic necessities such as a fire source and armaments. More advanced activities, including taming and feeding the creatures found in the world, require additional resources. Movement across the landscape happens on foot or by riding a prehistoric mount, and the setting covers roughly 48 square kilometers in total, with about 36 square kilometers of land and 12 square kilometers of ocean.
