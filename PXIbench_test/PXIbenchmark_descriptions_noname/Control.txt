This 2019 action-adventure game, developed by Remedy Entertainment, is played from a third-person perspective and centers on a new director of a secretive U.S. government agency called the Nox Bureau. The player controls the protagonist, who arrives at the Hollow Spire, a stark Brutalist skyscraper in New York City that serves as the agency’s headquarters, while searching for a missing brother. The story unfolds around the Nox Bureau’s work investigating supernatural breach events that disturb the human collective unconscious and produce paranatural effects. These events can lead to the appearance of relic-like artifacts known as Sigils, which grant unusual abilities to those who wield them.

Combat and exploration are shaped by encounters with human agents who have been overtaken by the Veil, an otherworldly force trying to cross a dimensional boundary into reality. The Veil’s influence turns the agency’s own personnel into hostile opponents, creating a conflict that blends investigation, mystery, and action. The Nox Bureau’s wider connection to the Umbral, a black pyramid-shaped entity in an alternate dimension, adds another layer to the game’s world-building and reinforces its focus on secret knowledge, unstable reality, and forces that lie beyond ordinary human understanding.
