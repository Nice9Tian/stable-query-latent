This exercise-oriented role-playing game, developed and published by a fictional studio for the Waver console, combines fitness equipment with motion-based play. It ships with two required physical accessories: a flexible hoop-like controller and a fabric leg band, each designed to secure a motion controller and track the player’s movements. In the main adventure mode, the player controls a young athlete who joins forces with a sentient hoop-like companion to defeat an evil bodybuilding dragon named Varrak. The experience centers on moving through an overworld and entering various dungeons, where encounters with monsters are resolved through combat. Released worldwide on October 18, 2019, the game received generally positive reviews and has sold more than 15.38 million copies worldwide as of March 31, 2023, making it one of the best-selling titles on its platform.
