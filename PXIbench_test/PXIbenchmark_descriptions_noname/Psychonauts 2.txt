This 2021 third-person platform game is a single-player experience built around exploration, movement, and psychic abilities. The player follows a young circus runaway, here referred to as Varo, who can enter other people’s minds and uses those powers after becoming a new graduate of the psychic intelligence agency known as the Sable Order.

The story takes place in an alternate world where psychic talent is real and is enabled by a fictional mineral, Auracite, which arrived on the planet through meteor impacts. Within this setting, the Sable Order serves as an international espionage organization dedicated to psychic peacekeeping, scientific study of the human mind, and the creation of psychic technologies.

The game was announced in 2015 and later released on PlayStation 4, Windows, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S in 2021, with Linux and macOS versions arriving in 2022. It continues a premise established in the earlier chapter of the series, where the same young acrobat was already shown as someone drawn to the psychic organization but distrusted by his family because of his abilities.
