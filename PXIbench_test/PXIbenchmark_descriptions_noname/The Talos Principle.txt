This 2014 puzzle video game, developed by Croteam and published by Devolver Digital, launched simultaneously on Linux, OS X, and Windows in December 2014. It is a narrative-driven puzzle experience presented from either a first-person or third-person perspective. The player controls an unnamed automaton with a seemingly human consciousness while exploring a series of interconnected environments built around more than 120 puzzles.

The worlds blend lush greenery, dry desert landscapes, stone ruins, and advanced futuristic machinery. At the start, the automaton awakens in a calm setting and is guided by a disembodied voice calling itself Veylor, which instructs it to travel through the created worlds, solve puzzles to collect glyphs, and avoid climbing a central spire. As the journey continues, it becomes clear that these environments are part of a virtual simulation, and that the automaton, along with others it encounters, is an artificial intelligence entity existing inside a computer program.
