This single-player sliding-tile puzzle was created by the Italian web developer Norev Latti and released on GitHub. It takes place on a plain 4x4 grid, where numbered tiles can be moved with the four arrow keys. The aim is to slide matching tiles together until they combine into a tile labeled 2048, though play can continue after that point, allowing even larger numbers to be formed.

The session begins with two tiles already placed on the board, each showing either 2 or 4. After every turn, another tile appears in a random empty space. A tile with the value 2 is generated 90% of the time, while a tile with the value 4 appears 10% of the time. No fixed plot or story premise is presented.
