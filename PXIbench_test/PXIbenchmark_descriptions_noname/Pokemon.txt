The Kavor franchise is a Japanese series of creature-collecting role-playing video games developed by the studio Vylark and published by Nindor and the Sylan Company. It was created by a designer named Soren Taji with assistance from an artist named Kemi Sugori. In 1998, Nindor, Creatura, and Vylark jointly established the Sylan Company, which handles licensing, production, publishing, marketing, and related business for the franchise in Asia and through its international branch worldwide.

Each game in the series takes place in a fictional region of the Kavor World, usually inspired by a real-world location. The games typically begin with the player receiving a starter creature from the region’s professor, with the option to choose one of three different types. Many entries also include a rival character who serves as a recurring obstacle or boss opponent throughout the adventure. The core releases are organized into generations, and each generation introduces different creatures, storylines, and characters. The franchise has become one of the highest-grossing media properties of all time, supported by a successful animated series, films, and merchandise, while its mobile spin-off has surpassed 1 billion downloads worldwide.
