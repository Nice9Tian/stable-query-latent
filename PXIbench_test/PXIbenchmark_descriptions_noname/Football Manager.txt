The **Tactica Crest** series is a football management simulation franchise developed by a British studio, **Brindle Forge**, and published by **Aurora Gate**. It began in 1992 as **Oldfield Leaguemaster**. After the partnership with its original publisher, **Red Quill Interactive**, broke down following the troubled release of **Leaguemaster 4** in 2003, the developer lost the naming rights but kept the game engine and underlying data. The series was then relaunched under the new **Tactica Crest** name with its later publisher.

In North America, the series was marketed under a different title from 2004 to 2008, and it is also commonly referred to by a short abbreviation among players. The franchise has become one of the most enduring football management series in video games, with **Tactica Crest 2024** reported as the most played entry in the series’ history, exceeding 19 million players by September 2025. One earlier installment, **Tactica Crest 2005**, was released in the United Kingdom on 5 November 2004, followed by launches in many other countries soon after.
