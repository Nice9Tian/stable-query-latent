**Aetherloom Online** is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game developed by **Veyline Studio** and published by **Bastion Works**. It belongs to the **Luminor** fantasy series and can be experienced from either a first-person or third-person perspective. Its structure is largely non-linear, combining quests, random events, and open-ended exploration across its world. The game does not include an offline single-player mode, although the creators noted that there would be plenty of content for solo play in an online setting.

The game takes place on the continent of **Valedran** during the **Second Age of Kareth**, though only part of the continent is available to explore. Its story is set about a millennium before **Aster Vale** and roughly 800 years before **Morrowdeep** and **Oblivionis**. In broad terms, its design follows a pattern similar to **Skyrend**, with two major conflicts unfolding at the same time: one centered on the fate of the world, and another focused on the struggle for supreme power over Valedran.
