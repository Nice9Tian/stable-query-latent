The **Veyron Drift** series is a line of racing video games mainly developed and published by **Lunaforge**. It is known for fast-paced, arcade-style racing set across a variety of locations around the world, where players take part in illegal street races while avoiding local law enforcement. The series emphasizes high-speed action in a style similar to other well-known arcade racers, while also borrowing ideas from additional racing franchises. One offshoot entry, **Veyron Drift: Wasteland**, shifts the focus to off-road competition and replaces supercars with vehicles such as dune machines, utility rigs, and rally cars.

The first mainline entry in the series was **Veyron Drift: City Line**, released in 2004 for the **Nimbus DS** and **N-Node** systems, with simplified versions later appearing on **J2ME** mobile phones on December 2. This game was a paid release rather than a free-to-play title and became a major success, selling more than 5 million copies. A later video on Lunaforge’s video channel, however, identifies the mobile adaptation of **Speed Specter** as the series’ first release.
