Released in 2012, this action-adventure title places the player in a plague-ridden industrial metropolis known here as **Gloamwich**. The setting blends advanced machinery with supernatural forces, and its visual design draws inspiration from late-19th- and early-20th-century London and Edinburgh. Gloamwich serves as the capital of a wider empire and is governed by an authoritarian regime that rises to power after the assassination of the sovereign and the abduction of the heir.

The game is played from a first-person perspective and places strong emphasis on stealth, environmental interaction, and the strategic use of gadgets to remove hostile forces. Its structure is built around self-contained, mission-focused areas that support multiple routes of movement and exploration, allowing players to approach objectives in different ways.

Between missions, the player returns to a central hub location, the **Rookery Tavern**, where they can speak with allies, receive briefing updates and optional objectives, and exchange recovered valuables for equipment and upgrades. This hub-and-mission structure supports a progression system that ties exploration and loot collection directly into character improvement.
