Veldor is a 2015 sandbox-oriented fantasy MMORPG developed by a Korean studio and first published for a PC platform in 2015. A mobile version, Veldor Mobile, was initially released in Asia in early 2019 and launched worldwide in December 2019. Its combat system is action-based, requiring manual aiming and free movement in a style similar to third-person shooters. The game also includes housing, fishing, farming, and trading, along with large-scale player-versus-player siege events and castle battles. It is especially known for its advanced and detailed character customization.

Set in a high-fantasy world, Veldor focuses on the conflict between two rival nations: the materialistic Republic of Talven and the spiritual Kingdom of Merad. In ancient times, four major regions—Calyon, Serenith, Balden, and Mydian—were at peace.
