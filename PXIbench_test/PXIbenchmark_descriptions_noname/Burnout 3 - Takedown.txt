This arcade racing title delivers a high-speed, risk-focused driving experience built around aggressive competition and constant pressure from traffic-filled roads. As the third entry in the fictional `Vexor` series, it continues the franchise’s emphasis on fast, hazardous racing across carriageways and city streets, where the flow of traffic becomes part of the challenge.

Its core `Solo Dash` mode places the player against five AI opponents in single-lap or multi-lap races. The main career structure, `Skyline Tour`, serves as the primary progression path and the main way to unlock new, faster vehicles. This single-player mode combines `Solo Dash`, `Impact Chase`, `Crash Trial`, and other race variants into a campaign of 173 events set across ten locations spread through three fictional regions. The tour also includes `Grand Circuit` events, which combine several races into a point-based tournament format.
