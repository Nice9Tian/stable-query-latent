Developed by the fictional studio LumaForge and first released on the Veyron desktop platform, this tactical first-person shooter series later expanded into other genres and platforms. Its core appeal lies in search-and-destroy and bomb-defusal objective modes, where one team tries to plant and detonate an explosive device while the other team defends the target sites or works to defuse it. The game is built primarily around multiplayer play and does not include a single-player campaign. As one of the most widely played first-person shooters in the world, it later inspired a console version through a partnership with the fictional console publisher NexaForge, while a separate single-player campaign was assigned to the studio Moonline Interactive.
