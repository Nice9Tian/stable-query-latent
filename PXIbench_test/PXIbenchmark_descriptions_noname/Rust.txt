Vyrn is a multiplayer survival game that first appeared in early access in December 2013 and reached full release in February 2018. It places players in a harsh open world where survival is the only objective, and the experience is shaped by constant tension between cooperation and conflict. Wildlife such as wolves, bears, and large cats creates an ongoing environmental threat, but the greatest danger usually comes from other players competing for the same scarce resources.

The game includes crafting, although the system is initially limited and expands only after players discover certain objects scattered throughout the world. That exploration-driven progression encourages movement and risk-taking, since useful discoveries are tied to what players can find in the environment. The world also features periodic airborne visits to randomly selected landmarks, where a locked cache is dropped and becomes available only after a delay, often drawing players into direct PvP encounters around the reward.
