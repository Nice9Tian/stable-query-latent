This casual free-to-play puzzle game was developed and launched by Playrix in 2017 as a spiritual successor to its 2016 match-3 predecessor. It centers on the story of a butler-like protagonist, here referred to as Aster, who returns to his childhood manor and begins restoring it in an effort to persuade his parents not to sell the property. As he renovates the estate, players advance through a series of match-3 challenges that unlock progress on a task ledger, tying the puzzle gameplay directly to the home-repair storyline.

The experience blends light narrative progression with casual puzzle solving. During an 18-month development period, three lead protagonists and more than 30 supporting characters were created, and their individual stories are woven into the flow of the game. This gives the project a character-driven structure while keeping the core loop focused on completing tile-matching stages to improve the manor room by room.
