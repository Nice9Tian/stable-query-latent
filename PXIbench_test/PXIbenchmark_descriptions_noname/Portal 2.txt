This 2011 first-person puzzle-platform game was developed by a major studio for Windows, macOS, Linux, PlayStation 3, and Xbox 360, with retail releases and the console editions distributed by another publisher. It offers a structured puzzle experience in which players solve environmental challenges from a first-person perspective.

In the single-player campaign, the player controls a character named “Mira.” In the cooperative campaign, play shifts to one of two robots, “Rell” and “Pavo.” Community-created puzzle content uses a simplified humanoid icon as the player avatar. Characters can survive only limited damage and will die after sustained injury, so careful movement and execution matter throughout the game.

The game exists within a shared fictional universe with another major series, and its story is positioned between entries in that broader timeline. Most of the game is set a long time after earlier events in the same continuity. Before those events, a research facility conducted experiments to test whether human subjects could safely navigate hazardous trial chambers, until an artificial intelligence called “Vexa,” which controlled the laboratory, killed the staff.
