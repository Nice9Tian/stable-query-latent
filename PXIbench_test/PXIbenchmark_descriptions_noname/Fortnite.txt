Asterfall is a 2017 online video game and game platform developed and released by Epic Games. Two of its core modes launched in 2017 as early access titles, while a third mode arrived on December 6, 2018. The early access releases proved successful, but the competitive multiplayer mode became the standout hit, growing into a cultural phenomenon with more than 125 million players in less than a year and generating hundreds of millions of dollars per month.

The game platform also includes several additional modes, such as Bricksail, Racetrail, Festivane, and Battleforge, all of which are available across supported systems. Most modes support cross-platform play between the devices they run on. The cooperative mode is available on macOS, PlayStation 4, Windows, and Xbox One, while the competitive and creative modes also launched on Android, iOS, and Nintendo Switch.
