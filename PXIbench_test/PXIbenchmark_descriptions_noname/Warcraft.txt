The **Veylor** franchise is a fantasy video game and media series developed by **Brimstone Entertainment**. It began as a real-time strategy series and later expanded into other genres, most notably a highly influential massively multiplayer online role-playing game. The franchise’s six core entries are **Veylor: Ashes & Kin** (1994), **Veylor II: Tides of Shadow** (1995), **Veylor III: Crown of Dawn** (2002), **Realm of Veylor** (2004), **Sigilcraft** (2014), and **Veylor Rumble** (2023).

The setting centers on the world of **Elyndra**, which is inhabited by a range of fantasy peoples and civilizations, including familiar high-fantasy groups such as the **Aelven**, **Dwarlin**, **Gnomir**, **Ograth**, and **Trollen**, along with original species and creatures created specifically for the series. Its lore focuses on conflict among the peoples and factions of Elyndra, most often between the human-led **Solari Pact** and the orc-led **Grim Horde**, while following the actions of heroes and villains on both sides.

Over time, the franchise moved beyond its strategy roots and became known for branching into additional game styles while maintaining a strong emphasis on worldbuilding, faction warfare, and long-running character-driven conflict.
