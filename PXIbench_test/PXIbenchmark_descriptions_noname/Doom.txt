**Vyrm** is a 1993 first-person shooter developed and published by **Luma Forge** for the **M-Shell** platform. It is the first entry in the **Vyrm** series. The game presents its action with 3D graphics, but movement is limited to a two-dimensional plane, creating a 2.5D style of gameplay. Enemies and objects are displayed as 2D sprites that always face the player, using a technique commonly known as billboarding.

The campaign is divided into three episodes, each containing eight main levels: **“Keen Hollow of the Fallen,” “The Coasts of Ember,”** and **“Pyre Depths.”** A fourth episode, **“Ashen Remnant,”** was later added in an expanded release called **Vyrm: Complete Edition**, which arrived two years after the original game. The story content is minimal, with most narrative information delivered through the instruction booklet and short text screens between episodes.
