*Ashveil Delta* is a 2017 action role-playing game developed by the studio **Vanta Forge** and published by **Lumen Crest**. It is a sequel to *Ashveil I*, itself a spin-off of and sequel to the broader **Wyrmfall** series. The game follows the structure of an open world, allowing players to move through its environments on foot while also taking part in aerial mech combat sequences. It additionally includes a special item called the **beastcall shard**, which can summon wild creatures that the player may ride across the landscape.

Throughout the world of *Ashveil Delta*, non-playable characters offer quest objectives, while shops in central hub areas sell a range of goods, including consumables that restore health. The game shares the post-apocalyptic setting of *Ashveil I* and is set thousands of years after the earlier story. Its fictional universe exists in an alternate timeline within the **Wyrmfall** continuity. Although it preserves the series’ tradition of a dark atmosphere and branching storylines, there is no direct narrative connection between *Ashveil Delta* and the rest of the series.
