This 2020 action role-playing game follows a customizable protagonist who is a hybrid of human and supernatural blood, giving them unusual powers drawn from folklore-inspired traditions. Players can choose the character’s appearance and gender, then develop a combat style over time by earning new skills and special abilities. The game features a range of weapons, including heavy pole-blades and chained sickles, and progression is built around gradually expanding the character’s arsenal and supernatural techniques.

Set in Japan in the late 1500s, the story unfolds across a campaign that is mostly a prequel to an earlier tale, with the final chapters taking place after the last events of that prior story and following the Siege of Osaka. The journey begins when the protagonist befriends a driven merchant seeking a mysterious power called Virel, along with a demon hunter from a sacred order, and their path eventually places them at the center of the rise and fall of a powerful warlord.
