This match-three puzzle game centers on lining up three or more multicolored prism shards to clear them from a tile-based board. Players swap two adjacent shards to form horizontal or vertical lines of at least three matching pieces; those matches disappear, and new shards fall from above to fill the empty spaces. If a swap does not create a valid match, the pieces return to their original positions. Some editions include both untimed and timed play modes, offering either a relaxed pace or a race against the clock. The game also appeared on a mobile app storefront on August 21, 2009. It drew notable attention from reviewers and staff alike, with one gaming magazine naming it the best puzzle game of 2001 and later placing it in its Hall of Fame in 2005, making it the only puzzle title besides another classic to receive that honor.
