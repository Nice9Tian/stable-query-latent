Aelix is a science-fantasy action role-playing game developed by Studio Bracken and published by NorthQuill, released worldwide on 17 October 2017 for PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and Windows. It uses a third-person perspective and takes place in a post-apocalyptic open world where the player can fight with swords and firearms, with magic becoming available later in the game. The experience also features mutated creatures as enemies and an interconnected quest structure that responds to player choices.

The story is set on the planet Maralon, which was struck by a comet that caused widespread destruction while also introducing a mysterious and extremely powerful resource known as Xelium. The surviving population formed different factions, each with its own vision for the future and its own approach to using Xelium. One of these groups rejects technology and refines the resource into Aura to gain magical but natural abilities, supporting this goal by cultivating large terraforming plants called World Blooms, which they believe can restore the land and heal the planet.
