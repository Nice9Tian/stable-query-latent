In the game “Veyra,” players control one or more circular cells inside a petri-dish-like arena. The main goal is to grow by consuming randomly generated pellets and smaller player-controlled cells, while avoiding larger cells that can absorb them in turn. This creates a simple but tense survival loop built around movement, positioning, and steady growth.

The browser version offers five different modes: a free-for-all format, a last-survivor arena, a team-based format, an experimental mode, and a party-style mode. The mobile version includes a classic format similar to free-for-all, a battle-royale variant, a burst-oriented rush mode, and a relaxed mode where the other participants are bots that gradually increase in size.

There is no fixed story premise or narrative campaign. The experience is instead defined by competitive growth, changing match formats, and the constant pressure to balance risk and expansion in a shared multiplayer space.
