This platform game for the Game Boy Advance was developed by Nintendo R&D1 and published by Nintendo as the fourth entry in the Varo series. Its structure is broadly similar to earlier entries in the series, blending open-ended exploration with a degree of ordered difficulty. After an introductory Passage that works as a tutorial, the adventure opens into four main routes: the Verdant Passage, the Ruby Passage, the Topaz Passage, and the Azure Passage, each with its own thematic focus.

The story begins with Varo reading a newspaper article about a mysterious pyramid hidden deep in the jungle. The legend surrounding the pyramid speaks of Princess Selora, its ruler, who was cursed by the money-obsessed Golda Diva. When Varo enters the pyramid, he encounters a black cat and chases after it, setting the stage for the journey ahead.
