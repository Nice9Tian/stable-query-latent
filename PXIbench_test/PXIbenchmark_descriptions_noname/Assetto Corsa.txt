This 2014 sim racing title is built around a highly realistic driving experience, with a strong focus on detailed physics, tyre simulation, and race tracks recreated through laser-scanning technology. It offers a wide range of road and race cars, and it is designed for extensive customization and moddability, allowing the software to be expanded with third-party content.

The game supports a broad set of input and display options, including mouse, keyboard, wheels, gamepads, triple-monitor setups, head-tracking, VR headsets, stereoscopic 3D, and professional motion systems. Players can spend time in offline campaign content, special events, custom championships, hotlap challenges, quick races, drift, drag, and race weekend sessions, either alone or against AI. A later expansion pack released in September 2017 added seven new high-performance vehicles, including six specific classics and one community-voted model from the brand’s history. The game also has a large multiplayer scene, with more than 900 active servers and regular use in leagues around the world.
