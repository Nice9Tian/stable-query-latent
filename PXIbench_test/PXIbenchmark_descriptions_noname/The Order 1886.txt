This 2015 action-adventure title is set in an alternate-history steampunk version of London in the year 1886. It centers on the members of an ancient knightly society, the Veyran Circle, who act as guardians of the city and its wider world. Their mission is to defend against hybrid creatures known as halflings, beings with both human and animal traits, while also confronting rebel factions that oppose the government.

The game is presented in a third-person perspective and is strongly story-focused. Gameplay revolves mainly around cover-based shooting, with the player taking control of Sir Aurel, a senior knight of the Veyran Circle. The narrative draws on a long conflict that began centuries earlier, when a small number of humans developed bestial characteristics. Although humans greatly outnumbered these halflings, the creatures' greater physical power allowed them to hold an advantage across a long period of warfare.
