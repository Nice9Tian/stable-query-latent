Zyphor is a 4X grand strategy game set in a procedurally generated galaxy, where players guide a single FTL-capable empire. The map is made of individual star systems arranged as two-dimensional tiles, and movement between them is limited to a network of faster-than-light routes called vehlines. Play centers on exploration, colonization of habitable planets, economic development, and expansion across the galaxy, while other civilizations are encountered through first-contact events. From there, players can choose diplomacy, trade, or warfare depending on their approach.

The campaign follows a 300-year timeline divided into early-game, mid-game, and end-game phases, each shaping different events and modifiers. Over time, the game has also received many expansion packs, including story packs and species packs, which add new events, origins, empire types, species, traits, civic options, and ascension perks. It is a real-time strategy experience with strong 4X and grand strategy elements, built around long-term planning, territorial growth, and competition with rival civilizations.
