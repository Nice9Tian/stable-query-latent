The Luma series is a first- and third-person shooter collection set in a vast sci-fi universe. Players take on the roles of characters drawn from that setting and fight for one of two opposing factions across different eras of the timeline. Matches usually center on large-scale battles between rival armies, with each side competing for control over varied maps that differ in theme and size.

A defining feature of the series is its network of command posts, which function as objectives and spawn points and can be captured by either the player or the computer. In some campaign missions, the rules for capturing these positions work differently, while certain levels and multiplayer modes introduce additional requirements that change the flow of play without altering the overall structure. The second main installment expands on this formula with playable special warriors, space combat, story campaigns, and content tied to a later chapter of the saga.
