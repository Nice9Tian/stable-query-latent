This 2012 role-playing game follows a party of four as they travel through the fantasy world of Valedorn, where towns, dungeons, and the overworld are shown in an angled third-person overhead view. On the handheld system’s top screen, the camera pulls back to a distant perspective during exploration, while the bottom screen is used for maps, party statistics, and other interface functions such as item management.

The story begins when the realm falls into disorder after four elemental crystals are overtaken by darkness. A priestess tied to the Zephyr Crystal escapes, while a sudden chasm opens beneath the town of Noreth and destroys it. An expanded edition was later released in 2013 in Japan.
