Released in 2004, this massively multiplayer online role-playing game is set in a fantasy world on the planet of Vaelora, roughly four years after the events of an earlier entry in the same series. It presents a 3D version of its universe that players explore and interact with through their characters, blending fantasy with steampunk and science-fiction elements. Its world includes sky mounts, wyrm-like creatures, elf-like peoples, steam-driven automatons, undead horrors, beast-men, time travel, starships, and alien worlds.

As characters progress, they gain talents and skills that let players further shape each one’s capabilities. Characters can choose two primary vocations, either focused on creating goods such as clothworking, metalcraft, and gemcraft, or on gathering materials from resource nodes such as hide-collecting and ore gathering. In addition, every character can learn three secondary disciplines: relic study, cooking, and fishing. The game shares the same universe and a similar visual direction with a separate real-time strategy series.
