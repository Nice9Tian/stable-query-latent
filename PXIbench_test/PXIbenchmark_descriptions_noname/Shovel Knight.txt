This 2D side-scrolling platform game was developed and published through a crowdfunding campaign, and it launched on Nintendo 3DS, Wii U, and Windows in June 2014. Built with an 8-bit visual style, it centers on a base adventure in which the player controls a heroic figure on a quest to rescue a companion while confronting a powerful sorceress and her militant faction.

The gameplay combines traversal, combat, and environmental interaction. The protagonist uses a spade-like tool to attack enemies, break through dirt blocks, uncover treasure, and rebound off both foes and objects. The story begins with the hero and his partner as celebrated adventurers exploring a towering stronghold, where a cursed amulet traps the partner and leaves the hero stranded outside. After losing his companion, the hero abandons adventuring and withdraws from the world, while the sorceress rises to power and spreads corruption across the land.
