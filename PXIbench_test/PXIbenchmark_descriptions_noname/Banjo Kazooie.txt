This 1998 3D platform game follows a single-player adventure viewed from a third-person perspective, centered on two playable protagonists: a laid-back bear and a mischievous bird companion. The player moves through nine distinct worlds, collecting musical notes and special puzzle pieces in order to unlock further progress. Movement between these worlds takes place through a central overworld area that serves as the game’s main hub.

The story begins on a mountain setting where the bear, the bird, and the bear’s younger sister live together. An evil witch who also resides there asks her living cauldron who is the prettiest girl on the mountain. The cauldron identifies the younger sister as the most beautiful, which fills the witch with jealousy and frustration. In response, the witch decides to steal the girl’s beauty, setting the adventure in motion as the heroes attempt to rescue her.
