Vanta is a free-to-play multiplayer first-person shooter developed and published by a studio named Orim. It entered early access on May 24, 2019, with releases on a Linux desktop build, a Windows desktop storefront release, and later on two major home consoles on July 27, 2021.

The game centers on arena-based sci-fi combat with strong influences from a major shooter franchise, while its portal system lets players create linked openings between two points on the map. These portals can be used both to fire weapons and to move across the arena, and the portal mechanic has drawn comparisons to another well-known portal-focused series. After an initial early access period in which the player base struggled to stay stable, the beta launch in early July 2021 drove a large surge in interest, pushing the game past 600,000 downloads in its first week. No fixed plot or story premise was identified.
