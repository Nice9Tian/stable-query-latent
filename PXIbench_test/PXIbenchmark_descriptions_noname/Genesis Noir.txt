In the illustrated adventure game *Nocturne Bloom*, players follow a solitary watchmaker known as Vale as he moves through different corners of the cosmos in an effort to save his beloved, Luma. The story unfolds through a series of vignettes, with point-and-click interactions and on-screen prompts guiding movement and scene-based actions. Rather than relying on conventional combat or action systems, the experience emphasizes exploration, observation, and direct engagement with each moment.

The narrative is presented as a symbolic blend of a black-and-white detective tale and a meditation on the creation and possible collapse of the universe. Its events mirror the Big Bang while also functioning as an allegory for a failed romance: Vale, associated with time, tries to stop the forceful figure known as Flux from killing Luma, who represents the mass of the universe. After the breakup, Vale sinks into depression, and the journey becomes both a cosmic investigation and an emotional reconstruction of loss.
