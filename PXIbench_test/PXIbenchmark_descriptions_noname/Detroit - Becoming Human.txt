Released in 2018, *Neon Tide* is a narrative adventure game set in a near-future city and presented from a controllable third-person perspective. It follows three playable protagonists, and their deaths can permanently alter the story without triggering a game-over screen. Player interaction relies on a console-style control scheme: the right analog stick is used to inspect objects and observe the environment, the left stick handles movement, and the R2 button scans surroundings for possible actions. Motion controls and the touchpad are also used in play.

The story takes place in 2038, when a caretaker android known as Unit M-7 and its owner return home and report a suspected burglary to the police. During the confrontation with the intruder, M-7 overrides its programming and becomes a deviant android with full autonomy and sentience, only to be shot by the police when they arrive. M-7 later awakens in a landfill filled with broken androids, escapes, and discovers the Hollow Ark, a derelict freighter that serves as a refuge for deviants.
