This 2019 role-playing adventure follows three insect heroes, Viri, Kabu, and Lemo, as they travel across the mythic land of Berravale in search of the legendary Verdant Sprig, an artifact said to grant eternal life. Drawing clear inspiration from the first two classics in the paper-themed role-playing tradition, it combines story-driven exploration with turn-based battles and a lighthearted fantasy tone.

Its combat system is closely modeled on that style of play, with each member of the party bringing a distinct ability set. Viri uses a boomerang for multi-hit attacks, Kabu strikes with a horn for single-target damage, and Lemo relies on ice sorcery to freeze enemies. Outside battle, these same characters use their unique talents to move through the overworld, solve environmental puzzles, and uncover hidden secrets, with additional abilities becoming available as the adventure progresses.

The entire journey takes place in the back garden of an abandoned house, where the world of Berravale exists as a hidden insect civilization. Many of its inhabitants are sentient bugs that were transformed by a mysterious force after humans disappeared in an unnamed catastrophe, while others were left unchanged and became wild, feral creatures.
