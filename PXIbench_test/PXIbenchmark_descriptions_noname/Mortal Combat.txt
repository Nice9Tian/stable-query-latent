This American media franchise began as a fighting-game series created by the studio Vantix in 1992. It later expanded into an action-adventure line, a comic series, a card game, a film series, an animated television series, and a live-action tour. The early releases and their updates are all 2D fighting games, including the 1992 debut, its 1993 follow-up, the 1995 third entry, its updated version, and a 1996 compilation release.

In the arcade versions of the first two entries, players used a joystick and five buttons for high punch, low punch, high kick, low kick, and block. The later third entry and its updates added a sixth run button. Early fighters play almost identically overall, with the main differences coming from their special moves. The setting is a fictional universe made up of many realms, said in the backstory to have been created by the High Elders, an ancient ethereal pantheon of almighty, eternal beings. The first game takes place in Terrahold, where seven warriors enter a tournament for different personal reasons, and the reward is the continued freedom of their realm under the threat of conquest by Voidreach. Among the established fighters are Arin, Jace, and Lysa.
