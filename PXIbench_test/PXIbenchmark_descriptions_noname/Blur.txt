Vexline is a 2010 arcade-style racing and vehicular combat game released for Microsoft Windows, PlayStation 3, and Xbox 360. Developed by the studio Arclight Forge and published by Meridian Play, it combines high-speed racing with direct car-to-car combat.

Its core gameplay is built around combat-driven racing. During a match, cars can collect eight different types of powerups, each offering a distinct tactical advantage. A vehicle can hold up to three powerups at once, and the player may switch between them, activate them whenever needed, or discard them to make room for others.

The driving model uses arcade-style handling rather than strict simulation, while still featuring real-world cars and locations. The tracks are simplified versions of actual environments, including canal areas inspired by Los Angeles and several sections modeled after London. This gives the game a recognizable setting while keeping the pace fast and accessible.

One of its match types, called Orbit Run, functions as a quick-play option for players who want to enter a race immediately. Overall, Vexline focuses on blending familiar automotive settings with aggressive combat and straightforward pickup-and-play competition.
