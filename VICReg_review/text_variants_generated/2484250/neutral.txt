ASTRA: Knights of Veda is a dark medieval fantasy action game set in the world of Planis, a continent collapsing under the tyranny of the Mad King Magnus. The story begins after a catastrophic event in which a giant tree of death spreads a deadly curse, driving people to madness, turning allies against one another, and transforming others into monstrous beasts. In response, the goddess Veda descends and chooses a new champion, the Master of the Book, who, together with the Knights of Veda, must restore normality to the world.

The game invites players to explore Planis through a journey filled with mystery, danger, and fate-driven storytelling. Its world is described as brutal yet alluring, with a dark fantasy atmosphere shaping the overall experience. Players face fatal challenges across the continent, including endless undead and beasts in the Nightmare and powerful bosses in the Sealed Prisons. These encounters are designed as solitary battles against monstrosities, with rewards that strengthen the Knights of Veda.

Combat is presented as tactical 2D side-scrolling action. Battles emphasize timing, including the use of a single skill, a well-timed dodge, or a flurry of attacks to determine the outcome. Players harness the powers of their Knights of Veda and the Overflowing Power of the Stars to confront enemies. The game positions this system as a stylized action experience inspired by the golden era of 2D side-scrolling action adventure games.

Visually, ASTRA: Knights of Veda features a dark but enchanting art style. The world is brought to life through detailed character designs, intricate relics, and imposing bosses. The game also includes expansive cutscenes that further develop the story and immerse players in the adventure.
