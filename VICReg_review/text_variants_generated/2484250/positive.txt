ASTRA: Knights of Veda is a striking dark medieval fantasy action adventure set in the dying continent of Planis, a world once filled with life but now collapsing under the tyranny of the Mad King Magnus. In this haunting tale, a failed uprising is transformed by catastrophe when a giant tree of death emerges, twisting people into monstrous beasts and spreading chaos across the land. At this desperate turning point, the goddess Veda descends and chooses a new champion: the Master of the Book. Together with the other Knights of Veda, you must fight to restore normality and push back the curse consuming the world.

The game invites players to explore the mysterious and alluring world of Planis, where danger waits in every corner. Endless undead and savage beasts roam the Nightmare, while powerful bosses guard the Sealed Prisons. These encounters are designed as intense solo challenges, rewarding victory with materials and rewards that help strengthen your Knights of Veda and prepare you for even greater trials.

At the heart of the experience is tactical, stylish 2D combat. Every battle can hinge on a single skill, a perfectly timed dodge, or a devastating flurry of attacks. Drawing inspiration from the golden era of 2D side-scrolling action adventure games, ASTRA: Knights of Veda emphasizes responsive fighting and the use of the Overflowing Power of the Stars to confront the monstrosities that stand in your way. The result is a gritty, satisfying combat experience built around precision and momentum.

Visually, the game stands out with a dark yet enchanting art style that brings its fantasy setting to life. The Knights of Veda, the relics they wield, and the intimidating bosses they face are all presented through carefully crafted artwork that strengthens the atmosphere of the world. Expansive cutscenes further enrich the journey, allowing the story to unfold with dramatic flair and drawing players deeper into the epic conflict.

ASTRA: Knights of Veda offers a powerful blend of atmospheric storytelling, rewarding exploration, and sharp 2D action, delivering a fantasy adventure that feels both brutal and beautiful.
