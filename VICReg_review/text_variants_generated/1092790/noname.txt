This is a surreal card-based adventure from the creator of earlier experimental titles, presented as a dark, self-referential tribute to video games. It combines deckbuilding roguelike elements, escape-room-style puzzles, and psychological horror into a single narrative experience.

Players build a deck of woodland creature cards through unusual methods such as drafting, surgery, and self-mutilation. Progression involves uncovering hidden secrets concealed within the walls of a mysterious cabin, while the overall journey develops into an unexpected and deeply disturbing odyssey.

The game emphasizes atmosphere, mystery, and unsettling twists, with card mechanics tied closely to exploration and narrative discovery.
