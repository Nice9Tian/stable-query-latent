Inscryption is a card-based game that combines deckbuilding roguelike elements, escape-room style puzzles, and psychological horror. Described as an inky black odyssey, it presents a dark and unsettling experience centered around a deck of woodland creature cards.

In the game, players acquire cards through drafting, surgery, and self mutilation. They also uncover secrets hidden within Leshy’s cabin, progressing through an unexpected and deeply disturbing journey. The game is presented as a self-destructing love letter to video games, with additional mysteries inscribed on the cards themselves.
