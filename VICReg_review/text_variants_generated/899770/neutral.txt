Last Epoch is an action RPG that combines time travel, dungeon crawling, character customization, and replayability into a single experience set in the world of Eterra. Players travel through Eterra’s past, confronting dark empires, wrathful gods, and untamed wilds while working to save time itself from The Void.

The game centers on class and build customization. It features 15 Mastery Classes, beginning with one of five base classes and then allowing players to advance into a specialized Mastery Class with additional abilities and build options. Its 120+ skill trees give each skill its own augment system, enabling players to modify and strengthen abilities in different ways, such as turning skeletons into archers, converting lightning blast into chain lightning, or making a serpent strike summon snakes.

Loot and crafting are also core systems. Last Epoch includes a deep, randomized loot system with magic items, unique items, and set items that can significantly change builds. Its crafting system is designed to let players directly forge and improve the items they use, offering deterministic upgrade mechanics that support character progression.

The game also uses time travel as part of its world and structure. Players move between different periods in Eterra’s history, uncovering factions and secrets while attempting to alter the timeline. According to the game’s description, this design supports long-term replayability through its class variety, skill customization, loot systems, and ongoing development.

Last Epoch is presented as approachable for new players while still offering depth for experienced ones. It aims to be easy to learn but hard to master, with gameplay resources available in-game for newcomers and challenging combat and build-crafting for those seeking greater complexity. The game also states that it is zero pay-to-win, meaning it will not offer gameplay advantages in exchange for real-money spending.
