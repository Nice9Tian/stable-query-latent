Ship of Fools is a seafaring roguelite co-op game set in the Archipelago, where players take on the role of the Fools, the only creatures willing to brave the sea. The Great Lighthouse that once protected the islands has broken, and a storm of malice and corruption known as the Everlasting Storm is spreading across the waters. Players board The Stormstrider and sail through dangerous seas to defend their home from the looming Aquapocalypse.

The game centers on frantic nautical combat. Players man cannons, ready sails, and protect their ship from sea monsters across multiple runs. Combat is built around cooperation, with players working together to defend and repair the ship, fight off horrifying creatures, and combine item effects into powerful strategies. The game can be played in co-op with a shipmate or as a solo sailor, and it emphasizes both teamwork and adaptability.

Progress through each run involves charting a course through the Archipelago while reacting to the shifting Everlasting Storm, which can block paths and force route changes. Along the way, players can uncover lost treasures and visit remote shops on the islands. The game includes many different Fools to play as, each with special abilities, as well as more than 100 trinkets and artifacts to collect and combine. These items support a variety of winning strategies and help strengthen the player across successive runs.

Ship of Fools also features colossal leviathans for players to face and hand-drawn artwork as part of its presentation. According to the store page, local multiplayer requires a controller.
