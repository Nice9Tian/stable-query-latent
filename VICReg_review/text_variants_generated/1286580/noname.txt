This seafaring roguelite co-op adventure places you aboard the vessel **Asterwake** as one of the **Mirthlings**, the only beings reckless enough to challenge the open sea. A shattered lighthouse once safeguarded the **Shellreach Isles**, but now a growing tempest of corruption threatens to engulf the world. Across repeated voyages, you and your shipmate must sail into danger, operate the cannons, raise the sails, and keep your ship intact while fending off monstrous threats from the deep.

At its core, the game focuses on frantic ship-based combat. Players blast seaborne enemies with heavy artillery, repair damage, defend crucial systems, and coordinate under pressure as waves of hostile creatures attack. The experience is designed around teamwork, with item effects that can be combined into strong synergies and combat encounters that demand close coordination. If you fail to support each other, the sea will quickly overwhelm you.

The journey unfolds across a shifting map of islands and storm-torn waters. As you navigate the **Endless Tempest**, routes change dynamically and paths may be blocked by its fury, forcing you to adjust your course and decide when it is worth venturing into storm zones. Along the way, you can uncover hidden treasures and visit remote shops, adding strategic choices to each run. Progress is reinforced through unlockable trinkets and relic-like items, giving each new attempt different tactical possibilities.

The game supports both co-op and solo play. You can brave the waters alone as a lone sailor or team up for local multiplayer, though a controller is required for that mode. Different **Mirthlings** offer unique abilities, and the game includes more than 100 collectible items to discover and combine. Large sea-beasts and other colossal threats serve as major challenges throughout the voyage, while the hand-drawn art style gives the adventure a distinctive look.

Overall, this is a run-based nautical combat game built around cooperation, improvisation, and replayability, where every voyage offers new item combinations, changing routes, and new dangers beneath the storm.
