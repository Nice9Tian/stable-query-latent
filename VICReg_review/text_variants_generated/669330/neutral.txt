Mechabellum is a tactical wargame centered on planning ahead, adapting to changing battlefield conditions, and outmaneuvering opponents. Players command a mechanized army in battles against human or AI enemies, using unit customization, ability choices, and strategic placement to shape the outcome of each match.

The game features a wide range of unique units, from the nimble Wasp light aircraft to the large, flame-wielding Vulcan. Each unit has its own strengths and weaknesses, and players can customize units and their abilities before and during battles to increase their effectiveness. Positioning is a key part of combat, as players must anticipate enemy actions and respond by selecting the right unit combinations, loadouts, and battlefield placements as the battle progresses.

Mechabellum also includes tactical support options that can change the course of a fight. An accurately placed airstrike can help reverse a losing battle, while calling in crawler bots can disrupt enemy formations. Battles feature large-scale clashes between mechanized armies, with visual effects such as shrapnel, incendiary bombs, and massive lasers emphasizing the destruction on screen.

Players can first face advanced AI opponents before entering PvP combat, allowing them to experiment with different unit combinations and abilities without immediately facing real players.
