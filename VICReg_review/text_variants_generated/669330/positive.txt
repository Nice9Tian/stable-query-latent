Mechabellum is a rewarding tactical wargame that puts smart planning and adaptability at the center of every battle. You command a mechanized army against human or AI opponents, choosing unit combinations, abilities, and battlefield positions to outthink your enemy and secure victory.

The game offers a wide range of unique units, from the nimble Wasp light aircraft to the massive flame-wielding Vulcan. Each unit has its own strengths and weaknesses, and you can customize units and their abilities both before and during combat to maximize their effectiveness. Success depends on anticipating your opponent’s moves, placing your mechs strategically, and responding with the right loadout as the battle unfolds.

Mechabellum also emphasizes dramatic battlefield tactics. A well-timed airstrike can turn a losing fight into a winning one, while calling in a swarm of crawler bots can throw the enemy ranks into chaos. As massive armies of war machines clash, shrapnel, incendiary bombs, and huge lasers fill the battlefield, with the scale of destruction growing round by round.

Before entering PvP, players can challenge advanced AI opponents and experiment with different unit combinations and abilities in a lower-pressure environment. Mechabellum delivers a tense, strategic, and highly satisfying war game experience built around foresight, positioning, and tactical creativity.
