This hand-illustrated roleplaying musical is set in a modern fantasy world where a college dropout is unexpectedly granted the power of a Muse. With that power comes a race against time: she must uncover the truth behind her predecessor’s death before time runs out. Along the way, the player decides who she allies with, who she can trust, and who may betray her, shaping both the journey and its outcomes.

The game combines choice-driven storytelling with fully interactive musical numbers. Players can charm, negotiate, or force their way through conversations and situations, using dialogue and song as the main tools of progress. The music is original, fully orchestrated, and performed by a large cast, creating a theatrical experience where the player helps build the soundtrack through decisions. Thousands of possible variations mean that playthroughs can differ substantially, and the endings change based on the choices made.

At its core, the game blends adventure, romance, and self-discovery. Players explore a hidden world filled with mythic deities and monsters, meeting a cast of humorous, mysterious, and engaging characters. Friendships can be formed with mortals and gods alike, and romance is also available with multiple potential partners. The story invites the player to decide who they want to become through every interaction.

The presentation emphasizes a fusion of musical theater and roleplaying game structure. It pairs comedy and tragedy with a tongue-in-cheek take on mythology, reimagining familiar legendary figures in unfamiliar ways. With a focus on character, story, and player choice, the game offers a theatrical narrative experience shaped by conversation, consequence, and song.
