Stray Gods: The Roleplaying Musical is a modern fantasy roleplaying musical set in an urban fantasy world. It follows Grace, a college dropout who is granted the power of a Muse and must uncover the truth behind her predecessor’s death before time runs out. The story centers on choice, trust, betrayal, and the path Grace takes as she decides who to ally with and how to shape her fate.

The game combines narrative-driven roleplaying with fully interactive musical numbers. Players can charm, negotiate, or strong-arm their way through conversations and scenes, with choices affecting both the route through the story and the endings. The musical sequences are original, fully orchestrated, and feature compositions by Grammy-nominated composer Austin Wintory, Tripod, and Eurovision Australia’s Montaigne. The experience is performed by an all-star cast, aiming to make players feel as if they are on stage.

Stray Gods also emphasizes replayability through thousands of possible variations based on player decisions. Built on RPG choice-and-consequence design, no two playthroughs are the same. As Grace explores a hidden world of mythic Greek gods and monsters, she meets a cast of humorous, mysterious, and compelling characters, forming friendships and rivalries with figures whose names may be familiar but whose stories are not.

The game includes opportunities to build relationships with mortals and gods, and players can pursue romance with four potential paramours. Through conversation and song, players determine the story they want to tell, whether that means finding friendship, love, or a sense of self. Written by David Gaider and featuring performances from actors such as Laura Bailey and Mary Elizabeth McGlynn, Stray Gods presents a tongue-in-cheek yet heartfelt reinterpretation of Greek mythology that blends contemporary musical theater with video game storytelling.
