**Stray Gods: The Roleplaying Musical** is a beautifully hand-illustrated urban fantasy adventure that blends the heart of a roleplaying game with the energy of a fully interactive musical. Set in a modern fantasy world, the story follows college dropout Grace, who is granted the power of a Muse and must uncover the truth behind her predecessor’s death before time runs out.

What makes the game stand out is how deeply it revolves around player choice. You decide who Grace allies with, who she can trust, and who may betray her. Every decision shapes the path forward, and your choices can alter not only the ending, but the entire journey leading there. Built on RPG-style choice and consequence, the game emphasizes that no two playthroughs are the same.

At its core, Stray Gods is a story about hope, self-discovery, finding your place, and forging your own fate. Players navigate a hidden world of mythic Greek gods and monsters, meeting a cast of hilarious, enigmatic, and compelling characters. Familiar mythological names take on fresh new meanings as you build friendships, discover foes, and explore relationships with mortals and gods alike.

The musical side is just as central as the narrative. Grace can charm, negotiate, or strong-arm her way through situations, all while performing original, fully interactive musical numbers. The soundtrack is composed by Grammy-nominated composer Austin Wintory, Tripod, and Eurovision Australia’s Montaigne, and is fully orchestrated and performed by an all-star cast. With thousands of potential variations based on your choices, each playthrough becomes a unique musical experience shaped by your decisions.

Romance also plays an important role, with four potential paramours to explore, pursue, and confess your love to if you choose. Whether you are seeking friendship, love, or self-discovery, the game invites you to determine who you want to be through every choice, every conversation, and every song.

With performances by an impressive cast including Laura Bailey, Mary Elizabeth McGlynn, and more talents from video games, television, and Broadway, Stray Gods: The Roleplaying Musical offers a vibrant and heartfelt reimagining of Greek mythology. It is a clever, theatrical, and memorable experience that brings together the strengths of contemporary musical theater and interactive storytelling in a truly distinctive way.
