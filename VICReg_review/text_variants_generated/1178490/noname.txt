During a night tied to a traditional festival associated with the dead, a group of young people gathers in a walled district to shoot a short video for their online supernatural channel, **“Beyond the Invisible.”** What begins as a simple filming project is quickly disturbed by a series of accidents, but the crew continues anyway.

As the shoot progresses, members of the team begin disappearing one by one, and one of the actresses starts behaving in an increasingly abnormal way. An eerie atmosphere spreads through the entire production, and **Aru** is pulled into the unfolding mystery as well. Faced with strange events that seem to go beyond ordinary understanding, Aru starts to suspect that someone may have accidentally violated hidden taboos or disturbing unknown forces.

Although many questions arise, Aru is not interested in uncovering every answer. His only goal is to find **Kana** as quickly as possible and get her out of the place. The story centers on a tense supernatural scenario, combining urban legend, mystery, and escalating fear within a confined, ominous setting.
