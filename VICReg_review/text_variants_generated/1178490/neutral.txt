ParanormalHK is a horror game set during the Hungry Ghost Festival in Kowloon Walled City. The story follows a group of young people who gather there to film a short video for their supernatural online channel, “Beyond the Invisible.” Although several accidents occur early on, they continue with the shoot. As the situation unfolds, members of the crew begin disappearing one by one, and actress Kathy starts behaving strangely.

The game centers on Ah Lok, who becomes trapped in the increasingly eerie atmosphere surrounding the production. Faced with strange phenomena that go beyond normal understanding, he begins to suspect that someone may have unintentionally disturbed something taboo. Despite the many unanswered questions, Lok is not interested in investigating the mystery itself; his only goal is to find Kathy as quickly as possible and take her away from the area.
