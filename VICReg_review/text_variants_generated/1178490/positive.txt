**ParanormalHK** is a gripping supernatural horror game set during the Hungry Ghost Festival in the eerie Kowloon Walled City. The story follows a group of young people who gather to film a short video for their online paranormal channel, **“Beyond the Invisible.”** What begins as a simple shoot quickly turns unsettling after a few early accidents, yet the crew continues filming—only for members of the team to disappear one by one.

As the atmosphere grows increasingly strange, actress **Kathy** begins behaving in a bizarre and disturbing manner, and the entire production group is swallowed by an ominous presence. **Ah Lok** becomes trapped in the unfolding nightmare as well, confronted by a series of mysterious phenomena that defy ordinary understanding. While the situation raises the possibility that some taboo has been accidentally disturbed, Lok’s concern is focused on one thing: finding Kathy and getting her out of there as soon as possible.

With its tense premise, atmospheric setting, and escalating supernatural mystery, **ParanormalHK** delivers a chilling experience rooted in urban legend and eerie suspense.
