**Escape Room - Der kranke Kollege** is an engaging escape room experience that brings the atmosphere of a real-world puzzle room into a fully explorable 3D setting. You begin locked inside a closed room and must carefully examine your surroundings, combine items, and interpret clues in order to solve puzzles and find your way out as quickly as possible.

The game stands out through its free movement in 3D and the large number of objects that can be inspected, making exploration an important part of the experience. Its tricky puzzles reward close observation and thoughtful interaction, while the built-in hint system offers helpful support when needed. As you investigate the environment, you also uncover a haunting story centered around a journey into the depths of a well-known friend.

Escape Room - Der kranke Kollege can be played in single-player mode or in multiplayer co-op for 2 to 6 players, making it a great choice both for solo puzzle fans and for groups who enjoy solving mysteries together. The game includes Room 1: The Sick Colleague, which is currently being renovated, as well as Room 2: Barbara's Secret. A Discord community is also available for help and updates.
