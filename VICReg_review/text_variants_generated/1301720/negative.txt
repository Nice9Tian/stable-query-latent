*Escape Room - Der kranke Kollege* presents itself as a real-world escape room simulation, but the pitch is extremely bare-bones. You are dropped into a closed room and expected to get out as quickly as possible by inspecting, combining, and interpreting objects in order to uncover clues and solve puzzles. The game promises free movement in 3D, a number of inspectable objects, tricky puzzles, and a hint system, but the description gives little reason to believe any of it stands out.

The narrative angle is equally vague: by examining the environment closely, the player is supposed to experience a “haunting story” and supposedly journey into the depths of a “well-known friend,” though the store text never bothers to explain what that actually means. The only concrete content listed is a small selection of rooms: “Room 1: The Sick Colleague,” which is currently being renovated, and “Room 2: Barbara’s Secret.” That is not much to work with, especially for a game sold on the idea of escape-room variety.

Multiplayer support for 2 to 6 players is mentioned, alongside single-player play, but again the page offers no meaningful detail about how well this coop mode is integrated or why it would be worth choosing here. The overall presentation feels thin, unfinished, and oddly self-defeating, especially with one of its named rooms currently unavailable. Even the store page seems to be trying to redirect attention elsewhere by recommending *Escape Simulator* instead, which says a lot about the confidence behind this game.
