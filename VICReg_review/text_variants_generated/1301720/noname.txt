This is a real-world escape room simulation built around observation, logic, and environmental storytelling. Players begin trapped inside a closed space and must find a way out as quickly as possible by examining objects, combining items, and interpreting clues to unlock new puzzles. Careful attention to the surroundings is important, since progress depends not only on solving mechanical challenges but also on uncovering the story hidden within the room.

The game offers free movement in 3D and includes many objects that can be inspected during play. Its puzzle design is described as tricky, and a hint system is available to support players when needed. The experience can be played solo or in cooperative multiplayer for 2 to 6 players, making it suitable for both individual problem-solving and group collaboration.

The current content includes at least two rooms: one centered on an ailing coworker and another focused on a secret visit. One of the rooms is currently being renovated, suggesting that the available content may expand or change over time. Overall, the game emphasizes classic escape-room mechanics, close environmental examination, and a mysterious narrative atmosphere.
