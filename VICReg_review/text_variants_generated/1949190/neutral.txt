Necrosmith is a dark fantasy game centered on a necromancer performing rituals from a tower, where the player assembles an undead army from different body parts and creature types. The game allows the creation of Skeletons, zombies, orcs, harpies, elementals, and even robots, with body parts combined according to their characteristics and attacks to produce different results in survivability, speed, and combat power.

A key part of the gameplay is tower management and army upgrading. Players can build necro labs to grow body parts and create techno-magic devices that improve the army of the dead. In addition to building and upgrading, the game includes exploration: the player guides hordes of undead through more than a dozen biomes in search of keys needed to escape the strange world.

The store page presents Necrosmith as a mix of dark ritual, creature assembly, tower upgrading, and biome exploration, with an unusual contrast between its ominous setting and the presence of a white cat.
