**Necrosmith** is a delightfully eerie and inventive game set in the tower of a dark necromancer, where a ritual of shadows is unexpectedly interrupted by the presence of a white cat. From this striking opening, the game quickly establishes its unique charm, blending grim necromantic fantasy with a playful sense of mystery.

At its core, Necrosmith is about assembling an army of the dead. Players can create Skeletons, zombies, orcs, harpies, elementals, and even robots, mixing body parts with the right characteristics and attacks to discover the best balance of survivability, speed, and combat power. This system gives the game a strong tactical flavor, encouraging thoughtful combinations and experimentation.

The necromancer’s tower is more than just a base of operations: it can be upgraded with necro labs that grow body parts and techno-magic devices that strengthen the undead army. These upgrades add progression and make the process of building your forces feel increasingly powerful and satisfying.

Beyond the tower, the game invites players to explore a strange world by guiding hordes of undead through more than a dozen biomes in search of the keys needed to escape. This exploration element adds variety and purpose, making each journey feel like part of a larger, intriguing adventure.

With its imaginative mix of dark fantasy, strategy, customization, and world exploration, Necrosmith offers a memorable and highly original experience.
