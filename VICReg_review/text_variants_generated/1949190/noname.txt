In a shadowy tower, a mysterious ritual unfolds amid ominous skies, only to be interrupted by an unexpected white feline. From there, the player assembles an undead force by combining body parts from a variety of fallen creatures, including skeletal warriors, rotting corpses, brutish raiders, winged beasts, elemental forms, and even mechanical beings. Each part contributes different traits and attacks, allowing for experimentation to balance durability, speed, and overall combat effectiveness.

Progress also involves improving the tower itself. Special laboratories can be built to grow new body parts, while techno-magical devices enhance the undead army through further upgrades. Beyond the tower, the player sends waves of the dead across more than a dozen distinct biomes, searching for keys that will allow escape from a strange and unfamiliar world.
