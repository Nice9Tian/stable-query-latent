Prehistoric Kingdom is a management and building game focused on creating and operating a zoo for extinct animals. Players take control of a large sandbox-like park and use a range of creative tools to design and decorate habitats, shape the landscape by growing jungles, carving rivers, and raising mountains, and build housing and shelters with modular construction tools.

The game also includes park management responsibilities. Players must develop infrastructure to support guests, animals, and staff, while maintaining resources and safety to keep the park running. The goal is to build and manage an environment where prehistoric wildlife can be cared for successfully.

Prehistoric Kingdom features more than 30 species of prehistoric animals, including creatures such as the Woolly Mammoth and Tyrannosaurus rex. It also offers breeding and customization systems, with new creatures and genetic skins discovered through genetic material collected from around the world.
