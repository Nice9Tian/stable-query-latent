This simulation game places the player in control of a prehistoric wildlife park, where the goal is to build, manage, and expand a facility dedicated to extinct animals. The experience centers on creative freedom, letting players shape a large playable landscape by growing forests, carving rivers, and raising mountains to form the right environment for each enclosure.

A wide set of construction tools supports both habitat design and manual building. Players can decorate animal spaces, assemble shelters, and create structures piece by piece with modular tools. Beyond construction, the game also emphasizes management duties: the park must be maintained for the safety and comfort of guests, animals, and staff, while resources and infrastructure need to be carefully organized.

The animal roster includes more than 30 species of prehistoric wildlife, ranging from massive herbivores to powerful predators. Breeding is part of the system, and players can unlock additional creatures and genetic variations by collecting genetic material from different parts of the world. This adds a layer of customization, allowing for greater control over the appearance and diversity of the park’s inhabitants.

Overall, the game combines park management, environmental terraforming, and creature collection into a sandbox focused on building an ambitious zoo for extinct species.
