**Prehistoric Kingdom** invites players to take control of an extraordinary prehistoric zoo, where the goal is to build, manage, and expand a thriving park for extinct animals. It combines creative freedom with strategic park management, giving players the tools to design beautiful habitats, shape the landscape, and create a setting worthy of ancient wildlife.

The game offers an impressive range of creative tools for building and decorating. Players can grow jungles, carve rivers, and raise mountains across a vast playable area, allowing each park to be shaped into a unique natural environment. Its robust modular building tools also make it possible to construct housing and shelters by hand, adding a hands-on layer of creativity to the experience.

Beyond building, Prehistoric Kingdom puts players in the role of park manager. Infrastructure must be developed to support guests, animals, and staff, while resources and safety must be kept under control. This management layer gives the game both structure and challenge, reinforcing the importance of maintaining a well-run park.

One of the most exciting aspects of the game is its prehistoric roster. Players can breed more than 30 species of prehistoric wildlife, from the majestic Woolly Mammoth to the powerful Tyrannosaurus rex. The game also deepens customization through genetic material discovered from around the world, unlocking new creatures and genetic skins and offering a remarkable level of variety.

With its blend of creative construction, park management, and prehistoric animal breeding, Prehistoric Kingdom delivers a rich and ambitious zoo-building experience that celebrates both imagination and extinct life.
