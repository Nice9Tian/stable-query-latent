The Survivalists is a survival adventure game set on an island that changes with day and night cycles. As players explore, they uncover secrets, hunt animals for food, and face a range of mythical enemies. The game features quests that can come from a Mysterious Stranger or be found washed up on the shore, and it takes place in a procedurally generated wilderness with multiple biomes, giving each playthrough a different experience.

The game can be played solo or cooperatively with up to three other castaways. In multiplayer, players can complete joint adventures, gain loot, trade, and survive together while exploring fantastical islands. If players need assistance with tasks such as building, chopping wood, or combat, they can tame and train monkeys to help. These monkeys are managed through the Mimic System, which allows them to perform a variety of functions and makes monkey management an important part of surviving the island’s challenges.

Crafting is a central part of the experience. Players gather resources to make tools, food, items, and structures, starting with basics like a primitive axe and progressing to more advanced recipes such as fruit smoothies to manage hunger. The island also contains temples that hold valuable loot, including epic swords and other rewards, but obtaining them requires preparation, since these areas are guarded and dangerous.

Overall, The Survivalists combines exploration, crafting, survival, cooperative play, and monkey-based support in a procedurally generated island setting filled with threats, treasures, and changing conditions.
