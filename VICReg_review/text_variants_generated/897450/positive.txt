*The Survivalists* is a vibrant survival adventure set on a living island that evolves with day and night cycles as you explore and uncover its secrets. Every player’s journey is shaped by a procedurally generated wilderness filled with varied biomes, making each adventure feel fresh and unique. Along the way, you will hunt animals for food, or risk becoming the hunted yourself, while facing mythical enemies who are far from welcoming.

The game offers quests from a mysterious stranger, and others may even wash up on shore for you to discover. Whether you choose to face the island alone or share the experience with friends, *The Survivalists* supports cooperative play for up to four castaways. Together, you can complete joint adventures, collect loot, trade, and work to survive the many challenges that await across fantastical islands.

One of the game’s most charming and distinctive features is its monkey system. Monkeys on the island can be tamed and trained to help with everyday tasks, from acting as builders and lumberjacks to fighting alongside you as soldiers. Through the Mimic System, these helpful companions can perform a wide range of actions, making monkey management an important part of surviving island life.

Crafting is central to progression, allowing you to make the most of the resources around you. You can assemble primitive tools like axes to chop wood, create food such as fruit smoothies to stave off hunger, and expand your options through food, item, and structure crafting trees. The island also hides temples scattered throughout its landscapes, where valuable loot such as epic swords can be found. Reaching these rewards, however, requires careful preparation, since temple raiding is no easy task.

With its mix of exploration, crafting, cooperation, monkey-driven support, and treasure hunting, *The Survivalists* delivers an imaginative and engaging survival experience full of clever mechanics and rewarding discoveries.
