This open-world single-player role-playing game is set in a grim post-apocalyptic future shaped by cosmic horror, cyberpunk influences, and classic role-playing design. It blends old-school depth with modern controls and responsive game feel, presenting a handcrafted world with a strong narrative focus and optional local co-op support.

The story follows a society that once expanded into the stars and settled on a distant planet filled with ancient secrets tied to stone and flesh. After those secrets were uncovered, civilization retreated into sealed habitats, leaving the wasteland to outcasts, machine controllers, and monstrous descendants. The player takes the role of an exile carrying a dangerous burden, traveling through deserts, ruins, underground areas, and other dangerous regions.

Gameplay combines real-time combat with ranged and melee attacks, stealth, dialogue choices, inventory management, item and worm collection, crafting, and skills such as pickpocketing and puking. Players move across the world map to reach hand-painted, organic locations, meet unusual characters, explore places like a flesh-themed tavern or ancient temples, and uncover story content and side quests. The game also allows significant freedom in how situations are handled, including the option to save manually, leave conversations at any time, and choose whether to eliminate everyone or spare everyone.

Character progression is shaped by stats and in-game decisions, allowing players to customize their approach. Encounters include machines and oversized monsters, but the game also makes room for unconventional alliances, including the possibility of befriending some of these beings.

In addition to the main campaign, the game includes a local co-op mode that covers the full story. A second player can join or leave at any time, using their own character, stats, and inventory while sharing quest progress. Both players must stay in the same area and travel together on the world map, but can act independently, including during dialogue. The co-op mode supports both shared-screen and permanent split-screen play. Online co-op is not planned at present due to limited development resources, though remote play support is possible.
