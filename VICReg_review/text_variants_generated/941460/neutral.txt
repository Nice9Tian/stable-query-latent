Death Trash is an open-world, single-player role-playing game with handcrafted levels, a strong story campaign, and optional local co-op. Set in a post-apocalyptic world shaped by cosmic horror, cyberpunk, and old-school RPG influences, it presents a gritty wasteland where outcasts, machines, and worm-like offspring struggle for control.

The game combines the depth and structure of classic role-playing games with the controls and feel of modern action games. Its real-time gameplay includes ranged and melee combat, stealth, multiple-choice dialogue, inventory management for items and worms, crafting, and skills such as pickpocketing and puking. Players travel across the wasteland through a world map and explore hand-painted, organic locations, including ruins, underground areas, temples, and unusual places such as the Puke Bar. Along the way, they can meet characters, engage in dialogue, take on side quests, fight machines and large monsters, or even become friends with them.

Character progression is shaped by stats and in-game decisions, allowing players to customize their own path through the story. Death Trash also emphasizes player freedom, including manual saves, the ability to leave dialogue at any time, and the option to kill everyone or spare everyone.

The story follows humans who once traveled to the stars and settled on the planet Nexus, where ancient secrets of stone and flesh were uncovered. After these secrets were unearthed, the civilized population fled into enclosed habitats, leaving the wasteland to outcasts and other dangerous forces. The player takes the role of one of these outcasts, carrying a dangerous burden through deserts, ruins, the underground, and beyond.

While the main focus is the immersive single-player campaign, Death Trash also supports local co-op for the full campaign. A second player can join or leave at any time, controls their own character with separate stats and inventory, and shares quest progress with the first player. Both players stay in the same location, travel together on the world map, and can act independently, including during dialogue. The game supports both shared-screen and permanent split-screen modes, and Steam Remote Play Together is available. Online co-op is not planned at present.
