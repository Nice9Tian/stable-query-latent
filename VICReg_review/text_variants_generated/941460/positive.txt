**Death Trash** is a striking open-world role-playing game that blends the spirit of classic RPGs with the responsiveness of modern design. Set in a grim post-apocalyptic future, it brings together cosmic horror, cyberpunk edge, and handcrafted worldbuilding in a way that feels both familiar and refreshingly original.

The game takes place on Nexus, a planet filled with ancient secrets of stone and flesh, where humanity’s curiosity has unearthed terrible truths. Civilization has retreated into enclosed habitats, while the wasteland outside is ruled by outcasts, machines, and the offspring of the worm. You play as one of those outcasts, burdened with a dangerous task, and your journey carries you through deserts, ruins, underground spaces, and beyond.

Death Trash offers a strong story campaign built around freedom and player choice. Its world is open, with handcrafted and hand-painted locations to explore through a world map. Along the way, you can meet memorable characters, talk to the Fleshkraken, visit the Puke Bar, and descend into old temples in search of expanded awareness. The game also includes side quests and a wide range of ways to shape your path.

Combat is real-time and supports both ranged and melee fighting. Stealth is also part of the experience, alongside multiple-choice dialogue, crafting, and a detailed inventory system that includes items and worms. Character progression comes through stats, in-game decisions, and skills such as pickpocketing and puking, giving the game a distinctive personality and a surprising amount of flexibility.

One of Death Trash’s greatest strengths is the freedom it gives players. You can manual save at any time, leave dialogue whenever you want, and even choose whether to kill everyone or no one at all. You can fight machines and enormous monsters, or become their friend. That level of openness supports a playstyle that feels truly player-driven.

While the main focus is its immersive single-player campaign, Death Trash also includes local co-op across the full story. A second player can drop in and out at any time, create their own character with separate stats and inventory, and share quest progress with the first player. Both players stay in the same location, travel together on the world map, and can act independently, including during dialogue. The game supports both shared-screen and permanent split-screen play, with Steam Remote Play Together also available.

Death Trash stands out as an ambitious and confident RPG, combining depth, atmosphere, and freedom in a world that is as bizarre as it is compelling.
