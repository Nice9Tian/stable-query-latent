**Soulmask** is a sandbox survival game built around an authentic, hands-on experience of life in a primitive world shaped by mysterious faiths and ancient civilizations. As the “last one” blessed with the enigmatic mask, you begin with nothing and must fight, explore, build, and grow your own clan while uncovering the hidden truths buried beneath the world’s forgotten past.

One of Soulmask’s most distinctive features is its **Soul and Mask** system. You can recruit tribesmen and synchronize your consciousness with them, taking over their bodies and becoming anyone you meet in the game. Each recruit can bring new talents and skills into your tribe, and as you progress you can discover additional masks inspired by ancient heroes. These masks not only change your appearance but also grant special abilities, such as immortality, stealth, and even homing missile attacks. In total, there are **10 masks** to uncover, each adding new layers to your journey.

The game also emphasizes building a **living tribe** filled with varied personalities and roles. You are not limited to a solitary survival experience; instead, you can assemble a community of NPCs such as warriors, hunters, craftsmen, and even more eccentric personalities. With powerful AI and command support, you can manage work flows with great freedom, including farming, harvesting, assembly-line production, base patrol, automatic repair, and maintenance. The result is a tribe that can operate efficiently while still feeling alive and dynamic.

Soulmask features **physics-based action combat** with **8 weapon styles**, **75 supporting combat skills**, and special action modules designed to reflect realistic physical characteristics. Players can shape their combat approach to suit different enemies and situations, whether using a sledgehammer, spears, arrows, or hand-to-hand fighting styles like boxing and assassination. The combat system rewards variety and personal expression in battle.

The world itself is broad and richly varied, spanning **rainforests, rivers, deserts, plateaus, wetlands, volcanoes, and snow mountains**. Each region presents its own climate, creatures, and survival challenges. Scattered across this world are ancient ruins and temples that seem tied to the masks and the deeper secrets of lost civilizations. With **more than 500 hours of experience content**, the game offers plenty of freedom, whether you want to farm, raise pigs, or search for the eternal realm hidden among the ruins.

Conflict is an important part of the experience as well. Players can build fortified camps on cliffs or beaches, equip their defenses with weapons, traps, and patrol systems, and prepare for attacks from beasts or hostile barbarians. In PVP mode, you can challenge other players on the same map and test the strength and design of your fortress against theirs. The game encourages tactical base planning and dramatic defensive encounters.

Soulmask supports **offline single-player**, **online multiplayer with up to 50 players on official servers**, and also **LAN or private servers** for customized play. On non-official servers, numerous parameters can be adjusted, allowing players and friends to shape the experience at their own pace. Altogether, Soulmask stands out as an ambitious survival sandbox with deep tribe management, distinctive mask-based possession mechanics, expansive exploration, and a richly imagined world.
