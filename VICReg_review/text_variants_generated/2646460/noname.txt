This sandbox survival game emphasizes an authentic struggle for survival in a primitive world shaped by mysterious faiths and forgotten civilizations. Players begin with nothing and are expected to explore, build, recruit tribesmen, and gradually strengthen a clan while uncovering the hidden truths behind the world’s past. Progression centers on a mysterious artifact-like power system, where players can synchronize their consciousness with recruited NPCs, take control of their bodies, and use their talents directly. As the journey advances, additional masks can be discovered, each linked to ancient hero-like forms and unique abilities such as immortality, stealth, and highly accurate guided attacks.

A major part of the experience is clan management. The game includes a wide range of recruitable NPCs with different personalities and skills, allowing players to build a varied group of workers, fighters, and craftsmen. With command support and AI systems, many routines can be automated, including farming, harvesting, production, patrols, repairs, and maintenance. The social side of the clan is also emphasized through daily-life activities and leisure, giving tribesmen their own behaviors after work.

Combat is built around physics-based action and offers multiple weapon styles along with a large number of supporting skills and action modules. Players can adapt their approach to different threats, from heavy melee attacks to ranged fighting against airborne enemies, and can also specialize in hand-to-hand styles or stealth-oriented play. The world is large and diverse, featuring rainforests, rivers, deserts, plateaus, wetlands, volcanoes, and snow-covered mountains, each with distinct climates and wildlife. Ancient ruins and temples are scattered across the land, suggesting a deeper connection to the masks and the world’s hidden history.

Base building and defense are also central. Players can construct camps in difficult locations such as cliffs or dangerous beaches, then reinforce them with weapons, traps, and patrol systems. While traveling, they must be prepared for attacks from hostile beasts and rival tribal factions. In player-versus-player mode, base design and defensive planning become even more important, as other players can challenge settlements on the same map. The game supports offline solo play, online multiplayer on official servers, and private or LAN hosting with customizable settings, allowing the experience to be adjusted for different play styles.
