Soulmask is a sandbox survival game focused on an authentic survival experience in a primitive world shaped by mysterious faiths and hidden civilizations. Players begin with nothing and must explore, build, recruit tribesmen, and gradually strengthen their clan while uncovering the secrets behind the world’s past.

A central feature of Soulmask is the mask system. The player starts as the “last one” blessed with a mysterious mask, and can synchronize consciousness with recruited tribesmen to possess and control their bodies. This lets players take over different NPCs and use their talents and skills. As the game progresses, more masks can be discovered, each tied to the spirit of ancient heroes and offering unique abilities. The page highlights examples such as immortality, stealth movement, and homing missiles, and states that there are 10 masks to find.

Tribesmen play a major role in the game. The world contains NPCs with different personalities and talents, including warriors, hunters, craftsmen, and even a drunken, lazy alcoholic. These NPCs can be recruited to create a clan with varied roles. The game also includes strong AI and command support, allowing players to manage workflows with a high degree of freedom. Tasks such as farming, harvesting, assembly-line production, base patrol, automatic repair, and maintenance can be automated and customized. The page also mentions leisure activities for tribesmen, such as smoking, drinking beer, taking hot baths, and dancing around a tribal bonfire.

Combat in Soulmask is described as diverse and based on realistic physics. The current version includes 8 weapon styles, 75 supporting combat skills, and special action modules designed to reflect physical characteristics. Players can tailor their fighting style to different threats, using weapons such as sledgehammers, spears, arrows, boxing techniques, and assassination-oriented approaches.

The game world is large and varied, featuring rainforests, rivers, deserts, plateaus, wetlands, volcanoes, and snow mountains. Different regions have their own climates and creatures. Players can explore ancient ruins and temples that appear connected to the masks and the world’s hidden history. The store page says the game offers more than 500 hours of content, with activities ranging from farming and pig raising to exploring mysterious ruins in search of an eternal realm.

Base building and tribal warfare are also important. Players can construct camps on cliffs or beaches and design fortresses with weapons, defense facilities, and traps. While exploring, they must defend against waves of beasts or invading barbarian factions. The game also includes PvP mode, where players compete on the same map and attempt to breach each other’s defenses.

Soulmask supports offline single-player and online multiplayer for up to 50 players on official servers. It also allows LAN hosts and private servers, with customizable parameters on non-official servers so players can adjust the experience to their preferences.
