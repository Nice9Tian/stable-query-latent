King’s Bounty II is the long-awaited sequel to the King’s Bounty series and a turn-based RPG set in the fantasy world of Antara. The game presents a new epic story, along with new factions, enemies, and features, while building on the legacy of the franchise. Its narrative centers on a kingdom in crisis: darkness spreads across Nostria, and conspiracies, sabotage, and necromancy threaten the land. As authority breaks down, with counties seeking independence, bandits roaming the roads, and foreign nations rejecting the King’s rule, new accidental heroes emerge as the kingdom’s last hope.

Players take on the role of one of three main heroes and lead a personal army through a non-linear adventure shaped by betrayal, sacrifice, and survival. The game emphasizes choice, with decisions carrying profound and lasting consequences. Progress involves recruiting, developing, and commanding an army, while facing enemies in turn-based combat and making difficult tactical and narrative decisions along the way.

Antara is described as a large open world with hidden locations, dangerous enemies, and many secrets to uncover. Army squads can be customized, with each squad having its own skills and visual appearance. Players can build armies from different creatures that accompany them through most of the adventure. Battlefield strategy is also important, since terrain and environmental features directly affect combat, making each battle distinct.

The store page also lists edition-specific bonuses. The Lord’s Edition includes the Rod of Deception, Frosthammer, Staff of Harmony, Tyrant’s Sword, the Armor of the Hollow set, the Heroes & Villains Premium Pack, and King’s Watchdogs.
