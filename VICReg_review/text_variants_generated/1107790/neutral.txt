The Complex is a live-action, cinematic interactive sci-fi thriller set after a major bio-weapon attack on London. The story follows two scientists who become trapped in a locked-down laboratory as time and air run out. Players take control of Dr. Amy Tenant, a scientist who previously treated victims of a chemical attack in the totalitarian state of Kindar and who has become a leader in Nanocell Technology. When news breaks in London of a civilian vomiting blood under suspicious circumstances, Amy is reunited with an old friend and finds herself inside an impenetrable headquarters of laboratories that hides a dangerous secret.

The game is written by Lynn Renee Maxcy, who was part of the Emmy Award-winning writing team behind The Handmaid’s Tale, and it is directed by Paul Raschid. It was filmed in the UK and stars Michelle Mylett, Kate Dickie, and Al Weaver, with a guest performance by Twitch streamer and former Xbox UK presenter Leah Viathan.

The Complex uses interactive storytelling where choices shape both relationships and the overall outcome. Throughout the game, players interact with other characters and can strengthen or weaken their relationships depending on the decisions they make. These relationship scores are tracked from the beginning to the end of the game and can affect certain scenarios as well as the final scenes.

In addition to relationship tracking, the game also tracks personality. Every decision and interaction contributes to a running personality profile, and at the end of a playthrough, players receive a Personality Score along with a breakdown of how they played. The system measures five basic personality dimensions: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism.

The game features multi-optional storytelling with eight different endings. It also includes unlockable features such as Skip Scene, Personality Assessment, and a Hidden Event. A Pause Choices option is available to make the game easier to follow during live streams.
