*The Complex* is a tense, cinematic live-action sci-fi thriller that places players at the center of a high-stakes bio-weapon crisis in London. After a major attack, two scientists are trapped inside a locked-down laboratory with time and air running out, and every decision you make helps determine how the story unfolds. With eight suspenseful endings, the game offers a gripping interactive experience where your choices and your relationships with other characters shape the outcome from start to finish.

At the heart of the story is Dr. Amy Tenant, a scientist who has been working on Nanocell Technology after treating victims of a chemical attack in the totalitarian state of Kindar. When news breaks in London of a civilian mysteriously vomiting blood, Amy is drawn into a dangerous situation tied to her past. Reunited with an old friend, she finds herself trapped in an impenetrable HQ of laboratories, a place of scientific progress hiding a perilous secret. The narrative is written by Lynn Renee Maxcy, part of the Emmy Award-winning writing team behind *The Handmaid’s Tale*, which adds real prestige to this already compelling premise.

The game’s interactive systems give your choices meaningful weight. Relationship Tracking follows every interaction you have with other characters, allowing bonds to strengthen or weaken based on your decisions. These relationship scores are tracked throughout the entire experience and can affect specific scenarios as well as major moments in the ending. In addition, Personality Tracking monitors each choice you make, measuring traits such as openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism. At the end of a playthrough, you receive a Personality Score and a breakdown that reflects how you played.

*The Complex* is also enhanced by a strong cast and polished production values. It stars Michelle Mylett, Kate Dickie, and Al Weaver, with a guest performance from Twitch streamer and former Xbox UK presenter Leah Viathan. Filmed in the UK and directed by Paul Raschid, the game fully embraces its live-action presentation to deliver an immersive sci-fi thriller experience. Additional features such as Skip Scene, Personality Assessment, a Hidden Event, and a Pause Choices option for live streaming make it especially flexible and engaging for different play styles.

With its suspenseful story, live-action presentation, and systems that reward careful decision-making, *The Complex* stands out as a smart, atmospheric interactive movie. It combines strong performances, thoughtful tracking mechanics, and multiple outcomes into a memorable thriller that keeps players invested in every choice.
