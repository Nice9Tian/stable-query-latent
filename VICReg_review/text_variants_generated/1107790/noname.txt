This live-action cinematic sci-fi thriller is set after a major bio-weapon attack has placed a city under lockdown and left two scientists trapped inside a sealed laboratory complex, with both time and air running out. The story follows a doctor who has previously treated victims of a chemical attack in an authoritarian state and is now a leading figure in the development of a futuristic cellular technology. When reports emerge of a civilian exhibiting alarming symptoms, the situation becomes personal, and a reunion with an old acquaintance leads to entrapment inside an impenetrable research headquarters hiding a dangerous secret.

The experience is built around interactive decision-making. Player choices shape both the immediate direction of events and the relationships between characters, ultimately leading to one of eight possible endings. Relationship values are tracked in real time from the beginning to the final scenes, and these values can alter specific scenarios as well as have significant consequences near the conclusion. In parallel, the game also monitors personality traits throughout each playthrough, then presents a final breakdown based on five dimensions: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism.

The production emphasizes filmed performances and a thriller atmosphere, with a cast that includes several notable actors and a guest appearance by a streamer and presenter. Additional replay-oriented features include scene skipping, a personality assessment, a hidden event, and an option to pause choices, which is intended to support audience participation during livestreams.
