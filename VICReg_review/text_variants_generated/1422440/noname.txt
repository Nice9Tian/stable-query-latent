In this real-time strategy and city-building experience, the player leads the last survivors after a world-shattering catastrophe in a land now overrun by corrupted monstrous forces. The core of the game combines large-scale fortification building with tactical defense, asking players to construct cities, outposts, and fortresses one piece at a time using a modular system centered on wood, stone, and carefully planned structural support.

Construction is highly flexible. Players can create defenses from scratch, use predesigned blueprints, or rely on community-shared creations. Buildings can be placed to exploit terrain features such as chokepoints and elevation, while resource access and map layout shape how each stronghold develops. Wooden structures are cheaper and faster to raise, while stone provides greater durability. Structural integrity matters throughout: load-bearing elements must be managed carefully, and collapsing pieces can harm both allies and enemies, sometimes creating tactical advantages if used deliberately.

The game also emphasizes logistics and expansion. Players venture beyond their relative safe zone to secure resources that improve construction options, military strength, and long-term survival. Resource extraction sites, recruitment facilities, housing, and other supporting structures all contribute to sustaining the defense effort. As the settlement grows, players can use rooftops, optimized supply routes, and newly unlocked tools to expand efficiently within limited space.

Combat centers on night assaults, when enemy waves strike in full force. During the day, players explore, gather, and prepare; at sunset, they position their troops for defense. Units include ranged fighters and explosive specialists, each with distinct roles and upgrade paths. Battles rely on careful placement, height advantages, traps, ambushes, and coordinated responses to breaches as threats evolve. If outer defenses fail, inner structures may still serve as a final line of resistance.

The game offers a single-player campaign, hand-crafted skirmish maps, and an endless challenge mode set on procedurally generated maps with escalating waves. A map editor allows players to create and share custom skirmishes, while the building tools also support blueprint sharing for fortress design. Overall, the experience blends architectural freedom, resource management, and tactical real-time defense in a campaign to protect humanity from total destruction.
