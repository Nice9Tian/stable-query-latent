Cataclismo is a strategy game set in the aftermath of a catastrophe known as the Cataclismo, where humanity’s last survivors struggle against a corrupted world and the hostile Horrors that emerge from it. Players lead these survivors, building cities, citadels, and fortresses brick by brick while defending them from night-time assaults. The game combines large-scale construction, resource management, and real-time tactical defense.

A central feature of the game is its modular building system. Players can create intricate structures made of wood and stone, placing each piece individually or using blueprints, including community-shared creations. Fortresses can be designed across multiple biomes and adapted to the surrounding terrain, taking advantage of chokepoints, elevation, and local resources. Wood allows for faster and cheaper construction, while stone provides greater durability. Structural integrity matters: load-bearing support must be considered, because collapsing pieces can kill allies and enemies alike. Collapses can also be used strategically to crush groups of Horrors.

As the game progresses, players expand their fortress network and establish outposts beyond the relative safety of the Last City. These structures help secure vital resources and support further development. Resource gathering, access to new areas, and construction efficiency are all important as players work to strengthen their military and defensive capabilities. Rooftop construction and carefully planned routes between resource deposits and warehouses help make use of limited space.

Combat emphasizes preparation and positioning. During the day, players explore, gather resources, and strengthen defenses, but at night the Horrors attack in full force. Players command a variety of units, including bowmen and cannoneers, and use their different abilities to respond to changing threats. Fortifications can be designed for layered defense, allowing units to fight from elevated positions and from behind traps, while enemies can be lured into chokepoints and ambushed with fire arrows and poison pots. As units upgrade, their special traits and abilities become more important, encouraging careful tactical placement.

The game also includes systems for supporting a growing settlement. Housing, recruitment centers, and resource-gathering outposts all contribute directly or indirectly to the defense effort. If the outer walls fail, even citizens’ homes can serve as a secondary line of defense, underscoring the need to protect the citadel at all costs.

Cataclismo offers three main modes: an epic single-player narrative campaign, a skirmish mode with hand-crafted maps, and a challenge mode with procedurally generated maps and increasingly difficult waves of Horrors. It also includes a map editor for creating and sharing skirmish maps, as well as a system for sharing architectural blueprints with other players.
