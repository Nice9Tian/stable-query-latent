Set on a tropical island nation frozen in time, this open-world action game places the player in the role of a local rebel fighting against an oppressive dictatorship. The ruler of the island seeks to restore his country’s former glory by any means necessary, while his son follows in his footsteps, and their rule has sparked a full-scale revolution.

The game can be played solo or in co-op, and it centers on joining a modern guerrilla movement to liberate the island. Players can explore jungles, beaches, and cities on foot, horseback, or in a wide range of vehicles, including boats and personal watercraft. The world is described as the largest in the series, emphasizing freedom of movement and varied routes across the landscape.

Combat focuses on a large arsenal of hundreds of weapons, supported by animal companions that assist the player during encounters. These allies add a distinct layer to the action and complement the game’s combat-heavy structure. In addition to the main campaign, the game includes post-launch content such as new special operations, crossover missions, a New Game Plus option, an extra-hard difficulty setting, and additional loadout slots.

The game’s expansion offerings also add substantial variety. One major add-on presents a surreal set of life-or-death trials built around deadly crystal enemies, multiple paths, and new lore. A separate content pass includes three downloadable episodes that let players experience the stories of three villainous characters, each set in an open world that reflects the mind of its respective antagonist. This pass also includes a retro-futuristic shooter classic.

Overall, the game combines large-scale exploration, cooperative play, and weapon-driven combat with ongoing updates and additional story content, creating a broad action experience centered on rebellion, survival, and liberation.
