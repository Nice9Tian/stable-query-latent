Far Cry® 6 delivers a vivid, high-energy open-world experience set in Yara, a tropical paradise frozen in time under the oppressive rule of dictator Antón Castillo. With his son Diego following in his footsteps, Castillo’s regime has sparked a full-scale revolution, and players step into the role of Dani Rojas, a local Yaran fighting alongside a modern-day guerrilla movement to help liberate the nation.

One of the game’s greatest strengths is the sheer scale and variety of Yara. Players can explore jungles, beaches, and cities on foot, horseback, or across a wide range of vehicles, including boats and Jet Skis. This expansive world gives the adventure a strong sense of freedom and momentum, making every journey feel dynamic and full of possibility.

Combat is equally exciting, with an arsenal of hundreds of weapons that supports a flexible and explosive playstyle. Dani is also joined by helpful amigos, including Chorizo the dog and Guapo the gator, who add personality and charm to the fight against Castillo’s forces. The game supports solo play as well as co-op, letting players experience the full campaign alone or with a friend.

Far Cry 6 also stands out for its ongoing support and added content. Since launch, it has received four new special operations, free crossover missions, and updates such as New Game Plus, Completionist Aid, an extra-hard difficulty mode, and four new loadout slots. These additions help expand the experience and give players more ways to enjoy the game.

The available editions add even more value. The Season Pass includes three DLC episodes featuring Vaas, Pagan Min, and Joseph Seed, each set in a new open world that represents the mind of its villain, along with Far Cry® 3 Blood Dragon Classic Edition. The GOTY Edition also includes Lost Between Worlds, a surreal expansion with 15 unique life-or-death trials, crystal enemies, multiple paths, new lore, and distinct gameplay challenges. Other edition bonuses such as the Ultimate Pack, Jungle Expedition Pack, Croc Hunter Pack, and Vice Pack further enrich the package.

Bold, large-scale, and packed with content, Far Cry® 6 offers a thrilling revolution across one of the series’ most expansive worlds.
