Far Cry 6 is an action-adventure game set in Yara, a tropical paradise frozen in time and ruled by dictator Antón Castillo. As Castillo tries to restore the nation’s former glory by any means necessary, his oppressive rule and the influence of his son, Diego, have sparked a revolution. Players take on the role of Dani Rojas, a local Yaran, and join a modern-day guerrilla movement to liberate the country.

The game can be played solo or in co-op. Players explore jungles, beaches, and cities on foot, horseback, or through a wide range of vehicles, including boats and Jet Skis. Combat features an arsenal of hundreds of weapons, supported by helpful amigos such as Chorizo the dog and Guapo the gator.

Far Cry 6 also includes additional content and post-launch features, such as four new special operations, free crossover missions, New Game Plus, Completionist Aid, an extra-hard difficulty mode, and four new loadout slots. The Season Pass contains three DLC episodes centered on Far Cry villains Vaas, Pagan Min, and Joseph Seed, with each episode featuring a new open world that reflects the mind of its villain. It also includes Far Cry 3 Blood Dragon Classic Edition.

The GOTY Edition includes Far Cry 6, the Lost Between Worlds expansion, the Far Cry 6 Season Pass, and the Ultimate Pack. Lost Between Worlds is described as a surreal expansion featuring 15 life-or-death trials, deadly crystal enemies, multiple player paths, new lore, and varied gameplay challenges.
