OBS Studio is a free and open-source program for livestreaming and video recording. It allows users to create scenes using multiple types of video sources, including computer displays, video games, webcams, video files, window captures, images, text, browser windows, and capture cards.

The program supports livestreaming to platforms such as Twitch, YouTube, Facebook Live, and other custom servers, as well as local recording for saving videos to a hard drive and sharing them later. It is designed for high-performance real-time video and audio capturing and mixing, with an intuitive audio mixer that includes per-source filters such as noise gate, noise suppression, and gain. It also supports VST plugins for additional audio control.

OBS Studio offers flexible scene management, allowing users to set up an unlimited number of scenes and switch between them seamlessly using custom transitions. Users can choose from various customizable transitions or add their own stinger video files. The software also includes hotkey support for many actions, such as switching scenes, starting or stopping streams or recordings, muting audio sources, and push-to-talk.

Its configuration options are extensive, with a streamlined settings panel for adjusting many aspects of broadcasting or recording. The modular Dock UI can be rearranged to suit different workflows, and each dock can be popped out into its own window. Studio Mode provides a preview of scenes and sources before they go live, making it possible to adjust or create new setups privately before viewers see them. Multiview offers a high-level overview of the production, letting users monitor up to 8 different scenes and cue or transition to any of them with a single or double click.
