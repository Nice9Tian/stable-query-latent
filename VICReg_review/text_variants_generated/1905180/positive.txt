OBS Studio is a powerful, free, and open-source program for livestreaming and video recording, built to give creators flexible control over their productions. It lets you create scenes using many different kinds of video sources, including your computer display, video games, webcams, video files, browser windows, images, text, and capture cards. Whether you want to stream live or record locally to share later, OBS Studio provides a strong and versatile foundation.

The software supports livestreaming to major platforms such as Twitch, YouTube, and Facebook Live, as well as custom servers. It also offers high-performance real-time video and audio capturing and mixing, making it well suited for smooth and professional broadcasts. Users can build an unlimited number of scenes and switch between them seamlessly with custom transitions, giving each production a polished and dynamic feel.

Audio tools are another standout feature. OBS Studio includes an intuitive audio mixer with per-source filters such as noise gate, noise suppression, and gain, along with VST plugin support for even more control. Its configuration options are powerful yet easy to use, and the streamlined Settings panel makes it simple to fine-tune nearly every aspect of a broadcast or recording. You can add new sources, duplicate existing ones, and adjust properties with ease.

The interface is designed for flexibility, featuring a modular Dock UI that can be rearranged to suit your workflow, with each dock even able to pop out into its own window. Scene transitions are customizable, and you can choose from a variety of built-in options or add your own stinger video files. Hotkeys can be assigned to nearly every action, including switching scenes, starting or stopping streams or recordings, muting audio sources, and push-to-talk.

For added confidence, Studio Mode lets you preview scenes and sources before they go live, so you can adjust or build new setups privately before your audience sees them. The Multiview feature gives you a high-level overview of your production, allowing you to monitor eight different scenes and quickly cue or transition to any of them with a single or double click.

OBS Studio stands out as an excellent choice for anyone looking for a capable, customizable, and reliable tool for streaming and recording.
