SCP: Secret Files is a vivid and imaginative adventure set within the fascinating universe of the SCP Foundation, a secretive global organization dedicated to “secure, contain, and protect” humanity from unimaginable anomalies. The game presents a world where reality is constantly threatened by horrifying creatures, bizarre objects, dangerous cults, and other mysterious phenomena that lie beyond ordinary understanding.

Players take on the role of Karl, a rookie researcher newly employed by the SCP Foundation and tasked with organizing top-secret files. But while browsing the archives, Karl experiences something inexplicable: his perception of the world begins to change, and his own psyche is deeply affected. This unsettling journey raises a compelling question throughout the experience: can sanity survive exposure to the Foundation’s strange and dangerous anomalies?

One of the game’s greatest strengths is its variety. SCP: Secret Files delivers multiple distinct gameplay experiences, from investigating a mysterious hydroelectric power plant to stepping into an uplifting fable or exploring a bizarre retro-inspired world. Each story brings its own atmosphere, mechanics, and cast of characters, ensuring a fresh and engaging experience throughout.

The game also celebrates the rich and wide-ranging SCP lore. Its universe spans people, creatures, objects, phenomena, abstract concepts, and even supreme beings, while its stories embrace horror, science fiction, comedy, mythology, and more. SCP: Secret Files is designed to be both a rewarding experience for longtime SCP fans and an excellent introduction for newcomers eager to discover the strange and wonderful world of the SCP Foundation.
