This game presents a world where humanity’s normal reality is under constant pressure from incomprehensible anomalies. Beneath the surface of everyday life, a secret global organization works to secure, contain, and protect people from threats that range from lethal creatures and disaster-causing cults to ordinary objects that have gained unnatural properties.

The player takes on the role of a junior archive worker named **Alden**, who is assigned to help sort classified records at a hidden institution known as the **Vesper Bureau**. While examining the files, Alden encounters something that causes his perception of reality to shift in unsettling ways, and his mental stability begins to deteriorate. The story asks whether it is possible to keep hold of sanity after being exposed to such phenomena.

The game offers multiple distinct experiences rather than a single uniform structure. Players can investigate a strange industrial facility, move through a hopeful fairy-tale-like episode, or explore a bizarre world inspired by retro aesthetics. Each segment features its own tone, mechanics, and set of characters, giving the overall work a varied and anthology-like format.

Its setting draws from a broad range of ideas: people, creatures, objects, phenomena, abstract concepts, and even overwhelmingly powerful entities. The stories also span multiple genres, including horror, science fiction, comedy, and mythology. As a result, the game is designed both for long-time fans of its larger mythos and for newcomers looking for an accessible introduction to its unusual lore.
