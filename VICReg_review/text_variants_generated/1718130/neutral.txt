SCP: Secret Files is a story-driven game set in the universe of the SCP Foundation, a secretive global organization dedicated to “secure, contain, and protect” humanity from anomalies beyond normal understanding. The game presents a world in which reality is threatened by strange and horrifying phenomena, including creatures that kill those who see their faces, cults capable of triggering worldwide disasters, and ordinary objects that possess supernatural powers.

The player takes on the role of Karl, a rookie researcher newly employed by the SCP Foundation. Tasked with organizing top-secret files, Karl begins to experience something inexplicable while browsing the archives. As the story unfolds, his perception of the world changes gradually, and his psyche is also affected. The premise centers on whether he can maintain his sanity after encountering these anomalies.

SCP: Secret Files is described as offering a variety of gameplay experiences. It includes investigations into a mysterious hydroelectric power plant, an uplifting fable, and a bizarre retro-inspired world. Each story segment has its own tone, mechanics, and cast of characters, giving the game a varied structure rather than a single uniform style of play.

The game also draws heavily from SCP lore, which spans a wide range of subjects such as people, creatures, objects, phenomena, abstract concepts, and even supreme beings like SCP-3999. Its stories explore themes including horror, science fiction, comedy, mythology, and more. According to the store page, the game is designed to appeal both to long-time SCP fans and to newcomers who want an introduction to the Foundation’s unusual lore.
