This dark fantasy tactical role-playing game presents a reimagined Arthurian setting where the player takes control of a fallen knight resurrected after a fatal clash with a legendary ruler. The story begins after both adversaries have died, only for the player character to return at the command of a mystical lady who governs an enchanted isle. Her purpose is to send the protagonist on a knightly quest to finish the conflict once and for all by confronting the ruler again, now transformed into something far worse.

The game combines turn-based tactical combat with character-focused role-playing systems. Players manage a squad of heroes on dangerous battlefields while also handling moral choices, party development, and the reconstruction of a ruined stronghold. The world is presented as a grim fantasy land filled with monsters, magical beings, castles, and forests shaped by the consequences of the central conflict. As the ruler’s suffering spreads through the mystical realm, the landscape itself becomes a nightmare version of a once-great kingdom.

A major part of the experience is building and managing a roster of heroes drawn from six distinct classes: Vark, Toren, Sylph, Brann, Myr, and Elda. These characters can be leveled up, assigned skill points, and equipped with gear obtained from missions. Combat decisions matter because heroes can die permanently, and injuries, curses, and diseases require time and resources to treat. Between missions, the player must ensure that the central fortress has the proper facilities to support recovery and prepare different heroes for different tasks.

The game also emphasizes interpersonal dynamics within the party. Each hero has personal traits, goals, rivalries, and bond-related benefits, meaning they are not simply disposable units. Their loyalty is affected by the player’s decisions, and they may approve, disapprove, leave, or even turn against the protagonist. Settling disputes, granting favors, and choosing compatible combinations of heroes are all important parts of maintaining a functional team.

The protagonist is not only a battlefield commander but also a ruler whose identity is shaped by actions and moral choices. A morality system tracks the character’s position between opposing values such as faith and tradition, as well as tyranny and righteousness. These choices influence both the narrative and gameplay, reinforcing the idea that leadership carries consequences beyond combat.

The game offers four difficulty settings, ranging from a more relaxed story-focused experience to highly demanding battles. For players seeking a harsher structure, a special mode adds a roguelite twist with no reloads, making every decision permanent. In this mode, choices made both in exploration and combat can have lasting effects, and the campaign can unfold differently across playthroughs with branching outcomes.

After completing the campaign, an endgame mode opens up additional content for experienced players. This includes tougher challenges on the map, mythic boss battles, random quests, more loot, and further character progression, ultimately leading to a final confrontation with a powerful monstrous god-king figure. The release also includes voice acting in one language and text localization in several others.
