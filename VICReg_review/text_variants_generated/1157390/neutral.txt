King Arthur: Knight’s Tale is a dark fantasy role-playing tactical game set in a reimagined Arthurian world. Players take on the role of Sir Mordred, the former black knight and nemesis of King Arthur. After Mordred kills Arthur and is struck down in return, both are restored to life by the Lady of the Lake, ruler of Avalon, who sends Mordred on a knightly quest to finish what he began: kill King Arthur, or whatever he has become.

The game combines turn-based tactical combat with character-focused RPG systems. Players manage a team of heroes while also making moral choices, handling hero progression, and rebuilding Camelot. The world is presented as a nightmare version of Britannia, where monsters and magical creatures inhabit castles and forests, and Arthur’s resurrection has twisted Avalon into something corrupted and dangerous.

A major part of the game is assembling and managing a squad of knights. Heroes can be chosen from six classes: Defender, Champion, Marksman, Vanguard, Arcanist, and Sage. They can level up, earn unique skill points, and equip items looted from missions. However, heroes can die, and injuries, curses, and diseases take time to treat. Between missions, players must ensure Camelot has the necessary facilities for recovery and prepare different heroes for different quests.

Hero management is also tied to relationships and loyalty. Each hero has a distinct personality, goals, rivalries, traits, skills, and bond benefits. They respond to the player’s decisions, and loyalty can be affected by many factors. Disputes may need to be settled, favours granted, and compatible heroes selected for specific missions. If loyalty drops too far, heroes may leave or even turn against the player.

The player character also develops through a Morality Chart, which tracks choices along two axes: Christianity versus Old Faith, and Tyranny versus Righteousness. These decisions affect both gameplay and narrative, shaping the kind of monarch Mordred becomes. The campaign includes four difficulty modes, ranging from more relaxed storytelling to very demanding battles. For players seeking an additional challenge, a Roguelite Mode is available, where decisions in and out of combat have lasting consequences and no reloading is allowed.

After completing the campaign, an endgame mode becomes available. This post-campaign content adds tougher map challenges, mythic boss fights, random quests, more loot, and further character progression, ultimately leading to a battle against Balor, the monstrous god-king of the Fomorians.

At release, the game includes English voice acting and text localization in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, and Hungarian.
