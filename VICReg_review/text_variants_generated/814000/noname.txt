This fantasy role-playing adventure is based on a long-running manga and anime series celebrating its 25th anniversary. It follows a legendary pirate captain, here referred to as Captain Sol, and his crewmates as they sail through a vast new sea in search of the next island and the next adventure. During their voyage, a violent storm strikes their ship and leaves them stranded on a lush tropical island surrounded by relentless tempests.

Separated from the rest of the crew, Captain Sol begins a new journey to reunite with his companions and find a way off the island. Along the way, players face dangerous enemies, powerful natural forces, and other challenges. The game lets players take control of multiple members of the crew in a classic role-playing format set within the world of the original series.

A premium edition is also available. It includes the main game, an additional scenario, an expansion pack, a bonus amount of in-game currency, and a special costume set. The expansion content becomes available only after clearing the main story, while the currency bonus is granted immediately upon purchase. Buyers are also warned that some included content may be available separately, so duplicate purchases should be avoided.
