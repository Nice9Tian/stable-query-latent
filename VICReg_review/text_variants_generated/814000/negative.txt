ONE PIECE ODYSSEY tries to sell itself as a major RPG celebration of the ONE PIECE franchise’s 25th anniversary, but the store page makes it sound more like a standard, cautiously packaged tie-in than a truly standout adaptation. The setup is familiar: Monkey D. Luffy and the Straw Hat Crew are sailing through the New World, get caught in a storm, and end up shipwrecked on a tropical island plagued by constant storms. Luffy is separated from his crew and has to set out to find them and escape the island, while facing dangerous enemies and natural threats along the way.

The game is presented as a classic RPG set in the world of the ONE PIECE anime, with players able to control various members of the Straw Hat Crew. Beyond that, the page offers little to suggest anything especially bold or inventive about the core experience. It leans heavily on the appeal of the license rather than on any clearly distinctive mechanics or systems in the description provided.

The Deluxe Edition framing also feels more like a sales bundle than a meaningful enhancement. It includes the main game, the Adventure Expansion Pack, the additional scenario ONE PIECE Reunion of Memories, a 100,000 Berries bonus, and a Sniper King Outfit Set. However, the expansion content is only available after clearing the main game, which undercuts its immediate value, and the page repeatedly warns about duplicate purchases, making the whole offering feel cluttered and overly monetized.

In short, ONE PIECE ODYSSEY appears to be a conventional RPG built around a popular brand, with a familiar shipwreck premise, party-based Straw Hat gameplay, and a pile of edition extras that do more to complicate the purchase than to inspire confidence.
