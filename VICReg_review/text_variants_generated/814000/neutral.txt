ONE PIECE ODYSSEY is a new RPG based on the hugely popular ONE PIECE manga and anime, created to commemorate the series’ 25th anniversary. The game follows Monkey D. Luffy, also known as Straw Hat Luffy, and the Straw Hat Crew as they sail through the New World in search of the next island and their next adventure. During their journey, they are caught in a storm and shipwrecked on a lush tropical island surrounded by relentless storms.

Separated from his crew, Luffy begins a new adventure to find his friends and escape the island. Along the way, players face dangerous new enemies and terrifying natural forces. The game lets players take on the roles of various members of the Straw Hat Crew in a classic RPG set in the world of ONE PIECE.

The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the main game, the Adventure Expansion Pack, the Additional Scenario: ONE PIECE Reunion of Memories, a bonus of 100,000 Berries, and the Deluxe Edition Bonus: Sniper King Outfit Set. The expansion content becomes available after the main game has been cleared, while the 100,000 Berries bonus is granted upon purchase.
