**ONE PIECE ODYSSEY** is a vibrant new RPG based on the hugely popular *ONE PIECE* manga and anime, created to commemorate the series’ 25th anniversary. It brings the Straw Hat Pirates into an exciting original adventure, following Monkey D. Luffy and his crew as they sail across the New World in search of the next island and the next great challenge.

After being caught in a storm and shipwrecked, the Straw Hats find themselves stranded on a lush tropical island surrounded by relentless weather. Separated from his crew, Luffy must set out on a grand journey to reunite with his friends and escape the island. Along the way, players face dangerous new enemies, terrifying forces of nature, and all the excitement that comes with a fresh *ONE PIECE* story.

The game lets players take control of various members of the Straw Hat Crew in a classic RPG experience set in the world of the beloved anime. It captures the spirit of adventure, friendship, and discovery that defines *ONE PIECE*, while offering a new story designed especially for fans of the series.

The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the main game, the Adventure Expansion Pack, the additional scenario **“ONE PIECE Reunion of Memories,”** a **100,000 Berries** bonus for the Adventure Expansion Pack, and the **Sniper King Outfit Set** as a Deluxe Edition bonus. The expansion content becomes available after clearing the main game, while the 100,000 Berries bonus is available upon purchase.
