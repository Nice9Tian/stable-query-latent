NieR Replicant™ ver.1.22474487139... is an updated version of NieR Replicant, a title that was previously released only in Japan. It is presented as the prequel to NieR:Automata, with modernized visuals and an expanded presentation that introduces the story to a wider audience.

The game follows a kind young man living in a remote village who sets out with Grimoire Weiss, a strange talking tome, in order to save his sister Yonah, who has fallen terminally ill to the Black Scrawl. To do so, he searches for the “Sealed verses.” The story is framed as a dark, twisting tale set in a distant future where humanity is on the brink of extinction, with a black scrawl disease and strange beasts threatening the world. Players experience the story through the eyes of the protagonist as a brother, and the game emphasizes uncovering the truth behind the world of NieR.

Gameplay features journeying alongside an unlikely group of allies through a ruined world filled with sickness and monstrous horrors. Combat combines swordplay and magic in a visceral, customizable system. Players can customize their character’s magic, skills, and weapons using the power of Words.

The game also includes a soundtrack newly recorded by Keiichi Okabe, who returns from NieR:Automata. The original core team is also highlighted, including director YOKO TARO and producer Yosuke Saito.

The store page also mentions “4 YoRHa,” a free downloadable content pack that adds 4 costumes and 4 weapons from NieR:Automata. This content may not be playable during some scenes due to the game’s story, and the full game is required to use it. If a latest patch is needed, it must be applied before use.
