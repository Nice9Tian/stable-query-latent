Set in a distant future where humanity is nearing extinction, this action RPG follows a kind young man from a remote village who sets out to save his little sister from a deadly illness and a mysterious curse. Joined by a talking tome and other unlikely allies, he travels through a ruined world filled with sickness, monstrous creatures, and hidden truths.

This updated edition presents a modernized version of an earlier release, featuring revitalized visuals and a story that was previously unavailable in some regions. The narrative is structured around a dark, twisting mystery that encourages players to question what they see as the journey unfolds. A free add-on is also available, offering additional outfits and weapons inspired by a related world, though its use may be restricted in certain story scenes and requires the base game and the latest update.

Combat combines swordplay with magic in a flexible system that lets players customize abilities, spells, and equipment through special enhancement effects. The soundtrack has been newly recorded, and the game brings together the original creative team behind the series, including its director, composer, and producer. Overall, it is a story-driven adventure centered on desperation, sacrifice, and the search for the truth behind a world shaped by disease and deception.
