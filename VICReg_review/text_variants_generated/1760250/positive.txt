Madden NFL 23 delivers a strong, feature-rich football experience that lets players shape their own path to greatness. Built around the thrill of making every decision count, the game offers multiple ways to take control of your NFL journey, from leading a franchise to building a dream roster in Madden Ultimate Team.

In Face of the Franchise: The League, players step into their fifth NFL season at a chosen skill position—QB, WR, RB, CB, or MLB—and begin a fresh chapter with a new team. You negotiate a one-year “prove-it” contract with one of 32 NFL teams, then earn rewards and level up through strong on-field performance. New Drive Goals and Drive Summaries help tailor each game by providing feedback and rewards based on how well you play, while weekly activities and training between games provide performance boosts. Madden Ratings Adjustor Chad Johnson also guides career development with ratings updates as you pursue the highest level of prestige on your Road to 99.

Franchise mode brings a refined approach to team building and long-term planning. Players can think like an NFL head coach with updated scouting, staff management, and weekly strategy features. Free agency is more dynamic thanks to athlete motivations such as team location and salary demands, while updated Trade Logic AI better reflects the value of star players to create fairer and more realistic offers.

Madden Ultimate Team gives players the tools to assemble a powerful fantasy roster made up of current NFL stars, Hall of Fame legends, and more. The mode introduces a first-ever Field Pass challenge and reward tracker, ensuring there is always something to play for. Players can earn MUT Champions entry tokens and choose when to compete, and simplified Set building makes it easier to manage the content binder and keep improving the roster throughout the season.

Madden NFL 23 offers a polished and engaging package for football fans, blending career progression, franchise management, and team-building depth into one exciting game.
