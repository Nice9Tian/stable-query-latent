Set in a grim medieval-fantasy realm torn by war, this hand-drawn RPG adventure follows a young rat heir as he fights to restore a shattered kingdom and drive back a merciless amphibian enemy led by a tyrannical ruler. The game combines punishing combat with exploration, side content, and a steady progression of upgrades and customization.

Players traverse a deceptively charming world made up of six distinct biomes, each filled with hidden paths, secrets, and dangerous creatures. The journey is supported by a narrated story delivered in a deep, gravelly voice, while optional quests and faction hunts provide extra rewards. Along the way, a variety of companions can join select missions to help balance difficult encounters.

Combat is the centerpiece of the experience and is deliberately brutal. Enemies range from undead amphibians to oversized grubs and other hostile creatures, and success depends on observing attack patterns, timing defensive actions, and striking back with precision. Available techniques include dodge-rolls, parry bashes, fatal executions, and ranged bow attacks, all of which contribute to a challenging, souls-like style of play.

Character build and equipment choices matter throughout. Heavy armor can improve protection but may slow movement, forcing players to weigh defense against agility. Weapon types also differ in feel and purpose, with options that emphasize power, speed, or balanced performance. New weapons and armor can be forged from blueprints discovered in the world, and rare ingredients can be gathered to prepare health-restoring meals.

The home base serves as more than a resting place. It includes family members who handle cooking, crafting, and quest updates, giving structure to progression and helping players prepare for harder battles. The game also emphasizes boss fights, especially against large amphibian generals, each designed as a distinct combat challenge.

Two free expansions extend the adventure after the main story. One adds a post-game questline with new difficulty modes, several bosses, special equipment, and a hidden secret, while the other introduces additional quests, arena challengers, expanded customization, and cosmetic upgrades for the home keep. Together, they broaden the endgame with more combat, more rewards, and more reasons to keep exploring the kingdom.
