*Tails of Iron* is a hand-drawn action RPG set in a grim, war-torn kingdom. Players take on the role of Redgi, heir to the Rat Throne, as he works to restore his broken realm by driving out the Frog Clan and their leader, Greenwart. The game combines exploratory adventure, side quests, and faction hunts with punishing combat inspired by the souls-like genre.

The world is presented as a deceptively charming medieval-fantasy rat kingdom, but its inhabitants face constant danger. Across six distinct biomes, players encounter enemies such as frog zombies, gargantuan grubs, and other threats, while uncovering hidden paths and secrets. The game is fully narrated by Doug Cockle, adding a distinct storytelling presence to Redgi’s journey.

Combat is built around careful observation and reaction. Players can use dodge-rolls, parry bashes, fatal executions, and ranged bow attacks to survive brutal encounters. Weapon choice and armor loadout matter: heavy armor offers more protection but may slow movement, while different weapons such as axes, spears, and broadswords provide different combat styles. Savage boss fights against oversized amphibian generals are a major part of the experience, each demanding specific tactics.

Redgi’s home base, the keep, is managed by his brothers Rem-Rem, Bam-Bam, and Dug. Rem cooks health-boosting meals using ingredients found in the world, Bam crafts new weapons and armor from blueprints, and Dug provides information about available quests. Players can also gather rare ingredients, uncover blueprints, and recruit companions to help on select missions.

The game’s feature list highlights its brutal combat, expansive kingdom, character customization, side quests, companion support, crafting systems, and 2D hand-drawn art style.

Tails of Iron also includes two free expansions. *Bloody Whiskers* adds a new post-game questline, two additional difficulty modes, five new bosses, special armours and weapons, and a secret tied to a deadly trial of combat for King Redgi. *Bright Fir Forest* continues Redgi’s journey with 13 new quests, three new challengers in the Mole Arena, new weapons and armor, and cosmetic upgrades for the Crimson Keep, while introducing a new end-game storyline centered on a growing amphibious threat.
