**Tails of Iron** is a striking hand-drawn RPG adventure set in a grim kingdom ravaged by war, where every step forward demands courage, skill, and determination. As Redgi, the heir to the Rat Throne, players take on the task of restoring a broken kingdom by driving out the merciless Frog Clan and their fearsome leader, Greenwart. The game combines a charmingly deceptive world with punishingly brutal combat, creating a memorable adventure full of danger, discovery, and hard-won triumphs.

One of the game’s greatest strengths is its atmosphere. The medieval-fantasy Rat Kingdom is brought to life through gorgeous 2D hand-drawn art, deep and raspy narration by Doug Cockle, and a world that feels both enchanting and perilous. Across six distinct biomes, players can explore hidden paths, uncover secrets, and take on side quests and faction hunts for extra rewards. The world is rich with unique companions who can help even the odds, making each journey feel supported by a cast of memorable allies.

Combat in **Tails of Iron** is built around precision and endurance. Inspired by the souls-like genre, battles require close attention to enemy patterns and careful reactions. Players can dodge-roll, perform parry bashes, execute fatal finishers, and fight from range with a bow and arrow. The game offers a flexible approach to combat style, allowing players to equip heavy armor for stronger protection, choose between punchy axes, nimble spears, or balanced broadswords, and adapt their loadout to each challenge.

Progression is also deeply satisfying. Redgi’s keep serves as a central hub where his brothers help him prepare for the road ahead: Rem cooks health-boosting feasts from ingredients found in the world, Bam forges weapons and armor from special blueprints, and Dug keeps track of new quests across the kingdom. This loop of exploration, crafting, and preparation adds welcome depth and gives every discovery practical value.

Boss fights are a major highlight, featuring oversized amphibian foes and other deadly threats that test everything the player has learned. From frog zombies to enormous grubs and the Frog Clan’s powerful generals, each encounter is designed to be distinct, demanding, and rewarding to overcome.

The game also includes substantial free expansions that expand its world and content. **Bloody Whiskers** adds a new post-game questline, two additional difficulty modes, five new bosses, special armours and weapons, and a hidden secret, while **Bright Fir Forest** continues Redgi’s journey with 13 new quests, new challengers in the Mole Arena, expanded customization options, and cosmetic upgrades for the Crimson Keep. These additions make the experience even richer for returning players.

With its brutal but rewarding combat, compelling presentation, and lovingly crafted world, **Tails of Iron** stands out as a polished and memorable adventure that rewards persistence, mastery, and exploration.
