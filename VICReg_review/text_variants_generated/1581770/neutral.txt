*SpellForce: Conquest of Eo* is a turn-based strategy game set in the fantasy world of SpellForce, where you inherit your master’s mysterious wizard tower and begin a quest for magical power. As the heir to this tower, you study spells, summon fantastical minions, and train warriors to move across the world and fight in fast-paced, tactical turn-based battles.

The game centers on expanding your domain across the Realm of Eo. You can recruit talented apprentices and individual heroes, send them on adventures, and use them to gather resources, artifacts, ingredients, and riches. Sources of Allfire are key to progression, as controlling them allows you to add new spells to your grimoire, craft powerful artifacts, and improve your tower with new rooms and upgrades.

Players can choose between three archetypes and six spell lists, each supporting different play styles. Available archetypes include Necromancer, Alchemist, and Artificer. The Necromancer creates large numbers of undead, the Alchemist prepares potions and explosive vials for combat, and the Artificer crafts glyphs and magical items to equip troops. The game features over 60 spells and more than 100 troop types, ranging from goblins to griffons.

Your tower itself is a major part of the experience. It can be expanded and furnished with new rooms, such as a necromancer’s crypt or a mystical crystal chamber. It can also levitate and travel the land, allowing you to explore Eo and pursue magical power wherever it leads.

As you explore, you will encounter many threats and rivals, including New Purity fanatics, hostile granite dwarves, bloodthirsty orcs, undead, demons, and other monsters. You may also face the armies of rival mages, whose ambitions can lead to direct conflict over control of the realm. In addition to battle, the game offers choices such as befriending cities, harvesting resources, hunting arcane artifacts, uncovering the secrets of the Shapers, and taking control of mythical locations to absorb their power.

The campaign structure is procedurally generated, with more than 600 different adventures possible across campaigns. Overall, *SpellForce: Conquest of Eo* presents a mix of tactical combat, spellcasting, tower building, exploration, and domain expansion as you attempt to become the greatest mage in the world.
