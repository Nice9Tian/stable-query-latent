This turn-based strategy game is set in a fantasy realm where the player inherits a mysterious wizard’s tower and steps into the role of a rising mage. From this base, the game centers on studying spells, summoning fantastical minions, training warriors, and spreading influence across the surrounding lands. Its combat system emphasizes fast-paced but tactical turn-based battles, while its broader structure combines exploration, domain expansion, and resource management.

A major part of the experience is traveling through the world and interacting with its many characters and locations. Heroes can be recruited and sent on adventures to recover artefacts, ingredients, and riches. Apprentices and workers help expand the player’s reach by gathering resources and supporting the growing domain. The world also contains hidden secrets, ancient powers, and mythical sites that can be controlled to gain additional strength. Campaigns are procedurally generated, and the game offers a large number of unique adventures, encouraging repeated playthroughs.

Progression is built around controlling sources of magical power, which unlocks new spells, allows the crafting of powerful artefacts, and supports the expansion and improvement of the tower. The tower itself is a mobile stronghold that can levitate and travel across the land. As it grows, it can be furnished with new rooms and specialized upgrades, each contributing to the player’s overall dominion. Examples include chambers and crypt-like facilities tied to different magical specializations.

Players can choose between three archetypes and six spell lists, creating distinct styles of play. One path focuses on raising undead forces, another on brewing potions and explosive items for battle, and another on crafting glyphs and magical equipment for troops. The game includes more than 60 spells and over 100 troop types, ranging from weak goblin-like creatures to majestic griffin-like beasts. Heroes and apprentices also play an important role in leading armies and shaping the player’s strategic options.

Conflict comes from rival mages, hostile factions, and many kinds of monsters encountered across the realm. Opponents include fanatics, dwarven forces, orcish warriors, undead, demons, and other threats found in ruins, villages, and contested territory. Players may fight directly against enemy armies and even attack competing towers, reducing them to ruins, though peaceful approaches and knowledge-sharing are also possible in some cases. Overall, the game blends magical empire-building, tactical warfare, and exploration into a campaign-driven strategy experience centered on becoming the most powerful mage in the world.
