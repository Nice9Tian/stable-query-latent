This remastered grand strategy title presents a refreshed version of a classic ancient warfare experience, bundled with two expansion packs and, for eligible owners, a copy of the original collection. Its presentation has been significantly updated, with support for 4K optimization, ultra-widescreen displays, and native UHD resolution. Visual improvements extend beyond sharper output to include rebuilt structures and objects, alongside environmental details such as dust effects and heat haze. Campaign maps have also been upgraded with higher-resolution models, while battlefield units have been redesigned and retextured for a more polished appearance.

Alongside the visual overhaul, the game introduces a range of modern interface and usability improvements. The campaign camera now allows for map rotation and a broader zoom range. New heat maps and icon overlays help communicate systems such as diplomacy and security more clearly. In battles, additional tactical tools—including a tactical map, unit display enhancements, and range markers—offer more precise control over engagements.

The gameplay layer has also been expanded. A larger roster of factions is available, including sixteen that were previously unavailable, bringing the total number of playable factions to thirty-eight. Multiplayer has been updated with cross-platform support between Windows, macOS, and Linux, allowing players to challenge friends regardless of system. A new agent class has also been introduced: these specialists can travel the world to establish trade links, secure access to resources, and outmaneuver rivals economically, strengthening a faction’s financial influence.

Overall, this version combines a classic campaign and battle structure with visual upgrades, interface refinements, expanded faction choices, cross-platform multiplayer, and new economic tools, while preserving the core strategic focus of commanding an ancient empire.
