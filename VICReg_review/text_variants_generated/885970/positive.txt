**Total War: ROME REMASTERED** brings one of the most celebrated entries in the series back with a polished presentation, modern conveniences, and a wealth of content. This remastered edition includes both the **Alexander** and **Barbarian Invasion** DLCs, and if you do not already own it, it also comes with a copy of the original **ROME: Total War Collection**.

The visual upgrade is a major highlight. **ROME REMASTERED** updates the classic look of Rome with **4K optimization**, **ultra-widescreen support**, and **native UHD resolution support**. Across the game, buildings and objects have been re-modelled, while environmental effects such as **dust clouds** and **heat haze** help bring the battlefield and campaign map to life. The refreshed campaign maps feature new high-resolution models, and units have been remodelled and retextured so they can look their best in combat.

The remaster also adds a strong set of modern features that improve how the game plays and feels. The in-game camera has been enhanced with **map rotation** and a **wider zoom level** in Campaign mode. New **heat maps** and icon overlays provide clearer information for mechanics like diplomacy and security. In battle, a new **tactical map**, improved **unit displays**, and **range markers** give players greater control and awareness on Rome’s vast battlefields.

Game mechanics have also been expanded in meaningful ways. Players can now grow their armies and experience **16 additional factions** that were previously locked, bringing the total to **38 playable factions**. For the first time in Total War, players can also take their chosen faction into **cross-platform multiplayer** between **Windows, macOS, and Linux**. Another welcome addition is the **Merchant** agent type, which can travel the world to establish trade links, access resources, and buy out rivals, strengthening both income and economic influence.

With its upgraded visuals, quality-of-life improvements, expanded faction roster, and new strategic tools, **Total War: ROME REMASTERED** offers a respectful and feature-rich return to a true classic.
