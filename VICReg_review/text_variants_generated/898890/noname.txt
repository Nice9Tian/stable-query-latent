This eco-conscious adventure places the player in the role of a mother Veyla struggling to keep her little Nyxlings alive in a world devastated by human activity. The story is set in a harsh environment where the landscape has been damaged by pollution, exploitation, and the ongoing destruction of natural resources. Through this perspective, the game presents a survival journey shaped by both environmental collapse and the responsibilities of parenthood.

The experience is presented through 3D side-scrolling areas that encourage exploration, stealth, and careful movement. By night, the player guides the litter toward safer locations while avoiding threats. During the day, the family rests in improvised shelters and prepares for the next journey. Survival requires planning, since every movement can carry serious risk for both the adult and the cubs.

A central part of the gameplay is caring for the young. The player must feed the cubs, protect them, and observe their growth, including their individual personalities and fears. The game also includes hunting, which is necessary to provide food, while also making the player vulnerable to becoming prey. In addition, the cubs can learn new skills, helping them become less vulnerable over time.

The world features devastated environments inspired by real current issues, and the player must search for new shelters to stay safe from both natural and unnatural dangers. The game emphasizes emotionally difficult decisions and tests the player’s survival instincts throughout the journey. At its core, it is a struggle to endure and to keep the family alive in an increasingly hostile world.
