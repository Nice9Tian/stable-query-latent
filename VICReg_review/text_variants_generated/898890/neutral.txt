*Endling - Extinction is Forever* is an eco-conscious adventure that follows the last fox on Earth as it struggles to keep its cubs alive in a world ravaged by humanity. Through the perspective of a mother fox, the game presents a landscape shaped by pollution, exploitation, and the depletion of natural resources, creating a survival-focused experience set against environmental collapse.

The game uses 3D side-scrolling areas for exploration and survival. Players are tasked with defending and caring for a litter of cubs: feeding them, watching them grow, and learning their individual personalities and fears. Nighttime is used as the safest window to move stealthily toward new areas, while daytime is spent resting in improvised shelters and planning the next move carefully.

Survival is central to the experience. Players must hunt other animals to feed their cubs, avoid becoming prey themselves, and search for new shelters that can protect the family from both natural and unnatural threats. The game also asks players to make difficult decisions that can have an emotional impact, while teaching the cubs new skills to reduce their vulnerability. It is set in devastated environments inspired by real current issues, and its core objective is simple: survive.
