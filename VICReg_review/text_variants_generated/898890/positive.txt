**Endling - Extinction is Forever** is a powerful and moving eco-conscious adventure that puts you in the paws of the last fox on Earth. Through the eyes of a mother fox, the game presents a ravaged world shaped by human greed, pollution, and exploitation, creating an experience that is both emotionally charged and deeply meaningful.

At its heart, the game is about survival and protection. You must keep your little cubs alive as you explore devastated environments inspired by real current issues. The journey unfolds across 3D side-scrolling areas, where careful movement, stealth, and planning are essential. By day, you rest in improvised shelters and prepare your next steps. By night, you guide your litter toward safer places, using the darkness as cover to avoid danger.

Caring for your kits is a central part of the experience. You feed them, watch them grow, notice their unique personalities and fears, and teach them new skills to help make them less vulnerable. The game also challenges you to hunt other animals to feed your cubs, while staying alert to avoid becoming prey yourself. Every decision matters, and the adventure asks you to make emotionally taxing choices as you fight to protect your family.

Endling - Extinction is Forever stands out for its heartfelt premise and survival-driven gameplay. It combines exploration, stealth, caregiving, and tough decision-making into an unforgettable journey that highlights both the fragility of nature and the strength of a mother’s determination.
