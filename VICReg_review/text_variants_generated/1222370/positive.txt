**Necromunda: Hired Gun** is a fast-paced, violent, and thrilling indie FPS set in the darkest reaches of Warhammer 40,000’s most infamous hive city. As a merciless hired gun, you navigate an ocean of crime and corruption, taking on dangerous bounty work for the right price and hunting down notorious gangers and mutants.

The game centers on intense combat and agile movement across massive environments. You are equipped with a sprawling arsenal and a body enhanced by a dozen customizable augments that let you run on walls and leap over chasms. A loyal cyber-mastiff fights alongside you, sniffing out and killing enemies, while your grappling hook adds quick, fluid traversal to the experience.

Progression is built around upgrading, advancing, and leveling up as you collect bounties. With endless weapon and augment customization, Necromunda: Hired Gun offers a strong sense of build variety and a brutal mercenary fantasy powered by speed, mobility, and firepower.
