*Necromunda: Hired Gun* is presented as a fast-paced, violent first-person shooter set in the grim depths of Warhammer 40,000’s Necromunda hive city, but the pitch feels more like a checklist of aggression than a compelling game. You play as a mercenary moving through an ocean of crime and corruption, taking on bounties to eliminate gangers and mutants for the right price. That premise is blunt and bleak, fitting the setting, yet it also sounds mechanically thin.

The game centers on shooting through hostile environments with a sprawling arsenal, while a cyber-mastiff assists by sniffing out and killing enemies. A grappling hook and body augments are meant to make movement more agile, letting you run on walls and leap over chasms. There is also customization, with a dozen customizable augments and “endless” weapon and augment options, alongside upgrade and leveling systems tied to collecting bounties.

On paper, it promises brutality, mobility, and progression, but the description leans heavily on familiar FPS features without giving much reason to believe they add up to anything memorable. It sounds less like a rich dive into Necromunda’s corruption and more like a noisy procession of upgrades, kills, and loot-driven advancement.
