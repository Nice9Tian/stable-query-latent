Set in a grim hive metropolis of crime and corruption, this indie first-person shooter casts the player as a ruthless mercenary working to restore a brutal balance through paid killings. The setting is a dark, lawless urban world where the promise of profit is matched only by constant danger, and survival depends on speed, firepower, and adaptability.

The game focuses on fast-paced, violent combat against dangerous gangs and mutated enemies. Players are given a wide-ranging arsenal and can enhance their character with customizable augment modules that support mobility and combat, including wall-running and long jumps across gaps. Movement is further expanded through a grappling device that helps navigate large environments quickly and fluidly.

A loyal cyber-companion plays an active role in battle, assisting by tracking down foes and attacking them. Progression is tied to collecting contracts, earning rewards, and improving both equipment and abilities over time. The overall experience emphasizes aggressive shooting, traversal variety, and extensive customization within a harsh science-fiction underworld.
