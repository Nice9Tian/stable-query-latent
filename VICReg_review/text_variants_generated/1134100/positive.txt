Frozenheim is a Viking strategy game that invites players to rise from the shores of a new settlement to become a legendary hero of their clan. Set in a world of harsh seasons and dangerous, fertile lands, the game blends city management, exploration, survival, and warfare into a cohesive and rewarding experience.

At its core, Frozenheim emphasizes building and sustaining a thriving Viking village. Players must make the most of each season by hunting, fishing, farming, and gathering resources before winter arrives. Exploration plays a vital role, as the surrounding lands offer both opportunity and danger. As the settlement grows, players can carefully plan and manage their city, shaping it into a stronghold capable of supporting future expansion.

Combat is equally important, and Frozenheim offers a tactical approach to warfare. Players can raise warriors and make use of their unique skills, forming shield walls, using volley fire to pin down enemies, and unleashing berserkers to break enemy lines. Depending on how a clan develops, it becomes possible to recruit powerful heroes, construct devastating siege engines and imposing longships, and even call upon the power of Ragnarok.

The game also provides a variety of modes to suit different playstyles. Free-build mode offers a relaxed creative experience, while the arena of the Jarl’s tournament presents a tougher challenge. Survival mode tests building and endurance skills, and the campaign mode lets players discover the story of Frozenheim through the perspectives of four major clans: Wolf, Deer, Crow, and Bear.

With its combination of settlement management, seasonal survival, tactical battles, and multiple game modes, Frozenheim delivers a strong and engaging Viking strategy experience.
