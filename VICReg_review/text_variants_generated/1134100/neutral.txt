Frozenheim is a Viking-themed strategy game about building, survival, and warfare. Players begin their journey on the shores of Frozenheim as the leader of a clan, aiming to become a legendary hero among their people and a feared enemy to rival jarls. The game emphasizes both city management and tactical combat, requiring players to guide their village toward prosperity while preparing for conflict.

A major part of the experience is surviving through the changing seasons. To keep the village growing, players must make use of each season by hunting, fishing, farming, and gathering resources before winter arrives. Exploration is important as well, since the land is described as both rich in resources and dangerous. Once the area has been explored and a stable supply line established, players can begin planning and managing their city.

Combat centers on raising warriors and using their unique abilities effectively. The game includes tactical options such as forming shield walls, holding enemies in place with volley fire, and sending berserkers into enemy lines. As the clan develops, players can also recruit powerful heroes, construct siege engines and longships, and even call upon the power of Ragnarok.

Frozenheim offers several modes to suit different play styles. Players can relax in free-build mode, test themselves in the Jarl’s tournament arena, face the challenge of survival mode, or play through the campaign. The campaign tells the story of Frozenheim from the perspectives of four major clans: Wolf, Deer, Crow, and Bear.
