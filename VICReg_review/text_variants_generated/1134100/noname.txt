This strategy game places you in the role of a legendary leader rising from humble beginnings on the shores of **Varnheim**, where the fortunes of your clan depend on careful planning, seasonal adaptation, and decisive warfare. Your early priority is to keep the village thriving by making the most of each season: hunting, fishing, farming, and gathering resources before winter arrives. The world is described as both generous and hazardous, making exploration an important part of securing survival and expansion.

As your settlement grows, the focus shifts toward city management and long-term development. You must organize your community, establish a steady supply of resources, and shape the growth of your clan according to your chosen approach. Military strength is a major part of progression, with warriors that can be trained and used in coordinated tactics. Battles emphasize formation-based combat, such as holding enemies in place with defensive lines and supporting them with ranged volleys before committing aggressive units to break enemy formations.

The game also includes a variety of powerful options tied to clan development. Depending on how you build your settlement and forces, you may gain access to notable champions, destructive siege machinery, large seafaring vessels, and even apocalyptic powers associated with the end of the world. This creates a flexible system where economic growth, military preparation, and strategic specialization all feed into one another.

Several modes are available to suit different playstyles. A free-building mode allows for relaxed construction, while a tournament-style arena offers a more competitive challenge. A survival mode tests your ability to build and endure under pressure, and a campaign mode presents the story of **Varnheim** through the viewpoints of four major clans: the **Wolven**, the **Stag**, the **Raven**, and the **Ursa**. Overall, the game blends settlement management, seasonal survival, and tactical clan warfare into a broad Viking-themed strategy experience.
