This survival game presents a reimagined dinosaur-filled world built from the ground up with next-generation technology. Players awaken on a mysterious island and are immediately drawn into a harsh but vibrant ecosystem where exploration, crafting, building, and combat all play major roles. The experience centers on surviving in a living environment while trying to rise to the top of the food chain.

A core feature is the ability to form a tribe, tame, train, breed, and ride hundreds of species of prehistoric and primeval creatures. The game also emphasizes managing basic survival needs such as food, water, temperature, and weather conditions. As players progress, they can harvest resources, farm crops, construct buildings, and customize their character’s appearance while uncovering the secrets of the island and other connected worlds.

The game’s visual and technical presentation has been extensively rebuilt using modern engine technology. It includes advanced lighting, realistic reflections, high-detail geometry streaming, dynamic water, and interactive foliage that responds to creatures, explosions, projectiles, and physical objects. Environmental destruction is also designed to feel tangible, with trees, buildings, and other elements reacting naturally when damaged or demolished.

Beyond the base island, the game includes access to multiple expansion worlds, with some available immediately and others planned to arrive later at no additional cost. It also introduces a wide range of quality-of-life improvements, including redesigned interfaces, improved creature navigation, photo mode, updated camera and map systems, tracking tools, and new structures, items, and creatures.

Multiplayer and community support are important parts of the package. Players can join online tribes across different platforms, with support for large public servers, smaller private sessions, and local split-screen play. The game also features cross-platform modding, allowing players to download and use custom maps, creatures, items, and game modes through an in-game mod collection, expanding the experience with community-created content.
