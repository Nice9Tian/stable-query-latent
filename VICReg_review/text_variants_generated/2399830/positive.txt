**ARK: Survival Ascended** is a thrilling next-generation dinosaur survival experience that reimagines ARK from the ground up with Unreal Engine 5. Set on a mysterious island filled with vivid landscapes, dangerous wildlife, and untamed wilderness, the game invites players to awaken, adapt, and survive in a world where every moment feels alive and full of discovery.

At its core, the game challenges you to form a tribe, tame and breed hundreds of species of dinosaurs and other primeval creatures, and fight your way toward the top of the food chain. Survival is about more than combat: you must watch your food, water, temperature, and weather patterns while exploring, crafting, building, farming, and expanding your settlement across a living ecosystem.

ARK: Survival Ascended showcases a major leap in visual and simulation technology. Its fully redesigned worlds take advantage of Unreal Engine 5 features such as Lumen for realistic dynamic global illumination and Nanite for extraordinary geometric detail. The game also introduces advanced physics systems, including dynamic water that reacts naturally as creatures move through it, and fully interactive physical foliage that responds to characters, explosions, projectiles, and objects. From trees crashing into other trees to grass shifting as an enemy sneaks nearby, the environment feels deeply reactive and immersive.

The game includes access to all of ARK’s worlds, including Scorched Earth, Aberration, Extinction, ARK Genesis Part 1, ARK Genesis Part 2, and more. The Island, Scorched Earth, Aberration, and The Center are available now, with additional expansion worlds planned to arrive regularly at no extra cost.

ARK: Survival Ascended also delivers extensive quality-of-life improvements, including redesigned user interfaces, intelligent creature pathfinding with dynamic navigation, Wild Babies, Photo Mode, new camera systems, a new map and tracking system, and new structures, items, and creatures. Cross-platform modding opens the door to a steady stream of player-created content, including maps, creatures, items, and game modes, all accessible through an in-game mod collection. Cross-platform multiplayer further strengthens the experience, allowing tribes to form across gaming platforms in public online sessions for up to 70 players, private sessions for up to 8 players, and local split-screen for 2 players.

With its stunning presentation, expansive worlds, deep survival mechanics, and vibrant community-driven content, ARK: Survival Ascended stands as an ambitious and exciting evolution of the beloved dinosaur survival formula.
