This game presents a horseback adventure set in a large open world, where the player returns to a family estate that has fallen into ruin. The central goal is to restore the property to its former glory while uncovering the secrets of the surrounding region and rebuilding the family’s reputation.

The experience combines exploration, relationship-building, ranch management, and horse care. Players travel across varied landscapes such as shores, forests, and coral lagoons, meeting local residents and forming alliances that support the restoration effort. Along the way, they complete missions and quests tied to the family’s legacy and the recovery of the estate.

A major part of the game focuses on horses. Players can breed, tame, train, and mount them, with each horse having distinct strengths, traits, preferences, and training requirements. Strategic breeding allows players to improve abilities and obtain unique coat colors, adding depth to the collection and management systems.

The ranch itself is highly customizable. Players can rebuild and personalize it using different buildings, stables, horse care facilities, supplies, and decorations. Resource gathering is also part of progression, supporting both the ranch’s reconstruction and the broader journey.

Overall, the game offers a poetic open-world riding adventure centered on restoration, discovery, horse mastery, and steady progression through quests and ranch development.
