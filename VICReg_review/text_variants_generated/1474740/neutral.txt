Horse Tales: Emerald Valley Ranch is a horseback adventure set in a large open world. The story begins when a vacation at your aunt’s turns into an unexpected challenge: the family ranch, once a grand estate, has fallen into ruin, and it is up to you to restore it. As you work to bring the ranch back to life, you explore the surrounding peninsula, meet local residents, and build friendships with key allies such as Gabriel, Mattéo, and Noella.

The game emphasizes free exploration across a variety of scenic environments, including seashores, forests, and a coral lagoon. While riding through these areas, players can discover hidden secrets, complete missions and quests, and encounter many animals, including wild horses. Horses play a central role in the experience: they can be tamed, mounted, trained, and bred. Each horse has its own strengths, characteristics, personality traits, preferences, and training needs, making horse management an important part of gameplay.

Another major feature is ranch restoration and customization. Players rebuild their ruined family property by constructing and improving buildings, stables, horse care facilities, and decorations. Resource gathering supports this process, while the overall goal is to save the family’s legacy and restore the ranch’s former glory. Breeding also allows players to improve horse abilities and obtain unique coat colors, adding another layer to the management of their stud farm. Throughout the adventure, players aim to develop their riding skills, care for their horses, and take part in thrilling races.
