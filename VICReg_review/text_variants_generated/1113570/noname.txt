This title is a card-based role-playing game set in a world of swords and sorcery, where the entire adventure is presented through cards rather than conventional visual scenes. The narrative follows a self-proclaimed hero who sets out to defeat a dragon that has recently awakened. The story is delivered in a tabletop-inspired format, with events unfolding through narration by a game master, giving the experience the feel of a guided board-game campaign.

The gameplay centers on a turn-based RPG structure, while the presentation emphasizes a nostalgic style with a modern twist. In addition to the main adventure, a separate prologue episode is available as a demo-like introduction, showing the day before the story’s events and following a group as they search for a stolen royal treasure. A larger set is also offered that combines multiple titles from the same series along with downloadable content for each one.

The downloadable content for this game includes several items that alter the design of in-game components such as cards and avatars. These additions can also restyle certain elements to match the appearance of other works by the same creators, and one item changes the look of all characters and enemies to a pixel-art style.

The project is associated with a creative team known for distinctive presentation and atmosphere, and the game has been described by reviewers as carefully crafted and welcoming. It is worth noting that parts of the game requiring an internet connection may stop being supported at some point.
