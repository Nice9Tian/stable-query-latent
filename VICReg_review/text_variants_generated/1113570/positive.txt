Voice of Cards: The Isle Dragon Roars is a beautifully crafted RPG set in a world of swords and sorcery, presented entirely through cards in a style that feels both fresh and nostalgically familiar. Guided by narration from the gamesmaster, the adventure unfolds like a tabletop RPG, giving every step of the journey a distinctive charm and atmosphere.

The story follows a self-proclaimed hero who sets out to slay a dragon that has recently awakened, promising a classic fantasy quest with a unique presentation. This card-based approach makes the game stand out, while still delivering the appeal of a traditional role-playing adventure.

Created by YOKO TARO as Creative Director, with music by Keiichi Okabe and character design by Kimihiko Fujisaka, the game brings together an acclaimed creative team for an experience that is both stylish and memorable. It has also been praised as “Excellently crafted” and “A welcoming turn-based RPG,” reflecting its strong presentation and inviting gameplay.

Players can also download a demo prologue episode, which takes place on the day before the events of the main game and follows the Ivory Order as they search for a stolen royal treasure. This adds an engaging preview of the world and its storytelling style.

For those who want even more variety, the game also offers DLC that changes the design of in-game components such as cards and avatars, including styles inspired by other YOKO TARO titles and even an option to transform all characters and enemies into a pixel art style. Voice of Cards: The Isle Dragon Roars delivers a distinctive, well-made RPG experience with a clever card-based identity and a strong sense of adventure.
