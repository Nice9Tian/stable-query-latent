Voice of Cards: The Isle Dragon Roars is a role-playing game set in a world of swords and sorcery, with its story presented entirely through cards. The game follows a self-proclaimed hero who sets out to slay a dragon that has recently awakened. Its presentation is inspired by tabletop RPGs, with events unfolding through narration from a gamesmaster.

The game emphasizes a distinctive card-based format for both storytelling and presentation, creating an experience that is described as new while also evoking a nostalgic feel. It is developed with creative direction by YOKO TARO, music by Keiichi Okabe, and character design by Kimihiko Fujisaka.

A demo is available as a prologue episode, showing what happened on the day before the events of the main game. In this episode, the Ivory Order searches for a stolen royal treasure.

The store page also mentions downloadable content sets. One set for Voice of Cards: The Isle Dragon Roars includes seven DLC items that can change the design of in-game components such as cards and avatars to styles based on other YOKO TARO titles, as well as an item that changes all characters and enemies to a pixel art appearance. The page also references a trilogy bundle containing all three Voice of Cards titles: Voice of Cards: The Isle Dragon Roars, Voice of Cards: The Forsaken Maiden, and Voice of Cards: The Beasts of Burden, along with DLC for each title.

Reviews highlighted on the page describe the game as “Excellently crafted” and “A welcoming turn-based RPG.” The page also notes that parts of the game requiring an internet connection may cease to be supported at any point.
