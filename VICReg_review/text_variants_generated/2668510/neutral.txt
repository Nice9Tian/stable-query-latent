Red Dead Redemption for PC includes the digital version of both Red Dead Redemption and Undead Nightmare, offering the complete single-player experiences of each game along with bonus content from the Game of the Year Edition. Multiplayer content is not included.

The game follows former outlaw John Marston as he tracks down the last remaining members of the notorious Van der Linde Gang, marking the PC debut of the critically acclaimed predecessor to Red Dead Redemption 2. Alongside the main story, players can also experience Undead Nightmare, the game’s zombie-horror companion story, in the same package.

The PC release adds several features designed for the platform, including HDR lighting, native 4K resolution at up to 144hz on supported hardware, support for ultrawide (21:9) and super ultrawide (32:9) monitors, adjustable draw distances, shadow quality settings, and more.
