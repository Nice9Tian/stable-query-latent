Red Dead Redemption on PC delivers a remarkable way to experience one of the most celebrated Western adventures ever made. This package includes the digital version of Red Dead Redemption and Undead Nightmare, giving players the complete single-player experiences of both games, along with bonus content from the Game of the Year Edition. While multiplayer content is not included, the offering focuses on what made the series so memorable: its story, atmosphere, and standout adventures.

Players follow former outlaw John Marston as he hunts down the last remaining members of the notorious Van der Linde Gang in the PC debut of the critically acclaimed predecessor to Red Dead Redemption 2. Alongside that unforgettable journey comes Undead Nightmare, the iconic zombie-horror companion story, making this a strong and varied bundle that can be enjoyed together in stunning detail.

The PC version also brings a range of enhancements designed to showcase the game at its best. These include HDR lighting, native 4K resolution at up to 144hz on supported hardware, support for Ultrawide (21:9) and Super Ultrawide (32:9) monitors, adjustable draw distances, shadow quality settings, and more. Altogether, Red Dead Redemption on PC stands as an excellent, feature-rich release of a classic experience.
