**Silica** is an ambitious and exciting crossover of FPS and RTS that delivers epic photorealistic battles on a grand scale. Set on the sandy dunes of planet Baltarus, the game lets you fight for domination as either humans or aliens, with the freedom to lead from above as a commander or join the assault directly from the ground.

One of Silica’s standout strengths is its combined arms warfare. Battles unfold across land and sky, with players able to take control of both ground and air vehicles to gain the upper hand. This creates a powerful sense of strategic variety and battlefield presence, where every unit can contribute to the struggle for control.

As an RTS commander, you can direct large-scale engagements from a top-down perspective, coordinating complex operations and using tactical skill to shape the flow of battle. At the same time, players who prefer direct action can jump into the FPS side and participate in the fight firsthand. This dual approach gives Silica a distinctive identity and makes every encounter feel dynamic and flexible.

The game also emphasizes realistic unit scale, presenting everything from small infantry squads to enormous harvesters and massive buildings with careful attention to size and proportion. This attention to detail helps create an immersive battlefield that feels vast, believable, and visually impressive.

Silica offers a compelling blend of strategy, action, and scale, bringing together the thrill of FPS combat and the depth of RTS command in one impressive experience.
