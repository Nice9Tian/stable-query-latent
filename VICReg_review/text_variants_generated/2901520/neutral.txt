An Alt Girl for Skoof is a visual novella-style first-person game built around the concept of “skoofs” and “altooshkas.” The store page presents it as an immersive skoof simulator, made in about one month, and describes it as a short experience that aims to capture the perspective of a man in his forties: unmarried, overweight, balding, and dissatisfied with life.

According to the page, the player begins as a middle-aged man whose worldview is already fully formed and whose life lacks happiness. The premise is that, by remembering a “Skoofuslugi” password, he can obtain his “rightful alt girl” in a few clicks. The game uses the terms “skoof” and “altooshka” in the Russian slang sense explained on the page: “skoof” refers to an unpleasant-looking man around 30–50 years old with signs of poor health or a difficult life, while “altooshka” means an alt girl, typically associated with alternative fashion, non-mainstream music, and left-wing views.

The game features three alt girls to choose from, intended to suit different tastes, and offers a choice system that the description says does not make much difference in the end. It includes at least five different endings. The inner voice of the protagonist is voiced by Prophet Sunboy. The page also credits comic book writer Vitaliy Terletski and viral anime video creator RIKANI as the authors, and names Nikita Kryukov, known for the soundtrack of Milk outside a bag of milk outside a bag of milk, as the composer.

Overall, the store text positions the game as a brief, comedic, and self-aware visual novella about a specific subculture pairing, emphasizing its unusual premise, multiple romance options, and branching endings.
