This title presents itself as a first-person visual-novel-style simulator centered on the life of a middle-aged, unmarried man whose outlook is already fixed and whose life lacks happiness. The premise is framed around the idea that, by recalling the login credentials for a fictional service, the player can obtain a supposed “rightful” alternative-style girl in just a few clicks. The tone is deliberately self-aware and satirical, with the game openly describing itself as a compact project created within one month and acknowledging that better examples exist in the genre.

The experience is built around an immersive point of view, aiming to place the player in the role of an overweight, balding man in his forties. The game uses a visual novella format and is presented as a short but polished experience. It draws on a slang-inspired setting where one fictional term refers to a man in his 30s to 50s who looks older than he is due to poor health, weight issues, hair loss, smoking, an unattractive family situation, or an unhappy job, while another fictional term refers to an alternative-fashion girl associated with goth or emo styling, non-mainstream music, and left-leaning views.

The gameplay includes a choice system, though the page notes that these decisions do not significantly change the outcome. Players can select from three different alternative-style girl options, which are described as broad enough to appeal to many tastes. The story offers at least five distinct endings, giving the narrative some replay value despite the limited impact of individual choices.

The page also highlights the creative contributors behind the game, including a comic book writer, a viral anime video creator, a composer known for work on another surreal narrative title, and a voice performance for the protagonist’s inner monologue by a performer described as real. Overall, the game is presented as a short, genre-specific satire focused on middle-aged loneliness, alternative fashion subculture, and choice-driven storytelling.
