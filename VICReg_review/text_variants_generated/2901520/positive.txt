**An alt girl for skoof** is a first-person skoof simulator presented in a visual novella format, offering a short but memorable immersion into an unusual romantic premise. The game places you in the role of a “skoof” — a slang term for a man in his 30s to 50s who looks older than he is, often associated with poor physical condition, obesity, baldness, smoking, an unpleasant job, and family frustration — and asks you to navigate a world where, after remembering your Skoofuslugi password, you can supposedly secure your rightful alt girl in just a few clicks.

The game openly embraces its identity as a compact, one-month creation, while still presenting itself as a unique experience in the genre. Its visual novella structure focuses on choices, dialogue, and atmosphere, with an “immersive” first-person perspective designed to let players feel the life of an unmarried, overweight, balding middle-aged man. The choice system is described as diverse, even if it does not change much in the end, and the game still offers at least five different endings to discover.

One of the central features is the selection of three different alt girls, giving players a range of options that should appeal to many tastes. The game also includes a notable creative team: comic book writer Vitaliy Terletski and viral anime video creator RIKANI are credited as the authors, while the music is composed by Nikita Kryukov, known for *Milk outside a bag of milk outside a bag of milk*. The main character’s inner voice is voiced by Prophet Sunboy, adding another distinct layer to the experience.

Overall, *An alt girl for skoof* presents itself as a bold, self-aware, and highly specific visual novella that combines humor, subculture references, branching endings, and a memorable premise into a short but distinctive game.
