Holdfast: Nations At War is an online multiplayer first- and third-person roleplay shooter that places community at the forefront. It features large-scale battles on land and at sea across multiple eras, with server populations of over 150 players.

The game lets players take part in historic conflicts ranging from the Napoleonic Wars to World War One. In these battles, players can charge into combat, fire coordinated volleys, and respond to orders in large formations. Land warfare includes open-field engagements, coastal siege battles, artillery support, defensive construction, and bayonet charges. The game also includes mounted combat, allowing players to ride horseback with swords, pistols, and carbines. Mounted hussar and dragoon classes are designed for fast movement across the battlefield.

Players can join the army, navy, or coast guard, and choose from 26 classes, each with its own traits and abilities that contribute to team survival. The game also offers naval combat aboard warships from the age of sail, where crews must work together to keep vessels afloat and master different ship types with distinct characteristics.

Progression and customization are part of the experience. Players can unlock more than 20 heads, equip muskets and melee weapons from other nations, and adjust their character’s voice pitch for shouts and calls. Factions include the British Empire, French Empire, Kingdom of Prussia, Russian Empire, Kingdom of Italy, Central Powers, and Allied Powers.

Holdfast: Nations At War also supports regiment play through clans and organized events, where players battle in formations relevant to the Napoleonic era. Alongside combat systems, the game emphasizes role-play features and proximity voice chat, bringing together role-players, regiment members, casual players, and those looking for humorous moments.
