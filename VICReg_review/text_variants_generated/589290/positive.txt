Holdfast: Nations At War is an online multiplayer first- and third-person roleplay shooter that puts community at the center of every battle. Set across historic land and sea conflicts, it lets players take part in large-scale warfare spanning multiple eras, with support for over 150 players per server. From Napoleonic field battles to the frontlines of World War One, the game captures the intensity of mass combat with a strong emphasis on cooperation, formation fighting, and coordinated teamwork.

On the battlefield, players can join the army, navy, or coast guard and take on a wide variety of roles. You may hold the line in open-field engagements, fire cannon in naval battles, operate artillery from the rear, build defenses, or charge forward with bayonet fixed. The game features 26 classes, each with its own traits and abilities that contribute to the survival and success of the team. Mounted combat is also a major part of the experience, with horse-mounted classes such as hussars and dragoons able to move quickly across the battlefield while fighting with swords, pistols, and carbines.

Naval warfare is another highlight, bringing players aboard warships from the golden age of sail. Crews must work together to keep their vessel afloat while mastering different ship types, each with distinct characteristics. Alongside these battles, players can enlist with a regiment and rise through the ranks, taking part in organized community events built around Napoleonic-era formations and tactics.

Holdfast: Nations At War also offers progression and customization that let battle-hardened soldiers show off their achievements. Players can unlock more than 20 heads, equip muskets and melee weapons from other nations, and even adjust the pitch of their character’s voice for shouts and calls. With allegiance options including the British Empire, French Empire, Kingdom of Prussia, Russian Empire, Kingdom of Italy, Central Powers, and Allied Powers, the game provides plenty of room to choose a side and fight for dominance.

What makes Holdfast especially memorable is its vibrant roleplay-driven community. Proximity voice chat and a range of role-play features help bring together regiment members, casual players, and those simply there for the unexpected humorous moments. It is a game built not only on historic battles, but also on the lively social experience that surrounds them.
