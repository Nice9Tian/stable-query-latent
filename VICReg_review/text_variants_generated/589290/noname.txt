This online multiplayer first- and third-person roleplay shooter places community at the center of its design. It features large-scale historic battles on land and at sea, with more than 150 players per server participating in conflicts set across multiple eras. Players can take part in coordinated line fighting, naval engagements, coastal defense scenarios, and open-field clashes where teamwork and formation discipline matter.

The game offers a broad military structure with options to join land forces, naval crews, or coastal defenders. Combat situations vary from steady musket volleys and artillery support to cannon fire, defensive construction, and bayonet charges. Mounted combat also plays a role, allowing players to fight from horseback with weapons such as swords, pistols, and carbines, while fast cavalry-style classes move quickly across the battlefield.

A wide class system provides 26 different roles, each with distinct traits and abilities that contribute to team survival. Progression and customization are also significant features, letting players unlock cosmetic head options, use weapons from other nations, and adjust the pitch of their character’s voice for shouts and calls. These systems support both personal expression and battlefield identity.

The game spans several historical settings, including Napoleonic warfare and World War One. Players can choose allegiances among multiple factions, including empires, kingdoms, and wartime alliances, and then fight for control and dominance. Naval combat is another major pillar, with several ship types that each handle differently and require coordinated crew effort to keep them operational.

Community organization is deeply integrated through regiments, which function as clans and support structured play, organized events, and formation-based battles. Proximity voice chat and roleplay features further expand the social experience, bringing together serious role-players, regiment members, casual players, and those looking for humorous emergent moments.
