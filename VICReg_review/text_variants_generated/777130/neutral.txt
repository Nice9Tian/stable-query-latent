Blush Blush is a dating game blended with idle game elements. It presents a lighthearted story in which the player must rescue a series of fun, attractive anime boys who have been cursed by a dark spell that can only be broken with the power of love.

The gameplay centers on managing time wisely. Players work various jobs, earn stat points, and arrange dates and gifts for their love interests. This creates a mix of strategy and story progression, as the player gradually becomes a more accomplished dater while working to woo more than a dozen characters, with more planned for the future.

The game features 22 and counting unlockable guys, along with 20 Phone Flings that let players text characters and collect cute selfies. It also includes over 240 high-resolution pictures to collect and more than 28,000 words of dialogue, with an option to hear a fully voiced version performed by a cast of actors. Players can also dress their characters in a large number of special outfits.

As an idle game, Blush Blush includes “Time Blocks” that can advance progress even while the player is not actively playing. Optional in-game purchases are available to help speed up progress.
