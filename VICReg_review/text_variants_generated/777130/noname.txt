This title combines the structure of a dating game with idle-game progression, wrapped in a lighthearted story about rescuing a group of charming anime-style boys who have been placed under a dark curse. As the player advances, the central goal is to break that curse through romance, using a blend of time management, strategy, and story-driven choices.

Gameplay is presented as straightforward and approachable: the player spends time by taking on different jobs, earning stat points, and organizing dates and gifts for potential love interests. Progress comes from choosing how to allocate time and resources efficiently, gradually building toward greater success as a dater. The experience is designed to unfold over a large cast of characters, with more planned beyond the initial lineup.

The game includes 22 unlockable male characters, along with 20 phone-conversation segments that let the player text and collect selfies. There are also more than 240 high-resolution images to unlock, and over 28,000 words of dialogue, with the option to hear a fully voiced version performed by a cast of actors. Customization is supported through a wide range of special outfits for the characters.

A notable idle mechanic called “Shift Blocks” allows progress to continue even while the player is away. Optional in-game purchases are also available to speed up advancement. Overall, the game emphasizes a mix of romance, collection, and incremental progression within a cheerful, character-focused structure.
