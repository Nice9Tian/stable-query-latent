Blush Blush is a charming blend of dating game and idle game, offering a lighthearted romantic adventure with a playful anime-inspired cast. In this story, the player takes on the task of rescuing a series of fun, attractive anime boys who have fallen under a dark curse—one that, naturally, can only be broken by the power of love.

The gameplay is simple to pick up yet engaging in its mix of strategy and story. Players must manage their time wisely by working different jobs, earning stat points, and planning dates and gifts for their love interests. As progress builds, the player gradually grows into an accomplished dater, with the opportunity to woo more than a dozen characters, and even more planned for the future.

Blush Blush also stands out with a generous amount of content. It features 22 and counting hunky guys to unlock, 20 Phone Flings for text conversations and cute selfies, over 240 high-resolution pictures to collect, and more than 28,000 words of dialogue, with a fully voiced version available from an incredibly suave cast of actors. There are also tons of special outfits to dress your husbandos in, adding even more personality and variety to the experience.

Its idle mechanics make the game especially convenient, since “Time Blocks” can keep advancing your progress even while you are not playing. Optional in-game purchases are also available to help speed things up further. With its upbeat tone, appealing cast, and abundance of collectible content, Blush Blush delivers a delightful and distinctive romantic idle experience.
