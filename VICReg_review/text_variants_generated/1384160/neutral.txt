GUILTY GEAR -STRIVE- is a fighting game developed by Arc System Works and created by Daisuke Ishiwatari. It is the latest entry in the Guilty Gear series and is presented as a fast-paced, heavy metal-inspired experience with stylish action and a strong audiovisual identity.

The game is designed to be accessible to newcomers while still offering depth for experienced fighting game players. According to the store page, it introduces new gameplay mechanics intended to make the fundamentals easier to learn without losing the series’ creative and technical combat. One highlighted feature is a wall-breaking mechanic that opens up new approaches to battle.

GUILTY GEAR -STRIVE- also continues the series’ emphasis on presentation. It features high-energy music composed by Daisuke Ishiwatari and uses detailed 2D/3D cell-shaded visuals. The game is described as offering striking, dynamic graphics alongside a soundtrack meant to match the intensity of the fights.

In story terms, the game follows the final confrontation with That Man, who is said to be responsible for the destruction and damage affecting the world and its inhabitants. The narrative includes returning characters such as Sol Badguy and Ky Kiske, and the starting roster contains 15 unique characters. The page presents this as the conclusion to a story that has been building for 20 years.

The store page also lists multiple editions and additional content, including the Blazing Edition, Season Passes 1 through 4, digital sound and artwork content, special colors, anniversary colors, and the original soundtrack “Necessary Discrepancy,” which is available separately on Steam.
