This life simulation game centers on rebuilding a devastated sect and reviving a mountain valley while pursuing immortality through spiritual cultivation. Players take on the role of a newcomer who joins a group of fellow disciples and helps turn a damaged settlement into a thriving village. The experience blends farming, cooking, crafting, exploration, and relationship-building into a progression-focused journey.

At its core, the game emphasizes daily life and long-term growth. Players manage farmland, grow crops across changing seasons and weather, and use magical tools to improve cultivation work. Beyond agriculture, they can learn a wide range of practical skills, including fishing, cooking, crafting elixirs, and making furniture. These abilities support both the restoration of the settlement and the character’s advancement on the path toward immortality. Training also plays an important role, with classes and tests serving as milestones in the cultivation journey.

Exploration expands the game beyond the home valley. Players can travel to distant lands and visit more than ten distinct mystical regions, where they search for treasures, gather crafting materials, and battle mythical creatures using magic. Different areas require different abilities and strategies, making progression tied to both preparation and experimentation.

Social interaction is another major element. The player can build bonds with their fellow sect members, learn about their personalities, backgrounds, and motivations, and assist them with everyday problems. These relationships unlock new items, missions, and skills, and some characters can even be invited to share the player’s living space.

Home building and customization are also included. By collecting spirit stones, unlocking new territories, and starting construction projects, players can create a peaceful sanctuary in the valley. With help from companions and through their own crafting abilities, they can add decorations, magical tools, and other improvements to their home and farm.
