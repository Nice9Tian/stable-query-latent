Immortal Life is a life simulation game centered on restoring the Guiyun Sect after its near-total destruction while pursuing immortality through spiritual cultivation. Set in the Misty Valley, the game has players help rebuild a settlement into a prosperous village by farming, cooking, crafting decorations, and working alongside new brothers and sisters in the sect. As the village grows, players learn more about the people around them, their stories, and their motivations, while also advancing on a cultivation path that eventually takes them beyond the valley.

The game combines farming and progression systems with spiritual training. Players can cultivate fields using magic, care for crops with enchanted tools, and manage farmland through changing seasons and weather. A variety of skills can be developed, including fishing, cooking, crafting elixirs, and making furniture. These skills contribute both to restoring the Misty Valley and to advancing the character’s cultivation journey. Players also attend classes and take tests to reach new stages in cultivation.

Exploration is another major part of the game. Players travel to distant lands and more than 10 Mystic Realms, where they can uncover treasures, gather crafting materials, and use magic to defeat mythical creatures. Different realms require different abilities and strategies in order to obtain needed items.

Immortal Life also emphasizes relationships within the sect. Players get to know their brothers and sisters, help them with daily problems, and strengthen their bonds. Each character has distinct personality traits and cultivation interests, and assisting them can unlock new items, missions, and skills. They can also be invited to share the player’s dwelling.

Home building and construction further expand the experience. By acquiring spirit stones and unlocking new territories, players can begin construction projects to create a peaceful sanctuary in the valley. Crafting skills and support from sect members allow players to obtain new decorations and magical tools for both the farm and the house.
