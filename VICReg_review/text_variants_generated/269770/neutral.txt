Secrets of Grindea is an old-school action RPG with co-op support for up to four players. Designed as both a tribute to and a parody of classic SNES-era games, it sends players on a journey through fantastical lands in search of truth, friendship, and rare treasures.

The game features a rich world with more than 300 unique, colorful characters and an engaging story that drives the adventure forward. Players can experience the full storyline together in co-op, or bring in extra help for more difficult challenges.

Character customization is a major part of the experience. Players can design a character to represent themselves and mix and match from 31 spells and skills spread across 9 different categories. The game also includes more than 30 boss fights, featuring enemies such as overgrown slimes, giant robots, and mimics.

Beyond the main story, Secrets of Grindea offers a range of side activities, including home design, fishing, crafting, pet taming, mini-games, and plant growing. In addition, Arcade Mode provides a separate roguelite experience from Story Mode, where players fight their way upward on a single life while building a town as they progress.
