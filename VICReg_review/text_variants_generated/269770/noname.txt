This old-school action RPG offers cooperative play for up to four players and takes clear inspiration from classic 16-bit-era adventures, sometimes paying tribute to them and sometimes playfully parodying them. Players travel through fantastical regions, fight a wide variety of enemies and bosses, and pursue a central quest focused on truth, friendship, and the search for the world’s rarest treasures.

The game features a large world populated by more than 300 distinct and colorful characters, with an engaging story that guides progression. Cooperative play is available throughout the full storyline, allowing friends to join in for the complete experience or to assist with tougher challenges.

Character creation and build variety are a major part of the experience. Players can create a personalized hero and combine any of 31 spells and skills drawn from 9 categories, making it possible to experiment with many different playstyles. The game also includes more than 30 boss encounters, ranging from oversized slimes and giant robots to shape-shifting foes.

Beyond the main story, there is a broad range of side activities. Players can design a home, fish, craft items, tame pets, play mini-games, and grow plants, providing plenty to do between major quests. In addition, the game includes a separate Arcade Mode, which offers a roguelite experience distinct from Story Mode. In this mode, players advance on a single life while building a town as they progress, testing how far they can go before failing.
