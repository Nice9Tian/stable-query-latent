*Sonic Origins Plus* is an expanded multi-game collection of classic *Sonic the Hedgehog* titles. It includes the four main games from *Sonic Origins* — *Sonic the Hedgehog*, *Sonic 2*, *Sonic 3 & Knuckles*, and *Sonic CD* — presented with remastered visuals, new opening and ending animations, and additional content and improvements.

The collection offers both Classic Mode and Anniversary Mode. Classic Mode retains the original resolution and limited lives, while Anniversary Mode features unlimited lives and a widescreen HD presentation. Players can also unlock a range of extras by completing missions, including Mirror Mode, new stages, challenges, behind-the-scenes content, a music player, animated shorts, and special stages.

A major addition in *Sonic Origins Plus* is its expanded roster of playable characters. In addition to Sonic, Tails, and Knuckles, Amy Rose is playable for the first time in *Sonic the Hedgehog 1*, *Sonic 2*, *Sonic 3 & Knuckles*, and *Sonic CD*. The bundle also adds playable Knuckles in *Sonic CD*.

Beyond the core games, the package includes 12 Sonic Game Gear titles, such as *Sonic Drift 2*, *Sonic Spinball*, and *Tails Adventure*. It also contains previously released add-on content, including the Classic Music Pack and Premium Fun Pack, which add Extreme Missions, Mirror Mode, additional screen borders, new character animations in menus, and more music from other Sonic titles.

Overall, *Sonic Origins Plus* is presented as a comprehensive way to experience these classic Sonic games with extra modes, characters, and bonus content.
