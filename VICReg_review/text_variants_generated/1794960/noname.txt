This expanded retro collection brings together multiple classic side-scrolling platform games in one package, presenting them with remastered visuals and a range of added features. It includes four main titles from the series, along with a separate set of twelve handheld entries, previously released add-on content, and additional improvements designed to modernize the overall experience while preserving the original gameplay structure.

The collection offers two main play styles. In the traditional mode, players experience the games with the original resolution and limited lives. A separate anniversary-style mode introduces widescreen HD presentation and unlimited lives, making it a more forgiving option for players who want a smoother run through the levels. Both modes retain the core focus on fast movement, jumping, and reaching the end of each stage across a variety of zones.

Several unlockable extras are available through mission completion. These include a mirrored version of play, new stages, special challenges, behind-the-scenes material, a music player, animated shorts, and special stages. The package also contains additional screen borders, extra music from other series entries, new menu animations for characters, and further mission-based content.

The playable roster is broader than in the original releases. Players can choose from multiple familiar heroes, and a new character is added to the lineup across the main titles, including the first time this character appears as playable in the included games. One of the main entries also gains an additional playable character as part of the updated bundle.

Beyond the core console titles, the collection includes twelve handheld games, offering more variety through a separate set of retro releases. Notable examples mentioned in the package are racing, pinball-style, and adventure-focused entries. Overall, the collection is presented as a comprehensive way to revisit these classic games with added content, updated presentation, and more ways to play.
