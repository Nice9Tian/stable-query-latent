Sonic Origins Plus presents itself as a definitive celebration of classic Sonic, but the reality is more like a repackaged collection padded with extras. At its core, the bundle includes the four main games from Sonic Origins: Sonic the Hedgehog, Sonic 2, Sonic 3 & Knuckles, and Sonic CD, each with remastered visuals and new opening and ending animations. That sounds substantial, but the structure remains heavily rooted in old content.

The collection offers two ways to play the main games. Classic Mode keeps the original resolution and limited lives, while Anniversary Mode switches to a widescreen HD presentation with unlimited lives. These options give the package some flexibility, though they do not fundamentally change the age of the games underneath. Missions can be completed to unlock Mirror Mode, new stages, challenges, behind-the-scenes content, a music player, animated shorts, and special stages, but much of this feels like a checklist of extras rather than meaningful reinvention.

The biggest additions are the bonus characters and side content. Amy Rose is playable for the first time in Sonic the Hedgehog 1, Sonic 2, Sonic 3 & Knuckles, and Sonic CD, and Knuckles is also playable in Sonic CD. The bundle also includes 12 Sonic Game Gear titles, such as Sonic Drift 2, Sonic Spinball, and Tails Adventure. There is also previously released add-on content, plus the Classic Music Pack and Premium Fun Pack, which add Extreme Missions, Mirror Mode, extra screen borders, new menu animations, and additional music from other Sonic games.

Even with all of this, Sonic Origins Plus still feels like a compilation built around nostalgia rather than a strong new experience. It offers a lot of content on paper, but most of that content is either old material or minor additions to older games. It may be the most complete way to access these classic titles, but that does not make it especially exciting.
