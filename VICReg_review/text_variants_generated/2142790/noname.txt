Set in a quiet village tucked between forest and sea, this life-simulation RPG begins with a new start on an overgrown homestead offered in exchange for helping restore a town damaged by an earthquake. As strange magic spreads through the land, the player is invited to rebuild the settlement, revive the surrounding area, and shape a personal life in a world full of opportunity.

The experience combines farming, fishing, mining, and crafting with exploration and community life. Players can customize their character, home, and farm, while developing the land into something of their own. Animals can be raised, including the possibility of breeding rare color variants, and magic can be unlocked to support both farming and adventuring.

Beyond daily work, the game emphasizes relationships and seasonal progression. It features more than 30 villagers with individual stories, as well as 12 potential romantic partners, though relationship progression beyond a certain point and marriage are planned for a future update. The changing seasons bring new quests, festivals, and even different villager outfits, adding variety to life in the village.

Adventure is also part of the structure, with battles against enemies and treasure hunting in ancient ruins. Overall, the game presents a blend of farming simulation, town restoration, social interaction, and fantasy exploration, framed as a spiritual successor to late-1990s farm-sim RPGs.
