*Fields of Mistria* presents itself as a cozy farm-sim RPG, but at the moment it reads more like a promise of a fuller game still to come. The setup is straightforward: you arrive in Mistria, an idyllic village between forest and sea, and are handed an overgrown homestead in exchange for helping restore a town damaged by an earthquake and affected by strange magic. The premise is charming on paper, yet the store page leans heavily on future potential rather than a complete experience.

The game advertises the usual array of life-sim activities: farming, fishing, mining, and crafting, all aimed at rebuilding the town and shaping your own homestead. Character, home, and farm customization are included, along with seasons, quests, festivals, and villager outfits. There is also a focus on social interaction, with more than 30 villagers to meet and 12 marriage candidates to pursue. However, the page itself undercuts that romantic hook by stating that heart progression beyond 4 hearts and marriage will be added in future updates, making that feature feel unfinished rather than truly present.

Beyond the daily chores, *Fields of Mistria* includes animals, breeding rare color variants, magic unlocked for farming and adventuring, and combat in ancient ruins where players can battle foes and find treasure. It is clearly trying to blend farming sim comfort with adventure-RPG structure. But for all the breadth of features listed, the description leaves the impression of an early access-like framework built on familiar genre staples, with several of its biggest draws still locked behind updates.
