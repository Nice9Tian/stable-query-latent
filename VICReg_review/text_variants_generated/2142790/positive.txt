**Fields of Mistria** invites players into a warm, hopeful new life in a charming village nestled between forest and sea. After an earthquake leaves Mistria damaged and strange magic begins to stir through the land, you are given an overgrown homestead full of potential and asked to help restore the town to its former glory. The result is a heartfelt farm-sim RPG experience inspired by the beloved classics of the late 90s and beyond, blending farming, exploration, relationships, and adventure into one appealing package.

At the center of the game is the freedom to shape your own story. You can customize your character, home, and farm to match your personal style, then build the farm of your dreams as you work to revitalize the town. Farming is only the beginning: you can also fish, mine, and craft as part of your daily life in Mistria. Along the way, you’ll raise adorable animals and even breed rare color variants, adding extra delight to the homestead experience.

Magic plays an important role in both farming and adventure. As you progress, you’ll unlock magical abilities that help you tend your land and support you during exploration. The world also offers plenty to discover beyond the farm, including ancient ruins where you can battle foes, uncover treasures, and venture deeper into the mysteries surrounding the land.

Mistria itself feels alive and full of character. There are more than 30 villagers to meet, each with their own stories to follow, and the changing seasons bring new quests, festivals, and even villager outfits to enjoy. The social side is equally inviting, with 12 marriage candidates to get to know and potentially pursue, with future updates planned to expand heart progression past 4 hearts and add marriage itself.

Altogether, **Fields of Mistria** presents a lovingly crafted world filled with charm, growth, and possibility. It offers the comforting rhythm of farm life, the excitement of adventure, and the promise of meaningful connections, making it a standout choice for players who want a richly detailed and optimistic life-sim journey.
