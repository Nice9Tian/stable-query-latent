Fields of Mistria is a life-sim and farm-sim RPG set in the idyllic village of Mistria, a place tucked between forest and sea. In the game, the player receives an overgrown homestead and is asked to help restore the town after an earthquake causes damage and strange magic begins to spread through the land.

The game presents itself as a spiritual successor to farm-sim RPGs from the late 1990s and beyond. Players can build and manage their ideal farm while exploring a world centered on magic, romance, and adventure. Character, home, and farm customization are part of the experience, allowing players to shape their surroundings and appearance to suit their style.

Core activities include farming, fishing, mining, and crafting, all of which contribute to revitalizing the town. The game also includes animal raising, with the added possibility of breeding rare color variants. Magic can be unlocked and used to support both farming and adventuring.

Fields of Mistria features 30+ villagers whose stories can be followed, as well as 12 marriage candidates. The store page notes that heart progression beyond 4 hearts and marriage itself will be added in future updates. As the seasons change, players can take part in new quests and festivals, and villagers may also wear seasonal outfits.

Adventure and combat are also part of the game, with players battling foes, uncovering treasures, and exploring ancient ruins. Overall, Fields of Mistria combines farming, social interaction, exploration, and progression in a changing world shaped by restoration and magic.
