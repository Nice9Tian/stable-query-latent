Sword of Convallaria is a tactical role-playing game set in Iria, a mineral-rich country whose magical resources attract dangerous external factions. As unrest spreads and riots break out, players take on the role of a mercenary leader navigating difficult situations while trying to determine the fate of the country. The game places strong emphasis on story, presenting a choice-based narrative in which player decisions affect how the town develops, how relationships and skills are built, and how the storyline unfolds.

Combat is turn-based and grid-based, with battles that require careful positioning and strategic use of the environment. Players can deploy unique allies against different enemy types and make use of battlefield features such as cliffs, boulders, obstacles, and even bangalores to gain an advantage. The game also focuses on building and managing a mercenary group by recruiting and training companions at the tavern, teaching them skills, forging equipment, and improving their stats in the training field before sending them on quests with different factions.

Visually, Sword of Convallaria uses a “NeoPixel” style that keeps pixel-art aesthetics while adding modern 3D rendering techniques such as real-time shading, full-screen bloom, dynamic depth of field, and HDR for a high-definition presentation. The game’s soundtrack is composed by Hitoshi Sakimoto, known for his work on Final Fantasy Tactics, Final Fantasy XII, and Tactics Ogre. It also features Japanese voice performances from more than 40 anime and game voice actors, including Kazuhiko Inoue, Aoi Yuki, and Takuya Eguchi.
