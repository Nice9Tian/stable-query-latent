This strategy role-playing game is set in a mineral-rich country whose magical resources have attracted the attention of hostile outside forces. As unrest grows and riots spread, the player takes on the role of a mercenary leader tasked with navigating political and social turmoil while trying to secure the future of the nation. The story is presented as a layered conflict involving danger, shifting alliances, and difficult decisions.

At its core, the game features grid-based tactical combat. Battles are turn-based and emphasize positioning, with players deploying distinct allies against varied enemy types. The battlefield itself is an important part of combat, since cliffs, boulders, obstacles, and explosive devices can all be used to gain an advantage. Success depends not only on team composition, but also on making effective use of the environment.

The game also includes a choice-driven narrative system. Player decisions affect how the town develops and can alter the direction of the story. Relationships and character growth play a meaningful role, and the narrative changes based on the choices made and the achievements earned along the way.

Visually, the game combines pixel-style presentation with modern rendering techniques. This hybrid approach adds effects such as real-time shading, full-screen bloom, dynamic depth of field, and HDR, aiming to create a polished high-definition look while preserving a retro-inspired style.

Progression centers on recruiting and developing a group of companions. Characters can be gathered at a tavern, trained in a field, outfitted with equipment at a forge, and taught new skills. These systems support the formation of a custom mercenary team that can be sent on quests involving different factions.

The game also places strong emphasis on audio and presentation. Its music is composed by a renowned soundtrack creator known for work on several classic tactical RPGs, and the cast includes more than 40 voice actors from anime and games, giving the characters a fully voiced performance.
