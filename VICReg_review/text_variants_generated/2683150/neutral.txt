Ale & Tale Tavern is a cooperative first-person fantasy tavern simulator set in an open world, combining elements of action, fishing, hunting, exploration, and questing with a strong focus on cooking and tavern management. It can be played solo or with up to four players in co-op mode.

Players take on the role of the owner of an abandoned tavern and work to restore it to its former glory and popularity. This involves managing the tavern, cooking dishes and drinks for visitors, decorating the space, crafting, delivering orders, and collecting coin from satisfied customers. The game also includes systems for growing plants, breeding animals, hunting, fishing, and exploring recipes.

The world is described as cozy, beautiful, and handcrafted, offering freedom to explore at your own pace. Players can complete a variety of story quests, meet unique and amusing characters, and take part in diverse activities across the game world. In addition to tavern-related tasks, there are dangers to face, including enemies and monsters, with combat supported by different types of weapons.

A notable feature is the presence of Owl Helpers, which automate tavern tasks. The game also emphasizes dynamic world elements and superior graphics. Overall, Ale & Tale Tavern offers a mix of management, adventure, combat, and open-ended exploration, allowing players to engage in whichever activities they prefer.
