*Like a Dragon Gaiden: The Man Who Erased His Name* is an action game centered on Kazuma Kiryu, a former legendary yakuza who fakes his own death and abandons his name to protect his family. Living under the codename “Joryu,” he is drawn back into conflict by a mysterious figure trying to force him out of hiding. The story takes place across vibrant locations such as Sotenbori and Yokohama, where players meet various characters and take part in different activities as the narrative unfolds.

The game features visceral melee combat with two distinct fighting styles that can be switched dynamically. The Yakuza style focuses on aggressive, powerful attacks that emphasize Kiryu’s strength and flair. The Agent style is built around speed and precision, and includes high-tech gadgets such as electrified bind wires that can stun enemies and send them flying. Players are encouraged to adapt strategically and use both styles to fight groups of enemies.

Beyond the main combat, the game offers a range of activities and side content. These include the secret Battle arena, karaoke, a live-action cabaret club, and pocket circuit racing. An informant named Akame also provides sub-missions, adding more challenges and encounters as players explore the game world.

The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the Legendary Fighter Pack, which adds playable characters in the Coliseum: Goro Majima, Taiga Saejima, and Daigo Dojima. The page also notes that a *Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth* Special Trial Version is unlocked after completing the game.
