This action-adventure title follows a former legendary underworld enforcer who has staged his own death and abandoned his identity in order to protect his family. Living under a new codename, he is pulled back into danger by a mysterious person trying to force him out of hiding. The story presents a tense personal conflict built around secrecy, survival, and a reluctant return to violence.

Combat is a central focus, with two distinct fighting styles that can be switched dynamically during battle. One style emphasizes aggressive, heavy melee attacks fueled by raw strength and flair, designed to overwhelm enemies through force. The other favors speed and precision, while introducing advanced equipment such as electrified restraints that can stun opponents and launch them through the air. Players are encouraged to adapt tactically, using both styles to handle groups of enemies in different situations.

The game also offers a range of side activities and optional entertainment. These include fighting in a hidden arena, performing a karaoke song, relaxing at a live-action cabaret venue, and competing in a pocket racing mode. In addition, an informant character provides extra sub-missions that expand the experience as players explore two major urban areas filled with activities and encounters.

A deluxe edition bonus adds access to a special fighter pack featuring three legendary playable characters in the arena setting, while completing the main game unlocks a trial version of a separate later entry in the series. The page also notes that the game may receive updates and additional content over time, and that English voice support is planned to arrive in a post-launch update.
