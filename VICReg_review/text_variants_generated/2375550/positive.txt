*Like a Dragon Gaiden: The Man Who Erased His Name* delivers a powerful action-driven chapter in Kazuma Kiryu’s story, blending dramatic storytelling with the series’ signature variety of gameplay and activities.

The game follows Kiryu, a legendary yakuza who faked his own death and abandoned his name in order to protect his family. Now operating under the codename “Joryu,” he is pulled back into conflict by a mysterious figure trying to force him out of hiding. The result is a gripping action narrative set across vibrant locations filled with memorable characters and plenty to do.

Combat is a major highlight, with two distinct fighting styles that can be switched dynamically in battle. The Yakuza style emphasizes raw power and aggressive offense, letting Kiryu overwhelm enemies with forceful attacks. The Agent style shifts the focus to speed and precision, adding high-tech tools such as electrified bind wires to stun foes and launch them away. Together, these styles create a flexible and satisfying combat system that rewards adaptation and smart decision-making.

Beyond the main story, the game offers a wide range of entertainment. Players can battle in the secret arena, sing karaoke, relax at the live-action cabaret club, or race in Pocket Circuit. An informant named Akame also provides sub-missions, adding more opportunities to explore the worlds of Sotenbori and Yokohama and uncover an expanding showdown.

The Deluxe Edition includes the Legendary Fighter Pack, featuring Goro Majima, Taiga Saejima, and Daigo Dojima as playable characters in the Coliseum on the mysterious container ship known as the Castle. It also includes an unlockable *Like a Dragon: Infinite Wealth* Special Trial Version after completing the game.

With its intense combat, rich character-driven premise, and abundance of side activities, *Like a Dragon Gaiden: The Man Who Erased His Name* stands out as a thrilling and generously packed experience.
