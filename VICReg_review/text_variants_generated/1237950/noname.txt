This multiplayer and single-player action game presents a large-scale galactic battle fantasy set across three eras of a long-running space saga. The edition described here includes the base game along with a wide range of customization content that was originally available through in-game purchase, covering appearances, emotes, voice lines, and victory poses. It also includes past and future free updates as they are released, though content added after a specified date is excluded.

Players can take part in varied battlefield experiences across prequel, classic, and new-trilogy settings, or follow a story-driven campaign centered on an elite special forces soldier. This campaign spans three decades, connecting events between two major films in the series. The story places the player in a role that is active both on the ground and in space, combining combat across different environments.

The game’s multiplayer component supports battles of up to 40 players, featuring iconic heroes alongside era-appropriate infantry and reinforcement units. Combat extends to land, air, and space, with a range of vehicles such as mounted creatures, tanks, speeders, and starfighters. Space battles have been redesigned to emphasize distinct handling, weapon systems, and customization, and include large-scale dogfights and assaults on capital ships.

Progression and customization are central to the experience. Players can upgrade heroes, starfighters, and troopers, each with unique abilities that can be improved or adjusted through progression systems and ability modifiers. These modifiers can add offensive effects, defensive advantages, status changes, or tactical support, allowing for different approaches against opponents.

The game also offers offline practice, letting players earn rewards and customize units before carrying those upgrades into online play. Overall, it is structured as a broad, continuously updated combat experience focused on character customization, vehicle warfare, and battles set within a familiar cinematic universe.
