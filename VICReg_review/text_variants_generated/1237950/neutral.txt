STAR WARS™ Battlefront™ II: Celebration Edition is a Star Wars action game that combines large-scale multiplayer battles with a single-player campaign set across the prequel, classic, and new trilogy eras. The edition includes the base game, all past and future free game updates as they release, and a large bundle of customization content originally obtainable through in-game purchase, including more than 25 hero appearances, more than 125 trooper and reinforcement appearances, more than 100 emotes and voice lines, and more than 70 victory poses. It also includes appearances inspired by Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker for Rey, Finn, and Kylo Ren. Customization content released after December 20, 2019 is not included.

The game offers an endless Star Wars journey with multiplayer battlegrounds spanning all three eras of the saga, along with an emotionally driven single-player story that covers thirty years. Players can customize and upgrade heroes, starfighters, and troopers, each with distinct abilities that can be used in combat. The game also features vehicles such as tauntauns, tanks, and speeders, and allows players to fight iconic characters including Kylo Ren, Darth Maul, and Han Solo.

The single-player campaign puts players in the role of an elite special forces soldier who is active on both the ground and in space. It tells a new Star Wars story set between Star Wars: Return of the Jedi and Star Wars: The Force Awakens.

In multiplayer, the game presents battles with up to 40 players as iconic heroes and era-specific troopers, supported by a wide range of vehicles in land and air combat. Space battles were redesigned from the ground up, with distinct handling, weapons, and customization options. Players can join squadron-based dogfights, fight through locations such as asteroid fields and an Imperial Dock Yard, and take on massive capital ships in matches supporting up to 24 players.

The game also includes offline play for practice, where players can earn rewards and customize troopers and heroes before bringing those upgrades into online multiplayer. Progression is tied to hero customization, allowing players to equip ability modifiers that alter core powers with offensive effects, status boosts, or tactical support options.
