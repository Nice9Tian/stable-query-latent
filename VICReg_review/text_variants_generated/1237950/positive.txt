*STAR WARS™ Battlefront™ II: Celebration Edition* delivers an expansive Star Wars battle fantasy that lets players step into the action as heroes, troopers, pilots, and legendary characters across the galaxy. This edition includes the base game along with all past and future free game updates, plus a vast collection of customization content originally available through in-game purchase up to and including items inspired by *Star Wars™: The Rise of Skywalker™*.

The Celebration Edition offers more than 25 Hero Appearances, including 6 Legendary Appearances and special Rey, Finn, and Kylo Ren looks inspired by *The Rise of Skywalker*. It also includes more than 125 Trooper and Reinforcement Appearances, over 100 Hero and Trooper Emotes and Voice Lines, and more than 70 Hero and Trooper Victory Poses. Customization content released after December 20, 2019 is not included.

Beyond its rich content selection, *Battlefront II* stands out as a complete Star Wars experience spanning all three cinematic eras: the prequel trilogy, the classic trilogy, and the new trilogy. Players can fight in large-scale multiplayer battles with up to 40 players, piloting vehicles on land and in the air, using iconic heroes and era-authentic troopers, and engaging in galactic warfare of impressive scope.

The game also features a story-driven single-player campaign that follows an elite special forces soldier across a 30-year span, bridging the events between *Return of the Jedi* and *The Force Awakens*. This gives the experience both cinematic depth and a fresh perspective within the Star Wars universe.

Combat and progression are highly customizable. Players can upgrade heroes, starfighters, and troopers, each with unique abilities that can be enhanced to suit different battle styles. Ability modifiers allow for even more tactical control, adding lethal effects, helpful boosts, or support options to better counter opponents. The game also includes offline solo play, where players can train, earn rewards, and customize their units before bringing those upgrades into online matches.

Space combat receives special attention as well, with redesigned handling, weapons, and customization options. Players can join squadron battles, dodge danger in asteroid fields, attack Imperial dockyards, and take part in high-stakes dogfights involving massive capital ships and legendary starfighters.

With tauntauns, tanks, speeders, starfighters, and the Force itself, *STAR WARS™ Battlefront™ II* captures the excitement of four decades of Star Wars storytelling in a polished, content-rich, and endlessly replayable battle experience.
