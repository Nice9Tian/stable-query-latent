This tactical stealth game offers a fresh take on real-time tactics by blending stealth, action, resource management, and base-building. Set on the Eastern Front during World War II, it follows a group of resistance fighters opposing German occupation. Players control a squad from behind enemy lines, planning encounters with tactical pause, studying terrain, and choosing ambush positions to initiate combat on their own terms.

The gameplay emphasizes freedom in how missions are completed. Each operation can be approached through direct action, stealth, ambushes, or the use of special tools designed to disrupt enemy ranks. Between missions, the focus shifts to maintaining a hidden woodland camp, where players develop the base, recruit new members, prepare for future assignments, and manage the resources needed for survival. Squad members can be selected and improved according to their strengths, allowing different combinations for different situations.

The campaign presents the daily reality of a resistance group, including the need to keep troops motivated while surviving under constant pressure. It also highlights the varied backgrounds of those who join the fight, from soldiers to civilians. The story centers on an escaped commander who gathers a team from stranded troops and local people after fleeing a prison camp. From there, the group carries out sabotage and attacks against occupation forces, eventually taking part in a broader operation against enemy supply lines intended to delay an offensive and give defenders more time to prepare.
