Partisans 1941 is a stealth tactics game set on the Eastern Front during World War II, combining stealth, action, resource management, and base-building. Players take command of a group of Partisans resisting the German occupation and decide how to approach combat, using tactical pause, environmental awareness, ambush positions, and special items to disrupt enemy forces.

The game emphasizes player choice in mission approach, allowing different ways to complete objectives. It also focuses on squad management, where players form a team of unique Partisans, level them up, and use their individual strengths to handle different situations. Over time, new resistance members can be found and recruited to expand the group.

Between missions, the game presents the day-to-day life of a resistance unit. Players develop a hidden camp deep in the woods, prepare for upcoming assignments, manage resources needed for survival, and keep their troops motivated. These base-building and management elements support the larger campaign of sabotage and resistance.

The story follows Red Army commander Alexey Zorin, who escapes a Nazi prison camp and gathers a Partisan squad from soldiers left behind enemy lines and local civilians. The campaign moves from small sabotage operations to attacks on occupation forces’ authority. It also connects to a larger operation organized by Red Army staff, in which Partisan teams strike German supply lines to help buy time for the defenders of Leningrad to prepare for the German attack.

Overall, Partisans 1941 presents a distinct perspective on World War II by focusing on the struggle of resistance fighters and the people who join them, both military personnel and ordinary civilians.
