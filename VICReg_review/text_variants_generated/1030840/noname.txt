This title is a full ground-up remake of the first chapter in a crime-saga trilogy, set in a Prohibition-era American city recreated with interwar architecture, period vehicles, and era-specific culture. The story follows a cab driver named **Tavro** after an unexpected encounter with the mob pulls him into organized crime. Although he is initially reluctant to become involved with the **Varelli** family, the benefits of working within the criminal underworld eventually become hard to resist.

The game presents itself as an interactive mob film, focusing on the rise of a gangster through the ranks of the criminal hierarchy. Its setting emphasizes a carefully rebuilt city atmosphere, with locations, sights, sounds, and period details designed to reflect the 1930s. The remake is described as faithful to the original while also expanding the story, gameplay, and original score.

In addition to the main experience, ownership of this edition also grants unlockable bonus content for the other two entries in the trilogy, including a special outfit and vehicle for use across those editions.
