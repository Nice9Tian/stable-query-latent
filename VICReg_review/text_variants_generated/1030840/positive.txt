*Mafia: Definitive Edition* is a striking reimagining of the first chapter in the Mafia crime saga, rebuilt from the ground up to deliver a powerful Prohibition-era experience. Set in the 1930s in the recreated city of Lost Heaven, Illinois, the game follows cab driver Tommy Angelo, whose accidental brush with the mob pulls him into the dangerous world of organized crime. What begins as reluctance soon turns into a climb through the ranks of the Salieri crime family, as the rewards of mafia life grow too tempting to resist.

The game presents itself as a true mob movie experience, letting players live the rise of a gangster during one of the most iconic periods in criminal history. Lost Heaven is brought to life with a carefully recreated 1930s cityscape, complete with interwar architecture, period cars, and culture to see, hear, and interact with. The setting plays a major role in creating the game’s atmosphere, making the world feel authentic and immersive throughout Tommy’s journey.

As a remake of a classic, *Mafia: Definitive Edition* stays faithful to the original while expanding the story, gameplay, and score. This combination gives longtime fans the experience they remember, enhanced with modern presentation and added depth. It is a refined and polished way to experience the origins of the Mafia series, capturing the drama, style, and tension of the era with impressive care.

Players who own *Mafia: Definitive Edition* also unlock Tommy’s Suit and Cab in both *Mafia II: Definitive Edition* and *Mafia III: Definitive Edition*, making it an excellent centerpiece for completing the trilogy.
