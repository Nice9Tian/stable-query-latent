Mafia: Definitive Edition is a ground-up remake of the first part of the Mafia crime saga, set in 1930s Lost Heaven, Illinois during the Prohibition era. The story follows cab driver Tommy Angelo, who is drawn into organized crime after an inadvertent encounter with the mob. Though he is initially reluctant to become involved with the Salieri crime family, the rewards and opportunities of mafia life soon become hard to ignore.

The game presents itself as a mob movie experience, letting players live through the rise of a Prohibition-era gangster as Tommy climbs through the ranks of the Mafia. The city of Lost Heaven has been recreated as a 1930s urban setting with interwar architecture, cars, and culture designed to be seen, heard, and interacted with.

As a remake, Mafia: Definitive Edition is described as faithfully recreated while also featuring expanded story content, gameplay, and an original score. In addition, owning Mafia: Definitive Edition unlocks Tommy’s Suit and Cab in both Mafia II: Definitive Edition and Mafia III: Definitive Edition.
