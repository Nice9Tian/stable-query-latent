This simulation game places the player in the role of a beginner working toward widespread online fame. Starting with very limited resources, the goal is to gradually improve your character, purchase better equipment, and eventually move from a low-quality neighborhood to a new area with stronger internet infrastructure.

A major part of the experience revolves around building a custom computer with the specifications you want and beginning a streaming career. During streams, you can chat with followers and receive donations, while also broadcasting the games you play. Success depends on timing and awareness of trends, since you can follow new games and events, buy new titles, and stream the right game at the right time to attract new viewers.

The game also includes competitive opportunities. By entering tournaments for popular games, you can try to prove yourself and earn prize money. In addition to streaming and competition, the player can take on different kinds of extra work to make more money. Interaction with the environment is part of this process, including searching through discarded items for useful objects, selling them at pawn shops, and earning additional income through various side jobs.

As progress is made, the money earned can be invested to increase overall wealth. The experience also includes purchasing pets and spending time with them, adding a lighter, more personal element to the life-simulation loop.
