Streamer Life Simulator is a simulation game about building a streaming career from the ground up. Starting in a poor neighborhood, players work to improve their character, buy new equipment, and eventually move to an area with stronger internet infrastructure. They can then build a custom computer with the desired features and begin streaming.

The game centers on growing an audience through live content. Players can chat with followers, collect donations, and stream the games they play. It also includes a progression and investment element, allowing earnings to be invested in order to increase money over time. Staying up to date with new games and events is part of the process, since playing the right game at the right time can help new people discover the streamer.

Beyond streaming, the game offers several ways to earn extra income. Players can take part in tournaments for popular games to win prizes, interact with the environment to find different work, search through trash for useful items, and sell them in pawn shops. Additional jobs around the area provide further opportunities to make money. As progress continues, players can also buy pets and spend time with them.
