Half-Life® 2: Episode Two is a standout continuation of Valve’s acclaimed Half-Life adventure, and the second chapter in a planned trilogy of new games. Picking up after the dramatic events at City 17, the game follows Dr. Gordon Freeman and Alyx Vance as they leave the collapsing Citadel behind and head into the White Forest. Their mission is urgent: fight and outpace Combine forces while delivering a crucial information packet stolen from the Citadel to a group of resistance scientists.

The game builds on the award-winning Half-Life gameplay and adds fresh elements that keep the experience exciting and dynamic. Players can expect unique weapons, vehicles, and newly-spawned creatures that expand the action and deepen the journey through this dangerous world. Episode Two delivers a strong mix of story, combat, and momentum, making it a worthy and impressive extension of the Half-Life saga.
