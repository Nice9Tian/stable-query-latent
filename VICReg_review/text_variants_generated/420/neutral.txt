Half-Life® 2: Episode Two is the second installment in Valve’s trilogy of new games that continues the Half-Life® series. It follows Dr. Gordon Freeman after his departure from City 17 with Alyx Vance, as the Citadel erupts amid a storm of unknown proportions. In this episode, players battle and race against Combine forces while traveling through White Forest in order to deliver a crucial information packet stolen from the Citadel to a group of resistance scientists.

The game expands upon the established Half-Life gameplay with unique weapons, vehicles, and newly spawned creatures.
