OTXO is a violent top-down shooter with roguelite elements that delivers a stylish, intense, and highly replayable experience. You play as a featureless, unnamed protagonist who enters an inexplicable mansion in search of his lost love. With no memory of how you arrived, only one purpose remains: she is waiting somewhere within the Mansion, and you cannot leave until you find her.

As you push deeper into the estate, its secrets begin to unfold. The game takes you through distinct areas filled with danger, mystery, and increasingly deadly encounters, while you meet new allies and confront the inner demons standing in your way. Armed with a massive variety of guns, more than 100 abilities, and the Focus mechanic that lets you dodge bullets in slow motion, you can kick down doors and carve a path through the enemies blocking your mission.

OTXO places a strong emphasis on replayability. It features 8 areas built from a randomly arranged selection of over 150 hand-crafted rooms, ensuring that no two runs feel the same. The game also offers deep customization through unlockable weapons and more than 100 abilities obtained from the game’s enigmatic bartender, allowing players to discover the setup that best suits their style.

Combat in OTXO feels powerful and controlled thanks to Focus, but the game still demands quick adaptation. A wide range of enemy types can turn the tide if you are not ready to respond. This balance of power and pressure creates a fast, satisfying rhythm that rewards skillful play.

The experience is completed by a gritty, pulse-pounding soundtrack composed by the developer himself, giving the game a brutal noir atmosphere that fully immerses players in its murder-ballet intensity.
