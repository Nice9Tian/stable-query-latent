This is a violent top-down shooter with roguelite elements set in a mysterious mansion. The player takes on the role of an unnamed protagonist who enters the estate in search of a lost loved one. With no memory of how they arrived, only the purpose remains clear: they must keep moving forward until they find the person they are looking for. As the journey continues deeper into the mansion, the story gradually reveals more of its secrets, while the protagonist faces inner demons and a growing sense of danger.

The game features fast-paced combat across a large number of hand-crafted rooms. It includes eight distinct areas, with each run drawing from a randomized layout of more than 150 rooms, ensuring that no two playthroughs are the same. Players can use a wide range of firearms, more than 100 abilities, and a slow-motion dodge mechanic that helps them avoid incoming fire and control the pace of battle. Combat also involves kicking down doors, engaging different enemy types, and adapting quickly as encounters become more difficult.

Customization is an important part of the experience. Players can unlock weapons and acquire abilities through a bartender-like figure, allowing them to experiment with different setups and find a style that suits them. The game emphasizes replayability, variation, and tactical adjustment, with each run offering new combinations of rooms, enemies, and upgrades.

A gritty, noir-inspired soundtrack supports the action and reinforces the tone of the mansion’s brutal, surreal atmosphere. Overall, the game combines intense shooting, procedural structure, and progression-based customization in a dark mystery-driven setting.
