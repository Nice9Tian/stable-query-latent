OTXO is a violent top-down shooter with roguelite elements. In the game, you play as a protagonist who enters an inexplicable mansion in search of his lost love. As you move deeper into the mansion, more of its secrets are revealed. The character is featureless, unnamed, and has no memory of how he arrived, but he remembers why he came: she is waiting somewhere inside, and he cannot leave until he finds her.

The game focuses on cutting through a variety of unique areas, meeting new allies, uncovering the mystery, and confronting inner demons. Combat gives players access to a large range of guns, more than 100 abilities, and a slow-motion Focus mechanic for dodging bullets. Players must break down doors and fight through enemies that stand in the way of the mission.

OTXO emphasizes replayability through its roguelite structure. It includes 8 areas, with a randomly arranged selection of rooms drawn from more than 150 hand-crafted rooms. As a result, no two runs are the same.

Customization is another major part of the experience. Players can unlock a selection of weapons and acquire over 100 abilities from the game’s bartender, allowing them to find a setup that suits their approach.

The Focus mechanic is designed to give players a sense of power and control, but combat can shift quickly if enemy types are not handled with the right adjustments. The game also features a gritty soundtrack composed by the developer, supporting its brutal noir-inspired atmosphere.
