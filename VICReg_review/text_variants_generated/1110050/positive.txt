**Evan's Remains** is a finely crafted mystery-thriller puzzle adventure built around the search for a missing boy named Evan. It blends thoughtful, logic-based platform puzzles with a story presentation inspired by Japanese graphic adventure games, creating an experience that is both intellectually engaging and emotionally intriguing.

At the heart of the game is a distinctive progression system: after solving two to three puzzles, the story advances and new mechanics are introduced. Rather than relying on fast reflexes or skill-based challenge, the puzzles emphasize understanding what needs to be done. As the difficulty rises, the game expands its complexity by combining two or more mechanics that have already been introduced, rewarding careful reasoning and attention to detail.

The story begins with Evan, a genius boy who has been missing for years. Then, a letter signed by him arrives, claiming that he is on an uninhabited island somewhere in the Pacific Ocean. For reasons unknown, he specifically asks for a girl named Dysis to come look for him. The player takes control of Dysis, a character whose background is initially shrouded in mystery. As her journey unfolds, answers emerge gradually, and the seemingly unrelated puzzle pieces begin to connect. Little by little, the truth comes into view, making Evan's Remains a compelling and satisfying adventure of discovery.
