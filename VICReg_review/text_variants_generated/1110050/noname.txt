This is a mystery-thriller puzzle adventure centered on the search for a missing boy. It blends logic-based platform puzzles with a narrative structure inspired by Japanese graphic adventure games. The experience is built around gradual progression: after solving two to three puzzles, the story advances and new mechanics are introduced. Rather than testing reflexes or precision, the puzzles focus on figuring out the correct approach. As the difficulty increases, later challenges combine multiple previously introduced mechanics, making the puzzle design progressively more complex.

The story follows a gifted boy who has been missing for years. A letter signed by him eventually appears, claiming that he is on an uninhabited island in the Pacific Ocean. For reasons not fully explained at first, he specifically asks a young woman to search for him. The player takes control of this protagonist, whose background is initially kept vague. As the journey unfolds, answers are revealed little by little, and seemingly unrelated puzzle pieces gradually begin to connect, slowly uncovering the truth.
