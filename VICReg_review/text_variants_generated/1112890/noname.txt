This cozy life-simulation game centers on restoring a small-town café and filling it with affectionate creatures. Players take on a charming rebuilding task: revive the café, decorate it with furniture and ornaments, and make it a lively place for animals and visitors alike. The experience is designed to feel relaxed and low-pressure, with an emphasis on creativity, warmth, and exploration.

A major part of the game is character creation, allowing players to design their own magical avatar. There are also many clothing options to discover and collect throughout the adventure, giving players plenty of freedom to customize their appearance.

Animals are a core feature of the world. Players can encounter many different creatures, and once they are befriended, they can be named, added to the player’s party, or sent to live at the café. Party animals travel alongside the player and respond to commands, while animals placed in the café interact with customers and help bring the space to life.

Café management includes both decoration and cooking. Players can furnish the café in different styles to create a comfortable atmosphere, then prepare baked goods and drinks to serve the town’s residents. Learning about local villagers helps players figure out which foods they like best, adding a light social and culinary element to the gameplay.

The game also features magical systems that bend the usual rules of play. These special potions create unusual possibilities and support playful experimentation. One example is a potion that can transform a single animal into a giant version, letting players spend time with oversized companions or even ride one.
