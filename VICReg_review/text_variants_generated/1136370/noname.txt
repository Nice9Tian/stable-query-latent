This laid-back management adventure follows a kind-hearted bear who sets out to run a woodland bed-and-breakfast business. With help from friends and a bit of teenage ingenuity, an abandoned shack is transformed into a profit-making inn for visiting travelers. As the enterprise grows, so does the sense of mystery surrounding the forest, leading the protagonist to uncover a deeper conspiracy hidden within the wilderness.

The experience centers on building and customizing an inn in detail. Players can create and personalize numerous guest rooms, bathrooms, parlors, and entertainment spaces, with each area fully adjustable from furniture to fixtures. The goal is to bring guests into the forest, keep them satisfied, and use their happiness to maintain reputation, earn income, and draw in more visitors.

Progress is driven by quests and storylines that unlock new items and perks for the inn. The world is filled with many unusual characters, most of whom have opinions about the player’s efforts and may offer assistance if approached. Along the way, the narrative blends business management with exploration, as the player moves through a story-rich setting full of strange personalities and hidden secrets.
