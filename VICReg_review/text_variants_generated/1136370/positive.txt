**Bear and Breakfast** is a charming, laid-back management adventure that puts you in the paws of Hank, a well-meaning bear determined to run a bed and breakfast in the woods. Alongside his friends, Hank discovers an abandoned shack and transforms it with teenage ingenuity into a money-making B+B scheme for unsuspecting tourists. As the business grows, so does the intrigue of the forest, leading Hank into a mystery that runs deeper than the wilderness itself.

The game offers extensive building and customization, letting you create and personalize your inn with dozens of guest rooms, bathrooms, parlors, and entertainment spaces. Every room can be individually tailored, from furniture to fixtures, giving you plenty of freedom to shape the perfect getaway. Your goal is to bring guests into the forest, keep them happy, maintain your reputation, earn money, and attract even more customers.

Progress comes through quests and storylines that reward you with new items and perks for your inn. As you explore the story-rich world of Bear and Breakfast, you’ll encounter dozens of interesting characters and oddball personalities, many of whom have something to say about your ambitious enterprise. By listening to them, you may uncover helpful opportunities that allow your humble shack to reach new heights.

With its mix of cozy management, engaging progression, and forest-bound mystery, Bear and Breakfast delivers a delightful adventure full of personality, creativity, and woodland charm.
