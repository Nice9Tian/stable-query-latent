Bear and Breakfast is a laid-back management adventure game in which you play as Hank, a well-meaning bear who tries to run a bed and breakfast in the woods. Along with his friends, Hank discovers an abandoned shack and turns it into a money-making B+B scheme for unsuspecting tourists. As the business grows, the mysteries of the forest become larger as well, and Hank gradually uncovers a plot deeper than the wilderness itself.

The game focuses on building and personalizing an inn. Players can construct dozens of guest rooms, bathrooms, parlors, and entertainment spaces, and each room can be customized individually, from furniture to fixtures. Guests can then be brought into the forest to stay at the inn, and keeping them happy is important for maintaining reputation, earning money, and attracting new customers.

Progress is driven by quests and story lines, which reward players with new items and perks for the inn. The world is story-rich and filled with many interesting characters and odd personalities, most of whom have something to say about Hank’s plans. By listening to them, players may gain help in improving the shack and expanding it to new heights. At the same time, not everyone Hank meets is equally helpful.

Overall, Bear and Breakfast combines management, customization, and story progression in a forest setting centered on a bear-run inn, with a tone that mixes business building, exploration, and mystery.
