Sun Haven is a fantasy farming sim with an RPG twist, set in a lore-rich, story-driven world filled with dialogue, quests, secrets, and customization options. The game emphasizes player choice, offering a customizable experience with adjustable day length, toggles for damage, seasonal pests, and weather effects, as well as the option to play with default settings. There is no stamina bar, and each playthrough can be tailored through numerous in-game settings.

Core farming gameplay centers on transforming a run-down plot into a productive farm across all four seasons. Players use upgradeable tools and magic spells to grow fruits, vegetables, and flowers, while managing time across three different farms, each with its own crops, fruit trees, and animals. Livestock includes familiar animals such as cows, chickens, sheep, and pigs, alongside fantastical creatures like silk moths, baby griffons, phoenixes, werewolves, tar slimes, and more. Pets and mounts can also be collected and taken along on adventures.

Sun Haven supports both solo play and online multiplayer for up to eight friends. Players can share a farm, and characters retain their inventories and experience levels even when used in both single-player and multiplayer. The game also includes extensive home and farm decoration, with up to eight houses to upgrade and customize, hundreds of exterior options, over 30 themed sheds, and many furniture and decor items that can be purchased, crafted, mailed, or traded in multiplayer.

The world spans four distinct areas: the medieval town of Sun Haven, the elven forests of Nel’Vari, the monster city of Withergate, and the underwater region of The Brinestone Deeps. Progression includes side quests, riddles, exploration, and interactions with hundreds of unique NPCs. Romance is a major feature, with 23 marriage candidates across all four towns, supported by gifts, dialogue, events, quests, and dates. After marriage, players can upgrade their house, maintain their relationship, and eventually have one child through magic.

The game also offers fishing, crafting, combat, mining, and character progression. The fishing minigame is designed to be easy to learn and hard to master, with over 130 fish to catch and use for selling, cooking, donating to the museum, gifting, or questing. Crafting includes more than 40 unique crafting tables for cooking, smithing, construction, farming, furniture, and alchemy, with town-specific items and recipes in each area. Combat features swords, crossbows, staffs, and magic spells, along with armor upgrades, food-based stat boosts, and a large skill tree. Players can also explore mines for resources, gems, and ore, using rusty keys for temporary access or smelted keys for permanent unlocks, while collecting chest rewards on every 10th floor and fighting enemies for loot.

For those seeking more challenge, the Combat Arena offers solo or multiplayer boss fights with rewards for victory. Character creation is highly flexible, with seven races, ten starting professions, and hundreds of clothing, hair, and other cosmetic options. Appearance can also be changed later at the Sun Haven Salon. Festivals, holidays, and seasonal events add further activities and exclusive shop items, while the museum system encourages collecting and donating items found through fishing, mining, foraging, and combat. The game also includes achievement hunting, with 177 Steam Achievements tied to its many systems, including romance, quests, storylines, festivals, skills, magic, combat, fishing, animals, and farming.
