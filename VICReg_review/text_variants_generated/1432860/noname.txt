This fantasy life simulation blends farming with role-playing elements, offering a broad, story-driven experience in a richly built world. Players can explore extensive dialogue, complete side quests, solve riddles, uncover secrets, and interact with hundreds of distinct non-player characters. Choices influence the journey and help shape the character’s legacy.

At its core, the game centers on building and managing a farm. It notably removes the usual stamina constraint, and its settings allow for extensive customization of playthrough pace and difficulty. Players can change day length, disable damage, turn seasonal pests on or off, adjust weather effects, or simply play with default options. Farming spans four seasons, with unique weather patterns and seasonal effects, and players use upgradeable tools and magic to grow crops, flowers, and fruit.

The game also includes management across multiple farms, each with its own crops, fruit trees, and animals. Livestock ranges from familiar animals such as cows, chickens, sheep, and pigs to more fantastical creatures. Pets and mounts can also be collected to accompany players during exploration.

Social and cooperative play are supported through online multiplayer for up to eight players, while solo play remains fully available. Inventory and experience progress are retained even when the same character is used across single-player and multiplayer modes. Home and farm decoration are highly detailed, with extensive exterior customization, themed sheds, and a large selection of furniture and décor items that can be obtained through crafting, purchase, mail, or multiplayer trading.

The world is divided into four major areas, each contributing to the story: a medieval town, an elven forest, a monster city, and an underwater region. Romance and family features are also prominent, with many available marriage candidates, dating events, marriage, and the possibility of having a child after meeting certain conditions.

Additional systems include fishing, crafting, combat, mining, museums, festivals, and progression through a large skill tree. Fishing features an easy-to-learn but difficult-to-master minigame with over 130 fish to catch, while crafting spans many specialized stations for cooking, smithing, construction, farming, furniture, and alchemy. Combat involves a range of weapons and spells, enemy encounters across the world, armor upgrades, stat growth through food, and skill-based character development. Mines provide resources, gems, ore, chests, and enemy loot, with temporary or permanent floor unlocking options.

Players can also challenge bosses in solo or multiplayer combat arenas, customize their characters through multiple races, starting professions, and extensive cosmetic options, and later change appearance through an in-game salon. Seasonal festivals, holidays, and events add recurring activities and offer exclusive items. The museum system encourages collecting trinkets from many activities, and completionists can pursue a large set of achievements tied to nearly every major system in the game.
