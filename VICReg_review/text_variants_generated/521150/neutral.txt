Another Brick in The Mall is a management and construction simulation centered on designing, building, and operating a large shopping center. Players can open a variety of businesses, including shops, supermarkets, restaurants, movie theaters, and bowling alleys, while also hiring and managing staff to keep operations running smoothly.

The game gives players control over nearly every aspect of the mall’s layout and infrastructure. Roads, parking lots, walls, floor tiles, doors, shelves, and decorative plants can all be placed and arranged as desired, allowing for both efficient layouts and personalized designs. The simulation includes thousands of characters and vehicles on screen, creating a busy, ant-colony-like environment.

Staff management is a major part of the experience. Workers are selected from a panel of applicants, and their skills must be matched to the right jobs. Proper workforce deployment is necessary to reduce queues, keep shelves stocked, and maintain clean floors. Customer satisfaction is also important, since unhappy customers will not return the next day, and very angry ones can damage the mall’s reputation.

The economic side of the game emphasizes careful planning and financial management. Players begin with limited funds, must open their first business, earn profits, take out loans, and expand gradually. The game also encourages selling staple goods to draw customers in and using their cravings and addictions to encourage impulse purchases. In addition to the standard progression, a sandbox mode is available for players who want unlimited money and unrestricted building freedom.
