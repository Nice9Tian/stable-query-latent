*Another Brick in The Mall* is a smart and satisfying management simulation that puts you in charge of designing, building, and running a massive shopping center from the ground up. From the very first road and parking space to every wall, floor tile, door, shelf, and plant, you shape the entire complex exactly as you want it, creating a unique commercial empire with a strong sense of freedom and style.

The game offers a rich variety of businesses to develop, including shops, supermarkets, restaurants, movie theaters, bowling alleys, and more. Its charm lies in the depth of its systems: thousands of characters and vehicles are simulated on screen, giving the mall a lively “ant colony” atmosphere as customers move through the center and interact with the spaces you have built.

Success depends on more than just construction. You must hire staff carefully from a pool of applicants, making sure their skills fit the job, then deploy them efficiently to keep queues short, shelves stocked, and floors clean. Customer satisfaction is essential, since unhappy shoppers will not return and angry ones can damage your reputation. At the same time, you can grow your profits by selling staple goods to draw in more visitors and by tapping into cravings and impulse purchases.

Starting with limited funds, the game emphasizes careful accounting and steady expansion. You begin by opening your first business, turning a profit, taking out loans when needed, and gradually building outward into a larger and more ambitious mall. For players who prefer complete freedom, sandbox mode offers infinite money and total creative freedom: “This is your world.”
