This management simulation centers on designing, building, and operating a vast shopping complex from the ground up. Players can open a wide range of businesses, including stores, supermarkets, restaurants, movie venues, bowling lanes, and more, while shaping the entire layout of the center to suit either practical efficiency or personal preference.

The game emphasizes detailed construction and planning. Nearly every structural and decorative element can be placed and arranged by the player, from roads and parking areas to walls, floor tiles, doors, shelves, and plants. The result is a customizable commercial environment that can be optimized for performance or designed as a distinctive retail empire.

A large number of characters and vehicles are simulated at once, creating a busy, ant-colony-like atmosphere. Staffing is a key part of the experience: applicants are selected from a hiring panel, and their skills must be matched carefully to the needs of each position. Efficient workforce management helps keep queues short, shelves stocked, and floors clean.

Customer satisfaction also plays a major role. Happy customers are more likely to return, while unhappy or angry ones can damage the center’s reputation. The business model encourages balancing essential goods that draw in foot traffic with tempting items that can encourage additional impulse spending.

Progress begins with limited funds, making budgeting and accounting important in the early stages. Players can open a first business, generate profits, take out loans, and expand gradually over time. For a less restrictive experience, a sandbox mode is available with unlimited money, allowing unrestricted experimentation and construction.
