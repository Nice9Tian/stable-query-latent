PGA TOUR 2K23 is a golf game built around authentic PGA TOUR competition, course creation, and a range of online and local multiplayer modes. The game’s MyCAREER mode lets players take their MyPLAYER onto the PGA TOUR, compete against other TOUR pros, and aim to become the next FedExCup Champion. It also includes licensed PGA TOUR pros and courses, with male and female pros playable in online and local play for the first time in the series, including Tiger Woods. Licensed courses mentioned on the store page include East Lake Golf Club, St. George’s Golf and Country Club, TPC Scottsdale, and TPC Sawgrass.

The game places strong emphasis on customization and progression. Players can level up their MyPLAYER through new Skills and Archetypes, while licensed gear and apparel from brands such as adidas, Callaway, Titleist, and Malbon are available to help personalize appearance. The Course Designer allows players to create custom fantasy courses using thousands of customizable objects, and cross-platform support makes it possible to share those creations for online and local play.

PGA TOUR 2K23 also includes Topgolf, which can be played solo or with others in competitive 1-4 player local and online play. In addition, the game offers multiple control and accessibility options, including a new 3-Click swing system and the traditional analog Swing Stick. Accessibility features listed include real-time tutorials, tips, shot suggestions, the TrueShot System, and Lie Management.

Multiplayer is a major part of the experience. Players can participate in local and online matches with modes such as Alt-Shot, Stroke Play, Skins, and 4-Player Scramble. Divot Derby also returns as a single-elimination, shoot-out style multiplayer mode. Online Societies let players manage tournaments and full seasons, with customizable entry rules, requirements, handicaps, and event options.

The store page also lists several special editions and bonus packs. The Deluxe Edition includes the Standard Edition plus the Michael Jordan Bonus Pack, the Deluxe Edition Bonus Pack, and the Golden Club Pack. The Tiger Woods Edition includes all of those bonuses as well as the Tiger Woods Edition Bonus Pack and Tiger Woods Signature Sunday Pack. Bonuses include playable Michael Jordan, cosmetic golf items, VC, golf ball sleeves of different tiers, and Tiger Woods-themed apparel and equipment.
