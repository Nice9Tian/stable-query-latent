PGA TOUR 2K23 delivers a polished and feature-rich golfing experience that blends authentic PGA TOUR competition with creative freedom and lively multiplayer fun. In MyCAREER, players can bring their MyPLAYER onto the PGA TOUR, aim for FedExCup glory, and build rivalries against other pros while chasing the next big championship. The game also stands out for letting players compete as both male and female pros, including Tiger Woods, across online and local play.

The roster of licensed courses adds strong real-world appeal, with venues such as East Lake Golf Club, St. George’s Golf and Country Club, TPC Scottsdale, and TPC Sawgrass featured among others. For players who enjoy creativity, the Course Designer offers an industry-leading toolkit with thousands of customizable objects, plus cross-platform sharing for online and local play. Topgolf is also included, bringing a fun 1-4 player experience that works both solo and with friends, whether in local or online competition.

Progression is supported through new Skills and Archetypes that help level up MyPLAYER, alongside licensed gear and apparel from brands like adidas, Callaway, Titleist, and Malbon. PGA TOUR 2K23 also offers flexible control options, including the new 3-Click swing system and the traditional analog Swing Stick, while accessibility features such as real-time tutorials, tips, shot suggestions, the TrueShot System, and Lie Management make the game approachable and rewarding.

Multiplayer is another major strength, with Online Societies letting players create and manage tournaments, seasons, entry rules, handicaps, and event options. Local and online match types include Alt-Shot, Stroke Play, Skins, and 4-Player Scramble, and the return of Divot Derby adds a frenzied single-elimination shoot-out mode to the mix. Altogether, PGA TOUR 2K23 offers a confident, stylish, and deeply customizable golf experience for fans of both competition and creativity.
