This sports title delivers a golf experience centered on career progression, competitive play, and extensive customization. Its main career mode lets players guide a custom golfer through a professional tour journey, building skill, style, and reputation while competing for a championship title. Rivalries with other pros are a key part of that structure, giving the campaign a clear competitive arc.

The game includes both male and female professional athletes in online and local play, along with several officially licensed courses. These venues are presented as recognizable real-world layouts, and the selection supports a traditional golf experience across different settings. In addition to standard course play, the game offers a powerful course-creation tool, allowing players to construct custom fairways using a large library of editable objects and then share those creations across platforms for both online and local matches.

A separate arcade-style mode adds a more casual option for solo players or groups, supporting competitive local and online play for up to four participants. This broadens the experience beyond standard golf simulation and gives the game an alternative way to play that is designed to be approachable while still competitive.

Player progression is handled through a customization system that includes skills and archetypes. As progress is made, the created golfer can be equipped with licensed apparel and gear from multiple real-world brands, reinforcing the emphasis on personalization. The game also offers multiple swing-control options, including a new three-click system and a traditional analog swing method. Accessibility features such as real-time tutorials, tips, shot recommendations, a precision shot system, and lie management are included to help players adapt to the game.

For social and community play, the title supports online clubs where players can organize tournaments, full seasons, and custom rule sets. Entry requirements, handicaps, and event settings can all be managed, giving groups substantial control over how competitions are structured. Multiplayer is available both locally and online, with several match formats such as alternate shot, stroke play, skins, and four-player scramble. A returning elimination-based party mode rounds out the package, offering a faster, more chaotic competitive option alongside the more traditional golf formats.

Several editions of the game add bonus content in the form of additional cosmetic items, virtual currency, and a playable guest athlete. These extras focus on style and personalization rather than changing the core gameplay, which remains centered on golf progression, competition, custom course design, and multiplayer variety.
