Tell Me Why is a narrative adventure game from DONTNOD Entertainment, the studio behind Life is Strange. Set in beautiful small-town Alaska, the game follows reunited twins Tyler and Alyson Ronan as they use their supernatural bond to explore memories of their loving but troubled childhood and uncover the truth behind them.

The game centers on story-driven choices, with player decisions affecting the twins’ relationship, the strength of their bond, and the direction of their lives. As the player revisits the past, conflicting childhood memories must be examined through the twins’ shared connection. The experience also includes puzzles that deepen the story and provide access to the twins’ fantasy world, “The Book of Goblins.”

Tell Me Why features true-to-life characters and mature themes, with a focus on building relationships and solving mysteries through realistic, compelling personalities. Its setting emphasizes the atmosphere of small-town Alaska, presented in detailed 4K and HDR visuals. The game also includes a distinctive approach to storytelling, with DONTNOD Entertainment and Xbox Game Studios conducting on-the-ground research and working with cultural, mental health, and gender advocates to shape a thoughtful and grounded experience.
