*Tell Me Why* is a beautifully crafted narrative adventure from DONTNOD Entertainment, the studio celebrated for *Life is Strange*, and it continues the developer’s talent for emotionally rich storytelling. Set in the stunning, highly detailed landscapes of small-town Alaska, the game follows reunited twins Tyler and Alyson Ronan as they use their supernatural bond to revisit memories of their childhood and uncover the truth behind a loving but troubled past.

At the heart of *Tell Me Why* is a powerful, character-driven mystery. Players explore conflicting memories, make meaningful choices, and shape the direction of the twins’ relationship and their futures. The story is grounded by true-to-life characters, mature themes, and choices that carry real emotional weight, giving every decision a sense of purpose and consequence.

The game also adds an engaging layer of discovery through puzzles that deepen the narrative and reveal more about the twins’ fantasy world, “The Book of Goblins.” As players match wits with these challenges, they unlock additional secrets and gain a fuller understanding of the siblings’ shared history.

Visually, *Tell Me Why* presents Alaska in gorgeous detail, enhanced by 4K and HDR support, making its setting feel both intimate and memorable. Beyond its presentation, the game stands out for its thoughtful approach to storytelling: DONTNOD Entertainment and Xbox Game Studios worked closely with cultural, mental health, and gender advocates, and conducted on-the-ground research to create an experience that feels respectful, authentic, and deeply considered.

For players who appreciate meaningful choices, emotional depth, and mystery wrapped in a heartfelt narrative, *Tell Me Why* offers a striking and unforgettable adventure.
