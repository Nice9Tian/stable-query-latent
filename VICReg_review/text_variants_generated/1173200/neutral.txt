Metal Unit tells a tragic story of human survivors fighting against monsters and machines in an attempt to regain control of their planet. Earth is under attack from multiple directions: ancient beings are emerging from underground, while alien machines descend from space. In this conflict, players take on the role of Joanna, a new soldier who can wear the powerful M-Unit suit. Her journey centers on saving humanity and seeking revenge against her traitorous sister, who has allied herself with the alien race.

The game features action-packed combat built around dashing, evasive movement, and offensive chaining of multiple weapon attacks into devastating combos. Players can use a wide range of melee and ranged weapons, allowing flexible approaches to combat and enemy encounters.

Metal Unit also offers large and varied environments. Players can explore the planet’s surface or venture into mysterious dungeons beneath the ground. Progressing across the world advances the story, while going underground can lead to greater rewards, though with greater risk.

As a roguelite, the game includes persistent progression through research points. When the player dies, all equipped items are converted into permanent research points, which can be used to unlock new items and skills. Weapons are varied in utility, requiring players to adapt their strategy according to their current equipment.

The game also provides options for different play styles. Assist Mode allows players to customize their experience, while Challenge Mode increases the difficulty and offers additional rewards for those willing to take on greater risk.
