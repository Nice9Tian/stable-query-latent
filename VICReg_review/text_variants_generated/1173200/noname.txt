This action game presents a tragic struggle for survival as humanity fights to reclaim a planet under siege by monsters and machines. Ancient beings rise from below the surface while alien machines descend from space, creating a world where the battle for control is fought on multiple fronts. The player takes on the role of a new soldier able to wear a powerful combat suit, following her journey to protect what remains of humanity and to confront a traitorous sister who has allied herself with an alien race.

Its combat system is fast and flexible, built around dashing to evade danger or press the attack with chained weapon strikes that can produce devastating combos. A broad selection of melee and ranged weapons gives players many ways to approach encounters, allowing them to adapt their tactics to the equipment they currently have.

The game also features large and varied environments. Players may travel across the planet’s surface or explore deep underground dungeons. Surface travel advances the story, while delving below ground can lead to greater rewards, though those rewards may come with serious risk.

Roguelite systems play a major role as well. When the player dies, all equipped items are converted into permanent research points that can be spent to unlock new items and skills. Because weapons are received with different levels of usefulness, players must continually adjust their strategy to fit their current loadout.

Accessibility and difficulty options broaden the experience further. An assist setting lets players tailor the game to their preferences, while a challenge setting increases the difficulty for those seeking greater tension and the chance to earn additional rewards.
