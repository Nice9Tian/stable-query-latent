*Metal Unit* tells a gripping and tragic story of humanity’s struggle for survival against overwhelming forces. As monsters rise from beneath the earth and alien machines descend from space, the planet is thrown into chaos. Players step into the role of Joanna, a new soldier equipped with the powerful M-Unit suit, and follow her on an epic journey to defend humanity while also pursuing revenge against her traitorous sister, who has allied herself with the alien race.

The game delivers action-packed combat built around speed, precision, and flexibility. You can dash to evade attacks or stay on the offensive by chaining together multiple weapon strikes to unleash devastating combos. With a broad selection of both melee and ranged weapons, Metal Unit gives players the freedom to approach battles in whatever way suits them best.

Its world is just as ambitious as its combat. Players can explore vast surface environments or venture deep into mysterious dungeons beneath the planet. Traveling across the world advances the story, while descending underground offers greater rewards at the cost of greater danger, creating a compelling sense of risk and reward throughout the adventure.

Metal Unit also features roguelite mechanics that make each run meaningful. When you die, all equipped items are transformed into permanent research points, which can be spent to unlock new items and skills. Since you are constantly receiving weapons with different uses, success depends on adapting your strategy to the equipment you have at hand.

To make the experience accessible and rewarding for different kinds of players, the game includes Assist Mode for customized play and Challenge Mode for those seeking a tougher, more thrilling test. Metal Unit stands out as an exciting blend of intense combat, varied exploration, and roguelite progression, all wrapped in a dramatic struggle to save humanity.
