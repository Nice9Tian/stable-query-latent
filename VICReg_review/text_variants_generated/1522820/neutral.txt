*Orcs Must Die! 3* expands the series’ orc-slaying action on a much larger scale. The game can be played solo or in 2-player co-op, and it gives players a wide arsenal of traps and weapons to use against waves of orcs. Combat is described in broad, destructive terms, with players able to slice, burn, toss, and zap enemies as they advance through the levels.

The game features a new story set more than 20 years after *Orcs Must Die! 2*. In this continuation, the War Mage and Sorceress have rebuilt the order and trained a new generation of apprentices. A major addition is the introduction of War Scenarios, which place players against the largest orc armies in the series so far. These battles take place outside the castles, where players must thin out huge waves of orcs before they break through the walls and reach the rift.

Another new element is War Machines, oversized traps designed for large-scale combat. Examples mentioned include a mega flip trap that can launch dozens of orcs and a mega boom barrel launcher for explosive attacks. Beyond the main campaign, the game continues to offer replayable content through Weekly Challenges and Endless Mode. It also includes the Drastic Steps campaign and its content for free, adding flying enemies, war guardians, and more tools for orc destruction.

For players looking for a more unpredictable challenge, Scramble Mode introduces orcs that evolve with increasingly difficult tricks, while players gather their own modifiers to counter them. Overall, *Orcs Must Die! 3* emphasizes bigger battles, more traps, more weapons, more upgrades, and additional post-campaign modes within the series’ action-defense formula.
