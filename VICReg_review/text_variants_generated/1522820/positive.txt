**Orcs Must Die! 3** brings orc-slaying chaos to an even greater scale, delivering a fast, explosive blend of action and trap-based defense for solo play or 2-player co-op. Armed with a massive arsenal of traps and weapons, players can slice, burn, toss, and zap relentless hordes of repugnant orcs in a worthy successor to the acclaimed series.

This installment expands on everything fans loved about the first two games, offering more orcs, more traps, more weapons, more upgrades, and improved visuals. The new story takes place more than 20 years after *Orcs Must Die! 2*, where the War Mage and Sorceress have rebuilt the order and trained a new generation of apprentices. 

A major highlight is the introduction of **War Scenarios**, which scale up the action with the largest orc armies ever assembled. Instead of fighting only inside castles, players face overwhelming forces on the battlefields around them, thinning out waves of hundreds before they can breach the walls and destroy the rift. To meet these enormous threats, **War Machines** provide oversized versions of the series’ deadly tools, such as mega flip traps that send dozens of orcs flying and mega boom barrel launchers that unleash devastating pyrotechnic destruction.

The game also offers strong replay value beyond the main campaign. After the story is complete, players can continue battling through **Weekly Challenges** or test their endurance in **Endless Mode**. Included free with the game is the **Drastic Steps** campaign content, featuring terrifying flying enemies, heroic war guardians, and even more tools for crushing orcs. For those seeking a tougher, more dynamic challenge, **Scramble Mode** introduces enemies that evolve with increasingly sinister tricks, while players earn their own modifiers each level to help turn the tide.

With its larger-scale battles, expanded arsenal, and nonstop combat variety, **Orcs Must Die! 3** powerfully elevates the series’ signature mayhem.
