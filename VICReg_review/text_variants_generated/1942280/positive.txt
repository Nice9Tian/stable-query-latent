**Brotato** is a fast-paced survival game with a clever premise and a highly replayable design. A spaceship from Potato World crashes on an alien planet, leaving Brotato as the sole survivor. As the only potato able to handle six weapons at once, Brotato must endure wave after wave of hostile aliens while waiting for rescue.

The game’s action is built around quick, intense runs that usually last under 30 minutes. By default, weapons auto-fire, letting you focus on movement and positioning, while a manual aiming option is also available for players who want more control. Each wave lasts between 20 and 90 seconds, and during that time the goal is simple: survive and eliminate as many aliens as possible.

One of Brotato’s biggest strengths is its variety. There are dozens of characters to choose from, each shaping your run in different ways, with options like one-handed, crazy, lucky, mage, and many more. On top of that, the game offers hundreds of items and weapons, including flamethrowers, SMGs, rocket launchers, and even sticks and stones, giving every playthrough plenty of customization and strategic possibilities.

Progression between waves adds another rewarding layer. By collecting materials, you gain experience and earn items from the shop, helping you build a stronger and more specialized character over time. The game also supports local co-op for up to four players, making the survival chaos even more enjoyable with friends.

Brotato also stands out for its accessibility options, allowing players to adjust enemy health, damage, and speed so the challenge can be tailored to the right level. With its quick runs, enormous build variety, and approachable yet exciting combat, Brotato delivers a fun and highly engaging survival experience.
