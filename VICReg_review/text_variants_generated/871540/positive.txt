BMX Streets is a standout freestyle extreme biking simulator that places you in a rich open world built around the spirit of BMX culture. The game invites players to discover their own riding style through practice, experimentation, and exploration, creating an experience that feels personal, expressive, and deeply rewarding.

One of its greatest strengths is its revolutionary control system. With bumpers acting as your hands and triggers as your feet, BMX Streets gives you precise command over every movement of the bike, whether you are riding on the ground, launching into the air, or grinding along a handrail. The controls strike an impressive balance between accessibility and depth, making it easy to learn the basics while still offering plenty of room to push your skills and creativity further.

The physics-driven gameplay is another major highlight. BMX Streets makes riding anything fun, from technical ledges and rails to massive roof drops and backyard bowls. Every environment becomes a chance to express your style, and the game encourages you to approach each obstacle in your own way.

Customization is equally ambitious, with over 1,000 bike parts available to collect and combine. This extensive system lets you build a bike that matches both your look and your riding preferences, making your setup feel truly your own.

The open world is inspired by landmark spots in Vancouver, Canada, and spans more than 25km of road network with hundreds of street spots to explore. It creates the feeling of a vast, living skate park where there is always somewhere new to ride, practice, and discover.

BMX Streets also delivers a wide variety of challenges, including Races, Trick Attacks, Secret Gaps, Tech Attacks, and more. These modes provide hours of gameplay and are designed to test your abilities in different ways. The game can be demanding, but that challenge is part of what makes progress so satisfying.

For those who enjoy riding with others, BMX Streets supports sessions with up to 3 friends anywhere in The World, adding a fun social dimension to the experience. The game also includes features such as a Replay Editor, Tweak System, Player Stats Menu, Online Multi-Player, Mod Support, Day-Night Cycles, and Free Running, all of which add depth and variety to the overall package.

With its physics-based BMX riding, modern control system, extensive customization, open-world freedom, and diverse set of challenges, BMX Streets delivers an impressive and authentic BMX video game experience.
