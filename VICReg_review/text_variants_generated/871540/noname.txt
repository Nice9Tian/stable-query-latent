This title is a freestyle extreme biking simulator set in a large open world, designed around the culture of BMX riding and the freedom to develop a personal style through practice, experimentation, and exploration. Its core experience focuses on expressive bike control, with an input scheme that treats one set of controls as the rider’s hands and another as the feet. This system is meant to offer both accessibility and depth, allowing players to learn basic movement and gradually push into more advanced riding techniques while maintaining full control on the ground, in the air, and during grinds.

The riding model is built around physics-driven interaction, making a wide range of terrain and objects usable for technical tricks and movement. Players can work ledges, rails, roof drops, and backyard bowls, shaping each line according to their preferred approach. Progression is less about following a fixed path and more about refining skill and expression through repeated experimentation.

Customization plays a major role as well. The game offers a large collection of bike components, with over one thousand parts available for assembling a personalized ride. These parts are collected and combined not only for appearance, but also to support the way each player wants to ride.

The world itself is expansive and urban, drawing inspiration from real landmark locations in Vancouver, Canada. It features more than 25 kilometers of road network and hundreds of street spots, creating a large playground for exploration and trick riding. The open environment is supported by day-night cycling and free-running elements, giving the world a living, continuous feel.

A wide variety of challenges adds structure to the sandbox. These include races, trick-focused contests, secret gap objectives, and technical attack modes, all intended to test different aspects of riding ability. The game also includes replay tools, tweak systems, player statistics, online multiplayer support, mod support, and the option to session with up to three friends anywhere in the world. Overall, the experience is presented as a feature-rich biking game centered on physics, customization, open-world discovery, and social play.
