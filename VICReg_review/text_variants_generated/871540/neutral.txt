BMX Streets is a freestyle extreme biking simulator set in an open world, designed around the culture of BMX riding and the freedom to develop a personal style through practice, experimentation, and exploration. The game places emphasis on player control and expression, allowing riders to learn the basics and gradually push their abilities further.

Its control system is built around a simple but distinctive input scheme: bumpers act as your hands and triggers as your feet. This setup is meant to give players full control of the bike whether they are riding on the ground, in the air, or grinding along a handrail. BMX Streets combines accessibility with depth, making it possible to perform both basic maneuvers and more advanced riding techniques.

The game’s physics system is a central part of the experience, with riding intended to feel responsive across a wide range of terrain and obstacles. Players can focus on technical riding over ledges and rails, take on large roof drops, or navigate backyard bowls, with the emphasis placed on style and personal approach.

BMX Streets also includes extensive bike customization, with over 1,000 bike parts available. Players can collect parts and build a bike that matches both their appearance and riding preferences. The open world is inspired by landmark locations in Vancouver, Canada, and features more than 25 km of road network along with hundreds of street spots, creating a large environment to explore.

In addition to free riding and exploration, the game offers a variety of challenges, including Races, Trick Attacks, Secret Gaps, and Tech Attacks. These activities are intended to test riding skills and provide extended gameplay. Players can also session with up to three friends anywhere in the world, adding a multiplayer element to the experience.

According to the listed features, BMX Streets includes physics-based BMX riding, a modern control system, a replay editor, a tweak system, BMX customization, pro riders, free running, an open world with day-night cycles, a player stats menu, online multiplayer, and mod support.
