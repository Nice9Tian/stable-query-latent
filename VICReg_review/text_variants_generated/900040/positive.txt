ELEX II is a bold sequel that expands the distinctive open-world role-playing experience established by ELEX, continuing the legacy of Piranha Bytes, the acclaimed creators of the Gothic and Risen series. Set once again in the striking post-apocalyptic science fantasy world of Magalan, the game offers a vast, hand-crafted landscape filled with diverse environments, multiple factions, and a strong sense of freedom that invites players to shape their journey on their own terms.

The story follows Jax several years after his victory over the Hybrid, as a new threat emerges from the sky and unleashes the perilous power of dark Elex, putting all life on the planet at risk. To protect peace on Magalan and safeguard his family, Jax must persuade the factions to unite against the invaders while searching for his separated son, Dex. This setup gives the adventure a clear personal and planetary stakes, driving the narrative through a world of moral choices and meaningful consequences.

One of ELEX II’s greatest strengths is the way it lets players explore Magalan with unprecedented freedom. Jax’s trusted jetpack is central to traversal, allowing him to move across the map and even fly, making the enormous world feel more accessible and dynamic. The game’s lived-in setting is reinforced by unique NPCs who remember the player’s actions and react accordingly. They may join or leave the group depending on behavior, and they can even be killed, with these outcomes affecting the story in tangible ways.

Combat is another major highlight, with fluid close and ranged fighting supported by a massively improved control system. Combined with the game’s branching consequences and immersive moral decisions, this creates an experience that feels responsive and personal throughout. Powered by the Piranha Bytes Engine Genome in its sixth iteration, ELEX II stands as a polished and ambitious adventure that celebrates exploration, player choice, and a richly imagined world.
