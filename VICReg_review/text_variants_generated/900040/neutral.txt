ELEX II is an open-world action role-playing game and the sequel to ELEX, developed by Piranha Bytes, the studio behind the Gothic and Risen series. Set in the post-apocalyptic science fantasy world of Magalan, the game offers large handcrafted environments that players can explore with significant freedom, including extensive traversal with a jetpack.

The story takes place several years after Jax defeated the Hybrid. A new threat arrives from the sky and unleashes the dangerous powers of dark Elex, putting all life on the planet at risk. To protect Magalan and his own family, Jax must work to convince the different factions to unite against the invaders while also searching for his separated son, Dex.

Gameplay emphasizes exploration, faction interaction, and consequence-driven choices. Players can traverse Magalan with a jetpack, including flying across the map. The world contains unique NPCs who remember the player’s actions and respond to them accordingly. Companions can join or leave the group based on behavior, and they can also be killed, affecting the story. Combat features both close-range and ranged encounters with an improved control system. The game presents a story in which player decisions have consequences, set within a diverse science fantasy universe with multiple factions.
