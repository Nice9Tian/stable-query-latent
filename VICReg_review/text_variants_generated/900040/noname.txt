This is a sequel to a vintage open-world role-playing game from the creators of the Gothic and Risen series. It takes place in a post-apocalyptic science-fantasy world filled with large, handcrafted environments and a variety of factions and regions. The setting emphasizes freedom of movement and exploration, including the use of a jet-powered device that lets players travel across the map and even fly.

Several years after the defeat of a major enemy, a new threat arrives from the sky and unleashes dangerous dark energy, putting the planet and its inhabitants at risk. The central character must work to protect the fragile peace, secure the safety of his family, persuade rival groups to unite against the invaders, and search for his separated son. The story unfolds through player choice, with actions affecting how events develop.

The world is designed to feel lived-in and reactive. It features distinctive non-player characters who remember what the player has done and respond accordingly. Companions may join or leave depending on behavior, and some can die, which can alter the course of the narrative. Combat combines close-quarters and ranged fighting, supported by an improved control system. Overall, the game presents an open-ended adventure in which exploration, faction politics, and moral decisions play important roles.
