*Beyond a Steel Sky* is a striking and highly acclaimed cyberpunk adventure from Charles Cecil, creator of the *Broken Sword* series, with art direction by Dave Gibbons, the legendary artist behind *Watchmen*. As the long-awaited sequel to the cult classic *Beneath a Steel Sky*, it brings a rich legacy to life with style, intelligence, and atmosphere.

Players take on the role of Robert Foster, who is determined to bring home a child taken during a brutal attack. His search leads to Union City, one of the last surviving mega-cities in a world scarred by devastating wars and political collapse. At first glance, Union City appears to be a shining utopia, where people enjoy life under the guidance of an altruistic AI, surrounded by attentive androids, elegant living spaces, piazzas, and bars. Yet beneath this polished surface lies a darker truth waiting to be uncovered.

The game delivers a dramatic, humorous, cyberpunk thriller experience, where engaging puzzles propel a fast-paced narrative through a dynamic world that reacts to the player’s actions. Its world is populated by willful characters with their own motivations, and players can subvert them using a unique hacking tool. This system opens up multiple puzzle solutions shaped by player choices, making each encounter feel thoughtful and rewarding.

At its core, *Beyond a Steel Sky* combines intelligent puzzle design with an intriguing story full of dark conspiracies, and a powerful antagonist to overcome. It explores timely themes such as social control, artificial intelligence, and total surveillance, giving the adventure added depth and relevance.

Visually, the game stands out with a beautifully detailed, comic-book-inspired art style that reflects the distinct vision of Dave Gibbons. Blending narrative ambition, clever mechanics, and a memorable setting, *Beyond a Steel Sky* offers a compelling and polished adventure for players who enjoy story-driven games with smart, interconnected puzzles.
