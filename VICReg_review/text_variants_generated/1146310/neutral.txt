Beyond a Steel Sky is a dramatic, humorous cyberpunk thriller and the long-awaited sequel to the cult classic *Beneath a Steel Sky*. Created by Charles Cecil, the creator of the *Broken Sword* series, with art direction by Dave Gibbons, the legendary comic book artist behind *Watchmen*, the game follows Robert Foster after a child is abducted in a brutal attack. Determined to bring the child home, Foster’s investigation leads him to Union City, one of the last remaining mega-cities in a world scarred by shattering wars and political collapse.

Union City presents itself as a utopia, where people live under the guidance of an altruistic AI, surrounded by attentive androids, designer living spaces, piazzas, and bars. However, beneath this polished surface lies a darker reality. The story centers on uncovering conspiracies and confronting a terrifying antagonist while exploring themes of social control, AI, and total surveillance.

Gameplay is driven by engaging, intelligent puzzles woven into a fast-paced narrative. The game takes place in a dynamic world populated by characters with their own motivations, which the player can influence or subvert. A unique hacking tool expands puzzle-solving by allowing multiple solutions based on player choices. The game’s comic-book styled presentation gives the world a detailed visual identity shaped by Dave Gibbons’ art direction.
