Blood of Steel is a competitive battle game set in a world transformed by the cataclysmic events at the end of the Fifth Epoch, when the Earth changed and countless islands merged into the continent of Blood of Steel. In this setting, a new generation of humans inherits memories of past abilities and begins rebuilding an ancient civilization, while heroes from across history awaken to join the struggle for imperial dominance.

The game emphasizes fair battles where victory depends on strategy, coordination, and skill rather than higher cultivation or pay-to-win advantages. Players command large-scale legions of thousands of units, using formations, unit coordination, and tactical maneuvers to secure victory in battlefield clashes.

Alongside these massive army battles, Blood of Steel features direct character control built around a classic four-directional mounted combat system. Its melee combat includes armor penetration, rolling, leaping attacks, and combinations of light and heavy strikes, focusing on close-range weapon combat.

The roster brings together emperors and generals from different civilizations and eras, including Roman, Three Kingdoms, Greek, and Chu-Han figures. Players can gather the heroes they admire, command them as comrades, and lead them into battle.

The game is designed for fast-paced matches, with battles that begin immediately and can deliver a full match experience in about 10 minutes. It also features hero customization with a variety of modern and fashionable styles, allowing players to mix and match outfits for their generals.
