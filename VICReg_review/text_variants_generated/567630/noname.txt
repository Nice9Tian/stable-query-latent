This strategy action game is set in a transformed world where a catastrophic upheaval merged many islands into a single continent, and a new generation of humans inherited memories of past abilities. In this setting, ancient civilizations are rebuilt and legendary leaders from multiple eras and cultures awaken once more, creating a large-scale conflict for imperial dominance.

The core experience focuses on fair competitive battles. The game emphasizes that victories are determined by skill, tactical judgment, and battlefield execution rather than by progression advantages or payment-based strength differences. Players command large legions made up of thousands of combat units, using formations, unit coordination, and tactical maneuvers to outplay opponents in massive clashes.

Alongside the army-level warfare, the game also features direct character control with a classic four-direction combat system. Mounted slashing and defense form the foundation of the action, while additional mechanics such as armor penetration, rolling, leaping attacks, and light-heavy combo sequences expand the melee system. This creates close-quarters combat built around weapons and timing.

A major feature is the recruitment of historical-inspired heroes drawn from different civilizations and periods, including ancient empires and famous conflict eras. These figures can be gathered under the player’s command and used as loyal companions in battle. The game also presents them with updated visual styles, mixing cultural influences and fashionable designs, allowing for varied outfit combinations.

Matches are designed to be fast-paced and immediate, with players leading their legion into enemy territory from the start. The result is a condensed battlefield experience that aims to deliver a complete and satisfying match in around ten minutes, combining large-scale strategy with direct action combat.
