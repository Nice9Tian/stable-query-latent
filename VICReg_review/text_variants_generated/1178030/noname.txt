This title presents a pilot installment of a larger project, with additional parts planned for release later. It follows two urban gang members as they set out to challenge the most formidable criminal figure in their world. Framed by the atmosphere of 1990s late-night television, the story emphasizes strange situations, unattractive personalities, and fast-moving action.

The player accompanies a gang known as **The Thornz**, beginning in the slums and working upward through the criminal hierarchy. Gameplay centers on action-driven encounters in which the player controls the protagonist’s sword with the mouse, making precise cuts to defeat enemies in aggressive, adrenaline-heavy battles. Exploration takes place across a decadent world populated by gangsters and other low-status figures, while progression involves climbing through the ranks toward becoming the most infamous criminal alive.

Along the way, the player fights rival gangs, assists eccentric characters, and uncovers disturbing secrets hidden within a notorious demilitarized region referred to as **The Gray Zone**. The experience combines a bizarre narrative with light role-playing elements and a strong focus on high-speed combat. Themes mentioned include sects, government influence, violence, strangeness, decadence, conspiracies, ugliness, and cute girls.
