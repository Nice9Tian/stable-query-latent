**Star Fetchers** is an action-adventure game described as a pilot installment, with future parts planned for later release. The game follows two inner-city thugs from the gang “The Thornz” as they try to rise from the slums and take on the toughest gangster ever. Its story draws on the atmosphere of 1990s midnight television and presents a world filled with bizarre situations, ugly characters, violence, decadence, and conspiracies.

Gameplay centers on guiding the main character’s sword with the mouse and making precise cuts to defeat enemies in fast, aggressive battles. Players explore a decadent setting populated by gangsters and other lowlifes, battle rival gangs, assist strange characters, and uncover the secrets of the notorious demilitarized district known as “The Gray zone.” The game also includes light RPG elements alongside its high-paced action.

Overall, Star Fetchers combines a bizarre narrative with sword-based combat, gang-focused progression, and a stylized world built around violence, weirdness, and criminal ambition.
