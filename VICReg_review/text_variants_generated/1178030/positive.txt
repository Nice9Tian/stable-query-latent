**Star Fetchers** is a bold and distinctive action-adventure that follows two inner-city thugs as they rise against the hardest gangster ever known. This pilot installment introduces **The Thornz**, a gang clawing their way out of the slums and toward the top in a world shaped by the energy and attitude of 90’s midnight TV.

The game stands out with its bizarre story, ugly characters, decadent atmosphere, and fast-paced action. Players guide the main character’s sword with the mouse, making precise cuts to slice through enemies in aggressive, adrenaline-filled battles. Along the way, they explore a grim and decadent world packed with gangsters, lowlifes, weirdos, and dangerous secrets.

As the story unfolds, players battle rival gangs, help strange characters, and uncover the horrible truths hidden within the infamous demilitarized district known as **The Gray Zone**. With its blend of action, light RPG elements, and a uniquely offbeat style, **Star Fetchers** delivers a gritty and memorable journey through violence, conspiracies, sects, government corruption, weirdness, and decadent chaos.
