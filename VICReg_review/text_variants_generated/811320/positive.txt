**Jupiter Hell** is a tough, stylish turn-based roguelike that proudly embraces the spirit of classic 90s sci-fi action. In this brutal universe, every move matters: when you act, your enemies act, and death is permanent. The game captures the feeling of old-school challenge without holding your hand, while presenting it through much sharper modern visuals and a striking CRT-inspired aesthetic.

At its core, Jupiter Hell throws you into a battlefield filled with demonic and alien enemies, armed with classic heavy-hitting weapons such as a shotgun, chaingun, railgun, and even a chainsaw. It is all about survival, smart decisions, and adapting to whatever nightmare waits around the corner. Each run is different thanks to procedurally generated levels, encouraging players to experiment with new builds and discover how to best endure the chaos.

Progress comes through unlocking new perks and strengthening your character for future attempts. If you survive long enough, you can also unlock additional challenges, and the game makes it clear that these will only raise the difficulty further. Developed by ChaosForge, the team behind the acclaimed Doom, the Roguelike, Jupiter Hell is a spiritual successor built on years of experience with top-down cosmic horror roguelikes. It delivers a demanding, rewarding, and atmospheric journey through a hellish sci-fi world.
