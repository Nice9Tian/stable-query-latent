This satirical spy-fi strategy game puts players in control of a diabolical mastermind determined to conquer the world from a custom-built island stronghold. The experience centers on constructing and managing an elaborate underground base, setting up a cover operation above ground, and developing a growing criminal organization capable of resisting repeated attacks from law enforcement forces.

A major part of the gameplay is designing the hidden lair. Players choose an island location, shape the interior layout to suit their preferred style, and build hazardous machinery and other sinister devices for their workforce to use. Expansion is tied closely to defense, since the base must be prepared to withstand increasingly frequent raids by the forces of justice.

The management layer also involves training a roster of minions and turning them into specialized experts. As the operation grows, players can recruit powerful henchmen to serve as key allies, with each one supporting different approaches to evil planning. These assistants act as more capable reinforcements when simple minions are not enough.

Defense is handled through a network of traps and combinations. Research unlocks new trap systems, which can be arranged to catch and disable intruders in creative ways. Different traps can be chained together for stronger results, adding a tactical layer to each confrontation with invading agents.

The broader campaign revolves around carrying out a series of schemes to advance a world domination plan and construct a doomsday device. These plots range from absurdly ambitious missions to a large pool of varied objectives, giving the game a mix of strategic base-building, villain management, and humorous scenario-driven progression.

The available season content expands the experience with additional campaign material, lair items, henchmen, and minion-focused packs, offering more content for players who want to extend their criminal empire further.
