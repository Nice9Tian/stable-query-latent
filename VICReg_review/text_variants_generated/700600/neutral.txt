Evil Genius 2: World Domination is a satirical spy-fi lair builder and direct sequel to the 2004 cult classic *Evil Genius*. In the game, players take control of an Evil Genius and begin setting a world-domination scheme in motion, combining base construction, minion management, defensive planning, and criminal plotting. The overall premise centers on building an evil operation strong enough to withstand the Forces of Justice while advancing toward the completion of a Doomsday Device.

A major part of the game is constructing a unique island lair. Players choose a location, shape the internal layout of their hazardous hideaway, and customize it to fit their preferred play style. The lair is not just a base of operations, but also a place to build sinister devices that support the minions working inside it.

Minion management is another core feature. Players train a force of criminal minions and can expand their workforce by creating new specialists to support their schemes. The game also allows the recruitment of Henchmen, described as powerful allies that can complement different play styles and serve as the Evil Genius’s right hand.

Defense plays an important role as well. Since the Forces of Justice arrive regularly, players can research and develop trap networks to protect their lair. These traps can be combined for more effective results, with examples including the Pinball Bumper and the Venus Spy-Trap. The game emphasizes that multiple traps can be used together to stop intruders.

Progress in the game is driven by nefarious plots and schemes. Players carry out evil operations to further their larger plan of creating a Doomsday Device and dominating the world. The page mentions a variety of specific objectives, including selling the British Royal Family, kidnapping the Governor of Maine, and “literally BAKE ALASKA,” indicating a broad range of mission content.

The store page also highlights the Season Pass, which includes additional content such as one Campaign Pack, one Lair Items Pack, two Henchmen Packs, and two Minion Packs. It is noted that the Season Pass is included with the Deluxe Edition.

Overall, Evil Genius 2: World Domination presents itself as a humorous strategy game about building an evil empire, managing minions and henchmen, defending a secret lair, and executing increasingly absurd schemes to take over the world.
