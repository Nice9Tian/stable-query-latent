**Evil Genius 2: World Domination** is a satirical spy-fi lair builder that puts players in control of an Evil Genius with one clear goal: world domination. As a direct sequel to the 2004 cult classic, it embraces a wonderfully wicked sense of humor while letting you build an evil empire from the ground up. When being bad feels this good, the Forces of Justice are in for a serious problem.

At the heart of the game is the creation of your own unique island lair and cover operation. Players can choose their paradise and shape the internal structure of their hazardous hideaway to suit their preferred style of play. The lair can be filled with sinister machinery and wicked devices, giving minions the tools they need to carry out your master plan in style.

Your criminal organization grows through the training of a force of minions, and as the operation expands, new specialist minions can be created to support increasingly ambitious schemes. For added muscle and personality, you can also recruit powerful Henchmen who complement your play style and serve as vital right hands to your mastermind.

Defense is just as important as planning. The Forces of Justice will inevitably come knocking, so Evil Genius 2 challenges you to research and develop trap networks that can turn intruders into unfortunate examples of your ingenuity. Traps such as the Pinball Bumper and the Venus Spy-Trap can be combined for even greater effect, making the lair’s defenses both strategic and gleefully destructive.

The game also features a wide range of nefarious plots to advance your plans for a Doomsday Device and global domination. These schemes include outrageous objectives like selling the British Royal Family, kidnapping the Governor of Maine, and literally baking Alaska. With hundreds of possible objectives, the game offers plenty of variety for players who enjoy elaborate criminal operations and a constant stream of comic villainy.

The Season Pass adds even more to the experience, including a new Genius, Campaign content, an Island Lair Location, Henchmen, Minion Packs, and a Lair Items Pack. It is also included with the Deluxe Edition, expanding the game’s already ambitious mix of strategy, building, and evil flair.

Overall, **Evil Genius 2: World Domination** stands out as a clever and entertaining lair builder that celebrates villainy with style. Its mix of construction, minion management, trap-based defense, and absurd global schemes makes it a fitting tribute to the cult favorite that inspired it.
