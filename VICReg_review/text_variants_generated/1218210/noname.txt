This creature-collecting RPG follows a newly recruited battle researcher of a global scientific society who begins their first day on a new world, only for a mysterious force to attack the workplace and trigger a larger conflict. From there, the player assembles a squad of collectible creatures, investigates the intruders, and confronts an escalating threat that endangers the planet.

The core gameplay centers on taming and training more than 100 distinct creatures, each defined by elements, traits, and skills. Players can catch them, develop them, and deploy them in battles against wild creatures, human trainers, and massive bosses. Combat uses a stamina-based system in which every action consumes energy, encouraging careful selection of attacks and abilities. Battles also involve a wide range of status conditions that can deal damage, drain enemy stamina, or disable opponents.

The adventure unfolds across six major regions and cities connected by numerous routes. These areas include varied environments such as frozen glacial caverns and scorching deserts, and each location contains quests, secrets, and collectible items. The story is presented as a classic Japanese-style role-playing narrative with twists and revelations, as the player follows the trail of an alien threat and aids the scientific society in stopping it.

The game also emphasizes player choice through extensive customization. Difficulty settings do more than adjust statistics, altering core mechanics to create either a relaxed or highly demanding experience. Special modes such as randomization and a challenge-rule mode further change the creature-catching formula for players seeking additional tension. Character creation includes hundreds of customization options for appearance, and the creatures themselves can also be visually customized, including color variants tied to elemental settings.

Additional features include online battles, where friends can test the strength of their squads against each other. Recent updates have also added creature color customization, a kitchen system that converts fruit into cake, and new cake recipes. Overall, the game presents itself as a modern tribute to classic monster-taming role-playing games, combining retro-inspired pixel art with updated systems and broad player-driven options.
