Othercide is a tactical RPG set in Humanity’s last hope, where players lead the Daughters, echoes of the greatest warrior to ever live, against Suffering and death. The game centers on commanding an army of Daughters in battle, with combat performance directly affecting their fate, abilities, and personality.

A core part of the experience is making difficult decisions, including sacrificing one Daughter to heal another. Survivors become stronger and are prepared for the next battle, reinforcing the game’s emphasis on loss, survival, and progression. Combat is described as an intricate system of actions and counters, encouraging players to plan ahead and create ability chains to outsmart enemies.

The game features epic boss battles against the sources of Suffering, presented as dread creatures drawn from humanity’s worst crimes against itself. It also includes the Dynamic Timeline System, which opens up many combat possibilities and supports the game’s strategic depth. Othercide presents a deep and complex narrative, and failure is not the end: players return with lost powers to face new nightmares.
