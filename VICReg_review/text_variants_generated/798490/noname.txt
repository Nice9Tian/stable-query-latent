This tactical role-playing game places the player in a bleak world where humanity’s last hope depends on leading a group of warrior echoes against overwhelming suffering and death. The player commands an army of young female fighters, each one shaped by battle experience, survival, and difficult choices. Success in combat affects their growth, personality, and overall effectiveness, while losses carry real consequences.

A core part of the experience is sacrifice: the player may need to give up one fighter in order to restore another, with those who remain becoming stronger and better prepared for future encounters. Battles are described as a careful and highly strategic exchange of actions and counters, where planning ahead and chaining abilities together is essential for overcoming enemies.

The game features large boss encounters against horrific beings tied to humanity’s darkest crimes against itself. It also includes a dynamic timeline-based combat system that allows for a wide range of tactical possibilities. Alongside this, the game presents a deep narrative structure and a progression loop built around failure, recovery, and renewed attempts. Defeat is not the end; instead, the player returns with lost powers and faces an even greater nightmare.
