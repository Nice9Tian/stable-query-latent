**Othercide** is a striking tactical RPG that casts you into humanity’s last desperate stand against Suffering and death. You lead the Daughters, echoes of the greatest warrior to ever live, and guide them through a bleak war where every decision matters. Their fate, their growth, and even their personalities are shaped by your skill in combat, making each battle feel meaningful and personal.

At the heart of the game is a deep and elegant combat system built around the **Dynamic Timeline System**, which creates endless tactical possibilities. Battles unfold like a carefully choreographed dance of actions and counters, rewarding forward planning and clever ability chaining. Rather than relying on brute force alone, success comes from outsmarting nightmarish enemies and making the most of every move.

Othercide also stands out for its demanding but rewarding progression. You must make difficult sacrifices, even letting one Daughter fall in order to heal another, ensuring the survivors grow stronger and remain ready for the next confrontation. This cycle of loss, evolution, and renewed resolve gives the game a powerful sense of momentum.

The experience is driven further by **epic boss battles** against the sources of Suffering themselves—terrifying creatures born from the darkest crimes of humanity against humanity. Combined with its deep, complex narrative, Othercide offers a haunting and memorable journey where failure is not the end. You return, stronger and wiser, to face each new nightmare.
