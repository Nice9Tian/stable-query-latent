This is a cooperative horror game designed for one to four players, where the main objective is to identify the kind of evil presence haunting cursed locations and then drive it out using the tools at your disposal, either by yourself or together with a team. Built with Unreal Engine 5, it emphasizes high-end visuals within the co-op horror genre.

Each session can unfold differently thanks to random events and ghost encounters that appear in every playthrough. Communication is part of the experience, but players must be cautious about what they say, since the haunted environments can react to spoken words in unexpected ways; in some cases, a single word may even reveal a hidden passage. Players can also choose from different paranormal locations to investigate and may invite others into their safe haven between missions.

The game includes a showcase area for displaying achievements, as well as a progression system tied to earned money. This currency can be used to upgrade the safe haven or purchase a new one entirely. A pet companion can also be bought to remain with the player in the safe haven. In addition, the game offers multiple maps, a variety of equipment, daily and weekly tasks, and special events and missions tied to specific dates.
