This simulation game centers on rebuilding, expanding, and improving a tavern-style establishment. Players are given the freedom to take different approaches to management while dealing with outside challenges, gradually shaping their own version of the story.

The gameplay begins with restoring the venue, where the player renovates the workspace, buys resources, and starts serving customers in order to earn money for further upgrades. Progress is tied closely to reinvestment, making the business more efficient over time.

A major part of the experience is food and drink preparation. The game includes actions such as pouring beer into large mugs, throwing vegetables into a cauldron, and chopping and tenderizing meat. Cooking is skill-based in the sense that the more time spent preparing dishes, the more skilled the player becomes. Improved cooking ability leads to better tips, reinforcing the value of practice and consistency.

Customer service is another core mechanic. Serving guests quickly and efficiently is essential for success, and customers expect their table to be filled with mugs of beer and appetizers at the same time. Meeting these expectations appears to be a key factor in keeping the business prosperous.

As the establishment grows, players must also manage supplies and operations. This includes ordering beer and cooking resources on time, maintaining a fire in the work stations, and controlling the quality of both food and drink. Players can also purchase improvements that make work more efficient. Over time, successful management increases the reputation of the tavern, allowing its fame to spread widely throughout the kingdom-like setting.
