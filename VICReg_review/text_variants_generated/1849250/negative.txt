EA SPORTS™ WRC 24 is presented as a rally expansion and content bundle rather than a truly complete reinvention, and that already makes it feel more like an add-on trying to stretch an existing package than a bold standalone attraction. Buying the game grants access to the EA SPORTS WRC Locations & Cars Content Pack, the Le Maestros Content Pack, and the Hard Chargers Content Pack, while the WRC 2024 Season Expansion adds Tet Rally Latvia and the Orlen 80th Rally Poland. It also includes newer vehicles such as the Ford Fiesta Rally3 Evo and Toyota GR Yaris Rally1 HYBRID '24, along with more than 50 new liveries and 52 official crews.

The game’s selling point is the promise to “build the car of your dreams” in what is described as the biggest rally game ever, and it is positioned as the official videogame of the FIA World Rally Championship. Developed by the team behind the DiRT Rally series, it leans heavily on pedigree and official licensing to justify its value. Still, the page mainly sells a feature list, not a compelling reason to be excited, and the emphasis on extra packs and seasonal expansion content gives the impression of a fragmented offering built around additions rather than a fully fresh experience.
