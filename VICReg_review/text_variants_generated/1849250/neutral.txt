EA SPORTS™ WRC 24 is presented as an expansion and content bundle for EA SPORTS™ WRC, the official videogame of the FIA World Rally Championship. Buying EA SPORTS™ WRC 24 grants access to the EA SPORTS WRC Locations & Cars Content Pack, the Le Maestros Content Pack, and the Hard Chargers Content Pack.

The WRC 2024 Season Expansion adds new rally content, including Tet Rally Latvia and the Orlen 80th Rally Poland. It also introduces additional vehicles such as the Ford Fiesta Rally3 Evo and the Toyota GR Yaris Rally1 HYBRID '24. Players can customize rally cars with more than 50 new liveries and compete against 52 official crews.

The base game is described as EA SPORTS™ WRC, a rally game focused on building the car of your dreams. It is identified as the biggest rally game from EA SPORTS, and the first official WRC videogame developed by the award-winning team behind the DiRT Rally series.
