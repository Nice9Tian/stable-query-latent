EA SPORTS™ WRC 24 delivers an exhilarating rally experience that expands the official FIA World Rally Championship game with even more content to explore. By purchasing EA SPORTS™ WRC 24, players receive the EA SPORTS WRC Locations & Cars Content Pack, the Le Maestros Content Pack, and the Hard Chargers Content Pack, adding valuable depth to an already impressive rally package.

The WRC 2024 Season Expansion elevates the experience further with new locations to master, including Tet Rally Latvia and the Orlen 80th Rally Poland. Players can get behind the wheel of the latest high-performance machines, such as the Ford Fiesta Rally3 Evo and Toyota GR Yaris Rally1 HYBRID '24, bringing fresh speed and excitement to the stages. More than 50 new liveries offer extensive customization options, while competition against 52 official crews adds even more authenticity to the championship atmosphere.

As the biggest rally game ever from EA SPORTS, WRC is built around the idea of creating the car of your dreams and pushing it to the limit across challenging rally stages. It is the all-new official videogame of the FIA World Rally Championship, developed by the award-winning team behind the DiRT Rally series, and it stands as a strong celebration of precision driving, official content, and high-speed rally action.
