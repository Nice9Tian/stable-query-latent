This rally title presents an expanded motorsport experience centered on official championship content and a season-based expansion. It offers a large-scale rally package with additional location and vehicle content, along with multiple themed content packs included in the purchase.

The game’s expansion introduces new rally environments, including two fresh stages set in distinct real-world-inspired locations. It also adds updated high-performance vehicles from the latest season, giving players access to modern rally machinery alongside a wider roster of official crews. More than 50 new liveries are available for car customization, allowing for greater visual variety across the garage and on the stage.

At its core, the game focuses on building and driving rally cars in a broad championship framework. It is presented as the official video game of an FIA-sanctioned rally championship and is described as the largest rally game the series has offered so far. The project comes from a studio known for earlier rally work, continuing its focus on authentic rally handling, competitive stages, and season content built around the structure of professional rally competition.
