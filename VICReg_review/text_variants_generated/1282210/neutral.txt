DEEMO -Reborn- is a story-driven rhythm adventure centered on a girl who falls from the sky and loses her past, and Deemo, a lonely pianist living in a treehouse world. Their accidental meeting begins a fairytale-like journey, accompanied by music played on the piano. The game’s story follows the girl as she seeks a way home, with the mysterious phrase “Never Left Without Saying Goodbye──” framing its atmosphere.

The game presents several interconnected elements. In its exploration-focused side, players control the little girl in an ancient castle and around a mysterious tree that grows when music fills the air. As the player investigates the castle, they can solve riddles and uncover hidden mysteries, collect sheet music falling from the heavens, unlock new areas, and gradually reveal the truth behind the setting.

Rhythm gameplay is also central to the experience. Players perform songs on the piano, and these performances help the mysterious tree grow while advancing the story. As more of the narrative is unveiled, the game builds toward the girl’s eventual return to her warm home.

DEEMO -Reborn- includes more than 60 classic songs, along with new music pieces to challenge players. The overall experience combines investigation, exploration, and piano rhythm gameplay into a single adventure.
