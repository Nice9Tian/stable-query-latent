A young girl falls from the sky with no memory of her past and arrives at a lonely treehouse world where a solitary piano player lives. Their unexpected meeting begins a quiet, fairytale-like journey shaped by music, mystery, and the search for a way home.

Set around an ancient castle and a strange tree that grows in response to melody, the game combines exploration, puzzle-solving, and rhythm-based play. Players control the girl as she moves through the castle, investigating hidden riddles and uncovering secrets along the way. Sheet music that descends from above opens new areas and gradually reveals more of the underlying story.

Music is at the center of the experience. Piano performances are used both as gameplay and as a narrative device, with each successful performance helping the mysterious tree grow and unlocking additional story content. The journey continues as the girl comes closer to returning to the warm home she longs for.

The game also offers a large collection of songs, including over 60 well-known classic pieces alongside new tracks designed to provide fresh challenges.
