Thunder Tier One is a realistic top-down shooter set in the 1990s, sometime after the fall of the USSR. Players join an elite special operations team tasked with stopping a terrorist organization in the Eastern European nation of Salobia. The game can be played solo with AI teammates or in 4-player co-op, with an emphasis on tactical planning, careful loadout selection, and communication.

Its story-driven campaign follows Thunder as it is deployed to locate SBR leader Marko Antonov and end the group’s campaign of terror. The 9-mission campaign is designed for cooperative play, but solo players are accompanied by three AI teammates who react intelligently to situations. Players can also issue individual and group commands, giving them control over how the AI supports each operation.

A major part of the game is weapon and loadout customization. Before each mission, players choose from a wide range of weapons, attachments, ammo types, armor, grenades, tools, and even camouflage patterns. The same customization applies to AI teammates. Loadouts are meant to support different strategies, whether that means using suppressed weapons, IR lasers, and NVGs for stealth, or heavier armor and extra ammunition for a more direct approach.

Thunder Tier One also focuses on realistic gunplay. Weapons have distinct attributes such as handling, rate of fire, muzzle velocity, effective range, and accuracy. Ammo type affects shot accuracy, damage against armored and unarmored targets, and whether rounds penetrate cover, as well as the damage and trajectory after penetration. Success depends on understanding these mechanics and using them effectively to complete objectives.

In addition to the campaign, the game includes four multiplayer modes. Exfil is a 4v4 mode centered on escorting a package to extraction or stopping the opposing team from doing so. Advance and Secure is a 4v4 mode where teams compete to capture sectors. Death Match is a free-for-all mode for up to 8 players. Domination is a PvE mode for up to four players, where the team must secure checkpoints, disarm explosives, and defend against counter-attacks under time pressure.

The game also supports full modding, with a tool planned to include Steam Workshop integration. This will allow players to add and modify missions, weapons, AI behavior, equipment, and other elements.
