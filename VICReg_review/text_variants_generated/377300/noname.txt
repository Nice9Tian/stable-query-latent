Set in the 1990s, after the collapse of the USSR, **Vanta Unit Zero** is a realistic top-down shooter in which players join an elite special operations squad assigned to stop a terrorist group. The game can be played solo with AI teammates or in 4-player co-op, and progression depends on careful gear selection, tactical decision-making, and coordination during each operation.

The campaign is story-driven and centers on a paramilitary group turned terrorist organization called **Ravel Syndicate**, which has launched attacks on civilian targets in the Eastern European nation of **Koravia**. The elite unit is deployed to track down the group’s leader, **Dorian Vek**, and end the campaign of violence. The 9-mission campaign is designed with teamwork and communication in mind, especially in co-op, while solo players are supported by three AI teammates that respond intelligently to changing situations. Players can issue both individual and group commands, deciding how directly to manage their squad.

A major part of the game is weapon and loadout customization. Before each mission, players choose exactly what to bring, selecting from a broad range of weapons, attachments, ammunition types and quantities, armor, grenades, and tools. Clothing camouflage can also be customized. The same level of control applies to AI teammates, whose gear can be configured to fit the mission plan. Different approaches are supported, from a stealth-oriented setup with suppressed weapons, IR lasers, and night vision equipment, to a heavily armored assault loadout with extra ammunition and explosives.

Combat emphasizes realism rather than simple cover shooting. Each weapon has distinct characteristics such as handling, rate of fire, muzzle velocity, effective range, and accuracy. These factors, along with the chosen ammunition type, affect shot precision, damage against armored and unarmored targets, and whether rounds penetrate cover. The resulting ballistics and damage behavior play a key role in whether objectives can be completed successfully.

In addition to the campaign, **Vanta Unit Zero** includes several multiplayer modes for both PvP and PvE play. One mode is a 4v4 extraction-style match in which attackers must exfiltrate a package before time runs out while defenders try to stop them. Another is a 4v4 sector-control mode where teams compete to secure areas and win the match. There is also a free-for-all deathmatch for up to 8 players. On the PvE side, a mode for up to four players tasks the team with racing against the clock to capture checkpoints, disarm explosives, and withstand counterattacks.

The game also promises full modding support after launch, with a tool integrated with Steam Workshop. This will allow players to create and modify missions, weapons, AI behavior, equipment, and other content.
