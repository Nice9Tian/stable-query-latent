Thunder Tier One is a realistic top-down shooter set in the 1990s, in the years after the fall of the USSR, where players join an elite special operations team on a high-stakes mission to stop a terrorist organization. Built around careful planning, tactical execution, and disciplined teamwork, the game offers a polished and authentic combat experience that stands out from typical shooters.

At the center of the game is a story-driven 9-mission campaign set in the Eastern European nation of Salobia, where a paramilitary-turned-terrorist group called SBR has launched a series of attacks on civilian targets. Thunder is deployed to track down SBR leader Marko Antonov and bring an end to the campaign of terror. The campaign is designed for 4-player co-op, with teamwork and communication playing a crucial role in every operation. For solo players, three AI teammates join the fight, reacting intelligently to changing situations while also responding to individual and group commands. Players have full control over how much they manage their squad, making coordination a central part of the experience.

Thunder Tier One also offers deep weapon and loadout customization. Before each mission, players choose exactly what to bring, from a wide selection of weapons and attachments to ammo type and quantity, armor, grenades, tools, and even camouflage patterns for clothing. The same level of control extends to AI teammates, allowing players to outfit the entire squad according to the demands of the mission. This flexibility supports multiple approaches, whether that means a fully suppressed stealth setup with IR lasers and NVGs or a heavily armed assault loadout with extra ammo and grenades.

The game’s gunplay is built with realism in mind. Each weapon has distinct attributes such as handling, rate of fire, muzzle velocity, effective range, and accuracy. These traits, along with the selected ammunition, affect shot precision, damage against armored and unarmored targets, and whether rounds can penetrate cover and continue with altered damage and trajectory. Success depends on understanding these mechanics and using them effectively to complete objectives.

Beyond the campaign, Thunder Tier One includes several multiplayer modes that challenge both PvP and PvE skills. Exfil is a 4v4 mode focused on extracting a package while the opposing team tries to stop it. Advance and Secure pits two 4-player teams against each other in a fight to capture and hold sectors. Death Match delivers a classic free-for-all experience for up to 8 players. Domination offers a PvE challenge for up to four players, who must race against the clock to secure checkpoints, disarm explosives, and defend against counter-attacks.

The game also features full modding support, with a tool and Steam Workshop integration planned for release soon after launch. This will allow players to create and modify missions, weapons, AI behavior, equipment, and more, extending the game’s tactical possibilities even further. Thunder Tier One delivers a strong combination of realism, customization, and tactical depth, making it an impressive and engaging choice for players who value thoughtful special operations gameplay.
