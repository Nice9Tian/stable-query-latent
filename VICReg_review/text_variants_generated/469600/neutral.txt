Legion TD 2 is a multiplayer tower defense game in which players defend a lane against endless waves of enemies while also trying to outmaneuver opposing players. The core loop centers on assembling an army from more than 100 unique fighters, each with distinct strengths and abilities, then using auras, spells, and mercenary attacks to influence the outcome of battle. Players must survive as many waves as possible before their defenses fail and their king falls, while also finding ways to break their opponents before they are broken themselves.

The game supports a range of playstyles. Players can build defensively to hold back enemy swarms, play aggressively to pressure enemy fighters, or focus on economy to scale into the late game. Success depends on adapting to each match while leaning into a chosen strategy. Legion TD 2 features 8 unique legions and over 100 fighters, with fighters from each legion combining into 12 million possible combinations. Some are designed to provide early-game advantages, while others enable stronger late-game synergies.

Its core features include party play for 1 to 8 players, matchmade 2v2 and 4v4 games, and a single-player/co-op campaign with about 8 to 12 hours of content, plus 10 levels of AI opponents. The game presents itself as friendly to new players, with an efficient 15-minute tutorial, an in-game companion called Game Coach, and matchmaking intended to ease players into PvP. It also emphasizes fast and fair matches, an active player base, and cross-region global servers.

Legion TD 2 highlights replayability and strategic depth. The store page describes it as offering infinite replayability, with no two games the same, and notes extensive balance work driven by data and feedback. It also includes ranked seasons, rewards, detailed player profiles with graphs and tables, full match histories, and leaderboards. Competitive play is supported by monthly $750 cash tournaments, while weekly challenges offer puzzle-like competitions centered on finding the best build with a fixed lineup.

The game is presented as free from pay-to-win mechanics, with all in-game purchases being cosmetic. It also includes a FairPlay detection system designed to deal quickly with AFKers, griefers, and toxic players. According to the page, Legion TD 2 began as a sequel to the Legion TD Warcraft 3 mod, also known as Squadron TD in Starcraft 2, and launched in early access in 2017 before fully releasing in October 2021. The developers state that the game has received monthly updates, quality-of-life improvements, and balance changes throughout its development, and that its player base has remained active since launch.
