*Biped* is a charming and inventive adventure about two little bipedal robots, Aku and Sila, who set off on a fun, bonding journey together. The game stands out with its simple yet clever control scheme: each robot’s two legs are controlled with two sticks or the left and right mouse buttons, creating a playful system that supports everything from basic walking and sliding to more advanced tasks like operating machinery or cutting wood.

Whether playing alone or teaming up with a friend or family member, *Biped* offers an enjoyable two-player co-op experience built around communication, coordination, and shared problem-solving. The adventure unfolds across a variety of beautiful and mysterious environments on the planet, including forests, valleys, waterfalls, and icy mountains, as players explore secret paths and work their way toward the planetary beacons.

The game also adds personality and fun through customization. Players can collect treasures, buy goofy hats, and dress up their biped however they like. Matching outfits with a partner is even encouraged, adding a lighthearted touch to the journey. For those who want even more, *Biped* includes Steam Workshop support, allowing players to create their own levels, share them with the community, and experience content made by others. With its creative controls, cooperative spirit, and inviting world, *Biped* delivers a delightful adventure where, truly, nothing feels impossible.
