Dragon Age™ Inquisition – Game of the Year Edition delivers a grand, heroic fantasy adventure set in the richly detailed world of Thedas, where chaos erupts from both mortal conflict and a devastating demonic invasion. You step into the role of the Inquisitor, the one leader capable of holding together a band of champions and guiding the fate of a world in crisis. From the very beginning, the game presents you with the weight of responsibility and the chance to become the savior Thedas desperately needs.

One of the game’s greatest strengths is its enthralling, choice-driven narrative. Dragon Age: Inquisition is not simply about picking the right fighters for each battle; it is about making meaningful decisions that shape the future of Thedas. Every choice carries consequences, and those consequences can lead to a variety of outcomes. This creates a deeply personalized journey in which your actions can even alter the physical world itself, making your version of Thedas, and your heroes, feel distinct and memorable.

The world itself is another major highlight. Thedas has never been larger or more detailed, and it invites players to explore freely across a vast, dangerous landscape. You can uncover enemy keeps ready to be seized, search hidden caves teeming with creatures, and discover secrets that may determine whether you succeed or fail. The sense of scale and discovery gives every expedition real purpose and excitement.

Combat is just as impressive, offering intense strategic battles with flexibility in how you play. The optional strategic view provides a god’s-eye perspective, pausing time so you can plan your moves carefully. At the same time, the game still allows a more direct approach if that suits your style better, letting you charge forward with crossbows blazing. This balance between tactical control and action-oriented momentum makes the combat system both accessible and rewarding.

The Game of the Year Edition adds substantial value with a wide range of included content. It features the base game, Flames of the Inquisition Arsenal, Armor, and Armored Mount, Dragon Age multiplayer, Deluxe Edition chests, the Skyhold Throne, Red Hart Halla and Bog Unicorn, as well as the Jaws of Hakkon, The Descent, and Trespasser DLC packs. It also includes Spoils of the Avvar, offering 5 new mounts, 2 new armor sets, and new ways to personalize your base at Skyhold, plus Spoils of the Qunari, which adds an armored war mount, new armor sets including one in the style of the Arishok, and Skyhold items.

The included expansions add meaningful new adventures to the experience. Jaws of Hakkon lets you discover the fate of the last Inquisitor and the powerful dragon he hunted. The Descent takes you underground to uncover the source of mysterious earthquakes threatening Thedas. Trespasser brings the story to a dramatic close as you decide the Inquisition’s final fate while new threats emerge.

Altogether, Dragon Age™ Inquisition – Game of the Year Edition stands as a rich, expansive, and highly engaging fantasy RPG. With its meaningful choices, expansive world, strategic combat, and extensive bonus content, it offers a powerful and satisfying journey through one of Thedas’s most important stories.
