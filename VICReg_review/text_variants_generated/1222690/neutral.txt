Dragon Age: Inquisition – Game of the Year Edition places the player in the role of the Inquisitor, a hero tasked with helping save Thedas during a time of widespread chaos. The world is caught between ongoing factional conflict and a larger demonic invasion, and the player leads a band of champions in an effort to hold everything together. The game presents this as a difficult journey shaped by leadership, responsibility, and major decisions.

A central feature of the game is its choice-driven narrative. Players do more than choose where characters go in battle; they make decisions that influence the future of Thedas. These choices can lead to different outcomes, and the game emphasizes that actions may affect even the physical state of the world, making each playthrough feel distinct.

The game also offers a large world to explore. Thedas is described as bigger and more detailed than before, with open areas that contain enemy keeps, hidden caves, and dangerous creatures. Exploration is tied to progression, as uncovering secrets can help determine whether the player succeeds or fails.

Combat is presented as strategic and flexible. The optional strategic view gives players a tactical, pause-based perspective for planning battles, while those who prefer a more direct approach can continue fighting in real time. The game highlights that there is no single correct way to play, aside from choices that lead to defeat.

The Game of the Year Edition includes the base game along with several bonus items and expansions. Its contents include Flames of the Inquisition Arsenal, Armor, and Armored Mount, Dragon Age multiplayer Deluxe Edition chests, the Skyhold Throne, Red Hart Halla, and Bog Unicorn. It also includes the Spoils of the Avvar and Spoils of the Qunari content, which add mounts, armor sets, and Skyhold customization items. In addition, the edition contains the Jaws of Hakkon, The Descent, and Trespasser DLC packs. These expansions add new story content, including discovering the fate of the last Inquisitor and the dragon he hunted, investigating the source of earthquakes threatening Thedas, and deciding the final fate of the Inquisition as new threats appear.
