This is a level-based adventure game that combines first-person shooting, roguelite progression, and role-playing elements. Players take control of distinct heroes, each with their own abilities, and move through procedurally generated stages while collecting randomly dropped weapons and other rewards. The structure is built around repeated runs, where death leads back into a new cycle and each restart offers a fresh experience.

The game can be played solo or in cooperative play with up to four players. Because every level is randomized, players encounter different layouts, enemies, weapons, items, checkpoints, and combat pacing each time they begin a new run. This makes experimentation with different builds an important part of the experience, as the gameplay is designed to support varied combinations across repeated attempts.

Its feature set includes more than 100 different items, a wide selection of weapons, multiple heroes, and additional game-plus style progression with changing mechanics. The visual presentation is described as having a distinctive art style, while the overall design emphasizes co-op, replayability, and randomized level-based adventure.
