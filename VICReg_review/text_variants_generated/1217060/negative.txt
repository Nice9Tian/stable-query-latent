Gunfire Reborn is a level-based FPS roguelite with RPG elements, but its appeal is largely built on repetition and randomness rather than any especially refined design. Players control distinct heroes with different abilities, move through procedurally generated stages, and collect randomly dropped weapons and items while trying to survive each run. The game supports solo play as well as 4-player co-op, and every restart produces a new randomized set of levels, enemies, combat rhythms, checkpoints, weapons, and other pickups.

On paper, it offers a broad mix of systems: FPS, roguelite, and RPG mechanics combined into a death-cycle structure where players are meant to build different setups over repeated attempts. It also advertises more than 100 items, various weapons, multiple heroes, and multiple game-plus modes with different mechanics. A unique art style is part of the presentation, but the core experience still depends heavily on procedural variation and endless reruns of familiar content.
