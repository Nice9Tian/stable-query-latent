This game presents a science-fiction strategy experience centered on construction, survival, and large-scale exploration on an alien planet. The player takes the role of an elite scientist-commando operating inside a powerful mechanized suit, tasked with entering a one-way portal to a distant world and establishing the infrastructure needed to create a return route. The suit is designed to endure extreme environmental conditions and supports a wide range of functions, including base construction, resource extraction, specimen gathering, and combat.

A major part of the gameplay revolves around building a complex industrial network. Progress depends on assembling mines, refineries, power plants, and research facilities to generate the energy required for advanced objectives. Rather than relying on simple early-game structures, the player must expand an entire production chain to support long-term development and mission goals.

Defense is equally important. As the player alters the environment and expands industry, hostile native creatures begin to react more aggressively. Bases must be protected with walls, barriers, and defensive towers, and attacks grow stronger over time. The game features large-scale combat against massive waves of enemies, creating a constant balance between expansion and protection.

Exploration is another core element. The planet is described as rich in minerals and unusual substances, with diverse biomes, unknown flora and fauna, and harsh weather conditions. Players can build outposts in resource-heavy regions and transport materials through rift-based technology, enabling remote resource collection across the world. The setting emphasizes a procedurally generated environment with varied terrain and ecological threats.

The campaign offers a long-form progression across multiple biomes, with persistent bases and a flexible approach to advancement. Players can choose their own priorities and decide which technologies to pursue, while researching alien materials and lifeforms and dealing with hostile creatures that resist human interference. In addition to the campaign, there is a survival mode focused on enduring for a set amount of time against increasingly difficult enemy waves. Each survival mission is randomized to encourage replayability.

For players seeking more control, a sandbox mode allows nearly full customization of the experience, including resources, enemy spawns, and weather conditions. The overall gameplay can also be tuned through many settings, such as attack frequency, enemy strength and numbers, resource abundance, weather events, and damage values. Multiple difficulty presets are available, making the experience accessible both to players looking for a demanding strategy challenge and those preferring a more relaxed base-building style.

The game also emphasizes community involvement in development, encouraging players to share feedback and help shape the experience through official channels.
