The Riftbreaker is a base-building, survival, and action strategy game set on the distant planet Galatea 37. Players take on the role of Captain Ashley S. Nowak, an elite scientist/commando equipped with a powerful Mecha-Suit called Mr. Riggs. She enters a one-way portal with the goal of establishing a base that can eventually support a return to Earth and enable further colonization.

A central part of the game is base building. Players must construct a two-way rift back to Earth, which requires a large amount of energy and a complex infrastructure. This includes mines, refineries, power plants, and research facilities. Resource extraction and industrial expansion are key to progressing the mission.

Defense is also a major focus. As the player builds and alters the planet’s environment, hostile creatures increasingly perceive the presence as a threat. Players must protect their base with walls, barriers, and defense towers while facing waves of enemies that grow stronger over time.

Exploration plays an important role as well. Galatea 37 features varied biomes, harsh weather conditions, and unknown flora and fauna. Rare minerals and substances are scattered across the planet, encouraging players to establish outposts in resource-rich areas and transport materials using rift technology. The game presents a procedurally generated world with a high degree of freedom, allowing players to choose the order of their priorities and the technologies they develop.

The campaign mode takes players across different biomes of Galatea 37, where they can establish multiple persistent bases and research alien substances and lifeforms while fighting hostile creatures. For those looking for a different experience, Survival mode tasks players with lasting through a set amount of time against increasingly difficult waves of enemies, with randomized missions for replayability.

The game also includes a Sandbox mode that gives full control over resources, enemy spawns, and weather conditions. In addition, gameplay can be customized through numerous settings, including enemy attack frequency, strength, numbers, resource abundance, weather events, and enemy damage. Multiple difficulty presets are available to support both hardcore strategy players and those seeking a more relaxed base-building experience.

The Riftbreaker emphasizes flexibility, replayability, and player choice, combining construction, combat, exploration, and customization on an alien world.
