Set in a quiet rural Japanese town, this role-playing adventure follows a group of teenagers through a coming-of-age story that begins with a chain of serial murders. The narrative centers on friendship, belonging, and the struggle to confront hidden aspects of the self, while the player gradually forms meaningful bonds with companions over the course of the journey.

The game combines story-driven exploration with the series’ familiar style of gameplay, presenting an experience built around character interactions, investigation, and thematic progression. It is presented as a highly acclaimed title with broad recognition and multiple awards, and it is noted as being especially well suited to controller play on PC.

On PC, the experience includes variable framerates and Full HD support. It also offers Steam Achievements and Trading Cards, along with a language option that lets players choose between Japanese and English voiceover.
