Nobody Saves the World is an action RPG from the creators of Guacamelee! in which the player takes on the role of Nobody as the ancient Calamity returns. The game centers on transformation, allowing players to unlock and switch between more than 15 distinct Forms, including options such as Slug, Ghost, Dragon, Rat, Rogue, Robot, and Egg. Each Form has its own unique gameplay mechanics, and progression comes through completing quests that expand the player’s available transformations and abilities.

A major part of the experience is building customized playstyles by mixing and matching over 80 abilities across different Forms. This system encourages experimentation, letting players combine skills in unusual ways to tackle increasingly challenging quests. The world also features unconventional quest design, with tasks tied both to the strange inhabitants of the world and to the specific quests of each Form, which can be completed in creative ways.

The game takes place in a large overworld that can be explored solo or with a friend online, while shape-shifting dungeons provide additional combat and progression challenges. These dungeons are procedurally generated and grow more difficult and complex as the player becomes stronger. In addition to the main adventure, the game includes local and online co-op, an endless endgame dungeon with changing content, and New Game+ with leveled-up enemies and remixed difficulty modifiers. The soundtrack features original music by composer Jim Guthrie.
