This action RPG centers on a world threatened by an ancient disaster returning after a long slumber, and places the player in the role of an unlikely hero called “Nobody.” Rather than relying on a single character class, the game is built around transformation, allowing players to switch between more than 15 distinct forms, each with its own mechanics, strengths, and playstyle. These forms range from small, agile creatures to powerful combat-oriented identities, encouraging experimentation and adaptation as the adventure unfolds.

Progression is driven by quests, many of which are tied to specific forms and can be completed in creative ways. Completing these tasks unlocks new forms and abilities, gradually expanding the player’s options. In total, more than 80 abilities can be mixed and matched, enabling highly customized builds and unexpected combinations. This flexibility supports a wide range of approaches, from direct combat to more unusual tactics, including forms that can leave trails of slime or launch fire-based attacks.

The game features a large overworld that can be explored solo or in online co-op with a friend. Alongside this are dungeons that change in layout and difficulty as the player grows stronger, with procedural generation helping keep encounters varied. The dungeon challenges continue beyond the main adventure as well, including an endless endgame mode designed to test builds and reward continued play with additional unlocks and a celebratory statue feature.

There is also a New Game+ mode, which reintroduces the adventure with tougher enemies, redesigned dungeon difficulty, and modifiers that alter the experience. The soundtrack is original and composed by Jim Guthrie, adding a distinct musical identity to the game’s unusual and flexible design.
