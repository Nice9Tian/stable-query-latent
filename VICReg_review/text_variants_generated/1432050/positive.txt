**Nobody Saves the World** is a clever and energetic action RPG that turns transformation into its greatest strength. When the ancient Calamity returns, the unlikely hero is “Nobody” — and that means you. From the creators of *Guacamelee!*, the game invites players to master a wide range of Forms, including Slug, Ghost, Dragon, Rat, Rogue, Robot, and many more, each with its own distinct mechanics and style of play.

Progress comes through completing quests, unlocking more than 15 different Forms, and experimenting with over 80 abilities that can be mixed and matched in surprising ways. This flexible system encourages creative builds and makes it possible to approach challenges in many different ways. The game also features unusual quests tied both to the world’s strange inhabitants and to each Form, rewarding inventive solutions and experimentation.

Exploration takes place across a large overworld that can be experienced solo or with a friend online, while shape-shifting dungeons provide additional challenge. These dungeons are procedurally generated and grow tougher and more complex as you become stronger, keeping the experience fresh throughout the adventure. The game also offers local and online co-op, letting friends join in for the full journey or just part of it.

Beyond the main quest, *Nobody Saves the World* includes an ever-changing endgame dungeon, New Game+ with leveled-up enemies and remixed difficulty modifiers, and a soundtrack of original music by acclaimed composer Jim Guthrie. With its inventive transformation system, creative quest design, and playful sense of humor, it stands out as a highly imaginative take on the action RPG genre.
