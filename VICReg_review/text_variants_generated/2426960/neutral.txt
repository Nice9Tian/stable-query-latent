Summoners War is a tactical RPG that centers on collecting and training monsters, building strategies, and battling in a variety of modes. The game features turn-based combat in the Sky Arena, where each monster has unique skills and can be customized with one of 23 Rune sets to gain additional abilities. Players can develop their own battle approach by combining monsters, runes, and tactics.

A major part of the game is its large collection system, which includes five attributes—Fire, Water, Wind, Light, and Dark—and 1500 different monsters. Players can expand their collection, train monsters, and use their own decks to win battles. The game also offers village decoration, dungeon exploration, PvP battles, and other activities designed to provide ongoing gameplay variety.

Summoners War includes cooperative real-time Raid battles for three players, where teams work together to defeat bosses using different strategies. It also features the Homunculus, a special monster tied to forbidden summoning magic, whose skill can be evolved according to the player’s choice. In World Arena, players can take part in real-time battles against summoners worldwide through a Pick & Ban system.

Guild content adds another layer of competition and cooperation, including Guild PvP battles in the Isle of Conquest and exploration of Tartarus’ Labyrinth and dungeons with guild members. The game also includes the Dimension Hole, where players face ancient guardians and encounter monsters with new awakening power, as well as Artifacts, which drop at max power-up levels and can enhance monsters with sub properties.
