This tactical fantasy RPG centers on strategic battles built around a large roster of collectible creatures and customizable combat options. Players assemble teams, equip them with one of many rune-like enhancement sets, and develop their own approaches to win battles in an arena-style setting.

The game offers a broad range of activities beyond standard combat. Players can decorate a village, battle other summoners, explore dungeons, participate in player-versus-player modes, expand their collection, and train creatures. The experience is designed around long-term progression and repeated play.

Collection is a major part of the game’s structure. The world includes five elemental attributes, and the roster contains a very large number of different creatures. Building effective decks and gathering more units are central goals, giving players many possible team combinations.

Several multiplayer features add cooperative and competitive variety. One mode supports real-time team battles with three players working together against a boss, while another pits players against summoners worldwide in a real-time arena featuring pick-and-ban drafting. Guild-based content also plays a major role, with competitive guild battles and shared exploration of labyrinths and dungeons.

The game further introduces special progression systems, including a unique creature that can evolve chosen skills, as well as dimension-based challenges against ancient guardians. Artifact-style equipment can also be obtained from powerful bosses and used to strengthen creatures through additional sub-properties. Overall, the game emphasizes tactical team building, collection, cooperative raids, guild competition, and ongoing progression across a wide range of modes.
