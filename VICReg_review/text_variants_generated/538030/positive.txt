**Xenonauts 2** is a gripping strategy game that places you at the head of a multinational military organization fighting to protect Earth from an escalating extraterrestrial invasion. Set in 2009, with the Cold War still raging and the Berlin Wall still standing, the game unfolds in a tense alternate history where UFO sightings, unexplained disappearances, and international panic hint at a far greater danger lurking in the shadows. As the commander of a secret global force, you must operate beyond political borders, gather intelligence, and respond wherever the alien threat appears.

At the heart of the experience is a rich strategic layer centered on building and managing a covert worldwide network of bases. Each base can be customized and specialized for detection, rapid response, research, manufacturing, and defense. Using a grid-based construction system, you must carefully lay out facilities while considering fuel ranges, squad deployment, radar coverage, and operational reach. From Alaska to Italy, from Kamchatka to Algeria and beyond, your global infrastructure becomes the backbone of humanity’s resistance.

Combat in Xenonauts 2 is both intense and highly tactical. On the ground, you will send operatives into dangerous missions against a wide variety of alien enemies, each with unique strengths, weaknesses, and fighting styles. Battles demand smart positioning, careful ammunition management, awareness of cover and line of sight, and intelligent use of terrain. Missions are not limited to fighting aliens alone: human agents known as Cleaners also serve the alien agenda, adding new troop types, objectives, and locations to the conflict. You can strike directly, using explosives and heavy firepower to eliminate threats quickly, or take a more deliberate approach by securing alien bodies and technology for autopsies and reverse-engineering back at HQ.

The game also features real-time tactical air battles, where fighter wings are scrambled to intercept UFOs and engage them in combat. You command pilots, choose their targets, manage fuel, and coordinate attacks without being locked into one-on-one duels. If necessary, multiple aircraft can gang up on a powerful enemy craft, making airpower a vital part of your global defense strategy. These aerial engagements are tightly connected to the broader strategic campaign, reinforcing the importance of a well-planned base network.

Research and development play a major role in your fight against the invasion. With over one hundred projects to unlock, the research tree offers access to advanced aircraft, vehicles, weapons, armor, battlefield equipment, autonomous combat platforms, sentry guns, and alien-derived technology. Resources are always scarce, so every decision carries weight: whether to build more dropships, improve your fighters, or accelerate the production of automated defenses, you must constantly balance immediate needs against long-term survival.

Your soldiers grow through both training and combat. Bases can include training centers that improve troops during downtime, while live missions provide experience based on the actions they take in battle. Survivors become stronger, earn medals, and develop into hardened veterans, but the game never shies away from the cost of war. Losses are expected, and every operative must face the reality of a brutal conflict where survival is never guaranteed.

Xenonauts 2 stands out for its deep simulation, interconnected systems, and global scale. Aliens actively move across the map, seek out your forces, and exploit cover and high ground, making reconnaissance and ambush tactics essential. With multiple bases, varied mission types, a wide range of research options, and punishing but rewarding tactical combat, the game offers a demanding and highly satisfying strategy experience. As a standalone title, it welcomes both newcomers and veterans alike into a dark, compelling defense of humanity.
