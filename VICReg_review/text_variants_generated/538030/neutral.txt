Xenonauts 2 is a strategy game in which the player commands a multinational military organization fighting an extraterrestrial threat. Set in 2009, during a continuing Cold War in a world where the Berlin Wall still stands, the game presents a secret struggle carried out beyond political borders as UFO sightings, disappearances, and public panic increase. The player must operate from the shadows to track the alien presence, respond to incidents around the globe, and gradually uncover the truth behind the invasion.

The game centers on managing a global network of bases and leading both air and ground operations. Bases can be built, designed, and specialized for functions such as detection, rapid response, research, manufacturing, and defense. Their placement must take fuel ranges into account, and construction uses a grid-based system. Players can maintain multiple bases in different regions, each with squadrons of fighter jets and transports, radar arrays, research teams, operatives, and more.

Research and development play a major role in closing the technological gap with the invaders. Xenonauts 2 features a large research tree with over one hundred projects, allowing players to unlock aircraft, vehicles, weapons, armor, and other battlefield equipment. Alien technology can be incorporated into gear, and engineering teams can also develop autonomous battlefield combat platforms and sentry guns. Resources, time, and money are limited, so decisions about what to produce or research involve tradeoffs.

Combat takes place both in the air and on the ground. Air engagements are real-time tactical battles in which players command fighter wings, direct maneuvers and target selection, and manage fuel and hangar proximity. There are no forced one-on-one duels; instead, players can gang up on stronger UFOs with multiple planes. On the ground, operatives are deployed into tactical missions that require careful management of positioning, ammunition, lines of sight, and cover. Missions can include recovering research samples, securing alien bodies for autopsies, and gathering intelligence from infiltrated enemy bases.

The alien threat is varied and dangerous. Players face many enemy species, each with distinct strengths, weaknesses, and fighting styles. Human agents known as Cleaners also cooperate with the aliens, adding more troop types, locations, and mission objectives. Since the enemy can actively maneuver across the map, seek out the player, and use cover and high ground, reconnaissance and ambushes are important to success. Tactical choices also matter in how aggressively to fight: players may use explosives and missiles to eliminate threats quickly, or take a more careful approach to preserve bodies and technology for later analysis.

Troops improve through both training centers and combat experience. Soldiers gain abilities based on their actions in battle and can earn medals for their service. The game emphasizes that combat is deadly and losses are inevitable; wounded and fallen soldiers are a normal part of the war. Veteran troops become especially valuable, but even recruits are expected to fight. Xenonauts 2 is presented as a standalone title, and it does not require experience with the original Xenonauts to understand the story or gameplay.
