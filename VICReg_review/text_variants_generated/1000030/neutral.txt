Cook, Serve, Delicious! 3?! is a sequel in the Cook, Serve, Delicious! series that sends players on a road trip across the United States to compete in the Iron Cook Foodtruck Championships. Set in a war-torn America of 2042, the game follows a story-driven campaign in which players cook hundreds of foods across hundreds of levels, including many dishes new to the series.

The game features a redesigned gameplay structure intended to deliver fast-paced action, alongside an optional Chill Mode that can be turned on or off at any time. Players can experience the campaign alone or in local co-op with a friend, with the ability to switch between solo and co-op on the fly. The food truck can also be upgraded with dozens of gameplay-affecting modules, and players can expand their food catalog to more than 200 foods. In addition, the truck can be decorated with dozens of trinkets collected across the US.

Accessibility is a notable part of the game, with options for motion, flashing or strobe effects, audio, colorblind support, and more. The campaign offers over 380 levels and more than 100 hours of gameplay. The game also includes an original soundtrack by award-winning composer Jonathan Geer.
