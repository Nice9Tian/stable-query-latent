This is a fast-paced culinary action game set in a heavily altered, war-torn version of America in the year 2042. In this sequel to a successful food-service series, players travel across the United States to compete in a major truck-based cooking championship, accompanied by a pair of robot helpers with support roles throughout the journey.

The game features a new story-driven campaign built around hundreds of levels and a redesigned structure intended to emphasize speed and pressure in the kitchen. Players prepare hundreds of different dishes, including many newly added items, while managing orders across a large and varied progression of stages. A separate relaxed option can also be enabled at any time, allowing for a more forgiving experience.

The campaign can be played solo or in local cooperative play with a friend, and the co-op setup can be adjusted on the fly. The game also includes a truck upgrade system with many modules that affect gameplay, as well as a wide collection of decorative items used to personalize the vehicle. Across the journey, players can unlock and work with more than two hundred food items.

Accessibility is a major part of the design, with options for motion, flashing and strobe effects, audio settings, and colorblind support, among other features. The overall experience offers more than a hundred hours of gameplay across over 380 levels, and it is accompanied by an original soundtrack from an award-winning composer.
