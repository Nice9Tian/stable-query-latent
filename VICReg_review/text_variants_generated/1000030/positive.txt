*Cook, Serve, Delicious! 3?!* is a thrilling and ambitious sequel that takes the beloved series on the road for a high-energy culinary adventure across the United States. Set in the radically changed, war-torn America of 2042, the game follows a story-driven campaign in which you compete in the Iron Cook Foodtruck Championships alongside your trusty robot crew, Whisk and Cleaver.

This entry delivers a completely redesigned gameplay structure built for fast-paced action, with hundreds of levels and hundreds of foods to prepare, including many brand-new dishes for the series. Players can enjoy the campaign solo or team up with a friend in local co-op, with the flexibility to switch between play modes on the fly. For a more relaxed experience, the all-new Chill Mode can be toggled at any time, making the game accessible to different play styles.

Beyond the core cooking action, the game offers extensive progression and customization. You can upgrade your food truck with dozens of gameplay-affecting modules, expand your menu with over two hundred foods, and decorate your truck with a wide variety of trinkets inspired by locations across the US. It also stands out for its strong accessibility support, including motion settings, flashing and strobe options, audio tools, colorblind features, and more.

With over 380 levels and more than a hundred hours of gameplay, *Cook, Serve, Delicious! 3?!* offers a generous and polished experience enhanced by an original soundtrack from award-winning composer Jonathan Geer. It is a bold, feature-rich sequel that brings new energy, depth, and flexibility to the series.
