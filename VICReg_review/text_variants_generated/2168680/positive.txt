**Nuclear Option** is an impressive combat flight game set in a dynamic, realistic near-future battlefield. It delivers a thrilling blend of aerial dogfights, ground attacks, stealth approaches, and large-scale explosive warfare, all presented with a strong focus on simulation and tactical choice.

At the heart of the experience is a diverse lineup of seven playable aircraft, each with scenarios that highlight its unique role. Players can take control of the CI-22 *Cricket* counter-insurgency aircraft, the T/A-30 *Compass* light attack/trainer, the SAH-46 *Chicane* stealth attack helicopter, the FS-12 *Revoker* air superiority fighter, the KR-67 *Ifrit* multirole fighter, the EW-25 *Medusa* electronic warfare aircraft, and the SFB-81 *Darkreach* strike bomber. Every aircraft is built with detailed flight physics, simulated across dozens of aerodynamic surfaces, and features a highly convincing damage model with 30 to 50 detachable parts whose condition directly affects how the aircraft flies.

The game excels in its cockpit presentation and flight systems. Each aircraft includes an immersive cockpit with a situational awareness HUD that identifies nearby targets and threats, along with a detailed flight HUD showing key information such as angle of attack, velocity vector, climb rate, G force, and radar altitude. Flight is designed to feel authentic, with stalls, spins, and g-force effects fully implemented. Control options are flexible as well, since aircraft can be flown with a variety of controllers or even with a mouse-controlled virtual joystick, with no special hardware required.

Combat in Nuclear Option is broad, powerful, and highly varied. Players can intercept bombers, duel fighters, fly beneath radar coverage, or unleash devastating nuclear weapons on enemy formations. The arsenal includes guns, air-to-air and air-to-ground missiles, bombs, cruise missiles, ballistic missiles, countermeasures like IR flares and radar jammers, and accurately simulated blast effects for explosions. Nuclear weapons are also modeled with realistic shockwave propagation, giving the game’s largest attacks real tactical weight.

The battlefield itself is designed to feel alive and persistent. AI-controlled ground vehicles and aircraft participate in large, dynamic battles, while wreckage and smoke remain on the battlefield after combat, reinforcing the sense of an ongoing conflict. Players can fight across a 100km-wide map with varied biomes and terrain types, including night missions supported by night-vision cockpit functionality.

Nuclear Option also offers strong creative and multiplayer features. Its included mission editor allows players to build custom missions and then fly them solo or with friends. Multiplayer supports both cooperative and competitive play through a player-hosted system with a public lobby browser. Altogether, it creates a flexible and rewarding combat flight experience that combines realism, variety, and large-scale destructive power in a compelling near-future setting.
