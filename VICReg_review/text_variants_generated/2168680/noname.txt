This combat flight game is set on a dynamic, near-future battlefield where players can influence large-scale engagements through air combat, precision strikes, electronic warfare, and strategic weapons. It presents a mix of offensive and defensive roles, allowing aircraft to intercept bombers, engage enemy fighters, fly low to avoid detection, or deploy devastating nuclear ordnance against hostile formations. The overall structure emphasizes battlefield variety, with persistent combat zones, lingering wreckage, and smoke creating the sense of an active war environment.

The game features seven flyable aircraft, each designed around a different role and supported by scenario content that highlights its strengths. These include a counter-insurgency aircraft, a light attack and trainer aircraft, a stealth attack helicopter, an air superiority fighter, a multirole fighter, an electronic warfare aircraft, and a strike bomber. Each aircraft is represented with a detailed cockpit interior, a situational awareness display, and a targeting view system that helps identify nearby threats and objectives.

Flight handling is built around realistic physics, including stalls, spins, and g-force effects. Aircraft are simulated through dozens of aerodynamic surfaces and up to 50 individually modeled physics parts, with damage affecting performance in a physically consistent way as components are lost or degraded. The control system is designed to be accessible, supporting multiple controllers as well as a mouse-based virtual joystick, so no special hardware is required.

Combat systems are broad and varied, with guns, air-to-air missiles, air-to-ground missiles, bombs, cruise missiles, ballistic missiles, and countermeasures such as infrared flares and radar jammers. Explosions have detailed blast behavior, and nuclear weapons are modeled with shockwave propagation. The game also includes night missions with cockpit night-vision functionality, adding another operational layer to mission planning and execution.

Players can create custom missions using the included editor and then fly them alone or in multiplayer. The multiplayer setup is player-hosted and includes a public lobby browser, supporting both cooperative and competitive play. Large battles are reinforced by AI-controlled ground vehicles and aircraft, and the map spans 100 kilometers with multiple biomes and terrain types, giving missions room to unfold across a wide operational area.
