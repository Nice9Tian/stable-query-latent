Nuclear Option is a combat flight game set on a dynamic, near-future battlefield. It focuses on aerial combat and mission-based play, letting players intercept bombers, duel fighters, fly low to evade detection, or use nuclear weapons against enemy formations. The game presents each aircraft through an immersive cockpit view with a situational awareness HUD that identifies nearby targets and threats.

The game features realistic flight dynamics, including stalls, spins, and g-force effects. Each aircraft is built from up to 50 individually simulated physics parts, and its detailed damage model means that damage to specific components affects how it flies. Players can control aircraft with various controllers or with a mouse-controlled virtual joystick, without requiring special hardware.

Nuclear Option includes seven playable aircraft, each with scenarios that highlight its role and capabilities: the CI-22 Cricket counter-insurgency aircraft, T/A-30 Compass light attack/trainer aircraft, SAH-46 Chicane stealth attack helicopter, FS-12 Revoker air superiority fighter, KR-67 Ifrit multirole fighter, EW-25 Medusa electronic warfare aircraft, and SFB-81 Darkreach strike bomber. The game also supports a range of weapons, including guns, air-to-air and air-to-ground missiles, bombs, cruise and ballistic missiles, as well as countermeasures such as IR flares and a radar jammer.

Combat takes place across large, persistent battlefields with AI-controlled ground vehicles and aircraft participating in dynamic battles. Explosions have simulated blast effects, and nuclear weapons feature shockwave propagation. The map spans 100 kilometers and includes different biomes and terrain types. Cockpits also provide a flight HUD with detailed information such as angle of attack, velocity vector, climb rate, G force, and radar altitude, along with a target view screen.

The game includes a mission editor for creating custom missions, which can then be played solo or with friends in multiplayer. Player-hosted multiplayer uses a public lobby browser and supports both cooperative and competitive play. Night missions are also supported, with night-vision cockpit functionality available.
