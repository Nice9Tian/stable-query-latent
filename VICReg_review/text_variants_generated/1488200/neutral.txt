*Symphony of War: The Nephilim Saga* is a 2D turn-based strategy RPG set in the world of Tahnra at the turn of the millennium, where the Empire of Veridia is recovering from the brutal Veridian Succession war. Under Empress Florina, peace seems to have returned, but that stability is shattered when a rogue general abducts her from the capital and raises his own rebel armies. As a young graduate of the Imperial War Academy, the player leads an expedition against this rising threat, becoming entangled in a larger conflict that affects the foundations of the world.

The game centers on squad-based warfare with a strong emphasis on grand-scale planning and logistics. Battles are designed around maneuvering squads of up to nine soldiers across the battlefield, making use of high ground, chokepoints, ambushes, weather conditions, and terrain advantages. Combat itself is resolved automatically, allowing the player to focus on strategy such as breaking enemy morale, protecting their own, capturing resources, liberating towns, and eliminating enemy squad leaders to disrupt their chain of command. The game presents these engagements as tactical encounters where planning and preparation matter as much as battlefield positioning.

Army management is a major part of the experience. Players can assemble squads from more than 50 unit classes, including heavy infantry, archers, shock cavalry, medics, arquebusiers, dragons, and battle mages. Units can be equipped with gear and artifacts earned through loot and victory, while conscripts can be trained into elite warriors or replaced with mercenaries for immediate strength. A faction tech tree allows further specialization or broader generalization across classes, adding more options for building armies and squads.

Beyond warfare, the game includes a story-driven campaign estimated at 30 to 40 hours, with significant replay value. It features a large cast drawn from multiple factions, including friends, heroes, allies, and rivals, and uses a dynamic relationship system that unlocks conversations, bonds, and bonuses between characters. The narrative follows the graduates of the Imperial War Academy as they attempt to contain a rebellion, only to uncover a much deeper conspiracy filled with malice, treachery, and ambition as peace in Tahnra begins to collapse.
