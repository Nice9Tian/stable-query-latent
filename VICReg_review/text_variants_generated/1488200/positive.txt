**Symphony of War: The Nephilim Saga** is a story-rich, 2D turn-based strategy RPG set in the world of Tahnra, where a fragile peace begins to collapse at the turn of the millennium. In the aftermath of the brutal Veridian Succession, the Empire of Veridia struggles to recover under Empress Florina—until a rogue general abducts her from the capital, gathers his own armies, and ignites a rebellion. As a young graduate of the Imperial War Academy, you are sent to lead an expedition against this growing threat, only to become entangled in a far-reaching conflict that will shake the foundations of the world.

The game emphasizes grand-scale strategy, squad-based command, and thoughtful planning. Instead of controlling every individual attack, you guide squads of up to nine soldiers across the battlefield while the battles themselves unfold automatically in thrilling fashion. Success depends on smart positioning and tactical awareness: seize high ground, hold chokepoints, ambush from forests, and keep an eye on the weather, since sudden blizzards or heat waves can change the outcome of a fight. You will also need to manage morale, break the enemy’s will to fight, and target squad leaders to disrupt the chain of command. In this system, tactics decide engagements, while logistics decide wars.

A major strength of the game is the immense depth of army building. You can assemble squads from more than 50 unit classes, including heavy infantry, archers, shock cavalry, medics, arquebusiers, dragons, battle mages, and more. Units can be equipped with gear and artifacts found through looting and victory, and your conscripts can be nurtured into elite warriors over time. If you need immediate strength, mercenaries can be hired to reinforce your forces quickly. Progression is further expanded through a faction tech tree, allowing you to specialize or generalize your troops across a wide range of classes to suit your preferred approach.

Beyond the battlefield, the game offers meaningful campaign management. You liberate towns, capture resources, and build up your reputation to sustain your armies through a long march toward peace. After each battle, the wounded are collected, troops are rallied, and preparations begin for the next engagement, reinforcing the sense of an ongoing military campaign. The 30–40 hour story campaign promises huge replay value, supported by the freedom to create limitless squad and character builds.

The narrative also places strong emphasis on relationships and character interaction. As the war unfolds, you will meet friends, heroes, allies, and rivals from different factions across the world. A dynamic relationship system unlocks iconic conversations, unbreakable bonds, and powerful bonuses between characters, adding another layer of strategy and emotional weight to the journey. Filled with malice, treachery, ambition, and the unraveling of a delicate peace, **Symphony of War: The Nephilim Saga** delivers an epic tale backed by deep tactics, rich progression, and commanding strategic scope.
