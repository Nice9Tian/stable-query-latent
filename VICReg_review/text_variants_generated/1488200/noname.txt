Set in the world of **Tahris**, this story-driven turn-based strategy RPG follows a young academy graduate sent to contain a rebellion after a brutal succession war has left an empire scarred and unstable. What begins as a military expedition quickly expands into a larger conflict that threatens the foundations of the entire world, pulling the player into a web of war, ambition, betrayal, and shifting alliances.

The game combines squad-based army management with large-scale tactical operations. Rather than controlling individual duels in real time, the focus is on planning, positioning, and outmaneuvering opponents across the battlefield. Each engagement features automatic combat resolution, creating battles that play out while emphasizing preparation and command decisions. Squads can include up to nine soldiers, and battlefield factors such as high ground, chokepoints, ambushes from wooded areas, weather conditions, and terrain advantages all play an important role. Players must also manage morale, seize towns and resources, and target enemy leaders to disrupt command structures.

A major part of the experience is building and customizing an army from more than fifty unit classes. These include roles such as heavy infantry, archers, shock cavalry, healers, gun units, dragons, and battle mages. Units can be equipped with gear and relics gained through looting and victory, while recruits can be developed into elite veterans or supplemented with hired mercenaries. A faction-based technology tree allows further specialization or broader development, supporting a wide range of army compositions and tactical approaches.

Outside of battle, the campaign presents a lengthy narrative with substantial replay value and a cast drawn from multiple factions across the world. A dynamic relationship system adds another layer of progression, unlocking character interactions, stronger bonds, and gameplay bonuses. Overall, the game blends strategic warfare, army logistics, and character-driven storytelling into a campaign centered on the slow, difficult march toward peace.
