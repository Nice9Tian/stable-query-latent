SimAirport is a detailed airport management simulation in which players control nearly every aspect of building and operating an international hub. The game offers both Career Mode, where the goal is to create an efficient and profitable airport, and Sandbox Mode, where players can build freely with unlimited funds and without credit rating concerns.

The game emphasizes large-scale planning and operational detail. Players construct terminals, hire staff, sign airline contracts, adjust daily flight schedules, configure standby gate availability, and design supporting infrastructure such as baggage handling systems, roads, taxiways, fuel systems, runways, gates, hangars, and service vehicle routes. The simulation extends to small details as well, including trash cans, with each element affecting airport performance.

SimAirport is designed to start simply: the starter airport allows aircraft to begin landing and revenue to flow relatively quickly. As the airport grows, however, the challenge increases. Managing a small airport may be straightforward, but larger facilities with thousands of passengers require careful planning to avoid bottlenecks. The game highlights strategies such as moving walkways, multi-story structures, additional terminals, one-way taxiways, staggered schedules, and thoughtful standby gate allocation as tools for handling complex operations.

Passenger flow and satisfaction are central to success. Restrooms, retail areas, seating, and other services must be placed thoughtfully, since passenger behavior can be influenced by distance and convenience. Keeping flights on time and ensuring passengers reach their gates helps maintain airline satisfaction as well, which can make it easier to raise prices and improve profits.

The game includes 16+ aircraft types ranging from turboprops to turbine aircraft, along with 15 airlines featuring realistic high-quality textures. It also offers 15+ research items that unlock improved transit, reporting, and expansion capabilities.

SimAirport features a comprehensive fuel system simulation. Fuel can be used as a profit source, a loss leader to improve airline relations, or an opportunity for arbitrage through the fuel marketplace if enough capacity is available. Its baggage handling system is deeply simulated, with every bag tracked from conveyor to carousel. Poor baggage performance can frustrate passengers and damage airline relations.

Additional systems include passenger information displays, kiosks, and public address speaker systems, supported by electronics zones for amplifiers. A robust queue system allows players to set up advanced queue configurations, including one-to-many desk setups and restrictions for different passenger types such as First Class, Coach, and Flight Crew.

The game also notes an active development team that has released hundreds of public builds since Early Access and frequently incorporates player feedback.
