This management simulation places you in full control of a large transportation hub, from high-level strategic choices to the smallest operational details. You can build and expand a facility in two main modes: a structured career-focused mode where efficiency and profitability matter, and a sandbox mode with unlimited funds for unconstrained building and experimentation.

The experience centers on designing and running a complex terminal ecosystem. You can construct terminals, hire personnel, negotiate with carriers, adjust daily flight plans, set standby gate availability, and lay out essential infrastructure such as baggage systems, roads, taxiways, fuel networks, runways, gates, hangars, and service vehicles. Even minor details can affect performance, including seemingly simple items like seating, restrooms, retail areas, and trash cans.

The simulation emphasizes operational depth. Aircraft, passenger flow, baggage movement, fuel handling, and scheduling all interact in ways that can create bottlenecks or smooth throughput. Early progress is designed to be approachable, with a starter facility that quickly gets traffic and revenue moving. As the hub grows, however, larger passenger volumes demand smarter planning, such as multi-level structures, additional terminals, one-way taxiways, staggered schedules, moving walkways, and careful gate allocation.

Passenger behavior is also an important factor. Keeping travelers informed through information displays, kiosks, and announcement systems can reduce wandering and improve the odds that they reach their flights on time. Queue management is highly flexible as well, allowing one queue to serve multiple stations and supporting restrictions for different passenger categories to create faster service paths when needed.

The game includes a range of aircraft and multiple airlines, along with research options that unlock improved transit, reporting, and expansion tools. A detailed fuel system adds another layer of strategy, letting you treat fuel as both a service and a potential financial lever through pricing decisions and marketplace opportunities. The baggage system is similarly intricate, with each bag tracked from conveyor to carousel, making careful planning essential to avoid losses and operational trouble.

Overall, this is a deeply simulated building and management game focused on balancing expansion, efficiency, passenger satisfaction, and airline relationships. Success depends on designing systems that scale, anticipating congestion, and making smart trade-offs as the facility becomes larger and more complex.
