**DON'T SCREAM** is a tense and inventive Unreal Engine 5 horror experience that draws inspiration from ’90s camcorder found footage to create a truly unsettling atmosphere. Its core premise is immediately memorable: survive a night in the Forest without screaming or dying. That sounds straightforward at first, but the game quickly proves otherwise with its unpredictable scares and relentless pressure.

One of the most striking ideas in DON’T SCREAM is its microphone-based rule: if you scream, gasp, or even whimper into your microphone, the game restarts. This simple mechanic makes every moment feel personal and intense, turning your own reactions into part of the challenge. Since time only progresses as you move, every step matters, and caution becomes just as important as courage.

The game emphasizes replayability through dozens of dynamic scares, ensuring that no two playthroughs feel the same. As you explore the forest, you’ll encounter different locations and discover relics that hint at forgotten stories from the past. At the same time, you must stay alert for batteries to keep your recording going, because running out means game over.

The forest itself is a major part of the experience: vast, haunted, and filled with bizarre terrors. Vile hunters stalk the woods, adding another layer of danger as you try to survive the night. With its combination of exploration, survival, and unpredictable horror, DON’T SCREAM delivers a distinctive scare-filled challenge that is especially well suited for sharing with friends and family.
