Returnal™ is an award-winning third-person roguelike shooter that brings bullet hell action to PC. The game follows Selene after she crash-lands on the shape-shifting planet Atropos, where she must search through the barren remains of an ancient civilization in an effort to escape. Isolated and alone, she fights to survive in a hostile world that forces her to restart every time she dies, turning each run into a new attempt to break the cycle.

The game is built around fast, relentless combat and extreme replayability. As the planet changes with each cycle, so do the items available to the player, creating new combinations and encouraging different strategies on every loop. Returnal features explosive, high-stakes encounters, procedural world generation, and a dark, decaying setting filled with visual contrast and surprises. Players explore, discover, and fight through an unforgiving journey shaped by evolving challenges and mystery.

The full PC version includes Suspend Cycle, Photo Mode, Co-op Mode, and the Tower of Sisyphus from day one. It also supports achievements with an in-game progress tracker, five graphical presets, performance metrics such as an FPS counter, GPU and CPU usage gauges, and a latency tracker. Visual options include 16:10, ultrawide 21:9, and super ultrawide 32:9 aspect ratios, with 4K support, ray-traced reflections and shadows on high-spec PCs, and performance technologies such as NVIDIA DLSS, NVIDIA NIS, and AMD FSR.

Returnal also supports the DualSense controller with haptic feedback and dynamic trigger effects when wired, alongside mouse and keyboard or other controller inputs with customizable button prompts. Expanded accessibility options include an alt-fire crosshair charging notification and a Co-op ping system. Audio support includes Dolby Atmos and selectable 3D audio options.

In Co-op Mode, two PC players can share a journey through Atropos. Each player controls a different version of Selene from different cycles, and the host uses the Chronosis device in each biome to summon a friend or match with another online player. The host’s progress determines the item pool available for the multiplayer session. Tower of Sisyphus, House sequences, and Challenge Mode are not available in Co-op Mode, and cross-play with PlayStation 5 players is not supported.

The Tower of Sisyphus is a nearly endless ascending combat gauntlet unlocked after obtaining the Icarian Grapnel. It focuses on combat, score buildup, progression, exclusive items, Scout logs, and previously unseen fragments of Selene’s story.

A Steam exclusive offer also lets players link their Steam account with PlayStation Network to unlock two in-game outfits while the accounts are linked: the ASTRA Model 9 Prototype Suit and the ASTRA Model 14 Tactical Suit.
