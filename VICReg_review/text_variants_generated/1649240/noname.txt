In this atmospheric third-person roguelike shooter, players are cast as a lone explorer who crash-lands on a hostile, shape-shifting world and must fight to survive while searching for a way to escape. The journey takes place across the ruins of an ancient civilization, where isolation, danger, and mystery define every step.

The game is built around a cycle of repeated attempts: each time the character dies, the journey begins again, but the world and available tools change with every loop. This structure creates a playstyle centered on adaptation, resource management, and learning through repetition. Equipment, upgrades, and item combinations vary from cycle to cycle, encouraging players to adjust tactics and experiment with different approaches to combat.

Combat is fast, relentless, and heavily influenced by bullet hell design. Players face ruthless enemies in explosive encounters across stark, contrasting environments. The game emphasizes high-stakes action, where movement, positioning, and quick reactions are essential for survival. Exploration is equally important, as alien technologies and scattered resources can be collected to improve future runs.

The overall experience is designed for replayability, with a procedurally shifting world that constantly introduces new challenges. Visual presentation is a major part of the appeal, combining striking effects, dark landscapes, and a sense of decaying beauty. On PC, the game includes a range of enhancements such as multiple graphical presets, performance metrics, support for achievements, and options that extend to ultrawide and super ultrawide displays, as well as 4K output.

For players seeking additional immersion, the game supports advanced lighting features on high-end systems, along with performance-boosting technologies such as upscaling solutions from major hardware vendors. It also offers multiple input options, including controller and mouse-and-keyboard play, with customizable prompts and accessibility features. Audio support includes spatial sound options intended to improve situational awareness and deepen the sense of place.

A cooperative mode allows two players to share the experience of the shifting labyrinth together. In this mode, each player controls a different version of the protagonist from a different cycle, and progression is shaped by the host’s current state. The game also includes a separate tower-based challenge mode focused on combat and score progression, featuring a near-endless ascent with exclusive rewards, logs, and story fragments. Some modes and sequences are not available in cooperative play.

Overall, the game combines intense action, procedural variation, and fragmented narrative into a survival-focused experience where defeat is part of the design. Each return offers a new chance to explore deeper, fight harder, and uncover more of the planet’s hidden story.
