Zombie Army 4: Dead War delivers a thrilling, spine-chilling shooter experience from the makers of Sniper Elite 4, bringing Hitler’s hordes back in a dramatic alternate-history battle through 1940s Europe. Set after the resistance has defeated Zombie Hitler and cast him into Hell, the game follows the undead’s return with even greater hunger, plunging players into a harrowing new campaign for 1-4 players.

The story continues the events of Zombie Army Trilogy across huge new levels, taking the Survivor Brigade through Italy and beyond as they uncover a sinister plan. Along the way, players fight through corpse-riddled canals, survive a Zombie Zoo, and explore dark, inexplicable places that no one was meant to visit and live to tell about.

At the heart of the experience is award-winning gunplay built on the genre-defining ballistics of the Sniper Elite series. The game enhances this with upgraded Kill Cams and dismemberment, including the return of the X-Ray Kill Camera, which shows bullets, bombs, and more tearing through bones and organs in gory slow motion. Enemies can lose limbs, and the undead keep coming, creating relentless and intense combat.

Zombie Army 4 also offers deep progression and customization, letting players rank up and shape a slayer to match their style. Unlockable outfits, emotes, and taunts add personality, while perks and abilities support different approaches to battle. Enhanced melee combat gives players more ways to survive when surrounded, with barges, stomps, and powerful takedown moves.

The arsenal is equally impressive. Players can harness fire, lightning, and divine essence to upgrade guns at workbenches, creating highly customized weapons such as assault rifles that shoot lightning or pistols with flaming bayonets. The game throws players against monstrous enemy types including ghoulish grabbers, armored elites, shadow demons, explosive suicide generals, and even zombie tanks, ensuring every encounter feels dangerous and varied.

For those who enjoy endless survival challenges, the expanded Horde Mode grows larger the longer players survive, raising the stakes as they fight to avoid joining the living dead. Additional seasonal content also expands the experience further, including new campaign missions, playable characters, weapon bundles, weapon skin packs, and character outfit bundles.

Season Pass One is now free with the base game, offering three additional levels, four character packs, nine weapon bundles, five weapon skin packs, and four character outfit bundles. With even more seasonal content available, including the 3-part Hell Cult campaign and Death From Above, Zombie Army 4: Dead War stands out as a packed, stylish, and explosive undead slaughterfest.
