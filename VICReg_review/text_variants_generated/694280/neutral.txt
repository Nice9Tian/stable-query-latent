Zombie Army 4: Dead War is a co-op third-person shooter set in an alternate 1940s Europe where Hitler’s undead forces have returned. Developed by the makers of Sniper Elite 4, the game follows the Survivor Brigade as they continue the fight after Zombie Hitler has been defeated and cast into Hell. The new campaign supports 1-4 players and takes players across Italy and beyond, into locations such as corpse-filled canals, a Zombie Zoo, and other dark and unexplained places.

The game emphasizes its ballistic gunplay, building on the Sniper Elite series’ trademark mechanics. It includes the return of the X-Ray Kill Camera, showing bullets, bombs, and other attacks destroying bones and organs in slow motion. Enemy dismemberment is also a major part of the experience, with the undead continuing to advance even after severe damage.

Zombie Army 4: Dead War features deep progression and customization. Players can level up their slayer, unlock new outfits, emotes, and taunts, and tailor their character to different playstyles. Enhanced melee combat adds close-range options such as barging through crowds, stomping on heads, and performing takedowns. The game also allows weapon upgrades at workbenches, adding effects like fire, lightning, and divine essence, including unusual combinations such as an assault rifle that shoots lightning or a pistol with a flaming bayonet.

The enemy roster includes ghoulish grabbers, armored elites, shadow demons, explosive suicide generals, and other threats, including zombie tanks. For players seeking more replayability, the expanded Horde Mode increases the size of the map the longer the team survives.

Season Pass One is now free with the base game and includes three additional campaign levels for 1-4 players, four character packs, nine weapon bundles, five weapon skin packs, and four character outfit bundles. Additional seasons are also available, including the three-part Hell Cult campaign and Death From Above, which add more missions, characters, weapons, and other content.
