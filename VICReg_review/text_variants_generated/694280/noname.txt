This third-person shooter presents an alternate-history campaign set in 1940s Europe, where a resistance force battles overwhelming undead armies after an earlier victory fails to end the threat. The game supports 1–4 players in its campaign missions and expands the experience with a large amount of additional content, including extra levels, playable characters, weapon bundles, cosmetic skins, and character outfits.

At its core, the game focuses on gunplay built around precise ballistics, with a strong emphasis on visceral combat feedback. Its signature camera system highlights shots in slow motion, showing destructive impacts on bones and organs, while dismemberment and other gory effects remain a central part of the presentation. Players can also engage in enhanced melee combat, using close-range takedowns, crowd-clearing moves, and stomps when enemies get too close.

Progression and customization play a major role. As players rank up, they can develop a character build suited to different team roles and play styles, unlocking outfits, emotes, and taunts along the way. The game also allows weapon upgrades at workbenches, adding elemental and supernatural effects such as fire, lightning, and other powerful enhancements. This creates a system where ordinary firearms can be transformed into highly specialized tools for surviving against the undead.

Enemy variety is another major feature, with hordes that include basic attackers, armored elites, shadowy demons, explosive enemies, and even heavily fortified machine-like threats. The campaign takes players through varied and dangerous locations, including canals, a zombie-infested zoo, and other ominous areas tied to the unfolding occult plot.

Beyond the story mode, the game includes an expanded horde-style survival mode in which the map grows larger the longer players survive, increasing the challenge over time. Additional seasonal content further extends the game with new missions, characters, weapons, cosmetics, and themed campaign chapters. Overall, it is a cooperative action shooter built around large-scale undead combat, extensive customization, and escalating survival-focused encounters.
