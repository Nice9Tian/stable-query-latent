This simulation centers on running an alchemical workshop in a medieval-inspired world, blending shop management, crafting, and role-playing. Its visual style draws from old manuscripts and medical texts, giving the experience a distinctive hand-illustrated look. The player handles ingredients and equipment through highly tactile, physics-based interactions, creating a sense of practical experimentation rather than simple menu-driven crafting.

At the core of the experience is a flexible sandbox structure. There are multiple ways to approach each task, and the game encourages players to find their own methods for reaching the desired result. The role-play aspect is similarly open-ended: one can act as a refined artisan, a selfish opportunist, a sinister mystic, or any other persona imagined within the setting.

Crafting focuses on combining a wide range of natural and mineral components. Botanical items, fruits, roots, berries, flowers, mushrooms, and stones all play a part in the process. Players grind materials more finely or coarsely depending on the need, then prepare mixtures in a cauldron by heating, boiling, and stirring them. The choice of base liquid also matters, with options such as water, oil, and other unfamiliar substances. The system begins simply but is designed to reward mastery through experimentation.

Recipe development is built around strategic planning on an alchemical route map, where different effects can be combined. This encourages thoughtful experimentation, while also introducing practical limits based on logic and market demand. Not every mixture will be useful to customers, and some combinations may be questionable or even contradictory in purpose.

Selling goods is another major part of the loop. Each day, townspeople arrive with needs and requests, and the player decides what to provide. These choices have consequences, influencing relationships, reputation, wealth, and broader political standing. Different groups may be attracted or alienated depending on how the workshop is run, and important figures can become allies or adversaries. Over time, the player’s actions may even shape the future of the town.

Ingredients can be obtained in several ways. They may be purchased from traveling merchants, though prices can be high and bargaining skills matter. Alternatively, materials can be grown personally, adding another layer of self-sufficiency to the shop’s operation.

There is also a strong focus on customization. Potions can be given unique visual identities by changing the shape of the bottle, the style of the label, the icon, and the colors. Players can add custom names and descriptions, then arrange their creations on shelves and tables to display them neatly or casually. Overall, the game combines hands-on crafting, open-ended role-play, and business management into a highly personalized alchemical simulation.
