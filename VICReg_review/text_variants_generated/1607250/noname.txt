This online team-based battle royale places players in a fast-changing arena where three-player squads compete to be the last team standing in matches of up to 24 participants. Success depends on communication, tactics, and combining each character’s unique abilities with those of teammates. The roster is organized into five archetypes — Vash, Brill, Taro, Nemi, and Luma — and each fighter has a distinct skill set shaped by a personal power system, making their strengths and roles vary widely.

During matches, players can search for items, interact with civilians, and defeat opponents to improve their abilities and gain an advantage over rival teams. Progression also comes through experience and mission completion, which unlocks additional characters and customization options. Seasonal events and ticket-based rewards provide further opportunities to obtain exclusive cosmetic items, while the game continues to expand over time with new characters, costumes, and other updates.

A recent season introduces a new playable character, a top-ranked hero known for exceptional speed and aerial combat. This newcomer uses a feather-based power that enables both attacks and flight, making them effective in offense and support roles. The current season also includes a partial overhaul of the central island stage, preserving its many buildings while adding new jump ramps to create a more dynamic battlefield. In addition, anniversary decorations and special items have been added across stages and menus to celebrate the game’s first year since launch.
