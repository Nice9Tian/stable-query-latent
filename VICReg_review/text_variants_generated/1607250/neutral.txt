MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE is a 24-player online battle royale set in the world of My Hero Academia, where players fight in teams of three using communication, tactics, and coordinated Quirks to be the last ones standing. The roster features characters divided into five archetypes: Strike, Assault, Rapid, Technical, and Support, with each character’s strengths shaped by their unique Quirk. Players can also improve their position during matches by finding items, interacting with civilians, and defeating opponents to strengthen their abilities and gain an advantage over rival teams.

Season 7 introduces Hawks, the top-ranked Pro Hero on the Hero Billboard Chart, also known as “The Man Who’s A Bit Too Fast.” His Quirk, Fierce Wings, lets him use feathers for attacks and flight, giving him strong mobility and making him effective in both offense and support roles. This season also partially revamps U.A. Island by keeping its many buildings while adding new jump ramps to create a more dynamic battlefield. In celebration of the game’s first anniversary, special items and decorations have been added across stages and various screens, along with new costumes and other seasonal content.

MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE is updated each season with new characters, costumes, and other elements. Players can unlock new characters and customization options by gaining experience and completing missions, and can also take part in seasonal events and use tickets for exclusive items. The game is online only and requires an internet connection to play.
