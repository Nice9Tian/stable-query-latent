MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE is a 24-player online battle royale that tries to ride on the popularity of My Hero Academia, but its appeal is mostly tied to constant seasonal additions rather than a particularly polished core experience. The game is built around 3-player teams competing to be the last squad standing, with communication, tactics, and Quirk synergy presented as the main pillars of play.

The roster is split into five archetypes: Strike, Assault, Rapid, Technical, and Support. Each character has unique strengths based on their Quirk, so matches are supposed to revolve around choosing the right hero or villain for the situation. In practice, the game pushes a heavy emphasis on team composition and character-specific abilities, with players expected to find items, interact with civilians, and defeat opponents in order to improve their capabilities during a match.

Season 7 introduces Hawks as a new character. His Quirk, Fierce Wings, lets him use feathers for attacks and flight, and the game highlights his speed and mobility as strengths for both offense and support roles. The season also partially revamps U.A. Island, keeping the existing buildings while adding jump ramps to make the battlefield more dynamic. On top of that, the first anniversary brings special items and decorations across stages and various screens, along with new costumes and other seasonal content.

Progression is tied to experience, missions, unlocks, and customization options, while seasonal events and ticket-based rewards are used to push exclusive cosmetics. The game is clearly structured around live-service updates, with each season adding new characters, costumes, and other changes. It is online-only, requires an internet connection, and cannot be played offline, which makes its entire experience dependent on ongoing service availability.
