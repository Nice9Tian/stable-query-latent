**MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE** is a vibrant online 24-player battle royale that brings the world of heroes and villains into fast-paced team combat. Built around the use of Quirks, the game encourages players to communicate, use tactics, and combine abilities with a three-person squad in order to become the last team standing.

One of the biggest highlights of Season 7 is the debut of **Hawks**, the top-ranked Pro Hero on the Hero Billboard Chart, also known as **“The Man Who’s A Bit Too Fast.”** Hawks stands out through his Quirk, **Fierce Wings**, which lets him attack with feathers and fly across the battlefield. His speed and mobility make him effective in both offensive and support roles, adding a powerful new option to the roster.

Season 7 also introduces changes to **U.A. Island**, which has been partially revamped. While the many buildings remain in place, the map now includes **jump ramps** that create a more dynamic battlefield and open up new movement possibilities. In celebration of the game’s **first anniversary since launch**, special items and decorations have also been added across stages and various screens, giving the game a fresh festive atmosphere. New costumes and additional content are also part of the season’s offerings.

The core gameplay centers on choosing from a diverse cast of characters divided into five archetypes: **Strike, Assault, Rapid, Technical, and Support**. Each character’s strengths vary based on their unique Quirk, making team composition and role selection important. During matches, players can **find items, interact with civilians, and defeat opponents** to grow stronger and gain an advantage over rival teams.

Progression is also a major part of the experience. Players can **unlock new characters and customization options** by earning experience and completing missions. Seasonal events and tickets provide opportunities to obtain exclusive items, adding even more variety and style to the game.

With its ever-changing seasonal updates, strong team-based combat, and faithful My Hero Academia-inspired roster, **MY HERO ULTRA RUMBLE** delivers an energetic and stylish battle royale experience that continues to evolve with each season.
