**Crow Country** is a survival horror game that invites players into the eerie calm of an abandoned theme park, where a charming exterior hides something deeply unsettling. As you investigate the park, you’ll face puzzles and riddles while gradually uncovering the truth behind Edward’s decision to shut the place down and his mysterious disappearance.

The game encourages exploration and backtracking as new areas open up, letting you slowly piece together the disturbing events surrounding Crow Country. Beneath the whimsical setting lies a story of ambition, greed, and consequences, with unsettling rumors that may be more real than they first seem. At the center of the mystery is Mara Forest, whose true identity becomes part of the game’s larger intrigue.

For players who want to focus on the environment and atmosphere, **Exploration Mode** offers a more relaxed experience, allowing you to roam the park, spot crows, and take in the sights without being attacked by the mysterious monsters that inhabit the area.

With its blend of horror, mystery, and environmental storytelling, **Crow Country** delivers a compelling and atmospheric adventure that keeps you curious from start to finish.
