Crow Country is a survival horror game set in an abandoned theme park, where players investigate the eerie calm of its surroundings while solving puzzles and riddles. As new areas are unlocked, players can backtrack through the park to uncover more of the story and gradually learn why Edward shut down the park and where he disappeared to.

The game presents a setting that appears whimsical at first, but something is clearly wrong beneath the surface. Players will encounter mysterious monsters roaming Crow Country, though an Exploration Mode is available for those who want to explore the park without being attacked. This mode lets visitors move through the environment more peacefully, focusing on spotting crows and taking in the sights.

At its core, Crow Country tells a story about reckless ambition, human greed, and the question of how far someone might go in pursuit of their goals. It also raises themes of guilt, redemption, and hidden truths, while placing the player in the middle of the mystery. The narrative centers on uncovering what really happened in the park and learning more about the identity of Mara Forest.
