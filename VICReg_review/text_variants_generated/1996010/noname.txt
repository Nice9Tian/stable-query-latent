A survival horror experience set in an abandoned theme park, this game asks players to navigate an unsettling contrast between cheerful surroundings and a deeply wrong atmosphere. As they investigate the park’s eerie calm, they must solve puzzles and riddles while gradually unlocking new areas and revisiting earlier locations to uncover more of the mystery behind the park’s closure and the disappearance of its owner.

The game is structured around exploration and discovery, with progression tied to backtracking and piecing together fragments of a larger story. Beneath the whimsical exterior lies a narrative built around ambition, greed, and the consequences of reckless choices, all of which are slowly revealed as the player advances. Rumors surrounding the park’s dark history add to the tension, though the truth is not immediately clear.

For players who prefer to focus on atmosphere and sightseeing rather than danger, an optional mode removes hostile encounters with the mysterious creatures that roam the park. This allows for a calmer journey through the environment while still preserving the sense of unease and curiosity that defines the setting.

At its core, the game blends exploration, puzzle-solving, and horror storytelling, placing the player at the center of a mystery shaped by hidden motives and long-buried secrets.
