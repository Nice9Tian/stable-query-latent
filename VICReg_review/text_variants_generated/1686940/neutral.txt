Bopl Battle is a couch and online multiplayer game in which players battle each other as goofy slimes. The goal is simple: squish and splatter opponents using a mix of wild and unique abilities and weapons, and be the last slime standing.

The game emphasizes unusual interactions and creative combinations. Players can attach a rocket engine to platforms to send them flying, cover the stage in flammable smoke, create a black hole that swallows the entire stage, or use a growth ray to permanently enlarge objects such as arrows, grenades, other players, and even the stage itself. Another ability lets players stop time and prepare deadly situations while enemies are frozen. It is also possible to grab the stage and move it around, opening the door to changing the battlefield itself.

Bopl Battle encourages experimentation with ability combinations, allowing players to invent their own strategies. Examples given include creating a platform, controlling it, and attaching a big sword to it, or combining flammable smoke, grenades, and an invincible rock transformation. The combinations become even more chaotic in team-vs-team mode, where cooperation can be used to push the game’s systems further.

The game is multiplayer only. There is no single-player mode and no bots, so players must bring a friend or use the built-in online matchmaking. For local play, only one player can use keyboard and mouse per computer, meaning additional local players need controllers. The game also includes a warning that it contains flashing lights and may not be suitable for people with photosensitive epilepsy or other photosensitive conditions.
