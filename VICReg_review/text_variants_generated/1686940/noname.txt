This couch-and-online multiplayer party game pits players against one another as playful slime-like creatures in fast-paced battles. The core objective is straightforward: outlast everyone else and become the final survivor. Matches center on creative combat, movement, and improvisation, with players combining unusual powers and weapons to produce chaotic results.

A defining feature of the game is its emphasis on flexible ability combinations. Players can modify the battlefield in surprising ways, such as attaching a rocket engine to platforms to launch them across the stage, filling the arena with flammable smoke, or creating a black hole that can consume the entire play area. Other powers allow players to enlarge objects or opponents permanently, stop time to set up dangerous situations while enemies are frozen, or even pick up and move the stage itself. The game encourages experimentation, letting players invent their own tactics by mixing different tools and effects.

Team-based play expands these possibilities further. In team versus team matches, coordinated combinations can become even more elaborate as players work together to push the mechanics to their limits. The game is designed around multiplayer competition, with no single-player mode and no bots. Local play requires controllers for additional players, since only one player can use keyboard and mouse per computer. Online matchmaking is also available for those who want to play without gathering locally.

The game includes a flashing-lights warning for players sensitive to visual effects, as it may not be suitable for people with photosensitive epilepsy or similar conditions. Overall, it presents itself as a chaotic multiplayer brawler built around inventive physics-based interactions, changing battlefield conditions, and emergent strategies.
