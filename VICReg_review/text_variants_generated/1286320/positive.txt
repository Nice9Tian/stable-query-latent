**Exoprimal** is an energetic online, team-based action game that throws humanity’s most advanced exosuit technology into battle against one of history’s deadliest threats: dinosaurs. Set in the year 2040, the game presents a world overwhelmed by sudden dinosaur outbreaks, where survival depends on the cutting-edge predictions of the Aibius corporation’s hyper-advanced AI, Leviathan, and the strength of its revolutionary powered suits.

At the heart of Exoprimal is its exosuit system, which gives players the freedom to adapt to the fight in real time. These exosuits are divided into three distinct roles: **assault**, **tank**, and **support**. Assault exosuits focus on dealing damage at close, medium, or long range, tank exosuits protect allies by drawing enemy fire and absorbing damage, and support exosuits help keep the team alive through repairs, buffs, and debuffs. Players can change exosuits during battle through a suit change, allowing for flexible team composition and dynamic responses to whatever the mission demands.

The game also features **rigs**, additional pieces of equipment that grant exosuits extra abilities. Only one rig can be equipped at a time, but the combination system encourages experimentation and personal playstyles. A close-range fighter can gain long-range capability with a rig, while a defensive exosuit can be equipped to provide healing and strengthen the frontline. Both rigs and exosuits can be changed at any time, reinforcing the game’s emphasis on tactical adaptation.

Exoprimal’s main mode, **Dino Survival**, centers on two squads of five racing to complete objectives under Leviathan’s directives. The teams must finish their mission before the opposing side to win. Depending on the situation, players may face direct combat against each other or cooperate with rivals to defeat a powerful enemy. Mission structures change as players progress, so each match can feel different even when familiar locations or goals appear. Playing Dino Survival also unlocks story sequences and rewards, including experience points for both player and exosuit progression.

The dinosaur threat itself is not ordinary. Through travel across spacetime and the vortexes, these creatures have been mutated into far more ferocious forms. Among them are **neosaurs**, mutated dinosaurs with unusual appearances, abilities, and attacks, intensified by highly unstable energy. This adds another layer of danger and spectacle to the game’s conflict, making the battlefield feel wild, unpredictable, and intense.

Exoprimal stands out as a bold, team-focused action experience built around rapid adaptation, role-based teamwork, and relentless battles against overwhelming dinosaur swarms. Its combination of exosuit roles, customizable rigs, evolving mission structure, and cooperative-versus-competitive scenarios creates a distinctive and exciting action game premise.
