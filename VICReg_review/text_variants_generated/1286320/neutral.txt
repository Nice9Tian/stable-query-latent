Exoprimal is an online, team-based action game centered on high-tech exosuits and battles against dinosaur swarms. Set in the year 2040, the game presents a world where sudden dinosaur outbreaks have created a global crisis. Humanity’s response comes from the corporation Aibius, which has developed a hyper-advanced AI called Leviathan to predict future outbreak locations, as well as powered suits known as exosuits. Players take on the role of exofighters and use these exosuits to fight for survival.

The game’s exosuits are divided into three main roles: assault, tank, and support. Assault exosuits are built for dealing damage at close, medium, or long range, depending on the specific suit and its loadout. Tank exosuits are designed to protect allies by drawing enemy attention and absorbing damage, serving as the front line against dinosaur attacks. Support exosuits focus on keeping the team alive through healing, buffs, and debuffs that improve combat effectiveness and help complete objectives faster. Players can switch exosuits at any time, even in the middle of battle, allowing them to adapt their team composition as the situation changes.

Exosuits can also be customized with rigs, which are additional pieces of equipment that grant extra abilities. Only one rig can be equipped at a time, and both rigs and exosuits can be changed at any time. This system allows players to experiment with different combinations to suit their preferred playstyle.

The main mode described is Dino Survival, where two squads of five compete to complete objectives. Teams must follow Leviathan’s directives and finish missions before their rivals to win. Some missions involve direct combat between squads, while others require cooperation with rival teams to defeat a powerful enemy. The missions change according to player progression, so matches can differ even when the settings or objectives appear familiar. By playing Dino Survival, players can unlock story sequences and earn rewards such as experience points that increase both player level and exosuit level.

The game also features dinosaurs and neosaurs as major threats. Dinosaurs have been transformed by traveling through spacetime via vortexes, making them more dangerous than before. Neosaurs are mutated dinosaurs with unusual appearances, abilities, and attacks, created by highly unstable energy encountered after passing through the vortex barrier. In this setting, humanity depends on exosuit technology to respond to the escalating crisis.
