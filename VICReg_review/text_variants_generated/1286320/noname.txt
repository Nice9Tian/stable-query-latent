This online team-based action game centers on humanity’s fight against overwhelming dinosaur swarms using advanced powered suits. Set in a future crisis year, the story follows a world where sudden dinosaur outbreaks threaten human survival. A powerful corporation has created a hyper-advanced predictive AI that can forecast future outbreak locations, alongside revolutionary exosuits that give people a chance to resist the disaster. Players take on the role of suit pilots and join this defense effort after proving their aptitude.

The core gameplay revolves around piloting exosuits in online-only team modes. These suits are divided into three main roles: assault, tank, and support. Assault suits are built for dealing damage at close, medium, or long range. Tank suits focus on defending allies by drawing enemy attention and absorbing damage. Support suits help the team survive by repairing allies and applying helpful or harmful effects that improve combat efficiency and objective completion. A key feature is the ability to change suits during battle, allowing players to adapt their team composition on the fly.

Another layer of customization comes from equipment modules that grant additional abilities. Only one module can be equipped at a time, but players can freely experiment with different combinations of suits and modules to create their preferred style of play. Examples shown on the page illustrate how a close-range suit can be given a long-range attack option, or how a defensive suit can be equipped with healing support.

The main multiplayer mode described is a mission-based race between two squads of five. Teams must follow the directives of the AI and complete objectives before the opposing squad. Some missions involve direct conflict between the two teams, while others require temporary cooperation to defeat a powerful enemy. Missions change as players progress, so matches can feel different even when the surroundings or objectives appear familiar. By playing this mode, players can unlock story sequences and earn rewards such as experience points that raise both player and suit levels.

The dinosaur threat itself is presented as more than just ordinary wildlife. Because the creatures travel through spacetime via vortexes, they become mutated and far more ferocious. A special class of mutated creatures, called neosaurs, exhibits unusual appearances, abilities, and attacks due to highly unstable energy. The game frames humanity’s response to this crisis as a contest of technology, teamwork, and adaptability, with exosuits serving as the primary weapon against the escalating prehistoric danger.
