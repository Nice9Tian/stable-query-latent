**Deceive Inc.** is a multiplayer espionage game centered on infiltration, disguise, and competition. Players take on the role of elite spies working for Deceive Inc., a private corporation that monopolizes the international espionage market. The core goal is straightforward: blend in, secure the objective, and extract successfully before rival spies do the same.

A key part of the game is deception. Each agent is equipped with a high-tech holographic watch that allows them to disguise themselves as guests, staff members, or even security guards. This enables players to move through restricted areas without immediately drawing attention. However, the disguise system has limits: it remains effective only as long as the player avoids actions that expose them, such as shooting or being shot.

The game features a roster of diverse agents, each with a unique weapon and skillset, allowing players to find a style that suits them. Loadouts can also be customized with a variety of gadgets. Examples mentioned include the Inflatable Mat, holo-mimic, and Bullet Proof Umbrella, giving players different tools for infiltration and escape.

Matches take place in sprawling locations filled with opportunities and risks. Players must navigate crowds, interact with electronics that can be hacked, enter restricted zones, and use environmental routes such as windows to move through the map. The path to the vault and the objective is left to the player, but rival spies are always working toward the same target, creating constant competition for the final payoff.
