In this multiplayer stealth-action game, players enter a world of corporate espionage where a powerful private organization dominates the global covert operations market. The premise centers on infiltration, deception, and competition: each player takes on the role of a highly capable operative tasked with blending in, reaching a target objective, and escaping with the prize before rival operatives do the same.

A core part of the experience is disguise-based gameplay. Using a high-tech wearable device, players can instantly imitate the appearance of different non-player characters such as guests, staff, or security personnel in order to move through restricted spaces without drawing attention. This system encourages careful observation of the environment and strategic timing, since exposing oneself through suspicious actions or open combat can break the disguise and put the player at risk.

The game features a roster of distinct operatives, each with their own weapon and skill set, allowing different playstyles. Players can also build customized loadouts from a range of versatile gadgets. These tools support many approaches to infiltration and disruption, including items such as a portable inflatable mat, a decoy projection device, and a protective umbrella-like defense tool.

Matches take place in large, elaborate locations populated by civilians, security forces, locked-off zones, hackable electronics, and traversal opportunities such as windows. Progress revolves around entering the area, collecting the objective, and finding a way out while navigating the efforts of other players who are pursuing the same goal. The structure creates tension between stealth, improvisation, and direct confrontation, with success depending on how well a player can adapt to the situation and outthink opponents.

The game has also received an extensive overhaul update focused on improving the overall flow of play. Changes include bot lobbies designed to help new players get started, reworked controller aim assist, improved security guard behavior, the addition of a gold keycard printer, and changes to shooting animations, alongside many more adjustments detailed across a large patch note set. A special edition is also available, offering themed outfits for all operatives, a custom profile portrait and title, and bonus in-game currency.
