American Truck Simulator delivers a polished and immersive trucking experience that lets players travel across the striking landscapes of California, Nevada, and Arizona while hauling a wide variety of cargo. Built on the proven foundation of Euro Truck Simulator 2 and expanded with new features, the game offers a compelling simulation of life on the road, combining detailed driving mechanics with the rewarding progression of building a transportation business from the ground up.

At its core, the game places you in the role of a hired driver entering the local freight market. Through safe and successful deliveries, you work your way up toward becoming an owner-operator and eventually grow into running one of the largest transportation companies in the United States. This journey is supported by a deep career system that includes buying garages, hiring drivers, managing your fleet, and maximizing profits as your company expands.

The trucking itself is richly detailed. Players can drive officially licensed trucks from iconic manufacturers and customize them extensively with different cabins, chassis, paint jobs, tuning accessories, and more powerful engines. Cargo variety is another major strength, ranging from food and machinery to hazardous materials. A broad selection of trailers is available as well, including reefers, flatbeds, dumpers, lowboys, and goosenecks, with especially long trailers up to 53 feet adding extra challenge when hauling and parking.

American Truck Simulator also pays close attention to realism. It features simulation settings that truck enthusiasts will appreciate, such as air brake simulation, multiple brake types including retarder, Jake brake, and trailer brake, as well as transmissions modeled after real trucks. Players can adjust their seat, mirrors, and head position to better see the road, use the route adviser for guidance, and must follow traffic rules and speed limits to avoid police fines. Weight checks add another layer of authenticity, ensuring deliveries stay within legal limits.

Beyond the driving, the game offers a comfortable and enjoyable experience with built-in music playback, radio streaming, a photo mode with editing options, and strong support for steering wheels, gamepads, and other input devices. Long-term support has also helped the game grow with new features over time, while Steam achievements and hand-painted trading cards provide additional goals for dedicated players.

The World of Trucks service adds another excellent layer of community-focused features. It allows players to share screenshots, favorite images, discuss screenshots with others, upload custom avatars and license plates, and even aim for a spot in the game’s hand-picked Editor’s Pick showcase. Registration is optional, but for those who join, it creates a vibrant online hub for truck simulation fans.

With its scenic routes, realistic trucking systems, extensive customization, and satisfying career progression, American Truck Simulator stands out as a confident and rewarding simulation that captures the spirit of the open road with remarkable care.
