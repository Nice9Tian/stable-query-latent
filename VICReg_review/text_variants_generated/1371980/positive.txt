**No Rest for the Wicked** is a visceral, precision-focused Action RPG from Moon Studios, the acclaimed team behind *Ori and the Blind Forest* and *Ori and the Will of the Wisps*. Set in the year 841, the game places you in a kingdom thrown into turmoil: King Harol is dead, his untested son Magnus has taken the crown, and the ancient Pestilence has returned after a thousand years to corrupt everything it touches. In the middle of this collapse lies Isola Sacra, a battered island where rebels, provincial власти, and church ambitions collide amid crumbling ruins.

You play as a **Cerim**, a member of a mystical order of holy warriors empowered to defeat the Pestilence at any cost. As the story unfolds, your mission becomes increasingly complex, pulling you into the struggles of the people and the larger political conflict shaping the fate of the kingdom. The narrative promises a mature, dark tone, with cunning rulers, fierce rebels, and a plague that twists the inhabitants into monstrous forms.

Combat is built around deliberation and strategy, rewarding careful timing and precision. Players face gruesome creatures, enemy soldiers, and punishing bosses in tense, weighty battles where each weapon has its own speed and move set. The game also supports deeper customization through runes, rare armor crafting, and bespoke character builds tailored to individual playstyles.

Beyond combat, *No Rest for the Wicked* presents a richly detailed painterly world, designed with striking artistry and remarkable attention to detail. From the sunlit Lowland Meadows to the grotesque shadows of the Nameless Pass, every region offers its own people, problems, secrets, and hidden treasures to uncover. The world feels alive, beautiful, and ominous all at once.

The town of **Sacrament** offers a place to rest and rebuild. Here, players can purchase property, decorate it, fish along the shores, till and harvest land for cooking ingredients, and interact with vendors and townsfolk. Restoring the town to its former glory becomes part of the journey, adding a satisfying layer of life and progression to the adventure.

The game also includes **online co-op for up to three friends**, letting players share quests, bosses, and the full expanse of Isola Sacra together. Friends can stay by your side or split off and explore independently, making the campaign feel flexible and shared.

With its striking world, demanding combat, deep customization, and rich atmosphere, *No Rest for the Wicked* stands out as an ambitious ARPG that aims to deliver both challenge and beauty in equal measure.
