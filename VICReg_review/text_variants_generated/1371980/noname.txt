Set in the year 841, this early-access action RPG presents a bleak kingdom in turmoil after the death of its king. Power shifts to an unproven heir, while an ancient plague known as the Pestilence returns after a thousand years, spreading corruption across the land. In the midst of this collapse, rival political forces clash on the island of Isola Sacra, where rebellion, religious ambition, and civic decline all intersect. Players take on the role of a Cerim, a sacred warrior order endowed with unusual powers and charged with destroying the plague, though the journey quickly becomes entangled with the struggles of the local population and the wider conflict shaping the realm.

The game emphasizes deliberate, strategic combat. Encounters are described as tense and visceral, with battles against grotesque creatures, hostile soldiers, and difficult bosses. Different weapon types each have their own movement patterns and handling, giving weight and speed distinct importance in play. Progression includes rune-based enhancement of weapons, the crafting of rare armor, and the creation of character builds tailored to individual playstyles.

Beyond combat, the game offers a dark narrative centered on a decaying society and the monstrous effects of the plague. Its world is presented through a painterly visual style with carefully detailed environments, ranging from bright meadows to grim, corrupted passages. Each area contains its own inhabitants, problems, hidden rewards, and secrets to discover. Players can also spend time in a central town hub, where they may buy property, decorate it, fish, farm ingredients, and prepare meals that restore health and improve statistics. The settlement can be gradually restored through interaction with vendors and townsfolk.

An online co-op mode supports up to three additional players, allowing them to share the campaign, quests, bosses, and the world itself, while still giving each participant the freedom to branch off independently if desired.
