No Rest for the Wicked is an early access action RPG from Moon Studios, the award-winning team behind Ori and the Blind Forest and Ori and the Will of the Wisps. Set in the year 841, the game begins after the death of King Harol, whose crown passes to his untested son Magnus. At the same time, the Pestilence, an ancient plague that has not appeared for a thousand years, returns to corrupt the land and its people. The story unfolds on Isola Sacra, where rebel groups and the provincial government struggle for control amid the island’s collapsing ruins. Players take on the role of a Cerim, a member of a mystical order of holy warriors sworn to defeat the Pestilence, while becoming drawn into the struggles of the region and its political conflicts.

The game emphasizes deliberate, strategic combat built around careful timing and weapon-specific move sets. Players face grotesque creatures, enemy soldiers, and difficult bosses in tense, visceral battles. Weapons can be empowered with runes, armor can be crafted, and character builds can be customized to suit different playstyles. The world also supports exploration across a dark, mature narrative landscape, with each location offering its own people, problems, hidden treasures, and secrets to discover.

Visually, the game presents a painterly world with a distinctive art style and strong attention to detail. Locations range from the sunlight of the Lowland Meadows to the grim, corrupted shadows of the Nameless Pass. Outside of combat, players can spend time in the town of Sacrament, where they can purchase property, decorate it, fish, farm ingredients, cook meals that restore health and provide stat boosts, and interact with vendors and townsfolk while helping restore the town.

No Rest for the Wicked also includes online co-op for up to three friends. In co-op, players can share the campaign and progress together, with the option to tackle quests, bosses, and exploration as a group or split up and adventure independently.
