In a near future marked by war, corruption, and environmental collapse, a powerful corporate initiative launches a last-ditch effort to preserve humanity. The player takes command of a colossal starship built for this mission, venturing into uncharted space to secure the future of the species. This deluxe edition also includes additional content such as an artbook, soundtrack, and extra downloadable material.

The experience centers on building and managing a modular spacecraft while navigating a vast, randomly generated universe. Runs are designed to differ from one another, with large procedural galaxies, multiple difficulty options, and a custom mode that allows further adjustment of the challenge. As the journey unfolds, the ship travels to new planets, avoids asteroid fields, and faces hostile boarding parties that can threaten both the vessel and everyone on board. Permadeath is a core rule: when all cloned crew members are lost, the mission ends. Some progress carries over between attempts in the form of recovered relics and newly unlocked corporate options, making each failure potentially useful for future runs.

The ship begins small and must be expanded over time into a fully functioning interstellar ark. Dozens of upgradeable modules can be assembled through a simple snap-together construction system, allowing the player to configure spaces such as living quarters, workshops, hangars, cloning facilities, and cultivation rooms. Once built, the vessel can also be explored in first person, giving direct access to its interior systems and layout.

Survival is complicated by hostile alien infestations that can spread rapidly through the ship, corrupting both the crew and the environment. These outbreaks must be contained before they overwhelm the vessel. To respond, the player can research weapons and defenses, deploy combat machines, and unlock additional tools and abilities. The game also includes methods for treating the crew using researched biological remedies, adding another layer to ship management and crisis response.

Crew growth is tied to advanced cloning technology. As the ship expands, new personnel must be created to meet its growing needs. DNA samples recovered from alien lifeforms can be combined with crew members to produce new beings with unusual abilities, offering a way to strengthen humanity’s chances of survival through genetic experimentation.

Resource gathering is another essential part of the loop. The player must land on dangerous planets to collect materials such as metala and radium, while also harvesting flora to support life aboard the ship. Exploration may uncover mysterious relics and information left behind by earlier expeditions, hinting at the fate of those who came before and did not return.
