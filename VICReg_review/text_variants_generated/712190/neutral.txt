Genesis Alpha One Deluxe Edition is a sci-fi survival and management game set in a near future devastated by wars, corrupt regimes, and severe pollution. In this setting, corporations launch the Genesis program as a last effort to preserve humanity. Players take the role of a Genesis starship captain and travel into uncharted space on a mission to build, manage, and expand a ship capable of sustaining life.

The game centers on constructing and maintaining a space vessel by combining a wide range of upgradable modules. These include crew quarters, workshops, hangars, clone labs, and greenhouses. Using an intuitive building menu, players can snap ship parts together and shape the vessel to suit their preferred style of play. Once the ship is developed, it can be explored in first person.

Genesis Alpha One features randomly generated galaxies, making each run different. Players can choose from three difficulty levels or use Custom Game Mode to adjust the experience. During exploration, they discover new planets, navigate asteroid fields, and face encounters with hostile groups such as the Mechanics and Framen, space pirates who board the ship in an effort to destroy it and its crew. The game uses a permanent death structure: when all clones are lost, the mission ends. Some progress carries over between playthroughs in the form of recovered artefacts and unlocked corporations, making later attempts more powerful.

Combat and defense are important parts of survival. Alien infestations can spread throughout the ship, contaminating both the vessel and its crew, and must be eliminated before they overwhelm the player. The game includes researchable weapons and defensive tools to fight these threats. Players can unlock powerful weaponry, study alien diseases to heal the crew, equip new abilities, and deploy warbots against alien enemies.

Another major system is cloning and genetic experimentation. As the ship expands, the crew must also grow to meet new demands. The cloning technology allows players to increase their workforce, and DNA samples taken from encountered aliens can be spliced with crew members. This provides humanity with new possibilities and abilities beyond what was previously imagined.

Resource gathering is also essential. To expand the ship and keep the crew alive, players must explore dangerous planets for materials such as Copper and Uranium, as well as harvest plants to support life aboard the vessel. Explorations may also uncover alien artefacts and information left behind by prior explorers who did not survive.

The Deluxe Edition includes the artbook, soundtrack, and the Rocket Star Corporation DLC.
