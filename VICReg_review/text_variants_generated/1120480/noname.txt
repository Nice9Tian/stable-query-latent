This sci-fi shooter places players in a battle for survival against endless alien hordes in a 1-8 player format. It supports intense 4-player cooperative play, while also offering a versus mode in which players can control the alien side and try to stop the survivors from reaching the end of the level. The game is designed around teamwork, but it also includes private matches against bots for practice, especially in the alien-control mode. At the same time, the current online population is described as small, so finding active public matches may be difficult without friends or an active community.

Combat is built around a wide range of futuristic weapons and gadgets. Players can use a stasis grenade to slow threats, a shield grenade to create instant cover, a device that revives teammates, and a laser-based rifle that can damage both allies and enemies. Additional content introduces new enemies, grenades, and weapons, including an experimental beta branch with even more chaotic offensive options. The game also supports special challenge-focused solo play through this beta content.

A major feature is the asymmetrical alien-versus-survivor structure. On the alien side, players can take direct control of distinct enemy types, each with different roles in disrupting the survivors. The available alien forms include a brute-style unit, a blinding attacker, a grabbing unit, a spreading creature, and a spawning support unit. In versus matches, the survivors must reach the end of the level while the alien team works to prevent their escape, with the mode tuned for teams of 2, 3, or 4 players.

The setting is a distant-future human civilization pushed to the edges of the galaxy by powerful corporations. After expanding across thousands of systems, humanity becomes complacent until an alien catastrophe forces survivors into a fight for their lives. The first season follows four unlikely survivors as they struggle against the invasion, and progression gradually reveals more of the larger mystery behind the attack.

The game uses an AI-driven system that controls enemy spawning, boss behavior, and the placement of weapons, gadgets, and health items. This design aims to create varied encounters that feel different each time, regardless of skill level, and to generate memorable moments during play. Progression also includes a perk system tied to special collectibles found throughout the game. These perks can be stacked to alter playstyle, such as improving a sidearm used in emergencies or strengthening defensive abilities. Unlocks can be shared with the rest of the team.

Community support is a major part of the experience. The game includes Steam Workshop integration for mods such as new campaigns, holdout maps, character skins, accessories, charms, animations, and other additions. Mods are visible to friends during play, and the community has also been active in sharing feedback, testing content, and participating in regular events.
