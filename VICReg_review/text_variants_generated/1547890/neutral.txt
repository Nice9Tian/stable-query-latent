**Let’s Build a Zoo** is a cute, expansive management sim about creating and running your own animal empire. The game puts players in charge of building a zoo, importing and breeding rare creatures, hiring staff, and keeping both visitors and animals happy. Along the way, it also presents a variety of unusual events to manage.

A major feature of the game is its animal variety. Players can build a zoo with over 500 different animals, and the game includes DNA Splicing that allows for more than 300,000 possible animal combinations. These can result in unusual creations such as a Giraffephant, a PandOwl, or a ChicKaka.

Management goes beyond just collecting animals. Players must place buildings, foliage, paths, and enclosure decorations to shape their zoo, and hire the right employees to handle different life events. The game also lets players control details such as what their animals eat and what eats them.

**Let’s Build a Zoo** also includes moral and financial choices. Players can choose to run the zoo above board or break the law to pocket extra cash. In addition, the game allows importing animals from other zoos around the world, breeding them into families, or creating entirely new animals through DNA splicing.
