It’s time to build a zoo-themed management simulation in which players create and run their own animal-focused empire. The game combines staff management, enclosure planning, breeding systems, visitor satisfaction, and a range of unusual events into a colorful and expansive experience. Players can import rare creatures, arrange habitats, and keep both animals and guests content while balancing the many demands of a growing park.

A major feature of the game is a DNA-splicing system that allows players to combine animal traits in highly varied ways. With access to more than 500 base animals and over 300,000 possible combinations, the system can produce a wide range of unusual hybrid creatures. The game emphasizes experimentation, letting players create oddities and unexpected results rather than relying only on standard breeding.

Park management extends beyond animals themselves. Players can place hundreds of different buildings, decorative plants, paths, and enclosure items to shape the zoo’s layout and appearance. They must also hire suitable employees to handle different life events and operational tasks, ensuring that the park continues to function smoothly.

The simulation gives players control over many aspects of animal care and park behavior, including what the animals eat and even what may eat them. It also includes a moral choice in how the zoo is operated: players can choose to run the park honestly or break the rules for additional profit. Overall, the game presents a flexible management experience centered on building, breeding, mutation, and the challenges of running a thriving wildlife attraction.
