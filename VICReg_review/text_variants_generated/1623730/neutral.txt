*Palworld* is a survival adventure game set in a harsh world inhabited by mysterious creatures called Pals. Players can choose to live peacefully alongside these creatures or take a more aggressive path by fighting off a ruthless poaching syndicate. The game centers on using Pals in many different ways: they can battle alongside the player, work on farms or in factories, and even be sold or eaten.

Survival is a major focus. Food is scarce, dangerous poachers roam the world, and players must make difficult decisions to stay alive. Exploration is supported through mounted Pals, which can be used to travel across land, sea, and sky. The game also emphasizes building, allowing players to assign Pals to construction tasks such as building structures, including even a pyramid.

Pals contribute to production by helping create fire, generate electricity, and mine ore. Farming is another core system, with different Pals suited to planting, watering, and harvesting crops. Automation extends this further through factories, where Pals continue working as long as they are fed.

The game also includes dungeon exploration, where Pals can help players survive dangerous areas, sometimes at great cost. Breeding and genetics are part of progression as well: offspring inherit traits from their parents, and rare Pals can be combined to create stronger ones.

Another aspect of the game is poaching and crime. Endangered Pals can be found in wildlife sanctuaries, and players can sneak in to capture rare Pals for profit. *Palworld* also supports multiplayer, including online co-op for up to 4 players and dedicated servers supporting up to 32 players. In multiplayer, players can adventure together, battle each other, and trade Pals.
