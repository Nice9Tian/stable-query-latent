This science-fiction adventure presents a vast procedurally generated galaxy to explore, where every star system can contain multiple planets, unfamiliar lifeforms, and constant risk. Travel is seamless, moving from deep space to planetary surfaces without loading screens or imposed limits, and the universe is designed so that players may encounter places and creatures that no one else has seen before.

The experience centers on freedom of choice. Players can explore alone or with others, and the journey may take many forms: combat-focused travel, resource trading, or open-ended discovery. A fighter can attack hostile targets and pirates, upgrading a spacecraft for speed and firepower. A trader can search forgotten worlds for valuable materials, expand cargo capacity, and pursue higher profits. An explorer can push beyond known borders, extend travel range, and improve survival in hazardous environments through ship, weapon, and suit upgrades.

The galaxy itself is active and shared. Trade convoys move between stars, rival factions compete for territory, pirates pursue vulnerable travelers, and authorities monitor activity. Other players exist in the same universe, and discoveries can be shared on a map covering known space, letting users see the impact of others’ actions as well as their own.

A long list of major updates has been included, along with cross-play and cross-save support across many platforms. The game is available on PC and Mac, and supports a wide range of systems including console, handheld, and VR platforms. Overall, it offers a large-scale voyage built around exploration, survival, trade, combat, and social sharing within a persistent shared universe.
