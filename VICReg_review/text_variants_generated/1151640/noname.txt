Set in a future where the earth belongs to no one, this action role-playing game follows a young outcast hunter on a journey to uncover her origins, understand her place in the world, and confront a looming catastrophe. The story centers on exploring a landscape ruled by deadly mechanical beings, where ancient mysteries, tribal tensions, and the protagonist’s personal fate are closely intertwined.

Gameplay combines open-world exploration with tactical combat. Players move through a vast environment filled with wildlife, danger, and rival factions, using careful strategy to defeat unique mechanical enemies and hostile tribes. Encounters emphasize planning and precision, with attacks designed to be both powerful and tactical rather than purely brute force.

This complete PC release also includes a major expansion that adds a new region, additional abilities, new weapons, and more mechanical threats to face. In addition to the core adventure and expansion content, the package contains several bonus equipment sets, weapon sets, travel packs, and a digital art book.
