Horizon Zero Dawn™ Complete Edition is an action role-playing game set in an open world where humanity no longer rules the Earth. Players follow Aloy, a young hunter and outcast from her tribe, as she uncovers the mysteries of her past, learns her destiny, and confronts a catastrophic threat to the future.

The game centers on exploration, combat, and discovery in a world populated by deadly Machines and rival tribes. Combat emphasizes tactical attacks, and players can use a variety of weapons and skills while moving through an open environment filled with wildlife and danger.

This Complete Edition includes the base game Horizon Zero Dawn and the expansion The Frozen Wilds, which adds new lands, skills, weapons, and Machines. It also includes additional bonus content: the Carja Storm Ranger Outfit and Carja Mighty Bow, the Carja Trader Pack, the Banuk Trailblazer Outfit and Banuk Culling Bow, the Banuk Traveller Pack, the Nora Keeper Pack, and a digital art book.
