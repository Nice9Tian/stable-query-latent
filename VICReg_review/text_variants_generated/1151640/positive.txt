**Horizon Zero Dawn™ Complete Edition** is a celebrated action role-playing adventure that invites players into a striking world where Earth is no longer humanity’s dominion. At the center of the experience is Aloy, an outcast hunter whose journey to uncover her origins becomes a sweeping quest to reveal the secrets of a civilization shaped by deadly Machines and to confront a threat that endangers the future itself.

The game stands out for its exhilarating combat, encouraging players to unleash devastating, tactical attacks against a wide variety of unique Machines as well as rival tribes. Exploration is equally compelling, with an expansive open world filled with wildlife, danger, and discoveries that reward curiosity at every turn. Its blend of action, mystery, and role-playing elements creates a rich and memorable adventure.

This Complete Edition for PC offers the full Horizon Zero Dawn experience along with the major expansion **The Frozen Wilds**, which adds new lands, skills, weapons, and Machines, deepening the game’s already impressive scope. It also includes a generous collection of bonus content: the **Carja Storm Ranger Outfit and Carja Mighty Bow**, **Carja Trader Pack**, **Banuk Trailblazer Outfit and Banuk Culling Bow**, **Banuk Traveller Pack**, **Nora Keeper Pack**, and a **digital art book**.

As a multi-award-winning title, **Horizon Zero Dawn™ Complete Edition** delivers an exceptional blend of world-building, combat, and story-driven adventure, making it a remarkable journey from beginning to end.
