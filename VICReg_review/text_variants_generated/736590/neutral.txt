Operation: Harsh Doorstop is a free-to-play tactical sandbox with strong support for modifications, single-player play, co-operative play, competitive multiplayer, and Steam Workshop integration. It is positioned as a mod-friendly game in the spirit of titles like Ravenfield and Garry’s Mod, while also drawing inspiration from military simulation games such as Squad and Arma III. In its unmodified state, the game is described as playing more like Squad or Insurgency: Sandstorm, but modified versions can be transformed into almost anything the community creates.

The game is free, with community donations helping to fund development. The developers present this free-access model as an important part of their philosophy, emphasizing accessibility and the health of the player community. Operation: Harsh Doorstop also has clear roots in tactical shooters such as Project Reality, Squad, and Red Orchestra, and includes developers who have worked on those projects. At the same time, it places a stronger emphasis on flexibility and customization, allowing players to adjust their experience to their preferences.

Development is intended to continue as long as community support remains, and even if official development stops, the game is designed so that the community can keep developing it independently through Steam Workshop. The stated goal is to create a game that can last indefinitely through ongoing player involvement and mod support.
