Operation: Harsh Doorstop is a free-to-play tactical sandbox built around flexibility, community support, and extensive modification support. Its base version plays in a style closer to military simulation shooters, with influences reminiscent of large-scale tactical combat games, while user-made modifications can transform it into almost any kind of experience. The game supports single-player, cooperative play, competitive multiplayer, and integration with a community workshop for sharing and expanding content.

The project is positioned as both a tactical shooter and a mod-friendly platform. It draws inspiration from realistic military games, but places a much stronger emphasis on customization than a traditional fixed-format shooter. Players are encouraged to approach it in whatever way suits them best, with ongoing plans to add more parameters that let each person shape the experience further.

A key feature of the game is that it is entirely free. Development is supported by optional community donations, with the stated goal of keeping the game accessible to as many players as possible. The developers also emphasize that this model helps build a healthier and larger player community.

The game is intended to remain open-ended over time. As long as support continues, it will keep receiving updates, and even if official development stops, the community can continue expanding and maintaining it through workshop-based creation. In this sense, it is designed not as a one-time release, but as a long-lasting platform for tactical play and player-driven content.
