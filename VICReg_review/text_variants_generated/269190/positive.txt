*Edge Of Eternity* presents a richly imagined fantasy world in crisis, where the planet Heryon has been torn apart by a devastating conflict between magic and technology. After the arrival of a mysterious alien force triggered an endless war, the situation grows even more desperate when the invaders unleash the Corrosion, a lethal disease that twists living beings into monstrous abominations. Against this backdrop of ruin and chaos, a young soldier is called to follow his destiny and begin an epic journey that will shape the future of Heryon.

The game highlights a compelling story filled with dramatic twists and emotional moments, supported by a beautiful fantasy setting that invites exploration. Its battles are built around a unique and strategic turn-based combat system, offering a thoughtful approach to epic encounters. Along the way, players meet charismatic and memorable characters, enjoy a stunning soundtrack by Yasunori Mitsuda, and engage with a crafting system that adds another layer to the adventure. The journey also includes the chance to befriend a fearless yet adorable beast, adding warmth to the game’s grand scale.

With more than 50 hours of gameplay, *Edge Of Eternity* promises a substantial and immersive experience. Developed by Midgar Studio, a small independent French team specializing in hardcore games for PC and consoles, the game stands out as an ambitious and heartfelt fantasy RPG full of atmosphere, strategy, and memorable moments.
