This package includes the main adventure along with two previously released expansions, offering a complete edition of the experience in one value-focused release.

The game presents a corruptive force that has infiltrated a secretive federal agency, placing humanity in danger. The player takes on the role of a central operative tasked with stopping this threat and restoring order. The story follows a supernatural investigation filled with unusual characters, strange events, and a search for a missing brother, gradually revealing the truth behind the protagonist’s arrival at the agency.

Gameplay is presented as a visually striking third-person action-adventure with open-ended environments and a strong emphasis on exploration and discovery. The setting is described as a hidden world inside a government organization, with shifting spaces that invite players to uncover more than what is immediately visible. Combat centers on transforming weaponry and telekinetic powers, allowing the environment itself to be used as a tool of destruction. Players can discover new ways to defeat enemies by combining supernatural abilities with interactive surroundings.

Progression includes mission-based play, challenging boss encounters, and rewards that improve abilities and allow customization of weapon options. The overall experience is positioned as intense, expansive, and built around a mix of mystery, exploration, and dynamic combat.
