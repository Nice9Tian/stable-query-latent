**Control Ultimate Edition** is a visually striking third-person action-adventure package that brings together the main game and both previously released expansions, **The Foundation** and **AWE**, in one great-value edition. Winner of over 80 awards, it delivers a gripping experience built around Remedy Entertainment’s signature world-building, storytelling, and intensely rewarding gameplay.

At the heart of the game is a mysterious crisis: a corruptive presence has invaded the **Federal Bureau of Control**, and humanity is at stake. As you search for your missing brother, you are drawn into an epic supernatural struggle filled with bizarre events, unexpected characters, and unsettling secrets hidden deep within the bureau. The story unfolds across open-ended, deeply atmospheric environments where danger and discovery go hand in hand.

One of Control’s most exciting features is its combat, where **the world itself becomes your weapon**. You can unleash destruction using transforming weaponry and powerful telekinetic abilities, turning objects and surroundings into lethal tools against your enemies. The game encourages creativity in battle, letting you discover new ways to annihilate threats as you master your growing set of abilities.

Exploration is equally compelling, as you delve into the shifting spaces of a secretive government agency that always seems to hold more than it first reveals. Every area is designed to keep you engaged, with an ominous sense of mystery and hidden depth surrounding the Bureau’s dark secrets.

Control also offers thrilling missions and challenging boss fights, rewarding progress with powerful upgrades that enhance your abilities and let you customize your weaponry. With its blend of supernatural storytelling, dynamic action, and immersive environments, **Control Ultimate Edition** stands out as an expansive and highly satisfying adventure from beginning to end.
