**Control Ultimate Edition** is a third-person action-adventure game that includes the main game along with the previously released expansions, **The Foundation** and **AWE**, in one package. The story centers on a corruptive presence that has invaded the **Federal Bureau of Control**, placing humanity at risk. As the player, you must uncover the truth behind the situation, search for your missing brother, and attempt to stop the threat before containment fails completely.

It combines open-ended exploration with the storytelling and world-building style associated with Remedy Entertainment. The narrative includes dark secrets, unexpected characters, and bizarre events as you progress through the Bureau’s hidden world.

Combat in **Control Ultimate Edition** emphasizes transformation and power. The player can use transforming weaponry and telekinetic abilities, turning nearby objects and the environment into weapons. Encounters include a relentless enemy, challenging boss fights, and missions that reward powerful upgrades. These upgrades can enhance abilities and allow customization of weaponry.

Described as a visually striking experience, the game is presented as one of intense action and discovery, where the environment itself becomes part of the combat and the mystery unfolds through exploration and progression.
