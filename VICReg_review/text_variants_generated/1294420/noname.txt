Set in the year 2030, this retrofuturistic action title presents a world where corporations hold power and the boundary between reality and performance has become increasingly blurred. The public is kept distracted by a brutal blood sport centered on spectacle, violence, and excess, while the player competes to rise through a high-stakes tournament and uncover the true motives of a powerful corporation.

The game is a third-person action shooter that combines fast-paced combat with fluid movement. Its core design merges shooting with skating-like motion, making tricks and grinds part of the combat loop rather than separate activities. Eliminations restore health, while performing tricks and grinds replenishes ammunition, creating a system that rewards momentum, style, and aggressive play.

Encounters are described as cinematic and visceral, with the player expected to balance mobility, accuracy, and resource management under pressure. The experience is built around skillful execution, with online leaderboards providing a way to compare performance and an unlockable extra mode offering a more demanding test of agility and combat mastery.

Presentation is also a major part of the experience. The game features an original soundtrack that blends sounds inspired by a past era with modern production, supporting the intensity of the action. Its comic-book-inspired visual style gives the world a distinctive identity, while the setting itself frames the tournament within a broader sinister conspiracy.

Overall, the game offers an original hybrid of shooting and skating mechanics, a strong emphasis on style-driven combat, and a narrative backdrop centered on corporate control, spectacle, and hidden agendas.
