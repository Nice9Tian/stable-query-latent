Rollerdrome is a BAFTA award-winning third-person action shooter that combines high-intensity combat with fluid movement to create a hybrid shooter-skater experience. Set in 2030, the game takes place in a retrofuture world where corporations rule and the public is distracted by the violent spectacle of a brutal blood sport called Rollerdrome.

In the game, combat and movement are tightly connected. Kills restore health, while performing tricks and grinds provides ammunition, encouraging players to keep moving and attacking with style. This structure makes the action both cinematic and demanding, with a focus on skillful play and momentum.

Players take part in the Rollerdrome tournament and aim to become champion while uncovering the secrets behind the Matterhorn corporation’s real intentions. The game also includes online leaderboards and an unlockable Out For Blood mode, which offers an even more difficult challenge for players looking to test their skill and agility further.

Rollerdrome features a fully original soundtrack inspired by classic era sounds and tones, paired with modern production. Its presentation is supported by a comic-book-inspired art style, giving the game a distinct visual identity. Developed by Roll7, the studio behind the OlliOlli series, Rollerdrome presents a dark, original conspiracy set within a striking retrofuture setting.
