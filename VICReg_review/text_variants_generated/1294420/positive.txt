**Rollerdrome** is a BAFTA award-winning third-person action shooter that stands out for its seamless fusion of high-octane combat and fluid movement. Created by Roll7, the acclaimed studio behind the *OlliOlli* series, it delivers a bold shooter-skater hybrid that feels fast, stylish, and entirely original.

Set in the year 2030, Rollerdrome takes place in a retrofuture world where corporations hold power and the boundaries between reality and performance have blurred. The public is captivated by a brutal new blood sport designed to distract them from the truth. As a competitor in this deadly spectacle, you must fight to become the Rollerdrome champion while uncovering the hidden motives behind the Matterhorn corporation.

The game’s combat and traversal are tightly connected. Kills restore health, while tricks and grinds replenish ammunition, encouraging players to keep moving with style and precision. This creates a dynamic action loop where cinematic, visceral firefights are matched by smooth skating mechanics, making every encounter feel intense and rewarding.

Rollerdrome also offers a strong test of skill. Players can take on the tournament’s unique challenges, compete through online leaderboards, and push their abilities further in the unlockable **Out For Blood** mode, designed as an even more extreme challenge of agility and mastery.

Its identity is strengthened by a fully original soundtrack that combines iconic era-inspired sounds with modern production, alongside a striking comic-book-inspired art style. Together, these elements give the game a distinctive atmosphere that complements its brutal action and mysterious narrative.

With its inventive gameplay, stylish presentation, and compelling dark conspiracy, **Rollerdrome** delivers an adrenaline-pumping experience that is both challenging and memorable.
