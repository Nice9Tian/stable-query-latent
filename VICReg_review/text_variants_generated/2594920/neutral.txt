Atelier Resleriana: Forgotten Alchemy and the Polar Night Liberator is an adventure set in the kingdom of Lantarna, where alchemy was once a flourishing art powered by the blessings of a white comet. After the comet disappeared, alchemy gradually faded from use and was eventually forgotten. The story follows Resna, a girl who has found hope in alchemy and is traveling to the Capital with the goal of reaching the World’s End, where the source of the miracle is said to lie. She is joined by Valeria, a girl who has lost her memories and works as an adventurer for the Moonlight Society. As their paths cross, the mysterious group known as the Polar Night Alchemists casts a shadow over their journey, and the two move closer to the truth hidden on the continent.

The game presents a new protagonist and an original adventure, described as the first of its kind in four years since Atelier Ryza. It features high-quality 3D characters created with technology developed for the Atelier series, aiming for a cinematic presentation comparable to current console titles.

Its battle system is tactical and timeline-based, using simple command battles with dynamic skill visuals. An “Effects Panel” adds strategic depth by providing various effects that can be used to gain an advantage in combat.

Alchemy remains a core feature of the series, and the synthesis system is designed to be easy to use while still offering meaningful choices. Players combine traits assigned to characters and materials to create their preferred results.

The game also includes a system for strengthening all characters. Party members can be improved through items and equipment made via synthesis, as well as Growboards that raise character parameters. This supports building a stronger party for the alchemical adventure ahead.

The staff includes TAKAHIRO as original story, series composition, and scenario supervisor; Shinichi Yoshiike as Atelier series supervisor; character designers Umiu Geso, tokki, and NOCO; and theme song and insert song vocalists including reche, Haruka Shimotsuki, Celeina Ann, Rico Sasaki, and SAK. Development and operation are handled by KOEI TECMO GAMES CO., LTD. The application is targeted at users age 16 and above.
