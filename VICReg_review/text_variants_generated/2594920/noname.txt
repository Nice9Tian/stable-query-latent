This fantasy role-playing adventure is set in a kingdom that once flourished under the blessing of a white comet. The art of drawing power from that blessing was known as alchemy, and those who practiced it were revered for their ability to shape the world through their craft. Over time, however, the comet vanished, its influence faded, and alchemy gradually disappeared from common knowledge.

The story follows two young women whose paths cross in a land where the old magic has become a distant memory. One is a determined heroine who sees hope in the forgotten craft and travels toward the capital, seeking the farthest edge of the world where the source of miracles is said to exist. The other is an amnesiac adventurer living in the city, taking on rough work for a local society. As their lives become intertwined, they are drawn into the shadow of a mysterious dark faction and move closer to the hidden truth buried across the continent.

The game presents a new main protagonist and emphasizes an all-new adventure centered on reviving the lost art. Its presentation features modern 3D character models designed with high-end visual quality, aiming to deliver a cinematic story with expressive scenes and polished animation.

Combat uses a tactical, timeline-based command system. Battles are built around simple turn planning with dynamic skill effects, while a special effects board adds strategic options that can influence the flow of combat. This creates a system that is easy to approach while still allowing for tactical depth.

A signature synthesis mechanic is also included, streamlined for accessibility but still designed to feel rewarding. By combining traits from characters and materials, players can craft solutions suited to their goals. In addition, all characters can be developed through multiple growth methods, including items and equipment made through synthesis and special boards that raise their parameters. Together, these systems support party building, character progression, and preparation for increasingly demanding challenges.

The project brings together a scenario staff led by well-known creators, character design by multiple artists, and theme vocals from several singers. It is developed and operated by a major Japanese publisher and is intended for players aged 16 and above.
