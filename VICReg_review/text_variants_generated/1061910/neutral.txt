*Metal: Hellsinger* is a rhythm-based first-person shooter set in the Infernal Planes, where players take on the role of The Unknown, a being who is part human and part demon and driven by vengeance. The game’s core premise is to fight through the domains of Hell on a journey to confront the Red Judge.

Its combat system is built around shooting to the beat. The more accurately the player stays in sync with the rhythm, the more intense the music becomes and the greater the destruction inflicted on enemies. Players can battle demon hordes using a skull-clad blade and a range of offensive weapons, and when the ultimate ability is charged, they can unleash it with the weapon ability of their choice.

The game also includes the Leviathan mode, in which players face increasingly difficult waves of demons in hellish arenas. As progression continues, players make decisions about upgrades that shape their combat effectiveness and push them toward becoming more powerful. Additional challenge content can be unlocked through special challenges and the Torment realms, with leaderboards available for competition.

Music is a major part of the experience. Every track was created specifically for the game by the composer duo Two Feathers, and the soundtrack features vocals from metal artists including Serj Tankian, Randy Blythe, and Alissa White-Gluz, among others. The game also features an epic storyline narrated by award-winning actor Troy Baker.

The Infernal Planes are described as a union of a thousand Hells, each with its own terrifying nature. To overthrow the Red Judge, players move through brutal regions such as the icy world of Voke and the maddening world of Stygia.

*Metal: Hellsinger* was developed by The Outsiders, an experienced FPS team, and is presented as a passion project from David Goldfarb, known for his work as Game Director on *Payday 2* and Lead Designer on *Battlefield 3* and *Battlefield: Bad Company 2*.
