This game is a rhythm-driven first-person shooter set in a vast infernal realm made up of many terrifying and distinct hells. Players take on the role of a half-human, half-demon warrior driven by vengeance, battling through the harshest domains of the underworld on a path toward a final confrontation with a powerful ruler.

The core gameplay centers on firing weapons in time with the beat. Shooting accurately on rhythm increases the intensity of the music and boosts the amount of destruction players can unleash. Combat includes both close-range attacks with a skull-themed blade and a range of lethal firearms. When a special meter is filled, players can activate a powerful weapon ability of their choice to create even greater devastation.

A separate mode offers wave-based survival battles in hellish arenas, where enemies become progressively more difficult. As players advance, they must make tactical choices about upgrades, gradually shaping their character into a heavily armed force of destruction. The game also includes special challenges and realm-based progression that reward stronger performance and improve leaderboard standing.

Music is a major part of the experience, with every track composed specifically for the game by a dedicated duo and supported by vocal performances from well-known metal singers. The story is presented as an epic narrative with voice-over narration from an award-winning actor. The overall structure combines fast, stylish shooting with musical synchronization, progression systems, and a dark campaign across multiple hostile worlds.
