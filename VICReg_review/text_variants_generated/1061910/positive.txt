**Metal: Hellsinger** is a fierce rhythm FPS that blends relentless demon-slaying action with the power of metal music in a brilliantly energetic way. You play as **The Unknown**, a half-human, half-demon warrior driven by vengeance, fighting through the deadliest domains of Hell on a mission to confront **the Red Judge** herself.

What makes the game stand out is its rhythm-based combat system: the more accurately you shoot on the beat, the more intense the music becomes and the more devastating your attacks grow. Every encounter rewards timing, precision, and aggression, turning each battle into a powerful fusion of combat and soundtrack. Armed with a **skull-clad blade** and an arsenal of murderous firearms, you can tear through demon hordes and, when your ultimate is charged, unleash havoc with the weapon ability of your choice.

The game also includes **Leviathan mode**, where players face increasingly difficult waves of demons in hellish arenas. As you advance, you make meaningful upgrade decisions, shaping yourself into a true machine of destruction. Beyond the core combat, **special challenges** and the **realms of Torment** offer opportunities to grow stronger and climb the leaderboards.

A major highlight is the soundtrack, which was created specifically for the game by **Two Feathers** and features vocals from metal legends such as **Serj Tankian**, **Randy Blythe**, **Alissa White-Gluz**, and others. The epic story is narrated by award-winning actor **Troy Baker**, adding even more weight to the journey through the Infernal Planes.

The setting itself is richly imagined: Hell is presented as a union of a thousand terrifying worlds, including the icy realm of **Voke** and the maddening world of **Stygia**. Developed by the experienced FPS team at **The Outsiders** and spearheaded by **David Goldfarb**, *Metal: Hellsinger* is a passionate and stylish fusion of shooter mechanics, metal music, and infernal spectacle.
