This is a complex colony simulation and roguelike set in a fully simulated, procedurally generated world. Rather than presenting only terrain or geometry, the game models the rise and fall of civilizations, along with personalities, creatures, cultures, weather systems, geology, and more. Each new world creates different conditions and challenges, making every playthrough distinct.

The player is tasked with establishing and managing a subterranean stronghold for a small people of miners and craftsmen, while trying to keep the settlement alive against threats such as starvation, hostile forces, wilderness hazards, madness, and the dead. Survival depends on careful planning, resource management, and adapting to the consequences of the world’s many interconnected systems. Failure is a regular part of play, and the game emphasizes learning from collapse before trying again.

Its simulation depth extends to combat, which includes detailed body-part interactions, material properties, aimed attacks, wrestling, pain, nausea, poison effects, and other factors. The world can also contain hundreds of animals and monsters, many of them generated anew for each world, as well as procedurally created poetry, music, instruments, and dances that the settlement’s inhabitants can learn and perform. Weather is dynamic, tracking wind, humidity, and air masses to produce fronts, clouds, storms, and blizzards, while the underground environment can include more than two hundred rock and mineral types arranged in realistic geological settings.

Construction and management are central to the experience. Players can build a fortress, set up food and drink production, craft treasures and furniture, divert water for farming, and create defensive systems to handle environmental dangers. As the settlement grows, it can develop cultural institutions such as taverns, libraries, temples, and other specialized spaces, while also supporting increasingly demanding leadership structures. The inhabitants have simulated thoughts and personalities, which adds another layer to keeping the colony stable and content.

The version described here includes visual presentation in pixel art, music by default, and in-game tutorials designed to help new players learn the basics of building and maintaining their first stronghold. It remains an ongoing project that has been expanded and updated for many years, with the stated aim of simulating as much of existence as possible.
