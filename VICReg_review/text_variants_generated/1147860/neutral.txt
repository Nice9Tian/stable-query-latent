UFO 50 is a collection of 50 single-player and multiplayer games created by the teams behind Spelunky, Downwell, and Catacomb Kids. It spans a wide range of genres, including platformers, shoot ’em ups, puzzle games, roguelites, and RPGs, with the goal of combining a familiar 8-bit aesthetic with new ideas and modern game design.

All 50 games are available from the start, and each one is a full experience rather than a minigame or microgame. The collection includes smaller arcade-style titles as well as larger open-world adventures, including a JRPG that can take many hours to complete. The games are presented as if they were created by a fictional 1980s video game company from an alternate universe, and some titles are sequels to others within the collection. Characters also appear across multiple games as part of a shared continuity.

UFO 50 emphasizes both authenticity and modernization. Each game uses a unique 32-color palette and is designed to look and sound like an 8-bit game from the 1980s, while also aiming to feel fresh and surprising for modern players. Half of the collection supports local multiplayer, including both co-op and versus play.

Examples from the collection include Campanella 2, a sequel with procedurally generated levels where players explore in a UFO, exit to enter shops, and venture into dangerous caves while learning more about the character Isabell’s past. Quibble Race is a sci-fi horse-racing game for up to three players centered on betting and bluffing, where players can also hire thugs or sponsor their own quibble to influence outcomes. Pingolf combines golf and pinball across 18 holes filled with bumpers, trampolines, and other obstacles. Seaside Drive is a car-based shoot ’em up where drifting powers up the gun. Grimstone is a streamlined JRPG set in the American Wild West after the land has been swallowed by Hell, featuring skill-based combat and the ability to avoid some overworld encounters strategically.
