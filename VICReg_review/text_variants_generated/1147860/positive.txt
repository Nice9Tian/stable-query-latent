UFO 50 is a remarkable collection of 50 single-player and multiplayer games from the creators of *Spelunky*, *Downwell*, and *Catacomb Kids*. It offers an impressive range of genres, including platformers, shoot ’em ups, puzzle games, roguelites, and RPGs, all brought together with a shared vision: to blend a familiar 8-bit aesthetic with fresh ideas and modern game design.

What makes UFO 50 especially appealing is that these are not minigames or microgames, but full, self-contained experiences. Each title runs from its opening screen to its ending credits, with some designed as compact arcade-style challenges and others expanding into larger open-world adventures, including a JRPG that can take many hours to complete. The result is a library that feels rich, varied, and genuinely substantial.

The collection is framed as an alternate-universe anthology, supposedly created by a fictional 1980s video game company that was obscure yet ahead of its time. This concept adds a wonderful sense of continuity and discovery, as some games connect to others in the set, with recurring characters and shared history woven throughout the lineup.

From the start, all 50 games are immediately available to play, making the whole collection feel like an instant library. Inspired by multicarts, retro compilations, and the joy of browsing a friend’s game shelf, UFO 50 invites players to jump between titles freely and explore at their own pace. That sense of curiosity and experimentation is a big part of the appeal.

Visually and aurally, the games aim to feel authentically 8-bit, with each one using a unique 32-color palette and careful attention to the look and sound of classic 1980s titles. At the same time, the collection is clearly designed with modern players in mind, avoiding rigid nostalgia and instead embracing surprise, variety, and inventive design.

UFO 50 also stands out for its strong multiplayer offering. Half of the collection includes local co-op or versus play, giving groups plenty to enjoy whether they want to compete in a new fighting or strategy game, or team up in a classic-style arcade challenge from an alternate timeline.

Among the many games included, several stand out. *Campanella 2* is a sequel with procedurally generated levels, letting players explore in a UFO, step out into shops, and venture into dangerous caves. Players follow the treasure-hunter Isabell, with the larger collection gradually revealing more about her past. *Quibble Race* is a clever sci-fi horse-racing game focused on betting, bluffing, and outmaneuvering opponents, with up to three players able to compete in turn-based play. *Pingolf* blends golf and pinball across 18 imaginative holes filled with bumpers, trampolines, and other obstacles, complete with a lively dot matrix display. *Seaside Drive* delivers stylish arcade action with a car-based shoot ’em up twist, where drifting across the screen powers up your gun. And *Grimstone* brings a full JRPG experience to the collection, set in a surreal Wild West where the land has been swallowed by Hell, featuring streamlined 8-bit RPG structure, skill-based combat, and strategically avoidable overworld encounters.

With 50 complete games, strong variety, thoughtful modern design, and an imaginative alternate-history premise, UFO 50 is an outstanding celebration of retro-inspired game creation. It feels both nostalgic and inventive, offering something distinctive for every kind of player.
