Empire of Sin is a strategy game set in the criminal underworld of 1920s Prohibition-era Chicago. Developed by Romero Games and Paradox Interactive, it puts players in the role of one of fourteen real and historically inspired mob bosses, including Al Capone, Stephanie St. Clair, and Goldie Garneau.

The game focuses on building and managing a criminal empire. Players assemble a gang, expand their influence, and defend their territory from rival gangs. Progress can be pursued through hustle, charm, intimidation, or force, with the goal of rising to the top of Chicago’s underworld and staying there.

Empire of Sin combines roleplaying, empire management, and turn-based combat. In the roleplaying aspect, players choose from fourteen unique bosses based on fictional and real-world gangsters. In empire management, they run the city’s underworld economy by overseeing establishments such as speakeasies and casinos, as well as supply chains, using business savvy, brutality, or notoriety to maintain control. In turn-based combat, players lead their gang in battles to defend and expand territory or send a message to opposing gangs.
