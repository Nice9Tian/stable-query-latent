Empire of Sin is a strategy game from Romero Games and Paradox Interactive that immerses players in the ruthless criminal underworld of 1920s Prohibition-era Chicago. Set against the city’s infamous gangster landscape, the game invites you to step into the shoes of one of fourteen real and historically inspired mob bosses, including Al Capone, Stephanie St. Clair, and Goldie Garneau, and fight your way to the top.

At its core, the game blends roleplay, empire management, and turn-based combat into a gritty criminal power fantasy. You begin by choosing a unique boss, then assemble a rag-tag gang, build and manage your criminal empire, and defend your territory from rival gangs. Success depends on how well you can hustle, charm, and intimidate your way through the city’s cutthroat underworld.

Empire management plays a major role in the experience. Players strategically oversee establishments such as speakeasies and casinos, manage supply chains, and shape Chicago’s underworld economy through business savvy, brutality, or rising city-wide notoriety. Every decision contributes to the growth and survival of your empire as you expand your influence across the city.

Combat is handled through turn-based battles, where you assemble your gang and take the fight to enemies to defend your turf or send a clear message to opposing crews. The result is a game that combines strategic planning, territorial control, and gangster roleplay in a distinctive Prohibition-era setting.

The available editions add even more value for players who want extra content. The Deluxe Edition includes the Mobster Pack with four recruitable and playable gangsters, the Golden Weapon Set with three exclusive weapon skins and “Romero” gun sound effects, and the Curb Stomp Execution for your boss character. The Premium Edition includes all Deluxe content, plus two post-launch expansions and Al Capone’s Fur Coat costume.

With its historical inspirations, strategic depth, and stylish criminal atmosphere, Empire of Sin offers a compelling look at the rise of an underworld empire in one of America’s most legendary eras.
