Set in a crime-ridden metropolis during the Prohibition era, this strategy game places players at the center of a ruthless underworld struggle. Taking control of one of fourteen boss characters inspired by both real and fictional gangsters, players must gather a rough crew, expand a criminal network, and protect their territory from rival groups.

The game combines role-playing, empire management, and turn-based combat. Players can build and oversee a shadow economy by managing venues, supply routes, and gambling operations, using business skill, intimidation, or sheer brutality to gain influence. As the criminal operation grows, careful strategic planning becomes essential to maintain control and outmaneuver competing factions.

Combat is handled in turns, with players assembling their gang to defend territory, push outward into new areas, or send a warning to enemies. Progression is driven by the need to hustle, negotiate, and dominate the city’s criminal landscape while staying on top of shifting threats and alliances.

The available editions add extra content, including additional recruitable gangsters, cosmetic weapon variants, special combat effects, a unique execution animation, two post-launch expansions, and an exclusive costume item.
