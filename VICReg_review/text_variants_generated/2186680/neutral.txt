Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader is a role-playing game set in the Koronus Expanse, a barely charted and dangerous region of space. Players begin their journey aboard a personal giant voidship and travel between numerous systems, exploring a vast area of the void that offers both peril and opportunity.

The game centers on building and leading a retinue. As a Rogue Trader, you do not travel alone: your companions can include righteous heroes, twisted psykers, and perfidious xenos. These characters can provide advice, fight alongside you, and help expand your power. In return, you can influence their personal stories and shape their destinies through your choices.

Player decisions are a major part of the experience. During quests, you may show mercy or disdain, remain loyal to the God-Emperor, or associate with enemies of Mankind. These choices, including those made during character creation, affect the in-game open world and the people within it. The game emphasizes that every action matters.

Combat is presented as fully fledged isometric turn-based tactical battles. Success depends on using cover, terrain, and positioning effectively, while companion abilities can be used to change the course of battle when ordinary tactics are not enough. The game is a video game adaptation of the classic Rogue Trader ruleset and offers a large number of possibilities for players to explore.
