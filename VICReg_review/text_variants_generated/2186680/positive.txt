**Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader** invites players to explore the Koronus Expanse, a vast and perilous frontier of space aboard a personal giant voidship. This barely charted region of the Imperium is presented as a dangerous but rewarding expanse, filled with hostile creatures, opportunities for profit, and the promise of discovery.

At the heart of the experience is the role of the Rogue Trader, a leader who never travels alone. Players gather a retinue of faithful heroes, twisted psykers, and perfidious xenos, each ready to journey into the darkness between the stars. These companions are not only valuable in battle, but also provide counsel and support while their personal stories unfold through the player’s guidance. By helping them through their own journeys, the player can shape their destinies and strengthen the bond of the crew.

Choice plays a central role throughout the game. As Lord-Captain, every decision matters, whether showing mercy or disdain, remaining loyal to the God-Emperor, or consorting with enemies of Mankind. Even choices made during character creation can influence the open world and the people within it, making careful planning essential.

Combat is a fully-fledged isometric, turn-based tactical system. Players can make use of cover, terrain, and smart positioning to defeat enemies, and when that is not enough, companions’ powerful abilities can turn the tide in the most desperate battles. Built as a video game adaptation of the classic Rogue Trader ruleset, the game offers a rich range of possibilities to explore.
