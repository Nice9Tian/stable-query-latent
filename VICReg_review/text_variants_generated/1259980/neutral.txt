RIDE 4 is a motorcycle racing game built around a large selection of officially licensed bikes, a wide variety of tracks, and a strong focus on realism. The game presents an experience designed for motorcycle fans, with content and systems intended to deliver detailed two-wheel racing across different conditions and event types.

The game includes hundreds of licensed bikes and dozens of tracks from around the world. These locations are described as being carefully designed with a high level of detail, using CAD data, laser scanning, and 3D scanning to achieve precision. RIDE 4 emphasizes authenticity in both its vehicles and environments.

Progression in RIDE 4 takes the player through an adventure shaped by their choices, from regional events to professional leagues. The available activities include challenging races, ability tests, track days, and a wide range of events. The game also allows players to become an official tester for well-known manufacturers.

RIDE 4 features a fully dynamic weather system and a complete day/night cycle, changing how races play out depending on the conditions. It also introduces an Endurance mode, which focuses on long races, strategy, and animated pit stops.

The AI system, called A.N.N.A. (Artificial Neural Network Agent), is based on machine learning and is designed to provide faster, smarter, and more accurate opponents. The game also offers expanded customization, letting players modify both the look and mechanics of their bikes, as well as personalize their rider’s outfit. A graphics editor allows for custom helmet, suit, and bike livery designs, which can be shared online or explored through community creations.

RIDE 4 also includes online multiplayer with dedicated servers, aiming to provide a complete and lag-free competitive experience.
