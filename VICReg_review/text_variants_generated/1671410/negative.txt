*Somerville* is presented as a sci-fi adventure built around the aftermath of catastrophe, but its premise leans heavily on familiar apocalyptic beats. You play through the collapse of a family separated in the final moments of a global conflict, trying to find the whereabouts of your loved ones after an alien invasion. Rather than offering a broad heroic fantasy, the game focuses on the intimate consequences of large-scale destruction, though the setup itself is not especially original.

The gameplay promises exploration, learning, and communication as you move through the devastation left behind by the invading alien force and the people who resisted it. According to the store page, progression is tied to uncovering more about the aliens’ motives and methods, which sounds more like environmental storytelling than a deeply layered narrative system. The core features are sparse: solving puzzles in an altered landscape covered in alien sediment, fleeing or hiding from dangerous encounters, and observing alien entities to learn from them. That suggests a slow, cautious experience, but also one that may rely on limited interaction rather than robust mechanics.

The page also mentions multiple endings, implying some branching outcomes, though it does not explain how meaningful those differences are. In practice, this can easily feel like a thin reward structure if the path to those endings is mostly linear. Overall, *Somerville* seems intent on delivering a moody, cinematic survival adventure, but the available information makes it sound more restrained than truly expansive.
