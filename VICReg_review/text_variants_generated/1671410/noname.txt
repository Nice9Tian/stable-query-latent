This sci-fi adventure follows a family struggling to reunite in the aftermath of a catastrophic alien invasion. Set against the backdrop of a global conflict, the story focuses on the personal consequences of large-scale destruction rather than on the conflict alone, showing how one family is separated in the final moments of the disaster.

The experience centers on exploring a devastated landscape altered by alien influence, while uncovering more about both the invading force and those who resisted it. As the journey progresses, players learn about the motives and methods behind the invasion through observation and discovery.

The gameplay emphasizes exploration, puzzle-solving, and survival. Players must solve puzzles in an environment covered in alien residue, and may need to flee, hide, or negotiate when facing dangerous encounters. Careful observation is important, as every alien entity may reveal useful information. The story also includes multiple endings, encouraging players to dig deeper to understand their meaning and significance.

Overall, it is presented as an independent work focused on delivering a distinctive interactive experience, combining emotional storytelling with environmental exploration and puzzle-driven gameplay.
