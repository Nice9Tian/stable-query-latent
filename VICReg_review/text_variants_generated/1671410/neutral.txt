Somerville is a sci-fi adventure game focused on the personal consequences of a large-scale alien conflict. Set in the aftermath of a catastrophic invasion, it follows a young family that has been separated in the final moments of a global crisis, and centers on the effort to find them again.

The game presents its story through the perspective of intimate struggle rather than battlefield spectacle. As you travel through the altered world, you explore the devastation left behind by both the invading alien force and those who resisted. Along the way, you learn more about the motives and methods of the alien civilization and observe the broader impact of the event on ordinary lives.

Gameplay emphasizes exploration, observation, and interaction. Players solve puzzles in an alien-altered landscape, navigate dangerous encounters by fleeing, hiding, or negotiating, and study alien entities to understand their behavior. The game also features multiple endings, with the player digging deeper into the events and their meaning as the journey progresses.

Somerville is developed by Jumpship, an independent studio founded by Chris Olsen in collaboration with Dino Patti, former CEO and co-founder of Playdead. The studio describes its goal as creating distinctive experiences that treat people with respect and expand the definition of interactive media.
