**Somerville** is a striking sci-fi adventure that places human emotion at the heart of a large-scale alien invasion. In the aftermath of catastrophe, you set out to reunite your family and uncover the personal cost of a global conflict. Rather than focusing only on spectacle, the game emphasizes the intimate repercussions of war and the struggle of a young family separated in its final moments.

The journey centers on **family**, **survival**, and **discovery**. As you search for your loved ones, you move through a devastated world shaped by hostile alien forces and those who resisted them. Along the way, you begin to understand more about the motives and methods behind the invasion, seeing firsthand how this catastrophic event has affected others.

Gameplay is built around **exploration, learning, and communication**. You explore an altered landscape covered in alien sediment, solving puzzles and navigating dangerous encounters. Depending on the situation, you may need to **flee, hide, or negotiate**, making every encounter feel tense and meaningful. A key part of the experience is observing and learning from each alien entity, gradually uncovering the logic of this strange world.

Somerville also offers **multiple endings**, encouraging players to dig deeper into the journey and reflect on their meaning and significance. With its thoughtful premise and emotionally grounded design, it stands out as a unique interactive experience that blends cinematic sci-fi atmosphere with puzzle-solving and survival-driven storytelling.
