Roots of Pacha is a relaxed farming simulation set in an ancient world, offering a fresh take on the genre. Players gather resources, farm, fish, craft tools, and explore a peaceful but mysterious setting filled with biomes, cave secrets, new flora and fauna, and mini-games. The game centers on building life with your Clan: contributing to village progress, developing tools, items, and buildings, and watching the community expand over time.

Progress comes through cooperation and shared effort, whether playing single-player or in co-op with friends. Along the way, players can befriend and domesticate animals, breed them for goods or keep them as pets, process and preserve food, and customize their character, home, and farm. Social activities also play an important role, with festivals, friendships, romance, and the possibility of starting a family.

Roots of Pacha emphasizes a calm pace and flexible progression. Seasons shape life in the world, but there is no need to rush, as ideas and prophecies can be completed in any season. Overall, the game focuses on exploration, community growth, and personal expression in a tranquil prehistoric setting.
