Set in an ancient world, this cozy farming sim offers a calm, open-ended experience built around gathering, farming, exploration, and community growth. Players can explore a hand-crafted world full of biomes, new plants and animals, mini-games, and cave mysteries while progressing at their own pace. The game emphasizes a relaxed rhythm, allowing all ideas and goals to be completed in any season without pressure.

Core gameplay revolves around managing crops, collecting resources, fishing, and learning how to process and preserve food. Players can also craft tools, items, and buildings to help develop their settlement and shape the surrounding society. Animals play an important role as well: they can be domesticated and bred either for useful goods or simply kept as pets.

Community is central to the experience. Players contribute to their clan to unlock new ideas and help the village expand over time. The game supports both single-player and cooperative play with friends, and it encourages cooperation as the primary way to strengthen the group and move forward. Alongside village development, players can attend festivals, build friendships, find a romantic partner, start a family, and customize their character, home, and farm to create a personal story.

Overall, this is a peaceful life simulation focused on exploration, social bonds, and gradual progress in a long-forgotten world.
