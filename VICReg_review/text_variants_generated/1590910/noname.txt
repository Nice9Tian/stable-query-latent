This first-person shooter presents a distinctive retro horror comic aesthetic inspired by classic dark literature. The player takes on the role of the last person with full senses, setting out to find answers and relief in a world shaped by dread, madness, and occult forces. The experience blends 90s-style FPS design with hand-drawn 2D visuals placed in a 3D environment, creating a look that feels like a moving comic book.

A central mechanic is a dynamic madness system that changes during play. This madness level affects both the audiovisual presentation and gameplay, and it can also grant additional power. Players must manage their humanity while using scripture-like tools in one hand and weapons fueled by an unknown substance in the other. Combat is built around customizing active abilities to match personal play style, evolving equipment, and facing waves of unpredictable enemies.

The game emphasizes classic shooter elements such as health packs, life points, non-reloading weapons, power-ups, and fast battles against numerous opponents. Levels include hidden and hard-to-reach areas, encouraging exploration and story discovery. The world is entirely hand-made and features a variety of climactic stages and enemy types, from possessed inhabitants to nightmarish monsters.

Players can choose between two protagonists, each with a separate progression tree, and experience the story from either a cleric-like figure or a reporter-like figure. Across the journey, the player uncovers the full narrative while navigating an atmosphere of confusion, hopelessness, and relentless horror.
