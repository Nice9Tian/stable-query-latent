*Forgive Me Father* is a striking retro horror FPS that blends the spirit of 90s shooters with the grim atmosphere of Lovecraftian fiction. Set in a hand-crafted world filled with dread, occult terror, and hidden secrets, the game casts you as the last person still in possession of full senses, setting out on a desperate journey to find answers and relief in a place consumed by madness.

One of the game’s most distinctive features is its dynamic madness system. As your madness rises and falls during play, it not only changes the audio-visual presentation but also affects gameplay itself, granting additional power while shifting the experience in meaningful ways. This creates a constant tension between preserving your humanity and embracing the strength needed to survive.

Combat is fast, intense, and deeply customizable. Armed with scripture in one hand and weapons fueled by an unknown substance in the other, you face hordes of unpredictable enemies while shaping your equipment and active skills to match your preferred play style. The game emphasizes classic FPS elements such as first aid kits, life points, non-reloading weapons, powerups, and dynamic battles against numerous opponents, all presented through timelapse animations and a unique 2D-in-3D visual approach.

The comic book presentation is a major highlight. Hand-drawn visuals ensure that nearly any frame could belong in a horror comic, reinforcing the game’s identity and making every environment feel vivid and memorable. This style pairs perfectly with the dark mood inspired by H.P. Lovecraft’s novels, where hopelessness, confusion, and the need to restore sanity drive the entire journey.

Exploration also plays an important role, with hidden and hard-to-reach areas scattered throughout the world, encouraging players to uncover the full story and solve the mystery of this twisted place. The game offers many climactic levels and a varied bestiary, including possessed inhabitants and nightmarish monsters, all created by hand to enrich the experience.

Players can also choose between two heroes: a priest or a journalist, each with a unique development tree. This adds another layer of replayability and lets you experience the story from two different perspectives. *Forgive Me Father* is a confident, atmospheric, and highly original FPS that stands out through its comic-book style, clever madness mechanics, and faithful Lovecraftian horror tone.
