Forgive Me Father is a first-person shooter set in a Lovecraft-inspired world, presented in a distinctive retro horror comic book style. The game follows a journey undertaken by the only person left with full senses, as they search for answers and relief while facing an eternal evil and uncovering the mystery behind a strange, corrupted place.

The game is designed to evoke the feel of a classic 1990s FPS, with features such as timelapse animations, 2D elements within a 3D environment, health pickups, non-reloading weapons, and power-ups that appear during dynamic combat against large numbers of enemies. Combat is shaped by a custom progression system in which the player chooses how equipment evolves and uses active skills based on their preferred play style.

A central mechanic is the dynamic madness level, which changes during gameplay and affects both the audiovisual presentation and the gameplay itself. As madness increases or shifts, it grants additional power, making it an important part of the overall experience. The player must balance this force while trying to preserve their humanity in the face of occult threats and unpredictable enemies.

The world is built by hand and includes hidden and hard-to-reach areas for exploration. Players are encouraged to uncover the full story as they move through varied levels and confront a diverse bestiary, from possessed inhabitants to monstrous creatures drawn from nightmares. The game also offers two playable heroes: a priest and a journalist, each with a unique development tree, allowing the story to be experienced from different perspectives.
