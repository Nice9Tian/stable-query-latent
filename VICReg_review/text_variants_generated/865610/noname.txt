This narrative adventure presents a bleak yet intimate post-noir mystery set in a dystopian urban landscape inhabited by anthropomorphic animals. The player takes on the role of a raccoon private investigator, drawn into a deeply personal case that gradually becomes a story about change, identity, and the limits of personal agency. Rather than offering a heroic power fantasy, the experience centers on an ordinary detective forced to confront uncomfortable truths in a world where transformation is constant but progress is uncertain.

The game is structured as a linear, cinematic journey that places storytelling and emotional expression at the forefront. Its dialogue system is rich and expansive, drawing inspiration from classic computer role-playing games, and allows players to shape the protagonist’s identity through conversation choices. These decisions influence what kind of person the detective becomes, even as the broader trajectory remains tightly focused on narrative development. Exploration also plays an important role, with players moving through detailed environments and interacting with a varied cast of characters while searching for answers.

The setting combines noir atmosphere with weird science fiction, post-Soviet visual influences, and modern political undertones, creating a world that feels both familiar and unsettling. The city is divided into contrasting districts, ranging from neglected and oppressed areas to places with traces of prosperity and life. This contrast reinforces the game’s themes of despair, resilience, and the possibility of meaningful human connection in an uncaring world. The story explores the pain of existence, loss, and the difficult task of deciding what kind of future, if any, can be built.

Visually, the game uses high-resolution pixel art enhanced with 3D effects such as dynamic lighting, heavy rain, volumetric fog, and neon illumination. These elements give the city a vivid, cinematic quality and help establish its atmosphere of decay and uneasy beauty. The soundtrack also plays a major role, blending doom jazz with electronic music to create an immersive and memorable audio landscape that supports the narrative tone. Together, the presentation and mechanics form a focused experience designed to subvert genre expectations while encouraging reflection on identity, connection, and change.
