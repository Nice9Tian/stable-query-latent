*Tails Noir* is a striking post-noir narrative adventure that places you in the paws of Howard Lotor, a raccoon private eye drawn into a case far bigger and more personal than he could have expected. Set in a dystopian Vancouver inhabited by anthropomorphic animals, the game delivers a deeply cinematic experience about change, identity, loss, and what it means to be a person in a world that offers little certainty.

At its core, *Tails Noir* is a linear story-driven journey that puts storytelling and emotional expression first. Rather than focusing on action or spectacle, it emphasizes rich dialogue, meaningful character interactions, and thoughtful exploration. Inspired by classic film noir, weird sci-fi, post-Soviet aesthetics, modern political regimes, the works of Lynch and Kaufman, and existential philosophy, it creates a distinctive atmosphere that both honors and subverts genre traditions.

As Howard, you will build genuine connections with a diverse cast of characters while searching for the truth behind a troubling mystery. Dialogue choices let you shape Howard’s identity and decide what kind of person he becomes by the end of the story. The journey takes you through a strange yet familiar version of Vancouver, where you can explore both bleak, downtrodden districts and more flourishing areas of the city. Through it all, the game asks you to confront the universal pain of existence and loss, while still holding onto the possibility of hope and change.

The world of *Tails Noir* is one of danger, uncertainty, and melancholy, but it is also full of warmth in the connections that emerge along the way. Its beautiful presentation makes this setting even more memorable, combining high-resolution pixel art with 3D effects such as dynamic lighting, pouring rain, volumetric fog, and neon glow. Iconic Vancouver locations like Gastown, Granville, and the Fairmont Hotel are reimagined with impressive style in this animal-filled cityscape.

Completing the experience is an evocative original soundtrack by Danshin and Arooj Aftab. Blending classic jazz with electronic music, the doom jazz score adds depth, atmosphere, and emotional power to every moment. The result is a game that feels cinematic, expressive, and unforgettable.

*Tails Noir* stands out as a bold and thoughtful narrative adventure—one that invites you to investigate, reflect, and decide what kind of detective, and what kind of person, Howard Lotor will ultimately become.
