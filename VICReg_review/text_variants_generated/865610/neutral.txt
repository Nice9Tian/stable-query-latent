Tails Noir is a post-noir narrative adventure set in a dystopian version of Vancouver inhabited by anthropomorphic animals. The player takes on the role of Howard Lotor, a raccoon private eye drawn into an especially difficult case that becomes deeply personal. The story presents Howard as someone with no heroic status or special destiny, but as a detective forced to confront change, identity, loss, and the inevitability of the end.

The game is strongly focused on storytelling and emotional expression. It is described as a linear, cinematic narrative experience that subverts genre expectations and explores what it means to be a person. Gameplay centers on rich dialogue, exploration, and making choices that shape Howard’s identity. Through conversations inspired by old school CRPGs, players form meaningful connections with a diverse cast of characters, decide how Howard responds to events, and determine which version of him reaches the story’s conclusion.

Tails Noir’s world is a bleak but not hopeless post-noir dystopia. Its Vancouver includes both downtrodden and flourishing districts, and the city is portrayed as dangerous, cold, and uncaring, yet still marked by genuine human-like connections and a desire for change. The game draws inspiration from film noir, weird sci-fi, post-Soviet aesthetics, modern political regimes, the works of Lynch and Kaufman, and existential philosophy.

Visually, the game combines high-resolution pixel art with 3D effects such as dynamic lighting, rain, volumetric fog, and neon illumination. Players can visit reimagined Vancouver locations including Gastown, Granville, and the Fairmont Hotel. The soundtrack is an original doom jazz score by Danshin and Arooj Aftab, blending classic jazz and electronic music to support the game’s cinematic atmosphere.

Overall, Tails Noir is a narrative-driven experience about investigation, identity, and transformation, centered on exploration, dialogue choices, and a richly stylized world.
