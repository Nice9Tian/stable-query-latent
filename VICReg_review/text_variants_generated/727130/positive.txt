Between the Stars is a compelling space action game that blends RPG growth with real-time combat, putting you in command of an interstellar cruiser on a mission to protect civilization from the Children of the Sun, a hostile faction expanding from the border planets. The journey carries you across the galaxy, where every warp jump brings new challenges, meaningful decisions, and opportunities to shape the fate of your ship, crew, and the universe around you.

At the heart of the experience are intense real-time space battles, where timing your ship’s special abilities can turn the tide in your favor. As you progress, you can unlock, upgrade, personalize, and purchase different playable ships and their systems, creating a powerful fleet-ready vessel suited to your preferred style of play. The game also lets you create a captain and develop them throughout the adventure, leveling up and earning attributes based on the decisions you make along the way.

The galaxy of Between the Stars is rich with procedural variety, offering an ever-changing stellar map filled with events, space stations, and planets spread across multiple sectors. Each journey feels dynamic, with hundreds of text-based events testing both your judgment and your crew’s resilience against countless dangers. You can hire, upgrade, and safeguard your crew, helping them grow stronger while managing casualties and keeping your team ready for whatever the universe throws at you.

Combat and progression are further deepened through crafting, salvaging, and investigating new weaponry to strengthen your offensive capabilities. Decisions made during events can influence the entire playthrough, giving each choice real weight and making every run feel distinctive. For players seeking flexibility, the game offers both a standard difficulty mode and a permadeath option for a more demanding challenge. Between the Stars stands out as an ambitious and rewarding interstellar adventure built on strategy, growth, and high-stakes exploration.
