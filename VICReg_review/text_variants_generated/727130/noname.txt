**Eclipse Vanguard** is a space action game with RPG progression and real-time combat that puts players in command of an interstellar cruiser. The journey centers on defending civilization from a hostile faction that controls border worlds and aims to spread its influence further. To carry out this mission, players travel across the galaxy, encountering new challenges at every warp jump while making difficult choices that can alter the ship, the crew, and the surrounding universe.

Combat is fast-paced and takes place in real time, with special ship abilities that must be used at the right moment to gain an advantage. Players can unlock, upgrade, personalize, and purchase different playable ships and their systems as they work toward galactic dominance. A captain can be created and developed over the course of the adventure, leveling up and gaining different attributes based on player decisions.

The game features a stellar map with procedurally generated events, along with space stations and planets spread across multiple sectors. Crew management is a key part of the experience: players can hire and upgrade crew members, keep them alive, cover losses, and improve them over time to meet the dangers ahead. The game also includes hundreds of text-based events that test both the captain and the crew against a wide range of threats.

Players can craft, salvage, and research new weaponry to strengthen offensive capabilities. Choices made during one event may influence the course of the entire playthrough. For added challenge, the game offers both a standard difficulty mode and a permadeath option.
