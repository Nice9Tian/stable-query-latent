Trek to Yomi is a cinematic action game centered on Hiroki, a young swordsman who swears to protect his town and the people he loves in honor of his dying master. After tragedy strikes and duty binds him to his promise, he must journey beyond life and death, confront himself, and determine the path he will take forward.

The game emphasizes a cinematic presentation, using dramatic camera angles and striking visuals inspired by classic samurai films to create a strong movie-like experience. Its combat is described as stylish and streamlined, built around traditional samurai weapons as Hiroki fights both vicious swordsmen and supernatural beings. The story follows his fall against evil and his heroic return as he attempts to make good on the promise he failed to keep.

Trek to Yomi also features a soundtrack designed to evoke the atmosphere of feudal Japan, supporting both the action and the more somber moments of the journey.
