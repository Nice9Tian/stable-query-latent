*SOUTH PARK: SNOW DAY!* is a lively new chapter in the journey of the New Kid, brought to life by South Park Digital Studios. Set during a massive blizzard that has thrown South Park into chaos and, more importantly, canceled school, the game celebrates the most magical day in any young child’s life: a snow day. Players join Cartman, Stan, Kyle, and Kenny in full 3D for an all-new adventure packed with the sharp humor and energy the series is known for.

At its core, *SOUTH PARK: SNOW DAY!* is a chaotic, action-packed co-op game where players can team up with up to three friends, use matchmaking, or play solo alongside ally bots. The journey takes place across the snow-piled, snow-covered town of South Park, where warring factions and an endless winter stand between the New Kid and the safety of the town. The game emphasizes fast-paced combat, letting players fight through hordes of enemies and battle epic bosses in a story where they make the rules.

Combat is built around a range of upgradable melee and ranged weapons, along with special abilities and powers that can be deployed to devastating effect. Players can equip unique weapons, unlock upgrades, and unleash coordinated attacks with allies for an especially powerful cooperative experience. The Season Pass expands the action even further with six DLCs in total, including an all-new game mode with hellish new rules and special challenges, a new ranged weapon called the Snowball, a new melee weapon to be announced later, a new weapon variation for the Daggers, and cosmetic packs such as the CRED pack and the Asspen-themed pack.

Customization is also a major highlight. Players can outfit their New Kid with a wide variety of iconic cosmetics and combinations, from beanies and Cheesy Poof T-shirts to chin balls, creating endless possibilities for a personalized look. With its cooperative gameplay, upgraded weapon systems, bold cosmetic options, and a fresh South Park story set against a massive snowstorm, *SOUTH PARK: SNOW DAY!* offers a fun and energetic way to enjoy a day without school.
