This 3D cooperative action game places players in a snow-covered town thrown into chaos by an overwhelming blizzard. The story follows a customizable young protagonist joining a group of familiar companions to confront warring factions, endless winter, and a world-spanning crisis, all while the town’s schools remain closed for the day.

The game supports up to four participants total, with online matchmaking available and the option to play solo alongside AI allies. Combat is described as fast-paced and frenetic, built around coordinated attacks, upgradable special abilities, and a mix of melee and ranged weapons. Players can equip and improve these weapons over time, unlocking new attack options, support effects, and distinct playstyles through upgrade cards.

A special edition expands the experience with additional downloadable content. Its included season content introduces a new game mode that alters the rules and adds harsher challenges, as well as new weapon types, a weapon variation that reworks an existing dagger-based loadout, and cosmetic packs centered on themed outfits and accessories. Some of these additions are planned as part of a seasonal rollout, with certain items arriving later than others.

Customization is a major feature, letting players personalize their character with a wide range of cosmetic items and combinations. Visual options include hairstyles, clothing, and accessories, allowing for extensive character expression alongside the combat-focused progression. Overall, the game combines cooperative action, weapon upgrades, class-like tactical variety, and heavy character customization within a humorous winter-themed adventure.
