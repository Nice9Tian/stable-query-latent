SOUTH PARK: SNOW DAY! is a 3D co-op action game from South Park Digital Studios, set in a snow-covered South Park after a massive blizzard has thrown the town into chaos and canceled school. Players join Cartman, Stan, Kyle, Kenny, and the New Kid in an all-new story centered on surviving the storm and saving the town from an endless winter.

The game supports up to three players in cooperative play, with matchmaking available, and can also be played solo with ally bots. Its combat is described as frenetic and action-packed, featuring battles against warring factions across the snow-piled town. Players equip and upgrade melee and ranged weapons, use special abilities, and face hordes of enemies as well as epic bosses.

A key part of the game is character customization. Players can use a wide range of iconic cosmetics to personalize the New Kid with many combinations, including items such as beanies, Cheesy Poof T-shirts, and chin balls. The game also emphasizes unique weapons and upgradable special powers that support varied combat approaches.

The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the base game plus a Season Pass with six DLCs in total. These additions include an all-new game mode with new rules and special challenges, an all-new ranged weapon called the Snowball, and a new melee weapon announced later in the season. Each new weapon includes six new upgrade cards and unique cosmetics. The Season Pass also adds a new weapon variation for the Daggers, which replaces some weapon upgrade cards with new powers that enable different play styles and tactics.

In addition, the Season Pass includes cosmetic packs. The CRED cosmetic pack contains a fresh hairstyle, a CRED hoodie, and a cape, while an Asspen-themed cosmetic pack with new jackets, hats, and other items is planned for later in the season.
