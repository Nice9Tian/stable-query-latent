**Twelve Minutes** is a gripping real-time top-down interactive thriller that stands out with its accessible click-and-drag interface and intense, tightly focused premise. Featuring the voices of James McAvoy, Daisy Ridley, and Willem Dafoe, the game begins with what should be a romantic evening with your wife, only to plunge suddenly into a terrifying nightmare.

A police detective bursts into your home, accuses your wife of murder, and brutally beats you to death. But death is only the beginning: you are instantly thrown back to the exact moment you opened the front door, trapped in a twelve-minute time loop and forced to relive the same horror again and again. The challenge is to use everything you learn from each cycle to alter the outcome and ultimately break free.

With its blend of dream-like tension, claustrophobic pressure, and fragmented storytelling, **Twelve Minutes** evokes the atmosphere of **The Shining**, the confinement of **Rear Window**, and the nonlinear structure of **Memento**. It delivers a tense and stylish experience built around observation, experimentation, and the thrill of unraveling a mystery one loop at a time.
