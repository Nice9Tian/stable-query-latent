*Twelve Minutes* is a real-time, top-down interactive thriller with an accessible click-and-drag interface. The game features performances from James McAvoy, Daisy Ridley, and Willem Dafoe.

The story begins with what should be a romantic evening at home with your wife, but the situation quickly turns into a nightmare when a police detective breaks into the apartment, accuses your wife of murder, and beats you to death. After this, you are sent back to the exact moment you opened the front door, trapped in a twelve-minute time loop and forced to experience the same events repeatedly.

The central gameplay premise is to use knowledge gained from each loop to alter events and try to break the cycle. By learning what is about to happen, the player must search for a way to change the outcome and escape the repeating sequence of terror.

*Twelve Minutes* combines the dream-like tension of *The Shining*, the claustrophobic atmosphere of *Rear Window*, and the fragmented structure of *Memento*.
