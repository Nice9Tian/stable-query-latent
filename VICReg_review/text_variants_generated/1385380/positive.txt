Across the Obelisk is a standout roguelite deckbuilding adventure that invites players to team up, strategize, and reshape each run through smart decisions and tactical play. Set in the troubled realm of Senenthia, the game casts you on a high-stakes quest to rescue the king’s daughter from the treacherous Lord Hanshek, with your party’s fate and the fate of the kingdom resting in your hands.

At its core, the game blends deep deckbuilding with party-based strategy. Players can build decks from more than 500 cards, use over 300 items, and choose from 16 heroes spread across four different classes. Each hero contributes to a party of four, and you can either control the entire team yourself or join forces with 2–4 players in online co-op. The result is a tactical experience where class combinations, card choices, and item synergies open the door to fresh strategies and new ways to play.

One of Across the Obelisk’s greatest strengths is its replayability. Procedurally generated events ensure that no two journeys are the same, and your choices shape how events, quests, and story moments unfold. The game encourages careful planning, but it also rewards adaptability, since each path through Senenthia presents different risks, opportunities, and consequences. Every run becomes a matter of weighing who to help, what to pursue, and how to guide the party through uncertainty.

The game also offers several ways to play. Beyond the main adventure, there is Obelisk mode, a tougher roguelike challenge where players draft unique decks and face a fully randomized journey that demands constant readiness. Weekly Challenge mode adds another layer of competition by giving all players the same predetermined deck and map, making success a matter of skill and execution. With three in-depth game modes in total, Across the Obelisk provides plenty of reasons to keep returning.

Cooperation is woven into the experience, and it extends across platforms as well. Crossplay allows players to team up regardless of platform, and Story Packs are shared with the whole party across all platforms, so friends can follow along on the adventure even if they do not own the same DLCs. While DLC heroes are not shared between players, co-op support remains flexible and welcoming. The game is available on Windows, Mac, Linux, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, and supports English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German, and French.

With its rich deckbuilding systems, tactical party combat, meaningful choices, and abundant replay value, Across the Obelisk delivers a compelling and rewarding adventure. It is a game that celebrates experimentation, teamwork, and clever planning, making every run through Senenthia feel engaging, dynamic, and worth taking again.
