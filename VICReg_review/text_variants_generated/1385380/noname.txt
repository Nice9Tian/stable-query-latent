In this cooperative roguelite deckbuilding adventure, players assemble a party of four heroes and set out across a troubled realm to complete a perilous rescue mission. The experience blends strategic card construction, tactical battles, and branching events, with each run shaped by procedural generation and player decisions. Choices made during the journey can alter how the story unfolds, affecting who is helped, who is left behind, and how each path through the realm develops.

The game centers on building and refining decks from a large pool of customizable content, including hundreds of cards, numerous items, and multiple hero types spread across several classes. As characters gain experience, players unlock perk points and upgrade their decks to enable stronger combinations. Party composition matters, since different class pairings can create new play styles, whether emphasizing direct force or more specialized approaches. Combat is designed to reward coordination, especially in online cooperative play for two to four players, though the game can also be played with full control of the party by a single player.

Progress is structured around four acts, each ending in a boss encounter, while random events and quests add uncertainty along the route. Players must weigh practical goals against moral choices, such as whether to aid the people encountered along the way or pursue better rewards. This makes each journey distinct, with no two runs unfolding in exactly the same way. The game emphasizes replayability through three substantial modes. One mode offers a more randomized roguelike challenge with drafted decks and unpredictable conditions, another features a predetermined deck and map for a competitive weekly test, and the main campaign focuses on story progression and deck growth.

Multiplayer support extends across several platforms, including PC systems and current-generation consoles, with cross-platform cooperative play available through account linking. Shared story content allows a group to experience adventure content owned by one player, though hero access tied to certain downloadable content is not shared. Regular updates and additional content are intended to keep the experience evolving over time, reinforcing its emphasis on experimentation, adaptation, and repeated playthroughs.
