Across the Obelisk is a roguelite deckbuilding game centered on party-based adventure, tactical combat, and co-op play. Players build powerful decks, manage a team of four heroes, and work through a quest to rescue the king’s daughter from the treacherous Lord Hanshek. The game emphasizes choice and consequence, with decisions affecting how events and story sequences unfold.

The game supports Windows, Mac, Linux, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, with language support including English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German, and French. Online co-op is available for 2 to 4 players, and players can also control the full party alone. Cross-platform co-op is supported, although a Paradox Account is required for play across platforms to manage muting and reporting. Players do not need to own the same DLCs to play together: Story Packs are shared with the whole party across platforms, but DLC heroes are not shared.

Gameplay combines deckbuilding, class-based party composition, and procedurally generated events. There are more than 500 cards, over 300 items, and 16 heroes spread across four classes. Players can customize and upgrade decks by earning XP and unlocking perk points, then use those builds in tactical battles against enemies and bosses. The game includes four acts, each with its own boss, and random events that add variety to each run. Different class combinations create different play styles, ranging from strong frontline warriors to more sneaky or tactical approaches.

Across the Obelisk offers several game modes designed for replayability. Obelisk mode provides a tougher roguelike challenge with unique drafted decks and randomized adventures, while weekly challenge mode gives all players the same predetermined deck and map, making success depend on skill. Regular updates and content drops are also part of the game’s ongoing support, reinforcing its focus on experimentation, repeated runs, and varied strategies.
