BlazBlue Entropy Effect is a roguelite action game focused on fast, stylish combat and flexible character building. It features charismatic character designs with distinct aesthetic styles, dramatic animations, smooth controls, and realistic combat feedback, all aimed at delivering an intense action experience.

The game offers ten playable characters, each with a unique combat style, personality, and immersive voice acting. Players can build around hundreds of moves, along with numerous elemental effects and legacy skills that can be passed between characters. This creates a wide range of combat combinations and allows for extensive experimentation in shaping a fighter.

Set in a future where the world has fallen into chaos, the story follows people entering a cyberspace called ACE in search of a way to resolve the crisis. The narrative centers on a truth known only to the player, with choices that are said to affect humanity’s future. The game also notes that it contains flashing visual elements that may trigger photosensitive epilepsy.
