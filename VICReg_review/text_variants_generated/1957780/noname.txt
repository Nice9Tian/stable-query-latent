This VR extraction shooter presents a hard-core survival experience built around staying alive, looting efficiently, and escaping dangerous combat zones. Players must manage survival systems such as health, hunger, and thirst, using food and medical items to recover and remain active in the field. The game combines exploration, scavenging, and shootouts across a variety of multiplayer maps, where both human opponents and armed AI threats can appear at the same time.

The core loop centers on entering hostile areas, collecting supplies, and choosing when to engage or avoid combat. Loot includes gear, weapons, attachments, ammunition, medical supplies, food, and water, and players can move through these locations alone or with a squad. Each run requires careful planning, since survival depends on reaching an extraction point with both life and equipment intact.

Progression is driven by trading, crafting, and upgrading. Players can buy and sell through vendors and player-to-player trading, earn money, and unlock better gear. Weapons and equipment can be customized, while ammunition can also be crafted in different types and sizes. A marketplace offers access to needed supplies, and missions from vendors provide access to special goods.

The game also emphasizes long-term collection and personalization. A safehouse can be developed into a personal showcase, with an armory used to display collected weapons, armor, and gear. The weapon selection ranges from handguns to assault rifles, supporting different loadouts and playstyles. Whether approaching raids aggressively or taking a stealth-focused route, the experience is built for repeated runs and varied strategies within large VR environments.
