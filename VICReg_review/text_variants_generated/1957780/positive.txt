Ghosts of Tabor is a hardcore extraction-based VR FPS PvPvE survival game that puts survival, strategy, and precision at the center of every raid. In a dangerous world inspired by the genre’s best-known extraction shooters, players must rely on their wits, skills, and resources to stay alive while facing both other players and armed AI.

The game features survival systems that demand constant attention, including health, hunger, and thirst. You must eat and heal to keep going, making every decision in the field matter. Across a variety of maps designed for multiplayer exploration, looting, and intense shoot-outs, you can engage in fast-paced firefights, long-range sniping, or careful scavenging as you push toward extraction.

Ghosts of Tabor offers a strong sense of progression through trading, crafting, and weapon customization. You can deal with vendors and P2P trading to earn money, acquire better gear, and build equipment. The game also lets you craft ammo in different types and sizes, customize weapons, and complete missions for vendors to unlock special goods. Its arsenal spans a wide range of weapons, from handguns to assault rifles, giving you plenty of options to shape your loadout.

Your safehouse becomes a personal showcase as well, with a large armory where you can display your weapons, armor, and gear collection. This adds a rewarding layer of persistence between raids, turning your growing arsenal into a visible reflection of your progress.

The game’s environments are a major highlight, including maps like Island of Tabor and Missile Silo, both presented as part of its closed beta launch. With simultaneous PvP and PvE, a wide variety of loot such as attachments, ammo, medical supplies, food, and water, and the ability to squad up or go solo, Ghosts of Tabor delivers a highly replayable VR survival experience. Whether you prefer a guns-blazing squad approach or a stealthy lone-wolf style, the game gives you the freedom to choose how you survive and escape.
