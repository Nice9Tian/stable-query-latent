This co-op horror game centers on a group of players trying to become famous by filming frightening events and uploading the footage for views. Up to four players can team up, use voice chat, and customize their faces with an ASCII-based editor before heading out with gear purchased from the in-game store.

The core loop revolves around descending into a dangerous underground area through a diving device and exploring a place referred to as the Old World. There, players encounter physics-driven monsters, cursed relics, and other mysterious artefacts. The goal is to capture as many scary moments as possible before the team runs out of oxygen, camera battery, or group members. If players survive, they must return to the diving device and bring back whoever remains.

Once back on the surface, the footage is uploaded to a fictional video platform, where players can watch their recordings and wait for the views to increase. Each run lasts three days, and the more unsettling content the team records, the more attention the videos attract. The resulting ad revenue can then be spent on upgrades that improve survival and video quality, including better equipment, emotes, props, medical items, and many flashlights. The game also includes sponsorship opportunities that provide a separate currency for purchasing island upgrades and hats.

In addition to the main co-op experience, the game supports saving in-game recordings and emphasizes the chaotic behavior of players and the funny situations that can emerge during a run. If the team gathers enough views within the three-day period, the career continues, offering the chance to keep building fame and fortune.
