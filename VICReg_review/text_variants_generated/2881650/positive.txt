**Content Warning** is a vibrant co-op horror game that turns fear into fame in a clever and highly entertaining way. In this online experience, you and up to three friends head into the mysterious Old World with one goal: film as much spooky, chaotic, and outrageous content as possible and make it go viral on SpöökTube.

The game begins with a playful preparation phase where players can squad up, customize their faces using the ASCII face customiser, and buy useful gear before descending into the depths with a diving bell. Once underground, the Old World becomes a tense and unpredictable space filled with scary physics-animated monsters, cursed relics, and other strange artefacts. The core challenge is to capture as many frightening moments on camera as possible before running out of oxygen, camera battery, or even friends.

What makes **Content Warning** especially engaging is how it blends horror with comedy and teamwork. Players must survive long enough to return to the diving bell, get back to the surface, and upload their chaotic footage to SpöökTube. After that, the game shifts into a watch-party style moment as everyone waits to see the views climb. Each run lasts three days, and the more scary footage you record, the more viral your team becomes. If you earn enough views within that time, you can continue your SpöökTube career and chase the promise of fame and fortune.

Progression is tightly tied to your success as a creator. The ad revenue from uploaded videos can be spent on upgraded gear, emotes, props, medical equipment, and other helpful items that improve both filming and survival. The game also supports sponsorship deals, allowing players to earn MC for island upgrades and hats, adding even more personality and long-term goals to the experience.

With features like in-game handheld video camera support, 2–4 player online co-op, voice chat, saved recordings, and a lively mix of horror and silliness, **Content Warning** delivers a fresh and highly memorable take on the genre. It celebrates teamwork, creativity, and ridiculous moments while keeping the tension high with its eerie monsters and time pressure. For players looking for a co-op horror game that is as funny and social as it is scary, **Content Warning** stands out as an inventive and exciting choice.
