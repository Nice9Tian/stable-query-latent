**Ghost Watchers** is a thrilling ghost-hunting game set in abandoned houses haunted by lost souls, demons, and even creatures with vampiric tendencies. The game creates a tense and intriguing atmosphere by placing players in the role of investigators who must study each paranormal presence carefully before attempting to capture it.

One of the game’s biggest strengths is its variety. It features **20 unique ghost types**, including human souls, strange creatures, and several kinds of demons. Each ghost comes with its own **distinct attack patterns and animations**, making every encounter feel different and requiring players to adapt their approach. The game also offers **more than 60 detailed tools** for both collecting evidence and fighting ghosts, giving players plenty of options as they work to uncover the truth behind each haunting.

A key part of the experience is the ability to **determine a ghost’s type, age, and mood**, adding depth to the investigation and encouraging careful observation. Players can explore **7 different locations**, each packed not only with clues for ghost capture but also with interesting Easter eggs that reward exploration. To further diversify the gameplay, the game includes **different modifiers** that change and expand the experience.

The gameplay centers on a simple but engaging loop: **explore the ghost, gather evidence, learn what kind of spirit it is, and then catch it**. Every step matters, from identifying the ghost’s age to understanding its mood, making each hunt feel methodical and suspenseful.

As an **Early Access** title, *Ghost Watchers* is planned to grow over time, with **new ghosts and new locations** to be added in future updates.
