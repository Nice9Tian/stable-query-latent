Many abandoned houses are haunted by supernatural entities, ranging from troubled human spirits to hostile demonic beings. Some are the souls of those who took their own lives, while others may appear as vampiric presences that drain the life of anyone who gets too close.

The game features 20 distinct types of ghosts, including spirits of people, unusual creatures, and multiple kinds of demons. Each one has its own attack patterns and animations, making every encounter feel different. To investigate and survive, players can use more than 60 detailed tools designed for both gathering evidence and confronting the haunting entities. These investigations allow players to identify the type of ghost, estimate its age, and determine its mood.

There are 7 different locations, each filled not only with clues for tracking ghosts, but also with hidden Easter eggs. A variety of gameplay modifiers is also available, adding further diversity to the experience.

The core gameplay centers on exploring the supernatural presence first and then attempting to capture it. In this kind of hunt, every detail matters: collecting evidence, discovering the entity’s age, and understanding its current mood are all part of the process. As an early access title, the game is planned to receive additional ghosts and new locations over time.
