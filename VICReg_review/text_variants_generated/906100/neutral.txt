Observation is a sci-fi thriller that follows the disappearance of Dr. Emma Fisher and the crew of her mission from the perspective of the station’s artificial intelligence, S.A.M. Players take on the role of S.A.M. by using the station’s control systems, cameras, and tools to help Emma investigate what is happening to the station, the missing crew, and S.A.M. himself. The game presents the player as part of the station itself: “You’re not on the station, you are the station.”

Combining narrative adventure, puzzles, exploration, and cosmic existential horror, Observation centers on uncovering the true nature of the events unfolding aboard the station, the identities and fate of the crew, and the mysterious hexagon on Saturn that is always present.
