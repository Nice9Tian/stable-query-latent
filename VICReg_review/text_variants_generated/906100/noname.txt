This sci-fi thriller follows a mysterious incident aboard a deep-space station, told through the perspective of the station’s artificial intelligence, **SYN**, and the mission crew member **Astra**. Rather than playing as a human explorer, the player takes control of the station itself, using its cameras, control systems, and onboard tools to help Astra investigate what has happened to the station, the missing crew, and SYN’s own identity. The premise emphasizes that the player is not merely on the station, but effectively is the station.

The experience blends narrative adventure, exploration, puzzles, and cosmic existential horror. As the story unfolds, players uncover the truth behind the station’s strange events, the fate of the vanished crew, and the deeper nature of the artificial intelligence at the center of the mission. A recurring mysterious geometric object connected to Saturn also plays a key role in the atmosphere and narrative mystery, adding to the sense of unease and discovery.
