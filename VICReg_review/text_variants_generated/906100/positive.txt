Observation is a striking sci-fi thriller that tells the story of Dr. Emma Fisher and the fate of her mission’s crew through the unique perspective of the station’s artificial intelligence, S.A.M. Rather than playing as a person aboard the station, you become the station itself, operating its control systems, cameras, and tools to help Emma uncover what has happened to the vessel, the missing crew, and even S.A.M. himself.

The game stands out by blending narrative adventure, puzzles, exploration, and cosmic existential horror into a compelling experience. Its premise is both imaginative and immersive: you are not on the station, you are the station. As the mystery unfolds, Observation invites players to piece together the truth behind the events on board, the identities and destinies of the crew, and the strange, ever-present hexagon on Saturn.
