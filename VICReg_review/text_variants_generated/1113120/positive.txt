*IXION* is a gripping city-builder set against the collapse of Earth and the desperate search for humanity’s next home. In the game, DOLOS A.E.C. sends the prototype space station Tiqqun into the unknown, only for it to become a drifting ark carrying the remnants of civilization. As Administrator, you are responsible for guiding this fragile sanctuary through the dangers of space and the far more complicated challenges within.

The core of *IXION* lies in careful management, constant reassessment, and adaptation. You must establish, maintain, and expand the Tiqqun’s infrastructure while overseeing its population and responding to their needs. Every decision matters, and the game makes clear that choices can have consequences either immediately or much later. Keeping the crew’s trust is essential, especially as you balance the mission’s goals with the demands of the survivors under your command.

The station itself can be developed across six unlockable sectors, each of which allows for more population, new jobs, and improved oversight through the Data Listening System. As resources become scarce, you will need to search for what the station lacks, research new solutions, and construct buildings to support the settlement. The Tiqqun can also be modified and improved to help it continue its journey.

Beyond the station, *IXION* offers a dangerous and mysterious frontier. Players send out probes to uncover hidden regions, deploy mining and cargo ships to gather resources, and commission science expeditions to reveal both secrets and threats in deep space. The game also introduces other survivor groups, letting you encounter pockets of humanity beyond DOLOS and bring them into the mission if you can.

Space itself is a constant hazard. Hull breaches, overloaded power supplies, and electrical fires all demand attention, and surviving these emergencies requires backup power solutions and support for Extra Vehicular Activity. The game’s story unfolds through a series of tense chapters, each introducing new opportunities and dangers as you push toward the final objective: a new home for humanity.

With its strong emphasis on survival, leadership, and hard choices, *IXION* presents a compelling and thoughtful take on the city-building genre. It combines station management, exploration, research, and story-driven progression into a tense odyssey where hope must be earned, protected, and carried forward.
