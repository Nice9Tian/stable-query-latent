IXION is a city-builder set in a future where Earth is on the brink of ecosystemic collapse and the DOLOS A.E.C. leads the search for a new home for humanity. The story begins aboard the Tiqqun, a prototype space station that becomes a drifting ark carrying the remnants of human civilization. As Administrator, the player is responsible for guiding the station and its crew through an ongoing struggle for survival.

The game emphasizes constant vigilance, adaptation, and long-term consequences. Players must establish, maintain, and develop the Tiqqun’s infrastructure while also managing its population and responding to crew concerns in ways that preserve trust. Decisions can have immediate effects or cause problems later, and the outcome of those choices can determine whether the station survives or fails.

Resource management is central to the experience. Players must uncover what space has to offer, extract resources, and research missing technologies and capabilities. The Tiqqun can be expanded by unlocking up to six sectors, each of which can house more people, support additional jobs, and enable more oversight through the Data Listening System. New buildings can be constructed to improve the settlement, and the station itself can be modified and upgraded as the journey continues.

IXION also includes exploration and expedition elements. Players can send probes to reveal hidden areas, deploy mining and cargo ships to gather resources, and commission science expeditions to uncover secrets and threats in space. There are new stellar maps to explore, and the player may encounter other survivors, including groups outside DOLOS’s control, as well as the wrecks and failures of others.

The game presents a range of hazards, both internal and external. Space-related dangers such as hull breaches, overloaded power supplies, and electrical fires must be managed through emergency response, backup power solutions, and Extra Vehicular Activity. At the same time, the player must maintain trust in the corporation that began the mission, since failure to do so can lead to mutiny. Through its story chapters, new threats and opportunities emerge as the Tiqqun moves toward the goal of finding a new home.
