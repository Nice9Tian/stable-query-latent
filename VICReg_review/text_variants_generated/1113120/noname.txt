Set in a future where Earth is nearing ecological collapse, this strategy game follows a stranded prototype space station carrying the remnants of humanity in search of a new home. The player takes the role of the station’s administrator, responsible for keeping the drifting ark operational, stable, and ultimately survivable.

At its core, the game is a city-builder focused on constant decision-making and adaptation. Players must establish, maintain, and expand the station’s infrastructure while also managing the population aboard. The crew’s needs, demands, and trust must be balanced carefully, since choices can have immediate consequences or create problems later. Failure to maintain confidence in both the mission and the corporate force behind it can lead to unrest or mutiny.

Survival depends on gathering resources, researching missing technologies, and constructing new facilities to meet the needs of the settlement. The station can be expanded through multiple unlockable sectors, each increasing capacity, enabling new jobs, and allowing closer monitoring of the crew through a data-based oversight system. The game also includes a strong narrative structure, with story chapters that introduce new threats, opportunities, and the wider search for a final destination.

Outside the station, the player must deal with the dangers of space itself. Hazards such as hull damage, overloaded power systems, and electrical fires require active management, along with emergency planning, backup power solutions, and support for external operations. Exploration is also an important part of play: probes can be sent to uncover unknown regions, mining and cargo vessels can gather materials, and science expeditions can be commissioned to reveal hidden dangers and discoveries.

The game presents encounters with other survivors and traces of failed expeditions, expanding the sense of a larger, uncertain universe. Progress involves taking in survivors, recovering cryogenic pods, and deciding how to respond to each new situation in a way that keeps the mission moving forward. Overall, it combines base management, survival, exploration, and narrative choice in a tense campaign centered on endurance and the search for a new world.
