PARANORMASIGHT: The Seven Mysteries of Honjo is a horror-adventure visual novel set in Sumida Ward, Tokyo, Japan, in the late 20th century. The game centers on the local legend of the “Seven Mysteries of Honjo” and a mysterious “Rite of Resurrection,” setting up a conflict among people who can curse one another. At its core, the story asks how far someone would go to bring another person back from the dead.

The narrative follows multiple characters whose hopes, dreams, and fears become entangled around the Seven Mysteries. Shogo Okiie, an ordinary office worker, visits Kinshibori Park at night with his friend Yoko Fukunaga to investigate the ghost story. He initially dismisses Yoko’s claims about a connection to the Rite of Resurrection, but strange events soon force him to take them seriously. Other characters are investigating as well, including detectives looking into a series of unusual deaths, a high school girl searching for the truth behind her classmate’s suicide, and a mother seeking revenge for her lost son. Their motivations overlap, leading the story toward a battle of wits and curses.

Gameplay involves conducting investigations in Showa Japan through immersive 360-degree backgrounds. These environments were created from real-life locations in Sumida, photographed with a 360° camera and recreated as photo spheres surrounding the player. Players explore the mystery through conversations with many characters, some of whom are suspicious. Careful movement and attention to clues are important, since choosing poorly can lead to death. The story unfolds through multiple points of view, allowing players to uncover new information as they progress.
