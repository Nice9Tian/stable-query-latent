**PARANORMASIGHT: The Seven Mysteries of Honjo** is a gripping horror-adventure visual novel that invites players to uncover an ancient mystery set in the Sumida Ward of Tokyo, Japan, in the late 20th century. Now Steam Deck Verified, the game delivers a tense, atmospheric experience built around the infamous local legend of the Seven Mysteries of Honjo and the dangerous “Rite of Resurrection.”

At the heart of the story lies a powerful question: how far would you go to bring someone back from the dead? As whispers of curses and resurrection spread, multiple characters become entangled in a deadly conflict where hopes, dreams, fears, and revenge collide. An ordinary office worker, Shogo Okiie, enters the mystery after a nighttime visit to Kinshibori Park with his friend Yoko Fukunaga. What begins as skepticism quickly turns into horror when strange events unfold before his eyes. At the same time, detectives investigating a series of bizarre deaths, a high-school girl searching for the truth behind her classmate’s suicide, and a mother driven by vengeance all pursue their own goals, each drawn deeper into the same cursed web.

The game stands out with its richly presented investigations in Showa-era Japan, brought to life through immersive 360° backgrounds based on real locations in Sumida, photographed with full support and cooperation from local organizations and the community. Players are encouraged to explore carefully, gather crucial clues, and converse with a wide range of intriguing and suspicious characters. Every movement matters, and a single misstep can lead to death.

With its multi-perspective storytelling, strategic curse-based gameplay, and a narrative that twists and turns toward a battle of wits, PARANORMASIGHT offers a haunting and memorable journey. Players must decide when to unleash their curses and when to hold back, all while uncovering new layers of the mystery. It is a smart, atmospheric, and deeply unsettling visual novel that brings its supernatural premise to life with remarkable style.
