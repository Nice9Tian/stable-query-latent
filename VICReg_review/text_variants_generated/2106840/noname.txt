This horror-adventure visual novel unfolds in a late-20th-century urban district of Japan, where whispers about an old local tale known as the Seven Enigmas of a certain neighborhood lead into a dangerous conflict over a mysterious Rite of Return. The story centers on several interconnected characters whose hopes, fears, and personal motives become entangled as deadly curses begin to shape their lives. The narrative asks how far someone might go to bring a loved one back from death, and develops through shifting perspectives that gradually reveal new information.

The player follows an ordinary office worker, **Haro**, who visits a park at night with a friend, **Mina**, to look into the local ghost story. At first, Haro dismisses the connection between the Enigmas and the Rite, but strange events soon force him to take the matter seriously. At the same time, other figures pursue their own investigations: detectives examining a string of unexplained deaths, a high-school girl searching for the truth behind her classmate’s suicide, and a mother driven by revenge for her lost son. Their stories cross and influence one another, building toward a struggle of curses and wits.

Gameplay focuses on investigation, conversation, and careful decision-making. Players examine clues, speak with a variety of interesting and suspicious characters, and must choose their movements wisely, since careless actions can lead to death. The game presents its setting through immersive 360-degree backgrounds based on photographed real-world scenery from a Japanese city district, giving the environment a strong sense of place. Strategic curse mechanics also play an important role: players must decide when to use their own curses effectively or risk being cursed in return.
