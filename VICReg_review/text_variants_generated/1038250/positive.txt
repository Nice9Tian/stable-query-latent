DIRT 5 delivers a fun, amplified off-road arcade racing experience from Codemasters, bringing a fresh and bold new chapter to the DIRT series while staying true to its racing heritage. Built around style, culture, and constant high-energy moments, the game invites players to blaze trails across the globe in spectacular, dynamic environments and push the limits of off-road competition.

The game features more than 70 unique routes across 10 global locations, with races set in striking conditions ranging from gravel, ice, snow, and sand to unforgettable landmarks and landscapes. Players can race on the frozen East River in New York, speed beneath Christ the Redeemer in Brazil, and compete under the Northern Lights in Norway, all while adapting to extreme and ever-changing terrain.

DIRT 5 offers an exciting roster of vehicles that spans rally icons, trucks, GT heroes, buggies, muscle cars, Rallycross machines, trophy trucks, and 900bhp sprint cars. This diverse garage is designed to handle the toughest terrain and deliver a wide variety of off-road racing styles, making every event feel distinct and rewarding.

A star-studded Career mode puts players in the spotlight as they rise under the guidance of an icon, earn sponsorships and unique rewards, conquer locations around the world, and face a fierce rival in the biggest Career in the series. The game also supports local split-screen for up to four players in offline modes, including Career, making it an excellent choice for couch multiplayer. Online, curated race playlists and innovative objective-based modes allow up to 12 players to compete in exciting off-road action.

Creative tools and presentation features further enhance the experience. DIRT 5 includes a detailed Photo Mode for capturing jumps and standout moments, along with the deepest-ever livery editor in the series for customizing every car. With new innovations and features throughout, DIRT 5 stands out as a vibrant, modern off-road racer full of energy, variety, and replayable excitement.
