DIRT 5 is a fun, amplified off-road arcade racing game created by Codemasters. It focuses on high-energy racing across a variety of environments, with routes set around the world and surface types including gravel, ice, snow, and sand. The game features a broad lineup of vehicles, ranging from rally icons and trucks to GT cars, along with rallycross, trophy trucks, buggies, and muscle cars.

The game offers over 70 unique routes across 10 global locations, with dynamic conditions and settings that include the frozen East River in New York, the area under Christ the Redeemer in Brazil, and Norway under the Northern Lights. DIRT 5 emphasizes extreme racing and highlight-reel moments, combining the franchise’s established identity with a more bold and modern presentation.

Career mode is a major part of the experience, featuring a star-studded structure where players work under the guidance of an icon, earn sponsorships and rewards, complete locations, and face a rival in the series’ largest Career mode. The game also includes local split-screen for up to four players in offline modes, including Career, making it suitable for couch multiplayer. Online, it supports curated race playlists for up to 12 players and objective-based modes.

DIRT 5 also includes a livery editor and Photo Mode, allowing players to customize cars and capture their best moments. The page also notes ongoing post-launch content, including the Wild Spirits Content Pack and a game update that adds new Stampede events in Morocco and additional real-world liveries.
