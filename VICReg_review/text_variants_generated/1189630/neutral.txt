Path of Wuxia is the fourth title from Heluo Studio, following the studio’s first game, Tale of Wuxia. It combines a wuxia setting with school-life simulation and an anime-inspired art style, bringing back the simulation gameplay that fans of the series know. Players take on the role of a young Xia with extraordinary abilities and enter the academy known as the Hermitic Pavilions of Xia, where they grow stronger alongside their schoolmates while searching for their own path of Xia.

The game is presented as a wuxia school life simulator set in an academy founded by the legendary master Dongfang. The Hermitic Pavilions of Xia were established to challenge the clannish traditions of wuxia sects and to train righteous young Xias regardless of their background. At the academy, players receive instruction from masters from different sects, train both mind and body, take on masters’ challenges and quests, practice four kinds of arts, develop their personality, build friendships or rivalries, and help those in need as they strive to act as a true Xia. The game asks what kind of Xia the player will become in this unusual school.

A key part of the experience is forming relationships with other schoolmates. Young Xias from different sects gather at the Hermitic Pavilions of Xia, and spending time with them can turn them into dependable allies on future quests. The years of training together can lead to different outcomes, including brotherhood in arms, romance, or lasting hostility.

Combat uses a turn-based system on a hexagonal battlefield, continuing the style introduced in Tale of Wuxia. Battles combine six types of weapon moves, internal styles, character traits, and tactical actions such as backstabbing or side assaults. The game also introduces the five elements system, where each character has an elemental affinity. Understanding the relationships between the five elements is important for exploiting enemy weaknesses and protecting one’s own. Players can also use special abilities to change the course of battle and overcome challenges with the help of their schoolmates.

As the story progresses, players uncover a long-sealed past connected to the world of the game. The peaceful life at the academy is described as only the calm before another storm, and the player’s actions as a righteous Xia will influence the fate of many. When everything begins to collapse, the game asks whether the player can confront the consequences of past choices and remain committed to the path of Xia.
