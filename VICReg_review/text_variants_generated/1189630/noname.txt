This title presents a wuxia-inspired school-life simulation set in an academy founded to train righteous young heroes from different sects and backgrounds. Blending an anime-style presentation with a martial-arts world, it returns to a simulation-focused structure centered on daily growth, relationships, and moral choices.

Players take on the role of a gifted young hero and enter an academy-like institution where training, study, and social life unfold together. The experience revolves around accepting instruction from masters, improving both mind and body, completing challenges and quests, practicing multiple types of arts, and shaping personal traits. Along the way, players can make friends and enemies, help those in need, and determine what kind of righteous path their character will follow.

A major part of the game is building trust and friendship with fellow students. These classmates come from different sects, and spending time with them can turn them into dependable allies on future journeys. Depending on the relationship developed, these connections may evolve into comrades, romantic partners, or rivals.

Combat uses a turn-based system on a hexagonal battlefield. It combines multiple weapon techniques, internal styles, character traits, and tactical positioning such as attacks from behind or from the side. In addition, each character has an elemental affinity, and understanding the strengths and weaknesses among the five elemental relations is important for gaining the upper hand. Players can also rely on special abilities and the support of schoolmates to overcome difficult encounters.

As the story progresses, the protagonist gradually uncovers long-hidden truths about the world and the past surrounding it. Beneath the calm atmosphere of training and daily life, larger conflicts and consequences emerge, and the choices made in the name of righteousness will influence the fate of many.
