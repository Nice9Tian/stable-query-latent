**Besiege** is an outstanding physics-based building game that invites players to design and construct mechanical machines for destruction, creativity, and experimentation. With its intuitive and flexible building system, you can assemble all kinds of creations — from tanks, planes, helicopters, sports cars, catapults, and rockets to giant mechs — using a rich collection of more than 70 blocks. Every machine is shaped by the game’s intricate physics simulation, making each success, failure, and collapse feel both satisfying and memorable.

The core experience offers a strong single-player campaign with 55 destructible levels spread across four island nations, each featuring its own environment and unique civilizations to conquer. Players can also enjoy large sandbox levels, where they can test prototypes, refine ideas, and unleash havoc on the inhabitants. For those who want even more variety, Besiege includes access to more than 200,000 community-made machines through the Steam Workshop, all ready to download and play.

Multiplayer adds another exciting layer through Besiege Multiverse, where players can connect, compete, and cooperate in a wide range of challenges. You can face off in machine combat, race to complete objectives, or work together to solve puzzles, conquer levels, and destroy armies and fortresses. The game also supports powerful level editing tools, allowing players to create custom environments, modify levels in real time, and design unique multiplayer experiences using the editor’s logic system. From robot wars and motor racing to football, the creative possibilities are remarkably broad.

Besiege’s strong community support makes the experience even richer. Built-in mod support, Steam Workshop integration, and an in-game modloader have helped foster hundreds of mods, thousands of custom levels, and skin packs. With a large and active community, players can easily find help, join building contests, and connect with others through Discord for multiplayer sessions. Overall, Besiege stands out as a highly creative and deeply engaging game that combines engineering, physics, and destruction in a truly rewarding way.
