This is a physics-based construction game centered on building mechanical machines to destroy armies, demolish castles, and clear difficult obstacles. Players can progress through a campaign or join multiplayer sessions with friends, using an intuitive and flexible building system supported by a large collection of more than 70 blocks. The core simulation relies on detailed physics, which affects both successful creations and unstable machines that may collapse during use.

The single-player campaign includes 55 destructible levels spread across four island nations, each featuring its own environment and opposing civilization. In addition to the campaign, large sandbox stages allow players to experiment freely, test prototypes, and cause destruction in open-ended settings.

A major part of the experience is community content. Players can access over 200,000 community-made machines through Steam Workshop integration and play them in their own game. The game also supports a multiplayer portal for competitive and cooperative play, including machine combat, objective-based challenges, custom game modes, puzzle-solving, and coordinated assaults on armies and fortresses.

Level editing tools add another creative layer, allowing real-time changes to levels and the creation of custom multiplayer experiences through a logic system. The game supports a wide variety of user-made modes and community creations, including custom levels, visual packs, and mods. An in-game mod loader and workshop support make these additions easy to install, while the active community also offers help, contests, and multiplayer matchmaking through its social channels.
