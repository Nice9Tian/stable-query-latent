Besiege is a physics-based building game centered on constructing mechanical machines to destroy armies, demolish castles, and overcome obstacles. It features an intuitive and flexible building system supported by more than 70 blocks, allowing players to create a wide range of machines such as tanks, planes, helicopters, sports cars, catapults, rockets, and giant mechs. Its detailed physics simulation shapes both construction and gameplay, making each machine’s performance and failure part of the experience.

The game includes a single-player campaign with 55 destructible levels spread across four island nations, each with its own environment and opposing civilization. In addition to the campaign, players can explore large sandbox levels to test prototypes and experiment freely. Besiege also offers access to a large amount of community-created content, with more than 200,000 machines available through the Steam Workshop for download and play.

Multiplayer is available through Besiege Multiverse, where players can compete in machine combat, complete objectives, and try custom game modes, or work together to solve puzzles and conquer levels. The game also includes level editing tools that support real-time changes and logic-based creation, enabling custom environments and multiplayer experiences. Beyond combat-focused gameplay, the editor can be used for activities such as robot wars, motor racing, and football.

Besiege further supports mods, custom levels, and skin packs through Steam Workshop integration and an in-game modloader. Its community includes more than 20,000 members, offering help with building, modding, and skinning, as well as building contests and multiplayer coordination through the community Discord.
