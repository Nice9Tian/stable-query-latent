Genesis Noir is a surreal adventure about No Man, a watch peddler caught in a love triangle with the cosmic beings Miss Mass and Golden Boy. When the affair ends in conflict, No Man witnesses a gunshot fired by a jealous god—the Big Bang—and is drawn into an expanding universe in an effort to prevent or destroy creation and save his love.

The game focuses on tactile puzzles and exploration. Players experiment with objects in small sections of the universe to discover how they can be manipulated to advance. Throughout the journey, you interact with a range of actions and moments, including dialing a rotary phone, destroying a civilization, planting a garden, improvising with a musician, and creating life. These activities are part of the search to save Miss Mass.

The Big Bang is presented not only as the origin of creation, but also as a gun blast frozen in time, with the bullet moving toward the one No Man loves. Players travel through pockets of time and explore moments across the lifespan of the universe, observing events from the first microseconds of life to trillions of years in the future. The game centers on uncovering what can be learned from these creations while searching for a way to alter destiny.
