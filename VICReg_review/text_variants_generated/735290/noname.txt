In this narrative puzzle adventure, you play as a humble watch seller known as **Voren**, who becomes entangled in a tense romantic conflict with two cosmic figures, **Luma Vale** and **Orin Gold**. When the situation escalates into a devastating confrontation, you witness a single gunshot from a jealous divine presence—an event that also marks the birth of creation itself. From there, the journey sends you into an expanding universe where your goal is to find a way to stop or undo creation in order to preserve your love.

The experience centers on tactile puzzles and exploration. You interact with objects inside small fragments of the universe, experimenting with how they can be manipulated to open new paths forward. Progress comes through hands-on actions such as operating a rotary device, dismantling a civilization, planting a garden, improvising alongside a musician, and bringing life into existence. Each encounter is tied to the larger search for a way to save Luma Vale.

The story frames the Big Bang not only as the origin of everything, but also as a frozen gunshot, with the bullet still traveling toward the one you care about. To intervene, you move through pockets of time and explore moments across the lifespan of the universe, from the earliest instants of life to an imagined future trillions of years ahead. Along the way, you observe the rise of new creations and are pushed to consider what they become, and what it would mean to oppose them in order to alter destiny.
