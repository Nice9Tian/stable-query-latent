**Genesis Noir** is a striking cosmic adventure that blends romance, mystery, and creation on a truly universal scale. You play as **No Man**, a watch peddler drawn into a love triangle with the cosmic beings **Miss Mass** and **Golden Boy**. When jealousy erupts into conflict, a gunshot fired by a jealous god—known as **The Big Bang**—becomes the event that launches the universe and threatens the one you love. Your mission is both intimate and impossible: plunge into the expanding cosmos and search for a way to prevent or destroy creation itself in order to save Miss Mass.

The game emphasizes **tactile puzzles and exploration**, inviting you to interact with objects in small pockets of the universe and discover how they can be manipulated to advance your journey. You might **dial a rotary phone, destroy a civilization, plant a garden, improvise with a musician, or create life** as part of your search for answers. These moments are woven into a journey through **time itself**, as you jump between fragments of cosmic history and future epochs, from the first microseconds of life to trillions of years ahead.

A defining element of **Genesis Noir** is its imaginative framing of creation: the Big Bang is not only the birth of the universe, but also a **frozen gun blast**, with the bullet still racing toward the object of your love. As you move through the lifespan of the universe, you observe its unfolding moments and experiment with what can be altered, all while confronting the consequences of creation and destruction. It is a beautifully conceived journey that turns cosmic scale into an emotionally driven search for fate, love, and meaning.
