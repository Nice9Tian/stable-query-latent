Yakuza 3 Remastered is presented as the third chapter of Kazuma Kiryu’s saga, bringing back restored content in 1080p and 60fps. The story follows Kiryu and Haruka Sawamura one year after the Kanto-Kansai all-out war, as they leave Kamurocho behind and move to Okinawa to run the Morning Glory Orphanage. What should be a quieter life is immediately undermined by the constant intrusion of Kiryu’s past, which drags the orphanage into a dangerous power struggle tied to the Tojo Clan and the political world.

The game shifts between the “sleepless city” of Kamurocho in Tokyo and the tropical setting of Okinawa, but the contrast does little to hide the familiar underworld turmoil at its center. The premise is another dramatic conflict built around Kiryu trying to protect the children at the orphanage from threats coming from both criminal and state power. In the end, it is framed as the completion of the Dragon of Dojima’s journey, though the store page makes clear that peace is once again out of reach.
