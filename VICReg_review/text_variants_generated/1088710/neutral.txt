Yakuza 3 Remastered continues the third chapter of Kazuma Kiryu’s saga, featuring restored content presented in 1080p and 60fps. Set one year after the Kanto-Kansai all-out war, the story follows Kiryu and Haruka Sawamura as they leave Kamurocho and move to Okinawa to run the Morning Glory Orphanage.

Although Kiryu tries to build a peaceful life, his past soon threatens that stability. The Morning Glory Orphanage becomes caught in a deadly power struggle involving the Tojo Clan and the political world. The story unfolds across two contrasting settings: the “sleepless city” of Kamurocho in Tokyo and the tropical landscapes of Okinawa.

The game centers on Kiryu’s attempt to protect the orphanage children from dangers emerging from both the underworld and state power. It presents the continuation of The Dragon of Dojima’s journey.
