Yakuza 3 Remastered continues the acclaimed Kazuma Kiryu saga with restored content, presented in 1080p and 60fps for a sharper, smoother experience. One year after the Kanto-Kansai all-out war, Kiryu and Haruka Sawamura have left the unforgiving streets of Kamurocho behind and settled in Okinawa, where they now manage the Morning Glory Orphanage. This change of pace brings a moving new chapter to the series, while still preserving the intense drama and danger that define Kiryu’s world.

Even in the peaceful surroundings of Okinawa, the shadows of Kiryu’s past remain close. The Morning Glory Orphanage is drawn into a deadly power struggle that reaches across the Tojo Clan and the political world, forcing Kiryu to confront threats that emerge from both the underworld and state power. The story moves between the vibrant, sleepless city of Kamurocho and the tropical landscapes of Okinawa, creating a striking contrast that enriches the drama.

At its heart, Yakuza 3 Remastered delivers a compelling continuation of The Dragon of Dojima’s journey, blending personal responsibility, emotional stakes, and the ever-present tension of the criminal underworld. It is a powerful and memorable chapter that completes another vital part of Kiryu’s story.
