This racing game centers on building a progression from amateur competitor to high-level motorsport contender. It features a large roster of more than 200 licensed-style race and road vehicles, over 120 global circuits, and a system of race conversion kits that expand how cars can be used. Players earn credits and experience through competition, then use those rewards to buy and own hundreds of vehicles, install realistic performance parts, and personalize machines with livery elements inspired by racing and community creations. Driver appearance can also be customized.

The driving model is designed to be approachable while still offering depth, with fully scalable assists for different skill levels and handling that aims to feel both convincing and enjoyable. Races take place under a full 24-hour cycle with dynamic seasonal and weather conditions, adding variety to track conditions. The game also emphasizes controller support, speed sensation, crash impact, car-to-car contact, and improved AI behavior.

Progression is structured around a new career mode that frames the player’s ascent from weekend racer to professional legend. Beyond single-player advancement, the game includes multiplayer and community-focused modes, along with daily challenges that provide additional goals and repeatable objectives.
