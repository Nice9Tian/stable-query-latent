Project CARS 3 delivers a rich and rewarding racing journey built around progression, personalization, and accessible high-speed competition. With more than 200 elite-brand race and road cars and over 120 global tracks, it offers a broad and exciting foundation for every kind of driver. The game invites players to earn Credits and XP through racing, then use those rewards to buy and own hundreds of cars, upgrade them with realistic performance parts, and transform them with race- and community-inspired liveries. Drivers can also personalize their own characters, adding another layer of identity to the experience.

The gameplay is designed to be enjoyable for all skill levels, thanks to fully scalable assists and a controller experience that feels responsive and satisfying. Project CARS 3 emphasizes convincing and fun handling, a strong sense of speed, intense crash effects and car contact, and enhanced AI that helps create lively, competitive races. Its dynamic all-seasons, all-weather system and 24-hour day-night cycle add variety and atmosphere to every event.

Beyond the action on track, the game offers a deeply engaging career mode where players rise from weekend warrior to racing legend. It also includes new multiplayer and community modes, along with fun Daily Challenges that keep the experience fresh. With its wide selection of cars, extensive track list, and rewarding progression systems, Project CARS 3 stands out as an energetic and well-rounded racing game.
