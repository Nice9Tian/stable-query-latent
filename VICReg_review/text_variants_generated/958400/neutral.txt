Project CARS 3 is a racing game focused on progression, customization, and accessible driving across a large selection of content. It features more than 200 elite-brand race and road cars and over 120 global tracks, giving players a wide range of vehicles and locations to explore. The game also includes race conversion kits, allowing cars to be adapted for different racing purposes.

Progression is built around earning Credits and XP through racing. These can be used to buy and own hundreds of cars, then upgrade them with realistic performance parts. Players can further personalize their vehicles with race liveries and community-inspired elements, and they can also customize their drivers.

The handling system is designed to support a broad range of skill levels, with fully scalable assists and what the game describes as convincing and fun handling. Other driving features include a 24-hour cycle, dynamic all-seasons and all-weather racing, an emphasized controller experience, a strong sense of speed, intense crash effects and car contact, and enhanced AI.

Project CARS 3 also offers a new career mode where players rise from weekend warrior to racing legend. In addition, it includes multiplayer and community modes, along with Daily Challenges for ongoing play.
