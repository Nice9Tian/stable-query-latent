**Barn Finders** is a charming and rewarding treasure-hunting business simulator that turns forgotten spaces into opportunities for profit. In the land of Ammeryka, players search abandoned barns and other hidden locations for valuables ranging from old clocks, musical instruments, and antiques to gold, weaponry, vintage cars, and motorcycles. Every item has value, so the game encourages careful exploration, thorough scavenging, and smart selling in your own pawnshop.

A key part of the experience is building and managing your family business. Players bid on auctions and sales to gain access to closed-off properties, then uncover whatever dusty treasures lie inside. The game features 12 original locations, with barns only serving as the beginning of a much bigger adventure that can even extend to places like an old military base or the moon. Along the way, mysterious stories connected to the locations and collected items add extra character to the journey.

Barn Finders also emphasizes shop development and item restoration. Found goods can be cleaned, repaired, and improved before being sold, helping empty shelves turn into a thriving business. Exclusive cars and motorbikes can be assembled from discovered parts and later sold for a major payout. Uncle Billy accompanies the player through the early stages, while the challenge eventually becomes a solo effort against competitors who are always ready to capitalize on mistakes.

With its mix of exploration, repair, collecting, and resale, Barn Finders delivers a distinctive and highly engaging loop. It even adds a playful touch by letting players shoot hoops in the backyard when business is slow, somehow turning downtime into another way to make money. It is a bold and amusing take on running a treasure-hunting pawnshop, full of valuable finds and satisfying discoveries.
