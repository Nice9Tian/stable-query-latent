This game is a business simulation centered on searching abandoned and restricted properties for valuable goods, then selling them for profit through a pawnshop. Players look through barns and other locations for a wide variety of collectible items, including clocks, musical instruments, antiques, gold, weaponry, and even vintage vehicles such as cars and motorcycles. Every item has value, so thorough searching is encouraged, and players are rewarded for collecting as much as possible.

Progress involves bidding on auctions and sales to gain access to closed-off properties, opening up new places to explore. The game includes 12 original locations in the fictional land of Ammeryka, a setting presented as being broadly similar to real America. Beyond barns, players may also visit unusual places such as an old military base and even the moon. Each location is described as containing its own secrets and hidden finds.

The player also manages and develops a shop. Empty shelves reduce income, while a well-stocked store helps bring customers back. Found goods can be cleaned and repaired before being sold, which improves their quality and potential value. If there are no customers, there is still a side activity available in the backyard, where players can play basketball and somehow earn money while doing so.

The game features family-business style progression, with a character named Uncle Billy initially helping with exploration and work, though the player eventually takes on the tasks alone. Competition is a part of the experience as well, with rival specialists waiting for mistakes. Additional goals include finding parts of exclusive cars and motorbikes, rebuilding them, and selling the completed vehicles for a large payout. The overall experience emphasizes scavenging, restoring goods, shop management, and uncovering unusual stories tied to the places and items encountered.
