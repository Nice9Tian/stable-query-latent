**Barn Finders** is a business simulation game centered on searching abandoned locations for valuables and selling them for profit. Players run a family pawnshop while exploring barns and other closed-off properties in search of items such as old clocks, musical instruments, antiques, gold, weaponry, vintage cars, and motorcycles. Since every item has value, the gameplay encourages thorough searching and collecting.

The game includes auctions and sales that can unlock access to restricted locations. It features 12 original locations set in Ammeryka, a fictional world that is largely similar to America but not quite the same. While barns are the starting point, the search expands to other places such as an old military base and even the moon.

A major part of the gameplay is developing the shop itself. Players must keep shelves stocked, since empty shelves lead to fewer customers and less money. Found items can be cleaned and repaired before being sold, improving their value. If there are no customers, players can also play basketball in the backyard, with the unusual note that money can somehow be earned while doing so.

Uncle Billy accompanies the player at the beginning and helps with exploring different locations, though the main work is ultimately done by the player alone. The game also emphasizes competition, as rival specialists are waiting for mistakes. Additional features include finding hundreds of items, rebuilding exclusive cars and motorbikes from parts for a larger payout, and uncovering the stories behind the locations and the items discovered.
