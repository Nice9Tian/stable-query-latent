**V. X. R. F.** is a demanding arcade-style roguelike first-person shooter built around permadeath and intense survival combat. Players control a squad of marines sent into dark, claustrophobic spacecraft overrun by lethal alien beasts. Each mission takes place inside a procedurally generated maze-like ship, making every run different as the team searches for crucial data caches and valuable upgrades while trying to stay alive.

The core objective is to locate and download all required data caches scattered throughout the vessel. At the start, marines are armed only with basic automatic rifles or shotguns, along with a simple tracker that can detect nearby movement and estimate the distance to the nearest data cache. These limited tools force players to move carefully through the ship’s narrow corridors, where enemies can appear suddenly and overwhelm the squad.

As data caches are found, they may contain upgrades that improve survival odds by providing stronger ammunition, a better tracker, and other helpful functions. Managing space is a major part of the game’s strategy: closing doors can isolate dangerous areas and reduce the chance of being attacked from multiple directions, but it can also make escape more difficult if the situation turns bad.

The spacecraft also contain automated sentry robots that can be activated to help hold off enemy waves. These provide significant extra firepower and can be decisive when the marines are under pressure, although friendly fire remains possible despite their safeguards. After all data caches have been collected, the mission shifts to disabling the ship’s coolant systems to trigger its destruction. From that point, the squad has only 90 seconds to reach the airlock and extract before the reactor overloads and the ship is obliterated.

The game uses both procedural level generation and procedurally arranged music, with audio and visuals closely synchronized to the action to create a highly intense combat experience. It supports both local and networked co-op for 1 to 4 players, using keyboard and mouse or game controllers, and both modes include split-screen viewing.
