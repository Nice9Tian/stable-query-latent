Space Beast Terror Fright (S.B.T.F.) is a very hard arcade-style roguelike, permadeath first-person shooter. Players control Marines who enter dark, claustrophobic spacecraft infested with Space Beasts in order to retrieve critical data and earn upgrades while trying to survive.

Each spacecraft is procedurally generated and maze-like, making every run different. The core objective is to search for DataCores and download them while facing highly lethal enemies. Marines begin with either automatic rifles or shotguns, plus a basic Tracker that detects nearby motion and the distance to the nearest DataCore. DataCores can also contain upgrades that improve ammunition, enhance the motion tracker, and add other useful functions that increase the odds of survival.

A major part of the game is managing space through doors. Players can close doors to seal off sections behind the team, helping prevent enemies from attacking from multiple directions, though this can also make retreat harder. Automated Sentry robots are also scattered throughout the spacecraft and can be activated to help defend against the continual waves of Beasts. These Sentries provide substantial firepower, but friendly fire is still possible even if they are programmed to avoid shooting Marines in their line of fire.

After all DataCores are collected, the final objective is to disable all Reactor Coolants to destroy the spacecraft. Once this is done, the Marines have 90 seconds to reach the Airlock and escape before the Reactor overloads.

The game uses both procedural level generation and procedurally arranged music scoring. Its audio and visuals are tightly synchronized to the action to create an intense combat experience. Space Beast Terror Fright supports local and networked co-op for 1-4 players using keyboard/mouse and Xbox 360 controllers, with split-screen viewing available in both local and networked play. A demo is available as a .zip download, though it is noted to be the original Greenlight submission and not the current version of the game.
