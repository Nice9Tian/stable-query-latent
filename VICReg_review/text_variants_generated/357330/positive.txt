Space Beast Terror Fright is a brutal, arcade-style rogue-like first-person shooter that delivers a tense and thrilling sci-fi survival experience. Players take on the role of Marines sent into dark, claustrophobic spacecraft overrun by terrifying Space Beasts, with the constant goal of extracting critical data, securing valuable upgrades, and making it out alive.

At the heart of the game is a procedurally generated, maze-like spacecraft that changes every time you play. Each run demands careful exploration as Marines search for DataCores to download while surviving against enemies that are described as extremely lethal. The ultimate mission is to recover all essential data and then set the Reactor core to overload, turning every mission into a desperate race against time.

The Marines begin with limited tools: either an automatic rifle or a powerful shotgun, plus a basic Tracker device that detects nearby motion and shows the distance to the nearest DataCore. As DataCores are found, they can also contain powerful upgrades that improve ammunition, enhance the motion tracker, and add other useful capabilities to increase survival chances. Intelligent door control is a major part of the experience, allowing players to seal off sections of the spacecraft to avoid being attacked from multiple directions at once, though doing so can also make escape more difficult.

To help stem the relentless attacks, automated Sentry robots are scattered throughout the spacecraft and can be activated for extra firepower. These Sentries can make the difference between life and death when the Marines are being overwhelmed, although friendly fire incidents can still occur if players stray into their line of fire. Once all DataCores are collected, the final objective is to disable all Reactor Coolants and destroy the spacecraft. After that, the Marines have only 90 seconds to reach the Airlock for extraction before the Reactor overloads and everything is obliterated.

The game also stands out for its procedural level generation and procedurally arranged music scoring, with audio and visuals tightly synced to the action for a highly intense combat atmosphere. Space Beast Terror Fright supports both local and networked co-op for 1 to 4 players, using keyboard and mouse or Xbox 360 game controllers, and both modes feature split-screen viewing of the action. It is a fierce, atmospheric, and richly designed co-op shooter that embraces tension, teamwork, and survival under pressure.
