**The Ascent** is a striking solo and co-op action-shooter RPG set on Veles, a dense cyberpunk world brought to life through the towering corporate metropolis of The Ascent Group arcology. This sprawling city stretches high into the sky and is populated by creatures from across the galaxy, creating a vivid and atmospheric setting for a tense and compelling survival story.

You play as a worker trapped under the control of the corporation that owns you and everyone else in your district. When The Ascent Group suddenly shuts down for unknown reasons, your home is thrown into crisis, and the survival of your district hangs in the balance. From there, you are drawn into a new mission to uncover the cause of the catastrophe and fight for your future. The game delivers a strong premise that blends corporate oppression, cyberpunk mystery, and high-stakes action.

The Ascent shines in both solo play and co-op, allowing you to experience the full campaign alone or team up with up to three friends in local or online multiplayer. Its explosive shooter combat emphasizes flexibility and tactical decision-making. You can aim low or high, switch weapons, equip deadly gadgets, take cover, and make use of destructible environments to gain the upper hand. As new enemies appear, you are encouraged to constantly adapt your tactics, keeping every encounter dynamic and intense.

RPG systems add depth to the action by letting you customize your character with cyberware that fits your preferred style of play. As you level up, you can allocate skill points and experiment with different augmentations, opening up creative ways to defeat enemies. This blend of shooter mechanics and character progression gives the game a rewarding sense of growth and personalization.

The world itself is another major strength. The Ascent presents a vibrant cyberpunk setting filled with allies, enemies, and loot to discover as you explore its many districts. From the grim deep slums to the elevated luxury spheres, the arcology offers a wide range of environments that reinforce the scale and richness of the setting. Altogether, The Ascent delivers an exciting combination of action, RPG depth, co-op play, and world-building in a truly memorable cyberpunk universe.
