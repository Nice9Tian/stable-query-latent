Set in a dense cyberpunk metropolis on the world of Veylos, this solo and co-op action-shooter RPG places the player inside a towering corporate arcology called the Skyforge Complex. The setting is a crowded, vertical city filled with beings from across the galaxy, where everyday life is controlled by a powerful company that owns the workers in its districts.

The story begins when the corporation abruptly collapses for unknown reasons, triggering a chain of catastrophic events that threatens the survival of the player’s district. As a worker forced into this unstable situation, the player must arm themselves and uncover what caused the disaster. The central premise focuses on survival, independence, and the question of whether life is possible without the system that controls everything.

Gameplay can be experienced entirely alone or in cooperative play with up to three others, either locally or online. Combat emphasizes fast-paced shooting, with the ability to aim high or low, switch between weapons, and use dangerous gadgets. Cover is important, and the environment can be destroyed and used tactically, encouraging players to adapt as different enemies appear.

Progression includes RPG-style customization through cybernetic enhancements that can be matched to different playstyles. Players gain skill points as they level up and can choose from a range of augmentations to approach combat in more creative ways. Exploration also plays a major role, as the game features a vivid cyberpunk world with multiple districts to discover, from lower slum areas to upper luxury zones, alongside allies, enemies, and loot scattered throughout.
