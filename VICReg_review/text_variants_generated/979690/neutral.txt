The Ascent is a solo and co-op action-shooter RPG set on Veles, a densely packed cyberpunk world. The game takes place in The Ascent Group arcology, a corporate-run metropolis that rises high into the sky and is inhabited by creatures from across the galaxy. You play as a worker enslaved by the corporation that owns you and everyone else in your district. When The Ascent Group suddenly shuts down for unknown reasons, your district’s survival is put at risk, and you are forced to take up arms to uncover what caused the crisis.

The game can be played alone or with up to three friends in local or online co-op. Its combat emphasizes flexible shooting mechanics: you can aim low or high, switch weapons, equip lethal gadgets, use cover, and take advantage of destructible environments while adapting your tactics to new enemies. RPG systems are also part of the experience, allowing you to customize your character with cyberware, allocate skill points as you level up, and experiment with different augmentations to handle enemies in creative ways.

Exploration is set within a vivid cyberpunk setting featuring a range of districts, from deep slums to luxury areas higher up in the arcology. As you move through the world, you meet allies and enemies and collect loot along the way.
