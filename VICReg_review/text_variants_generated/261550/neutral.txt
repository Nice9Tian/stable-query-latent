Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord is a medieval combat simulator and role-playing game set in the war-torn world of Calradia, where civil war has fractured an empire and new kingdoms are rising beyond its borders. Players take on the role of a character they can create and develop to match their preferred play style, then explore a large medieval sandbox filled with opportunities to raid, conquer, trade, craft, and build power. The game emphasizes that no two playthroughs are the same.

As players progress, they can raise armies, engage in politics, recruit companions, and manage a fiefdom while working to establish their clan among Calradia’s nobility. Combat is a central feature, with large real-time battles that can be fought in first- or third-person view. The game uses Mount & Blade’s skill-based directional combat system, allowing players to command and fight alongside their troops.

Bannerlord also includes multiplayer PvP, offering ranked matchmaking and casual modes, along with the option to host private servers using Dedicated Server files. In addition, the game supports extensive customization through the Modding Kit, and players can share their creations through Steam Workshop.
