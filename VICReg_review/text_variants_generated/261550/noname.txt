In a war-torn medieval realm, an empire fractures under the pressure of civil conflict while new kingdoms emerge beyond its borders. Players are called to arm themselves, gather followers, and ride into battle in a large-scale sandbox where the goal is to build power, expand influence, and shape the future from the ruins of the old order.

The experience combines action combat with role-playing progression. Players create and develop a character suited to their preferred style, then move through a world that supports raiding, conquest, and a variety of long-term strategic decisions. Each playthrough can unfold differently, with opportunities to raise armies, take part in politics, trade goods, craft weapons, recruit companions, and manage a personal domain while working to secure a place among the nobility of the land.

Combat is presented as a major feature of the game. Players can lead and fight alongside their troops in first-person or third-person view, taking part in massive real-time battles built around a directional, skill-based system that emphasizes timing, positioning, and control. Beyond the single-player campaign, the game also includes multiplayer PvP with both ranked matchmaking and casual modes, as well as the option to host private servers.

The game further supports customization through modding tools, allowing players to alter the experience and create new adventures. Those creations can be shared with others through a community workshop, extending the game’s replayability and giving players additional ways to reshape the world and its systems.
