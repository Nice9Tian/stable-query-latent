Automobilista 2 is a remarkable celebration of motorsports, delivering a comprehensive simulator that places players at the heart of racing’s thrills across the decades. Built on genre-leading technology, it combines an immersive driving experience with a huge variety of cars, tracks, and features, while still staying fun and accessible. It is an advanced racing simulator that offers both depth and approachability, making it easy to move from one racing era to another within just a few clicks.

The game’s scope is especially impressive. Players can jump from a rental kart in a modern race in downtown Sao Paulo to a historic Grand Prix car tearing around Monza’s famous banking as if it were the 1960s. Automobilista 2 allows players to run championship seasons provided in the game or create fully custom seasons with their own classes, tracks, points systems, and weather settings. It also encourages creative racing scenarios, from authentic historical events to entirely imaginative matchups that would never happen in real life.

One of the game’s greatest strengths is its celebration of motorsport history. Players can drive legendary machines such as the Lotus 72E, the Porsche 962C Group C prototype, and the McLaren F1 GTR on the tracks where they truly belonged. The game also includes unique race cars equipped with inventive technologies that have become motorsport folklore, such as the Brabham BT46B fan car and the McLaren MP4-12 with its brake steer system. This historical variety gives Automobilista 2 a distinctive identity and a strong sense of authenticity.

Automobilista 2 also stands out for its broad range of racing disciplines. From rental karts and high-performance prototypes to classic Grand Prix cars, modern GT machines, and racing trucks, it offers a rich catalogue of content across beautifully detailed venues on five continents. The game combines world-class graphics with optimized performance, delivering striking visuals even on less powerful systems, and it is presented as one of the best VR experiences available in a racing game.

The racing itself is highly dynamic. Automobilista 2 features advanced systems for weather and track conditions, including 24-hour cycles, barometric pressure modeling, wind variations, and temperature changes, ensuring that no two laps feel the same. Its Real Weather system connects the in-game weather to live global meteorological data, allowing races to reflect current conditions at real-world locations. It even includes historical weather data for every day over the last 40 years, making it possible to recreate classic races with impressive fidelity.

The game also offers a strong framework for rules and regulations, supporting one of the most complete sets found in any racing simulator. This allows players to emulate the rules of many major series, including oval racing and Full Course Yellow support. Multiclass events are highly customizable as well, with precise control over the number of opponents in each class.

With its growing library of legendary cars and historic tracks, Automobilista 2 continues to invite players to relive the great eras of motorsport. Backed by a long-term development cycle and regular monthly updates, it is an ever-evolving project that continues to expand with new features, improvements, cars, and tracks. It is a rich, ambitious, and exciting racing simulator that truly deserves praise for its variety, authenticity, and enduring passion for motorsport.
