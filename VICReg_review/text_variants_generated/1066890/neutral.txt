Automobilista 2 is a motorsport-focused racing simulator designed to deliver a broad and varied driving experience across different eras and disciplines. It combines advanced simulation technology with an accessible structure, allowing players to quickly move between a wide range of vehicles and events, from rental karts and modern race cars to historic Grand Prix machines, prototypes, GT cars, and racing trucks.

The game emphasizes variety in both content and race creation. Players can participate in championship seasons included in the game or build custom seasons by choosing classes, tracks, points systems, and weather settings. Automobilista 2 also supports recreating historical motorsport events, including races with legendary cars such as the Lotus 72E, Porsche 962C, and McLaren F1 GTR, on the circuits where they originally raced. In addition, it offers unusual vehicles and technologies that are rarely modeled in racing simulators, such as the Brabham BT46B fan car and the McLaren MP4-12 with its brake steer system.

A major part of the experience is the game’s dynamic racing environment. Automobilista 2 includes advanced weather and track condition systems, 24-hour day-night cycles, barometric pressure simulation, and changes in wind speed and temperature. These systems create race conditions that can shift from session to session and lap to lap. The game also features Real Weather integration, connecting its weather system to real-world meteorological data so races can reflect current conditions at a chosen location and time. It also includes historical weather data spanning the last 40 years, enabling players to recreate past races with weather conditions that closely match real events.

Automobilista 2 also offers a detailed rules and regulations system intended to emulate the structure of major racing series. This includes support for multiclass racing with customizable opponent counts per class. In version 1.4, the game expanded further with oval racing and Full Course Yellow support. The game’s content continues to grow through regular monthly updates, with new cars, tracks, features, and improvements added over time.

Visually, Automobilista 2 aims to combine high-quality graphics with performance optimization, including strong VR support. Overall, it presents itself as an ever-expanding simulator built around motorsport history, race customization, dynamic conditions, and a wide selection of vehicles and tracks across five continents.
