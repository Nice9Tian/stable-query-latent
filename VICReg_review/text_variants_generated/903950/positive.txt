Last Oasis is a Nomadic Survival MMO set in a striking post-cataclysmic world where Earth has stopped rotating, leaving humanity trapped in a narrow band of habitable land between searing heat and frozen wastelands. In this harsh and beautifully imagined open world, survival depends on constant movement, smart planning, and making the most of every precious resource.

At the heart of the experience are Walkers: wooden, wind-powered machines inspired by Theo Jansen’s strandbeests. These versatile siege engines serve as transport, harvesters, combat platforms, and mobile homes, and can be customized with a wide range of structures, attachments, and upgrades. They are central to travel across the planet’s many interconnected oases, each spanning over 100 km² and offering distinct biomes, limited resources, and unique challenges.

The game presents a world shaped by extremes. To the west, the sun scorches the land into dust, while beyond the horizon lies a frozen, uninhabitable expanse of ice. Between these deadly halves, Nomads must find water, hunt dangerous creatures, fend off pirates, brew powerful Vitamins, and uncover hidden Walker technologies. The environment is unforgiving, with severe weather such as constant sandstorms making travel between oases even more hazardous.

Last Oasis also offers a rich player-driven economy and a wide range of paths to success. Players can rise from humble nomadic traders to mercenary kings, fleet commanders, or wealthy merchants. Clans may control territory and influence trade, but solo players are also given meaningful opportunities to thrive through trading, mercenary work, hunting, piracy, and the use of remote and autonomous weapon technology developed by Flotillan engineers. Voice chat and social features make it easy to connect with others, team up, and take on even the largest creatures.

One of the most notable regions in the game is the Flotillan safe zone, where violence between Nomads is forbidden and trade, training, and socializing can flourish. Outside this protected area, the world becomes dangerous and lawless, populated by hostile creatures, savage Nomads, and powerful threats. Among the most terrifying are the giant sandworms known as “The Long Ones,” ravenous beasts that require coordinated groups of experienced hunters to defeat. Successful hunts can yield some of the rarest and most valuable resources in the game.

Combat is fast, skill-based, and directional, with a strong emphasis on melee mastery. Players can wield one-handed and two-handed swords, maces, axes, and quarterstaves, or equip their Walkers with ranged weapons such as ballistas, scattershot guns, repeaters, and giant slingshots. Grappling hooks add another layer of mobility and skill, allowing Nomads to board Walkers, traverse terrain inaccessible to their machines, and move with agility across the world.

Building and improving a base is also a vital part of survival. Whether in a Hangar on the edge of death or within a clan-controlled region, home provides safety, warmth, and a place to recover before venturing out again. Clans can grow into political and economic powers, form alliances, and defend their territory as rival groups constantly challenge their control.

With its blend of nomadic survival, large-scale exploration, Walker customization, directional combat, and a living economy, Last Oasis delivers a compelling and ambitious MMO experience built around mobility, strategy, and resilience.
