In this nomadic survival MMO, humanity endures on a planet where rotation has slowed to a crawl, leaving only a narrow habitable band between lethal heat and freezing wastelands. Players must keep moving across a vast open world made up of interconnected oases, each larger than 100 km² and filled with distinct biomes, limited resources, and escalating dangers. Survival depends on constant exploration, careful planning, and the ability to adapt to shifting environmental pressures such as severe sandstorms and harsh, lawless regions beyond protected areas.

A defining feature of the game is the use of large, customizable wooden machines powered by wind and inspired by real-world strandbeests. These mobile structures serve many purposes: travel, transport, harvesting, combat, and even siege warfare. Players can personalize them with a wide range of attachments, structures, and upgrades, turning them into specialized tools for different roles. Autonomous machines can also be built to harness wind power, adding another layer to base development and logistics.

The game supports both solo play and group-based progression. Individuals and small teams can pursue many paths to success, from trading and hunting to piracy, mercenary work, and resource control. Larger clans may dominate territory and influence the economy, but solo players can still find viable niches. Social features such as voice chat help players coordinate, meet others, and assemble groups to take on major threats, including some of the game’s largest creatures.

The world’s factions and wildlife create a tense atmosphere. A guarded safe zone exists under the rule of a unifying faction, where violence between nomads is prohibited and punishment for breaking the decree is severe. Outside that zone, the world becomes increasingly hostile, with dangerous creatures, aggressive rival groups, and reports of enemies using repurposed technology and captured equipment. Among the most feared threats are enormous sand-dwelling beasts that require coordinated groups to defeat. Entering their territory unprepared is likely fatal, but success can yield extremely valuable resources.

Combat is skill-based and directional, with a focus on melee weapon handling and timing. Players can choose from one-handed and two-handed weapons such as swords, maces, axes, and quarterstaves. Ranged options also exist through machine-mounted weapons, including siege-style launchers and other projectile systems. Vertical movement is another important aspect of play, with grappling tools used to board machines, cross terrain, and reach places that cannot be accessed by vehicles. Mastery of movement and positioning can be just as important as raw equipment.

A player-driven economy links the oases through trade networks, allowing quick and observant traders to profit from supply and demand. Rare schematics and materials can be found in remote or dangerous areas, encouraging risk-reward decision-making and competition with other groups. Clans can grow into political and economic powers by controlling regions, forming alliances, and defending their income from rivals. At every stage, the game emphasizes mobility, preparation, resource management, and the constant pressure of survival in a world where staying still is rarely an option.
