Last Oasis is a nomadic survival MMO set on a transformed Earth where the planet has stopped rotating. Humanity survives in a narrow habitable band between extreme heat and freezing cold, forcing players to remain constantly on the move across a large open world in search of resources.

A central feature of the game is the Walker: a wooden, wind-powered machine inspired by Theo Jansen’s strandbeests. Walkers function as adaptable siege engines that can be configured for travel, transportation, harvesting, and combat. Players can personalize them with a variety of structures, attachments, and upgrades to suit different needs and playstyles.

The world is divided into extreme environments. One side of the planet is burned by the sun and reduced to dust, while the other is frozen in ice. Survival depends on securing water, hunting dangerous creatures, repelling pirates, brewing Vitamins, and uncovering Walker technologies. Players can also build autonomous machines that harness wind power.

Progression supports multiple roles. A player may rise from a nomadic trader to a mercenary king, fleet commander, or wealthy merchant. Both solo players and clans can succeed: clans may control territory and influence the economy, while traders, mercenaries, hunters, and pirates can all find viable paths on their own. Social features such as voice chat make it easy to meet others and cooperate, especially when facing some of the world’s largest creatures.

The game also includes Flotillan safe zones, where Nomads can trade, socialize, train, and prepare for travel into uncontrolled regions. Violence between Nomads is prohibited there, and breaking the decree leads to banishment. Outside these territories, the world is harsh and lawless, with more dangerous creatures and hostile Nomads.

Enemies and threats include the Rupu, who have reportedly begun using Flotillan weapons and repurposed Walker technology, as well as taming creatures to use as war machines. The most dangerous threat described is the sandworm known as “The Long Ones,” which can only be defeated by experienced hunters working together, and underprepared players risk certain death.

Last Oasis features a player-driven economy with trade networks between discovered oases. Smart traders can profit from supply and demand, while others may seek rare schematics or resources from dangerous beasts. Clans can defend operations, challenge rivals, and attempt to dominate regions, but competition can quickly lead to conflict.

The open world consists of many interconnected oases, each over 100 km², with unique biomes and limited resources to plunder. Travel between them is complicated by severe weather, including constant sandstorms. Exploration is necessary, but it is also dangerous.

Combat is skill-based and directional, with a focus on melee weapons such as swords, maces, axes, and quarterstaves. Ranged combat is also supported through Walker-mounted weapons like ballistas, scattershot guns, repeaters, and giant slingshots. Players can specialize in a single style or try to master multiple approaches.

Base building and improvement are important for survival. The game also emphasizes vertical movement through grappling hooks, which are used to board Walkers, move through areas inaccessible to them, and navigate the environment. Skill with movement and traversal plays a major role in how effectively a Nomad can survive and operate in the world.
