Risk of Rain 2 is an action game set on an alien planet, where players must survive through a series of handcrafted locations filled with challenging monsters and massive bosses. The goal is to fight through to the final boss and escape, though the game also allows runs to continue indefinitely to test how long survival is possible. A scaling system drives the experience forward, causing both the player and enemies to grow stronger over the course of each run.

A major part of the game is its item system, with more than 110 items that help keep each attempt varied. As items are collected, their effects can combine in increasingly complex ways, creating unexpected outcomes and new strategic possibilities. The item logs also contribute to the game’s lore and strategy.

Risk of Rain 2 also offers a range of playstyles through its eleven unlockable survivors, each with a distinct combat style and alternate skills to learn. Players can further modify the experience by unlocking and using Artifacts, which change rules such as friendly fire, random survivor spawns, and item selection. Since stages, enemies, and items are randomized, each run plays out differently.

The game can be played solo or in online co-op with up to three friends. It also includes the rotating challenge mode Prismatic Trials. Alongside classic survivors like the Engineer, Huntress, and Commando, newer characters such as the Captain and MUL-T are also part of the roster.
