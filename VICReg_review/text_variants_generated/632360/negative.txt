*Risk of Rain 2* presents itself as a survival-driven action game on an alien planet, but its appeal is largely built on repetition, escalation, and chaos rather than elegance or balance. Players are dropped into over a dozen handcrafted locales filled with hostile monsters and oversized bosses, then pushed toward a final boss or encouraged to keep going indefinitely. The core idea is simple: survive as long as possible while the game relentlessly ramps up the difficulty for both you and your enemies through a scaling system that never stops growing.

The item system is one of the game’s main hooks, though it often feels more like a stack of unpredictable modifiers than a carefully controlled progression. With more than 110 items, every run is meant to feel different, and combinations can create strange and surprising effects. The game also frames these items as a source of lore and strategy through logs, but the actual experience is still dominated by the random accumulation of power and the resulting imbalance it creates.

There are eleven playable survivors, each with distinct combat styles and alternate skills, which gives the game some variety on paper. In practice, much of this variety is buried beneath randomized stages, enemies, and item drops that ensure no run follows a stable rhythm. The Artifacts system adds extra rule changes such as friendly fire, random survivor spawns, and item selection, but these are more about altering chaos than refining the experience.

The game supports solo play, online co-op with up to three friends, and the rotating Prismatic Trials challenge. It also includes survivors like the Captain and MUL-T alongside returning characters such as the Engineer, Huntress, and Commando. Despite this breadth of content, *Risk of Rain 2* remains a game defined by uncertainty, constant escalation, and a structure that often feels more punishing than rewarding.
