This action-packed survival game places players on an alien world filled with danger, offering more than a dozen handcrafted locations, each populated by hostile creatures and massive bosses. The core objective is to fight through these environments, reach the final boss, and escape, though runs can also continue indefinitely for those who want to test how long they can endure. A distinctive scaling system ensures that both the player and enemies grow stronger over the course of a run, steadily raising the stakes.

A major part of the experience comes from discovering and combining over 110 different items. Each run can feel different as item effects stack in unexpected ways, creating new strategic possibilities and fresh challenges. Additional in-game logs expand the world’s lore while also providing useful context for planning and decision-making.

The game also emphasizes replayability through unlockable survivors. There are eleven playable characters, each with a unique combat style and alternate skills to master. Players can also uncover special modifiers through artifacts, which can alter the rules of play in ways such as enabling friendly fire, changing survivor spawns, or affecting item selection. Because stages, enemies, and items are randomized, every run offers a different experience.

It can be played solo or in online co-op with up to three other players. For those seeking a different kind of challenge, there is also a rotating trial mode. The roster includes both new and familiar survivors, each contributing to the game’s varied combat and progression systems.
