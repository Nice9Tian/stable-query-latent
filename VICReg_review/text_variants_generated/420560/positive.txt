*Firefighting Simulator - The Squad* delivers an exciting and authentic firefighting experience, letting you step into the boots of an active member of a major US city’s fire department. Set in a detailed 15k-acre townscape inspired by the North American West Coast, the game offers more than 40 diverse deployment locations across neighborhoods such as industrial areas, suburbs, and downtown districts, creating plenty of opportunities to respond to emergencies and save lives.

At the heart of the experience is realistic firefighting action. Players can operate faithfully reproduced Rosenbauer America fire trucks, including licensed vehicles such as the TP3® Pumper and the T-Rex® hydraulic platform, even from a cockpit view. The game also features authentic equipment from well-known North American firefighting brands, including helmets, firefighter boots, breathing apparatus, Cairns®, MSA G1® SCBA, and HAIX®. With lights and sirens engaged, you race to the scene, take the shortest route, and battle the flames before every minute counts.

The game supports both cooperative and single-player play. In multiplayer, up to three friends can join together in co-op mode, with each player free to take on the role that suits them best. In single-player mode, you lead an experienced firefighting team and use an intuitive command UI to assign tasks to AI teammates while jumping directly into the action yourself.

Fire behavior is simulated in impressive detail, with systems for water, smoke, heat, backdrafts, flashovers, grease fires, and various ignition causes such as electronics, chemicals, and explosions. A complex physics system adds realism by modeling the destructive spread of fire dynamically. This depth, combined with several possible ways to handle each of the 40 deployment locations in both day and night conditions, gives the game strong replay value.

The presentation is enhanced by a comprehensive tutorial, radio communications, character recordings in English, subtitles in multiple languages, and faithfully recreated engine sounds that strengthen the atmosphere. With support for standard steering wheels and gamepads, *Firefighting Simulator - The Squad* offers a polished and immersive firefighting simulation that captures the urgency, teamwork, and responsibility of protecting a city in crisis.
