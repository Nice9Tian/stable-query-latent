This simulation game places players in the role of an active firefighting team member responding to emergencies across a large North American city. The setting is a detailed urban map spanning around 15,000 acres, with districts such as industrial zones, suburban neighborhoods, and downtown areas. Across more than 40 deployment locations, players handle missions that involve extinguishing fires, rescuing civilians, and reaching incidents quickly before conditions worsen.

Gameplay centers on operating realistically modeled emergency vehicles and using authentic protective equipment. Players can drive multiple licensed fire engines and an aerial platform, with cockpit view available for a more immersive driving experience. The game also features gear such as helmets, boots, and breathing apparatus equipment from real-world manufacturers, presented here as fictionalized brands like VaroX, Mintair, and Helixon.

The experience can be played solo or in cooperative multiplayer. In co-op mode, up to three friends can join the same team and divide responsibilities as they see fit. In single-player mode, the player leads an AI-assisted crew and uses a command interface to assign tasks while also taking part directly in the firefighting. A comprehensive tutorial introduces the basics and helps players learn the system.

A major emphasis is placed on fire behavior and environmental realism. The simulation includes water, smoke, heat, backdrafts, flashovers, grease fires, and multiple fire sources such as electronics, chemicals, and explosions. A physics system governs how fire spreads and how destruction develops dynamically over time, adding tactical pressure to each response.

Presentation also supports immersion through radio communication, voice recordings in English, subtitles in several languages, and recreated engine sounds. The game supports standard steering wheels and gamepads, and missions offer replay value by allowing different approaches both during the day and at night.
