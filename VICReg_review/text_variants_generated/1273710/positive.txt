**King of Crabs** delivers fast-paced, real-time multiplayer action with a fun and competitive twist. Set across a huge island world, the game challenges players to fight, scavenge, and survive as they work toward becoming the biggest crab on the island. With matches supporting up to 100 players, every battle feels intense, chaotic, and full of opportunity.

One of the game’s biggest strengths is its variety. Players can collect a weird and wonderful range of crab species, discover advanced skills, and unlock unique skins. These crabs can be upgraded and customized, adding a satisfying sense of progression as you climb the ranks and push for the top of the leaderboard.

The gameplay offers plenty of ways to test your skills, with multiple modes including PVE, PVP io-style combat, Battle Royale, Team play, and Daily Special Events. Whether you prefer battling waves of crustaceans, competing against other players, or joining team-based challenges, there is always a new way to prove your worth.
