King of Crabs is a real-time multiplayer game centered on becoming the largest crab on the island. It supports matches with up to 100 players, placing players in a large island world that can be explored and contested.

The game focuses on fast-paced combat and progression. Players can scrap, scavenge, and fight through waves of crustaceans while competing to climb the leaderboard and prove they are the strongest crab. It features a variety of crab species to collect, along with advanced skills and unique skins that can be unlocked.

Customization and upgrades are part of the experience, allowing players to improve their crabs as they rank up. The game also includes several modes, such as PVE, PVP iO-style gameplay, Battle Royale, Team modes, and Daily Special Events. Additional content, including new crabs, skins, maps, special events, and game modes, is planned for future updates.
