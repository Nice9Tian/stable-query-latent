In this action-focused multiplayer game, players compete to become the largest and strongest crustacean on a sprawling island. Matches support up to 100 participants at once, creating a fast-paced environment where survival, dominance, and progression all matter. The world is presented as a large island to explore and conquer, with direct competition forming the core of the experience.

Gameplay revolves around moving through the environment, gathering resources, and fighting through successive waves of shell-bearing enemies. Players must scrap, scavenge, and defeat opponents to grow stronger and rise through the ranks. A leaderboard tracks performance, giving players a clear goal as they attempt to prove themselves as the most powerful contender on the island.

Progression includes collecting a wide range of creature variants, each with its own unusual traits. The game also features advanced abilities and distinctive cosmetic appearances that can be unlocked and gathered over time. These elements can be improved and customized, allowing players to tailor their approach while working toward higher positions in the standings.

Several modes are available, including player-versus-environment, player-versus-player in an io-style format, battle royale, team play, and daily special events. The game also promises future additions such as new creature types, cosmetic options, maps, special events, and modes, suggesting an experience that continues to expand over time.
