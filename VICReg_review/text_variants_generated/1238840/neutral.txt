Battlefield™ 1 is a first-person shooter set during The Great War, or World War I, placing players in a global conflict shaped by new technology and major changes in warfare. The game is presented as a critically acclaimed entry in the Battlefield series and is recognized for its large-scale battles and historical wartime setting.

The Battlefield 1 Revolution edition is described as a complete package that includes the base game, the Battlefield 1 Premium Pass, and themed content packs such as the Red Baron Pack, Lawrence of Arabia Pack, and Hellfighter Pack. The Premium Pass adds four themed expansion packs—They Shall Not Pass, In the Name of the Tsar, Turning Tides, and Apocalypse—bringing new multiplayer maps, new weapons, and other content.

The base game offers both a campaign and multiplayer. In the campaign, players can experience a world at war through an adventure-focused storyline. In multiplayer, they can join team-based battles with up to 64 players. Combat includes fighting as infantry as well as taking control of vehicles on land, in the air, and at sea.

A key feature of Battlefield 1 is its varied battlefields and dynamic environments. The game takes players across locations around the world, including besieged French cities, open areas in the Italian Alps, and vast Arabian deserts. Environments are fully destructible, and weather changes over time, making battles feel different from moment to moment.

The game also emphasizes large and powerful vehicles. Players can use tanks and biplanes, as well as massive Behemoths such as an Airship, an Armored Train, and a Dreadnought. These vehicles are designed to have a major impact on the outcome of battle.

Another multiplayer feature is Operations mode, which connects several multiplayer battles across multiple maps. Attackers try to break through defensive lines and advance to the next map, while defenders work to stop them. Overall, Battlefield 1 focuses on large-scale warfare, teamwork, and the varied tactics made possible by its infantry combat, vehicles, and changing battle conditions.
