This first-person shooter presents a large-scale depiction of a global war, set during the era of early modern mechanized conflict. It offers a single-player campaign built around a world at war, alongside team-based multiplayer battles that support up to 64 players. Players can fight on foot as infantry or take control of a wide range of vehicles across land, air, and sea.

A complete edition of the game bundles the base experience with additional expansion content, adding new multiplayer maps, weapons, and further material across multiple themed packs. It also includes several themed bonus packs inspired by notable figures and units, providing exclusive weapons, vehicles, and emblems.

The game emphasizes varied battlefields in locations around the world. Combat can unfold in besieged European cities, open mountain landscapes, or vast desert environments. These settings are shaped by destructible structures and dynamic weather, making the battlefield feel different from moment to moment. Gunfire can break fortifications apart, while explosions can reshape the terrain with craters and debris.

Multiplayer is designed around large, coordinated battles. Players can charge forward as infantry, lead cavalry assaults, and rely on teamwork to survive intense encounters. Vehicle play is a major part of the experience, with options ranging from tanks and aircraft to enormous special-purpose war machines. These powerful units can dramatically influence the outcome of a match, whether by delivering heavy bombardment from the air, crushing ground forces by rail, or shelling targets from the sea.

One of the featured multiplayer modes focuses on multi-stage operations across several maps. In this mode, attackers try to break through defensive lines and advance the conflict to the next battlefield, while defenders work to stop the push. The result is a connected sequence of clashes that gives each match a broader sense of progression and escalation.
