Battlefield™ 1 delivers a powerful and acclaimed first-person shooter experience that brings players into the epic battles of The Great War. Celebrated by critics and recognized with major awards, it stands out as a bold and memorable entry in the Battlefield series, offering large-scale warfare, dramatic settings, and intense team-based action.

Battlefield 1 Revolution provides the complete package. It includes the base game, where players can experience the dawn of all-out war through an adventure-filled single-player campaign or dive into epic multiplayer battles with up to 64 players. Combat is varied and dynamic, allowing players to fight as infantry or take control of remarkable vehicles across land, air, and sea. The package also includes the Battlefield 1 Premium Pass, which adds four themed expansion packs — They Shall Not Pass, In the Name of the Tsar, Turning Tides, and Apocalypse — bringing new multiplayer maps, new weapons, and more. In addition, the Red Baron Pack, Lawrence of Arabia Pack, and Hellfighter Pack provide themed weapons, vehicles, and emblems inspired by famous heroes and units.

Set in World War I, Battlefield 1 captures a time when new technology and global conflict transformed warfare forever. The game invites players to take part in battles across the world, command massive vehicles, and carry out maneuvers capable of shifting the course of an entire fight. Its environments span besieged French cities, the wide-open spaces of the Italian Alps, and the vast Arabian deserts, giving the war a grand global scale.

One of the game’s greatest strengths is its changing battlefield. Fully destructible environments and ever-changing weather ensure that no two battles feel the same. Gunfire can tear apart fortifications, explosions can leave craters in the ground, and the landscape itself evolves moment to moment, keeping every encounter fresh and exciting.

The multiplayer experience is equally impressive, with huge battles supporting up to 64 players. Players can charge in on foot as infantry, lead cavalry assaults, and rely on teamwork to survive the game’s intense and complex conflicts. Vehicles also play a game-changing role, from tanks and biplanes to massive Behemoths. These powerful machines can turn the tide in dramatic fashion, whether it’s an Airship raining fire from above, an Armored Train cutting through the battlefield, or a Dreadnought bombarding from the sea.

Battlefield 1 also introduces the Operations multiplayer mode, a strong and strategic addition to the experience. In this mode, players take part in a series of connected multiplayer battles across multiple maps, with attackers working to break through defensive lines and advance the conflict, while defenders fight to hold them back.
