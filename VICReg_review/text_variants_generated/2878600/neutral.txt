Harry Potter: Quidditch Champions is a Quidditch-focused game set in the wizarding world, designed for solo play or shared play with friends and family. The game emphasizes flying competition and offers a variety of ways to experience Quidditch without microtransactions.

Players can take on one of four classic Quidditch roles: Chaser, Seeker, Keeper, or Beater. Each position has its own distinct play style. Matches take place across legendary Quidditch arenas, along with maps that feature areas of the wizarding world not previously seen.

The game includes several modes. In Career Mode, players progress from backyard battles at the Weasley Burrow to the Quidditch World Cup, either solo or in online co-op with teams of up to three friends. Exhibition Matches allow players to choose teams, maps, and difficulty, and play alone or in online co-op with up to three players. Player vs Player mode lets teams compete in online matches against other players.

Progression and customization are also part of the experience. Players can level up, spend skill points to refine their play style for each role, and switch between different brooms, leveling them up to change flight style. The game also offers cosmetic customization and the option to play as either the player’s own character or iconic Harry Potter characters.

The Deluxe Edition includes House Packs for Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Slytherin, and Ravenclaw, as well as 2,000 Gold.
