This fantasy sports game invites players to experience an aerial competition set in a magical world, either solo or with friends and family. It emphasizes cooperative play and competitive online matches, while also highlighting that no microtransactions are included.

Players can choose among several distinct roles, each with a different play style: attacker, scorer, defender, or disruptor. Matches take place in legendary arenas and in additional maps that reveal new areas of the wizarding setting.

The game offers multiple modes. In a progression-based campaign, players move from informal backyard-style contests to major championship-level events, either alone or in online co-op with teams of up to three friends. There are also custom exhibition matches, where teams, maps, and difficulty can be selected freely, and online player-versus-player matches for competitive play.

Progression and customization are central to the experience. Players can level up, spend skill points to refine their chosen role, and switch between different flying vehicles, improving them over time. Visual customization is also available, allowing players to create their own look or use classic character designs. A deluxe version includes four house-themed packs and a currency bonus.
