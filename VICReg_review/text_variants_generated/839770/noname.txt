This tactical strategy game presents a devastated world in which humanity is fighting to survive against a rapidly mutating alien threat. Players lead a secret resistance organization tasked with rebuilding civilization, repelling the invasion, and uncovering the truth behind the outbreak. The experience combines global management, base construction, technology research, resource allocation, exploration, and turn-based combat across a shattered planet.

A major part of the game revolves around interaction with several competing human factions. Each group follows its own ideology, objectives, and interpretation of the alien crisis, creating opportunities for cooperation, manipulation, or open conflict. Players can build diplomatic relationships, exploit subterfuge to obtain resources and advantages, or overpower rivals through military force. These choices shape the campaign and affect how the broader struggle unfolds.

Combat emphasizes tactical decision-making and direct battlefield control. Units are equipped and commanded in missions where players can also manually aim shots, allowing precise targeting of enemy weak points, weapons, or other specific areas. This system adds a layer of granular control to each encounter, supporting different approaches depending on the situation.

The alien enemies are not static: they mutate and adapt in response to player tactics, creating an escalating challenge that remains relevant even as the player’s forces grow stronger and more advanced. The narrative also plays an important role, offering a layered story with multiple endings and encouraging repeated playthroughs to uncover hidden details about the origins of the infection, the mutants, and the organization at the center of the conflict.

The game includes substantial post-release support, with updates, fixes, added content, and workshop integration for mods. Players can subscribe to modifications, enable them, adjust existing ones, and tailor campaigns to their preferences, including custom campaign options. Overall, it is a modern strategy title rooted in classic design principles, blending large-scale management with detailed combat and a reactive world full of competing interests.
