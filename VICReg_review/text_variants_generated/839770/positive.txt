Phoenix Point is an acclaimed strategy game from the creator of X-COM, now enhanced with Steam Workshop support. Set on an Earth overrun by a mutating alien menace, the game places players in command of the Phoenix Project, a secret organization made up of the best minds and bravest soldiers left on the planet. As humanity’s last hope, you must research and develop new technologies, explore a ravaged world, build bases, manage resources, and lead your forces across intense tactical battlefields.

One of Phoenix Point’s greatest strengths is its living, hostile world. The alien threat, known as the Pandorans, does not remain static; it mutates and adapts to your tactics, creating a relentless challenge that continues to evolve even as your team grows stronger and more advanced. The game also features a rich narrative tied to the Pandoravirus, with secrets, multiple endings, and story revelations that unfold across several playthroughs.

Diplomacy adds another compelling layer to the experience. The Phoenix Project is not alone in the struggle to reclaim Earth, and factions such as New Jericho, Synedrion, and the Disciples of Anu each bring their own values, ambitions, and attitudes toward the Pandorans. Players can cooperate with these groups, use subterfuge to steal what they need, or confront them directly with military force. Every choice carries consequences, and victory is never simple.

Combat is equally distinctive, thanks to Phoenix Point’s free-aiming system. In addition to commanding units and managing equipment, players can directly aim at enemy weak spots, weapons, valuables, or center mass, giving every shot a tactical purpose. Combined with the game’s modern presentation and the classic strategy heritage of Julian Gollop, creator of the original X-COM series, Phoenix Point delivers a powerful blend of tradition and innovation.

With years of updates, fixes, community-suggested content, and full Steam Workshop support for mods, Phoenix Point offers a substantial and highly customizable strategy experience. It stands as a bold, feature-rich evolution of tactical strategy, rewarding careful planning, adaptability, and decisive leadership.
