Redout 2 is a futuristic anti-gravity racing game and the sequel to Redout. Set in the dystopian wastelands of a semi-abandoned Earth, it presents racing as one of the galaxy’s most popular sports and focuses on reaching extreme speeds across high-intensity tracks.

The game is built around fast arcade racing with an emphasis on both accessibility and depth. Its control system is designed to be intuitive enough to pick up quickly, while also offering a high skill ceiling for players who want to master its handling. Races feature sharp bends, loops, swoops, and large jumps, reflecting its inspiration from classic anti-gravity racers such as Wipeout and F-Zero.

Redout 2 includes an extensive single-player career mode with hundreds of events spread across 36 unique racetracks, all of which are reversible. Event types include Arena Races, Time Attacks, Last Man Standing, and Boss Races. For multiplayer, the game supports intense 12-player online races and adds fresh challenges through regularly added custom content and seasonal content with unique aesthetic rewards.

Customization is a major part of the experience. Players can choose from 12 chassis and personalize their hovership with a wide range of parts and options, including propulsors, stabilizers, rudders, intercoolers, flaps, magnets, wings, spoilers, rocket engines, paints, and more. The game also includes a Photo Mode for capturing and sharing highlights.

Its soundtrack is another highlighted feature, featuring electronic artists such as Giorgio Moroder, Zardonic, and Dance with the Dead. The music is dynamically mixed by the game’s algorithms based on real-time race data.
