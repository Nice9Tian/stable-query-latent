**Redout 2** is a thrilling tribute to classic arcade anti-gravity racing, delivering some of the fastest speeds in the universe with style and confidence. As the sequel to the critically acclaimed *Redout*, it takes players across the dystopian wastelands of a semi-abandoned Earth, where futuristic racing stands as one of the galaxy’s most popular sports.

At its core, *Redout 2* is built around pure velocity and precise control. Drawing inspiration from legendary racers like *Wipeout* and *F-Zero*, it offers blazing-fast anti-gravity action that is easy to pick up, yet deeply rewarding for players who want to master its high skill ceiling. The tracks are filled with extreme bends, loops, swoops, and jumps, creating races that feel intense, exhilarating, and unforgettable.

The game features an extensive career mode with hundreds of events spread across 36 unique racetracks, all of which are reversible. Players can take on a wide variety of race types, including Arena Races, Time Attacks, Last Man Standing challenges, and demanding Boss Races, all while pushing toward the finish line at impossible speed.

For those who prefer competition against real players, *Redout 2* includes intense 12-player online multiplayer. The experience is kept fresh with regularly added custom content and seasons that offer unique aesthetic rewards, making the competitive side feel lively and constantly evolving.

Customization is another major strength of the game. Players can choose from 12 distinct chassis and fine-tune their hovership with a huge range of parts, including propulsors, stabilizers, rudders, intercoolers, flaps, magnets, wings, spoilers, rocket engines, paints, and more. A Photo Mode also lets players capture and share their best high-speed moments.

To complete the experience, *Redout 2* features a powerful soundtrack with superstar electronic artists such as Giorgio Moroder, Zardonic, and Dance with the Dead. Its music system dynamically mixes tracks based on real-time race data, adding even more energy to every race.

Fast, stylish, and packed with depth, *Redout 2* stands out as a premier anti-gravity racer.
