This high-speed futuristic racing title presents a tribute to classic arcade racers, set in a dystopian version of a semi-abandoned Earth where anti-gravity competition is a major interstellar sport. Players are invited to reach extreme velocities across a large single-player campaign and in competitive online races, with an emphasis on precise handling, deep vehicle control, and extensive customization.

The game centers on intense speed combined with a control system designed to be accessible at first, while still offering a high skill ceiling for players who want to master it. Tracks are filled with dramatic bends, loops, jumps, and other demanding course designs that test reflexes and route knowledge. The racing experience is built around the sensation of rapid movement and the challenge of maintaining control through complex airborne sections.

Its career mode includes hundreds of events spread across 36 tracks, all of which can be raced in reverse. Event types vary widely, including arena-style races, time trials, elimination formats, and boss encounters. This structure gives the single-player portion a broad range of objectives and pacing.

Multiplayer supports up to 12 players online, offering direct competition against others. The game also adds fresh custom content over time and includes seasonal progression with unique cosmetic rewards, giving the online side ongoing variety.

Customization is a major feature. Players can select from 12 chassis types and modify their vehicle with a large set of components and cosmetic options, such as propulsion units, stabilizers, steering parts, cooling elements, aerodynamic attachments, and paint schemes. A photo mode is included for capturing and sharing high-speed moments.

The presentation is rounded out by an electronic soundtrack featuring notable artists, with music that dynamically changes according to race conditions. Overall, the game emphasizes fast arcade racing, competitive replayability, and detailed vehicle personalization in a futuristic setting.
