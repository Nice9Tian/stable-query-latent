This upcoming single-player strategy adventure is a story-driven spin-off set in the 1980s, combining tower defense, base building, and management elements. The player takes on the role of a young leader in a small town threatened by a mysterious force that has escaped from the shadows. The goal is to rally neighborhood kids, gather resources, and organize defenses to protect the town from relentless attacks by an enemy that seeks a powerful family heirloom for unknown purposes.

Gameplay centers on recruiting a small team of three companions, each with distinct abilities, and expanding a local resistance by bringing in more kids to help. These recruits can be assigned to tasks such as collecting coins, building defenses, and running small businesses to support the town’s survival. Players must also manage their people carefully, unlock new tools, and equip the group to withstand the dangers that arrive after dark.

The game presents its story in a comic-like format, focusing on a town under threat and the mystery of why the adults are missing. As the player repels waves of hostile creatures, clues about the larger situation gradually emerge. The presentation uses handcrafted pixel art with a neon-inspired visual style, paired with a synth-heavy soundtrack designed to evoke the atmosphere of the 1980s.

In addition to the story campaign, the game includes a survival mode built around arcade-style challenge waves. This mode features multiple episodes, progressively harder enemy attacks, and changing conditions such as disabled building abilities and random mounts. It emphasizes quick decisions, tactical adaptation, and long-term endurance against increasingly difficult threats.
