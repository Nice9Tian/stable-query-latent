Kingdom Eighties is an upcoming single-player story-based tower defense spin-off of the award-winning Kingdom series, set in the 1980s. It combines base building and strategy elements with a neon, pixel-art presentation and a soundtrack inspired by 80s synth music.

In the game, you play as The Leader, a kid trying to save the town of Monarch from the mysterious Greed monsters after something dangerous breaks free from the shadows. Your mission is to protect your family heirloom, the Crown of Creation, while uncovering what happened to the town’s adults and the strange force threatening the community. The story is presented as a comic-sized adventure centered on defending the town from attacks and pushing the Greed back.

Gameplay revolves around recruiting friends and other kids from the neighborhood, including three unique companions with special skills. You gather people to build defenses, collect coins, and manage activities such as lemonade stands and pizza shops. You also unlock tools and gear up your crew to prepare for night-time threats. The game emphasizes managing the people you recruit and organizing your defenses against increasingly dangerous attacks.

Kingdom Eighties also features a new arcade-style mode called Survival Mixtapes. This mode includes four episodes, such as “Back to the Suburbs” and “Greed, First Goo,” where players face progressively harder waves of Greed. It adds extra challenge through random mounts and by disabling certain buildings’ special abilities, requiring quick tactical decisions.

Visually, the game retains Kingdom’s handcrafted art style with an 80s neon touch, and it is accompanied by a synth-heavy soundtrack by Andreas Hald. The overall setting evokes summer camps, bike riding, and the atmosphere of the era.
