Kingdom Eighties is a stylish, story-driven tower defense spin-off in the award-winning Kingdom series, set in the vibrant spirit of the 1980s. This single-player adventure blends base building, strategy, and a strong sense of nostalgia, all wrapped in neon-soaked pixel art and a synth-heavy soundtrack inspired by the era’s iconic sound.

In this adventure, you step into the role of The Leader, a determined kid tasked with defending the small town of Monarch after something dark breaks free from the shadows. Alongside your three unique companions, each with their own special skills, you recruit neighborhood kids to help gather coins, build defenses, and keep the town alive against the relentless Greed monsters. From managing your growing group of recruits to unlocking tools and outfitting your crew, every decision matters as you prepare for the dangers that arrive at night.

The game also features a captivating story centered on the mystery of a dangerous force that has resurfaced after centuries, with clues to uncover as you repel the Greed and search for answers about what happened to everyone else. The setting captures the carefree wonder of summer camps, bike rides, and endless possibilities, while the handcrafted art style returns with a fresh neon twist that perfectly matches the ‘80s theme.

For players looking for an extra challenge, Kingdom Eighties includes Survival Mixtapes Mode, an arcade-style mode with four episodes, including “Back to the Suburbs” and “Greed, First Goo.” Here, you face progressively tougher waves of Greed while adapting on the fly to changing conditions, such as disabled building abilities and random mounts. It’s a demanding test of strategy that adds even more depth to an already exciting experience.

With its compelling story, creative tower defense mechanics, and unforgettable retro atmosphere, Kingdom Eighties stands out as a charming and energetic tribute to the best of the 1980s.
