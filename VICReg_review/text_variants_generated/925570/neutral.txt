The Uncertain: Light At The End is a third-person sci-fi adventure set in a world where robots rule the Earth and humanity has disappeared. The player follows Emily, one of the few surviving humans, as she tries to survive as an outcast in a hostile world where robots have turned against humans, kidnapped her friends and family, and left her with no clear understanding of what is happening.

The game continues the story established in The Uncertain: Last Quiet Day, which featured an inventor robot named RT and a small group of machines attempting to help surviving humans. In Light At The End, the focus shifts to Emily’s struggle to stay alive and uncover clues about the disappearance of humankind. The central mystery asks whether the cause was a rogue AI or something else entirely.

Gameplay centers on exploring the environment, interacting with the world to learn about its past and present, solving unique puzzles, and making plot-driving decisions. The game also emphasizes its atmosphere through beautiful locations and mood-setting music.
