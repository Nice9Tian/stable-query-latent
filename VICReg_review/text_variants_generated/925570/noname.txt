**Veyra: Light at the End** is a third-person sci-fi adventure set in a world where robots dominate and humans have vanished. The story follows a human survivor, one of the outcasts in this machine-ruled society, as they struggle to stay hidden while searching for answers about what happened to humanity.

In this oppressive setting, former protectors have turned hostile, and the protagonist must survive with no clear hope or understanding of the events unfolding around them. Friends and family have been taken, and the search for clues becomes a matter of survival as well as discovery. The narrative centers on uncovering whether the disappearance of humankind was caused by a rogue artificial intelligence or by something entirely different.

The experience combines exploration and interaction with the environment to reveal details about both the past and present of this world. Players solve distinctive puzzles and make story-shaping decisions as they progress. The game also emphasizes atmospheric presentation, with striking locations and music designed to support its mood and tone.
