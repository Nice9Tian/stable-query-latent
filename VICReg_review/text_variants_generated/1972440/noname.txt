This game presents a darkly humorous twist on a familiar board-game structure, turning a strategic duel into a solitary campaign of survival. Nearly an entire army has defected, leaving the player with only a royal shotgun and the determination to reclaim lost ground. The core loop is built around alternating between movement and ranged attacks: on each turn, the king can either reposition or fire at opposing pieces, and shooting requires an additional move afterward to reload. The objective on each floor is straightforward but demanding—avoid being trapped in checkmate while eliminating the enemy king.

Progression is structured around floors, and after clearing one, the player must choose between two random paired upgrades: one benefit for themselves and one for the opposing side. This introduces a constant tradeoff, since every improvement comes with a corresponding risk. Success across successive floors is the path toward reclaiming the kingdom, with each run typically lasting around 20 minutes.

The game also offers a substantial amount of replayability. It includes 15 difficulty ranks, unlockable cards, more than 100 achievements, an endless mode, and a chase mode in which threats continue to attack from all directions. Overall, it combines tactical movement, resource-like reload timing, and escalating challenge into a compact and replayable strategy experience.
