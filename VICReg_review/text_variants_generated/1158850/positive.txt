The Great Ace Attorney Chronicles is a richly entertaining double-pack that invites players into a dramatic, charming, and witty world of evidence-gathering, deduction, and courtroom battles. Following the rookie lawyer Ryunosuke Naruhodo, ancestor of series mainstay Phoenix Wright, the collection unfolds across late 19th century Japan and England, delivering a classic Ace Attorney experience filled with murder mysteries, surprising twists, lively humor, and unforgettable turnabouts.

Each of the two titles includes five gripping case episodes, offering dozens of hours of attorney action. As the story progresses, players work to uncover the truth behind an international criminal conspiracy and a haunting cold case, while navigating a series of challenging trials. Investigation is a key part of the experience: you speak to witnesses, examine clues and evidence, and piece together the dastardly intent behind each crime before heading into the courtroom.

Once in court, the drama intensifies. You hear testimonies, cross-examine witnesses, and present evidence in order to expose contradictions and defend your client. When the facts are on your side, you can even shout the iconic “OBJECTION!” to challenge the prosecution’s claims. For newcomers, the game also includes a helpful assistance feature, making it easy to jump into these amateur sleuthing adventures even if it is your first Ace Attorney game.

With its engaging mysteries, clever courtroom gameplay, and accessible support features, The Great Ace Attorney Chronicles offers a compelling and highly polished detective experience. Players can enjoy the game with English audio or with Japanese audio and English subtitles, making this acclaimed collection an excellent way to experience a memorable legal adventure.
