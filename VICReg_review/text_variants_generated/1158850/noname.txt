This double-pack presents a dramatic yet charming courtroom adventure centered on evidence-gathering, deduction, and legal battles. Set across late 19th century Japan and England, it follows a rookie lawyer and unfolds through ten murder-mystery episodes, each built around a mix of humor, twists, and surprising reversals. The stories explore an international criminal conspiracy as well as a puzzling cold case, while the player navigates a series of challenging trials.

Gameplay is divided between investigation and courtroom segments. During investigations, players speak with witnesses, examine evidence and clues, and work to infer the hidden motive behind each crime before moving into trial. In court, testimonies must be heard carefully, witnesses cross-examined, and evidence presented at the right moment to challenge the opposing side’s claims and defend the client. The experience emphasizes contradiction, deduction, and the satisfaction of exposing inconsistencies through careful reasoning.

An assistance feature is included to help players who reach an impasse during investigations, making the game approachable even for newcomers to the series. The package offers substantial content, with many hours of legal drama and mystery-solving, and supports either English audio or Japanese audio with English subtitles.
