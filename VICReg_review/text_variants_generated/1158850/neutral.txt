The Great Ace Attorney Chronicles is a double-pack of courtroom adventure games centered on rookie lawyer Ryunosuke Naruhodo. Set in late 19th century Japan and England, the collection presents five murder-mystery episodes in each title, combining evidence-gathering, deduction, and courtroom battles in the style of the Ace Attorney series.

Across the cases, players speak to witnesses, examine clues and evidence, and work to determine the intent behind each crime before moving into trial proceedings. In court, the gameplay involves hearing testimonies, cross-examining witnesses, and presenting evidence to challenge the prosecution’s claims. The familiar “OBJECTION!” moment is part of the experience when players believe they have the facts needed to defend their client.

The story follows Ryunosuke Naruhodo, an ancestor of Phoenix Wright, as he uncovers the secrets behind an international criminal conspiracy and investigates a haunting cold case, while also navigating a series of trials. The store page describes the game as offering dozens of hours of content, with dramatic, witty, and twist-filled cases.

An assistance feature is included to help players if they get stuck during investigations, making the game approachable even for first-time Ace Attorney players. The game supports either English audio or Japanese audio with English subtitles.
