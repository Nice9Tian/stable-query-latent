*Veyra: The Hollow March* is an open-world, tactical turn-based RPG set in a bleak low-fantasy realm. Players lead a band of wanderers through the ruins of an ancient civilization, recruiting new followers, scavenging from defeated enemies, and trying to endure a harsh world that offers little mercy.

The game emphasizes survival, exploration, and combat. Its battles take place on large maps and are shaped by tactical positioning, environmental hazards, and a carefully designed class-and-skill system. Combat is turn-based and supports a variety of approaches: enemies can be pushed into spikes or off high cliffs, allies can be shielded, counterattacks can be triggered when opponents strike, ranged units can provide support from a distance, and melee actions can be combined into numerous possible combo attacks.

A key feature of *Veyra: The Hollow March* is its procedural survival campaign, which ensures that each run unfolds differently. Another unusual mechanic is the ability to extract character upgrades directly from fallen foes. These extracted traits can significantly alter a unit’s role in battle, granting abilities such as forcing an enemy backward on a critical hit, retaliating when hit, surviving what would otherwise be a fatal blow, or executing a lifestealing counterattack. Characters can also learn new skills by performing specific actions during combat, rewarding attentive play and tactical experimentation.

The setting is intentionally grounded and unforgiving. It contains no elves, dwarfs, dragons, or other classic fantasy creatures, and magical elements are present only in a limited form. The world’s history is marked by the disappearance of a race of giants, once peaceful neighbors of humanity, whose extinction followed a devastating war. Centuries later, scientists discovered a method of extracting the Life Essence of these beings from their bones. This substance was believed to hold curative powers, but in practice it often caused mutation or deadly illness instead. Despite the danger, experiments on unwilling subjects continued.

The story follows the protagonist, **Veyra**, who escapes from a secluded medical facility where such experiments are performed on abducted commoners. After exposure to the Life Essence, Veyra suffers a severe mutation that worsens over time, forcing a desperate search for a cure. As the condition progresses, the character’s survival becomes increasingly uncertain, creating the central tension that drives the journey.

Visually, all graphical assets are hand-drawn, including characters, monsters, objects, the world map, and the battlefields, giving the game a distinct illustrated style that matches its dark atmosphere.
