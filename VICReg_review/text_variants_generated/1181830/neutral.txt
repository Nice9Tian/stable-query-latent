Urtuk: The Desolation is an open-world, tactical turn-based RPG set in a low-fantasy, dark world. Players lead a band of adventurers through the ruins of an ancient civilization, recruiting followers, looting fallen enemies, and trying to survive in a harsh and unforgiving landscape. The game emphasizes combat and exploration, with a procedurally generated survival campaign and large battle maps shaped by environmental factors.

Combat is turn-based and tactically focused. Battles take place on expansive maps where positioning matters, and the game supports a range of interactions with the environment. Enemies can be shoved into spikes or over high cliffs, while allies can be shielded, and counterattacks can be triggered when opponents strike. Archers provide ranged support, melee attacks can be combined into various combo sequences, and different battlefield hazards can be used to influence the outcome of fights.

A distinctive system in Urtuk: The Desolation is the extraction of character upgrades directly from defeated enemies. Skills and traits can be taken from fallen foes and applied to player units, potentially changing a character’s role in combat. These extracted abilities include effects such as pushing enemies on a critical hit, retaliating when hit, avoiding a lethal strike, and performing a lifestealing counterattack. Characters can also learn new skills by performing specific actions during battles, rewarding attentive play and experimentation.

The game’s setting is explicitly grounded in a bleak form of fantasy. It does not feature elves, dwarves, dragons, or other classical fantasy creatures. Instead, the world is defined by suffering, toil, and darkness, with only light magical elements and no overwhelmingly powerful sorcerers. The lore describes a long-vanished race of Giants that once lived peacefully alongside humans until a war led to their extinction. Centuries later, scientists discovered how to extract the Life Essence of these ancient Giants from their bones, creating a serum that was believed it might cure disease, heal wounds, and even slow aging. However, the process often caused mutations or deadly illnesses instead.

The story follows Urtuk, who escapes from the Sanatorium, where abducted commoners are used in these experiments. After being severely mutated by exposure to the Life Essence, Urtuk’s condition continues to worsen over time. He travels the world in search of a cure, and unless he can find a way to stop his evolving mutation, he is destined to die. The game’s presentation is fully hand drawn, with artwork covering characters, monsters, objects, the world map, and battlefields.
