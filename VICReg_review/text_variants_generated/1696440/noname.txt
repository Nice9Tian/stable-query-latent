This open-world role-playing game is set in a wuxia-inspired martial arts world where players can shape their own path and create their own stories. Rather than following a fixed role, the game encourages experimentation across many different life choices, letting each playthrough become a distinct experience. It supports both traveling through a vast wilderness and taking on a leadership role, with the freedom to build a personal world from the ground up or explore larger generated worlds filled with varied terrain, resources, creatures, and factions.

A major part of the experience is character customization and progression. Players can define a new identity for each life, learn different combat techniques, and assemble a personal skill deck to approach battles in their preferred style. Physical combat is only one option: the game also includes a specialized verbal confrontation system, where unlocking certain techniques allows players to defeat opponents without direct fighting. In addition, a meridian-based inner cultivation system provides buffs and helps handle internal injuries, hidden ailments, illnesses, and other negative effects, reinforcing the wuxia theme of self-cultivation and martial discipline.

The game also emphasizes construction and social simulation. Players can freely build structures such as taverns, hot springs, prisons, and homes, then assign functions to them. Production systems are also present, including the ability to set up equipment manufacturing in a forge-like workshop. Interaction with non-player characters is broad and still being expanded, allowing players to team up with friends or family members, form close relationships, have children, and raise descendants. At the same time, the game offers more unconventional options such as setting fires, keeping animals, building a clan, issuing orders to followers, and imprisoning disliked characters.

Sect management is another important layer. Players can establish their own sects, recruit disciples, manage sect facilities, and oversee land taxes. Animal taming adds further utility, with different creatures offering different support functions: for example, one type can beg, another can be ridden and used with carriages, and another can assist with lockpicking. Stealth training and specialized equipment also make it possible to develop into a thief-oriented character within the martial world.

The game is presented as an independently developed project that continues to grow, with ongoing updates planned. Its current version includes manually checked translations, and players are encouraged to report language issues through the in-game menus. Overall, it is a sandbox-like wuxia RPG built around freedom, personalization, social systems, and multiple ways to survive and thrive in a living martial world.
