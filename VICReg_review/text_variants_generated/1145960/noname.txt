This point-and-click adventure follows a man who awakens in a mental health facility with severe memory loss. The story centers on his strict daily routine, his dreams, and the gradual recovery of forgotten memories. The experience is designed around interactive storytelling, where progress comes from following a structured schedule and piecing together the past through playful investigation.

Gameplay is presented in a split-screen format, creating a distinctive way to experience events inside the facility. Each day brings its own atmosphere, suspense, and a range of unexpected, surreal occurrences. The game combines accessible pick-up-and-play design with brain teasers and riddles that support the narrative and encourage close attention to detail.

The overall presentation emphasizes an absorbing mood, reinforced by an immersive and haunting soundtrack. In addition to the main story, the game includes special achievements and hidden secrets for players to uncover.
