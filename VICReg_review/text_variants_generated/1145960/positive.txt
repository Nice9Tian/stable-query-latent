**The White Door** is a compelling point-and-click adventure from the creators of the *Cube Escape* and *Rusty Lake* series, offering a distinctive and memorable experience built around mystery, memory, and atmosphere.

The game follows Robert Hill, who wakes up in a Mental Health facility with severe memory loss. From there, players guide him through the facility’s strict daily routine, exploring his dreams and helping him piece together fragments of his past. This setup gives the story a strong sense of intrigue, while the interactive narrative keeps the experience engaging from beginning to end.

One of the game’s standout features is its split-screen presentation, which brings a fresh and inventive twist to the adventure format. This design supports the game’s playful approach to storytelling and puzzle-solving, making each step of Robert’s stay in the facility feel both unusual and immersive. The gameplay is easy to pick up, yet it remains hard to put down thanks to its mix of brain teasers, riddles, and unfolding memories.

The atmosphere is another major strength. Each day in the facility carries its own mood, combining suspense with unexpected and surreal events. Paired with an immersive and haunting soundtrack composed by Victor Butzelaar, the game creates a striking emotional tone that perfectly complements its mysterious world.

With special achievements and plenty of secrets to uncover, **The White Door** offers a rich and absorbing adventure that rewards curiosity. It is a beautifully crafted experience that blends storytelling, atmosphere, and clever gameplay into something truly distinctive.
