The White Door is a point-and-click adventure developed by the creators of the Cube Escape and Rusty Lake series. The game follows Robert Hill, who wakes up in a mental health facility suffering from severe memory loss. Players guide him through the facility’s strict daily routine, explore his dreams, and help him recover his memories.

The game centers on an interactive storyline built around recollection and routine. As Robert progresses through each day, players encounter brain teasers, riddles, and other playful elements that support the memory-based narrative. The experience also uses a unique split-screen presentation, presenting Robert’s stay in the facility through an innovative gameplay structure.

Atmosphere is a major part of the game’s design. Each day in the facility brings its own mood, suspense, and a range of unexpected and surreal events. The experience is further supported by an immersive and haunting soundtrack, with atmospheric theme songs composed by Victor Butzelaar.

The White Door is described as easy to start but hard to put down, making it accessible while still offering layers of secrets to uncover. It also includes special achievements, giving players more to discover beyond the main story.
