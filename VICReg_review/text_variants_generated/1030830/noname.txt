This title is a remastered crime drama set in the 1940s and 1950s, presented in HD and included as the complete package with all downloadable content. It follows a war veteran, here referred to as **Vero** Scaletta, who becomes involved with organized crime in a city called **Empyre Bay** after trying to help settle his father’s debts. Working alongside his close friend **Jaro**, Vero rises through the ranks of a powerful family, taking on increasingly dangerous jobs that bring greater rewards, status, and consequences.

The game emphasizes the atmosphere of the golden age of organized crime, drawing inspiration from classic mafia films and stories. Its setting is a post-war American city filled with opportunity, where booming industries and criminal networks coexist. The experience is framed as a character-driven drama about ambition, loyalty, and the difficulty of escaping a criminal life once drawn into it.

As a definitive edition, the release presents the full story and all DLC in one package, with the original game refreshed in high-definition detail. It also includes cross-game bonus content: owning this edition unlocks a special **Vero** outfit and vehicle in related companion titles from the same trilogy.
