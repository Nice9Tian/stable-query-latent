**Mafia II: Definitive Edition** is a remastered HD crime drama that lets players live the life of a gangster in the golden era of organized crime. Set in 1940s–50s Empire Bay, NY, the game follows war hero **Vito Scaletta**, who becomes entangled with the mob in an effort to pay off his father’s debts. Together with his friend **Joe**, Vito works to prove himself, rising through the family ladder as each crime brings greater reward, status, and consequence.

Inspired by classic mafia dramas, the game captures the allure and inescapable tension of life as a wise guy. Empire Bay is portrayed as a sprawling post-World War II city filled with opportunity, where organized crime thrives alongside the booming industries of post-war America. The result is a richly framed crime saga that emphasizes atmosphere, ambition, and the cost of power.

This Definitive Edition includes the **main game and all DLC releases**, presenting the full Mafia II experience in one complete package. It also offers a special bonus: owning **Mafia II: Definitive Edition** unlocks **Vito’s Leather Jacket and Car** in both **Mafia: Definitive Edition** and **Mafia III: Definitive Edition**.
