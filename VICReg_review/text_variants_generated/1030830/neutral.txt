Mafia II: Definitive Edition is the remastered HD version of the second part of the Mafia crime saga, presented as a complete package that includes the main game and all DLC releases. Set in 1940s–50s Empire Bay, New York, it follows war hero Vito Scaletta as he becomes involved with the mob in order to pay off his father’s debts.

Working alongside his friend Joe, Vito rises through the family hierarchy by taking on crimes that bring greater reward, status, and consequence. The game is described as a Golden-era organized crime drama inspired by classic mafia films, focusing on the appeal and inescapability of life as a wise guy. Empire Bay is portrayed as a post-World War II city full of opportunity, where organized crime flourishes alongside the booming industries of post-war America.

As a remastered edition, Mafia II: Definitive Edition presents the story in stunning HD detail and offers the complete Mafia II crime drama in one package. Owning the game also unlocks Vito’s Leather Jacket and Car in both Mafia: Definitive Edition and Mafia III: Definitive Edition.
