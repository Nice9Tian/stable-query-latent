This mechanical action title delivers a new combat experience built around fast-paced battles and highly mobile units. Drawing on the developer’s established experience with mech-focused games and signature action design, it emphasizes intense encounters across large battlefields, with movement possible both on the ground and in the air.

A key feature is its omni-directional combat system, which lets players pilot their customized war machine through dynamic engagements from multiple angles. The scale of the stages and the flexibility of movement encourage constant repositioning, making mobility an important part of survival and success.

The game also centers on deep part customization. Different components can be selected to match a wide range of playstyles, and these choices do more than alter weapon behavior: they also change movement options and overall combat approach. As a result, each mission can be tackled with a different machine setup and strategy.

Encounters with powerful boss enemies further highlight the game’s tactical variety. Players are encouraged to combine offensive and defensive methods at both close and long range, adapting to each opponent’s pressure patterns and attack behavior. In addition to the base game, the deluxe release includes an exclusive digital art collection and original music soundtrack.
