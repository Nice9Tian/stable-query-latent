ARMORED CORE™ VI FIRES OF RUBICON™ is a mech action game from FromSoftware that combines the studio’s experience with mech games and its signature action gameplay. It offers a new action experience for the series, built around fast-paced combat and highly flexible mech customization.

The game features dynamic, omni-directional battles across large stages, with players piloting their mech on land and in the air to gain an advantage in combat. Mobility is a central part of the experience, allowing for movement and attacks from multiple directions.

Customization is a key mechanic. Players can equip different Armored Core parts to fit a wide range of playstyles. These parts do more than alter weapons and attacks: they also change movement and overall battle style, enabling different strategies for different missions.

The game also includes challenging boss battles that require the use of offensive and defensive tactics at both close and long range. In addition to the standard edition, a Digital Deluxe Edition is available, including the full game, an exclusive digital artbook, and the original soundtrack.
