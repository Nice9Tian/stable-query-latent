ARMORED CORE™ VI FIRES OF RUBICON™ delivers a powerful new action experience that builds on FromSoftware’s long-standing expertise in mech games and its signature combat design. The result is a thrilling, high-speed battle game that emphasizes mobility, strategy, and the excitement of piloting a fully customized mech across massive stages.

One of the game’s standout features is its dynamic, omni-directional battle system. Players command their Armored Core in fast-paced combat that makes full use of movement on both land and in the air, creating encounters that feel fluid, intense, and highly responsive. The large battle environments and versatile mobility options give every fight a strong sense of scale and momentum.

Customization is another major strength. Players can tailor Armored Core parts to match a wide variety of playstyles, and these choices do more than alter attacks—they directly influence movement and overall battle behavior. This makes every build meaningful and allows each mission to be approached with a distinct mech strategy.

The game also features thrilling boss battles that challenge players to combine offensive and defensive tactics at both close and long range. These encounters highlight the depth of the combat system and reward careful adaptation, making each victory feel hard-earned and satisfying.

For players looking for even more, the Digital Deluxe Edition includes the full game along with an Exclusive Digital Artbook and Original Soundtrack, offering an excellent package for fans who want to enjoy the world of ARMORED CORE VI FIRES OF RUBICON even more deeply.
