This crafting adventure is set in an expressive land of gothic fables and fairytales. Players take on the role of a mysterious old witch who explores a strange countryside, gathers magical ingredients, brews sorcerous enchantments, and delivers twisted judgement on a capricious cast of characters and creatures. The experience is framed around teaching others the moral of the story through magical intervention and mischief.

The game features a lovingly rendered storybook art style and emphasizes collecting, growing, and crafting bizarre ingredients such as a poisonous fungus, a reptilian eye, and captured fear. Players can research and concoct devious spells and sorceries, including transformations that punish the greedy and curses that trick the wicked. Exploration takes place across an allegorical world of tall tales, where colorful personas and fantastical narratives are uncovered.

Gameplay also includes solving puzzles and using wit and guile to overcome strange forest monsters. Overall, the game combines crafting, exploration, puzzle-solving, and dark fairy-tale storytelling into a whimsical but mischievous adventure.
