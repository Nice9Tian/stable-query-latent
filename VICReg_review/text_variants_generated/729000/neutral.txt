Wytchwood is a crafting adventure game set in an expressive world of gothic fables and fairytales. Players take on the role of a mysterious old witch of the woods and explore a strange countryside filled with colourful personas, fantastical narratives, and curious creatures. The game centers on gathering magical ingredients, brewing sorcerous enchantments, and using twisted judgement to shape the fate of a capricious cast of characters.

Its gameplay focuses on collecting, growing, and crafting bizarre ingredients such as toxic toadstools, eye of newt, and bottled fear. Players can research and concoct devious spells and sorceries, including curses that turn the greedy into frogs or otherwise trick the wicked. The game also includes puzzle-solving and encounters with strange forest monsters, with progress driven by wit and guile.

Wytchwood is presented in a lovingly rendered storybook art style, supporting its allegorical world of tall tales and moral lessons.
