Professional Farmer: Cattle and Crops delivers a confident and richly detailed farming simulation that invites players into the countryside and showcases the depth of modern agriculture. Designed as a new-generation farming simulator, it focuses on realism, satisfying farm work, and the many interconnected systems that make running a farm both challenging and rewarding.

At the heart of the game is authentic fieldwork with original machines. Players can work their fields manually or coordinate tasks with workers, creating an experience that blends hands-on control with farm management. The game covers the full production cycle, from sowing and fertilizing to harvesting, while also emphasizing livestock care and the broader rhythm of agricultural life. Its machinery is presented in detail, with realistic vehicle physics and a wide range of functions, supporting an immersive and believable farming experience.

A major strength of Professional Farmer: Cattle and Crops is its attention to farming mechanics. Crop growth depends on proper fertilization and nutrient management, including mineral and organic fertilizers such as nitrogen, phosphate, and potassium. Grassland work is also deeply modeled, with mowing, turning, windrowing, and baling, along with realistic drying behavior for field grass. Bale handling is physical as well, allowing players to collect and stack square bales using a front loader.

The game also brings weather and land management into the simulation in a meaningful way. Weather is based on real weather data and affects both soil and crop growth. Fields are dynamic and show lanes and tracks created by machinery, which remain even after saving, reinforcing the sense of working on a living landscape. Support for steering wheels and controllers further enhances the authenticity and comfort of the experience.

For players who enjoy organization and teamwork, the game offers an intelligent helper system. Staff can autonomously operate machines across the field and road, and tasks can be assigned through a comprehensive calendar-based planning system. Employee management supports cooperative work and helps keep the farm running efficiently. The interface includes convenient vehicle control through a radial menu and quickbar, making the many machine functions accessible.

Professional Farmer: Cattle and Crops also provides a well-rounded structure for players of different preferences. It includes career mode, tutorials, scenarios, and a sandbox mode, along with extensive tutorials that ease newcomers into the game and scenarios that offer background knowledge about agriculture. Progress is supported through experience levels, XP, reputation, and achievements, while difficulty settings range from arcade gamer to simulation professional.

With mod support for machines and maps, the game encourages players to expand their experience with new tractors, attachments, trailers, and player-created content. Developed in close cooperation with the national agricultural publisher Landwirtschaftsverlag Münster, Professional Farmer: Cattle and Crops stands out as a serious, feature-rich, and highly detailed farming simulation built for players who appreciate realism, depth, and the rewarding work of the farm.
