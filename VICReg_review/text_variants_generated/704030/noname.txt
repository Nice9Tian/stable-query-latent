This farming simulation presents a detailed countryside experience built around modern agricultural work and a strong focus on realism. It places players in charge of running a farm where the daily routine includes cultivating fields, managing livestock, and maintaining a full production cycle from sowing to harvest.

At the center of the experience are highly detailed machines with realistic vehicle physics and a wide range of functions. Vehicle control is designed to be convenient and flexible, with commands accessible through a radial menu and a quick-access bar. Field work follows authentic farming principles, including sowing, fertilizing, and harvesting, while plant growth depends on proper nutrient management using mineral and organic fertilizers such as nitrogen, phosphate, and potassium.

The game also expands farming beyond crop production. Grassland work is represented through mowing, turning, windrowing, and baling, with realistic drying behavior for field grass. Bale handling is physical as well, allowing square bales to be collected and stacked with a front loader. Livestock care is part of the broader farm cycle, adding another layer to the management side of the experience.

Environmental systems are designed to support realism. Weather is simulated using real weather data and affects both soil and crop growth. Fields remain dynamic over time, with tracks and lanes formed by machinery that persist even after saving and reloading. The game also supports steering wheels and controllers for a more immersive setup.

To help manage the workload, the game includes an intelligent helper system. Workers can operate machines independently across fields and roads, and tasks can be organized through a calendar-based planning system. Employee management is also available for cooperative tasks, giving players more control over farm operations.

The structure includes extensive tutorials for newcomers and scenario-based content that provides background knowledge about agriculture. Players can progress through career mode, tutorials, scenarios, and a sandbox mode, while experience points, reputation, and achievements support long-term progression. Multiple difficulty levels range from a more arcade-like style to a demanding simulation-focused experience.

Additional customization is available through mod support for vehicles, implements, trailers, and maps, allowing players to add their own content or use creations from others. Overall, the game aims to deliver a broad and technically detailed farming simulation centered on authenticity, management, and the many challenges of modern agriculture.
