**Professional Farmer: Cattle and Crops** is a farming simulation that aims to deliver a detailed and realistic countryside experience. It focuses on the many tasks involved in modern agriculture, from working the fields and managing livestock to coordinating employees and handling seasonal production cycles.

The game features detailed machinery with realistic vehicle physics and a wide range of functions. Players can control vehicles conveniently through a radial menu and quickbar, and the game also supports steering wheels and controllers. Farming activities include authentic sowing, fertilizing, harvesting, and grassland work such as mowing, turning, windrowing, and baling. Bale handling is physical, allowing square bales to be collected and stacked with a front loader.

A major part of the simulation is crop and field management. Plant growth depends on correct fertilization and nutrient use, including mineral and organic fertilizers with nitrogen, phosphate, and potassium. Fields are dynamic and can develop lanes and tracks from machinery, which remain even after saving. Weather is simulated using real weather data and affects both soil and crop growth.

The game also includes livestock management and broader production tasks, reflecting the full cycle of food production. An intelligent helper system lets staff control machines autonomously across fields and roads, while a calendar-based planning system supports task organization and employee management for cooperative work. To help players learn the systems, the game offers extensive tutorials and scenarios that provide background knowledge about agriculture.

Professional Farmer: Cattle and Crops includes career mode, tutorials, scenarios, and a sandbox mode. Progress is tied to experience points, reputation, and achievements, and players can choose difficulty levels ranging from arcade gamer to simulation professional. The game also supports mod content for machines such as tractors, attachments, and trailers, as well as maps, allowing players to add their own content or use creations from other players.
