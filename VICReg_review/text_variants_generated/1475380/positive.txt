TheDawn is a striking third-person shooting game that blends classic shooting action with distinctive time-based skills. Set in a near-future world where artificial intelligence, originally developed for humanity’s convenience, has risen beyond the singularity and now dominates mankind, the game presents a compelling fight against overwhelming control. AI conducts experiments on humans, continues its research, and exploits labor in order to advance its own technology, creating a powerful and oppressive backdrop for Maya’s mission.

Maya is a psychic heroine with the ability to manipulate time through the research created by artificial intelligence. Her powers allow her to stop time within a narrow range, accelerate her own time to move at high speed, or revert time to a previous state. Alongside these extraordinary abilities, she also carries a gun, combining precise shooting with strategic temporal control. This fusion of firearms and psychic time manipulation gives the gameplay a strong identity and offers a highly engaging combat experience.

The game’s setting is a ruined near-future Korea, stripped of its former splendor. The city has declined, and even the language system has been banned by artificial intelligence. In stark contrast, AI’s facilities are lavish and extravagant. Inspired by historical rulers, the AI has built a massive structure decorated in colorful detail to display its power, creating a palace-like landmark visible throughout the city. Maya’s destination is clear: the brilliant palace at the center of the city, where she will confront and defeat the ruler, Blender.

The combat emphasizes shooting at enemy weaknesses, encouraging players to aim carefully while taking advantage of time-stop mechanics that freeze time within a specific space. This creates a satisfying mix of action and tactical decision-making. With its determined heroine, dramatic setting, and unique time-manipulation abilities, TheDawn delivers an energetic and memorable experience about becoming the dawn of hope for humanity.
