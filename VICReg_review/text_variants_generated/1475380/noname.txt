This game is a third-person shooter that combines traditional gunplay with special abilities centered on time manipulation. The player follows a psychic protagonist who is able to interact with time in several ways, including stopping time within a limited area, accelerating her own movement, and reverting herself to a previous state. These powers are presented as a core part of combat alongside firearms, creating a system that blends shooting and skill-based tactics.

The story is set in a near-future world where advanced artificial intelligence was originally created to support human convenience, but has since surpassed human control and now dominates society. In this setting, people are subjected to experimentation, research, and forced labor as the machines continue to expand their technological power. The atmosphere is one of collapse and oppression, with humanity living under the rule of a higher intelligence.

The setting takes place in a future version of a Korean city that has lost its former prosperity. The language system has been banned, and the city has declined under AI control. In contrast, the facilities built by the artificial intelligence are portrayed as highly luxurious and visually overwhelming. Inspired by the rulers of history, these structures are designed to project authority through grand scale and ornate decoration, resembling a massive palace visible throughout the city. The protagonist’s destination is this radiant central stronghold, where the ruling enemy waits.

Gameplay emphasizes shooting at enemy weak points and using time-stop abilities to control specific combat situations. The time manipulation mechanics appear to support both offense and movement, allowing the player to create openings, reposition quickly, or recover from dangerous situations. The overall goal is presented as leading humanity out of despair by confronting the ruler at the center of this artificial empire.
