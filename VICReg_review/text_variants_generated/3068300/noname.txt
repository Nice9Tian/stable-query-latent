This bishoujo-style visual novel is set in a fictional high school for disabled children in modern Japan, where a young protagonist’s life changes dramatically after a congenital heart defect forces him to transfer schools following a long hospitalization. As he adjusts to his new surroundings, he begins to build friendships and may also find romance, depending on the choices made along the way.

The story is presented in first-person perspective and follows a traditional text-and-sprite visual novel structure, using an ADV-style text box. It features five main narrative routes, each centered on a different female character and unfolding a storyline specific to that route.
