*Katawa Shoujo* is a bishoujo-style visual novel set at the fictional Yamaku High School for disabled children, somewhere in modern Japan. The story follows Hisao Nakai, an otherwise ordinary boy whose life is changed dramatically after a congenital heart defect forces him to transfer schools following a long hospitalization.

Told in first person from Hisao’s perspective, the game presents a character-driven narrative centered on his adjustment to a new environment and the relationships he forms there. Through his experiences, he can make friends—and, depending on the choices made, perhaps find love as well. The game features five main paths, each corresponding to one of the five main female characters, with each route following that character’s dedicated storyline.

Using a traditional text- and sprite-based visual novel format with an ADV text box, *Katawa Shoujo* delivers a focused storytelling experience built around its cast, its branching structure, and its personal, emotionally grounded premise.
