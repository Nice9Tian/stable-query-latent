This kingdom management RPG places you in charge of a medieval realm where petitioners arrive in the throne room each turn seeking advice, aid, and judgment. Each request forces you to balance compassion against survival, since resources are limited and not every visitor has the kingdom’s best interests at heart. You must decide whom to support, whom to refuse, and when to conserve supplies for more critical needs.

The experience centers on managing a steady stream of characters with distinct personalities and concerns. Some bring practical troubles, while others add humor or present difficult moral choices. You are also responsible for your own family, supporting them through their personal struggles and deciding how their situations should be handled. At the same time, you can recruit specialized helpers such as Gritwardens, Hexweavers, and Trappers to strengthen your efforts.

Political alliances and military preparation are important parts of play. To secure support for an approaching battle, you must satisfy the demands of powerful nobles and rulers, though some may require questionable actions in return for their loyalty. The setting is a fictional medieval world shaped by northern folk myth, where monsters and arcane practices are part of daily life. Villagers seek help with threats such as monster attacks, as well as more ordinary problems that affect their comfort and well-being.

Overall, the game combines resource management, decision-making, family concerns, and alliance-building in a narrative-driven structure. Its central challenge is maintaining stability while navigating conflicting demands, unpredictable visitors, and the constant pressure to keep everyone satisfied.
