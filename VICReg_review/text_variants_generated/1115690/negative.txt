*Yes, Your Grace* is a kingdom management RPG that sounds more engaging than it actually is. Each turn, petitioners shuffle into the throne room with their troubles, forcing you to make yet another round of constrained, unpleasant decisions: help them, or save scarce resources for something supposedly more important. The game constantly reminds you that supplies are limited and that not everyone has the kingdom’s best interests at heart, which means most choices feel less like meaningful leadership and more like damage control.

The premise centers on Davern, a medieval kingdom ruled by King Eryk, set in a fictional world where monsters and arcane practices draw from Slavic folklore. Villagers come seeking aid for all kinds of problems, from monster attacks to wanting more places to relax and have fun. Some requests are meant to be humorous, while others are designed to be difficult moral trade-offs, but the repeated structure makes the process feel burdensome rather than compelling. You are also expected to deal with your family’s personal problems and decide their fate, adding more pressure to an already strained setup.

The game offers a few supporting systems: you can hire Generals, Witches, and Hunters to assist your efforts, and you must prepare for battle while trying to win the favor of lords and kings. These alliances are not straightforward either, since some nobles demand dirty deeds before they will support you. In the end, *Yes, Your Grace* appears built around juggling obligations, satisfying demands, and trying not to disappoint everyone at once. That constant balancing act may be the point, but it also makes the experience sound exhausting and emotionally drained rather than enjoyable.
