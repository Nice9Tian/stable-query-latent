Yes, Your Grace is a kingdom management RPG that places you on the throne as King Eryk of Davern, a medieval realm inspired by Slavic folklore. Each turn, petitioners arrive in your throne room with requests for advice and assistance, and every decision matters: you must choose whether to help them, preserve limited supplies for more urgent needs, and judge who truly has the kingdom’s best interests at heart.

The game blends governance, resource management, and narrative choice in a compelling way. You will listen to villagers facing problems ranging from monster attacks to a simple lack of places to relax, while also handling humorous and difficult situations that test your judgment. At the same time, your family requires attention and support, adding a personal layer to your responsibilities as ruler.

To strengthen your position, you can hire Generals, Witches, and Hunters, prepare for battle, and build alliances with lords and kings by satisfying their various whims. These political relationships can come with uncomfortable demands, forcing you to weigh loyalty, morality, and survival. With its quirky characters, branching stories, and constant pressure to balance competing needs, Yes, Your Grace offers a rich and engaging royal management experience where every choice carries real weight.
