Yes, Your Grace is a kingdom management RPG set in the medieval kingdom of Davern, ruled by King Eryk. The game takes place in a fictional world shaped by monsters and arcane practices inspired by Slavic folklore.

In the game, petitioners arrive in the throne room each turn to ask for advice and assistance. The player must decide whether to help them or conserve limited supplies for more important needs. These choices involve balancing the needs of villagers, the royal family, and the kingdom as a whole, while also judging who can be trusted and who may not have the kingdom’s best interests at heart.

The game includes a variety of characters with different roles and personalities. Players can hear the stories of quirky and determined visitors, support family members with their personal problems, and determine their fate. The kingdom’s people may request help with issues ranging from monster attacks to the lack of places to relax and enjoy themselves, and their requests can range from humorous to difficult.

To strengthen the kingdom, players can hire Generals, Witches, and Hunters. They must also prepare for battle and make alliances by satisfying the demands of lords and kings. However, some alliances may require performing morally questionable deeds, making it difficult to keep everyone satisfied. Overall, Yes, Your Grace centers on managing scarce resources, navigating difficult decisions, and balancing the competing demands of family, subjects, and powerful allies.
