Pax Dei is a social sandbox MMO set in a medieval-inspired world where myths are real, ghosts exist, and magic is unquestioned. The game is built around player freedom, with thousands of players sharing a vast open world and choosing how they want to participate in it. Players can build homes, forge reputations, gather resources, craft items, and create their own stories in a living world shaped largely by player activity.

The game is currently in Early Access and remains heavily in development. The developers state that it is not yet feature-complete and that balancing, polish, and expansion are still ongoing. Major updates during Early Access may require a wipe and a fresh start. Early Access is scheduled to last until at least June 2025, and possibly longer.

Pax Dei’s world is divided into large regions, including five Provinces spanning nearly 300 km², with more than twenty valleys available for settling. Players can travel through the relative safety of the Heartlands to find land for a home, or venture into the Wilderness, where they may face hostile creatures, uncover mysteries, and compete with rival Clans over power and resources. The game emphasizes that the farther players go from home, the more dangerous and unfamiliar the world becomes.

Building and crafting are central systems. Players can place plots of land, construct homes, villages, or even large fortresses, and use a structural integrity system to shape their builds. Everything in the game is produced by players, including clothes, walls, food, weapons, armor, tools, and other materials. The world includes dozens of resources and hundreds of recipes, and players can harvest, craft, and specialize in different roles through skills and item combinations.

Clan play is another major feature. Players can join together to pool resources, combine plots, build settlements, and work cooperatively in dungeons or against other Clans. The game also presents different ways to engage with its fully player-driven environment, stating that all play styles can connect and complement one another.

Combat and danger are present throughout the world, especially in ruins, hidden dungeons, and wilderness areas guarded by strong enemies. A PvP-focused province called Lyonesse is also available, where divine peace does not apply and death has consequences. The developers note that this is the first iteration of PvP and that more content and systems are planned over time.

Pax Dei is offered through Founder’s Packs during Early Access. Purchasing a Founder’s Pack grants game access for the duration of Early Access, along with a specified number of land plots, character slots, founder status in Discord, and exclusive recipes. The available packs differ in the amount of plots and character slots included, with some packs offering items such as the Founders Outfit, Hunters Lodge, and Wyvern Building Set. The purchase also covers the initial game purchase required at full release and permanently retains the included character slots and exclusive recipes. The future business model has not been finalized, but the developers say it will be similar to classic MMORPG subscription models, involving an initial purchase after Early Access and a recurring fee for ongoing access and plots.
