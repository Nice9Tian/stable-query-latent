This is a vast social sandbox MMO set in a medieval-inspired world where myths are real, ghosts exist, and magic is accepted as part of everyday life. It places players in an open-world environment built around exploration, settlement, crafting, cooperation, and conflict, with thousands of others sharing the same living world.

A central feature of the experience is building a home in safer regions known as the Heartlands. Players can find a plot of land, establish a village, and grow it with the support of a Clan. The world is described as largely player-made: clothes, walls, food, tools, weapons, armor, flowers, and even mysterious reagents are all produced through player effort and skill. This creates a strong social and economic interdependence, where different play styles contribute to the same shared world.

Beyond the protected areas lies a more dangerous Wilderness filled with hostile creatures, ancient mysteries, rival Clans, and contested resources. Adventure in these regions can bring glory, wealth, and knowledge back to a player’s Clan, but the farther one travels from home, the more unpredictable and dangerous the world becomes. The game also includes hidden ruins and dungeons guarded by powerful foes, encouraging group play and preparation.

The current Early Access version already offers a large world spanning five Provinces and nearly 300 square kilometers, with more than twenty valleys to settle in. Players can harvest resources across multiple biomes, unlock hundreds of recipes, and use dozens of building pieces to construct homes and villages. Clans can combine plots and work together to build larger settlements or even fortified structures, while dungeons and PvP zones add conflict and risk. In one province, divine protection does not apply, and death has meaningful consequences.

Progression is built around crafting, item variety, and skill selection, allowing players to define their own role through combinations of equipment and mastered abilities. The game is still heavily in development and not feature-complete, so balancing, polish, and expansion are ongoing. Major updates may also require world wipes, and the developers plan to continue adding content and systems throughout Early Access.

The business model after Early Access is not fully finalized, but it is intended to resemble a classic subscription-style MMORPG structure. The current founder purchase options provide unlimited access during Early Access, include varying amounts of land plots and character slots, and grant exclusive recipes that are retained permanently. These purchases also unlock a special community role and support the project’s continued development.
