*I Wani Hug that Gator!* is a multi-ended, choices-matter visual novel that leans on a familiar setup: a miserable senior gets uprooted by his parents and forced to start over halfway across the country. Inco, the protagonist, is already socially stranded before the move, and the game uses that misery as its main premise for a story about second chances and “accepting who you are.” That sounds meaningful enough, but the page makes it clear that the real focus is not on anything especially deep so much as on watching Inco stumble through yet another attempt at having a normal social life.

The game centers on shaping how Inco spends his final year and how his relationships develop along the way. The store text highlights one major relationship in particular: Olivia, a bold and brash character who seems to be the main source of “fuzzy inside” feelings and awkward romantic tension. The page frames this as the game’s big hook, presenting their dynamic as some kind of “prehistoric romance” despite the fact that it is just another visual novel pairing waiting to be resolved through player choices.

Mechanically, the game is straightforward: make friends, reconcile enemies, and decide how badly Inco’s social life can be repaired. The choices you make expand relationships with Inco’s classmates and ultimately determine which of the four unique endings you get. There is no attempt here to disguise the structure; it is plainly a branching narrative where your decisions steer the outcome. If anything, the tone of the page suggests that Inco is constantly being judged for his inability to function socially, as if the game is less about drama and more about repeatedly reminding you how socially pathetic he is.

Overall, *I Wani Hug that Gator!* is presented as a relationship-driven visual novel built around branching choices, multiple endings, and a central romance route with Olivia. It promises a story of personal growth, but the page itself comes off more like a sarcastic rundown of a lonely teenager’s problems than an especially uplifting sales pitch.
