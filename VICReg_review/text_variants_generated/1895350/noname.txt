This is a multi-ending, choice-driven visual novel centered on second chances, self-acceptance, and the social challenges of a final school year. The story follows a protagonist whose family suddenly relocates him across the country just as he is preparing to enter his senior year, forcing him to start over in an unfamiliar environment.

The game focuses on shaping how the protagonist spends this last year and how his relationships develop over time. Although he previously had very few, if any, friends, the new setting gives him an opportunity to rebuild his social life. The narrative emphasizes making friends, repairing damaged relationships, and navigating the consequences of social decisions.

A major part of the experience is a central relationship with a bold, assertive classmate who quickly becomes important to the protagonist. Their dynamic is presented as a key emotional thread in the story, with the ultimate outcome left uncertain. The game frames this connection as especially significant compared to the rest of the cast.

Choices play a major role in how the story unfolds. Player decisions expand relationships with classmates and determine which of four unique endings is reached. The structure encourages replayability, as different choices lead to different outcomes and relationship paths.
