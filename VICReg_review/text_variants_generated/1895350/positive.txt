**I Wani Hug that Gator!** is a multi-ended, choices-matter visual novel that tells a heartfelt story about second chances and learning to accept who you are. The game follows Inco, whose plans for senior year are suddenly disrupted when his parents move the family halfway across the country. Starting over is never easy, especially for someone who had few friends, or none at all, at his old school. Yet this unexpected relocation opens the door to new relationships and a chance to reshape his final year.

At the center of the story is Olivia, a bold and brash classmate who quickly becomes an unforgettable part of Inco’s new life. Their dynamic drives much of the game’s appeal, with the narrative teasing a relationship full of tension, chemistry, and emotional possibilities. The story presents this as a distinctive romance with a playful, memorable tone, while leaving the final outcome in the player’s hands.

Gameplay focuses on making choices that influence Inco’s social life and the way his relationships develop. Players can make friends, reconcile enemies, and help Inco navigate the challenges of connecting with others. Every decision matters, expanding his relationships with classmates and shaping which of the four unique endings the player will receive. The result is a visual novel that rewards thoughtful choices and character-driven engagement, all while building toward a story about growth, acceptance, and the possibility of a new beginning.
