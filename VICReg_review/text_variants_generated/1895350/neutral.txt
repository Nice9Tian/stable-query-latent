I Wani Hug that Gator! is a multi-ended, choices-matter visual novel centered on second chances and self-acceptance. The story follows Inco, who is forced to start over when his parents suddenly move the family halfway across the country just as he is preparing to enter his senior year. Although he had few friends at his previous school and little social standing, the move becomes the beginning of a new final year that can unfold in different ways depending on the player’s decisions.

The game focuses on shaping how Inco spends his last year of school and how his relationships develop over time. A major part of the story involves Olivia, described as bold and brash, who quickly becomes an important presence in Inco’s life. The game presents their connection as a central relationship, while leaving the outcome of that pairing open.

Gameplay revolves around making choices, building relationships with classmates, and reconciling enemies as Inco tries to repair his poor social life. The choices made throughout the story expand relationships and determine which of four unique endings the player receives.
