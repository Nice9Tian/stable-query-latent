This single-player open-universe role-playing and third-person shooter experience presents a vast galactic setting built around exploration, progression, and player choice. It combines role-playing, mining, trading, piracy, crafting, and deep discovery into a large-scale adventure where the player’s overarching aim is to build a new empire across the galaxy. How that empire is formed is left entirely open, allowing different approaches to shape the journey.

The game world is enormous, offering tens of thousands of distinct solar systems to explore. Each system contains a unique structure with different planets, and every location is procedurally generated so that systems, worlds, and points of interest remain varied and individual. Planets feature large surfaces with their own structures, climates, and gravity, and they can include cities, villages, caves, outposts, and hidden secrets. This creates extensive opportunities for exploration across a galaxy filled with different destinations and environmental conditions.

The player operates through two central elements: a pilot and a ship. A character customization system supports the creation of lifelike characters, while ship modification allows vehicles to be adjusted to suit personal preferences. The presentation also emphasizes realistic rendering, reinforcing the sense of a detailed and immersive space-faring setting.

A major part of progression comes from the ability to establish and manage a personal faction. The player can define the faction’s internal operations, policies, governing principles, and organizational structure. Expansion is achieved through military growth, the capture of stations and solar systems, or the construction of new stations, with the long-term goal of rising to supreme power in the galaxy.

Diplomacy is another important layer. The game includes a rich diplomatic system involving loyal regional leaders and connected houses, each with its own characteristics, stations, planets, cities, and fleets. As relationships change, the political balance of the galaxy can shift significantly, making decisions more consequential and requiring careful consideration.

The journey is not solitary. Companions can assist in combat and lead a team, and different roles can be assigned to crew members on board according to their skills. In addition, there are seven guilds available for players to join. Each guild offers its own side quests, letting players advance in rank and gain access to unique benefits tied to that organization’s area of expertise.

Beyond the main storyline, the galaxy is filled with side missions that provide extra rewards, new information about the world, and additional character interaction. The setting is designed as a living galaxy, where communication with other ships and random encounter events can lead to unexpected stories and challenges. Overall, the game focuses on freedom, scale, and a wide variety of systems that support exploration, politics, combat, and empire-building.
