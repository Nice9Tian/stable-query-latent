This arena combat game places the player inside a robotic body equipped for close-quarters survival, with the central premise built around fast battles, dismemberment, and constant progression through upgrades. The tone is intentionally irreverent, while the actual gameplay focuses on sharp movement, timing, and positioning in deadly encounters where a single hit can be fatal.

The game includes several single-player modes. One story-driven mode presents an epic resistance narrative alongside sword combat. An endless mode challenges players to push through a large set of level variants distributed across multiple difficulty tiers, with the goal of reaching the highest rank. There is also a stream-integrated mode in which viewers influence the match by earning coins, placing bets, and spawning enemies. For players seeking restrictions, a challenge mode offers weapon-limited runs, such as bow-only or hammer-only scenarios.

Multiplayer content expands the experience with several formats. One mode is a battle-royale-style survival competition built for short sessions and supporting up to fifteen players. Another allows private one-on-one duels with friends. Cooperative online play is also available, letting players team up to survive the arena together.

Combat is defined by voxel-based dismemberment, which is not only a visual feature but a core mechanic. Limbs can be severed during battle, and some enemies must be reduced in size before they can be finished off. The combat system emphasizes precision and mobility, with danger coming from any direction. A range of hostile machine types creates escalating difficulty, including sword users, ranged attackers, arachnid-like units, and massive hammer-wielding foes.

Progression comes through a set of robot upgrades such as mobility tools, ranged attacks, kicking, deflection, cloning, armor, and a fire-breathing ability. The game also includes a commentary system from in-universe announcers who react to the player’s actions during combat.

Additional features support creativity and personalization. A level editor lets players build and share custom challenges, while a workshop-style library offers community-made content. Achievements unlock upgrades, emotes, and multiplayer skins. Emotes add a playful layer to matches, allowing characters to dance, taunt opponents, or perform exaggerated gestures.
