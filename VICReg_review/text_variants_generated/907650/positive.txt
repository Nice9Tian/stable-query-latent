Stronghold: Warlords is the latest chapter in Firefly Studios’ real-time strategy series, and it brings the series’ castle economy formula to East Asia for the first time. Set across the battlefields of Mongol hordes, imperial warriors, and samurai clansmen, the game focuses on besieging Japanese castles and fortified Chinese cities with a strong emphasis on large-scale strategy and historical warfare.

At the heart of the experience is the new Warlords system, which lets players recruit, upgrade, and command AI lords directly on the battlefield. These warlords provide unique perks, characteristics, and upgradeable abilities, allowing players to assign them to tasks such as supporting industry, strengthening borders, forging weapons, or coordinating pincer attacks. This adds a fresh strategic layer to every campaign mission, skirmish, and multiplayer battle.

The game offers a substantial amount of content, including 31 campaign missions, 45 skirmish trail scenarios, 28 multiplayer maps, skirmish mode, and a Free Build sandbox with AI invasions. Players can relive historical campaigns spanning from 3rd century BC China to the rise of the Tokugawa shogunate and the coming of the Mongol Empire, while mastering castle-building and siege challenges across the skirmish trails.

Stronghold: Warlords also expands combat with new units such as imperial fire lancers, disciplined samurai, Mongolian horse archers, and deadly ninja. To support its siege-focused gameplay, it introduces gunpowder-fuelled weapons and machines like the hwacha arrow launcher, siege tower, and fire oxen. Alongside warfare, players can shape their domain through taxation, medieval entertainment, and torture, choosing whether to rule through fear or admiration.

Multiplayer is another major part of the game, with battles supporting 4 human and 4+ AI players across 28 maps, giving players plenty of room to create their ideal online battleground. With its mix of historical settings, expanded siege warfare, and the innovative Warlords system, Stronghold: Warlords offers a rich and distinctive strategy experience.
