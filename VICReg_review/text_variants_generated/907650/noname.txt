This real-time strategy title presents a castle-economy experience set in East Asia, where players lead armies such as nomadic riders, imperial fighters, and elite sword clans in assaults on fortified strongholds and walled cities. The game combines historical conflict, siege warfare, and kingdom management across a broad range of modes and scenarios.

A defining feature is the introduction of battlefield commanders known as **Zenthords**. These AI leaders can be recruited, upgraded, and directed during play, each offering distinct perks, traits, and abilities. They can be assigned to support industry, defend borders, produce weapons, or assist in coordinated attacks. Their presence adds a strategic layer that changes from mission to mission, skirmish to skirmish, and match to match.

The game includes a large single-player campaign structure, with missions inspired by eras ranging from ancient China to the rise of major shogunate-style rule and the expansion of a great steppe empire. In addition, there are numerous skirmish trail scenarios designed to test castle-building and siege skills, as well as a sandbox-style free build mode that can include AI invasions. Multiplayer is also supported through a wide set of maps, allowing human and AI participants to compete across custom battlefields.

Combat emphasizes siege tools and gunpowder-era warfare, with new equipment such as arrow-launching platforms, siege towers, and fire-based assault units. The unit roster includes imperial fire specialists, disciplined sword soldiers, mounted archers, and stealth-oriented assassins. Players can expand their power by unlocking stronger castles, larger armies, and more advanced abilities while managing the pressures of warfare and governance.

Beyond military conquest, the game also incorporates social and administrative systems, including taxation, entertainment, and punishment, allowing players to shape their ruler’s reputation through fear or respect. Overall, it offers a mixture of historical campaign play, tactical battlefield command, and fortress management built around the unique mechanics of commanding allied lords on the field.
