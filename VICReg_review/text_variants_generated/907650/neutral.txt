**Stronghold: Warlords** is a real-time strategy game from Firefly Studios and a new entry in the Stronghold series. It is presented as the first game in the franchise to recreate the castle economies of East Asia. In the game, players command Mongol hordes, imperial warriors, and samurai clansmen while laying siege to Japanese castles and fortified Chinese cities.

The game includes 31 campaign missions, 45 skirmish trail scenarios, 28 multiplayer maps, skirmish mode, and a Free Build sandbox mode with AI invasions. It spans historical settings from 3rd century BC China to the rise of the Tokugawa shogunate and the coming of the Mongol Empire.

A key feature of Stronghold: Warlords is the introduction of AI lords called “Warlords.” These can be recruited, upgraded, and commanded across the battlefield. Each warlord provides unique perks, characteristics, and upgradeable abilities, and can be assigned to tasks such as supporting industry, fortifying borders, forging weapons, or joining a pincer attack. Different campaign missions, skirmish games, and multiplayer battles feature different warlords, changing how each session plays.

The game also adds new units and siege tools. Recruitable units include imperial fire lancers, samurai, Mongolian horse archers, and ninja. Siege warfare is expanded with gunpowder-based weapons such as the hwacha arrow launcher, siege tower, and fire oxen.

Players can also build and manage their own lordship using systems of medieval entertainment, taxation, and torture. In multiplayer, battles support up to 4 human players and 4 or more AI players across 28 maps, allowing for different combinations of opponents and allies.
