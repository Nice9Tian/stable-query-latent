Need for Speed™ delivers an exciting street racing experience set in the sprawling urban playground of Ventura Bay. Behind the wheel of iconic cars, players can tear through dense city streets, coastal roads, harbor routes, and canyon stretches while building a reputation and shaping their dream car. The game blends fast-paced racing with an immersive story, inviting players to become the ultimate racing icon across overlapping storylines and multiple paths to victory.

A major strength of Need for Speed is its rich and authentic customization. Players can build a car that reflects their personality through extensive visual and performance options, ensuring each ride looks as impressive as it performs. The game also offers five distinct ways to play and win: Speed, Style, Build, Crew, and Outlaw, giving players freedom to approach the experience in their own way.

On PC, the game is optimized to shine with an unlocked frame rate and 4K resolution support, along with steering wheel and peripheral compatibility from leading manufacturers such as Logitech, Thrustmaster, and Fanatec. The Deluxe Edition adds extra value with a lifetime 10% discount on in-game currency purchases, a tricked-out starter car, styling and performance packs, exclusive wraps, a unique identifying sticker, and a VIP icon.

Need for Speed also keeps players engaged with live content, including Eddie’s challenge, Snapshot Pro, and opportunities to earn Ken Block’s Hoonicorn and Morohoshi-San’s Diablo. With its stylish cars, deep customization, and the energetic streets of Ventura Bay, it offers a polished and compelling racing adventure that rewards skill, personality, and persistence.
