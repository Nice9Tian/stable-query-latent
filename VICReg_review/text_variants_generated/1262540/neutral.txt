Need for Speed is a street racing game set in Ventura Bay, a large urban playground that includes dense city streets, winding roads to the harbor, and canyon routes. Players get behind the wheel of iconic cars, build their reputation, and work toward creating a dream car while progressing through overlapping storylines.

The game emphasizes replayability through five distinct ways to play: Speed, Style, Build, Crew, and Outlaw. These routes let players earn reputation and win in different ways. It also features extensive visual and performance customization, allowing cars to be tailored to reflect the player’s style.

Need for Speed is optimized for PC, offering an unlocked frame rate and 4K resolution, along with support for steering wheels and peripherals from manufacturers such as Logitech, Thrustmaster, and Fanatec. The game also includes a “Living Game” component with free updates in Eddie’s challenge and Snapshot Pro, plus opportunities to earn Ken Block’s Hoonicorn and Morohoshi-San’s Diablo.

A Deluxe Edition is available and includes a lifetime 10% discount on all items bought with in-game currency, a tricked-out starter car, a styling and performance pack, exclusive wraps, a unique identifying sticker, and a VIP icon.
