**Red Solstice 2: Survivors** is a real-time tactical strategy game set in a sci-fi world where humanity faces extinction from the STROL mutant invasion. As the Executor, you are awakened in the year 117 After Earth to command the Cell, a secret task force created to stop the spread of the virus and find a cure before mankind is lost.

The game combines a deep strategy layer with real-time combat missions. Players choose missions, select loadouts, and lead a squad into battle, making quick decisions in a hostile environment. It can be played in single-player or in co-op with up to seven other players, blending tense action with strategic planning.

A major part of the experience is its survival-focused campaign. The planet can be explored through an ongoing campaign centered on humanity’s survival, while research and technology upgrades unlock new battlefield options. The game also features classic real-time strategy controls, designed to give players more freedom and the ability to plan, execute, and improvise.

Combat takes place on an open battlefield in real time, where players must adapt on the move. Missions involve deploying alone or with others to contain a spreading biomass infection, managing resources, exploring the map, and securing areas. The game includes more than 15 main missions and over 20 side missions, with mission types such as exploration, stealth, and survival.

Players can choose from six classes: Assault, Demolition, Heavy Support, Marksman, Medic, and Recon. Each class offers a different gameplay style. The game also supports 8-player co-op for joining other players’ campaigns and missions, or letting others join yours.

Weapons and battlefield effects are described as powerful, with the ability to cause mass destruction and wipe out battlefields and colonies. The biomass infestation is another central threat, changing the environment and adapting to player actions.
