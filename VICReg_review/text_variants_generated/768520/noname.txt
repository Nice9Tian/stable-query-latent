Set in a distant future after a planetary collapse, this tactical sci-fi game places the player in command of a secret taskforce awakened to confront a spreading mutant threat and search for a cure to a deadly virus. The experience blends long-term strategy with real-time combat, requiring players to choose missions, equip their squad, and adapt quickly to changing battlefield conditions.

The game can be played solo or in cooperative mode with up to seven other players. Its structure combines an overarching campaign with individual combat deployments, creating a loop of planning, execution, and improvisation. Players manage resources, explore an open map, secure areas, and push through hostile environments while the threat continues to expand across the planet.

A key part of the design is its use of classic real-time strategy controls, which emphasize freedom of movement and proactive decision-making. Between missions, players can research and upgrade technologies, then bring those improvements into combat. Battles take place in real time on open maps, where positioning and on-the-fly adjustments matter as much as preparation.

The game offers more than fifteen main missions and over twenty side missions, with mission types that include exploration, stealth, and survival. Players can choose from six distinct classes, each providing a different style of play: Assault, Demolition, Heavy Support, Marksman, Medic, and Recon. Cooperative support is also built into the structure, allowing players to join ongoing campaigns and combat missions or host their own for others to enter.

Combat is centered around powerful weaponry, environmental destruction, and fighting a spreading biomass-like infection that can alter the battlefield and react to player actions. Overall, the game presents a tense blend of squad-based tactics, survival pressure, and mission-driven progression in a hostile science-fiction setting.
