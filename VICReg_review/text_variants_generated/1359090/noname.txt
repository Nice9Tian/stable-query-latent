This tactical first-person shooter emphasizes slow-paced, methodical combat built around online team play and realistic resource management. Set across multiple locations in Bangladesh with real-life scale, it offers a grounded setting where careful planning and coordination matter as much as aim. Players can join cooperative missions with up to 10 participants, or play solo alongside up to 9 AI squadmates.

The game includes several mission types, such as hostage rescue, bomb defusal, and deathmatch, alongside dynamic 5v5 PvP encounters. Competitive play is supported by a leaderboard and tier system. Loadout choice is deliberately restrictive: weapons and gadgets are limited, and they are lost when the player dies, making each decision significant.

A planning table allows players to review operations before beginning a mission, reinforcing the strategic focus. Across more than 18 maps, the environment adds tactical variety through features such as functional elevators, bulletproof glass, dynamic shutters, and building lights controlled by breaker switches. The game also offers more than 23 weapons and 18 utilities, with customization options that let players modify rails, sights, and foregrips. Additional realism comes from checking remaining bullets in magazines and repacking them for efficiency.

Door handling is treated as a major gameplay element. Doors can be trapped or alarmed, while attackers have multiple breach options, including kicking, ram usage, explosive entry, and combined breach tactics. Enemy AI is also described as dynamic, with different behavior patterns such as suicide attacks, long-range sniping, and situational reactions to incoming fire.

A companion feature adds another layer to the experience: players begin as handlers of a small, nameable puppy that grows into an available mission companion after completing three missions. This dog can accompany the player in operations, giving the mode a distinct identity within the overall tactical framework.
