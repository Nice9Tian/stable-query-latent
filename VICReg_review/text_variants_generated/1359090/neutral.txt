Zero Hour is a serious, slow-paced tactical shooter centered on online, team-based action. Set across various locations in Bangladesh with real-life scale and resource management, the game emphasizes deliberate decision-making, coordination, and methodical execution.

The game supports cooperative missions for up to 10 players, or solo play with up to 9 AI squad mates. It features hostage rescue and bomb defusal scenarios, along with a dynamic 5v5 PvP mode where strategy and teamwork are essential. Players can also engage in deathmatch, and competitive play is supported by a leaderboard and tier system.

Gameplay in Zero Hour is built around tactical planning and limited resources. Loadouts are restricted, and guns and gadgets are lost on death, making each choice meaningful. Before missions, players can use a planning table to review operations and prepare their approach. The game also includes story-driven NPC interactions and mission objectives that shape the route through different situations.

The maps are a major part of the experience, with more than 18 locations across Bangladesh. These environments include interactive features such as functional elevators, bulletproof glass, dynamic shutters, and breaker switches that can control building lights. Players have access to over 23 weapons and 18 utilities, along with detailed weapon customization for rails, sights, and foregrips. The game also adds realism through magazine bullet counting and repacking mechanics.

Entry and movement are shaped by dynamic door mechanics. Doors can be trapped or alarmed by terrorists, while attackers have multiple breach options, including kicking, using a door rammer, C2 explosives, and a back kick combined with grenades. These systems add another layer of tactical choice to each mission.

Zero Hour also features dynamic suspect AI with varied behaviors. Some suspects use suicide bombs, while others may adopt sniper tactics on long-range maps. They can also sense incoming fire and time their advances strategically, making encounters less predictable.

A K9 attack dog feature is also included. Players begin as a dog handler with a small, nameable puppy that starts in a cage, becomes interactive, and follows the player through the training facility. After completing three missions, the canine companion becomes available for mission deployment.
