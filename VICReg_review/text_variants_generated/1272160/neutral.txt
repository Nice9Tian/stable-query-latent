The Life and Suffering of Sir Brante is a narrative-driven RPG presented through the pages of the protagonist’s journal. Set in the Great Arknian Empire, it tells the story of a man born into a harsh social order where every life is determined by estate and dissent is ruthlessly suppressed. As a commoner with no rights or title, Sir Brante must confront tradition, prejudice, and the rigid hierarchy of a world shaped by the Twin Gods and their division of mortals into Lots.

The game follows his life from birth to true death, with each stage shaped by difficult decisions, personal hardship, and major upheavals. Choices affect not only the protagonist, but also his family, loved ones, and potentially the stability of the Empire itself. Players can aim to protect those close to them, support imperial rule, seek wealth, or try to change the world.

The structure is chapter-based and tracks the player’s actions, acquired skills, and overlapping circumstances to create a unique storyline in each playthrough. Character development plays an important role, with traits such as determination, sensitivity, and endurance influencing Sir Brante’s personality, worldview, relationships, and the talents and storylines that become available. Skills gained even from infancy contribute to how the story unfolds.

Multiple branching paths encourage replayability, and a full first playthrough can take over 15 hours. Depending on choices, Sir Brante may become a noble judge, train with the inquisition, join a secret society’s revolution, or pursue another path entirely. The game also includes a resurrection system that lets the character retain experience after death, allowing players to rethink earlier decisions and adjust their approach across cycles of death and rebirth.
