*Varren’s Ledger* is a narrative-driven RPG presented through the pages of the protagonist’s journal. It is set in a harsh empire where dissent is crushed and social position is determined at birth, placing the player in the role of a common-born figure with no rights or title. The story follows a lifelong struggle to survive, challenge inherited prejudice, and pursue a personal destiny while balancing the demands of family, duty, and ambition.

The game centers on choice-driven storytelling. Divided into chapters, it tracks the player’s actions, acquired skills, and overlapping circumstances to create a distinct plotline for each playthrough. Decisions carry lasting consequences, shaping not only the protagonist but also their relatives, allies, and the broader imperial order. Players may choose to protect loved ones, serve imperial authority, seek wealth, or attempt to transform the world itself.

Progression is tied to character development through attributes such as determination, sensitivity, and endurance. Skills gained from early life onward influence personality, worldview, relationships, and the story paths that become available. The narrative is structured around multiple branching routes, allowing for different roles and ambitions across playthroughs, such as becoming a judicial figure, entering an inquisitorial path, joining a hidden revolutionary movement, or pursuing other possibilities.

A resurrection mechanic adds another layer to the experience by allowing the character to retain experience after death, encouraging players to reassess previous choices and refine their strategy. This creates a cycle of death and rebirth in which the player can continue pushing toward their goals while adapting to the severe demands of the world.
