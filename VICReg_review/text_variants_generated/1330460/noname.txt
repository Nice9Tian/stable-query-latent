This fantasy strategy game centers on building, managing, and defending a fortified settlement against nightly assaults. The player begins by choosing a suitable location for the central keep and then constructing a defensive layout around it. Core fortifications include walls, gates, and towers, which can be used to create layered protection. Archers can be stationed on these structures to attack enemies after dark.

A major part of the experience is economy management. Resources must be gathered to construct new buildings, support workers, and maintain an army. The settlement can be expanded by capturing additional territory, unlocking access to more resources and allowing the construction of more housing. As the population grows, more workers become available, which in turn strengthens production chains and helps sustain a larger military force.

Military preparation is another important system. Troops can be trained in advance of enemy attacks, and they can also be upgraded with stronger equipment through an armory-style building. Defensive machines such as larger ranged siege weapons can be placed on towers to help repel attackers, but they require sufficient ammunition to function effectively.

The game follows a day-and-night cycle in which the daytime is used for building, resource collection, and preparation, while the nighttime brings siege events. Each night, the settlement is attacked by increasingly powerful moon beasts, making long-term survival progressively more difficult. Enemy forces may include ram units and tower-based assault structures, adding pressure to the fortress defenses. The overall challenge is to see how long the settlement can endure under repeated siege.
