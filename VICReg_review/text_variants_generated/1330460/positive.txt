**Becastled** is a charming and strategic castle-building game set in a fantasy world, where your main goal is to build, defend, and expand your stronghold against relentless sieges. The game blends city management, fortification planning, and real-time defense into a satisfying cycle of day and night gameplay.

At its core, **Becastled** challenges you to find the ideal location for your keep and construct a fortress worthy of surviving the coming attacks. You can place **walls, gates, and towers** to shape your defenses, then position **archers** on top to rain arrows down on enemies during the night. The game makes castle design feel meaningful, encouraging thoughtful layouts and strong defensive planning.

Beyond the walls, you must also **manage resources and your city’s economy**. Gathering resources is essential for building new structures, maintaining your workers, and supporting your army. The game includes **production chains**, adding depth to the economic side and keeping the daytime busy with productive decisions and expansion.

Military preparation is another major part of the experience. You can **train troops** so your forces are ready when night falls, and you can even **upgrade them with shining armor in the armory**. As your settlement grows, you are able to **expand into new territories**, unlocking more resources and building more houses for incoming citizens. More workers mean a stronger economy and a larger army, allowing your city to grow into a more powerful fortress.

For extra defensive power, **siege engines** such as **trebuchets and ballistas** can be placed on top of towers. These machines require enough ammunition, but when properly supplied, they can play an important role in protecting your castle from danger.

When night comes, the real test begins. Your castle will be attacked by **moon monsters**, and their assaults become stronger the longer you play. Enemies may come equipped with **rams or siege towers** to break through your walls, making every siege a tense and exciting challenge. The question at the heart of **Becastled** is simple and compelling: **how long can you survive?**

With its strong focus on castle construction, resource management, army training, and nightly sieges, **Becastled** offers a rewarding fantasy strategy experience full of planning, pressure, and defense.
