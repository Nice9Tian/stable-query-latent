*Jurassic World Evolution 2* is a thrilling and ambitious management simulation that expands greatly on the groundbreaking foundation of Frontier’s 2018 original. As the much-anticipated sequel, it delivers an all-new narrative campaign voiced by cast members from across the *Jurassic World* film franchise, along with exciting new features, four engaging game modes, and a broader roster of awe-inspiring prehistoric species.

In Campaign mode, players step into a compelling original story set in the aftermath of *Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom*. Working alongside iconic characters such as Dr. Ian Malcolm, voiced by Jeff Goldblum, and Claire Dearing, voiced by Bryce Dallas Howard, you take on the challenge of controlling, conserving, and containing wild dinosaurs now rampaging across the USA. It is a true test of park management mastery, placing you at the center of the action.

The game offers deeper management tools and creative freedom, allowing you to construct customizable new buildings, hire Scientists, and shape your park around the needs of both guests and dinosaurs. Sandbox mode provides a space to unleash your creativity, while Challenge mode tests your abilities across diverse locations and environmental calamities.

One of the most exciting aspects of the game is its “what-if” approach to the *Jurassic World* and *Jurassic Park* films. Players can experience key moments from all five movies across different eras and locations, seeing how events unfold when they are in control. The result is a fresh and engaging way to revisit the franchise’s most memorable scenes.

The prehistoric life on display is more alive than ever, with over 75 species included in the game. This roster features highly requested flying and marine reptiles, and the creatures behave with impressive intelligence as they claim territory, fight for dominance, and react to the world around them. Bioengineering adds another layer of depth, letting players customize dinosaurs with bold new colors and alter their genomes to unlock traits.

The Deluxe Edition adds even more value with five spectacular prehistoric animals, including new marine and flying reptiles: Geosternbergia, Attenborosaurus, Pachyrhinosaurus, Huayangosaurus, and Megalosaurus. It also includes six unique building signs and three vehicle skins inspired by the Dinosaur Protection Group, available for the Ranger Team, Capture Team, and Mobile Vet Unit in Challenge and Sandbox modes.

With its rich campaign, flexible modes, advanced management systems, and remarkable selection of prehistoric species, *Jurassic World Evolution 2* offers an exciting and polished evolution of the series.
