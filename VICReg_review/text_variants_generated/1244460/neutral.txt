Jurassic World Evolution 2 is the sequel to Frontier’s 2018 management simulation, Jurassic World Evolution. It expands the series with an all-new narrative campaign voiced by cast members from the Jurassic World film franchise, along with new features, four game modes, and a larger roster of prehistoric species.

In Campaign mode, players follow an original story set after the events of Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom. The mode places the player at the center of efforts to control, conserve, and contain wild dinosaurs across the USA. Familiar characters from the films appear, including Dr. Ian Malcolm, voiced by Jeff Goldblum, and Claire Dearing, voiced by Bryce Dallas Howard.

The game offers deeper management tools and more creative options than its predecessor. Players can build a variety of customizable new buildings, hire Scientists, and shape a park to satisfy both guest and dinosaur needs. Sandbox mode allows for free-form creativity, while Challenge mode tests players with different locations and environmental calamities.

Jurassic World Evolution 2 also includes key moments from Jurassic World and Jurassic Park films presented as “what-if” scenarios. These levels span eras and locations from all five movies, letting players explore alternate outcomes while managing their own park and dinosaur populations.

A major part of the experience is its expanded prehistoric roster. The game features over 75 species, including flying and marine reptiles. These animals have more detailed behavior, with territory, dominance, and reactions to the surrounding world playing a greater role than before. Players can also use bioengineering to customize dinosaurs with new colors and to alter genomes for trait changes.

The Deluxe Edition adds five prehistoric animals: Geosternbergia, Attenborosaurus, Pachyrhinosaurus, Huayangosaurus, and Megalosaurus. It also includes six unique building signs and three vehicle skins inspired by the Dinosaur Protection Group for the Ranger Team, Capture Team, and Mobile Vet Unit in Challenge and Sandbox modes.
