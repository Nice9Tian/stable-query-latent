This management simulation places the player in charge of building and running a prehistoric wildlife park while responding to the aftermath of a major film-based disaster. It expands on the earlier entry in the series with an original, voice-acted campaign, deeper control systems, and four distinct game modes that shape how the experience unfolds.

In the story-driven campaign, the player works to control, conserve, and contain wild prehistoric creatures that are now spreading across the United States. The narrative features recognizable film characters, though the focus remains on park operations and crisis management. Beyond the campaign, the game offers a creative sandbox for unrestricted building and a challenge-oriented mode that tests the player across different environments and emergencies. It also includes scenario-style stages inspired by key moments from the franchise’s films, reimagined with alternate outcomes.

Park construction and management are central to the experience. Players can create and customize a variety of buildings, recruit specialists, and adjust their park to meet the needs of both visitors and animals. The management layer is described as more detailed than before, giving players additional tools and creative freedom.

The creature roster is large, with more than 75 prehistoric species represented, including flying and marine animals as well as land-dwelling ones. These creatures are presented as more lifelike, with behavior that includes territory control, dominance struggles, and intelligent reactions to their surroundings. Bioengineering systems allow further customization through altered appearance and genome traits.

A premium version adds several extra prehistoric species, along with unique building signs and themed vehicle appearances for different park response teams, available in the game’s challenge and sandbox modes. Overall, the game combines park simulation, creature management, and scenario-based progression into a broad strategy experience centered on prehistoric life.
