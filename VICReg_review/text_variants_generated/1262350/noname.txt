Set in a bleak retro-futuristic nightmare, this survival horror game follows a technician-like android who awakens from cryostasis aboard a damaged vessel and begins a lonely search for a lost partner and fragments of a vanished past. Stranded on a frozen world, the protagonist pushes onward into unknown depths, uncovering unsettling truths while navigating a tense, melancholic atmosphere shaped by cosmic dread and psychological horror.

The world is a dystopian future in which humanity has spread across the solar system under the control of a totalitarian regime that relies on surveillance and propaganda. Humanlike androids live alongside ordinary citizens, filling roles such as workers, civil servants, and protectors while resembling the people they are built to imitate. As the journey unfolds, the player explores a ruined spaceship, investigates the fate of the inhabitants of a doomed facility, and searches for what lies beneath the surface of the disaster.

Gameplay centers on fear, caution, and survival. Players must manage limited resources carefully, solve challenging puzzles, and confront disturbing creatures and other strange horrors. The experience emphasizes exploration, apprehension, and discovery, with the player moving through dim, oppressive environments and piecing together a science-fiction story about identity, memory, and the terror of the unknown.

Visually, the game presents a brutalist, industrial nightmare brought to life with fluid 3D character animation, dynamic lighting and shadows, and intricate transparency effects. Its presentation is further reinforced by cinematic sci-fi anime storytelling, creating an experience that blends classic survival horror structure with surreal, atmospheric science fiction.
