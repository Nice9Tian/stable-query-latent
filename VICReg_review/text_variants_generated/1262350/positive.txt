SIGNALIS is a striking survival horror experience that blends classic psychological dread with a haunting retro-futuristic vision. Set in a dystopian future where humanity has spread across the solar system, the game follows Elster, a technician Replika who awakens from cryostasis aboard a wrecked vessel and finds herself stranded on a cold, unknown planet. From there, she embarks on a lonely journey in search of her lost partner and the dreams she has lost along the way.

The world of SIGNALIS is defined by tension, mystery, and melancholy. It takes place under the oppressive rule of Eusan, a totalitarian regime sustained by surveillance and propaganda, where humanoid androids called Replikas live among the population as workers, civil servants, and Protektors. As Elster explores a derelict spaceship, uncovers the fate of a doomed facility, and pushes deeper into the unknown, the game builds an atmosphere of cosmic dread and unsettling discovery.

Gameplay centers on careful survival and observation. Players must face strange horrors, manage scarce resources, and solve challenging riddles while navigating terrifying environments filled with nightmarish creatures. The experience emphasizes fear and apprehension, rewarding patience and attention as the player searches for solutions and uncovers buried truths.

SIGNALIS also stands out through its presentation. Its brutalist nightmare aesthetic is brought to life with fluid 3D character animations, dynamic lighting and shadows, and detailed transparency effects. These visuals are paired with cinematic sci-fi anime storytelling, creating a distinctive and memorable atmosphere that complements its themes of identity, memory, and the terror of the unknown.

Inspired by classic cosmic horror and the works of Stanley Kubrick, Hideaki Anno, and David Lynch, SIGNALIS delivers a deeply atmospheric science-fiction tale that is as beautiful as it is unsettling. It is a remarkable journey into a surreal retrotech world, where every step forward leads further into mystery.
