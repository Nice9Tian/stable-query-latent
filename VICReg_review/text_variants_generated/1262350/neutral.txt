SIGNALIS is a psychological survival horror game set in a dystopian future where humanity has colonized the solar system under the control of the totalitarian Eusan regime. The setting combines retro-tech imagery with a bleak, surreal atmosphere, presenting a science-fiction world shaped by surveillance, propaganda, and industrial decay.

The story follows Elster, a technician Replika, who awakens from cryostasis in a wrecked vessel and finds herself stranded on a cold planet. Searching for her lost partner and her lost dreams, she ventures into dangerous and mysterious places in an attempt to uncover what happened and what lies beneath the surface of the world around her.

Gameplay focuses on exploration, tension, and survival. Players encounter strange horrors, manage scarce resources carefully, and solve challenging riddles while moving through dim, hostile environments such as a derelict spaceship and a mysterious facility filled with secrets. The experience emphasizes fear, apprehension, and the constant pressure of limited supplies and unknown threats.

SIGNALIS also features nightmarish creatures, cosmic dread, and a melancholic tone. Its narrative explores themes of identity, memory, and the terror of the unknown, drawing inspiration from classic cosmic horror as well as the works of Stanley Kubrick, Hideaki Anno, and David Lynch. Visually, it uses fluid 3D character animations, dynamic lighting and shadows, transparency effects, and cinematic sci-fi anime storytelling to create its brutalist nightmare atmosphere.
