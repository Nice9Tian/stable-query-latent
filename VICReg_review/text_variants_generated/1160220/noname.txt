This open-world mystery game takes place on an island outside reality, where a rogue human civilization seeks to resurrect dead alien gods through psychic power. Each island eventually decays under demonic corruption until a new alternate reality is created, and the story begins on the eve of one such rebirth, when the ruling council is murdered and the island itself is destroyed.

The player is sent back from exile as an investigation specialist to identify the culprit and assemble a convincing case. Rather than following a single fixed solution, the game allows multiple possible murderers and multiple ways to construct an accusation. The central challenge is to gather evidence, record testimony, and interpret the facts before presenting a case at trial. A conviction is required to formally solve the crime and move the story forward, but the game emphasizes that “truth” can be shaped by the player’s interpretation.

Exploration is a major part of the experience. Players examine strange relics to uncover the island’s surreal history, interact with hidden clues, and choose whom to accuse. Everyone on the island has secrets and motives, so familiar figures can become suspects as the investigation progresses. The player must weigh evidence, personal judgment, and broader consequences when deciding who to charge.

Progress is also tied to environmental puzzles. Ancient, corrupted computers featuring hieroglyph-style challenges unlock new areas, uncover concealed evidence, and reveal further secrets. A personal computer and electronic assistant accompany the investigation, helping guide the player through the process.

This version adds new music, new mysterious beings, additional quests and rewards, extra character skins, more collectibles, performance improvements, a mode without the interface display, and achievement support. It also includes updated visual technology such as modern rendering support, ray tracing on compatible hardware, and advanced upscaling options. The game additionally offers a range of accessibility settings so the experience can be adjusted to different needs.
