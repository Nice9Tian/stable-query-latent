**Paradise Killer** is a bold and atmospheric open-ended murder mystery set on an island outside reality, where a rogue human civilization attempts to resurrect dead alien gods. At the heart of its strange world is Paradise, an island that regenerates every few millennia, only to be corrupted each time by demonic influence—until the Council creates a new alternate reality. On the eve of the next rebirth, the Council is murdered and Paradise itself is destroyed, setting the stage for a “crime to end all crimes.”

Players step into the role of Lady Love Dies, an “investigation freak” summoned from exile to uncover the culprit. The game invites you to collect evidence, record testimony, examine bizarre relics, and interpret the facts to build your own case. You are free to choose who to accuse, and the path to conviction is entirely in your hands. Every resident of Paradise has secrets, motives, and hidden ambitions, making each suspect a compelling part of the mystery.

A standout feature of the experience is its emphasis on interpretation and personal truth. There are many possible killers, and many ways to construct a convincing prosecution. Even after a successful conviction, the game encourages you to question whether the mystery is truly solved. This makes every investigation feel meaningful, thoughtful, and uniquely your own.

Paradise Island is filled with cryptic lore, surreal history, and unsettling discoveries. Players can solve hieroglyph puzzles on ancient nightmare computers to unlock new areas, uncover concealed evidence, and reveal terrifying secrets. With its distinctive setting and layered storytelling, the game offers a rich world that rewards curiosity and careful investigation.
