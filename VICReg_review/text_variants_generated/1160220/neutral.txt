Paradise Killer is an investigation-driven adventure set on Paradise Island, an island outside of reality where a rogue human civilization seeks to resurrect dead alien gods. The island regenerates every few millennia, but each cycle is threatened by demonic corruption, leading the Council to create a new alternate reality. On the eve of the next rebirth, Perfect 25, the Council is murdered and Paradise is killed. In response, Lady Love Dies, an “investigation freak” exiled from the island, is summoned to uncover the culprit.

The game centers on building a case, selecting a suspect, and presenting evidence at trial. Players are tasked with collecting evidence, recording testimony, and interpreting facts to support an accusation. A conviction is required for the crime to be considered solved and for Paradise to be saved. However, the game emphasizes that there are many possible killers and many possible interpretations of the evidence, so the player’s chosen suspect and argument shape their own version of the truth.

Paradise Island is presented as an alternate-future setting filled with cryptic lore, surreal history, and hidden mysteries. Players examine bizarre relics, solve hieroglyph puzzles on ancient nightmare computers, discover new areas of the island, and uncover concealed evidence. The cast is defined by secrets and competing interests, and even old friends can become suspects. Players must weigh evidence, the greater good, and personal beliefs while constructing a case.

This updated and enhanced version includes new music, new mysterious beings, additional quests and rewards, extra Starlight skins, more collectibles, performance improvements, a HUDless mode, and Steam achievements. It also adds DX12 support, ray tracing in DX12 mode, and advanced upscaling options including NVIDIA DLSS and AMD SR. The game also features accessibility options that allow players to tailor the experience to their needs.
