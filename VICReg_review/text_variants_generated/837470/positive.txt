Untitled Goose Game is a charming slapstick-stealth-sandbox that turns mischief into an art form. Set in a peaceful village, it puts you in the feathers of a thoroughly horrible goose, free to wander from people’s back gardens to the high street shops and the village green, causing delightful chaos wherever you go.

The game’s pleasures come from its simple but wonderfully effective design: setting up pranks, stealing hats, honking loudly, and generally ruining everyone’s day. A dedicated honk button adds to the fun, making every act of nuisance feel immediate and memorable. The result is a playful experience built around experimentation, comedy, and the joy of being as troublesome as possible.

Untitled Goose Game also features a free two-player cooperative mode, letting you enjoy the entire game with a friend as two horrible geese. Together, you can double the honking, team up to plan pranks, and spread even more cheerful disruption across the village. With its clever premise and consistently mischievous spirit, it offers a wonderfully funny adventure that fully embraces being bad in the best way.
