This is a slapstick-stealth-sandbox set in a quiet village, where the player takes control of a disruptive bird and is let loose among unsuspecting residents. The experience revolves around moving through different parts of town, including back gardens, shop streets, and the village green, while causing mischief by setting up pranks, stealing hats, honking frequently, and generally interrupting the daily routine of everyone nearby.

The core appeal lies in its playful, absurd premise and simple tools for creating chaos. A dedicated honk action is part of the experience, reinforcing the game’s comedic tone and the sense of being an intentionally troublesome presence. The village itself is filled with ordinary people trying to go about their day, which forms the basis for the stealth and prank-driven gameplay.

The game also includes a cooperative two-player mode available through a free update. In this mode, the entire game can be played with a second goose, allowing players to coordinate pranks, honk more often, and cause trouble together across the full adventure. The structure remains the same, but the addition of a second player changes the dynamic by making the disruption more chaotic and collaborative.
