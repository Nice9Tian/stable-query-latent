Untitled Goose Game is a slapstick-stealth-sandbox game in which the player controls a goose loose in an unsuspecting village. The game presents a mischievous premise: it is a lovely morning in the village, and you are a horrible goose. Players move through different parts of town, including people’s back gardens, the high street shops, and the village green, where they can set up pranks, steal hats, honk frequently, and generally disrupt the daily lives of the villagers.

The game’s features emphasize its playful and disruptive style. It includes a town full of people trying to go about their day, while the goose actively interferes with them. A dedicated honk button is also highlighted as a central part of the experience.

Untitled Goose Game also includes a two-player cooperative mode. In this free update for all owners, players can play through the entire game with a friend as two horrible geese. The co-op mode allows players to honk twice as much, team up to plan pranks, and continue ruining everyone’s day together.
