This is a large-scale multiplayer space game centered on construction, exploration, resource gathering, crafting, trading, and combat. Set in a vast and persistent universe, it gives players broad freedom to shape their own path through building custom spacecraft, stations, and automated industrial systems, or by taking part in exploration, hauling, mining, and battles.

A defining feature of the game is its fully destructible environment. Using a hybrid voxel-and-vertex simulation, structures can be damaged, disassembled, or destroyed with the appropriate tools and weapons. This system also affects flight and combat, since ship durability, structural design, and elemental damage all matter. Poorly balanced builds can fail under stress, while careful engineering can produce highly capable machines. The smallest construction unit is a single bolt, allowing extremely detailed editing and repair of ships, stations, and devices.

Players can design vehicles and facilities in fine detail, then even sell their plans as blueprints. Ships and stations can include complex electrical and data networks, and the use of programming chips adds another layer of automation and customization. The game encourages inventive layouts, smart logistics, and creative problem-solving as ways to improve performance and efficiency.

Combat takes place both on foot and in ships, with seamless transitions between FPS action and space warfare. Large numbers of players can participate at once, and teamwork can be important for crew-based roles, coordinated maneuvers, and faction-level cooperation. Solo play is also possible, but the game places strong emphasis on group organization, alliances, and shared goals.

Exploration is another major component. Players can travel through an expanding universe to find stations, asteroid fields, ship wrecks, social hubs, and remote locations. Mining asteroids, salvaging wreckage, and transporting cargo are all part of the progression loop, alongside buying, selling, and trading in a player-driven economy. Goods include ship components, materials, weapons, tools, ammunition, and design plans.

The game also includes safe zones around starting stations to support new players, as well as configurable safe zones on player-owned stations. Over time, players can expand from a single planetary orbit to moons, other planets, and eventually distant star systems, building stations and factions that help define the evolving universe. Although it launches in an early alpha state, its core systems are established, and the experience already offers a broad mix of creation, cooperation, and conflict.
