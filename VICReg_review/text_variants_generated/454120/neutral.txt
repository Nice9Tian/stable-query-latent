Starbase is a massively multiplayer online game set in a vast, persistent universe where players build and design spaceships and stations, explore space, gather resources, craft, trade, and engage in combat. The game emphasizes creativity and player freedom, allowing players to construct everything from small ships to large automated factories, as well as electricity and data networks. Its environment is fully destructible, and its hybrid voxel/vertex-based simulation supports detailed physics, structural damage, and elemental durability.

At the core of Starbase is ship and station construction. Players can design, build, repair, and modify ships, stations, and devices down to the smallest building unit, a single bolt. Even small ships can contain thousands of parts, each with its own damage model. The game also supports in-game programming chips, which expand automation and ship design possibilities. Players can sell blueprints and develop more advanced builds by combining engineering, creativity, and clever layouts.

Starbase also features seamless combat on foot and in ships, with thousands of players able to participate simultaneously. Players can fly solo or work in crews, dividing responsibilities during battles and other activities. The fully destructible world means that anything can be dismantled or destroyed with the proper tools and weaponry, and poorly designed ships can fail under stress, collision, or other damage if their structural choices are weak.

Exploration and economy are also major parts of the game. Players can travel through an expanding universe to find stations, asteroid fields, ship wrecks, social hubs, and other travelers. They can mine asteroids, salvage ship parts, trade at stations, and buy or sell ship components, materials, weapons, tools, ammo, and designs in a player-driven economy. The game also includes piracy, cargo hauling, and opportunities to earn credits through different means.

Social play is supported through friendships, alliances, and factions. Players can group up to build massive stations and take on larger challenges together. Starting station safe zones are intended to protect the new player experience, and dynamic safe zones can be configured on player-owned stations. The game’s universe is described as open-ended and still expanding, with players beginning around large stations in orbit of a single planet and eventually reaching moons, other planets, and distant star systems.

Starbase is developed using Frozenbyte’s internal engine, built specifically to support the game’s scale, simulation, damage systems, building mechanics, and large player counts. The game is in Early Access and described as being in a clear alpha stage, though its core features are already established.
