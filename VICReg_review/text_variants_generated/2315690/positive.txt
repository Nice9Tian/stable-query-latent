WWE 2K24 delivers a powerful celebration of sports entertainment, led by the milestone Forty Years of WrestleMania showcase that revisits some of the most unforgettable, career-defining moments in WWE history. As WrestleMania’s legacy unfolds, players can experience a gripping retelling of the biggest stage in wrestling through 2K Showcase of the Immortals, while stepping into a roster packed with legends and modern superstars alike.

The game features an iconic lineup that includes WWE greats such as “Stone Cold” Steve Austin, Undertaker, and Andre the Giant, alongside current stars like “The American Nightmare” Cody Rhodes, John Cena, Rhea Ripley, and Roman Reigns. Their larger-than-life entrances and signature moves are brought to life with ultra-realistic graphics, creating an authentic and high-impact presentation worthy of WWE’s biggest names.

WWE 2K24 also introduces exciting new match types, including Guest Referee, Casket, Gauntlet, and Ambulance matches, along with support for multiple Superstars in Backstage Brawl. These additions expand the action and give players even more ways to experience the chaos and spectacle of WWE competition.

In MyRISE, players can prove themselves and rise from aspiring talent to undisputed champion through two distinct career arcs, featuring original storylines from both the men’s and women’s divisions. MyFACTION lets players build a dream team of WWE Superstars and Legends by collecting cards, assembling a dominant faction, and competing in online multiplayer and ranked quickplay, all while earning seasonal rewards. Universe mode remains the ultimate sandbox for shaping rivalries, weekly shows, and championship drama, now enhanced with expanded rivalry actions, all-new cutscenes, and support for double-title matches. MyGM rounds out the experience by letting players take control of the biggest brands, make contract decisions, trade Superstars, and outmaneuver rival general managers in the race for supremacy.

Several special editions add even more content to the package. The Bray Wyatt Edition celebrates the legacy of one of WWE’s most captivating Superstars with bonus content including a brand-new playable version of The Fiend, a Mattel Bray Wyatt action figure, updated Uncle Howdy featuring Bo Dallas, a Fiend-themed championship belt, a Firefly Funhouse MyFACTION manager card, and 15,000 VC. The Forty Years of WrestleMania Edition includes the WrestleMania 40 Arena, Gold MyFACTION cards, alternate WrestleMania attires for multiple Superstars, the Nightmare Family Pack, a Season Pass, and Showcase Superstar Unlock. The Deluxe Edition and Bray Wyatt Bundle further expand the experience with bonus packs, VC, and additional content, making WWE 2K24 a rich and impressive tribute to WWE’s past, present, and future.

With its mix of legendary moments, stacked rosters, fresh match types, and deep modes, WWE 2K24 stands as an energetic and faithful celebration of wrestling at its finest.
