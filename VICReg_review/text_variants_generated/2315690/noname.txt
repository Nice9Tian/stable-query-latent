This sports entertainment title presents a large-scale wrestling experience built around spectacle, roster depth, and multiple ways to play. One of its central themes is a forty-year celebration of a major annual showcase event, presented through a mode that revisits memorable, career-defining matches from that event’s history. The package is designed to highlight both legacy moments and modern presentation, blending historical content with current-era production values.

The game features a broad lineup of selectable performers, including well-known legends and active stars. Their entrances, signature moves, and overall presentation are emphasized through detailed visuals. Beyond the standard roster, several premium editions and bundles add alternate attires, special playable variants, themed manager cards, virtual currency, and other bonus content. Some releases also include post-launch downloadable content, additional packs, and unlock features tied to the game’s showcase content.

In terms of match variety, the game expands beyond traditional one-on-one competition. It includes several new match types, such as guest referee, casket, gauntlet, and ambulance matches, along with support for multiple performers in backstage brawls. This gives players a range of rule sets and scenarios that differ from standard bouts.

Career progression is offered through a story-driven mode with two distinct arcs, one for each division, where players work toward championship status through original storylines. Another mode focuses on collecting cards, assembling a custom faction, and competing online, including ranked quick play and seasonal reward progression. A separate sandbox mode lets players shape ongoing shows, rivalries, and major events, with expanded rivalry actions, new cutscenes, and support for double-title matches. There is also a management mode where players run a brand, handle contracts, make strategic decisions, trade talent, and compete against rival managers.

Overall, the game combines historical celebration, a large roster, varied match formats, career storytelling, card-based team building, sandbox booking, and brand management into a single wrestling package. It is structured to appeal to players interested in both the entertainment spectacle and the strategic systems behind it.
