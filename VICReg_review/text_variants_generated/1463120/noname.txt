This choice-driven adult visual novel follows a freshman as they begin their college life at a fictional campus. The story presents a campus setting shaped by parties, romance, social tension, and personal ambition, where the player decides whether to remain an ordinary student or pursue a path toward leadership and status.

The game is built around player choice. Throughout the experience, decisions influence the direction of the story, the characters encountered, and the opportunities that become available. Different paths can lead to new scenes, altered relationships, and changes in how the wider campus community reacts to the player. A reputation system tracks these outcomes, affecting character perception and progression options.

A major focus of the experience is relationship building. The player can meet a range of characters with distinct personalities, interact with them in free-roam sections, and unlock individual storylines through continued engagement. The game emphasizes connections that feel personal and reactive, with choices shaping how relationships develop over time.

The presentation also includes interactive adult scenes designed to place the player at the center of the action, along with a scene selector that allows revisiting earlier moments and exploring alternate outcomes. In addition, the game features a fighting mode that adds a separate gameplay element, described as challenging yet rewarding. Hidden content, side stories, and other mechanics contribute to replay value and variation between playthroughs.

Overall, the game is a free-to-play, story-focused experience combining narrative branching, relationship progression, reputation management, interactive adult content, and combat. It bundles multiple episodes into a single complete release for a unified playthrough.
