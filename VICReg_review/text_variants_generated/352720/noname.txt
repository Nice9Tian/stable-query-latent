This strategy city builder blends settlement management with divine intervention, placing the player in the role of a guiding force over a fledgling sentient species known here as the “Nivans.” The experience begins in a primitive era and gradually carries the civilization from early survival through advanced technological development and, ultimately, into interplanetary expansion.

A key part of the game is direct supernatural control. The player can command more than 15 different godlike abilities to protect, steer, or punish the population. These powers include dramatic natural events such as tornadoes, lightning strikes, and earthquakes, giving the experience a strong mix of management and hands-on intervention. The tone can shift between benevolent guidance and harsh discipline, depending on how the player chooses to shape the society.

The Nivans are not passive units; they are described as living, evolving beings with their own behaviors, needs, and struggles. The player can observe them as they work, survive, and respond to conflicts, disasters, alien life, and encounters with other civilizations. Their development is tied to the player’s decisions, creating a system where growth depends on both careful planning and reactive problem-solving.

Progression is supported by more than 200 research-based advancements across technological and social development. These unlock new capabilities and help the civilization advance through successive eras. As the population grows, the game emphasizes managing resources, maintaining stability, and helping the settlement adapt to changing demands.

Environmental management is also central. Each world features delicate ecosystems, and the player’s actions, along with the population’s behavior, can affect planetary climate and create lasting ecological consequences. The planets themselves are procedurally generated, with unique ecosystems and challenges, so each world presents a different combination of opportunities and risks. Colonizing the galaxy becomes a broader objective as the civilization expands beyond its home world.

Beyond the simulation and progression systems, the game also includes a deeper cosmic narrative that gradually reveals hidden layers about existence and identity. This gives the overall experience an additional mystery-driven structure, adding story context to the management and god-game mechanics.
