The Universim is a strategy city builder with god-game elements in which players guide a civilization of “Nuggets” from the Stone Age to the Space Age. The game centers on taking the role of a deity-like force, using divine powers to influence, protect, or punish a sentient species as it develops over time.

Players can command more than 15 godly powers, including summoning tornadoes, striking followers with lightning, and shaking the earth. At the same time, they observe the Nuggets as they work, survive, and evolve through changing eras. Their development is supported by more than 200 technological and societal advancements, allowing the civilization to progress and expand.

A major part of the game is managing resources and maintaining ecological balance. Planetary ecosystems are delicate, and both player decisions and Nugget actions affect global climates and can lead to environmental consequences. Nuggets also have their own needs and behaviors, and players must guide them through conflicts, natural disasters, alien creatures, and interactions with other civilizations.

The Universim also emphasizes exploration and replayability. Players can discover and colonize a galaxy of procedurally generated planets, each with unique ecosystems and challenges. In addition to managing civilization growth, the game includes a deeper cosmic story that gradually reveals more about the player’s existence and introduces unexpected twists.
