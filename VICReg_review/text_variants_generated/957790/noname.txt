This title is a large-scale VR shooter built around 32v32 multiplayer battles across expansive battlefields. Its design emphasizes fast-paced action, balanced mechanics, and an arcade-style approach rather than strict realism. Matches take place on a mix of official and player-created maps, with day-and-night environments and vehicle combat adding variety to each session.

The core mode centers on conquest-style objectives, where teams capture control points while fighting opponents across wide terrain. Players can earn XP to unlock a broad range of equipment, including weapons, accessories, skins, and ammunition types. Four classes are available: Axiom, Forge, Pulse, and Ranger. The game also includes multiple vehicle types, several gun categories, support deployables, grenades, and a large selection of weapon cosmetics, allowing for substantial loadout customization.

Movement and traversal are important parts of the experience. Players can move quickly across large maps using vehicles, squad-based spawning, and aerial mobility options. The VR locomotion system offers three movement styles, with controller- or headset-directed aiming for movement, plus smooth or snap turning. The game supports both standing and seated play.

A notable feature is its modding support. Custom content can include conquest and team deathmatch maps, private or public servers, and modifications to maps, weapons, vehicles, and player avatars through a template included with the game files. The game is built with compatibility around a widely used engine, which supports community creation and ongoing expansion.

The multiplayer structure includes official servers in multiple regions, and matches may contain real players and bots depending on availability. Bots fill empty slots in official rotations, helping maintain full 64-soldier battles. The player base is described as active, with higher participation during peak hours and weekend events. Development is ongoing, with regular updates, fixes, optimization, and new content added over time through community-led effort.

Overall, the experience is aimed at players who want large VR battles, flexible customization, and accessible combat with a strong community and modding scene. It combines tactical objective play with arcade-like gun handling, large maps, and persistent development support.
