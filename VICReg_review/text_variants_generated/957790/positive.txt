War Dust VR: 32v32 Battlefields delivers a thrilling large-scale VR shooter experience built around fast-paced, arcade-style combat and huge multiplayer battles. Set across expansive battlefields, the game emphasizes fun, balanced mechanics, and constant action, creating an immersive warzone where players can jump into intense firefights and keep coming back for more.

At the heart of the experience are massive 32v32 multiplayer battles across a variety of official maps, along with additional player-created maps. These diverse landscapes include vehicles, dynamic day and night environments, and plenty of room for tactical movement. The scale of the combat is one of the game’s defining strengths, and it stands out by combining large battlefield design with the immediacy and energy of VR.

War Dust VR uses conquest-style gameplay, where capturing control points and defeating enemies are central to victory. Players can progress through an XP system to unlock a wide range of weapons, accessories, skins, and ammunition types. Four distinct classes are available—Assault, Engineer, Support, and Scout—giving players clear roles and strategic options in battle. The game also offers an impressive selection of content, including 5 types of vehicles, 15 types of guns, 3 support spawnables, 2 types of grenades, and more than 80 weapon skins.

The game’s movement and combat options further enhance its flexibility. Players can maneuver across the battlefield using paragliding, flying, driving vehicles, and squad-based spawning, making it easier to move quickly and stay engaged in the fight. Gunplay follows an arcade approach rather than strict realism, with some recoil and spread on all weapons, and physical interaction for loading and removing magazines using the grab button. Weapons are held in a customizable virtual belt area around the waist, which resets after death.

War Dust VR also offers strong modding support, giving players the opportunity to shape their own experience. The game includes support for Conquest and Team Deathmatch maps, customizable public or password-protected servers, and compatibility with the Unity engine. A modding template included in the game files allows players to modify maps, weapons, vehicles, and player avatars, making creativity a major part of the community-driven experience.

The game is backed by an international player base and official servers in the US East, EU, Asia, and Aus. It continues to receive small and regular updates, with ongoing work focused on new features, maps, bug fixes, optimization, and polishing. With support for multiple locomotion options, including thumbstick, pro-loco, and fitrun, along with smooth or snap turning and both standing and sitting play, War Dust VR is designed to be accessible and adaptable for many VR players.

For fans of large-scale VR combat, War Dust VR offers a polished, energetic, and highly customizable battlefield experience. Its combination of massive battles, class-based gameplay, vehicles, modding support, and active ongoing development makes it a standout choice for players who want action, scale, and freedom in a VR shooter.
