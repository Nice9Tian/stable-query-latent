This action role-playing game is set in a fantasy universe where magic is tightly controlled by an authoritarian order in a single kingdom. The story follows a spell-stealing mage named **Vayren**, who escapes unjust imprisonment and turns the chains that once restrained them into a weapon. Their goal is to challenge a regime that suppresses magic users in the name of order and to help ignite a rebellion against tyranny.

The gameplay centers on fast-paced combat where magic is a core part of every encounter. Players can master a range of spells, from burning fire to lethal frost, and shape their approach through customization. Vayren can fight by using stolen enemy magic, striking directly with chained attacks, or relying on hand-to-hand combat. This flexibility allows different play styles depending on how the player wants to engage enemies.

Beyond combat, the game includes a rebellion-building layer. Players are encouraged to search the world for other magic users and recruit them to the cause. As the resistance grows, it can be used to improve the base and unlock new abilities, adding progression and strategic development to the adventure.

The narrative also emphasizes alliance-building and exploration of the kingdom’s history. Players face powerful adversaries while meeting memorable allies, both familiar and new, as they uncover hidden truths about the kingdom’s past. These discoveries shape the struggle for the future and support the larger fight against the oppressive order.

A deluxe version is also available, offering several themed bonus packs that add additional supplies, home-related content, stolen-spell content, and alternate skin options.
