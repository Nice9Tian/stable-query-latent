*The Mageseeker: A League of Legends Story™* is a powerful action RPG set in the world of *League of Legends*, developed by Digital Sun, the creators of *Moonlighter*. Set in the kingdom of Demacia, the game tells the story of a land where the Mageseekers enforce oppression against magic-users in the name of order, imprisoning them or forcing them into hiding. You play as Sylas, a spell-stealing mage who has escaped their captivity and now fights to free his homeland from tyranny.

At the heart of the experience is a satisfying magic-driven combat system. Magic is everywhere on the battlefield, and players can master a range of spells, from searing flames to deadly ice, to shape their own combat style. Sylas can wield the chains that once bound him, using enemy magic against them or striking foes directly with his fists. This flexible approach makes every encounter feel dynamic and personal.

Beyond combat, the game emphasizes rebellion and leadership. Demacia is ready for revolution, and players must seek out mages across the land, recruit them to the cause, and grow a resistance. As the movement expands, the base can be upgraded and new unique abilities can be unlocked, adding a strong sense of progression and strategy to the journey.

The game also delivers a compelling narrative of discovery and resistance. Players battle formidable enemies and form alliances with memorable characters, both new and familiar. Through these encounters, the buried secrets of Demacia’s past are revealed, giving Sylas the chance to reshape the kingdom’s future.

The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the Silverwing Supply Station Pack, the Home Sweet Cave Pack, the Hijacked Spells Pack, and the Unchained Skins Pack, offering additional content for players who want an expanded experience.

With its bold premise, distinctive combat, and empowering rebellion theme, *The Mageseeker: A League of Legends Story™* presents an exciting and richly imagined adventure about breaking chains, reclaiming magic, and leading a revolution.
