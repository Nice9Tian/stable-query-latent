Millennia is a historical turn-based 4X game that lets you create your own nation and guide it across 10,000 years of history, from the dawn of humanity to possible futures. Every playthrough offers a different timeline, allowing you to shape an epic story through your decisions as you lead your people through crises, discoveries, and moments of transformation.

The game spans ten ages, beginning with the first cities and continuing all the way to space travel. Along the way, your choices can trigger a Variant Age, an alternate history period with new rules, new technologies, and new units. These shifts create exciting opportunities to steer civilization in unexpected directions. At the same time, Crisis Ages add tension and challenge, as chaos and disorder can send the world into a future marked by war, sickness, or ignorance. Even so, a strong ruler can turn a crisis into an advantage, making each age feel meaningful and consequential.

A major strength of Millennia is the way it lets you customize your nation through unique Nation Spirits. You can shape your people into warriors, explorers, builders, cultural icons, or financial powers, with your choices echoing across history. This gives each campaign a distinct identity and encourages thoughtful roleplay as well as strategic planning.

The economy is equally deep and rewarding. You begin with basic resources such as food, wealth, and production, then gradually convert them into more advanced goods through research and improvements. Regions can be specialized to produce iron, wood, gold, and more, turning raw materials into weapons, tools, paper, lumber, coin, or jewelry. Surplus goods can be shipped where they are needed, supporting distant colonies, feeding your empire’s breadbasket, or supplying your troops near the front lines. This creates a satisfying network of growth, trade, and industrial development.

Progress through knowledge is central to success. Scientific and cultural research unlocks new units, buildings, and improvements that expand your cities and strengthen your armies. Technologies such as Defense, Smelting, and Guilds illustrate the breadth of advancement available, from archers and iron production to wine-making. Variant and Crisis Ages also introduce unique possibilities, including Cloud Estates in the Age of Aether and underwater cities in the Age of Utopia, giving the game a remarkable sense of variety and imagination.

Ultimately, Millennia builds toward a Victory Age, where dominance can determine the winning conditions and push the world into a final contest for triumph. Whether you aim for victory in the Renaissance or carefully prepare for success in the modern era, the game rewards bold planning, adaptability, and long-term vision. It is a rich and ambitious strategy experience that celebrates the rise, evolution, and fate of civilizations across the full sweep of history.
