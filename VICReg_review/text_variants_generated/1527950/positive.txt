**Wartales** presents a harsh and compelling medieval open world where survival, profit, and ambition define every step of the journey. Set a century after the fall of the Edoran Empire, the land has been left in the grip of plague, banditry, thievery, and widespread destitution. Honor has all but disappeared, and players are invited to lead a band of mercenaries through a world that offers no promise of heroism—only the challenge of enduring and thriving against relentless adversity.

At its core, Wartales is about guiding a group of unscrupulous characters across a vast open world in pursuit of riches and recognition. Players can recruit companions with distinct specializations, skill sets, weapon preferences, and personalities, then shape their development through an intuitive RPG progression and crafting system. Equipment, abilities, and appearance can all be customized, while the camp itself can be expanded with luxuries, tools, and gear designed to help the group recover and persevere through each difficult day.

The world is rich with places to explore and stories to uncover. Players can travel through lively villages and the remnants of a forgotten age, while venturing into abandoned mines, tombs, and camps to piece together the history of this unforgiving land. Contracts and bounties provide constant opportunities for work, ranging from protecting the innocent from thieves to hunting down some of the realm’s most notorious criminals. In Wartales, no job is too small to be worthwhile if it brings profit.

Combat is delivered through a tactical turn-based system that emphasizes planning and strategy. Each battle rewards thoughtful decisions, careful positioning, and the right combination of characters, gear, and tactics. Whether confronting dangerous enemies in single-player or coordinating with up to 4 players in cooperative multiplayer, the game encourages teamwork and preparation before engaging the many hostile forces that roam the world.

Wartales also shines in the bonds formed between companions. Players can share money, loot, and resources, then end the day gathered around a roaring campfire with a hearty meal. These moments of rest and camaraderie strengthen the group, helping forge the unity needed to overcome the many challenges ahead. With its expansive world, rewarding tactical combat, and strong sense of survival-driven progression, Wartales offers a richly grounded mercenary adventure full of grit, strategy, and hard-earned success.
