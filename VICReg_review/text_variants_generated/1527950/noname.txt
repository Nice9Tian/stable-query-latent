Set in a medieval world scarred by collapse, this game places you in the role of a mercenary leader struggling to survive in a land shaped by plague, lawlessness, and greed. Rather than presenting a heroic journey, it focuses on survival, profit, and the harsh realities of life in a hostile open world. The player guides a band of hardened companions through a region filled with banditry, thievery, and danger, where every day is defined by combat, loss, and the pursuit of wealth.

The core experience centers on recruiting and managing a group of unique mercenaries, each with distinct specializations, skill sets, weapon preferences, and personalities. As the group grows, players can shape their development through an RPG progression system, customize their equipment and appearance, and craft gear to improve their chances of survival. A camp-building layer also plays an important role, allowing the party to develop luxuries, tools, and equipment that support recovery and endurance between encounters.

Exploration is a major part of the game, with a large open world containing villages, ruins, abandoned mines, tombs, and camps. These locations reveal fragments of the world’s past while also providing opportunities for profit. Players can take on bounties and contracts, ranging from protecting the innocent from minor criminals to confronting the land’s most dangerous figures. The game emphasizes that no contract is too small if it brings a reward.

Combat uses a tactical turn-based system built around planning and strategic decision-making. Success depends on choosing the right combination of characters, equipment, and tactics for each battle, with encounters designed to reward careful preparation. In multiplayer, up to four players can traverse the world together, coordinate tactics before fights, and face hostile enemies as a team. Shared resources, loot, money, and campfire meals reinforce the sense of camaraderie as the group endures the challenges of this unforgiving land.
