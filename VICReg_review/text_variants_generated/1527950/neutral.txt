Wartales is a medieval open-world role-playing game set a century after the fall of the Edoran Empire, which collapsed under the spread of a devastating plague. In the wake of that disaster, the land has become a place of mercenary work, banditry, thievery, and survival, where honor is largely forgotten and wealth and endurance are the main goals. Players do not take on the role of a traditional hero, but instead lead a group of unscrupulous characters through a harsh world where combat, death, and the pursuit of riches shape everyday life.

The game focuses on guiding a band of mercenaries on a dangerous journey for fortune and recognition. Players can recruit companions with different specializations, skill sets, weapon preferences, and personalities, then shape the group through an RPG progression system. Equipment, skills, and appearance can all be customized, while camp development adds another layer of management through luxuries, tools, and equipment that help the party recover and endure the hardships of the road.

Wartales takes place in a large open world filled with villages and traces of a fallen era. Exploration leads to abandoned mines, tombs, and camps, where players can uncover the history of the setting. The game also features bounties and contracts, ranging from protecting civilians from petty thieves to confronting some of the most notorious figures in the land.

Combat is tactical and turn-based, emphasizing planning and strategy. Success depends on choosing the right combination of characters, gear, and tactics for each battle. The game also supports cooperative play for up to four players, allowing a group to travel across the open world together, plan before encounters, and face hostile inhabitants as a team. Shared money, loot, and resources, along with meals around the campfire, reinforce the sense of companionship as players build their mercenary company and work to survive in a world defined by danger and greed.
