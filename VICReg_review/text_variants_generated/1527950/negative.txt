Wartales presents a grim and joyless medieval mercenary life in a world that has already collapsed under plague, greed, and banditry. Set a century after the fall of the Edoran Empire, it offers no heroic fantasy, no noble crusade, and no uplifting destiny—only survival, profit, and the slow grind of leading a group of unscrupulous companions through a hostile open world. The game frames this harsh setting as something to endure rather than enjoy, with combat, death, and the hunger for riches dominating every decision.

Its core loop is built around recruiting mercenaries with different specializations, skill sets, weapon preferences, and personalities, then trying to manage them through an intuitive RPG progression and crafting system. You can customize skills, equipment, and appearance, while also developing a camp with luxuries, tools, and equipment meant to help the team recover from the day’s hardships. The emphasis is clearly on maintenance, attrition, and damage control, not empowerment.

The open world promises villages, ruins, abandoned mines, tombs, and camps, but even these are tied to the same bleak premise: scavenging fragments of history while chasing notoriety and wealth. Contracts and bounties range from minor theft prevention to hunting down notorious figures, reinforcing the idea that there is no deed too small if it pays. Exploration exists, but it serves a world defined by destitution and decay rather than wonder.

Combat is turn-based and tactical, demanding careful planning, strategic positioning, and the right mix of characters, gear, and tactics for each encounter. Even this seems designed to be unforgiving, forcing players to think constantly just to survive. The game also supports up to four players, allowing them to traverse the open world together, share money, loot, and resources, and end the day around a campfire with a meal. That cooperative layer may encourage camaraderie, but it still sits inside a fundamentally bleak framework where every success is measured in survival and scraps of reward.
