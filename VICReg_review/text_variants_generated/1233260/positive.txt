*FAIRY TAIL* brings the beloved manga and anime series to Steam for the first time, delivering a faithful RPG experience that captures the spirit of the original work. The game lets players step into the role of powerful mages from multiple guilds, with a total of 16 playable characters and appearances from characters beyond the Fairy Tail guild. It also features an original episode, adding a fresh story element for fans to enjoy.

Battle in true *Fairy Tail* fashion with magic combat inspired by the source material. Signature techniques such as Unison Raid and Extreme Magic are included, alongside Unison Raids created specifically for the game. These battles showcase the flashy, collaborative magic that defines the series.

The game also recreates important locations with care, including Magnolia town and the inside of the guild hall. Players can take on requests from the board, complete missions, and raise their guild’s rank together with their comrades as they work toward becoming the No. 1 guild.

A Digital Deluxe edition is also available, including the base game along with additional costumes and Growth Lacrima bonuses.
