FAIRY TAIL is a game based on the popular FAIRY TAIL series and is available on Steam for the first time. It presents a playable cast of 16 characters, including mages from guilds beyond Fairy Tail, and also features an original episode. 

The game centers on magic battles that reflect the style of the source material. Players can use familiar techniques such as Unison Raid and Extreme Magic, along with Unison Raids that are original to the game. 

Progress is tied to the goal of becoming the number one guild. The town of Magnolia and the guild interior are fully represented, and players take on missions from the request board, work with their comrades, and raise their guild rank. 

A Digital Deluxe edition is also available. It includes the base game, a Lucy costume collaboration with Gust and Ryza, several “Fairy Tail Team A” costumes for Natsu, Lucy, Gray, Erza, and Wendy, plus five Limited Edition Growth Lacrima items. The page notes that some included items may become available for purchase later, and that the Limited Edition Growth Lacrima is a bonus tied to purchasable content.
