This game is a Steam release based on a popular fantasy manga and anime series, presented here in a digital deluxe package that includes the base game plus several additional cosmetic items and a set of growth materials. The extra content consists of themed costumes for multiple party members, one collaboration outfit, and a small bundle of bonus enhancement items. The store page also notes that some of these bonus items may later be sold separately or reappear as bonuses in future content.

The game allows players to control a roster of 16 playable characters, including mages from guilds beyond the central one, and it also features an original episode created specifically for the game. Its combat system centers on magical battles that aim to recreate the feel of the source material. Signature techniques from the original work, such as combined attacks and high-level magic, can be used in battle, and the game also introduces original combined attacks exclusive to this version.

Progression is tied to guild life and advancement. The city hub and the interior of the guild headquarters are fully depicted, and players take on missions from a request board, work alongside allies, and raise their guild rank with the goal of becoming the top guild. The page also warns that a separate deluxe set containing the base game is available, so buyers should avoid duplicate purchases.
