Gray Zone Warfare is a tactical first-person shooter set after a mysterious event on a fictional Southeast Asian island has led to a full international quarantine. With most of the civilian population evacuated by the United Nations, three private military companies are sent by their unknown clients to investigate the region, uncover its secrets, and recover anything valuable.

The game emphasizes realistic tactical combat and ballistic simulation, using authentic ballistics models and real-world values. Encounters are high stakes: every action matters, and death can mean losing hard-earned gear and progression. Weapons are highly customizable, allowing players to build firearms to match their needs by adding, removing, or exchanging hundreds of parts, mounts, and accessories. This system changes how each weapon behaves and performs, making loadouts adaptable to different situations.

Gray Zone Warfare also features a unique health system that moves away from traditional hit points. Injuries are modeled in a more realistic way, with each wound affecting perception and performance. Players must identify injuries correctly and use the right treatment to survive and support teammates.

The game takes place in a persistent MMO open world spanning 42 km² of the Democratic Republic of Lamang, a fictional Southeast Asian country inspired by the landscapes of Laos. Players can travel through the world using explorable Landing Zones and helicopters. The world continues to exist even when the player is offline.

Its narrative is presented as a mature storyline delivered through numerous missions. Players meet a varied cast of characters, each with their own secrets and motives, and must decide who to trust while uncovering the truth behind a web of lies. The game frames its story around ambiguity rather than clear moral divisions.

The Standard Edition includes starting equipment to begin the journey. This consists of a basic locker and secure lockbox, in-game currency, and a range of weapons, gear, medical supplies, food, and ammunition. The page also notes that equipment is added after every game reset, that specific equipment types may change in future resets, and that editions can be upgraded by paying only the difference.
