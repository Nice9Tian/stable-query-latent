This title is a tactical first-person shooter set after a mysterious event on a fictional Southeast Asian island, where the area has been placed under international quarantine. With most civilians evacuated, three private military companies are sent by their hidden clients to investigate the region, uncover its secrets, and recover anything of value.

At its core, the game focuses on demanding tactical combat with realistic ballistic simulation based on precise real-world values and authentic ballistics models. Every decision carries weight, since each encounter can lead to the loss of equipment and progress. The weapon system is highly flexible, allowing players to build firearms that suit their preferred style by swapping, removing, or adding hundreds of parts, mounts, and accessories. These modifications can significantly change how each weapon handles and performs.

The game also features a distinctive health system that does not rely on traditional hit points. Instead, it simulates the body more realistically, with injuries affecting perception and performance in different ways. Learning to identify injuries and respond appropriately is an important part of survival, especially when supporting teammates in combat.

Exploration takes place in a large persistent open world covering 42 km² of a fictional Southeast Asian country inspired by Laos. Players can move through explorable landing zones and use helicopters to travel across the world, which continues to exist even when they are offline. The world is presented as a living MMO environment rather than a static mission space.

Narratively, the game offers a mature story told through numerous missions. Players meet a varied cast of characters, each with their own secrets and motives, and must navigate a web of deception while deciding whom to trust. The story emphasizes ambiguity and tension rather than clear-cut morality.

The available starting package includes a basic locker and secure storage, along with a substantial amount of initial gear. This bonus equipment provides weapons, ammunition, protective items, medical supplies, consumables, and in-game currency to help players begin their journey. The game also notes that equipment from editions is added after each game reset, that the specific contents may change in future resets, and that editions can be upgraded by paying only the difference.
