Black Skylands is a steampunk game that combines open-world exploration, sandbox elements, top-down shooting, and action-adventure gameplay. The story follows Eva, a young marshal who sets out to protect her people from the invading Falcons and Swarm while uncovering hidden mysteries in the dark clouds above Aspya.

Players command their own airship and travel across a world of floating islands and boundless skies. The game emphasizes both aerial and ground combat, with battles taking place in the skies and on land. Weapons and the ship itself can be upgraded through gathered resources, allowing players to increase their firepower and adapt their approach.

Airship choice is part of the experience, with options ranging from a maneuverable sloop to a spacious barge, or something in between depending on playstyle. Exploration extends across forests, farms, snowy landscapes, and other parts of the open world, while players work to liberate the floating islands, restore the land, and defend inhabitants against future threats.

In addition to the main storyline, Black Skylands includes raid missions that challenge players after completion of the campaign. These missions feature diverse enemies, large-scale combat encounters, and unique rewards for those who push their skills further.
