This title presents a steampunk-inspired blend of open-world exploration, sandbox building, top-down shooting, and action-adventure design. It follows the journey of a young marshal, here referred to as **Astra**, who fights to protect her people from invading factions known as the **Gales** and the **Mireborn**. The player takes command of a personal airship and travels across a vast world of open skies, where progression is driven by resource gathering, equipment crafting, ship upgrades, and the discovery of ancient secrets.

A core part of the experience is combat, which takes place both in the air and on the ground. Encounters support a range of upgradable weapons, allowing players to confront enemies during aerial battles as well as direct skirmishes on land. The airship system adds another layer of choice, with different vessel types offering distinct styles of movement and handling, from a nimble small craft to a larger, more spacious carrier-like ship.

Exploration is centered on a world made up of floating islands and varied environments such as forests, farms, and snowy regions. Players can move through these areas to collect materials used for stronger upgrades to both weapons and ship. The game also emphasizes defending island settlements and reclaiming territory from hostile forces, while uncovering hidden mysteries within dark cloud-covered regions of the sky.

After the main storyline, the experience continues with raid-style missions designed to test player skill against larger enemy groups and additional challenges. These missions offer unique rewards and extend the game’s combat-focused progression beyond the core campaign.
