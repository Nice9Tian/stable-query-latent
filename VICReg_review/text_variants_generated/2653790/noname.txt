This short walking simulator places the player in an endless underground passageway where careful observation is the core of progression. The objective is to reach a final destination known here as **“the Eighth Exit”** by paying close attention to the environment and reacting correctly to unusual changes. The central rule is simple: if an anomaly is detected, the player must immediately turn back; if nothing seems unusual, the player must continue forward without turning around.

The experience is designed around liminal spaces and a tense, repetitive corridor setting inspired by Japanese underground passages and backroom-like environments. Rather than relying on action or complex systems, it focuses on atmosphere, pattern recognition, and the player’s ability to notice subtle differences in the surroundings. The full playtime is short, typically lasting about 15 to 60 minutes.

The game supports Japanese and English, and it includes a small set of configurable options aimed at improving comfort and accessibility. These include graphics settings, mouse sensitivity, camera shake, camera acceleration, and motion blur. Players who are sensitive to motion or camera movement can reduce or disable effects such as camera shake and motion blur, while also adjusting sensitivity and acceleration for smoother control. A lower graphics preset is also recommended for systems with limited performance, particularly to help avoid discomfort.

Overall, this is a concise, atmospheric walking experience built around vigilance, environmental anomalies, and a single repeated decision: continue when everything seems normal, and turn back the moment something feels wrong.
