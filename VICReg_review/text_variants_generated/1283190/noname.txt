This title presents a slapstick stealth-comedy experience built around the enjoyment of witnessing an unfortunate rival’s escalating misfortunes. Framed as a fictional trash-TV production from the early 2000s, the player takes on the role of a mischievous lead whose goal is to stage increasingly outrageous pranks for the sake of entertainment and rising audience numbers.

The adventure unfolds across a series of episodes, beginning in the rival’s home and later expanding to a cruise liner and multiple holiday destinations around the world, including locations inspired by China, India, and Mexico. Across these settings, the player must create disorder and chaos while staying out of sight. The rival is not the only potential target of the pranks; his mother and various travel companions can also become involved in the chaos.

Gameplay centers on stealth, timing, and creative trap-setting. The player is accompanied by a camera crew that observes the action, reinforcing the show-like presentation. Success depends on causing as much disruption as possible without being caught. If the rival or his companions detect the player, the consequences are immediate and the production may be cancelled. If the player performs well, ratings rise and awards can be earned.

The package includes two remastered entries presented together as one release, with 25 episodes in total. It features newly rendered Full HD graphics, animations running at double frame rate for smoother movement, an easy-to-use interface, cartoon-style visuals, and a big-band soundtrack.
