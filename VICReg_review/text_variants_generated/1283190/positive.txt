**Neighbours back From Hell** is a sharp, mischievous slapstick game built around the playful idea of *schadenfreude*—taking delight in someone else’s misfortune. Inspired by early 2000s trash TV, it casts you as Woody, a vengeful prankster starring in a fictional hit show where the goal is to keep the audience entertained by escalating chaos against a grumpy neighbor.

The game combines both **Neighbours from Hell 1 and 2 Remastered** into a single package, offering **25 episodes** that take place in the neighbor’s apartment, aboard a cruise liner, and across holiday destinations around the world. As the pranks grow more outrageous, the action moves from the neighbor’s home to **China, India, and Mexico**, where even his vacations are turned into a nightmare. You can also target not just the neighbor, but his mother and fellow travelers, adding even more mayhem to the mix.

Gameplay centers on **stealth, timing, and creativity**. Your camera crew is always watching, and the better your pranks, the higher the ratings and the greater your chances of winning prestigious awards. But you must be careful: if the neighbor or his companions catch you in the act, they will retaliate and your show could be canceled. The result is a fun, strategic prank spree where smart mischief pays off and every successful trick pushes the chaos further.

This remastered release features **new Full HD graphics**, **doubled animation framerate** for smoother movement, an **easy-to-use interface and controls**, **superb cartoon-style visuals**, and a **big band soundtrack** that perfectly supports its comic, exaggerated style.
