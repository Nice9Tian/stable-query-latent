**FATAL FRAME / PROJECT ZERO: Maiden of Black Water** makes its Steam debut as a hauntingly atmospheric horror-adventure that celebrates the 20th anniversary of the FATAL FRAME / PROJECT ZERO series. Set on the eerie Mt. Hikami, a place once revered as sacred and now associated with grisly incidents and supernatural mysteries, the game weaves together the stories of three protagonists—Yuri Kozukata, Miu Hinasaki, and Ren Hojo—as they each venture into the mountain’s shadowed paths to uncover its secrets.

At the heart of the experience is the iconic **Camera Obscura**, a special camera used to repel vengeful ghosts and seal away their powers. As players advance through missions led by different protagonists, they rely on this tool alongside the **Shadow Reading** ability, which reveals lingering memories left behind by the missing. These traces guide the investigation deeper into the mountain, where malicious spirits await. Defeating them through the Camera Obscura not only provides thrilling encounters, but also unlocks the **Glance** ability, allowing players to witness the final moments of a spirit’s life. The camera is also useful for revealing hidden details invisible to the naked eye and recovering lost items, making exploration and discovery an essential part of the journey.

The game brings several appealing new elements, including **improved screen resolution**, **new outfits and accessories**, a **new Snap Mode** for creatively placing characters and spirits in custom shots, an **updated Ghost List**, and **updated controls**. After completing the main story, players can also unlock a special post-game mission featuring **Ayane** from the NINJA GAIDEN series. In this chapter, she brings a distinct playstyle of her own: unlike Yuri and Miu, she cannot use the Camera Obscura, but instead relies on her ninja skills, stealth, and nighttime proficiency to avoid ghosts and complete her mission.

For players seeking even more content, the **Digital Deluxe Edition** includes a **20th Anniversary Digital Artbook** with artwork and BGM tracks from past FATAL FRAME / PROJECT ZERO titles, as well as a **Past Protagonists Costume Set** featuring outfits inspired by beloved characters from across the series. Elegant, mysterious, and deeply rooted in the franchise’s legacy, *Maiden of Black Water* offers a compelling return to one of horror gaming’s most distinctive worlds.
