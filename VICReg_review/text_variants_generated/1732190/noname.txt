This atmospheric horror-adventure follows three protagonists as they explore a cursed mountain steeped in old beliefs, water worship, and a long history of strange and tragic incidents. The setting is a secluded sacred site where many people have vanished or died, and the narrative gradually reveals the hidden connections between these events through separate mission-based chapters led by different characters.

At the center of the experience is a special device known as the **Astra Camera**, a supernatural camera used to repel hostile spirits and seal away their power. Players must investigate the mountain, search for clues, and confront malicious entities that lurk along hidden paths. A key mechanic called **Echo Sight** allows the protagonists to detect lingering traces of memories left behind by missing people, guiding them deeper into the mountain’s secrets. The camera is not only used for combat; it can also reveal hidden objects and aid in recovering lost items, making it an essential tool for both exploration and progression.

Combat and investigation are closely tied together. When spirits appear, the camera becomes the main means of defense, and once an enemy is defeated, a special **Afterimage View** ability can be used to witness the final moments of its existence. This reinforces the game’s eerie tone while also expanding the story through environmental discovery and supernatural clues.

The edition described here includes several bonus features. It offers a digital art archive with artwork and music from earlier entries in the series, along with a set of themed costumes inspired by previous protagonists. The game also adds improved visual resolution, new outfits and accessories, an expanded ghost compendium, updated controls, and a creative **Snapshot Mode** that lets players freely position characters and spirits for customized images.

After completing the main story, players can unlock an additional mission featuring a stealth-focused side character from another series. In this chapter, the character cannot use the Astra Camera and instead relies on ninja skills, concealment, and movement in the shadows to avoid spirits and complete the objective. This bonus scenario provides a different style of play and adds replay value beyond the main campaign.

Overall, the game blends exploration, mystery, and supernatural combat into a structured horror experience centered on uncovering the secrets of a haunted mountain.
