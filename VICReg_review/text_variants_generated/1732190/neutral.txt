FATAL FRAME / PROJECT ZERO: Maiden of Black Water is a horror-adventure game that comes to Steam for the first time to mark the 20th anniversary of the FATAL FRAME / PROJECT ZERO series. The Digital Deluxe Edition includes a 20th Anniversary Digital Artbook with artwork and BGM tracks from past titles, as well as a Past Protagonists Costume Set featuring outfits inspired by characters from earlier games in the series.

The story is set on Mt. Hikami, a mountain once revered as a spiritual place and associated with a religion centered on worshipping water as a deity. The mountain is said to have been the site of many gruesome incidents and mysterious phenomena. The game follows three protagonists—Yuri Kozukata, Miu Hinasaki, and Ren Hojo—as they each explore the ominous mountain and uncover the secrets hidden there.

Gameplay centers on the Camera Obscura, a special camera used to repel vengeful ghosts and seal away their powers. The story is divided into separate missions, each led by a different protagonist. The characters also possess Shadow Reading, an ability that lets them sense the remnants of memories left behind by people who disappeared on the mountain. These traces guide players deeper along secret paths, where malicious spirits await. To fight them, players must capture them with the Camera Obscura. After defeating a spirit, the Glance ability can be used to view the final moments of that spirit’s life. The Camera Obscura is not only used in combat, but also to reveal things hidden from the human eye and assist in recovering lost items, helping players gradually unravel the mountain’s mysteries.

The game includes several new elements, such as improved screen resolution, new outfits and accessories, a new Snap Mode that allows players to freely place characters and spirits for creative shots, an updated Ghost List, and updated controls.

After completing the main story, special missions become available in which players can control Ayane from the NINJA GAIDEN series. The Ayane Chapter unlocks after all sections from “First Drop” to “Last Drop” have been completed. Unlike Yuri and Miu, Ayane cannot use the Camera Obscura. Instead, she relies on her ninja abilities, stealth, and the ability to hide from view and avoid ghosts in order to complete her mission, offering a different style of play in this bonus chapter.
