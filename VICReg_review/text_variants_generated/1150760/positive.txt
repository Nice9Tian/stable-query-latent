*Gloomwood* is a stealth horror FPS that pulls you into the mystery of your own abduction and drops you into a forgotten, twisted Victorian metropolis undergoing a horrifying transformation. With its fog-soaked streets, haunted tunnels, and decadent mansions, the game delivers a richly gothic immersive sim atmosphere that is both eerie and compelling.

At the heart of *Gloomwood* is freeform exploration through an intricate, hand-crafted city. From towering rooftops to hidden passages, the routes you take and paths you create are entirely up to you. The game encourages careful movement and observation, supported by a detailed stealth and sound system that tracks your visibility in the light and carries noise realistically through the world. Staying unseen and unheard is essential, though the game also gives you the tools to make a louder stand when needed.

Your arsenal is varied and well-suited to different situations. You can fight with a stealthy canesword, a six-shot revolver, a folding shotgun, hand-placed traps, a rope-slinging harpoon rifle, and more. The game also emphasizes player interaction and environmental awareness, letting you lean to eavesdrop, peek through door cracks, check the bullets in your firearms, and mantle onto ledges with ease.

The city is populated by ghastly denizens and monsters with sharp senses, including the clawed Crowmen and the terrifying Corpse Duster. Beyond combat and stealth, *Gloomwood* invites you to manipulate the world around you, with objects you can pick up, throw, climb, smash, and use to your advantage. It is a grim, atmospheric journey full of mystery, tension, and discovery, with far more hidden within the city than first appears.
