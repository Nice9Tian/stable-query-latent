Gloomwood is a stealth horror first-person shooter set in a forgotten Victorian metropolis undergoing a horrifying transformation. The game follows a mysterious abduction and places the player in a fog-shrouded city filled with haunted streets, dark tunnels, and decadent mansions. Its atmosphere is described as gothic and immersive, with the player uncovering an accursed mystery hidden within the fog.

The game emphasizes freeform exploration in an intricately hand-crafted city. Players can move through multiple routes and create their own paths, from towering rooftops to hidden passages. Gloomwood also features a detailed stealth and sound system that tracks light visibility and accurately propagates noise through the world, encouraging careful movement and attention to detection. Players are urged to tread lightly, though they can also choose to become more aggressive when needed.

Combat and survival are supported by a varied arsenal, including a stealth-focused canesword, a six-shot revolver, a folding shotgun, hand-placed traps, and a rope-slinging harpoon rifle. The game also includes deep player interactivity, such as leaning to eavesdrop, peeking through door cracks, checking bullets in firearms, and mantling onto ledges. The city is populated by dangerous denizens and monsters with acute senses, including the sharp-clawed Crowmen and the corpse-seeking Corpse Duster.

In addition, Gloomwood allows players to pick up, throw, climb, smash, and use objects throughout the environment to their advantage. Presented by the producers of DUSK, AMID EVIL, ULTRAKILL, and FAITH, the game combines stealth, horror, exploration, and immersive sim mechanics in a twisted gothic setting.
