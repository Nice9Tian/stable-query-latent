Mr. Prepper is a survival crafting game centered on preparation, secrecy, and resistance under an authoritarian government. The player takes on the role of Mr. Prepper, a man living in a country that has changed from a land of freedom into one ruled by a controlling regime. With the threat of nuclear war in the air and the government’s secretive Agency watching closely, he must quietly work toward escape while avoiding attention.

The core of the game is building an underground shelter and turning it into a functional base. Players craft machines and facilities to help Mr. Prepper survive, including farms, greenhouses, tools, and vehicles. Progress depends on gathering the right materials, managing time, and keeping plans hidden from the authorities.

Exploration and scavenging are important parts of the experience. Players visit atmospheric locations to find materials and useful items, while also trading with neighbors to obtain what they need and provide what others want. The game also includes quests from other rebellious characters, creating opportunities to build relationships and support the opposition.

Mr. Prepper’s goal is not just survival, but action. He must make a plan, complete the work needed for it, and outsmart the secret police, who want him controlled rather than prepared. The game focuses on defying the regime, helping others in resistance, and breaking the status quo through careful crafting, trading, exploration, and quest completion.
