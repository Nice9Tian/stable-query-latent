The Sinking City is a compelling adventure and investigation game set in a vast open world inspired by the dark, haunting universe of H.P. Lovecraft, the master of horror. In the half-submerged city of Oakmont, supernatural forces have taken hold, creating an oppressive atmosphere that perfectly supports its eerie story and mysterious tone.  

Players step into the role of a private investigator tasked with uncovering the truth behind the city’s strange possession and the corruption spreading through the minds of its inhabitants. The game encourages exploration across its richly imagined world, which can be traversed on foot, by boat, or even in a diving suit, adding variety and depth to the investigation.  

One of the game’s most impressive features is its open investigation system, which offers high replay value. Each case can be solved in multiple ways, and the choices made throughout the journey can lead to different endings, making every decision feel meaningful. To survive the horrors lurking in Oakmont, players also have access to an arsenal of 1920s weapons for confronting nightmarish creatures. At the same time, managing mental health becomes an important part of the experience, as preserving sanity is key to untangling the truth behind the madness.
