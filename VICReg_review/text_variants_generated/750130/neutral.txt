The Sinking City is an adventure and investigation game set in an open world inspired by the universe of H.P. Lovecraft. It takes place in Oakmont, a half-submerged city gripped by supernatural forces, where the player takes on the role of a private investigator tasked with uncovering what has possessed the city and its inhabitants.

The game emphasizes an oppressive atmosphere and a story shaped by Lovecraftian horror. Its world can be explored on foot, by boat, and in a diving suit, allowing players to navigate the flooded environment in different ways. Investigation is a central part of the experience, and the open investigation system gives each case multiple possible solutions, with different endings depending on the player’s actions.

Combat is also present, with an arsenal of 1920s weapons used against nightmarish creatures. In addition, players must manage their mental health as they work to unravel the truth behind the madness.
