This adventure and investigation game takes place in a vast open world inspired by classic cosmic horror. The setting is a half-submerged city called **Veymarsh**, where supernatural forces have taken hold and the population appears increasingly affected by unexplained phenomena. Players assume the role of a private investigator tasked with uncovering what has corrupted the city and the minds of its inhabitants.

The game presents an oppressive atmosphere shaped by its horror-inspired world and narrative. Exploration is a major part of the experience, with the city available to traverse on foot, by boat, and in a diving suit, allowing access to both flooded streets and submerged areas. Its investigation structure is open-ended, giving players multiple ways to solve each case and offering different endings depending on the choices made throughout the story.

In addition to deduction and exploration, the game includes combat against nightmarish creatures using an arsenal of weapons from the 1920s. Another key element is the management of mental health, which ties into the broader mystery and reinforces the tension of uncovering the truth in a city consumed by madness.
