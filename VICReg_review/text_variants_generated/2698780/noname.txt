This open-world simulator begins with a modest rise from near-poverty in a port town, where the player takes on local jobs to earn an initial income. From there, the experience centers on building and managing a small company of up to four people, with work divided through role authorization and team coordination.

A major part of the game focuses on property ownership and construction. Players can buy land, design a house, build it, and decorate it with personal touches. Once completed, the property can even be sold at auction, making land ownership and creative control an important part of progression.

The game also supports co-op play, allowing friends to manage the entire process together. One player acts as the executive leader and controls authorizations for the group, affecting actions such as purchasing land or taking bank loans. This makes communication and coordination an important element of the multiplayer experience.

Beyond construction, the game includes a range of practical and physical tasks. Players can demolish large houses with a sledgehammer or clean very large homes and mansions. There is also a logistics system in which cargo deliveries provide another way to earn money, with different cargo types requiring attention during transport.

Progression is driven by skill points earned through experience. These points can be invested into different skills or tools, encouraging players to specialize and allowing coworkers to complement one another through separate areas of expertise. The entire business structure is contract-based, with assignments that may involve decoration, demolition, construction, logistics, or combinations of these job types.

Overall, the game combines company management, property building, co-op coordination, task-based work, and character progression in a sandbox-style open world centered on contracts and growing from a very small start into a larger operation.
