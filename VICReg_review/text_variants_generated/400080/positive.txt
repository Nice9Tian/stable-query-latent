**Out of Space** is a charming local and online co-op multiplayer game that turns the challenge of shared living into a clever spacefaring adventure. Set inside a house in space, it asks you and your friends to settle into a procedurally generated spaceship home, survive a deadly alien infestation, and work together to transform chaos into a clean, cozy, sustainable place to live.

At its heart, the game blends **couch-fun accessibility** with **strategy-driven decision making**. Matches are short and easy to jump into, making it simple for anyone to grab a controller and join in. At the same time, success depends on smart planning, teamwork, and balancing both immediate needs and long-term goals. Players must generate resources, recycle trash and alien goo, purchase and place new technologies, and keep every system running efficiently.

The spaceship home is more than just a battlefield against infestation—it is a living environment that must be maintained. You will power up your ship by recycling alien goo and producing energy batteries, harvest and care for productive gardens to keep everyone fed, and clean rooms using mops and water while containing the alien threat. As the ship expands, you can conquer new rooms, automate tasks with robots, and build technologies that improve sustainability and efficiency. Even rest has its place, with the option to take a nap on the sofa while your friends scramble for help.

Out of Space also adds delightful layers of variety and progression. Its **procedural spaceship generator** ensures that every run feels different, while missions guide player progression without losing the game’s easygoing multiplayer spirit. New content can be unlocked through achievements, giving players more to discover as they improve. You can play **single-player, local multiplayer, or online multiplayer**, and the game fully embraces its friendly cooperative identity.

One of the most distinctive aspects of Out of Space is its commitment to non-violent problem solving: **there are no weapons of any kind**. Instead, you must rely on teamwork, cleaning, logistics, and careful coordination to survive. The game even adds lovable touches such as adopting dogs, who will appreciate your care and help however they can.

Developed by **Behold Studios**, the team behind *Knights of Pen & Paper*, *Chroma Squad*, and *Galaxy of Pen & Paper*, Out of Space is being shaped with strong community involvement, with openly shared builds, milestones, and feedback-driven improvements. It is a thoughtful, playful, and highly collaborative experience that captures the joys and chaos of living together—only this time, among the stars.
