Set in a post-apocalyptic wasteland, this western-inspired motorvania follows a tribe living under occupation and centers on the personal journey of a mother coyote warrior driven by vengeance to reclaim what her people have lost. The experience takes place in a large, hand-drawn world and combines exploration, traversal, and combat around a fast and agile motorbike.

Players drive, jump, and fight across the wasteland, racing through its harsh terrain and attempting dangerous jumps. Combat includes shooting enemies in slow motion, and reloading is tied to performing a backflip. The game also features skill-based power-ups and difficult battles against large bosses. Its structure includes a varied world, many missions, and vehicular combat as a core mechanic.

Visually, it presents a beautiful hand-painted post-apocalyptic setting, while its narrative emphasizes themes of vengeance, loss, and the relationship between a mother and daughter.
