Laika: Aged Through Blood is a western-inspired motorvania set in a post-apocalyptic wasteland. It follows a tribe living under oppressive occupying forces and centers on the story of a mother coyote warrior who embarks on a path of vengeance to reclaim what her people have lost.

The game puts players on a fast and agile motorbike, traveling through a large hand-drawn world. Its gameplay combines driving, jumping, and combat, with dangerous jumps, slow-motion shooting, and a reload mechanic performed by doing a backflip. It also features skill-based power-ups, challenging battles, and boss encounters.

Beyond its action-focused mechanics, the game presents a hand-painted post-apocalyptic setting and includes a story about motherhood, loss, and revenge. The world is described as varied, with many missions and epic boss fights. Laika: Aged Through Blood is presented as the first “motorvania,” blending vehicular combat with exploration and progression through its wasteland environment.
