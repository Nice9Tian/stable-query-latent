Bodycam is an ultra-realistic multiplayer first-person shooter developed using Unreal Engine 5. Created by two French game enthusiasts aged 17 and 20, it is presented as a project focused on pushing realism and photorealism in the genre. The game emphasizes next-generation visuals, immersive sound effects, and a shooting and movement system designed to feel revolutionary.

In Bodycam, players enter intense combat phases with teammates and rely on strategy, equipment, cooperation, and communication to overcome opponents. The game features ultra-detailed environments intended to support immersion and tactical play. It includes classic modes such as Deathmatch and Team Deathmatch, as well as a new Body Bomb mode designed for competition.

The game is planned as an early access release with ongoing updates. Its 2024 roadmap mentions community-requested features, with players able to submit suggestions and vote on ideas through Discord. The developers note that the roadmap may change over time as development progresses and recruitment expands the team. Bodycam is also expected to receive regular correction and content patches based on community feedback after its beta release.
