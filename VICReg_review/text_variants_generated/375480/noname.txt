This action role-playing adventure is set in a world where the final evil has already been defeated, the last quest has been completed, and the last treasure chest has been opened. In that age of legend, the deeds of forgotten champions are preserved through a powerful magical relic called the **Veylchron**, a device that allows selected participants to enter the memories, and even the souls, of ancient heroes. Each year, a few chosen individuals are granted the chance to relive those stories, and the player takes on that role.

The game is structured around **five large and varied acts**, each with its own storyline. It features **four distinct classes**: the **Aurel**, **Ravager**, **Sentinel**, and **Hexer**. Progress through the game takes place in **procedurally generated dungeons and areas**, ensuring that each playthrough offers different layouts and challenges. The experience also supports **local co-op for up to four players** on the same machine, along with **Remote Play Together** support.

Character growth and customization are central to the design. The game includes **over 400 unique items** with specially designed powers, as well as **more than 700 additional items** with randomized enchantments, qualities, build-altering effects, and powerful set bonuses. Players can also learn and enhance **over 900 skills, abilities, and perks**, creating a wide range of possible builds. Beyond that, character advancement continues indefinitely through **endless Mastery ranks**.

Several systems deepen equipment customization. Players can engage in **crafting** that allows them to **enchant, gem, empower, and inscribe gear with runes**. For those seeking a tougher challenge, there is a **Hardcore mode**. Endgame content expands the experience further with **randomized dungeons, bosses, and endgame-only crafting mechanics**.

The game is presented as a character-building, loot-driven adventure with a strong emphasis on replayability, cooperative play, and long-term progression. It also includes **full controller support**, with optional use of an **Xbox-compatible controller**.
