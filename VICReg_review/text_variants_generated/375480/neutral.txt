*Chronicon* is an action RPG set in a world where the last great evil has already been defeated and the last quests have already been completed. Its story centers on the Chronicon, a magical device that allows chosen heroes to enter the memories, and souls, of the legendary Heroes of Old. Each year, a few are selected to relive those ancient tales, and this time you are the one chosen.

The game features five large and varied acts, each with its own storyline, and four distinct classes: Templar, Berserker, Warden, and Warlock. Its areas and dungeons are procedurally generated, so each playthrough differs. *Chronicon* also supports local co-op for up to four players on the same machine, with Remote Play Together fully supported, and it includes full controller support.

Progression is built around a large amount of loot and character customization. The game offers more than 400 unique items with special powers, over 700 items with randomized enchants and qualities, build-changing powers, and set items, plus more than 900 skills, abilities, and perks to learn and improve. It also includes infinite character progression through endless Mastery ranks, along with Hardcore mode for players who want a harsher challenge.

Crafting is a major part of the experience as well. Equipment can be enchanted, gemmed, empowered, and inscribed with runes. Endgame content includes randomized dungeons, bosses, and crafting mechanics available only at that stage of play.

The store page also notes that the trailer footage and screenshots show high-level characters with decent equipment, and that the early game is expected to move at a slower pace.
