Growing Up is a coming-of-age life simulation game in which players shape a character’s life from toddlerhood to adulthood, and then continue the cycle through the next generation. The game emphasizes choice-driven progression, allowing players to decide what their character learns, who they befriend, how they spend their time, and whether they pursue romance. As the character grows, they develop skills, manage a daily schedule, explore the city, participate in activities, and navigate school life. Upon reaching adulthood, the character’s future career and romantic partner are revealed, and the player can then raise a child of their own, beginning a new life path.

The game is set in a handcrafted 1990s-inspired world with more than 30 locations to explore. Its setting reflects the era’s pop culture and social atmosphere, with places such as a cinema, a street shop, and a city filled with nostalgic details. The store page highlights this environment as a central part of the experience, framing the city as a space where players can take their time and discover different activities and interactions.

Growing Up is designed around replayability and branching outcomes. Each playthrough can differ depending on the character created and the choices made, leading to varied challenges, relationships, and story developments. The narrative contains more than 1000 lines of dialogue for each character and includes multiple endings for both the player and their friends. According to the page, decisions made throughout the game influence the full journey of the character.

Relationships play an important role in the experience. The game features 19 characters whose stories unfold alongside the player’s own. Players can form relationships with them in different ways, whether romantic or platonic, and these connections are presented as part of the broader path to adulthood.

The game also includes several systems intended to support character development. Players can use a handcrafted mini-game to build mind and aptitude, manage daily activities through scheduling, take on challenging exams, and participate in extracurricular activities. In addition, Growing Up features an in-depth skill system with more than 200 skills to learn and master, which can lead to one of 42 unique careers. These careers range from astronaut and CEO of a gaming company to famous actress and even President of the United States of America.

Customization is another element of the game. Players can change their character’s appearance using clothing and hairstyles, and the appearance of the character’s room can also change depending on the skills learned. The page also notes that different choices can unlock new locations, characters, and certain features for future offspring.

Overall, Growing Up presents itself as a narrative-driven life simulation about growing up, forming relationships, making choices, and shaping a family legacy across generations.
