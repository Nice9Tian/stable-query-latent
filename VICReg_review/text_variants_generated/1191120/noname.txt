This life simulation game presents a full coming-of-age journey shaped by player choice, beginning in childhood and continuing into adulthood and parenthood. Players guide a character through early development, deciding what they learn, who they befriend, and how they spend their time. The experience includes skill growth, daily schedule management, city exploration, social relationships, and school life, with the character’s future career and romantic path eventually revealed in adulthood. The cycle then continues through the player’s child, creating a generational structure with new possibilities each time.

Set in a richly crafted 1990s-inspired world, the game features more than 30 locations to explore, including places such as a cinema and street shops. The setting emphasizes the atmosphere of an era marked by social change and pop culture, with a nostalgic city environment that remains open for leisurely exploration. The game is designed to produce different outcomes across multiple playthroughs, since each created character can encounter different challenges, people, and narrative paths.

Choice plays a central role in the structure of the story. Dialogue is extensive, with more than 1000 lines for each character, and decisions can lead to multiple endings for both the main character and their friends. The narrative branches according to the player’s actions, making each run feel distinct. The game also includes 19 supporting characters whose stories unfold alongside the player’s own, allowing relationships to develop in romantic or platonic directions.

Gameplay combines several systems. Players can train the mind through a handcrafted mini-game, organize daily activities, take exams to enter the best schools, and participate in extracurricular activities. A large skill system offers more than 200 abilities to learn and improve, which in turn shapes future opportunities. These choices can unlock different locations, characters, and features related to a future child. The result is a wide range of possible careers, totaling 42 unique paths, from high-level corporate roles to public office and entertainment professions.

Customization is also a significant part of the experience. Players can alter their character’s appearance with various clothing items and hairstyles, and the look of the character’s room changes according to learned skills. Overall, the game combines life simulation, branching narrative design, social interaction, and long-term progression into a structured but highly variable experience focused on shaping a personal life story.
