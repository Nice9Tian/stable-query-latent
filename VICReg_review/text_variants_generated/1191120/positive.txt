**Growing Up** is a heartfelt coming-of-age life simulator that puts your entire future in your hands. From toddlerhood to adulthood, the game invites you to shape a character’s childhood, relationships, education, and ultimately their legacy through a deeply branching journey filled with meaningful choices. Every decision matters, influencing what you learn, who you befriend, how you grow, and which path your life will take.

Set against a lovingly crafted 1990s backdrop, the game captures the charm and atmosphere of a nostalgic era of pop culture and social change. With more than 30 locations to explore, the city feels vibrant and alive, whether you are visiting the cinema, browsing street shops, or simply taking in the scenery. The hand-crafted environments create a warm, immersive setting that enhances the feeling of living through an unforgettable decade.

One of the game’s greatest strengths is its variety. No two playthroughs are the same, because each life brings different challenges, characters, and outcomes. The branching narrative features more than 1000 lines of dialogue for each character, along with multiple endings shaped by your choices. Your actions echo throughout the journey, making every run feel personal and impactful.

Friendship and romance are central to the experience. Growing Up features 19 characters whose stories unfold alongside your own, allowing you to build relationships that are either romantic or purely platonic. These bonds add emotional depth to the journey and reinforce the idea that growing up is never something done alone.

The game also offers a rich set of systems to keep each day engaging. You can manage your schedule, participate in extracurricular activities, improve your mind through handcrafted mini-games, and face challenging school exams. With more than 200 skills to learn and master, there is impressive freedom in shaping your character’s abilities and future. That flexibility leads to one of 42 unique careers, ranging from Astronaut to CEO of a gaming company, famous actress, or even President of the United States.

Customization adds another rewarding layer. Players can personalize their character with clothing and hairstyles, while the appearance of the room changes based on the skills learned. Choices can also unlock new locations, characters, and features for a future child, extending the sense of progression across generations.

With its rich storytelling, nostalgic setting, dynamic systems, and impressive range of life paths, Growing Up delivers a thoughtful and beautifully realized journey through childhood, adulthood, and parenthood. It stands out as a creative and charming experience that celebrates the many possibilities of life.
