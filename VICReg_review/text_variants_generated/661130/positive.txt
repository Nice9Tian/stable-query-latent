ChilloutVR is a community-driven VR and desktop sandbox game that invites players and creators to explore and shape a vast metaverse. Designed to be playable in both desktop and VR environments, it focuses on giving users a secure and performant base for social play, world creation, and avatar customization. With an AlphaLink account required to play, the game opens the door to a persistent online space where imagination and social connection come together.

One of ChilloutVR’s standout strengths is its emphasis on freedom and expression. Players can create their own avatars or use existing ones, then step into the world as fully expressive characters. Custom avatars can support full-body tracking, lip sync, and eye-tracking, while full-body tracking itself remains optional. VR is also not required, making the experience accessible to a wider audience. Whether you want to craft a unique identity or simply explore with an avatar you enjoy, the game provides plenty of room for personalization.

The world-building side of ChilloutVR is equally ambitious. Players and creators can build their own immersive worlds, complete with interactable objects, and share them with others. The game includes a growing collection of publicly available worlds that can be explored alone, with friends, or alongside new people met along the way. With just a click, players can open new worlds and move through the metaverse with ease.

ChilloutVR also shines as a social experience. You can add friends, join their instances, and use portals to bring them along as you move across different spaces. Public instances offer opportunities to meet new people, while groups provide a way to connect with communities and spend time with like-minded players. Events, talks, and group gatherings add another layer of life to the metaverse, making it easy to participate in shared experiences and create your own.

The game’s feature set further supports its expansive vision. It includes 3D spatialized audio, multi-regional instancing for EU, US, and AS, support for user-created avatars and worlds, and the ability to meet friends around the world. It is built with modularity in mind, and while its in-house scripting toolkit “Newton” and advanced scripted game modes are not yet available, the foundation is clearly designed for future creativity. Potential experiences mentioned include game modes such as Trouble in Terrorist Town, co-op tower defense, and MMORPG-style worlds.

Overall, ChilloutVR presents a welcoming and flexible platform for social exploration, creative building, and avatar-driven self-expression. By combining desktop and VR support, community-created content, and a strong emphasis on connection, it offers an appealing metaverse space with plenty of room to grow.
