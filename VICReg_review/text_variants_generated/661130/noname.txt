This community-driven virtual reality and desktop sandbox is designed as a flexible metaverse where players and creators can explore, socialize, and build. It supports both desktop and VR play, offers immersive 3D spatialized audio, and includes multi-regional instancing across Europe, the United States, and Asia. The game also supports full-body tracking, as well as user-created avatars and worlds.

A central focus of the experience is personal expression and exploration. Players can create their own avatar or use existing ones, then move through a large and growing collection of public worlds, either alone or with friends. New worlds can be opened with a single click, and users may also create their own spaces to share with others. The game emphasizes a secure and performant foundation for both players and creators, with an account requirement that must be completed after launching the game.

Avatar creation is a major feature, allowing fully expressive, full-body tracked characters with lip sync and eye tracking. Full-body tracking and VR are both optional, making the game accessible in desktop form as well. World creation is similarly open-ended, with support for fully immersive environments that include interactable objects.

The game is also built with modularity in mind. Although its in-house scripting toolkit is not yet available, the planned system is intended to let creators build interactive worlds and full game modes. Examples mentioned include social deduction experiences, cooperative tower defense, and large-scale role-playing worlds, with the main limits being imagination and player hardware performance.

Social play is deeply integrated. Players can add friends, join them across instances, drop portals to guide others through the metaverse, and enter public instances to meet new people. Group features allow users to connect with communities or spend time with specific circles of friends. Events, talks, and community gatherings are supported as part of the broader online experience, making the world feel active and socially driven.
