The Quarry is a cinematic teen horror game set on the last night of summer camp at Hackett’s Quarry, where the teenage counselors celebrate with a party that quickly spirals into terror. With no kids, no adults, and no rules, the night descends into a struggle for survival as the counselors are hunted by blood-drenched locals and something even more sinister. Players control all nine camp counselors across a branching story in which relationships, survival, and fate are shaped by every decision.

The game is built around choice-driven horror. Players face situations such as investigating strange noises, checking trap doors, or deciding whether to help friends or run for their lives. These decisions influence who survives, who dies, and how the story unfolds. The narrative is presented as a cinematic experience with facial capture, filmic lighting, and performances from a cast of Hollywood talent.

The Quarry also includes several ways to experience the story. In online multiplayer, up to seven friends can watch and vote on key decisions, creating a shared version of the story. A couch co-op option lets each player take control of a counselor and manage their actions. For players who prefer a more passive experience, Movie Mode turns the game into a selectable cinematic thriller. Adjustable difficulty settings are available for gameplay elements, making the experience accessible to different skill levels.

The Deluxe Edition includes the full game plus bonus content: ‘80s-themed character outfits, instant access to the Death Rewind system, the Gorefest Movie Mode option, and the Horror History Visual Filter Pack. The visual filter pack offers three cinematic styles: an 8mm-style film grain filter for Indie Horror, a retro VHS look for ’80s Horror, and a black-and-white Classic Horror filter.

A free trial version is also available, letting players experience the prologue chapter. Trial players can be invited to online multiplayer by full-game owners through Wolf Pack Mode, where they can vote on key decisions, and story progress carries over if they later purchase the full game on the same Steam account.
