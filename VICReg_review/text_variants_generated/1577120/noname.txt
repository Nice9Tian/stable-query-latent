This interactive horror title takes place on the final night of summer camp, when a group of teenage counselors at a remote camp celebrate the start of their freedom with a party that quickly spirals into terror. With no children, no adults, and no rules to keep them in check, the night turns dangerous as the counselors are hunted by violent locals and another, more sinister threat. What begins as friendly banter and flirtation becomes a tense struggle for survival, with relationships shifting under pressure and every decision carrying serious consequences.

The game follows nine playable counselors through a cinematic branching story in which choices shape the outcome. Players can switch between different characters, make life-or-death decisions, and determine who survives until morning. Any counselor can become central to the story, or die before dawn, and the narrative is built around a web of possible paths based on player action.

The presentation is designed to feel like an interactive horror film, using facial capture, cinematic lighting, and a large ensemble cast to create a movie-like experience. The game also offers multiple ways to play with others. In online multiplayer, up to seven invited players can watch, vote on major decisions, and help shape the story together. A local party-style mode lets each player control a counselor and share the experience. For players who prefer not to directly control the action, a movie-style mode lets them watch the story unfold as a horror feature.

A free trial version includes the prologue chapter and can be played with friends through the online group mode if they own the full version. Story progress from the trial carries over if the full game is later purchased on the same account.

The title also includes extensive customization options. Adjustable difficulty settings are available for gameplay elements, and there are three visual filter styles that change the look of the experience: an 8mm-inspired grainy horror style, a retro VHS-style horror look, and a black-and-white classic horror filter. The deluxe edition adds extra content such as throwback character outfits, immediate access to a death rewind system, an exaggerated gore-focused movie mode option, and the visual filter pack.
