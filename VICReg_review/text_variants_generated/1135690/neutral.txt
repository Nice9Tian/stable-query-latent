Unpacking is a zen game centered on the familiar experience of taking possessions out of boxes and placing them into a new home. Combining block-fitting puzzle elements with home decoration, it invites players to build a satisfying living space while gradually uncovering clues about the life of the person whose belongings are being unpacked.

Across eight house moves, the game offers a quiet, intimate experience focused on environmental storytelling. The character is never seen, and the story is never directly told, but it emerges through the objects that arrive at each new home and the items that are left behind.

The gameplay is meditative and has no timers, meters, or scores. Players unpack spaces ranging from a single bedroom to an entire house, exploring domestic environments and arranging everyday items such as plates, towels, and bookshelves. The game also features a soundtrack by BAFTA award-winning composer and audio director Jeff van Dyck.
