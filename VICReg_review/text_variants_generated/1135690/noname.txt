This title is a calm, reflective puzzle experience centered on the familiar act of moving into a new home and arranging personal belongings. It combines block-fitting gameplay with home decoration, asking players to unpack items from boxes and place them into living spaces in a way that feels orderly and satisfying. As each room is prepared, the game gradually reveals details about the unseen life of a character through the objects that travel with them from one residence to the next.

Across eight house moves, players progress from a single bedroom to an entire home, shaping domestic spaces filled with small details and everyday items. The gameplay is meditative and deliberately free of timers, meters, or scores, allowing players to explore each environment at their own pace. Objects such as plates, towels, and books can be stacked, hung, and arranged throughout spaces with many corners and surfaces to discover.

The experience is designed to create a sense of intimacy through belongings rather than direct storytelling, with some items carried forward and others left behind. A soundtrack by a BAFTA-winning composer and audio director supports the atmosphere, reinforcing the game’s quiet, thoughtful tone.
