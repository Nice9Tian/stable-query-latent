**Star Wars: Empire at War Collection** delivers a powerful and authentic strategy experience set in the Star Wars galaxy, bringing together galactic warfare, planet-scale conquest, and underworld domination in one definitive package.

In **Star Wars: Empire at War**, players become the supreme commander of the Galactic Civil War, choosing to lead either the **Rebellion** or the **Empire**. The game lets you command everything from individual troops to starships and even the **Death Star**, fighting campaigns across the ground, in space, and throughout the galaxy. Instead of relying on tedious resource gathering, it drops you directly into the action, where every decision shapes the next battle and every battle influences the fate of the galaxy. Players can conquer or liberate more than **80 ground and space locations**, including **Kashyyyk, Tatooine, and Dagobah**, each offering unique strategic value. Iconic characters such as **Obi-Wan Kenobi, Luke Skywalker, Darth Vader, and Boba Fett** can be commanded in battle, while classic clashes like **X-wings versus TIE fighters** and massive **Star Destroyer fleets** capture the scale and excitement of Star Wars warfare.

The expansion, **Star Wars: Empire at War: Forces of Corruption**, adds a compelling new perspective by letting players embrace the **corrupt side** of the conflict. As **Tyber Zann**, you use ruthless tactics such as **piracy, kidnapping, racketeering, and bribery** to rise as the most notorious criminal leader since Jabba the Hutt. This expansion expands the experience with **over 28 new underworld units**, along with new Rebel and Imperial forces and heroes, across **13 new planets**. It also introduces expanded multiplayer with **three-faction battles** and the option to play the same side against itself through **mirror play**.

Altogether, this collection offers a rich and thrilling strategy experience that captures the scale, variety, and legendary characters of Star Wars while giving players multiple paths to dominate the galaxy.
