This strategy collection presents a large-scale interstellar conflict in which players can command a galactic faction and shape the outcome of a civil war across land, space, and entire systems. The core experience focuses on directing forces at every level, from small squads and individual troops to capital ships and orbital superweapons. Rather than relying on traditional resource gathering, the game moves quickly into combat and strategic decision-making, emphasizing continuous action and the impact of each choice on future battles.

A major part of the campaign is territorial control across more than eighty locations spread across ground and space. Each area offers its own strategic value, encouraging players to choose when to conquer, defend, or liberate key worlds. Battles are not limited to one front: players can fight in space with fighter craft, heavy fleets, and massive war machines, then send ground forces down to secure the planet below. The structure also allows decisions in one battle to influence the larger galactic situation, giving the campaign a dynamic, evolving feel.

The game includes a roster of recognizable heroic and villainous leaders, each able to participate in the conflict as command figures. Players can deploy classic starfighters against enemy interceptors, field large fleets of destroyer-class capital ships, and even use an enormous planet-destroying weapon as part of their strategic options. The setting supports a full reconquest of the galaxy through military force, with campaigns that span multiple environments and strategic layers.

A second expansion-style portion shifts the focus to criminal power in the galaxy’s hidden networks. Here, the player takes control of a shadowy syndicate leader seeking to rise as the most feared figure in the underworld. This part introduces illegal and subversive tactics such as piracy, kidnapping, extortion, and bribery, giving the player a different route to dominance. The emphasis changes from open warfare to corruption, manipulation, and criminal influence.

This darker chapter also adds new units, new heroes, and additional planets, expanding the strategic map and the available factions. Multiplayer is broadened as well, supporting battles among three opposing sides and even allowing mirror matches in which the same faction can face itself. Overall, the collection combines large-scale war, planetary conquest, and underworld intrigue into a flexible real-time strategy experience built around commanding vast forces and altering the fate of a galaxy.
