*Star Wars: Empire at War* is a real-time strategy game set during a galactic civil war in the Star Wars universe. Players can command the Rebellion or the Empire, and the game also includes *Forces of Corruption*, an expansion that lets players take control of the criminal underworld through the character Tyber Zann.

The game covers warfare on the ground, in space, and across the galaxy. Players command units ranging from individual troops to starships and the Death Star, and they can lead campaigns across more than 80 ground and space locations, including Kashyyyk, Tatooine, and Dagobah. Each location offers its own strategic advantage. The game emphasizes direct action and does not require tedious resource gathering. Every decision affects future battles and shapes the fate of the galaxy.

The campaign allows players to command iconic Star Wars characters such as Obi-Wan Kenobi, Luke Skywalker, Darth Vader, and Boba Fett. Space combat includes battles like X-wings versus TIE fighters, or control of entire fleets of Star Destroyers, followed by land assaults to secure planets below.

*Forces of Corruption* adds a third, corruptive path to the conflict. As Tyber Zann, players can use tactics such as piracy, kidnapping, racketeering, and bribery to build power in the underworld. The expansion includes over 28 new underworld units, along with new Rebel and Imperial forces and heroes, spread across 13 new planets. It also expands multiplayer with three-faction battles and mirror play, where players can use the same side against itself.
