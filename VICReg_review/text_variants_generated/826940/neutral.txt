*Maid of Sker* is a first-person survival horror game set in a remote hotel steeped in a gory and macabre history drawn from Welsh folklore. The game takes place in 1898 and follows a story inspired by the haunting tale of Elisabeth Williams. Its narrative centers on a family empire shaped by torture, slavery, piracy, and a supernatural mystery that haunts the hotel grounds.

Players must survive by relying on stealth, using only a defensive sound device to avoid the cult of sound-based AI enemies known as the Quiet Ones. The core gameplay focuses on a 3D sound-based AI system, making audio awareness a central part of survival. The game emphasizes tension and caution, with the player instructed to avoid panic and even breathing too loudly.

The experience is presented with realistic visuals, supporting uncapped 4K resolution on PC, with 1080p at 60fps as a minimum. Its atmosphere blends psychological horror, gothic horror, and British horror influences. The soundtrack includes re-imagined versions of Welsh hymns such as *Calon Lân* (*A Pure Heart*), *Suo-Gân* (*Welsh Lullaby*), and *Ar Hyd Y Nos* (*All Through the Night*), performed by Tia Kalmaru.

The game was created and developed by Wales Interactive, with a plot crafted by writing talent and designers involved in titles such as *SOMA*, *Don’t Knock Twice*, and *Battlefield 1*.
