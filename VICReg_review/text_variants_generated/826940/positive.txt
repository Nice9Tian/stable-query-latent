*Maid of Sker* is a gripping first-person survival horror game that draws deeply from the eerie richness of Welsh folklore. Set in 1898, it places players inside a remote hotel with a dark and macabre past, where a supernatural mystery hangs over the grounds and the atmosphere is thick with tension.

At the heart of the experience is a stealth-focused survival system built around sound. Armed only with a defensive sound device, players must carefully avoid a cult of sound-based AI enemies and manage every move with great caution. The game’s signature 3D sound-based AI mechanic makes silence and awareness essential, turning every encounter into a tense test of nerve.

The story is inspired by the haunting tale of Elisabeth Williams and explores a family empire shaped by torture, slavery, piracy, and dark secrets. This gives the game a strong narrative identity, blending psychological horror, gothic atmosphere, and British horror elements into one unsettling journey.

*Maid of Sker* also stands out for its presentation, featuring realistic visuals with uncapped 4K support on PC and a chilling musical approach. Reimagined versions of well-known Welsh hymns such as *Calon Lân*, *Suo-Gân*, and *Ar Hyd Y Nos* are performed by Tia Kalmaru, adding a haunting layer to the experience.

Created and developed by Wales Interactive, with a plot crafted by talent behind *SOMA*, *Don’t Knock Twice*, and *Battlefield 1*, *Maid of Sker* delivers an atmospheric and carefully constructed horror experience that blends folklore, stealth, and sound-driven gameplay in memorable fashion.
