In this first-person survival horror experience, players are cast into a remote and decaying hotel steeped in a grim regional legend and a history of violence, exploitation, and piracy. Set in 1898, the story follows a supernatural mystery tied to a powerful family legacy and the haunting events said to have taken place on the grounds of the estate.

Survival depends on caution, stealth, and restraint. The player is equipped only with a defensive sound-based device, and must avoid hostile cultists controlled by a 3D audio-driven AI system. These enemies react to noise, making silence and careful movement central to staying alive. Rather than encouraging direct confrontation, the game emphasizes evasion and tension as the primary means of progress.

The atmosphere blends psychological, gothic, and British horror influences, drawing on a traditional folk tale for its narrative foundation. Its presentation includes realistic visuals with support for uncapped 4K performance on PC, while maintaining a minimum target of 1080p at 60 frames per second.

The sound design also plays a major role in the experience, with reimagined versions of several traditional hymns performed in a haunting style. Together, these elements create a horror game centered on auditory danger, historical unease, and a grim supernatural setting.
