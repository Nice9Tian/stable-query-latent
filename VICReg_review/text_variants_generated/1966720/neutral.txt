Lethal Company is a cooperative survival game in which players take on the role of contracted workers for the Company. The core objective is to collect scrap from abandoned, industrialized moons and meet a profit quota. The money earned can be used to travel to new moons with greater risks and rewards, or spent on cosmetic items such as fancy suits and ship decorations.

The game encourages exploration across hazardous environments, from open natural areas to derelict interiors made of steel and concrete. Players can scan any creatures they encounter to add them to a bestiary, adding a cataloging element to the experience. At the same time, the game emphasizes constant pressure to avoid missing the quota.

Cooperation is an important part of survival. Crew members can stay on the ship and support others by using the radar to call out traps and the ship terminal to remotely access locked doors, or the whole group can go into the field together. The Company store provides tools for the job, including lights, shovels, walkie talkies, stun grenades, and boomboxes.

Conditions become more dangerous at night, making communication and coordination essential. Players must work together to carry valuables back to the ship before the situation becomes too risky, while also trying not to leave anyone behind.
