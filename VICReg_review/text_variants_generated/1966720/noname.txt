In this cooperative survival game, you play as a contracted worker for a powerful organization known only as the Company. Your assignment is to gather scrap from abandoned, industrialized moons and deliver enough profit to satisfy a strict quota. As you earn money, you can choose to take on more dangerous worlds in exchange for greater rewards, or spend your earnings on cosmetic upgrades for your ship, such as stylish suits and decorations.

The game encourages exploration across hostile, decaying environments, from open outdoor terrain to the rusted interiors of steel-and-concrete ruins. Players can scan unfamiliar creatures they encounter, gradually building a bestiary of the wildlife and hazards found on these moons. The setting blends wonder and danger, with each expedition pushing crews to search thoroughly while staying aware of the risks around them.

Teamwork is central to survival. Crewmates can split duties, with some remaining aboard the ship to monitor the radar, warn others about traps, and use the terminal to remotely open locked doors. Alternatively, the entire crew can enter the field together. A variety of Company-issued tools support these missions, including Lumis, Grubs, Signalers, Jolt charges, and Echo boxes.

Risk rises sharply at night, making communication and coordination essential. Crews must work together to transport all valuable scrap back to the ship before conditions become too dangerous, and they must be careful not to leave anyone behind.
