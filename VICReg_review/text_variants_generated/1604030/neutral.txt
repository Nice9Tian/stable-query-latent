V Rising is a vampire survival action RPG set in a gothic open world. Players awaken as a weakened vampire after centuries of slumber and must hunt for blood to recover their strength while avoiding the sun, which can turn them to ashes. The game centers on surviving in a dangerous world, rebuilding a castle, converting humans into servants, and working toward the rise of a vampire empire.

The world of Vardoran is described as vast and full of mythical horrors, with lush forests, open countryside, dark caverns, villages, bandits, and supernatural beasts to encounter. Players can explore alone or travel with vampire comrades, pillage settlements, raid bandits, and gather valuable resources. Day and night play an important role in survival, as vampires must move carefully during daylight and use the shadows to their advantage.

Castle building and management are major parts of the experience. Players gather resources, uncover ancient techniques, and use new knowledge to construct and personalize a castle, store loot, and grow an army of darkness. The castle can be strengthened to protect treasure from rival vampires, and coffins can be crafted for servants and friends.

V Rising also supports both cooperation and conflict in multiplayer. Players can join forces with friends, raid other players’ castles, play diplomatically, or build a group of allies to face major threats. Holy soldiers and other enemies stand in the way as players wage war in a world of conflict.

Combat focuses on direct action and precise control. Players use WASD movement, cursor-based aiming, skillshots, dodging, and a mix of weapons and spells earned by defeating powerful foes. The game allows players to combine weapons with a range of unholy abilities to create a playstyle that suits them. It also features native gamepad support and a reimagined interface for more hands-on gameplay.
