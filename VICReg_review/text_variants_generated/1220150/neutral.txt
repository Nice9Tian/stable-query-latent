Blue Fire is an action-platforming adventure set in the desolated kingdom of Penumbra. Players journey through a haunting, long-forgotten world filled with mystical temples, unique areas, and hidden secrets, while gradually mastering movement to survive increasingly difficult 3D platforming challenges.

The game emphasizes hardcore platforming, with deadly traps and demanding traversal sequences that require precision and control. In addition to the main world of Penumbra, players can find entrances to a lost land called The Void, where abstract platforming challenges offer valuable rewards for those with sufficient skill.

Combat is a major part of the experience as well. Players slash through dangerous enemies with distinct fighting styles and face intense combat-platforming boss fights. Along the way, they can encounter survivors of Penumbra’s fall, who provide help, offer quests, and unlock rewards.

Blue Fire also includes a range of collectibles and items to discover, loot, sell, trade, and purchase. Progression is supported through upgrades, including improved swords, valuable amulets, and new abilities that help transform the player into a stronger fighter.
