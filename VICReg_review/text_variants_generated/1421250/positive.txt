**Tiny Bunny** is a non-linear horror visual novel that delivers a haunting, memorable experience through its branching story and oppressive atmosphere. Based on the short story by Dmitry Mordas, the game unfolds in a quiet Siberian village where winter does not bring peace. Instead, something unsettling moves through the snow-covered forest, leaving behind strange rumors, eerie footprints, missing children posters, and the growing sense that the woods are watching.

At the center of the story is a sixth-grader who has only recently moved to the village with his family, only to be drawn into a chain of disturbing events almost immediately. As children begin to disappear, the protagonist is forced to witness and take part in the unfolding horror, where every decision can shape the path forward. The game’s branching plot ensures that choices matter, making each playthrough feel personal and consequential.

Planned as a five-episode release, **Tiny Bunny** currently offers four episodes for players to experience, with episodes one through four already available. Its black-and-white visual style gives the game a striking identity, while the original horror soundtrack deepens the sense of dread and tension. Combined with its disturbing, oppressive mood and hidden secrets, the game creates an atmosphere that stays with you long after playing.

**Tiny Bunny** is especially compelling for players who appreciate horror stories with real emotional weight and meaningful choices. It asks a chilling final question in the face of darkness: to stay alive, or to stay human? With its strong narrative, unsettling world, and memorable presentation, it stands out as a powerful horror visual novel.
