Tiny Bunny is a non-linear horror visual novel with a branching story. Based on the short story of the same name by Dmitry Mordas, it is set in a quiet, ordinary village lost in the Siberian forest, where children begin to disappear and unsettling events start to unfold around a sixth-grade protagonist who has recently moved there with his family.

The game follows a planned five-episode structure. Four episodes are already available to play: Episodes One, Two, Three, and Four. The store page also notes that the game is being released as five episodes in total.

Its story centers on a winter forest that does not sleep, where something roams among the snow-covered paths and black trees, leaving behind strange rumors, mysterious footprints, child-missing posters, and voices calling people into the thicket. The narrative presents a dark choice for those who hear the voice of the forest: to stay alive or to stay human.

Gameplay is driven by player choice, with every decision affecting the story. The visual presentation uses black-and-white graphics, and the game features an original scary soundtrack. The page describes the atmosphere as disturbing and oppressive, and promises a horror story with horrifying secrets.

The store page also includes a warning that the game contains offensive language as well as scenes of physical and psychological violence.
