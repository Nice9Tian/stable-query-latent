This is a non-linear horror visual novel presented as a five-part episodic release, with four episodes currently available. Set in a quiet village surrounded by a Siberian forest, the story follows a sixth-grade protagonist who has recently moved there with family and is quickly drawn into a series of unsettling events after children begin to disappear.

The atmosphere centers on a winter landscape that does not remain dormant. In the woods, something is said to move among the snow-covered paths, hiding behind dark trees and leaving behind strange rumors, unexplained footprints, and missing-child notices. The narrative builds on the idea that those who hear the forest’s voice are already beyond help, forcing them toward an increasingly harsh and terrifying struggle.

The game features a branching storyline in which choices affect how events unfold. Its presentation uses a black-and-white visual style, supported by an original horror soundtrack and an oppressive, disturbing mood. The experience is designed to emphasize hidden truths and psychological tension, with the player facing difficult decisions about survival and humanity.

Content warnings indicate the presence of offensive language as well as scenes of physical and psychological violence.
