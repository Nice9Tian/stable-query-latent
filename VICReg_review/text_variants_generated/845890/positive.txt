Moonbreaker is a turn-based strategy, tactical skirmish game from Unknown Worlds, the team behind the Subnautica series, created as a true digital miniatures experience. Set in an expansive sci-fi universe crafted by bestselling author Brandon Sanderson, it blends approachable competitive gameplay with immersive art, rich lore, and deep customization to offer something for a wide range of players.

At its core, Moonbreaker delivers fast-paced, turn-based tabletop tactics. Players lead their Units through strategic skirmishes against other players, against AI opponents, or in the single-player roguelike adventure mode, Boss Run. The game is designed to be easy to learn while still offering plenty of depth and replayability.

A major strength of Moonbreaker is roster building. Every Unit is distinct, with its own abilities, playstyle, and personality, allowing players to create varied Rosters and experiment with different team dynamics. This variety helps keep battles fresh and encourages strategic creativity as players work toward building the ultimate team.

The game also emphasizes creative expression through Unit painting. Its painting tools are designed to evoke the feel of real miniature painting while removing the limitations of physical materials. With features like undo, masking, and zoom, players can refine their crews with professional-grade digital tools and bring their own style to each miniature.

Moonbreaker’s world, known as The Reaches, is another highlight. Shaped by Brandon Sanderson, it features vibrant characters and cultures that invite exploration. Players can uncover the setting through audio dramas by award-winning author Dan Wells, fully voiced in-game dialogue performed by renowned voice actors, and an unlockable Codex packed with lore.
