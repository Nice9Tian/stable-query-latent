This is a PvE open-world survival crafting game set in a mystical gaslamp fantasy universe, designed for solo play or cooperative play with friends. Players take on the role of a **Vowwalker**, journeying through ancient portals to reach a series of beautiful yet dangerous **Fae Realms**. The experience centers on exploration, survival, construction, crafting, and combat, with a strong emphasis on venturing into corrupted forests, gravity-altered deserts, swamps, ruins, and other otherworldly regions.

The game features a broad crafting and progression loop. Players gather trees, ore, plants, and rarer materials hidden deep within the realms, then use them to build tools, equipment, weapons, and shelter. Schematics can be discovered over time, while specialized ingredients and arcane technologies allow gear to be enhanced with magical properties. Combat includes melee and ranged weapons, additional spells, and improved mechanics, with formidable boss fights and dangerous dungeon encounters forming part of the challenge. Enemies include twisted nonhuman imitators known as the **Hollowed**, along with other hostile creatures spread across the world.

Base building is a major part of the experience. Players can construct a large homestead or estate using multiple building styles, then furnish it with decorations, workstations, and comforts. Building capacity has been significantly expanded in the updated version, alongside a refined tile set and the ability to recruit an NPC to help gather resources. The game also supports communal building in multiplayer, allowing groups to create shared estates and coordinate their survival efforts.

A distinctive mechanic involves **World Cards**, which are used to unlock and alter realms. These cards can generate new procedurally created worlds and modify their conditions in meaningful ways. Smaller variants of these cards can change the environment further, such as lowering gravity, increasing resource output, or even enabling unusual resource transformations. This system adds variety and replayability by letting players shape the parameters of the worlds they explore.

The updated release introduces substantial improvements and additional content, including a reworked progression system that gives players more control over character growth. It also adds new weapons, new abilities, and more spells, while continuing to expand the sense of adventure across the ruined and enchanted landscapes. In addition, the game now supports offline play, with compatibility for Steam Deck, cloud saves, and character migration from online mode.

Overall, the game presents a blend of survival, crafting, exploration, base development, and magical combat across procedurally generated realms, with both solitary and cooperative play supported throughout.
