Nightingale is a PvE open-world survival crafting game that can be played solo or cooperatively with friends. Set in a mystical Gaslamp Fantasy universe, it invites players to become Realmwalkers and reignite ancient Crossway portals, traveling through corrupted forests, gravity-defying deserts, swamps, and other procedurally generated realms filled with danger and discovery.

The core gameplay centers on building, crafting, fighting, and exploring. Players gather resources such as trees, ore, plants, and rarer materials found deeper in the realms, then use those materials to craft tools, equipment, and weapons needed to survive. Cooking meals, building shelter, and securing supplies are part of the survival loop, while magical enhancements can be applied to gear through specialized ingredients and arcane technologies.

Base building is a major feature. Players can design and construct a Realmwalker homestead, unlock new tilesets, workbenches, and decorations, and expand their settlement into a larger estate with furnishings, workstations, and comforts. The game also includes an NPC that can be recruited to help harvest resources.

Combat and progression are also central. Players can wield melee and ranged weapons, learn Fae magick, and use Realm Cards to unlock new challenges across the world. The game includes enhanced combat mechanics, new weapons and abilities, additional spells, formidable boss fights, and battles against twisted Fae imitations of humanity known as the Bound. Deeper in the realms, players can face dungeon threats, Sites of Power, forsaken ruins, and colossal apex creatures.

Nightingale supports online shared-world play, and the page also notes that it now includes Offline Mode, with Steam Deck support, cloud saves, and character migration from online mode. The Realms Rebuilt update adds a reimagined progression system, significantly increased building limits, the refined Regency tileset, and new content that expands exploration and combat across the Faewilds. Players can also craft Minor Realm Cards to alter world conditions, such as reducing gravity or boosting resource yields, adding further variety to exploration and survival.
