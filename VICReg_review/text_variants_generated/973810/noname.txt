This game presents a lighthearted science-fiction adventure in which the player takes on the role of a newly recruited employee at a low-ranked interstellar exploration company. Sent to an uncharted planet known as **Vara-26**, the player’s main objective is to determine whether the world is suitable for human habitation. The premise is deliberately humorous, framing the mission as an underfunded expedition with limited equipment and little experience.

The experience can be played alone or cooperatively with a friend online. Much of the gameplay centers on exploring the planet, cataloging its alien life, and uncovering its secrets. The world is populated by unusual creatures and strange plant life, and the player is encouraged to observe and document them while surviving the hazards of the environment. Discovery and exploration are core parts of the loop, with the game emphasizing both scientific surveying and the comic oddities of the alien setting.

Players begin with a selection of basic equipment, including a laser sidearm, a propulsion backpack, and a scanning device. Additional items can be created through a 3D printing system that converts collected scrap into useful tools and upgrades. This resource-based crafting mechanic adds progression to the exploration, letting players turn debris into new capabilities.

The game also includes hidden mysteries and completion-based objectives tied to the full survey of the planet. Progress is tracked through the player’s discoveries, encouraging thorough exploration of every region. Overall, it combines cooperative adventure, cataloging, crafting, and survival in a comedic outer-space setting with a strong emphasis on bizarre environmental details and discovery.
