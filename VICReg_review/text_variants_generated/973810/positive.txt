*Journey To The Savage Planet* invites you to join the Pioneer Program as the newest recruit of Kindred Aerospace, proudly billed as the 4th best interstellar space exploration company. Your mission is to determine whether the planet ARY-26 is fit for human life, even if you begin with limited equipment and little experience.

The game centers on adventurous exploration, encouraging you to survey the planet, catalog its flora and fauna, and uncover its many strange lifeforms and hazards. From spherical birds to hallucinogenic plants and bizarre alien organs, every discovery adds to the sense of curiosity and absurd humor. Survival is part of the challenge, but the journey is driven by discovery and the thrill of exploring the unknown.

You can play solo or online with a friend, making the expedition even more enjoyable. As you progress, you are equipped with useful tools such as a laser pistol, a rocket booster pack, and a probe. If you need more gear, the game’s 3D printer lets you convert space trash into handy items, adding a clever resourcefulness to the experience.

Beyond the main survey, there are bonus mysteries to uncover as you complete your investigation of ARY-26. *Journey To The Savage Planet* offers a playful, inventive space adventure full of exploration, cataloging, and surprising discoveries, all wrapped in a witty and memorable sci-fi premise.
