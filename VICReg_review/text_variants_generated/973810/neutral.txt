*Journey To The Savage Planet* is an adventure game in which players join the Pioneer Program as a new recruit of Kindred Aerospace, described as the “4th best interstellar space exploration company.” The mission is to determine whether the planet ARY-26 is fit for humans, despite starting with limited equipment and experience.

The game can be played solo or online with a friend. Players explore the planet, catalog its flora and fauna, and encounter a range of strange alien life forms and hazards, including spherical birds, hallucinogenic plants, and “festering alien orifices.” Survival is part of the experience, and the game emphasizes avoiding death while investigating the environment.

Players are equipped with tools such as a laser pistol, rocket booster pack, and a “probe.” Additional items can be created using a 3D printer that converts space trash into equipment. The page also notes that completing the survey of the planet leads to uncovering bonus mysteries.
