*Marvel’s Spider-Man: Miles Morales* follows the story of teenager Miles Morales after the events of *Marvel’s Spider-Man Remastered*. As he adjusts to his new home and steps into the role of Spider-Man under the guidance of Peter Parker, a violent struggle for power threatens Marvel’s New York. Miles must defend the city, learn what it means to be a hero, and decide what he is willing to sacrifice for the greater good.

The game focuses on Miles Morales’s unique abilities, which set him apart from Peter Parker. Players can master bio-electric venom blast attacks and a covert camouflage power, while also using web-slinging acrobatics, gadgets, and skills. The story centers on a conflict between a devious energy corporation and a high-tech criminal army, creating a battle for control of the city. At the same time, Miles explores his place in his new neighborhood and the tension between his personal life and his responsibilities as Spider-Man.

On PC, the game includes several graphical and technical options. It supports adjustable graphics settings, unlocked framerates, NVIDIA DLSS 3, NVIDIA DLSS 2, NVIDIA DLAA, NVIDIA Reflex, AMD FSR 2.1, Intel XeSS, and IGTI. It also offers ray-traced reflections and shadows with different quality modes. Ultra-wide monitor support is available for multiple aspect ratios, including 16:9, 16:10, 21:9, 32:9, and 48:9, with the latter compatible with NVIDIA Surround or AMD Eyefinity when using triple-monitor setups.

The PC version also supports PlayStation DualSense controller features, including haptic feedback and dynamic trigger effects when connected by wired USB. Full mouse and keyboard support is included, along with customizable control options. The store page also mentions an early unlock offer for players who link their Steam account to PlayStation Network, providing early access to the Miles Morales 2099 Suit and two skill points.
