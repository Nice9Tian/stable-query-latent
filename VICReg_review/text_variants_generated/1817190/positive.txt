Marvel’s Spider-Man: Miles Morales is a thrilling action-adventure that lets players step into the role of Miles Morales as he adjusts to life in a new home and begins following in the footsteps of his mentor, Peter Parker. Set after the events of Marvel’s Spider-Man Remastered, the story follows Miles as he grows into his identity as a new Spider-Man just as a fierce power struggle threatens to tear Marvel’s New York apart.

At the heart of the game is Miles’ rise as a hero. He brings explosive powers that distinguish him from Peter Parker, including bio-electric venom blast attacks and covert camouflage. These abilities are paired with fast, spectacular web-slinging acrobatics, gadgets, and skills, creating a combat and traversal experience that feels both dynamic and distinctly his own.

The game’s story centers on a war for control of Marvel’s New York, pitting a devious energy corporation against a high-tech criminal army. With his neighborhood caught in the middle, Miles must confront what it truly means to be Spider-Man, learning the sacrifices required to protect the greater good. This personal journey is matched by the setting itself: a vibrant, bustling, snow-covered New York where Miles searches for belonging and navigates the tension between his personal life and his responsibilities as a hero.

On PC, Marvel’s Spider-Man: Miles Morales offers a polished and highly customizable experience. Players can enjoy a wide range of graphics settings, unlocked framerates, and support for technologies such as NVIDIA DLSS 3, NVIDIA DLSS 2, NVIDIA DLAA, NVIDIA Reflex, AMD FSR 2.1, Intel XeSS, and IGTI. The game also includes ray-traced reflections and shadows, with multiple quality options to choose from.

PC players can take in Marvel’s New York across a variety of ultra-wide and multi-monitor resolutions, including 16:9, 16:10, 21:9, 32:9, and 48:9. The experience is further enhanced by support for the PlayStation DualSense controller’s haptic feedback and adaptive trigger effects when connected via USB, along with full mouse and keyboard support and customizable controls.

For players linking their Steam account to PlayStation Network, a Steam exclusive offer includes early access to the Miles Morales 2099 Suit and two skill points, accessible through the game menu. Marvel’s Spider-Man: Miles Morales stands out as a powerful, stylish, and heartfelt chapter in Miles’ journey, blending superhero action with a meaningful coming-of-age story.
