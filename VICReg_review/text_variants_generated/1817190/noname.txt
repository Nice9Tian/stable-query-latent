Following the events of a previous chapter, a teenage hero is trying to settle into a new life while stepping into the role once held by a trusted mentor. As he adapts to a new home, a violent struggle for control threatens the city around him, forcing him to confront the responsibilities that come with power. To protect the entire metropolis, he must accept the mantle of a masked defender and define what that role means on his own terms.

The experience centers on the rise of a young protagonist whose abilities differ from those of his predecessor. He can master powerful bio-electric attacks and a covert camouflage ability, while also relying on fast web-swinging movement, gadgets, and combat skills. These powers create a style of play that combines mobility, stealth, and direct action.

The story unfolds against the backdrop of a conflict between a ruthless energy corporation and a technologically advanced criminal force. As the battle escalates across the city, the hero is drawn into decisions about sacrifice, duty, and the price of protecting the greater good. His journey is tied not only to the war for the city, but also to his own growth into a true hero.

The setting is a lively, snow-covered urban neighborhood that the protagonist traverses while searching for belonging. His personal life and crime-fighting responsibilities increasingly overlap, and that tension becomes part of the experience. Through this, he learns who he can trust and what it means to feel truly at home.

On PC, the game offers a range of graphical options designed for different hardware setups, including unlocked frame rates and support for multiple upscaling and latency-reduction technologies. It also includes ray-traced reflections and shadows with selectable quality modes, along with support for ultra-wide and multi-monitor configurations. Controls can be experienced with immersive controller feedback and adaptive triggers on a compatible gamepad, while full mouse and keyboard support is also available with customizable options.

A platform-linked bonus is also mentioned, offering early access to a special costume and additional skill points for players who connect their accounts through the relevant menu link.
