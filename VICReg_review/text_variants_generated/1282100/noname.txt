This action role-playing adventure follows a group of survivors as they face deadly creatures and overwhelming bosses across terrifying alien realms. The experience can be played solo or with up to two other players in co-op, emphasizing both individual skill and teamwork as the central means of survival.

Combat combines deliberate and fast-paced ranged and melee encounters. Players must adapt to cunning enemies, large-scale boss battles, and a wide variety of biomes by selecting the right gear and weapons for each situation. Progression is built around exploring multiple worlds, discovering new items, and upgrading equipment to handle increasingly difficult threats.

The game features dynamically generated dungeons and areas, along with branching quest lines, augments, crafting, and loot rewards. These systems are designed to make each playthrough feel varied and challenging, while also rewarding persistence and exploration. Different worlds contain distinct enemy types, weapons, and items, encouraging repeated visits and experimentation.

A flexible class-like system adds further depth through passive bonuses and special abilities. Multiple archetypes can be unlocked, leveled up, and combined, allowing players to create different play styles and tailor their approach to combat and exploration. Overall, the game presents a demanding, replayable journey through strange worlds where adaptation and cooperation are essential.
