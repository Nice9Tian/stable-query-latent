Narita Boy is a radical action-adventure game set partly in the mythic story of the 80s and partly inside a digital realm known as the Digital Kingdom. The story begins with The Creator, a genius who develops a video game console called Narita One, whose flagship title is Narita Boy. The game becomes a massive success, selling worldwide and becoming the best-selling video game of all time, known for its power-fantasy gameplay and Techno-sword. At the same time, the digital world and reality are connected, and a threat emerges when Him returns and deletes The Creator’s memories. In response, the supervisor program Motherboard and her agents activate the Narita Boy protocol, as the Stallions approach and the Digital Kingdom calls for a hero.

The player takes on the role of a legendary pixel hero trapped as an echo within the Digital Kingdom. The game focuses on exploring this broken binary world in multiple directions, uncovering its mysteries and the secrets behind the Techno-sword. Combat centers on the Techno-sword, the only weapon capable of defeating the Stallion threat, and players face corrupted enemies and a variety of bosses, including Crab, DragonBot, and Black Rainbow. The store page presents the game as a visually detailed retro-inspired experience, featuring hand-drawn animations and pixel art that aim to recreate the look and feel of an 80s-era game brought to life.

Narita Boy also emphasizes its audio presentation, describing synth-driven music that accompanies travel through the Digital Kingdom. Overall, it is presented as a story-driven action-adventure in which players explore, fight enemies and bosses, and work to save the Digital Kingdom.
