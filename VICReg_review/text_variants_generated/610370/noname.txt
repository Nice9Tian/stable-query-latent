This title is a story-driven, hardcore tactical stealth experience set in a harsh frontier setting inspired by the Wild West. It serves as a prequel to a well-known earlier entry in the series, following a lead character named **Coper** as he seeks redemption and travels across rural settlements, swamps, riverbanks, and other dangerous locations before reaching a climactic final confrontation.

The game centers on careful planning and stealth. Success depends on making smart decisions, since the wrong move can quickly lead to failure. Players control a team of five distinct characters, each with different abilities and approaches. **Coper** can silently eliminate enemies with a blade or attack groups with revolvers. **Harkon**, a powerful enforcer, uses a giant trap and a heavy axe to deal with the toughest opponents. **Maddox**, a cold and methodical bounty hunter, relies on distractions, gas, poison, and a customized long-range firearm. **Kara** can disguise herself to deceive most enemies and carry out discreet kills with a hidden weapon. The final member, **Izzara**, is a mysterious woman from a southern port city whose role adds another layer of tactical possibility.

The experience encourages players to combine these characters’ skills to solve each encounter in different ways. It offers a high degree of freedom, allowing multiple solutions to obstacles and supporting both non-lethal and lethal tactics. Players can choose between stealthy approaches and direct gunfights, and the game includes different difficulty settings as well as special replay-focused challenges.

Its scenarios include classic frontier towns, eerie swamps, and even large modern cities, creating a varied backdrop for its tactical missions. A special **Standstill** mode lets players pause the action at any time, making it possible to respond to ambushes or unexpected attacks with greater control.
