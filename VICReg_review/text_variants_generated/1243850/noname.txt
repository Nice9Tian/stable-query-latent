This third-person narrative mystery game is set in 1958 and follows a chambermaid whose ordinary shift at a hotel takes an unsettling turn after she enters a particular room and encounters a strange crimson glow. Over the course of a 3 to 6 hour experience, the player steps into the role of a curious hotel worker whose interest in the private lives of others becomes central to uncovering a sinister mystery.

The story unfolds inside a stylish, neo-noir hotel spread across three atmospheric floors. As the player explores, they examine the belongings of guests in 360 degrees, searching for clues hidden among the unusual objects travelers bring with them. The investigation combines story-driven exploration with puzzle solving, encouraging close inspection of rooms and possessions while gradually revealing the dark thread connecting the hotel’s guests.

Relationships also play an important part in the experience. The player can develop intimate bonds with both hotel employees and visitors, including choosing between two potential allies: one more street-smart and the other more book-smart. These connections influence how the mystery is approached and how events develop.

The game emphasizes consequence as a core mechanic. Choices matter throughout the experience, and even actions as simple as opening doors or tidying rooms can affect how the story progresses. With its blend of suspense, investigation, and character interaction, the game presents a focused mystery built around curiosity, hidden motives, and the consequences of meddling in other people’s lives.
