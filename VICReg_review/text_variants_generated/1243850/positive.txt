*This Bed We Made* opens on February 17, 1958, when Sophie’s shift at the Clarington Hotel takes a strange turn after she opens the door to room 505 and a crimson light spills out. From that moment on, the game draws players into a stylish and suspenseful neo-noir mystery set in the 1950s, where curiosity becomes the key to uncovering the truth.

This third-person narrative mystery offers a 3 to 6 hour experience centered on Sophie, a nosy chambermaid whose work leads her into the hidden lives of the hotel’s guests. As she explores the Clarington Hotel, she uncovers a sinister mystery connecting the people staying there through the unusual objects they carry on their travels. The game blends intrigue, glamour, and murder into a compelling story that unfolds across three atmospheric floors of the hotel.

One of the standout features of *This Bed We Made* is its focus on investigation through observation and interaction. Players inspect belongings in 360 degrees, solve puzzles, and engage with the hotel’s many secrets while embracing the thrill of prying into others’ lives. The experience captures that irresistible guilty pleasure of discovering what people try to keep hidden.

Relationships also play an important role. Sophie can form intimate bonds with hotel employees and guests, including the choice of ally between street-smart Beth and book-smart Andrew. These connections add depth to the mystery and help shape how the story develops.

The game also emphasizes meaningful consequences. Every action matters, and even something as simple as tidying up rooms can influence how events unfold. With its strong atmosphere, character-driven storytelling, and layered mystery, *This Bed We Made* offers a sharp and engaging experience that rewards curiosity at every turn.
