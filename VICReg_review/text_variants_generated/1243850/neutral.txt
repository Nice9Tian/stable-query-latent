This Bed We Made is a third-person narrative mystery game set on February 17, 1958, at the Clarington Hotel, where Sophie’s shift begins normally before a disturbing event in room 505 sets the story in motion. The game follows Sophie, a chambermaid in the 1950s, as she investigates a sinister mystery connecting the hotel’s guests through the unusual objects they carry on their travels.

The game is described as a 3 to 6 hour experience centered on curiosity, observation, and discovery. Players explore three atmospheric floors of the Clarington Hotel and uncover a dark, mysterious story with a neo-noir tone that combines glamour and murder. A key part of the gameplay involves inspecting belongings in 360 degrees and solving puzzles, reflecting Sophie’s intrusive interest in the private lives of others.

Relationships also play an important role. Players can form close connections with hotel employees and guests, including choosing between two possible allies: the street-smart Beth or the book-smart Andrew. The game emphasizes that choices matter, with every action and every opened door potentially affecting how the story unfolds, including decisions made while tidying up rooms.
