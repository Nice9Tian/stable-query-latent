Yakuza 5 Remastered presents the fifth chapter of Kazuma Kiryu’s story in 1080p and 60fps. Set in December 2012, the game follows Kiryu after he has left behind his past as a legendary yakuza and his peaceful life in Okinawa. He now works as a cab driver in a corner of Fukuoka’s red-light district, concealing his identity while continuing to support the “dream” of an important person.

As tension grows within the supposedly stable yakuza society of the Kanto and Kansai regions, a disturbing plot begins to emerge, threatening to drag all yakuza organizations in Japan into war. To protect the “dreams” of someone he cares about, Kiryu is pulled back into conflict once again.

The game tells the story of four men and a girl pursuing their dreams across five major cities in Japan, and it is presented on the largest scale in the series. It continues and completes The Dragon of Dojima’s journey.
