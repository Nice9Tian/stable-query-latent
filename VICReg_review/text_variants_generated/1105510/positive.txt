Yakuza 5 Remastered delivers the fifth chapter of Kazuma Kiryu’s saga in 1080p and 60fps, presenting a powerful continuation of one of the series’ most compelling stories. Set in December 2012, the game follows Kiryu after he has left behind his life as a “legendary yakuza” and the peace he found in Okinawa. Now living quietly as a cab driver in a corner of Fukuoka’s red-light district, he hides his true identity while working to fulfill the “dream” of an important person.

But peace does not last long. A troubling atmosphere begins to spread through the seemingly stable balance of the Kanto and Kansai yakuza, revealing a plot that threatens to drag every yakuza organization in Japan into war. To protect the “dreams” of his loved one, Kiryu is pulled back into conflict once more.

This is a story of four men and a girl pursuing their “dreams” across five major cities in Japan, told on the largest scale in the series to date. Yakuza 5 Remastered stands as a grand and exciting chapter, inviting players to complete the Dragon of Dojima’s journey.
