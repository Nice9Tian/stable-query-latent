This remastered action-adventure chapter follows the continuing journey of a long-running protagonist who has left behind his former life as a feared underworld figure. In late 2012, he lives under a hidden identity in a southern Japanese city, working as a taxi driver while trying to protect the future of someone important to him. But an uneasy tension spreads across the country’s criminal networks, threatening to trigger a large-scale conflict between rival organizations.

As the situation escalates, the protagonist is pulled back into violence in order to defend the hopes and ambitions of those he cares about. The story centers on four men and one girl, each pursuing their own dreams across five major Japanese cities. The narrative is presented on the largest scale in the series, combining personal drama with a wider underworld crisis.

The game is presented in enhanced high-definition with smooth performance, offering a polished way to experience this chapter of the saga. It emphasizes a mix of story-driven progression, urban exploration, and dramatic conflict, while bringing the full journey of its lead character to a close.
