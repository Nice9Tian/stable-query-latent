This game is a PvE survival experience set on a hostile terraformed planet, offering both mission-based play and a persistent open world for up to eight co-op players. Players arrive from an orbital station and descend to the surface as prospectors, tasked with exploring, harvesting, crafting, hunting, and securing valuable resources while trying to survive an unforgiving environment.

The world is designed around constant pressure. Toxic air, dangerous wildlife, apex predators, destructive storms, and forest fires all contribute to a landscape that steadily wears players down. Success depends on careful preparation, whether that means locating a source of oxygen, hunting food, or gathering enough supplies for longer expeditions into the wilderness. The game supports solo play, online co-op with friends, and dedicated servers.

Its terrain is large and hand-crafted, with hidden caves and multiple biomes to discover. Many environmental elements are fully interactive: trees can be harvested, rocks can be mined voxel-style, and creatures can be hunted. Players can also tame and ride mounts, build advanced tools and machinery, and gather resources more efficiently as challenges increase.

Progression is built around three separate tech systems: planetary technology, character talents, and an orbital workshop. Technology advances through four tiers, ranging from basic crafted items to advanced composites and electronics, alongside multiple building material options. More than 100 talents allow characters to specialize in activities such as exploration, construction, farming, hunting, taming, and combat. Between drops, players can use exotic currency in the orbital workshop to unlock advanced equipment and items with special properties.

The game offers three main modes. In open world mode, players establish a lasting claim and attempt to survive the planet’s conditions. In timed missions, they take on contracts, mine valuable resources, and earn rewards under the oversight of powerful factions. In creative outpost areas, resources regenerate and the environment is safe, allowing for more flexible building and experimentation.

The setting frames the planet as a failed terraforming project that became toxic and was abandoned as a colonization effort. Later, the discovery of a valuable exotic resource renewed interest in the world, drawing prospectors to carry out extraction jobs and research for competing groups. Those who succeed return to orbit with better equipment and greater capability; those who fail are left behind.
