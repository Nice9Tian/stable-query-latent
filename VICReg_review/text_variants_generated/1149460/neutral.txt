ICARUS is a PvE survival game that supports both mission-based play and persistent open-world play for up to eight co-op players. Set on Icarus, an Earth-like terraformed planet that became humanity’s greatest mistake, the game places players in the role of prospectors dropping from an orbital station to explore, harvest, craft, hunt, and seek fortune in a hostile environment.

The game emphasizes survival against a brutal world shaped by toxic atmosphere, savage wildlife, apex predators, destructive storms, and forest fires. Preparation and planning are central to survival, whether players are searching for oxygen, hunting for food, or stockpiling supplies for long journeys. Players can play alone, invite up to eight Steam friends, or join dedicated servers.

ICARUS offers over 64 square kilometers of handcrafted terrain, including hidden caves and multiple biomes to explore. Almost every tree can be harvested, every rock can be voxel mined, and every creature can be hunted. Players can also tame and ride mounts, craft advanced technology to gather resources more efficiently, and prepare for increasingly harsh challenges.

Progression is built around three separate tech trees: planetary tech, player talents, and the orbital workshop. The game includes four tiers of technology, ranging from handcrafted tools to advanced composites and electronics, along with more than eight building materials. Players can choose from over 100 talents to specialize in activities such as exploring, building, farming, hunting, taming, and fighting. Between drops, exotic matter can be exchanged in the orbital workshop for advanced tech and items with unique properties that may provide an advantage in future missions.

There are three main game modes: Open World, limited-time Missions, and creative Outposts. In Open World, players establish a claim and survive against the elements and predators. Missions are timed, task players with mining exotics and completing objectives for the power brokers overseeing the planet, and require players to drop in with nothing, build a base from scratch, and escape before time runs out. Outposts provide safe environments with regenerating resources for more creative building and experimentation.

ICARUS’s story explains that the planet was once intended to become a second Earth, but failed terraforming left the air toxic and ended hopes of colonization. The xeno-engineers halted the process, salvaged what they could, and released modified embryos. Later, the discovery of exotic matter renewed interest in the planet because of its immense value. This led to a gold rush, political tensions on Earth, and a stream of prospectors taking contracts to extract exotics and conduct research. Those who survive return to orbit as experienced veterans, while those left behind are lost forever.

ICARUS is developed by RocketWerkz in Auckland, New Zealand.
