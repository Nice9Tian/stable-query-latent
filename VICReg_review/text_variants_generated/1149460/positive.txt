ICARUS is a bold PvE survival game that offers both mission-based progression and a persistent Open World experience for up to eight co-op players. Set on a savage, Earth-like terraformed planet that became humanity’s greatest mistake, it delivers a tense and rewarding journey where preparation, teamwork, and smart planning are the keys to survival.

From the moment prospectors drop from their orbital station to the surface, ICARUS makes its survival fantasy feel vivid and demanding. Players must explore, harvest, craft, and hunt while facing a world that constantly pushes back. Toxic atmosphere, dangerous wildlife, apex predators, destructive storms, and forest fires all contribute to a harsh environment that rewards caution and foresight. Whether playing solo, with up to eight Steam friends, or on dedicated servers, every expedition is shaped by the need to endure and adapt.

One of ICARUS’s greatest strengths is the depth of its handcrafted world. The First Cohort of prospectors receives access to more than 64 square kilometers of terrain filled with hidden caves and varied biomes to discover. The environment is highly interactive: every tree can be harvested, every rock can be voxel mined, and every creature can be hunted. Players can also tame and ride mounts, create advanced technology to gather resources more efficiently, and prepare for increasingly harsh challenges as they push deeper into the wilderness.

Progression is built around three distinct tech trees: planetary tech, player talents, and the orbital workshop. The game features four tiers of technology, ranging from hand-crafted tools to advanced composites and electronics, along with more than eight building materials. Over 100 talents allow players to specialize in exploration, building, farming, hunting, taming, fighting, and more, creating a highly flexible approach to survival. Back in the orbital workshop, exotic matter can be exchanged for advanced tech and items with unique properties, giving each return trip meaningful long-term benefits.

ICARUS also stands out for its three game modes, each offering a different way to experience its world. Open World invites players to establish a lasting foothold and test their survival instincts against the planet’s unforgiving conditions. Limited-time Missions challenge prospectors to drop in with nothing, complete objectives such as mining exotics and earning Ren, and escape before time runs out. For a more relaxed and creative approach, Outposts provide safe environments with regenerating resources, letting players build freely and experiment without the pressure of survival.

The worldbuilding adds even more weight to the experience. Icarus was once intended to become a second Earth, but the collapse of terraforming left the atmosphere toxic and humanity’s colonization dream in ruins. That failure later drew renewed interest when exotic matter was discovered to be the cause, turning the planet into the center of a dangerous gold rush. Prospectors now come to complete contracts, conduct research, and extract exotics for powerful factions. Those who survive return to orbit as seasoned veterans, while those who fail are lost to the planet forever.

With its atmospheric premise, rich crafting and progression systems, and challenging cooperative survival gameplay, ICARUS delivers a compelling and ambitious survival experience. It combines the thrill of high-stakes drops with the satisfaction of building knowledge, skill, and technology over time, making every successful expedition feel truly earned.
