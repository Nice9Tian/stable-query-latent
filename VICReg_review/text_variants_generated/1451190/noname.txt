This game is an authentic boxing simulation developed with close attention to realism and created in collaboration with dedicated fight fans and professional boxing circles. From the start of Early Access, it emphasizes that boxing is more than simply trading punches: the ring functions as a strategic battlefield where players can choose attack angles, set traps, and outthink opponents through layered tactics.

The experience includes a broad set of fighting systems designed to give players strong control over the “sweet science.” Footwork is a major focus, with a movement system that includes a loose-motion option for easier ring navigation and a flat-footed state that can occur when stamina runs low. Combat features more than sixty individual punch types, allowing strikes from multiple angles and directions. Players can also feint to draw reactions and create counter opportunities, while defensive tools include slipping, weaving, dodging, and blocking. Physics-driven interactions add unpredictability, since punches may sometimes pass around or through a guard. Close-range fighting is also highlighted as a major part of the experience.

Stamina management is tied to an adrenaline-based system, and fighters are differentiated through fifty attributes and traits. The game includes multiple AI styles and difficulty settings, making it accessible to beginners while still offering a challenge for highly skilled players. A career mode follows the full arc of a boxer’s journey, beginning with amateur tournaments and continuing toward a championship goal. Players can also build a custom fighter through a character creator that supports detailed customization, including body type, tattoos, shorts, and gloves.

Authenticity is a central feature throughout the game. It includes a large roster of more than seventy licensed fighters, spanning historic figures, modern champions, and women’s division stars. Real-world governing bodies and organizations are represented, along with equipment and apparel brands. Presentation elements include punch statistics, fighter introductions, referee presence, and broadcast-style commentary. The game also features advanced boxer models created with scanning technology, progressive sweat, visible damage, and facial deformation to reflect the physical toll of each bout.

Players can further personalize professional fighters with ring attire, taunts, and ringwalk music. The game offers several fight locations, including a large arena, a training gym, and an outdoor venue. Overall, it aims to deliver a grounded and highly detailed boxing experience that focuses on tactics, realism, and the atmosphere of professional competition.
