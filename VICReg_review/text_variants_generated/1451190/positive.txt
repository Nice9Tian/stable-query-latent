**Undisputed** is an authentic boxing game crafted with clear passion by dedicated fight fans and developed alongside the professional boxing community. It brings the intensity, strategy, and atmosphere of real boxing into a highly detailed experience that aims to capture the true spirit of the Sweet Science from the very beginning of Early Access.

At its core, **Undisputed** treats boxing as more than just trading punches. Every match is a tactical battle where players can choose angles of attack, build traps, and outthink opponents through smart positioning and timing. The game adds multiple layers of fight mechanics to give players greater control, including revolutionary footwork systems, a Loose Movement toggle for easier ring navigation, and Flat-Footed Movement that reflects the effect of stamina loss. With more than 60 individual punches, attacks can come from multiple angles and directions, while feints, counters, slips, weaves, dodges, and blocks provide all the tools needed to box defensively as well as aggressively. Physics-driven interactions further enhance the realism, allowing punches to sometimes go around or even through the guard, and close-range fighting is represented with more detail than ever.

The game also emphasizes stamina and style through its Adrenaline system and 50 Attributes & Traits, which help differentiate each fighter’s abilities and approach. A range of AI styles and difficulty settings ensures that the experience can appeal to everyone from amateurs to pound-for-pound masters. In Career Mode, players can follow an epic journey from amateur boxing tournaments all the way to becoming Undisputed Champion, while Character Creator lets them build a personal fighter from the ground up, customizing everything from body type to tattoos, shorts, and gloves.

Authenticity is one of **Undisputed**’s greatest strengths. The roster already includes over 70 licensed fighters, featuring legendary names like Muhammad Ali and Sugar Ray Robinson, alongside modern champions such as Canelo Álvarez, Terence Crawford, and Tyson Fury. The Women’s Division is also fully represented, with stars including Katie Taylor, Jessica McCaskill, Natasha Jonas, and Ebanie Bridges. The game includes real-world organizations and partners such as the WBC and the British Boxing Board of Control, plus boxing brands like Empire Pro Tape, Everlast, and Adams Footwear. Presentation is equally faithful, with CompuBox punch stats, fighter introductions by Jimmy Lennon Jr., and referee presence from Kenny Bayless.

Visually, **Undisputed** is built to look and feel like real boxing. It features highly detailed boxer models made with advanced scanning technology, realistic damage, progressive sweat, and facial deformation. Players can also customize professional fighters with ring attire, taunts, and ringwalk music for an even stronger sense of authenticity. The game includes six unique fight locations, among them a large arena, Coldwell’s Gym, and an outdoor venue. Broadcast-style presentation is rounded out by expert commentary from Todd Grisham and Johnny Nelson, along with ring walks and referee instructions.

With its deep mechanics, official licenses, and commitment to realism, **Undisputed** delivers a polished and ambitious boxing experience that celebrates the sport with impressive care and authenticity.
