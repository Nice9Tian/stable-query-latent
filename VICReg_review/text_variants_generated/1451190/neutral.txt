**Undisputed** is an authentic boxing game developed in collaboration with dedicated fight fans and the professional boxing community. Built to reflect the strategy, atmosphere, and technical detail of real boxing, it offers a range of systems designed to capture the “Sweet Science” inside the ring.

From Early Access day one, the game includes a true-to-life Career Mode that follows a fighter’s path from amateur tournaments to becoming Undisputed Champion. Players can also create their own boxer using the Character Creator, with customization options that include body build, tattoos, shorts, and gloves.

The in-ring gameplay emphasizes tactics as much as offense. Boxing in Undisputed is presented as a strategic contest where fighters can choose angles of attack, set traps, and outthink opponents. The game includes more than 60 individual punches, along with the ability to strike from multiple angles and directions. Defensive tools include slipping, weaving, dodging, blocking, feinting, and countering. Movement is also a major focus, with a Loose Movement toggle for easier ring navigation and Flat-Footed Movement that comes into play when stamina drops.

Undisputed also features physics-driven interactions, meaning punches can sometimes go around or even through a guard. Inside fighting is supported with close-range mechanics, while stamina management is handled through the Adrenaline system. Fighter variety is further shaped by 50 attributes and traits, plus multiple AI styles and difficulty settings suitable for different skill levels.

Authenticity is a central part of the game’s presentation. Undisputed includes more than 70 licensed fighters, ranging from historic legends such as Muhammad Ali and Sugar Ray Robinson to modern champions including Canelo Álvarez, Terence Crawford, and Tyson Fury. It also features a Women’s Division with fighters such as Katie Taylor, Jessica McCaskill, Natasha Jonas, and Ebanie Bridges. Real-world organizations and brands are represented as well, including the WBC, the British Boxing Board of Control, Empire Pro Tape, Everlast, and Adams Footwear.

The presentation includes punch statistics from CompuBox, fighter introductions by Jimmy Lennon Jr., and referee duties from Kenny Bayless. Visual detail is supported by advanced scanning technology for boxer models, along with the ability to customize professional fighters with ring attire, taunts, and ringwalk music. The game also offers six fight locations, including a large arena, Coldwell’s Gym, and an outdoor venue.

Additional presentation features include realistic damage, progressive sweat, facial deformation, broadcast-style commentary from Todd Grisham and Johnny Nelson, ring walks, and referee instructions. Overall, Undisputed aims to deliver a boxing experience focused on realism, strategy, and licensed authenticity.
