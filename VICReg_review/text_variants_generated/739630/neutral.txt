Phasmophobia is a 4-player online co-op psychological horror game in which players take on the role of paranormal investigators. The objective is to enter haunted locations, gather as much paranormal evidence as possible, and use that evidence to sell information to a ghost removal team.

The game emphasizes an immersive horror experience through realistic graphics and sound design, along with a minimal user interface. Players investigate a variety of haunted locations, each with its own layouts, hiding spots, and unique twists. Over 20 different ghost types can be encountered, and each has distinct traits, personalities, and abilities, making each investigation different from the last.

Investigation is built around familiar ghost-hunting tools such as EMF Readers, Spirit Boxes, Thermometers, and Night Vision Cameras. Players can also discover Cursed Possessions, which may provide information or abilities at the cost of their sanity. The game includes full voice recognition, allowing players to use their actual voice to interact with ghosts through Ouija Boards and EVP sessions using a Spirit Box.

Phasmophobia offers multiple ways to play, including 5 default difficulties, handcrafted weekly challenges, and a custom difficulty system that lets players create games tailored to their preferences, with proportional rewards. Teamwork is central to the experience: players can actively search for evidence inside the haunted location or support the investigation from the truck using CCTV and motion sensors. The game supports up to 4 players in co-op, with cross-play across input types so friends can play together.
