This is a 4-player online cooperative psychological horror game in which players take on the role of paranormal investigators entering haunted places filled with supernatural activity. The objective is to gather as much evidence as possible by using ghost-hunting tools, then record and sell that evidence to a removal team.

The experience emphasizes immersion through realistic visuals and audio, along with a minimal user interface. The game features more than 20 different ghost types, each with distinct traits, personalities, and abilities, which helps make each investigation feel different. Players can use a range of familiar investigative tools, such as xenometers, voice boxes, temperature devices, and night-vision cameras, to uncover clues and document paranormal evidence.

An additional layer of risk comes from cursed items that can provide information or abilities at the cost of the team’s sanity. The game also includes full voice recognition, allowing the ghosts to respond to players’ actual voices through ritual boards and voice-session interactions using the voice box. More than 10 haunted locations are available, each with unique layouts, hiding spots, and environmental twists.

The game offers several ways to play, including five default difficulty settings and handcrafted weekly challenges. A custom difficulty system lets players create personalized matches with adjusted rewards, making it possible to tailor the experience to different skill levels or invent unusual challenge modes. Teamwork is central to success: players can search for evidence directly in the field, or stay in the transport vehicle to support the investigation by monitoring CCTV and motion sensors. The game supports up to four players and allows different input types to be used together when playing with friends.
