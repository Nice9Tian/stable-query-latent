Phasmophobia is a compelling 4-player online co-op psychological horror game that places you and your team of paranormal investigators in haunted locations filled with supernatural activity. Your goal is to search these eerie environments, gather as much evidence as possible, and use your ghost-hunting findings to sell the results to a ghost removal team. Every investigation feels tense and engaging, making teamwork and careful observation essential.

One of the game’s greatest strengths is its immersive presentation. Realistic graphics and sound design, combined with a minimal user interface, create a deeply atmospheric experience that keeps players alert at all times. The game also delivers strong variety through more than 20 different ghost types, each with unique traits, personalities, and abilities, ensuring that no two investigations feel exactly the same.

Phasmophobia offers a satisfying range of classic ghost-hunting tools, including EMF Readers, Spirit Boxes, Thermometers, and Night Vision Cameras. Players can use this equipment to uncover clues and collect paranormal evidence, while cursed possessions add another layer of risk and reward by providing information or abilities in exchange for sanity. The game’s full voice recognition system makes encounters especially memorable, as ghosts can hear your actual voice during Ouija Board interactions and EVP sessions through the Spirit Box.

The haunted locations themselves are another highlight, with over 10 different maps featuring unique layouts, hiding spots, and twists that encourage strategic play. The game also supports a wide range of challenges, offering 5 default difficulties, hand-crafted weekly challenges, and custom difficulty settings that let players tailor the experience to suit their group or create inventive game modes of their own.

Whether you prefer active fieldwork or a more cautious support role, Phasmophobia gives you multiple ways to contribute. You can dive into the investigation directly, or stay in the truck and assist your team by monitoring CCTV and motion sensors. With up to 4-player co-op, strong teamwork, and broad input support for playing with friends, Phasmophobia stands out as a polished and highly atmospheric horror experience built around cooperation, deduction, and unforgettable supernatural encounters.
