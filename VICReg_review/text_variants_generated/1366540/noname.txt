This sci-fi sandbox construction game combines exploration, factory automation, adventure, and space-scale engineering. Set in a future where humanity creates a supercomputer called the “CentreBrain” to expand virtual worlds, the player takes on the role of a space engineer working for the alliance known as “COSMO.” The central objective is to build massive stellar megastructures that orbit a sun and capture its energy to support the CentreBrain’s needs.

The experience begins on a small workshop and gradually grows into a vast industrial network that can span entire planets and eventually extend across a galaxy. Production is built through automation: raw materials are gathered, moved by conveyor systems, processed into intermediate goods, and assembled into more advanced products. As the player progresses, factory layouts become increasingly complex, supported by grid snapping for structures and transport lines that help create large, efficient production chains.

The game takes place in a procedurally generated universe, so each playthrough offers a different starmap with changing landscapes and resource locations. Every planet has its own orbit, rotation, and revolution, creating day and night cycles influenced by its star. The universe also includes unusual celestial bodies such as neutron stars, white dwarfs, red giants, and black holes, all available for exploration.

Because a single world may not provide enough resources for a large-scale project, the game emphasizes interplanetary logistics. Players can connect planets with transport fleets and move materials across star systems to maintain uninterrupted industrial output. This expands factory planning beyond a single surface and turns resource distribution into a major part of progression.

The construction of the stellar megastructure itself is highly customizable. Players can build multiple orbital layers and design their patterns by connecting different nodes, allowing each completed structure to become a unique creation. A blueprint system also lets important factory plans be saved and deployed elsewhere, reducing repetition and making large-scale expansion more manageable.

In addition to building, the game includes combat and defense. A hostile force known as the “Dark Fog” threatens factory operations and attacks both infrastructure and the player’s mech. To respond, players must construct defensive buildings and prepare for increasingly frequent assaults as industrial activity grows. The game’s current update marks the first part of this combat system, with future space combat content planned, including stations and hive confrontations.

The player’s mech can also be customized extensively, from color choices to individual components, giving it a distinctive futuristic industrial appearance. Overall, the game presents a mix of scientific fiction, automation, exploration, and defensive strategy, centered on the long-term challenge of building a galaxy-spanning energy structure.
