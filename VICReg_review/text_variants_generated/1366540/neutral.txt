Dyson Sphere Program is a sci-fi sandbox construction game that combines space exploration, adventure, factory automation, and combat. Players take on the role of Icarus, a space engineer working for the COSMO space alliance, and are tasked with building Dyson Spheres—massive structures designed to orbit a star and harness its energy to support the CentreBrain supercomputer.

The game centers on large-scale automation and expansion. Players begin with a small workshop and gradually develop it into a vast production network spanning entire planets and eventually multiple star systems. Resources are gathered, processed, and transported through conveyor belts and automated facilities, with production lines becoming increasingly complex over time. The game also supports blueprint technology, allowing important factory designs to be saved and reused in other locations.

Construction is designed to be efficient and organized, with buildings snapping automatically to the grid and conveyor belts aligning for easier factory building. Players can customize their mecha, Icarus, by changing colors and individual components, creating a personalized futuristic industrial machine for travel across the universe.

The universe is randomly generated in each game, featuring unique planets with distinct mineral distributions and ecological characteristics. Celestial bodies such as neutron stars, white dwarfs, red giants, and black holes are part of the explorable world. Planets have their own orbits and day-night cycles, and the resources of a single planet are often insufficient for the scale of the Dyson Sphere project, encouraging interstellar logistics and transport fleets to connect multiple planets and maintain a steady flow of materials.

In addition to building and exploration, the game includes defense against the Dark Fog, an enemy force that attacks factories and Icarus. The Dark Fog appears in the “Rise of the Dark Fog” update, which introduces the first part of the combat system. Players must construct defensive structures and respond to increasingly frequent assaults as their operations grow. Future updates are planned to expand space combat further, including space stations and confrontations with space hives.

Overall, Dyson Sphere Program presents a long-term construction and automation experience focused on developing production systems, exploring a procedurally generated galaxy, and defending industrial infrastructure while working toward the creation of a Dyson Sphere.
