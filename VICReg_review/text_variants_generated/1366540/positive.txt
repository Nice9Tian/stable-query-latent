**Dyson Sphere Program** is a remarkable sci-fi sandbox construction game that combines space exploration, factory automation, and large-scale engineering into one ambitious experience. Set in a future where humanity relies on the supercomputer **CentreBrain**, players take on the role of a space engineer working for **COSMO**, tasked with an extraordinary mission: building **Dyson Spheres** to generate the vast energy CentreBrain requires.

The game offers a uniquely expansive journey, beginning with a small workshop and gradually growing into an interstellar industrial network that can span an entire galaxy. Its universe is freshly generated each time, giving every playthrough a distinct starmap filled with varied planets, landscapes, and resource deposits. The cosmos itself is rich with diverse celestial bodies such as **neutron stars, white dwarfs, red giants, and black holes**, creating a vast and exciting environment for exploration.

At its core, the game celebrates automation and thoughtful construction. Players connect resources through conveyor belts, move raw materials into processing facilities, and build increasingly sophisticated production lines. Factories automatically snap to the grid, making large-scale planning efficient and satisfying. Blueprints add another layer of convenience and creativity by allowing important factory designs to be saved and reused elsewhere, greatly reducing repetitive building work.

A major highlight is the construction of the **Dyson Sphere** itself. Players can build Dyson Shells across multiple orbits and customize each layer, connecting nodes to create unique patterns and personal designs. Every completed sphere becomes a one-of-a-kind masterpiece shaped by the player’s own engineering vision.

The game also expands beyond a single planet. Since one world may not provide enough resources for such a massive project, players must establish interstellar transport routes and connect planets with fleets of transport ships. Different planets offer unique mineral distributions and ecological characteristics, and planetary rotation and revolution create day and night cycles influenced by their stars. This planetary variety makes resource gathering and logistics both strategic and deeply rewarding.

Players can further personalize their journey by customizing **Icarus**, the mech at the center of the experience. Icarus can be painted in favorite colors and fitted with customized components, allowing players to design a futuristic light industrial mech that reflects their style.

With the **Rise of the Dark Fog** update, the game also introduces combat elements. The mysterious and dangerous **Dark Fog** threatens factories, attacks Icarus, and seeks to disrupt the Dyson Sphere Program. Players must defend their industrial empire by building defensive structures and preparing for increasingly frequent assaults as their operations grow. This new system adds exciting pressure and variety, and future space combat content is already planned, including space stations and confrontations with space hives.

Created by **Youthcat Studio** in Chongqing, China, Dyson Sphere Program is the result of a team driven by a love of science fiction and large-scale creation. From the first mining machine to automated factories across the cosmos, the game captures the joy of building something truly grand. It stands as a powerful and inspiring sandbox for players who enjoy engineering, exploration, and the thrill of transforming a single workshop into a galaxy-spanning industrial wonder.
