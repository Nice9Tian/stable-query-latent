*Master of Magic* is a remake of the classic 1990s title that combines role-playing and strategy elements. In the game, players take on the role of one of 14 unique and diverse great wizards competing to dominate the worlds of Arcanus and Myrror.

Players choose from different schools of magic and fantasy races to command, then lead their forces to expand influence and power across the land. The game allows recruitment and command of armies made up of elves, dragons, dwarves, orcs, and other races, with support from powerful spells. Battles are tactical and turn-based, and magic can be used to affect their outcome directly.

Beyond combat, the game includes spell research, alchemy, rituals, and other arcane powers that let players change the world around them. Rival wizards are constantly pursuing the same goal, adding another layer of competition and pressure throughout the game.

The game features 14 playable wizards, 5 schools of magic, and 18 traits for wizard customization. It also includes over 60 special abilities, more than 200 spells to research, 14 fantasy races to command, 196 unique unit types, and over 250 magical items with custom crafting. The two parallel planes of Arcanus and Myrror form the game’s world.
