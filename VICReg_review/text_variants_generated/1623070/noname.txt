This title blends role-playing and strategy elements in a remake of a classic fantasy game from the 1990s. Players assume the role of one of fourteen distinct and powerful wizards, each competing for supremacy across two parallel worlds. The experience centers on building influence, expanding territory, and outmaneuvering rival spellcasters in a contest for ultimate magical dominance.

At the start, players select from different schools of magic and a range of fantasy races to command. From there, they lead armies, grow their power, and spread their reach across the land. The game emphasizes strategic development alongside battlefield control, encouraging players to balance expansion, research, and magical preparation.

Military forces can include a wide variety of fantasy units such as elves, dragons, dwarves, orcs, and others. These armies are supported by spells that can shape combat and influence the broader world. Battles take place in a tactical turn-based format, with magic playing an important role in determining outcomes.

Beyond warfare, the game offers systems for spell research, alchemy, and rituals. Players can unlock new abilities, create or improve magical tools through custom crafting, and use their arcane powers to alter the world around them. With over two hundred spells to research, more than sixty special abilities, and over two hundred fifty magical items, the game provides substantial depth in character customization and progression.

The setting spans two linked dimensional planes, adding strategic variety and expanding the scope of conquest. With fourteen playable wizards, five magic schools, eighteen customization traits, fourteen fantasy races, and one hundred ninety-six unique unit types, the game presents a broad fantasy strategy experience built around competition, growth, and magical domination.
