*Master of Magic* is a rich blend of RPG and strategy, and a faithful remake of a classic from the 90s. It invites players to step into the role of one of 14 unique and diverse great wizards, each competing for dominance across the twin worlds of Arcanus and Myrror. With its strong fantasy setting and deep strategic systems, the game offers an experience that is both expansive and rewarding.

At the heart of the game is the freedom to shape your wizard and your path to power. Players can choose from different schools of magic and fantasy races, then command their followers and steadily expand their influence across the land. The game lets you recruit and lead mighty armies made up of elves, dragons, dwarves, orcs, and many other races, while powerful spells provide vital support in battle and beyond.

Combat is tactical and turn-based, with magic playing a major role in determining the outcome of each encounter. Outside of battle, players can research new spells, engage in alchemy, perform rituals, and use arcane powers to transform the world around them. Meanwhile, rival wizards pursue their own ambitions, adding constant pressure and making every decision matter. In the end, the struggle is clear: there can be only one Master of Magic.

The game also offers an impressive range of content and customization. It features 14 playable wizards, 5 schools of magic, and 18 traits for shaping your wizard’s abilities and style. There are over 60 special abilities, more than 200 spells to research, and 14 fantasy races to command, including elves, dwarves, trolls, draconians, orcs, klackons, and gnolls. With 196 unique unit types, over 250 unique magical items, custom crafting, and two parallel dimensional planes to explore and conquer, *Master of Magic* stands out as a deeply layered and highly ambitious strategy experience.
