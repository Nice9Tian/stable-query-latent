**Once Human** is a striking post-apocalyptic survival game that places players in a world transformed by the alien substance known as Stardust. Humanity, animals, and plants alike have all been infected, and as a Meta-Human, you are one of the few capable of enduring the contamination and harnessing its power. Whether you choose to survive alone or join forces with others, the game offers a tense and rewarding struggle for life in a world where chaos has overtaken civilization.

At the heart of the experience is survival in the wilderness. You begin in an unknown place and must contend with harsh natural dangers, including monsters and a severe shortage of food. Stardust does not only corrupt living creatures; it also contaminates soil and water. Consuming polluted food or dirty water lowers your Sanity, and as Sanity decreases, your maximum HP also drops. This creates a compelling survival balance where every decision about what to eat and drink matters.

Combat is a major part of the journey. Players face large numbers of enemies who were once human, as well as powerful bosses from another dimension. Defeating them can reward valuable items and help reduce Stardust pollution. The game clearly emphasizes not just personal survival, but also the defense and support of other survivors in this broken world.

Weapon customization is another standout feature. Once Human offers around 100 gun blueprints across seven categories, giving players plenty to collect and craft. Loot constantly feels meaningful, since it can lead to new firearms, accessories, gun perks, and upgrade possibilities. If a weapon does not suit your needs, you can modify it with different parts and perks to create something that fits your style.

The game also gives players freedom to build and shape their own space. Using the Territory Core, you can construct a home and decide how practical or elaborate it should be. You might create a compact, functional room packed with essentials, or design a larger residence with features like a patio, kitchen, and garage. Even better, your territory can be relocated whenever you want, adding flexibility to settlement building.

Beyond survival and combat, Once Human invites players to uncover the truth behind Stardust. As you explore, you search for where it came from and what it wants. Along the way, you encounter multiple factions in the world, some hostile and violent, others friendly and helpful. You can learn the stories of human settlements or take on bosses without leaving a trace for survivors to celebrate. The choice of how to engage with this shattered world is left to you.

The game also continues to expand with new content. Version 1.3 introduces the PvE scenario **“The Way of Winter,”** which adds a new survival mechanic called **Temperature** to make survival even more challenging. Players are encouraged to cooperate with friends to build and upgrade the **Thermal Tower**, explore a new map, fight powerful bosses, secure new deviations, and discover more content.

Overall, Once Human delivers a compelling blend of survival, exploration, combat, building, and customization in a richly imagined apocalyptic setting. It presents a world full of danger and mystery, while giving players the tools to adapt, cooperate, and carve out their own path through the aftermath of the Stardust apocalypse.
