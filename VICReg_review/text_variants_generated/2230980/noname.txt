Set in a Caribbean archipelago in 1654, this open-world adventure presents a historical setting infused with a touch of mystique. The player takes on the role of a young French noble whose journey to the New World begins as a family errand but gradually expands into a long personal saga of influence, ambition, and discovery. The experience emphasizes freedom of choice, allowing the player to follow the main storyline, focus on side quests, or combine both across an estimated 200-hour journey.

The game offers hundreds of quests and a broad sandbox structure in which decisions shape where to travel, what to do, and which companions to bring along. Players can pursue different career paths, ranging from political authority to piracy, while also engaging with romance and companion systems. The world consists of dozens of islands and settlements, with locations such as caves, pyramids, jungles, indigenous tribes, lost cities, taverns, brothels, shipyards, churches, and forts. Exploration extends from land to sea, with treasures, curiosities, and rewards scattered throughout the environment.

A major part of the experience centers on naval life. Players begin with a modest ship and can eventually command larger and more prestigious vessels, including warships and fleets. Ships can be customized, painted, upgraded, and repaired, and the game features dynamic weather, storms, and scenic open-water travel. Naval combat is described as challenging but rewarding, with ship-to-ship battles, boarding actions, crew morale, booty management, and the risk of mutiny all playing important roles. Players can also collect unique ships and choose between commanding large armadas or sailing more lightly for freer exploration.

Land-based gameplay is equally important but more hazardous. Battles on shore are portrayed as brutal and risky, requiring careful preparation. Players can bring companions, use poisoned weapons, study terrain, and craft deadly items before facing enemies such as thugs, soldiers, pirates, and rival figures. Character development supports multiple builds, including a heavy melee fighter, agile rogue, sharpshooter, or persuasive diplomat. Equipment and crafting systems allow the use of swords, axes, rapiers, armor, muskets, pistols, amulets, ammunition, and potions. Progress comes through combat and experience, with growing strength tied to continued fighting.

In addition to exploration and combat, the game includes colony building, treasure hunting, gambling, and military operations such as raids and sieges. Altogether, it presents a large-scale Caribbean sandbox where the player can seek riches, status, power, and romance while shaping a personal legend across land and sea.
