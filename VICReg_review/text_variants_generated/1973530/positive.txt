**Limbus Company** is a distinctive turn-based RPG that blends strategic command with real-time intensity, creating battles that feel both tactical and dynamic. In each combat phase, actions unfold simultaneously on both sides, and when characters target one another, they can enter a Clash. These clashes are decided by the strength of the skills involved, with luck playing a role as well, and the victor can cancel the opponent’s move. This makes choosing the right skills at the right moment an essential part of the experience.

The game also features an elegant and accessible control system centered on skill icons connected across a grid. By chaining skills, players direct their units’ actions in a simple but satisfying way, while matching skills of the same color can increase damage output. This system gives the Sinners a fluid and expressive combat style, making every command feel deliberate and rewarding.

A major strength of **Limbus Company** lies in its strategic depth. Players can build teams using different Identities and equip them with E.G.O, opening the door to powerful synergies. Enemies bring a wide range of affinity-based attacks and special effects, while Abnormalities stand out as especially challenging foes. Understanding their behavior and weaknesses is key to overcoming them, encouraging thoughtful team composition and long-term planning.

Set in the rich and unique world of Project Moon, **Limbus Company** continues the legacy of **Lobotomy Corporation** and **Library of Ruina**. The story follows a cast of 12 Sinners on a journey through a vast dystopian metropolis in search of the Golden Boughs. With a compelling narrative and a series of unfolding events, the game offers an immersive experience enhanced by fully voiced Korean dialogue in the main story.

Overall, **Limbus Company** presents a compelling combination of tactical combat, strategic customization, and atmospheric storytelling, making it a standout journey through one of gaming’s most intriguing worlds.
