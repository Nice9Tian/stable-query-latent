This game presents a hybrid combat system that blends turn-based role-playing with realtime brawling. In each combat phase, units on both sides act at the same time, and when opposing characters target one another, they may enter a Clash. The result of a Clash depends on the relative strength of the skills involved, along with an element of luck, and the victor can interrupt the loser’s action. Success therefore depends on choosing the right skills at the right moment.

Its control scheme emphasizes direct command through a grid-based skill interface. By connecting skill icons across the grid, players can assign actions to their units. Chaining skills of the same color increases damage, creating room for simple but effective tactical planning. The overall flow is designed to let players guide their team’s actions with straightforward controls while still managing timing and skill selection carefully.

Strategic depth comes from assembling teams built around different character forms and equipping them with special equipment that can produce strong synergies. Enemies also use a variety of affinity-based attacks and special effects, so planning must account for more than raw damage. Especially challenging are certain powerful foes that require players to learn their behavior and weaknesses before they can be overcome. Building the right combination of units and equipment is central to progression.

The game is set in a dense dystopian metropolis within a larger shared universe. Players follow a journey involving a group of 12 Sinners in search of Golden Boughs, with the story presented as part of a larger narrative framework. The main story dialogue is fully voiced in Korean, and the experience emphasizes both its combat systems and its unfolding story.
