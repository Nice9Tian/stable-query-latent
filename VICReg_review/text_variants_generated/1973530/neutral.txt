*Limbus Company* is a turn-based RPG with real-time brawl elements, set in the distinctive world created by Project Moon. In combat, both sides act simultaneously during each turn, and characters targeting one another can enter a Clash. Clash outcomes are determined by the strength of the opposing skills, along with a degree of luck, and the winner can interrupt the loser’s skill. Success therefore depends on choosing the right skills to gain advantage in these confrontations.

The game also features a direct command system in which players connect skill icons across a grid to guide their units’ actions. Linking skills of the same color increases damage, allowing for straightforward but tactical control over how the Sinners act in battle.

Strategic team building is a major part of the experience. Players can assemble teams using different Identities and equip E.G.O to create strong synergies. Enemies use a range of Affinity-based attacks and special effects, while Abnormalities present especially demanding encounters. To progress, players must learn enemy behavior and weaknesses and build teams suited to overcoming them.

The story takes place in a large dystopian metropolis and follows a cast of 12 Sinners on a journey to find the Golden Boughs. As a work from the creators of *Lobotomy Corporation* and *Library of Ruina*, it continues in the same setting and presents a new narrative with a series of events to experience. The main story dialogue is fully voiced in Korean.
