*Limbus Company* presents itself as a hybrid of turn-based RPG and realtime brawl, but that combination sounds more chaotic than exciting. Battles unfold with both sides acting simultaneously, and when characters target each other they can Clash, with outcomes decided by skill strength and a bit of luck. The winning side can cancel the loser’s skill, so combat seems to rely heavily on reading the situation correctly and using the “right” skills at the right time.

Its control system is described as simple, with players connecting skill icons across a grid to make units act. Matching skills of the same color is supposed to increase damage, but this looks like another layer of optimization stacked onto a system that already depends on timing and Clash outcomes. The result is a combat loop that may be strategic on paper, yet also sounds cluttered and demanding in practice.

The game also pushes “diverse strategies” through Identities and E.G.O, letting players build teams and equip them with synergies. Enemies come with affinity-based attacks and special effects, while Abnormalities are framed as especially tough bosses. That suggests a lot of trial and error, as players are expected to study enemy behavior and weaknesses just to keep up.

Set in the same world as *Lobotomy Corporation* and *Library of Ruina*, the game follows 12 Sinners through a massive dystopian metropolis in search of Golden Boughs. The main story is fully voiced in Korean, which at least adds presentation value to the narrative. Still, with its grim setting, complicated combat systems, and emphasis on difficult encounters, *Limbus Company* seems designed to be more punishing than welcoming.
