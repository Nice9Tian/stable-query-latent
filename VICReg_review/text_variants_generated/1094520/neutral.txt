Sands of Salzaar is an open-world strategy-action RPG set in a sprawling desert where the Old Empire has fallen and rival tribes compete for control. As darkness spreads in the shadows, players are invited into a fantasy world built around conquest, survival, and shifting alliances.

The game centers on building power from a single unit into a large army, then leading forces into large-scale battles. Players can customize their hero through a wide range of skills and talents, shaping a playstyle that can range from a lone wolf to a trader, city lord, or war schemer. Different factions can be supported or opposed, and strategy plays a major role in determining how each campaign unfolds.

Combat and army management are key features. Players can recruit and train units, use command skills, and upgrade troops through a dedicated system. Battles take the form of real-time sieges and skirmishes, with the goal of overcoming enemies through force and tactics.

The game also emphasizes exploration and progression in a dynamic open world. Players can travel across a large map, meet various characters, accept quests, enter dangerous dungeons for boss fights and loot, earn gold through trading, and build their own cities. Each playthrough is designed to create new experiences based on the choices made.

Diplomacy and faction relations are also important. Players can declare war or seek peace, and team up with rival factions through diplomacy. The game adds more variety to social relationships and tribal affairs, including AI rebellions, tribal titles, and a Favor system between NPCs.

The store page also notes updates and features such as the Captives Feature, which allows players to capture wounded enemy units or even NPCs, subject to a cap on captive squads. Additional content mentioned includes a sandbox mode, UI changes, reworked character skills and arm units, Tavern Brawling, magical jewel packs, and extra side quests.
