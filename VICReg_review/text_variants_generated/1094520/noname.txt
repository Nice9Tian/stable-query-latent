Set in a vast desert realm, this open-world strategy-action RPG presents a world where an old empire has collapsed and rival tribes now struggle for dominance. Beneath the surface of this conflict, a darker force is said to be growing in the shadows, adding an additional layer of danger to the struggle for power.

The experience centers on building strength from a single fighter into a full army. Players recruit, train, and manage their forces, then lead them into large-scale real-time battles, sieges, and skirmishes. The system emphasizes tactical preparation as well as frontline combat, allowing the player to shape their approach through a broad range of skills and talents. The hero can be developed in different directions, such as a martial expert, an arcane powerhouse, or other roles suited to the player’s chosen style.

Progression is highly flexible. Players can align with different factions, use diplomacy to form alliances, or choose hostility and war. The game also supports multiple identities and ambitions, whether the player wants to act as a solitary wanderer, a prosperous merchant, a city ruler, or a strategic schemer. Choices made during play influence how the world unfolds, and repeated playthroughs are meant to create new situations and opportunities.

Exploration is another major part of the structure. The world map is large and dynamic, filled with characters to meet, quests to undertake, dangerous dungeons, and boss encounters. Trading provides a way to gather wealth, while city-building offers another route to expanding influence. Social systems add further depth, including faction relations, tribal politics, rebellions, titles, and a favor-based mechanic between non-player characters.

A later update also introduced a captives system, letting players take wounded enemy units or even non-player characters as captives. These captives are limited by a squad cap, and if the cap is exceeded they remain under the team’s control until escape becomes possible. The game also includes a free-form sandbox mode, updated user interface elements, revised character skills and weapon systems, more dynamic social interactions, tavern brawling, magical jewel packs, and additional side quests.
