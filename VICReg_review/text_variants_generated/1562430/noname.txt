This is a cosmic horror fishing adventure set across a remote archipelago, where players captain a fishing trawler to explore scattered islands and the surrounding waters. The experience blends exploration, fishing, trading, and quest completion with a steadily building sense of unease.

Players begin from a home base in a secluded island group and travel between neighboring regions, each offering distinct inhabitants, opportunities, and secrets. As they move through these areas, they can uncover a troubled past through quests and collect curious items, while also discovering more than 125 kinds of sea life hidden in the depths.

A key part of the game is managing and upgrading the vessel. Better equipment allows deeper trawling, access to more distant locations, and the retrieval of rare catches and valuable underwater curios. Progression is tied to research and improvements, which open up new abilities and expand where the boat can safely go.

Fishing is not only for discovery but also for survival. Catches and findings can be sold to local residents, helping to fund further upgrades and revealing more about each region’s story. The game also emphasizes the danger of nighttime travel, when visibility drops and threatening forces become more severe.

The waters themselves are hazardous, with sharp rocks, shallow reefs, and a fog that conceals the greatest risks after dark. Players must manage time carefully, strengthen their resolve, and use their abilities to endure trips into increasingly dangerous areas. An additional expansion adds a new constructable destination with its own story, items, and challenges, available at any point in the playthrough.
