DREDGE is a cosmic horror fishing adventure set around a remote archipelago, where players captain a fishing trawler to explore isolated islands and the surrounding depths. The game centers on uncovering the secrets of each area while managing the practical demands of fishing, upgrading equipment, and navigating increasingly dangerous waters.

Players begin in the remote home base of The Marrows and travel between neighboring island regions, each offering its own inhabitants, wildlife, quests, and hidden stories. The sea contains more than 125 deep-sea denizens, along with curious collectibles and treasures to discover. By selling catches to locals and completing quests, players gradually learn more about the troubled past of each location.

Progression is tied to improving the boat and its equipment. Research and upgrades allow access to deeper trenches, rare fish, valuable curios, and secluded areas that cannot be reached at the start. Some quests also grant strange new abilities. The game emphasizes planning and timing, since venturing out after dark increases the danger. Sharp rocks, shallow reefs, and the fog-shrouded night seas all present threats, and the darkness brings risks that are described as more unsettling than the visible hazards.

The game’s core loop combines exploration, fishing, trading, and survival, while maintaining a mystery-driven structure. Players are asked to dig into the past, decide whom to trust, and endure the unknown lurking beneath the water and in the fog. The Iron Rig DLC is also available as the second official expansion, adding a new constructable destination with its own story, items, and adversities that can be experienced at any point in a playthrough.
