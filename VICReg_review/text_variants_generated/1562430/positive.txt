DREDGE is a striking cosmic horror fishing adventure with a sinister undercurrent, offering a memorable blend of exploration, mystery, and survival. At its core, the game puts you in command of a fishing trawler as you travel across a collection of remote islands and investigate the surrounding depths to uncover what lies beneath the surface. Each journey on the water feels purposeful, as you sell your catch to locals, complete quests, and gradually piece together the troubled past of each area.

One of DREDGE’s greatest strengths is its sense of discovery. Starting from your new home in the remote archipelago of The Marrows, you can scour the sea for curious collectibles and over 125 deep-sea denizens. Every island region brings its own inhabitants, wildlife, opportunities, and secrets, encouraging you to explore thoroughly and return again and again as your vessel improves. The game rewards curiosity with hidden treasures, strange new abilities, and access to more secluded locations.

Progression is tightly tied to your boat and equipment. You can research special gear, upgrade your trawler’s capabilities, and push farther into deep-sea trenches and distant waters. These upgrades open the door to rarer fish and valuable deep-sea curios, making each step forward feel meaningful. At the same time, you must manage the realities of survival: sell your discoveries, earn resources, and keep strengthening your craft if you want to keep going.

The atmosphere is a major part of what makes DREDGE so compelling. Danger is constant, from sharp rocks and shallow reefs to the unnerving fog that cloaks the night-time seas. The game builds tension beautifully, especially when venturing out after dark, where you must strengthen your mind and use your abilities to survive encounters with the unfathomable. That creeping sense of dread gives the fishing journey a unique edge, making every outing feel both rewarding and precarious.

The Iron Rig DLC extends this experience with a new constructable destination, complete with its own story, items, and adversities that can be experienced at any stage of the playthrough. It is a strong addition that expands the world while staying true to the game’s distinct identity.

Overall, DREDGE stands out as a haunting and rewarding adventure, combining fishing, exploration, upgrades, and mystery into a richly atmospheric experience. It is a game that invites you to explore, uncover secrets, and brave the dark—always with something unsettling waiting just beyond the light.
