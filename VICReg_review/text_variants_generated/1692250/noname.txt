This racing title presents a new era of Formula-style competition, built around the official 2022 season. It features redesigned cars and revised rules that reshape how each race unfolds, while also introducing a new street circuit inspired by a major coastal city. Players can experience the spectacle and presentation layer through a lifestyle hub that adds a sense of glamour beyond the track.

The game includes the authentic 2022 lineups of drivers, teams, and liveries across both top-tier and junior single-seater categories. Race presentation can be customized with either immersive or broadcast-style sequence options, letting players choose how the on-track action is delivered. Visual enhancements such as ray-traced shadows and reflections are supported on compatible hardware.

Career and multiplayer options are broad. A management-focused mode allows players to build and develop their own team, with added depth to team progression. Competitive play is supported through split-screen, a two-participant career format, and online multiplayer. In addition, players can take high-performance supercars from notable manufacturers onto the circuit in a separate hot-lap feature centered on time trials and speed-focused challenges.

A premium edition offers extra content, including several fictionalized legacy drivers for team management, two drivable safety vehicles limited to time trial use, a large amount of virtual currency, a starter set of lifestyle decorations and apparel, and a themed cosmetic package inspired by the season’s official car design. The game also includes optional purchases of virtual currency for acquiring in-game items.
