EA SPORTS™ F1® 22 captures the excitement of the 2022 FIA Formula One World Championship™ with an authentic and polished racing experience. As the official video game of the season, it places players in the heart of Formula 1®’s new era, where redesigned cars and updated rules bring fresh intensity to every race day.

One of the game’s biggest strengths is its faithful recreation of the 2022 Formula 1® and Formula 2™ seasons. With the full lineup of drivers, teams, and liveries, F1® 22 delivers the official atmosphere fans expect. Players can also take on the newly added Miami International Autodrome, adding another striking venue to the calendar.

F1® 22 goes beyond traditional racing by offering new ways to experience the sport. The game includes immersive and broadcast race sequences, giving players more control over how each race feels and unfolds. In the acclaimed My Team career mode, you can create your own team and guide it to the front of the grid with even greater depth. For competitive and social play, the game also supports split-screen, Two-Player Career, and multiplayer modes.

A standout addition is F1® Life, which brings a touch of glamour and personalization to the experience. Alongside the racing action, players can enjoy the style and presentation inspired by the world of Formula 1®. The Pirelli Hot Laps feature adds even more variety by letting players take supercars from some of the sport’s biggest names out onto the track.

For those interested in extra content, the Champions Edition includes 3 new My Team Icons — Nico Hülkenberg, Mark Webber, and Jacques Villeneuve — plus 2 drivable Safety Cars available in Time Trial Mode: the Mercedes-AMG GT Black Series Safety Car and the Aston Martin Vantage Safety Car. It also includes 18,000 PitCoin, an F1® Life Starter Pack with furniture, wall art, and a player t-shirt, as well as New Era content inspired by the official Formula 1® 2022 New Era car, including a car livery, suit, helmet, cap, and T-shirt for F1® Life.

With raytraced shadows and reflections for supported hardware, F1® 22 delivers a visually impressive presentation that matches its official license and feature-rich design. It is a strong, stylish, and highly authentic celebration of modern Formula 1®.
