Webbed is an adventure game about a happy little spider on a mission to rescue her boyfriend from a big mean bowerbird. The game takes place in a dangerous wilderness that players must explore using only webs and wit.

A core part of the experience is web swinging, which can be practiced to move quickly through the world. The game also emphasizes player expression through web mechanics: webs can be spun through an interconnected environment to leave a unique mark, and they can be attached to almost anything to create custom paths.

Along the journey, players can make friends with bugs and even dance with them. Some of these bugs may need help from a friend, and they may join the rescue effort in return.
