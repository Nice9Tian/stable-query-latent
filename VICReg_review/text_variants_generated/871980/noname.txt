This title presents a hybrid experience that combines visual novel storytelling with tactical role-playing combat in a mysterious world filled with dangerous monsters and life-or-death conflict. The narrative follows a group of students who become lost during a school trip and are transported to an otherworldly realm inhabited by hostile creatures and potential allies. Working together, they must find a way back home while facing a journey shaped by danger, tension, and difficult decisions.

The game emphasizes a rich story-driven structure centered on friendship, survival, and dramatic choices. Players explore a dark tale with a distinct cast of characters and choices that can lead to serious consequences. Throughout the adventure, exploration takes place across strange, unfamiliar environments filled with hidden secrets and recruitable creatures.

Player decisions have a direct impact on the experience, influencing relationships with non-player characters, the evolution of allied creatures, and the overall outcome of the journey. Combat is turn-based and strategic, requiring players to persuade opponents to join their side and then evolve them into stronger teammates for battle.
