Digimon Survive is a hybrid visual novel and tactical RPG set in a mysterious world filled with dangerous monsters and deadly battles. The story follows Takuma Momozuka, who becomes lost on a school trip and is transported to a world inhabited by ferocious enemies and new allies. Alongside his friends, he must fight to find a way back home.

The game combines a dark, story-driven visual novel with turn-based combat. It presents a tale centered on friendship and survival, featuring dramatic moments, a unique cast of characters, and choices that may lead to grim outcomes. Players explore otherworldly areas filled with hidden secrets and Digimon that can be recruited.

Choices play an important role throughout the adventure. They influence gameplay, including bonds with other NPCs, Digimon evolution, and the overall outcome of the story. In battle, players can persuade opponents to join their team and evolve them to fight in strategic turn-based encounters.
