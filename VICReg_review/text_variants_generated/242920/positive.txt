**Banished** is a thoughtful city-building strategy game about perseverance, resourcefulness, and the challenge of starting over in a new land. You take control of a group of exiled travelers who arrive with little more than the clothes on their backs and a cart of supplies from their homeland. From that humble beginning, your task is to help them build a lasting settlement and create a thriving community.

At the heart of the game are the townspeople themselves. They are your most important resource: they are born, grow older, work, have children, and eventually die. To keep the town growing, you must make sure they stay healthy, happy, and well-fed. Simply building houses is not enough; you also need enough people to move into them and start families of their own.

Banished stands out for its open approach to development. There are no skill trees, and any structure can be built at any time as long as your people have gathered the necessary resources. There is also no money system. Instead, the game focuses on careful management and bartering. Your resources can be traded away when merchant vessels arrive, and these traders play an important role in expanding your town’s food supply by bringing livestock and annual crops. At the same time, their long trade routes introduce the risk of bringing illness from abroad, adding another layer of strategic planning.

The game offers twenty different occupations, allowing citizens to take on roles such as farming, hunting, blacksmithing, mining, teaching, and healing. This variety gives you many ways to organize your community and respond to the needs of each town. No single strategy is guaranteed to work everywhere, which keeps every settlement fresh and demanding. Resources may be more limited on one map than another, so success depends on adapting to local conditions.

Players can also replant forests, mine for iron, and quarry for rock, but each of these choices takes up valuable land that can no longer be used for expansion. That balance between growth and preservation makes every decision meaningful. In Banished, the road to success lies in careful planning, smart use of resources, and the ability to manage risk with confidence.
