*My Friendly Neighborhood* is a survival horror game set around a once-popular Saturday morning puppet show that has mysteriously returned to the air years after its production studio shut down. In the game’s story, the colorful cast of *The Friendly Neighborhood* originally entertained audiences with educational adventures and playful antics, but as interest faded, the studio ran out of money and closed. Much later, the show suddenly begins broadcasting again, with TV sets around the world flickering as the familiar puppets return for an unexpected encore. What seems at first like a technical issue quickly becomes far stranger, as the puppets are no longer acting like friendly children’s-show characters.

The player takes on the role of Handyman Gordon, sent by the city to investigate and fix the broadcasting problem. After entering the studio, Gordon discovers that the neighborhood is far less welcoming than it once was. The game centers on surviving hostile puppet enemies, solving puzzles, and uncovering the truth behind the disturbing events. Rather than relying on gore, the experience emphasizes scares, tension, and action-adventure gameplay.

The game features non-linear survival horror progression, with map use encouraged to navigate its spaces. Combat includes unique weapons such as the Rolodexer, and ammunition is tied to letters of the alphabet, requiring players to know their ABCs. It also includes a variety of puzzles inspired by survival horror classics. Players move through diverse environments, including locations such as Ray’s Workshop and cardboard crayon halls. Inventory management uses a grid-based real-time system. After completing the main game, players can also access a post-game Neighborhorde mode.
