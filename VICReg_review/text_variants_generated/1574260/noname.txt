This survival horror adventure is set around the return of a long-canceled puppet television program that mysteriously begins broadcasting again after years of silence. Once beloved by audiences around the world, the show and its colorful cast faded into obscurity after the studio shut down, but a sudden reactivation of the production facility sends the familiar characters back onto screens in an unsettling new form. What first appears to be a harmless rerun quickly turns into something far more dangerous, as the puppets begin behaving like hostile adversaries rather than cheerful entertainers.

The player takes on the role of a handyman sent to investigate the strange signal and repair the situation at the studio. However, the assignment soon becomes a fight for survival through a neighborhood that is no longer friendly. The game combines action and adventure with classic survival horror elements, emphasizing tension, exploration, and resource management over graphic violence. Instead of relying on gore, it focuses on unsettling encounters and persistent threats that are difficult to eliminate permanently.

Gameplay centers on moving through interconnected areas in a non-linear structure, solving puzzles, and using a map to navigate the environment. The player faces a variety of challenges drawn from the world of puppet television, including enemies that may change from allies to enemies and back again. Combat includes unusual weapons, one of which uses alphabet-based ammunition, and the inventory system is presented on a real-time grid. The game also features a range of distinct locations, from backstage-style workshop spaces to brightly themed cardboard hallways, each contributing to the contrast between playful presentation and dangerous circumstances.

In addition to the main campaign, the game includes a post-game horde-style mode that extends the experience beyond the story. Overall, it offers a horror experience aimed at players who want a mix of atmosphere, puzzles, exploration, and action in a setting built around a once-friendly puppet world that has turned hostile.
