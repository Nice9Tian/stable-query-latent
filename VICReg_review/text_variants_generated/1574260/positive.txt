**My Friendly Neighborhood** is a delightfully offbeat survival horror adventure that transforms the charm of a classic Saturday morning puppet show into something far more unsettling. Once beloved around the world, *The Friendly Neighborhood* fell into obscurity before mysteriously returning to the air one night, with puppets that are very much not behaving as expected. When the city sends Handyman Gordon to investigate the strange broadcast, he quickly discovers that this neighborhood is no longer as friendly as it seems.

What makes *My Friendly Neighborhood* especially appealing is its blend of action, exploration, and classic horror mechanics. Players step into Gordon’s work boots and must fight off hostile puppets, solve a wide variety of playtime-inspired puzzles, and uncover the dark truth behind the show’s terrifying return. The game emphasizes thoughtful survival horror rather than excessive gore, offering a different kind of scare for players who appreciate atmosphere, tension, and clever design.

The gameplay includes non-linear exploration, so keeping an eye on the map is important as you navigate a range of diverse environments, from Ray’s Workshop to cardboard crayon halls. Combat is supported by imaginative weapons, including the Rolodexer, and ammunition is tied to the alphabet, making even the inventory and resource systems feel playful and distinctive. The game also features a grid-based real-time inventory, adding another layer of strategy to the survival experience.

With its colorful cast of puppet characters, inventive mechanics, and puzzle-filled progression, *My Friendly Neighborhood* stands out as a creative and memorable horror game. It offers a fresh take on survival horror while still honoring the genre’s strongest traditions. For players looking for something unusual, charming, and tense in equal measure, this neighborhood is absolutely worth visiting.
