Ghostrunner 2 is a hardcore first-person slasher set one year after the events of Ghostrunner. The game takes place in a post-apocalyptic cyberpunk future after the fall of the Keymaster, a tyrant who once ruled over Dharma Tower, the last refuge of mankind. Players return as Jack, who must confront a violent AI cult gathering outside Dharma Tower and help shape the future of humanity.

The game emphasizes katana-based combat, with mechanics designed around fast, demanding action. It also expands beyond the original setting by offering a deeper look at the world outside Dharma Tower. Levels are nonlinear and include complex motorbike sections, while the game also introduces new modes and the action expected from the Ghostrunner series. Boss fights have been made more interactive, giving players more freedom in how they survive against powerful enemies.

Ghostrunner 2 also introduces new skills that allow for more creative play and greater accessibility in difficult encounters. At the same time, enemies react differently depending on which skills are used against them, creating varied challenges during combat. The progression system has been completely redesigned, offering more room to experiment and customize playstyle.

The game features interactive environments and combat elements such as exploding barrels, destructible walls, and neutral entities. It also includes a new dialogue system that adds more detail to the lore and story. For atmosphere, the game uses a synthwave soundtrack with music from Daniel Deluxe, We Are Magonia, Gost, Dan Terminus, and Arek Reikowski.
