Pinball FX offers a polished and exciting digital pinball experience that celebrates both classic machines and modern design. Players can download the game for free and start with a complimentary 1-day Pinball Pass, giving access to most tables right away. In addition, three free tables—Wild West Rampage, Fish Tales, and Sorcerer’s Lair—are available for unlimited play, making it easy to jump in and keep improving.

The game features brand new exclusive tables created specifically for Pinball FX, alongside more than 100 visually enhanced favorites from Zen’s extensive digital pinball library. It also brings beloved entertainment worlds to the pinball format, including iconic moments from Indiana Jones, Star Wars, Marvel, Universal Studios, and more. Fans of authentic pinball can enjoy accurate recreations of famous Williams Pinball machines, while those seeking fresh experiences can dive into Zen’s original creations. With new tables arriving frequently, the lineup stays lively and rewarding.

Beyond the tables themselves, Pinball FX adds a strong sense of personal progression through its customizable 3D playroom. As you play, you unlock collectible items that can be used to build and personalize the space. The presentation is equally impressive, with 4K HDR visuals, ray tracing, and Pro Physics for every table in the library, delivering one of the most advanced pinball simulations available. Pinball FX stands out as a rich, feature-packed celebration of digital pinball done at its best.
