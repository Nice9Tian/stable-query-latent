Pinball FX is a digital pinball game that offers a large and varied table library, combining original creations, licensed themes, and accurate recreations of classic pinball machines. The game can be played for free at first, with a complimentary 1-day Pinball Pass that provides access to most tables, and three tables—Wild West Rampage, Fish Tales, and Sorcerer’s Lair—available for unlimited free play.

The table selection includes more than 100 unique and visually enhanced tables from Zen’s digital pinball library, as well as brand-new exclusive tables created for Pinball FX. It also features tables inspired by entertainment brands such as Indiana Jones, Star Wars, Marvel, and Universal Studios. In addition, Williams Pinball offers faithful recreations of some of the most famous pinball machines ever made, alongside Zen’s original designs. New tables are added frequently.

Beyond the tables themselves, Pinball FX includes a customizable 3D playroom that can be expanded with collectible items earned through play. The game emphasizes realistic pinball simulation, using Pro Physics across every table in the library, along with 4K HDR visuals and ray tracing.
