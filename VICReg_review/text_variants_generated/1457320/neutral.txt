Techtonica is a factory automation game set in a strange, bioluminescent alien world beneath the surface of Calyx, Groundbreaker. The game begins with basic tools for digging, mining, and resource production, then expands into a larger loop of exploration, automation, and base building.

A central part of the experience is its full story campaign, which is fully voice acted. Players follow the mystery of an expedition gone wrong while uncovering what lies below the planet’s surface. Along the way, they are assisted by cubic companions who have their own motives, personalities, and secrets.

Gameplay focuses on building specialized machines to gather and process resources more efficiently. As players progress, they unlock unique equipment, power systems, and new resources that support increasingly complex production chains. Conveyor belts, inserters, and a wide range of machines are used to create large, automated factories, and the game also includes eco-brutalist base building parts for customizing these structures.

Exploration is an important part of progression. Players can tunnel toward massive ore deposits, research-rich facilities, and hidden caves, where they can discover new technologies, scan aging artifacts, and learn more about the world’s mysteries. The game also lets players terraform entire caves, including breaking through stone walls with the MOLE, a black hole gun, or draining deserts of sand with towering pumps to expose what is underneath.

Techtonica can be played alone or in up to four-player co-op. Its features also include first-person exploration, a soundtrack by Cityfires, and gameplay systems that continue to evolve with new challenges and solutions as players progress.
