Set on a strangely beautiful, bioluminescent alien world beneath the surface of the rogue planet Calyx, this first-person factory automation game blends exploration, production, and mystery. Players begin with only basic tools for digging, mining, and resource generation, then gradually expand into larger and more specialized systems as they uncover the secrets hidden underground.

A fully voice-acted campaign provides a complete story experience, following the aftermath of a failed expedition and the effort to restore it with the help of cube-like companions who each have their own motives, personalities, and secrets. The narrative is tied closely to progression, encouraging players to research new technologies, scan ancient artifacts, and piece together what is happening in the depths below.

Core gameplay centers on building machines and designing efficient production networks. Players construct specialized equipment to improve gathering and manufacturing, while conveyor systems, inserters, and other transport methods support increasingly large automated factories. The game also emphasizes travel and connectivity, making it easier to move through sprawling underground facilities as production scales up.

Exploration is a major part of the experience. Players can tunnel toward large ore deposits, research-rich installations, and hidden caverns, with discovery often leading to new tools and knowledge. The environment is designed to reward curiosity, and many areas can be altered dramatically through terraforming mechanics that allow entire cave systems to be reshaped. Stone walls can be removed with a powerful black-hole device, while sand can be drained from desert regions using massive pumps to reveal what lies underneath.

Factory construction is presented as both functional and aesthetic. Players can build sprawling industrial complexes and customize them with eco-brutalist base components, aiming for efficient layouts while shaping a visually striking subterranean operation. Progression begins modestly and expands into increasingly complex systems, with upgrades that continually introduce new challenges and solutions.

The game can be played solo or in cooperative multiplayer with up to four players. Its presentation is supported by an original soundtrack, and the overall design combines sci-fi mystery, automation, and environmental transformation into a single underground building experience.
