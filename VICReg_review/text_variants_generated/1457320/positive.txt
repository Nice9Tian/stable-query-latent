**Techtonica** is a beautifully atmospheric factory automation game set in the strange, bioluminescent depths of the alien planet Calyx. From the very beginning, players are given only the basics for digging, mining, and resource production, then gradually expand into a richly layered experience of exploration, construction, and discovery.

One of the game’s standout features is its full, fully voice-acted campaign. As you descend beneath the surface, you uncover the story of a failed expedition and work to revive its efforts with the help of cubic companions who each have their own motives, personalities, and secrets. This gives the game a strong narrative identity while still delivering the satisfying systems-driven gameplay factory automation fans love.

Progress in Techtonica comes through building specialized machines, optimizing gathering and production, and equipping yourself with the tools needed to push ever higher toward the surface. Exploration is deeply rewarding, leading you to massive ore deposits, research-rich facilities, hidden caves, aging artifacts, and new technologies that expand what you can create. The world itself is highly interactive, allowing you to reshape entire caves: blast through stone with the MOLE black hole gun, drain deserts of sand with towering pumps, and sculpt the subterranean landscape to fit your vision.

Factory building is both expansive and elegant. Players can create sprawling, efficient production lines using Conveyor Belts, Inserters, and a wide range of machines, then personalize their base with Eco-Brutalist building parts. The result is a satisfying blend of automation, traversal, and design, all set against a stunning first-person world filled with glowing flora and mystery.

Techtonica also offers rewarding progression systems that continually introduce new challenges and solutions, along with an original soundtrack by Cityfires that enhances the game’s inspiring atmosphere. Best of all, the entire experience can be enjoyed solo or in up to four-player co-op, making it a strong and flexible choice for players who want both a compelling sci-fi mystery and a deep factory-building adventure.
