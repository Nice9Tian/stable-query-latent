This is a free-to-play open-world survival RPG set four years after a global catastrophe has left the world shattered and overrun by infected creatures. Players take on the role of a survivor in a seamless post-apocalyptic landscape, where survival depends not only on fighting hostile creatures but also on dealing with other human factions.

The experience combines PvE and PvP, creating constant pressure from both environmental dangers and rival survivors. Players must manage multiple survival indicators, including hunger, body type, vigor, health, hydration, and mood, all of which are influenced in real time by changing weather and environmental conditions such as rain, heat, snow, and storms. The game also supports character appearance and outfit customization, as well as player-to-player trading of weapons and resources.

Its open world is large and varied, featuring plains, mines, deserts, swamps, and abandoned cities. These regions include animals, plants, and dynamic weather systems, while exploration can lead to interactive environmental encounters, fortified strongholds, weekly events, and side quests. Players are encouraged to craft tools, master different weapons, build shelters, and search for allies in order to stay alive as infected threats can emerge at any time.

Base-building is a major part of the game. Players can construct and expand a home base inside a large manor area, using a flexible building system that offers more than 1,000 furniture and structure types and styles. The settlement can grow over time, and players can also form alliances with nearby outposts to defend against infected attacks and strengthen their chances of survival.

Teamplay and faction conflict are also central. Survivors can join a squad and face off against multiple opposing factions competing for territory and control. The setting presents a society split into different survival groups, each with its own rules and priorities, adding another layer of conflict beyond the infected.

Combat and traversal are supported by a broad equipment and vehicle system. Players can use a wide selection of weapons and armor, along with tactical tools such as melee weapons, drones, decoy explosives, and automated turrets. More than 50 vehicle types are available for supply runs and exploration, and players are encouraged to adapt tactics to different infected zones and environments in order to protect their base and expand their reach.
