**Undawn** is a free-to-play open-world survival RPG for mobile and PC, developed by LightSpeed Studios and published by Level Infinite. Set four years after a worldwide disaster, it drops players into a shattered apocalyptic world overrun by infected, where survival means facing both dangerous creatures and hostile humans. With a strong blend of PvP and PvE, Undawn offers a tense and atmospheric experience built around exploration, cooperation, and endurance.

At the heart of Undawn is a deep survival system that challenges players to adapt to a harsh and ever-changing environment. Rain, heat, snow, and storms all affect the world in real time, and players must monitor survival indicators such as Hunger, Body Type, Vigor, Health, Hydration, and Mood. These mechanics give weight to every journey and make survival feel grounded and meaningful. Players can also customize their character’s appearance and outfits, trade weapons and resources with others, and fight to protect what they have earned.

The game features a seamless open world created with Unreal Engine 4, filled with realistic detail and varied terrain. Players can travel across plains, mines, deserts, swamps, and abandoned cities, each with its own ecosystems of animals, plants, and weather patterns. Exploration is rewarded through interactive environmental items, embattled strongholds, dynamic weekly events, and side quests. Along the way, players learn to craft tools, master different weapons, build shelters, and search for survival buddies, all while staying alert to sudden infected encounters.

Undawn also shines in its base-building and settlement systems. Players can rebuild a home and create a base of operations within a massive 1-acre manor, using a robust free-building system that includes more than 1,000 types and styles of furniture and structures. Bases can grow over time, and players may search for other outposts to form alliances and fight back against the infected together. This gives the game a strong sense of progression, creativity, and shared survival.

Teamplay is another major strength. Players can join the Raven Squad and take part in a world where survivors have split into factions after the disaster. The game introduces conflicts with groups such as the Clowns, Eagles, Night Owls, and Reivers, adding territorial struggle to the broader fight for survival. This faction-driven world helps build tension and gives players clear reasons to unite, defend, and push forward.

Combat and equipment are equally varied. Undawn offers a wide selection of weapons and armor, along with tactical tools such as melee weapons, drones, decoy bombs, auto turrets, and more. Players can also make use of more than 50 vehicle types for supply runs and exploration, and tailor their tactics to different infected zones. With its mix of survival mechanics, open-world exploration, building, faction conflict, and combat variety, Undawn delivers a rich and ambitious apocalypse experience.
