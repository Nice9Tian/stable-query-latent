This story-driven cooking simulator invites players to manage a cozy pie shop and take on the role of a chef who must complete customer orders, earn enough money to keep the restaurant running, and steadily expand the business. Progress is tied to a chapter-based storyline, where each new phase introduces fresh rivals, new seasonal events, and additional restaurant features.

The gameplay centers on assembling custom orders with a range of ingredients such as spicy slices, savory links, and chopped bulbs, while also upgrading kitchen tools, unlocking new decorations, and changing the look of the shop. The experience blends restaurant management with light progression systems, encouraging players to improve both efficiency and presentation as they advance through the story.

A major part of the experience is its large cast of customers, each with distinct personalities and order preferences. The game features hundreds of customers and an enormous variety of possible orders, helping each day in the shop feel different. It also includes a fictional news broadcast that delivers pizza-related updates, adding flavor to the overall world.

The story is divided into five chapters, with four currently released and a fifth in development. Each chapter presents a different conflict: defending the shop against a nearby rival, facing a mysterious cult, competing against elite cooking contestants, and resisting an industrial takeover backed by robotic chefs. Later content is teased as introducing new ancient adversaries and changing the scene around the restaurant. Seasonal events and limited-time decorations appear throughout the year, adding more variety to the setting.

This version is a premium release with adjusted balancing and no in-app purchases. It is presented as a polished cooking game with simple controls, accessible gameplay, and a challenge level that grows over time. The game has been widely downloaded, has been localized into many languages, and is also available on a desktop platform. However, this edition uses a separate save system and does not transfer progress with other compatible devices.
