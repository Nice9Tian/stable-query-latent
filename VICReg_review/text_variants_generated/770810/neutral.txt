Good Pizza, Great Pizza is a story-driven cooking simulation game that lets players run a cozy pizza shop and manage the day-to-day demands of making pizzas for customers. In the game, players act as a chef, complete pizza orders, earn money to keep the restaurant open, and progress through an evolving storyline. Along the way, they can upgrade the restaurant with new toppings, decorations, and kitchen equipment, while also competing against pizza rivals across different chapters.

The game features five chapters in its overall structure, with four chapters currently released and a fifth in development. Each chapter introduces a different storyline and set of challenges. Chapter 1 focuses on defeating a grouchy neighboring rival to keep the new restaurant open. Chapter 2 involves mysterious pizza cult customers testing the player’s skills. Chapter 3 centers on competing with renowned pizza contestants and aiming for the top crown. Chapter 4 features Angelica Béchamel and robot chefs, with the player joining the WPA to defend traditional pizza restaurants. Chapter 5 is described as introducing new ancient rivals and scene-changing features. The game also adds seasonal events and limited-time decorations on a regular basis.

Good Pizza, Great Pizza includes over 300 customers, each with unique personalities and more than 1 million unique pizza orders. Its pizza-making system includes toppings such as pepperoni, sausage, onions, and more. Players can also upgrade equipment, choose from different decorations and shop styles, and follow the Pizza News Network, a pizza-themed newscast built into the game.
