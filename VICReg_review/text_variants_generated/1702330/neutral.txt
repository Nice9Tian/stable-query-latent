Rune Factory 5 is an action RPG set in the border town of Rigbarth, where mysterious events are disrupting the runes that maintain the balance between humanity and nature. As the newest ranger for the peacekeeping organization SEED, players are tasked with protecting the town, rounding up monsters with an official SEED-issued spell seal, and helping the community through farming, festivals, and friendships.

The game combines fast-paced combat with everyday life simulation elements. Players can venture into the wilderness with friends from Rigbarth and use link attacks that emphasize teamwork and strategy. Farming is a major part of the experience, with monsters able to assist as farmhands and crops grown on the backs of dragons. Town life also includes activities such as cooking, crafting, fishing contests, and seasonal festivals like stargazing events.

Rune Factory 5 also features relationship-building and romance. Players can get to know the eligible marriage candidates by completing their unique storylines, then fall in love, propose, and start a family. The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the main game and the Rune Factory 4 Marriage Candidates Outfit Set, adding 13 extra outfits inspired by Rune Factory 4 characters.
