Set in the border town of **Luneward**, this fantasy adventure combines action RPG combat, farming, and social simulation in a world where mysterious disturbances are disrupting the balance between people and nature. As a newly appointed member of a peacekeeping group called **Veyr**, the player is tasked with protecting the region by using an official sealing tool to subdue unruly monsters and restore order.

Outside of missions, the game focuses on helping the town grow through farming, festivals, and relationships. Players can work alongside the townspeople to support daily life, build friendships, and gradually become part of the community. As the situation in the land worsens, the story shifts toward a larger quest in which the player teams up with local residents to uncover the source of the crisis and prevent further chaos.

Combat is described as fast-paced and action-oriented, with exploration through expansive wilderness areas. Friends from the town can join the player in battle, where teamwork is rewarded through powerful coordinated attacks. The game also emphasizes farming in unusual ways: monsters can be tamed to assist with farm work, and crops can be grown in a variety of ways, including on the backs of dragons.

The town-life side of the experience offers a slower pace, with activities such as cooking, crafting, fishing, and seasonal festivals. These events provide opportunities to socialize and participate in contests or special outings. Relationship systems are also a major part of the game, as the player can befriend eligible townspeople, follow their individual storylines, pursue romance, propose marriage, and start a family.

A premium edition includes the main game along with an extra outfit pack based on characters from an earlier entry, adding multiple additional costumes for use in the adventure.
