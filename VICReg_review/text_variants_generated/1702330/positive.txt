**Rune Factory 5** invites players to sow the seeds of a new life in Rigbarth, a sleepy border town where mysterious events are disrupting the runes that maintain the balance between humanity and nature. As the newest ranger for the peacekeeping organization SEED, you are tasked with protecting the frontier town by using your official spell seal to round up unruly monsters and restore order.

At its heart, the game beautifully blends action, farming, and social life. When you are not out on a mission, you can work alongside the people of Rigbarth to help the town thrive through farming, festivals, and friendships. As the balance of peace begins to shift, you will team up with townsfolk and set out on a grand adventure to uncover the mystery threatening the land.

The combat is fast-paced and energetic, with vast wilderness areas to explore alongside friends from Rigbarth. Together, you can unleash powerful and stylish link attacks that reward both teamwork and strategy. Farming also reaches impressive heights, letting you tame monsters to help as farmhands while growing a wide variety of crops on the backs of dragons.

Life in Rigbarth offers plenty of peaceful pleasures as well. You can enjoy cooking, crafting, and fishing contests, or invite your sweetheart to stargazing during one of the town’s seasonal festivals. The game also encourages meaningful relationships, allowing you to befriend the eligible marriage candidates of Rigbarth, experience their unique storylines, fall in love, propose, and even start a family.

The Digital Deluxe Edition includes the main game plus the **Rune Factory 4 Marriage Candidates Outfit Set**, offering 13 additional outfits inspired by beloved characters from Rune Factory 4. With its mix of adventure, farming, romance, and community life, **Rune Factory 5** delivers a warm and exciting fantasy journey full of charm and variety.
