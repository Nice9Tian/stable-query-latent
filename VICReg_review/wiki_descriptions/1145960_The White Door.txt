The White Door is a puzzle video game developed by Dutch studio Rusty Lake. It was released on Steam and iOS on January 9, 2020 and on GOG.com on April 14, 2020.
The White Door's story follows Robert Hill, who wakes up in a mental health facility having lost his memory. The player needs to follow a set of instructions and puzzles to find about the background of the main character and help him restore his memory.
